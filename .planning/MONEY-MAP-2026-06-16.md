## Money map
errorcodefixes.com's real audience is NOT the homepage's consumer-DIY framing. It is industrial/commercial buyers landing deep on exact fault-code pages: the field technician with a downed $40k VFD/CNC/UPS/chiller (corporate P-card, zero price sensitivity), the facility/maintenance manager (recurring B2B buyer with purchasing authority), the pro repair business (highest value-per-visitor on the site), the consumer homeowner (mostly bots, a distraction), and the AI engine (a traffic MULTIPLIER, not a revenue line). The single unifying fact: a downed industrial asset is a REPAIR-vs-REPLACE decision ($2,000-$4,000 send-in repair) and a same-day field-service call ($100-$500+ exclusive commercial lead), both of which dwarf a 5% part commission, and the buyer often converts to $0 affiliate because they buy direct from AutomationDirect/Galco/OEM. So across every persona, the revenue ladder is the same: commercial lead-gen first (highest $/action, the only model that yields real dollars at 92 sessions), the chatbot as the routing layer second (it sits on the exact moment of decision and currently recommends nothing), fixed deep-linked affiliate third (floor revenue, ~70% of links are broken generic searches), email-list monetization fourth (the list already exists and collects, it just has no offer behind it), then display/sponsorship/premium/data-license which only matter at 10-100x traffic. I verified the repo directly: AdSense is NOT installed (zero adsbygoogle/ca-pub in source), Skimlinks is NOT installed (so all RepairClinic/Grainger rewrite revenue is phantom $0), there is NO Product/Offer JSON-LD (the part+price fact engines most want is unmarked), the chatbot worker recommends no part and the chatbot_query analytics event fires nowhere, ContractorCTA hardcodes the Angi-HVAC URL and ignores its equipment prop and renders on ZERO of the 79 Doosan/Fanuc CNC pages, EmailCapture IS live and beehiiv-segmented, and 3,900 of ~5,576 Amazon links are low-CVR /s? searches vs only 1,676 /dp/ ASIN links.
