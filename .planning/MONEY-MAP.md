## 2) PATTERNS — the families that drive the money (template these)

Six part categories repeat across every brand. Because the part is identical in function, exact-match buyable, and DIY-doable, **one page template per family ranks for dozens of brand+code combinations.** Build the template once, swap the part SKU and brand string.

1. **DRAIN PUMP family (the single richest vein).** Washer + dishwasher "won't drain / standing water" codes converge on a $40-120 pump. Codes: washer LG OE, Samsung 5E/5C, WP F9E1, Frigidaire E20; dishwasher LG OE, Bosch E24/E25, Samsung 5C, GE C2, WP F9E1, Frigidaire i20. Highest frequency, cleanest 1:1 part map, best EPC. Build these deepest.

2. **NO-HEAT family (dryer element + thermistor + thermal fuse + gas igniter).** "Not drying / no heat" is the top dryer complaint. The win is the **repair KIT** (element + thermal fuse + thermostat, or gas igniter + coils + sensor) which raises cart value above a single part. Codes: Frigidaire E64, Samsung HE/tE, WP F22/F01, plus the universal gas "won't heat" igniter page. Lead the CTA with Amazon repair kits here.

3. **DOOR LOCK / LID SWITCH / KEYPAD family.** "Won't start / won't spin / door won't lock" (appliances) and "stuck key" (oven/microwave keypads). Washer: Maytag F5E3, LG dE, Samsung dC. Oven: GE F7/F9, Samsung SE, WP F2E0. Microwave: Samsung SE, GE F3, WP F2. Clean $30-180 part, high won't-start symptom volume.

4. **WATER INLET VALVE family.** "Won't fill / no water to ice maker" across washer + dishwasher + fridge. One $25-80 valve covers three appliances. Codes: washer Samsung 4E/4C, LG IE, WP F8E1; dishwasher GE C5, LG IE; fridge Frigidaire 40E. Universal merchant availability.

5. **FRIDGE EVAPORATOR FAN + DEFROST + THERMISTOR family (the winnable fridge layer).** Avoid the compressor/sealed-system trap entirely. The evaporator fan motor and the 3-part defrost kit are the only DIY fridge money. Codes: Frigidaire SY EF, Samsung 21E/22E, LG FF/dH, GE FF/dF. "Fridge warm, freezer cold, compressor runs" symptom page feeds these.

6. **TEMP SENSOR / RTD family (oven).** Nearly every oven code is a $20-50 RTD probe with no pro-only escape hatch. Codes: GE F2/F3/F4, Frigidaire F10/F30/F31, Samsung E-27. Cheapest-to-rank, evergreen, 10-minute fix.

**Two cross-cutting revenue rules baked into the ranking:**
- **Multi-brand single-code leverage:** Whirlpool-family (WP/KitchenAid/Maytag/Amana share F9E1, F8E4, F2E0) and Frigidaire/Electrolux (share i20, i30, E64, F10) let ONE page rank for 2-4 brand queries with the same SKU list. Best effort-to-revenue ratio on the site.
- **Winnability tilt for a low-authority site:** lead with the less brand-dominated long tail — **Frigidaire, Electrolux, Bosch, GE, Maytag, KitchenAid, Kenmore** code+part pages — which OEM sites and RepairClinic under-serve. Samsung/LG/WP are high-volume but you rank slower.

**Merchant placement template (every money page):** lead the in-content CTA with **PartSelect (~7%) or RepairClinic (~6%, 365-day returns)** for the exact OEM part with install video, and offer **Amazon as the secondary "or get the repair kit"** to capture Prime buyers and kit bundles. Blended EPC ≈ $0.10-0.35, affiliate RPM ≈ $8-25 / 1,000 visits before display ads.
