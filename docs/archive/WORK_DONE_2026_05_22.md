## Shipped to production (live at errorcodefixes.com)

### 1. Awin MasterTag JS pixel installed

`src/layouts/Layout.astro` now loads `https://www.dwin1.com/2905561.js`
with `defer` before `</body>`. This is required for the 10 pending Awin
advertiser applications to attribute clicks once any approves. Without

fingerprint visitor → click → conversion.

Confirmed live: `curl https://errorcodefixes.com/posts/carrier-13-error-code/ | grep dwin1` returns the script tag.

### 2. UTM-tagged 970 outbound merchant URLs

`scripts/utm-on-affiliate-links.py` walked the whole 1,288-article catalog
and appended `?utm_source=errorcodefixes&utm_medium=affiliate&utm_campaign=<slug>`
to every outbound link pointing at one of 17 whitelisted merchants
(RepairClinic, Parts Town, AutomationDirect, Galco, Wolf Automation,
Grainger, MSC, McMaster, Home Depot, Lowe's, Johnstone Supply,
TruTech Tools, HVAC Parts Shop, PEX Universe, Wayfair, eBay,
PartSelect).

Idempotent — re-running is safe, no double-tagging.

Why: Plausible captures outbound `link.href` on click as the goal value.
Pre-UTM, every row in the "Outbound Link: Click" goal showed just the bare
URL with no way to attribute to article. Post-UTM, every row has
`utm_campaign=<slug>` and we can group revenue by article.

Amazon URLs were deliberately excluded because they already carry
`ascsubtag` (the Amazon-native equivalent) plus `tag=errorcodefixes-20`.

### 3. Stripped 517 leftover `?aff=PLACEHOLDER-*` placeholders

Same script. These were left behind by an earlier amazon-link injector
when it couldn't resolve real affiliate IDs. They're user-visible junk
(`https://www.galco.com?aff=PLACEHOLDER-GALCO`) that confer no
attribution. Skimlinks intercepts the click and adds its own tracking
at runtime, so removing them costs nothing.

### 4. Repaired 78 broken `*/PLACEHOLDER-*` URL paths

`scripts/fix-placeholder-urls.py` found 40 `amzn.to/PLACEHOLDER-<token>`
links that 404'd and converted them to working Amazon search URLs
(`amazon.com/s?k=<token>&tag=errorcodefixes-20`), plus 38 other-domain
placeholder paths that got stripped to bare root.

Zero `PLACEHOLDER` strings remain anywhere in `src/data/blog/`.

### 5. Awin link-wrapper component pre-built

`src/components/AwinLink.astro` is a drop-in `<a>` wrapper that emits
the correct `awin1.com/cread.php` click-tracking URL with `awinmid`,
`awinaffid=2905561`, and `clickref` (defaulting to current slug).
Unused today because no Awin merchant has approved. As soon as any of
the 10 pending apps approves, drop the `awinmid` from the Joined tab
