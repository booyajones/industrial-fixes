## Scoring Methodology

Each topic scored 0–100 across four dimensions:

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Commercial intent | 35% | Does the searcher have a wallet open? Urgency, severity, purchase readiness |
| Part / job AOV | 25% | Avg order value of the part or job the article drives |
| Search demand | 25% | Est. monthly organic searches for the primary + related queries |
| Affiliate fit | 15% | Can we earn commission today (Amazon, Impact.com)? How directly? |

**Composite = weighted sum → 0–100. Tier 1 = 80+. Tier 2 = 65–79. Tier 3 = 50–64.**
