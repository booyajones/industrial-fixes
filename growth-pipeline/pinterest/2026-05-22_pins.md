# Pinterest Pin Batch — 2026-05-22

**50 pins ready to publish.**

## Workflow
1. Sign up for a Pinterest business account at pinterest.com/business/create if you haven't (5 min, instant).
2. Build a single 1000x1500 vertical pin template in Canva (30 min, free tier). Brand-consistent header bar, error-code on left, fix-summary on right, save-for-later corner.
3. For each pin below, duplicate the template, swap title text, save as PNG.
4. Upload to Pinterest with the title, description, hashtags, and destination URL shown.
5. Schedule 1-2 pins per business day. Pinterest's algorithm rewards consistency over volume.
6. Optional: use Tailwind ($15/mo) to schedule the whole batch in one sitting.

## Critical do-not-do
- Do NOT pin commercial-refrigeration, VFD, or CNC content. Pinterest audience is residential.
- Do NOT use generic stock images. Real appliance failure images or branded reference charts only.
- Do NOT crowd the pin with text. Two phrases max.
