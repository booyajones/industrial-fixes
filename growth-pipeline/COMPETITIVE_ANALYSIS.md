## Where competitors actually beat us

1. **Domain Authority.** Our DA is probably 5-15 (new domain, few backlinks).
   Top competitors: cnccookbook (~50), partstown (~70), doityourself (~80),
   justanswer (~85). This is the single biggest gap and the slowest to close.
   **Mitigation:** outreach pipeline runs Tue-Fri 10:30am ET. 11 personalized
   pitches sent so far; expand to 50+ over next 30 days.

2. **Manufacturer official-source position.** When someone searches "Carrier
   58STA error code", the actual Carrier.com page outranks us on brand-trust
   signals. We can't beat that on authority, but we can on UX (Carrier's
   PDFs are behind login walls; ours load in 1 sec).

3. **Forum / community Q&A.** cnczone, justanswer, doityourself have
   accumulated thousands of community threads that rank for long-tail
   queries. We can't replicate this, but we can rank ABOVE them on the
   head terms ("Carrier 13 error code") with our depth.

4. **Specific deep articles.** dimaticcontrol's 8-9k word Carrier 13 article
   is probably better than ours. We need to selectively expand the top 50
   highest-traffic articles to match this depth.

## How "leadership" is actually defined for SEO

Three measurable signals to claim leader status:

1. **Rank #1 for ≥10 head terms** like "Carrier error codes", "Fanuc alarm
   list", "Hoshizaki error codes" (currently we don't rank #1 for any of
   these).

2. **>5 referring domains from authoritative sites** (cnccookbook,
   partstown, manufacturer docs, trade publications). Currently 0.

3. **Cited by AI assistants** when users ask brand-and-code questions.
   Currently chatgpt.com is starting to send referral traffic — early
   signal but real.

Hitting all 3 = market leader. Hitting 2 = strong contender. Hitting 1 =
where we are today.
