## Problem (10-15 seconds)

> Mini split P5 means water is backing up in the drain pan or the condensate pump is dead. Pump replacement is the obvious fix and usually wrong.

**On-screen text:** "Your equipment this code: not what you think"

## Fix sequence (25-30 seconds — show each step on screen)

**Step 1:** Pour a cup of one-to-one vinegar in the drain pan. Run cool mode 20 minutes.

**Step 2:** If P5 clears, algae was the issue, not the pump.

**Step 3:** If it does not clear, then test the pump float and pump motor before replacing.
