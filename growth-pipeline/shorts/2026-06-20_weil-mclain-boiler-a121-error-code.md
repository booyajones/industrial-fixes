## Fix sequence (25-30 seconds — show each step on screen)

**Step 1:** Pull the flame sensor — single rod with a porcelain base. Clean it with fine sandpaper, not steel wool.

**Step 2:** Check flame microamps after cleaning. Should read 2 to 6 microamps DC.

**Step 3:** Below 0.7 microamps after cleaning means the sensor is shot. Replace it.
