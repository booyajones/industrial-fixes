## Fix sequence (25-30 seconds — show each step on screen)

**Step 1:** Pull the filter. If it is grey or matted, swap it for a MERV 8 or MERV 11 max.

**Step 2:** Check the blower wheel. Even half an inch of dust cuts CFM by 30 percent.

**Step 3:** Test the limit switch with a multimeter — should read closed below 130 F.
