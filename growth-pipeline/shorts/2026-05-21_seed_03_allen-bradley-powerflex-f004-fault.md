## Fix sequence (25-30 seconds — show each step on screen)

**Step 1:** Pull the fault history first. Parameters D361 through D365 on a PowerFlex 525.

**Step 2:** Check incoming line voltage with a meter that captures peaks — 5 second dips kill drives without showing up on a slow meter.
