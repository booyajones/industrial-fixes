## Fix sequence (25-30 seconds — show each step on screen)

**Step 1:** Pull the condenser coil and clean it with a fin comb plus coil cleaner.

**Step 2:** Check water flow over the evaporator — should be 4 gallons per minute.

**Step 3:** Only after both of those, gauge the system and check superheat.
