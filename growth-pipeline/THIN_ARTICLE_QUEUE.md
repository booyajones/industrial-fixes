## Per-article expansion pattern

Each article should grow to 1,500-3,000 words with:

1. **Code table** with every numeric or alphanumeric fault, plain-English
   description, severity (warning/fault/lockout), and "first thing to
   check" — sortable, scannable, ranks well as a featured snippet.

2. **Top 3-5 codes deep-dive** with diagnostic trees: voltage / resistance
   ranges per component, OEM part numbers with current pricing context,
   when to call a tech.

3. **Programming-related codes** if applicable (VFD parameter faults, etc.)
   with example parameter values.

4. **"Reset procedure"** section — keypad sequence, power-cycle protocol,
   when reset clears the fault vs. when it requires service.

5. **Related articles** internal-link block (already auto-generated by
   add_internal_links.py).

6. **Schema markup** for HowTo / FAQ to win rich snippets.
