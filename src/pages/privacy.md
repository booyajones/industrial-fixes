## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 22, 2026

Industrial Error Code Fixes (`errorcodefixes.com`) takes your privacy
seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it,
and how you can control it.

### Information we collect automatically

When you visit the site, our servers and our analytics providers
automatically receive standard request data:

- IP address (truncated for analytics)
- Browser type and version, operating system, device class
- Pages you visit, search queries on this site, time spent on each page
- Referring URL (the page that linked you here, if any)

We collect this through:

- **Google Analytics 4** - first-party `_ga` cookies, anonymized IP
- **Cloudflare Pages** - server logs and edge analytics
- **Google Search Console** - search query and ranking data Google
  shares with the site owner

We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers unless you
voluntarily provide them (for example by emailing us at the address
listed below).

### Cookies and similar technologies

We set the following cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 visitor statistics | up to 24 months |
| Amazon Associates cookies | Affiliate attribution if you click an Amazon product link | 24 hours |
| Awin / Impact / Skimlinks cookies | Affiliate attribution if you click a tracked product link | typically 30 days |
| AdSense cookies (`__gads`, `__gpi`) | Advertising personalization, when enabled | up to 13 months |

You can clear these cookies at any time in your browser settings, or
install a tracking blocker. The site will continue to function normally
with cookies disabled.

### Advertising

We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partner
ad networks may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based
on your prior visits to this site or other sites. You can opt out of
personalized advertising at
[adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based
on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's
use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads
based on visits to this site or other sites on the internet.

### Affiliate links

This site participates in affiliate advertising programs. When you
click an outbound product link, the destination retailer may set a
cookie to track that the click came from us. If you make a qualifying
purchase, we may earn a commission. This does not affect the price you
pay. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/disclosure/) for the full list of
partner programs.

### Information sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share data with our analytics
and advertising providers (Google, Cloudflare, affiliate networks)
strictly for the purposes listed above.

If we ever receive a binding legal request (subpoena, court order),
we will comply only to the extent legally required.

### Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request:

- Access to the data we hold about you
- Correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion of your data
- A copy of your data in a portable format
- Withdrawal of consent for analytics or advertising

To exercise any of these rights, email
[info@errorcodefixes.com](mailto:info@errorcodefixes.com) with the
subject line "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

**California residents:** under the CCPA you also have the right to
know what categories of personal information we have collected about
you and to opt out of any sale. We do not sell personal information.

**EU and UK residents:** under GDPR / UK GDPR you have additional
rights including the right to lodge a complaint with your local
supervisory authority.

### Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe we have,
please contact us and we will delete it.

### Updates to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change. The "Last updated"
date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes
are announced on the site for at least 30 days before taking effect.
