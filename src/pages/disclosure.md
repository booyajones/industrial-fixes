## Affiliate Disclosure

Industrial Error Code Fixes (errorcodefixes.com) is a participant in affiliate
advertising programs designed to help readers find the right replacement parts
and tools. When you click an outbound product link on this site and make a
qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission **at no extra cost to you**.

We participate in:

- **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program** — an affiliate advertising
  program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by
  advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from
  qualifying purchases.
- **Impact partner network**, including (but not limited to) Repair Clinic,
  SupplyHouse, Grainger, JB Tools, and JRacenstein.

### How this affects you

- Affiliate links never change the price you pay.
- We do not write fix guides around products. Recommended parts are chosen
  based on the actual diagnostic step in the article — typically with the
  manufacturer's official part number first.
- We surface multiple sources where possible (Amazon plus a parts specialist)
  so you can pick the vendor that's fastest or cheapest for your situation.
- Editorial judgement is independent of which retailer pays the highest
  commission.

### Identifying affiliate links

Outbound product links may include affiliate tracking parameters (for example
`tag=errorcodefixes-20` on Amazon links or `irgwc=...` on Impact links). Other
outbound links — to manufacturer documentation, service bulletins, or
non-commercial references — are not affiliate links.

### Editorial standards

Repair guidance is written by, or reviewed against, primary-source service
documentation. Industrial Error Code Fixes is not affiliated with the
equipment manufacturers we reference. Brand names and model numbers are used
only to identify the equipment a fix applies to.
