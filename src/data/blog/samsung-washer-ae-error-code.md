## Samsung Washer AE Error Code — What It Means

The AE error code on a Samsung washer indicates a signal communication error between internal control electronics. Samsung groups AE, AC, AC6, ACb, AE3, AE4, AE5, AE6, AE8, and AEB together as communication faults. The AE6 variant specifically points to an inverter communication issue. This is not a water level, drain, or load balance problem. The fault lies in the washer's internal control communication path, usually between the main control board and the inverter or motor control board.

In many cases the error is a temporary electronic glitch that clears after a power cycle. When it persists, the root cause is typically a loose or damaged wire harness connection, a failed inverter PCB, or a failed main control board.

## Common Causes

- **Temporary electronic glitch** A brief software or communication hiccup that resolves itself after power is removed and the system restarts.
- **Loose or unseated wire harness connectors** Vibration or improper seating causes intermittent or lost signal between the main control board and the inverter or motor control module.
- **Damaged wire harness or connector pins** Bent pins, frayed insulation, or heat damage interrupt the communication path between control boards.
- **Failed inverter PCB** The inverter board that drives the motor has lost the ability to communicate with the main control, especially common with AE6 codes.
- **Failed main control board** The primary control PCB can no longer send or receive signals to other modules in the washer.
- **Moisture intrusion or connector corrosion** Water exposure or long-term humidity creates resistance or shorts in connectors along the communication harness.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off the washer and unplug it** from the wall outlet, wait three to five minutes, then restore power and attempt to run a cycle again to see if the code clears.
2. **Remove the back or top panel** (depending on your washer type) to gain access to the inverter board and main control board, following your model's disassembly steps.
3. **Inspect all wire harness connectors** running between the main control board and the inverter or motor control board for proper seating, bent or corroded pins, and any visible heat discoloration or damaged insulation.
4. **Reseat each connector firmly** by unplugging and reconnecting it, ensuring you hear or feel a positive click and that no pins are misaligned.
5. **Check the inverter PCB and main control PCB** for visible signs of burn marks, bulging capacitors, or cracked solder joints that would indicate board failure.
6. **Run a test cycle** after reseating connectors and inspecting boards to confirm whether the AE code returns or the washer operates normally.
7. **Replace the inverter PCB or main control board** if the error persists after all connections have been verified and no harness damage is found, consulting your model's service manual for board-specific part numbers.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the error code returns after you have completed a power reset and inspected and reseated all accessible connectors. Board-level diagnosis often requires a service manual with model-specific wiring diagrams and sometimes a multimeter to trace communication signals. If you are uncomfortable working inside the control cabinet or if your washer is still under warranty, a Samsung-authorized technician can retrieve fault logs and replace boards under coverage. Persistent AE codes that do not clear with basic resets almost always require part replacement rather than adjustment.
