## State Water Heater E106 Error Code — What It Means

The E106 error code does not appear in verified State Water Heaters (A. O. Smith) service documentation, so its exact meaning cannot be confirmed. State tankless units under the A. O. Smith family commonly trigger ignition-related faults when air intake screens are blocked, gas pressure is low, or water flow is restricted by a dirty inlet filter. Without an official code definition, treat E106 as a potential ignition or sensor communication fault and work through the most common physical blockages and supply issues that prevent the unit from lighting or operating normally.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screen** Lint, dust, or debris covering the combustion air inlet restricts oxygen flow and prevents ignition.
- **Low or interrupted gas supply** Insufficient gas pressure or a closed isolation valve stops the burner from firing.
- **Restricted water flow** A clogged inlet filter or closed fixture valve reduces flow below the minimum threshold needed to activate the unit.
- **Sensor or wiring fault** A loose connection or failed sensor may prevent the control board from reading correct ignition or flow signals.

## When to Call a Pro

If cleaning the air intake and inlet filter and verifying gas supply do not clear the E106 code, call a licensed plumber or gas technician. Persistent ignition faults can involve internal gas-valve problems, faulty flame sensors, or control-board communication errors that require diagnostic tools and access to the manufacturer's full service manual. Because the exact meaning of E106 is not published in available State documentation, professional diagnosis is the safest path when basic cleaning steps fail.
