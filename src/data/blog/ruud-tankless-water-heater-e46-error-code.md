## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E46 Error — What It Means

The E46 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters signals a fault detected by the unit's control system. The exact meaning of E46 varies by model and firmware version, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit cover for the specific definition. Many error codes in this range relate to sensor issues, safety lockouts, or communication problems between components.

Because Ruud does not publish a universal code list across all tankless models, the best first step is to verify the code definition in your documentation. Once you know what the code represents on your specific unit, you can target the right component or system for testing and repair.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many homeowners replace the main circuit board when the true cause is a single faulty sensor or loose wiring harness. Always test sensors with a multimeter and inspect all connectors before ordering expensive control boards.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty temperature or flow sensor (~35%)** A sensor that has drifted out of calibration or failed completely will trigger a fault code and prevent normal operation.
- **Loose or corroded wiring harness (~25%)** Vibration and heat can loosen connectors or corrode terminals, interrupting signals between the control board and sensors.
- **Control board software glitch (~20%)** A transient power surge or minor firmware hiccup can log a false code that clears with a power cycle.
- **Failed control board (~15%)** The main circuit board can develop solder cracks or component failures over time, registering spurious error codes.
- **Incorrect installation or configuration (~5%)** DIP switches set wrong or incompatible gas valve settings can cause the unit to report a fault during startup.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the electrical breaker and close the inlet water valve to make the unit safe to service.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the fasteners and lifting the panel away to expose the control board and wiring.
3. **Locate the error code definition** on the wiring diagram inside the cover or in the owner's manual to understand what E46 means for your model.
4. **Inspect all wiring connectors** at the control board, sensors, and gas valve for loose pins, corrosion, or burn marks; clean and reseat as needed.
5. **Test each sensor** with a digital multimeter set to measure resistance, comparing readings to the values in the service manual for your model.
6. **Replace the faulty sensor or board** if testing identifies a failed component; match the part number printed on the original part.
7. **Restore power and water** and run a hot-water tap to verify that the unit ignites normally and the error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical wiring, if testing sensors requires equipment you do not own, or if the fault persists after replacing the obvious failed component. Tankless water heaters combine electrical, gas, and water systems, and misdiagnosis can lead to expensive parts orders or unsafe conditions. A qualified technician will have model-specific service literature, the correct test equipment, and the gas-fitting expertise to safely complete the repair and verify proper combustion afterward.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
