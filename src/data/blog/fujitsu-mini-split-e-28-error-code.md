## Fujitsu E:28 Error Code — What It Means

E:28 on a Fujitsu mini-split indicates a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units. The two halves of your system are not able to exchange data at startup or during operation. This is not a refrigerant or sensor issue. Instead, the problem lies in the wiring, connectors, or control boards that allow the indoor evaporator and outdoor condenser to talk to each other.

Because Fujitsu fault codes can vary by model and remote type, always verify the exact meaning of E:28 in your specific unit's service manual or fault table. That said, manufacturer troubleshooting material groups this type of error under indoor-outdoor communication failure, with diagnostic focus on interconnect wiring, PCB connectors, and the main or controller boards.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or damaged interconnect wiring** The cable between indoor and outdoor units may be loose at terminals, reversed, damaged, or poorly crimped.
- **Loose connector on a PCB** The indoor controller PCB, outdoor main PCB, or external I/O board may have a connector that has worked loose or become corroded.
- **Defective main or controller PCB** The main board in the outdoor unit or the controller board in the indoor unit may have failed and cannot send or receive communication signals.
- **Wrong indoor unit configuration** The indoor unit type may not match the outdoor unit or system configuration, or addressing and setup are incorrect.
- **Voltage drop or electrical noise** Poor grounding, voltage sag on the supply line, or electrical interference can disrupt the low-voltage communication path.
- **Mis-terminated remote or control wiring** In wired-remote systems, screened cable may be terminated incorrectly or wiring rules may be violated, causing communication failure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker and wait two minutes, then restore power to see if the fault clears on its own after a full reset.
2. **Verify your exact model number** and consult the unit's service manual or fault-code table to confirm E:28's specific meaning for your model and remote type.
3. **Inspect the interconnect wiring** between indoor and outdoor units for loose terminal screws, reversed conductors, visible damage, or poor crimps, and re-seat or repair as needed.
4. **Check all PCB connectors** on the indoor controller board, outdoor main board, and any external I/O board for looseness, corrosion, or incomplete seating, then firmly reconnect.
5. **Confirm indoor-unit configuration** matches the outdoor unit and that any addressing, DIP switches, or system-setup parameters are correct per the installation manual.
6. **Test for supply-line problems** by checking that line voltage is stable, grounding is solid, and there is no obvious electrical noise or voltage drop at either unit.
7. **Replace the suspected PCB** (main board or controller board) if wiring, connectors, and configuration are all verified and the E:28 fault persists, then cycle power and retest communication.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not confident working inside electrical enclosures on both indoor and outdoor units, if you cannot locate or identify PCB connectors, or if the fault persists after you have verified wiring and reseated all connectors. Communication-fault diagnosis often requires a multimeter, model-specific wiring diagrams, and the ability to safely test low-voltage control circuits while the system is energized. A qualified technician can also confirm that the indoor and outdoor units are properly matched and configured, and can substitute suspect boards under controlled conditions to isolate the defective component.
