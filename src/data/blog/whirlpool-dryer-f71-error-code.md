## Whirlpool Dryer F71 Error Code — What It Means

The F71 error code on a Whirlpool dryer signals a communication failure or internal fault within the electronic control system. This typically means the main control board is not communicating properly with other components, or the control board itself has a fault. The code may appear on the display during normal operation or after a power interruption.

Because Whirlpool dryer error codes can vary by model, always cross-reference your specific model number with the owner's manual or wiring diagram. In most cases, F71 points to a control board issue rather than a mechanical or sensor problem.

## Before You Replace Anything

Some homeowners replace the control board without first checking harness connectors and wiring. Reseat all connectors and inspect for corrosion or broken pins before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed main control board (~60%)** Internal circuit failure or component degradation on the board prevents normal communication and triggers the fault code.
- **Loose or corroded harness connector (~25%)** Poor contact at the control board or user-interface connector interrupts signal flow and mimics a board failure.
- **Damaged wiring harness (~10%)** Chafed insulation or broken wires between the control board and other modules cause intermittent or permanent communication loss.
- **Power surge or voltage spike (~5%)** A recent electrical event can corrupt firmware or damage the control board, triggering persistent error codes.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** from the wall outlet or turn off the circuit breaker to remove all power before beginning any work.
2. **Remove the console panel** by taking out the screws along the top or rear edge and tilting or lifting the panel to expose the control board and wiring harness.
3. **Reseat all connectors** on the main control board and user-interface board, pressing firmly until they click into place, and inspect pins for corrosion or damage.
4. **Inspect the wiring harness** for chafed insulation, broken wires, or burn marks along the entire run from the control board to other components.
5. **Replace the main control board** if connectors and wiring are intact, transferring each harness plug one at a time to avoid cross-connections and noting their positions.
6. **Reassemble the console panel**, secure all screws, and restore power to the dryer to verify the error code no longer appears.
7. **Run a test cycle** with a small load to confirm normal operation and that no additional codes are displayed on the control panel.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electronic control boards or if the dryer shares a high-voltage circuit with other appliances. Also seek service if you have replaced the control board and the F71 error persists, since the fault may lie in a secondary module or require advanced diagnostic equipment. Technicians can test signal continuity and voltage levels that are difficult to measure without specialized tools.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $100-200 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $200-350.
