## Mitsubishi Mini-Split P6 Error — What It Means

The P6 code on a Mitsubishi Electric mini-split signals a freezing or overheating safeguard operation. The indoor unit has shut down because the evaporator coil is getting too cold during cooling mode (below approximately 5°F) or too hot during heating mode (above approximately 158°F). This protection prevents damage to the heat exchanger.

The system uses temperature sensors on the indoor coil to detect these extremes and locks out operation until the condition clears. The fault is almost always triggered by restricted airflow across the coil or refrigerant-side problems that cause abnormal coil temperatures.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or clogged air filters** Blocked filters reduce airflow across the indoor coil, causing the coil to freeze in cooling or overheat in heating.
- **Dirty blower wheel or indoor coil** Dust and debris buildup on the blower wheel or evaporator coil restricts airflow and heat transfer.
- **Indoor fan motor or fan control fault** A failing fan motor or control board issue can prevent the blower from moving enough air across the coil.
- **Low refrigerant charge or refrigerant restriction** Insufficient refrigerant or a blockage in the refrigerant circuit causes abnormal coil temperatures and triggers the protection.
- **Air recirculation or airflow bypass** Short-cycling return air or improper installation can starve the coil of fresh airflow.
- **Faulty heat-exchanger temperature sensor** A damaged or drifted thermistor on the indoor coil can falsely signal a freezing or overheating condition.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if the fault persists after cleaning filters and checking airflow, or if you are not comfortable working with refrigerant or electrical diagnostics. Refrigerant work requires recovery equipment, gauges, and EPA certification. A pro should also diagnose sensor or control-board faults, verify system charge and pressures with accurate instruments, and perform any refrigerant leak repair or component replacement that involves brazing or soldering refrigerant lines.
