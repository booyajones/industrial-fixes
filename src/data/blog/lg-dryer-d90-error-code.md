## LG Dryer d90 Error Code — What It Means

d90 is a flow sense airflow warning. The dryer measures how restricted the exhaust is, and d90 means roughly 90 percent of the airflow is blocked by lint or a crushed vent.

The higher the number after the d, the worse the blockage. At d90 the dryer dries slowly and runs hot, so the warning should not be ignored.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged lint filter** A filter packed with lint chokes airflow before it even reaches the vent.
- **Blocked exhaust hose** Lint builds up inside the flexible hose behind the dryer and restricts flow.
- **Crushed or kinked hose** A dryer pushed too close to the wall crushes the hose and cuts airflow.
- **Clogged outside vent** Lint or a bird nest at the exterior vent hood blocks the exhaust.
- **Vent run too long** An over-long or twisty duct run adds resistance the blower cannot overcome.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Clean the lint filter** and wash it if it has a waxy film from dryer sheets.
2. **Pull the dryer out** and disconnect the exhaust hose from the back.
3. **Vacuum the hose** and the dryer outlet to clear packed lint.
4. **Straighten the hose** so it is not crushed or sharply kinked behind the dryer.
5. **Clear the outside vent** by removing lint and debris from the exterior hood.
6. **Reconnect everything** and leave a few inches of space behind the dryer.
7. **Run a cycle** to confirm the d90 warning clears.

## When to Call a Pro

If you cleaned the filter, hose, and outside vent but d90 keeps showing, the blockage may sit deep in a long wall duct. A vent cleaning service or appliance pro has the tools to clear a full duct run and confirm airflow is restored.
