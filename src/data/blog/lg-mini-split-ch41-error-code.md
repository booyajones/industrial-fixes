## LG Mini Split CH41 Error Code — What It Means

CH41 indicates a problem with the discharge temperature sensor circuit on your LG mini split. The control board cannot read the sensor that monitors the temperature of refrigerant leaving the compressor. LG's technical documentation describes this as an inverter compressor discharge sensor fault or indoor unit discharge air sensor fault, depending on the platform. The sensor itself, its wiring, or the board input reading that sensor is at fault.

## Common Causes

- **Failed discharge sensor thermistor** The sensor can open, short, or drift out of the correct resistance range, preventing the board from reading temperature correctly.
- **Damaged or loose sensor wiring** Pinched, corroded, or disconnected harness wires between the sensor and PCB break the signal path.
- **Connector contact fault** Corrosion, bent pins, or moisture at the sensor plug or PCB header interrupt the connection.
- **Defective PCB input circuit** If the sensor and wiring test good, the control board's input or processing circuit has failed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the unit** at the breaker and wait two minutes to discharge capacitors before opening any panels.
2. **Locate the discharge sensor** on the refrigerant line leaving the compressor, noting the harness routing and connector at the outdoor PCB.
3. **Inspect the sensor harness and connector** for pinched wires, corrosion, moisture, or loose contact, reseating the plug firmly at both ends.
4. **Disconnect the sensor from the PCB** and measure its resistance with a multimeter, comparing to LG's published values (237 kΩ at 20°C, 168 kΩ at 30°C).
5. **Check that resistance changes with temperature** by warming the sensor gently, if it reads open, shorted, or stuck, replace the sensor.
6. **Test wiring continuity** from the sensor plug to the PCB header pins if the sensor measures correctly, verifying no breaks or high resistance in the harness.
7. **Replace the outdoor PCB** if the sensor and wiring both test good but the fault persists, then clear the error and retest for stable discharge temperature readings.

## When to Call a Pro

CH41 diagnosis requires multimeter work, refrigerant-side access, and interpretation of thermistor curves. If you are not comfortable working inside energized HVAC equipment or do not have the tools to measure sensor resistance and continuity, call a qualified HVAC technician. PCB replacement also demands proper handling of static-sensitive electronics and confirming the correct board revision for your system. Pros carry the diagnostic tables, replacement sensors, and boards specific to your LG model, and they can verify that the repair clears the fault without introducing new issues.
