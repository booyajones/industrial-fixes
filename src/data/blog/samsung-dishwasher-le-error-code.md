## Samsung Dishwasher LE Error Code — What It Means

The LE error code (sometimes displayed as LC) indicates that your Samsung dishwasher has detected moisture or water in the base pan under the tub, where it should not be. This is a leak-detection fault, not a motor or load problem. The dishwasher uses a sensor in the lower chassis area to monitor for leaks. When water or enough moisture is present, the unit halts operation and displays the code to prevent further flooding or damage.

## Common Causes

- **Leak at supply or drain hose connection** Water supply lines, drain hoses, or their fittings can develop leaks that drip into the base pan and trip the sensor.
- **Water already sitting in the base pan** Water from a previous leak, transport tilt, or splash can remain pooled in the base and trigger the sensor even after the original leak is fixed.
- **Damaged or misaligned door gasket** A worn, torn, or improperly seated door seal allows water to escape during the wash cycle and collect below.
- **Excess suds from too much detergent** Over-sudsing or using the wrong detergent type can cause overflow or foam buildup that reaches the leak sensor.
- **Faulty leak sensor or wet wiring** The moisture sensor itself or its connector may be wet, corroded, or defective and reporting a false positive.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and water** by unplugging the dishwasher and closing the shut-off valve under the sink to prevent additional leakage during inspection.
2. **Pull the unit forward** and inspect all visible hose connections, the supply line, drain hose, pump area, and the floor under the dishwasher for signs of water, stains, or mineral deposits.
3. **Tip the dishwasher back slightly** (with help) and check the base pan underneath for standing water or dampness. Use towels or a wet-dry vacuum to remove all moisture and dry the sensor and pan completely.
4. **Examine the door gasket** for tears, deformation, or debris that would prevent a tight seal. Clean the gasket and door edge, or replace the seal if damaged.
5. **Verify the water inlet valve** is fully open and inspect the inlet screen for clogs or debris that could affect water flow or cause pressure issues.
6. **Reconnect power and run a short test cycle** after drying everything and confirming no visible leaks. If the code reappears immediately, check sensor connectors for moisture or corrosion.
7. **Replace the identified faulty component** such as the leak sensor, door gasket, or leaking hose. If the code persists after all leak sources are repaired and the unit is bone dry, the control board may need replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you cannot locate any visible leak after thorough inspection, if the base pan and sensor are completely dry but the code returns immediately, or if you are not comfortable tilting the dishwasher or accessing internal components. A technician can test the sensor circuit, check for hidden leaks behind panels or at the pump, and verify control board function. Also call if you have repaired the leak and replaced the sensor but the LE code still appears after a hard power reset.
