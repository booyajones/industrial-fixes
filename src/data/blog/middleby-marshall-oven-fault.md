### Err 1 — Thermocouple Fault
The cavity temperature sensor is open or failed. Middleby Marshall uses Type K thermocouples. Disconnect the thermocouple from the controller and measure resistance — an open thermocouple reads infinite resistance. Check the terminal block at the controller for corrosion. Replace the thermocouple if open or reading outside normal range.

### Err 3 — Ignition Failure
Gas ignition did not occur during the timed ignition trial. Steps:
1. Verify gas is on — check upstream shut-off and manual valve at oven
2. Check gas pressure at manifold — natural gas 4–7 inches WC, propane 10–11 inches WC
3. Listen for spark during ignition — if no spark, check igniter lead and ignition module
4. Check flame sensor rod — clean with fine steel wool if coated with grease
5. Check gas valve coil — should read 30–50 ohms

### HI LIM — High Limit Tripped
The high limit thermostat (non-resettable or manual-reset type depending on model) has tripped. Allow the oven to cool completely before attempting reset. Find and correct the cause of the temperature excursion before returning to service. Check: setpoint on controller, cavity thermocouple accuracy, and insulation condition.

## WOW Oven Notes

Middleby Marshall WOW ovens use dual-sided impingement (top and bottom). Both sets of jets must be clear for proper baking. Blocked jet fingers cause hot spots and can drive up oven temperature.

## Jump to Fix

- **Err 1** → Check thermocouple connector → Measure resistance → Replace
- **Err 3** → Check gas → Inspect igniter → Test flame sensor → Check gas valve
- **HI LIM** → Cool oven → Identify root cause → Reset thermostat

## When to Call a Pro
Middleby Marshall authorized service centers handle gas system repairs and control board replacement. For gas component work, use a licensed commercial kitchen technician.
