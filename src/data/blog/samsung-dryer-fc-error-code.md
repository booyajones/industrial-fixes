## Samsung Dryer FC Error Code — What It Means

FC, FE, and 1 FC are frequency errors on Samsung dryers. They indicate the control board has detected an invalid or abnormal power-source frequency coming into the appliance. This is not a vent blockage or airflow problem. The issue is almost always related to the electrical supply feeding the dryer, whether from unstable incoming power, incorrect hookup, or a poor connection at the outlet or cord.

## Common Causes

- **Incorrect or unstable incoming power frequency** The wall supply delivers inconsistent or out-of-spec frequency that the control cannot accept.
- **Wrong electrical setup for the market** The dryer is installed on a grid or in a country it was not designed for, causing frequency mismatch.
- **Extension cord or adapter use** Nonstandard hookups can distort or interrupt the supply signal seen by the control board.
- **Loose or defective cord, outlet, or breaker connection** Intermittent or poor contact at any point in the supply path can create abnormal power delivery.
- **Control board misreading the supply** If the incoming power checks out stable and correct, the main control may be faulty.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the code.** Verify the display shows FC, FE, or 1 FC and note whether it appears on startup or during a cycle.
2. **Power-cycle the dryer.** Unplug or turn off the breaker for two minutes, then restore power and retest. Samsung notes that restarting may clear a frequency error.
3. **Inspect the installation.** Confirm the dryer is on a dedicated circuit with no extension cords or adapters, and that the plug, outlet, and breaker match the model's electrical requirements.
4. **Measure the supply at the outlet.** Use a multimeter to check line voltage and stability at the wall receptacle to confirm it delivers appropriate power for the dryer's market.
5. **Check the cord and terminal connections.** Look for loose, overheated, or damaged wires at the dryer's terminal block and inside the outlet box.
6. **If the code returns after a reset, treat it as a persistent supply fault.** Samsung advises service when the error continues, so recheck all supply-side connections and wiring before suspecting internal components.
7. **Replace the control board only if the supply is verified stable and correct.** If every external electrical check passes and the code persists, the main control is the remaining suspect.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working inside a 240 V dryer circuit, if you cannot verify stable incoming power with test equipment, or if the code returns after a power cycle and you have confirmed the outlet and cord are in good condition. Samsung directs persistent frequency errors to service rather than repeated resets. A qualified appliance technician or electrician can measure line quality, inspect branch wiring, and determine whether the fault is in the supply or the control board.
