## Maytag Refrigerator FF Error Code — What It Means

The FF code on Maytag refrigerators is not a single universal fault defined by the manufacturer across all models. In practice, FF most often appears when the unit has been accidentally placed into demo (showroom) mode, which disables cooling but keeps the display active. On some model families, FF can also indicate a freezer fan fault, typically caused by frost or ice buildup blocking the fan blade or a fan motor that is not running normally. Because Maytag does not publish a consistent FF definition in their official literature, the exact meaning depends on your specific model and how the control board behaves.

If your refrigerator is not cooling and the display shows FF, check first whether demo mode is active. If cooling works but you still see the code, or if you hear no fan noise from the freezer, the evaporator fan circuit is the likely culprit. Frost accumulation from door seal problems or extended door-open events is the most common field cause of fan restriction.

## Common Causes

- **Demo mode activated** The control was accidentally placed into showroom mode, which disables cooling and may display FF on the panel.
- **Frost or ice blocking the freezer fan** Ice buildup on the evaporator coils prevents the fan blade from turning, triggering a fan fault or FF code.
- **Failed freezer fan motor** The evaporator fan motor has burned out or is not operating normally, so the control registers a fault.
- **Door seal or alignment issues** Poor door closure allows warm, humid air into the freezer, which freezes on the coils and restricts airflow or binds the fan.
- **Wiring or connector fault in the fan circuit** A damaged wire, loose connector, or control board issue prevents the fan from receiving power or signals correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the FF code returns after a full defrost and power reset, if you cannot exit demo mode using the button sequence, or if the freezer fan motor tests require disassembly of the evaporator cover and sealed-system components. A pro should also diagnose the issue if you see repeated frost buildup despite good door seals, because that may point to a defrost heater, defrost thermostat, or control board fault that is not directly related to the fan. Any work involving the control board, sealed refrigeration system, or electrical testing beyond the fan motor is best handled by a qualified appliance repair service.
