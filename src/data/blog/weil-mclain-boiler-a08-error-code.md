## Weil-McLain Boiler A08 Error Code — What It Means

The A08 error code on Weil-McLain boilers typically indicates a low water condition or a problem with the low water cutoff system. The boiler's safety system has detected that water level has dropped below the safe operating threshold, or the probe and sensor assembly that monitors water level is malfunctioning. When this code appears, the boiler will lock out and refuse to fire until the issue is resolved and the system is reset.

Because the exact meaning of error codes can vary across Weil-McLain models and control types, always consult your specific boiler's installation and operating manual or the wiring diagram inside the jacket to confirm the code definition for your unit. Low water conditions can result from leaks, faulty automatic fill valves, or closed water supply valves, while sensor problems often stem from mineral buildup on the probe, loose wiring, or a failed low water cutoff module.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the entire low water cutoff assembly before checking the actual water level in the boiler or cleaning mineral deposits off the probe. First verify the sight glass or gauge shows proper water level and inspect the probe for scale buildup.

## Common Causes

- **Low water level in the boiler (~40%)** A slow leak, closed water feed valve, or failed automatic fill valve allows water level to drop below the low water cutoff probe, triggering the safety lockout.
- **Mineral buildup on the low water cutoff probe (~30%)** Scale and sediment coat the probe over time, insulating it from the water and causing false low-water readings even when the boiler is properly filled.
- **Failed low water cutoff control module (~15%)** The electronic or mechanical low water cutoff device itself has failed and sends a false alarm signal to the boiler control board.
- **Loose or corroded wiring at the low water cutoff (~10%)** Poor electrical connections at the probe or control module interrupt the signal and the control interprets the open circuit as a low water condition.
- **Faulty boiler control board (~5%)** The main control board misreads or misinterprets the signal from an otherwise healthy low water cutoff system and posts the A08 code in error.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the boiler at the disconnect switch or circuit breaker and allow the unit to cool.
2. **Check the sight glass or gauge** on the side of the boiler to verify the current water level against the manufacturer's recommended fill mark.
3. **Inspect for leaks** around all piping, valves, circulators, and fittings; repair any leaks and note water loss before proceeding.
4. **Verify the automatic water feed valve** is open and the pressure-reducing valve is set to the correct fill pressure, then manually add water if needed to bring the level to the proper mark.
5. **Locate the low water cutoff probe** (usually mounted in a tapping on the boiler or in a separate chamber) and disconnect power, then remove the probe assembly and inspect for mineral scale, rust, or sediment coating the probe rod.
6. **Clean the probe** with a wire brush or fine-grit sandpaper to remove all deposits, and wipe the probe and chamber clean; reinstall the probe and tighten securely.
7. **Check all electrical connections** at the low water cutoff module and control board for tightness and corrosion, and repair or replace any damaged wires or terminals.
8. **Restore power and reset** the boiler according to the manufacturer's instructions (typically by pressing and holding the reset button), then monitor the unit through a complete heating cycle to confirm the A08 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed boiler technician if you are uncomfortable working with the water feed and drain valves, if you cannot locate or identify the low water cutoff components, or if the A08 code persists after verifying proper water level and cleaning the probe. Professional diagnosis is also needed if you find a hidden leak in the heat exchanger or piping, if the automatic fill valve requires replacement or adjustment, or if the control board must be tested and replaced. Gas-fired boilers involve combustion safety, and any work on the control system or pressure-relief components should be performed by a qualified professional to protect your home and warranty.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-450.
