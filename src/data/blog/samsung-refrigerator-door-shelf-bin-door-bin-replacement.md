## Samsung Refrigerator Door Bin Replacement — What This Part Does

The door shelf bin is a removable plastic storage compartment that clips into tabs or slots molded into the refrigerator door liner. It holds bottles, condiments, and other items at various heights on the door. These bins are designed for a specific side and location on your model, and they rely on plastic tabs and locking lips to stay attached during door movement.

Failure happens most often from overloading the bin until the plastic tabs or bin lip cracks, impact damage from heavy bottles or closing force, or aged plastic becoming brittle and snapping during use or cleaning. Once the mounting tabs break or the bin cracks, it won't lock into the door liner properly and must be replaced with the correct part for your model and door position.

## Signs It Needs Replacing

- **Bin cracked or broken along the mounting tabs or sidewalls** Visible cracks or missing plastic pieces mean the bin cannot hold weight or lock into the door liner.
- **Bin falls off or pops out when the door opens or closes** The mounting tabs or slots are damaged and the bin no longer stays clipped to the door liner.
- **Bin rattles or shifts side-to-side when touched** Loose fit indicates broken retaining features or a mismatched replacement part.
- **Bin won't snap into place after removal for cleaning** Worn or broken tabs on the bin or door liner prevent proper locking.
- **Bin is missing completely from the door** A previous failure or lost part requires ordering a new bin matched to model and location.
- **Door liner tabs or slots are cracked or deformed where the bin attaches** Damage to the door liner itself can prevent even a new bin from staying secure.

## How to Replace It {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator or switch off the circuit breaker to the unit for safety during door work.
2. Locate the model and serial number on the rating label inside the fresh food compartment or on the side wall to confirm your exact refrigerator model before ordering the bin.
3. Identify whether you need a left, right, or middle door bin, as Samsung bins are position-specific and not interchangeable between locations.
4. Remove the damaged bin by lifting upward or pushing up and out to release the plastic tabs from the door liner slots.
5. Inspect the door liner mounting tabs and slots for cracks, missing pieces, or deformation that could prevent the new bin from locking in place.
6. Align the new replacement bin with the slots and tabs in the door liner, matching the correct side and height position.
7. Press the bin down firmly until you hear or feel it click and lock into the door liner.
8. Test the fit by gently tugging the bin and opening and closing the door to confirm it stays secure under normal movement.
9. Restore power to the refrigerator and return it to service once the bin is confirmed stable.

## When to Call a Pro

This is a straightforward snap-in replacement that most homeowners can handle with no tools. Call a technician if the door liner mounting tabs or slots are broken or deformed, as that may require door liner replacement or professional evaluation. Also call a pro if the new bin does not lock securely after installation, since misfit or hidden door damage may need diagnosis.
