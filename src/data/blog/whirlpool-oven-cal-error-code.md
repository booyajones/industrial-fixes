## Whirlpool Oven CAL Error Code — What It Means

CAL is not an error code in the traditional sense. It indicates that your Whirlpool oven is displaying its current temperature calibration offset, shown as something like '0°F CAL' or '00' on the control panel. This display appears when you enter the calibration menu or when the control is showing the active adjustment value. Calibration is a user-accessible feature that allows you to shift the oven's temperature setpoint up or down to correct baking accuracy if your oven runs too hot or too cold.

The CAL display itself does not mean a component has failed. It simply shows that the oven is in calibration adjustment mode. Whirlpool allows you to adjust the offset in steps of 5°F or 10°F depending on your control type, within a total range of ±30°F. If your oven continues to show incorrect temperatures even after calibration, the underlying issue is likely a faulty temperature sensor, wiring problem, or control board rather than the calibration feature itself.

## Common Causes

- **User entered calibration menu** Holding specific key combinations or using the Settings/Options menu activates the CAL display as part of normal operation.
- **Oven temperature runs too hot or too cold** Actual baking temperature differs from the setpoint by 15°F or more, prompting the need to adjust the calibration offset.
- **Faulty oven temperature sensor (RTD)** A defective sensor sends incorrect readings to the control, causing persistent temperature error that calibration cannot fix.
- **Loose or damaged sensor wiring** Corroded terminals or broken harness connections between the sensor and control board create intermittent or inaccurate temperature signals.
- **Control board failure** If the electronic oven control or clock is defective, the oven may not respond correctly to calibration adjustments or may display erratic behavior.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify actual oven temperature** by placing an oven thermometer on the center rack, preheating to 350°F, and comparing the thermometer reading to the setpoint after a full preheat cycle.
2. **Access the calibration menu** through your control panel by holding the appropriate key combination (often Bake + Broil, or through Settings/Options/Tools) and note the current offset value displayed.
3. **Adjust the calibration offset** in 5°F or 10°F steps up to ±30°F total to correct the temperature difference you measured, then save the new setting per your model's instructions.
4. **Retest oven temperature** with the thermometer at 350°F to confirm the calibration adjustment resolved the temperature error.
5. **Inspect the oven temperature sensor** if calibration does not solve the problem by disconnecting power, locating the sensor probe in the rear oven wall, and checking for physical damage or loose connections.
6. **Disconnect power for 60 seconds** to reset the control board if the CAL display persists or behaves erratically, then restore power and retest.
7. **Replace the control board or sensor** if temperature remains inaccurate after calibration and sensor inspection, as this indicates a failed component in the temperature sensing or control circuit.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with live electrical circuits or if the oven shows persistent temperature errors after you have completed calibration and inspected the sensor. A qualified appliance technician can safely test the sensor circuit with a multimeter, verify control board output, and replace components that require disassembly of the range or oven chassis. Also call a pro if the CAL display is accompanied by other error codes or if the oven fails to heat at all, as this may indicate a more complex fault involving relays, igniters, or bake elements beyond simple calibration.
