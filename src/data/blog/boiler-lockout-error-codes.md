## Boiler Lockout Error Codes: All Brands Guide

Boiler lockout means the control has decided it is safer to stop trying than to keep cycling with a fault present. Different brands use different codes, but the triggers are familiar: failed ignition, flame loss, low water pressure, overtemperature, blocked venting, and sensor failures.

## Common Boiler Lockout Codes

| Symptom / Code | Common Meaning | Typical Brands |
|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Ignition lockout | No flame established after several tries | Navien, Rinnai, Lochinvar, Burnham |
| Flame loss | Flame started but did not stay proven | Navien, Viessmann, Weil-McLain |
| Low water pressure | Heating loop pressure too low | Combi and hydronic boilers |
| Overtemp | Supply or outlet temperature exceeded limit | Most modern boilers |
| Vent / air pressure | Blocked intake or exhaust | Condensing boilers |
| Sensor lockout | Critical thermistor or pressure sensor out of range | Most modern boilers |

## Why boilers lock out

The board is preventing repeated unsafe ignition attempts or runaway temperature conditions. A lockout is a symptom of a real issue, not an annoying software feature to bypass.

## Most common field causes

Low gas pressure, dirty flame sensors, condensate drain problems, low system pressure, frozen vent terminations, and failed pumps or fans lead the list.

## Reset culture is risky

One reset after a clear correction is fine. Five resets in a row without diagnosis is how people flood combustion chambers or miss a serious venting problem.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Read the exact code first** — Every boiler gives you a stronger starting point than 'it locked out'.
2. **Check system pressure** — Low hydronic pressure is easy to confirm and common on combi units.
3. **Inspect venting and condensate** — Condensing boilers hate blocked drains and blocked terminations.
4. **Confirm gas supply** — If other gas appliances are also weak, the boiler is not the only issue.
5. **Reset once and observe** — Watch the whole startup sequence after a correction.
6. **Stop on repeated safety lockouts** — If ignition, rollout, or overtemp faults return, bring in a licensed tech.

## When to Call a Pro

Repeated boiler lockouts, especially around gas, flame, venting, or overtemperature, are not DIY territory for most people. Boilers combine combustion, water pressure, and temperature safety in a small box, and that deserves respect.
