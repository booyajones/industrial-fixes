## State Water Heater E30 Error Code — What It Means

The E30 error code on State water heaters is not officially documented in widely available State support materials, but it follows a common pattern used across many hybrid and tankless water heater brands. In those systems, E30 typically indicates a water flow issue. This means the unit is detecting insufficient water moving through the heater, either because something is blocking the flow or because incoming water pressure is too low.

Because State produces multiple product families with different control platforms, the exact meaning of E30 can vary by model. Always scan the QR code on your unit's rating plate to pull the correct model-specific manual and troubleshooting guide. That document will confirm whether E30 applies to your specific heater and provide the manufacturer's recommended diagnostic steps.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked inlet screen or filter** Sediment, scale, or debris accumulates on the inlet screen and restricts water entering the heater.
- **Low incoming water pressure** Municipal supply pressure drops below the minimum required for the unit to operate, or a pressure regulator is set too low.
- **Supply valve not fully open** The cold water shut-off valve feeding the heater is only partially open, limiting flow.
- **Kinked or restricted supply line** Flexible supply lines are bent or compressed, or internal pipe buildup narrows the water path.
- **Faulty flow sensor or control logic** The sensor that monitors water movement through the unit has failed or is reporting false readings to the control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or water heater technician if you cannot locate the inlet screen, if incoming water pressure is consistently below spec and requires main-line work, or if the E30 code returns after you have cleaned the screen and verified flow paths. A pro should also handle any diagnostics involving the flow sensor or control board, especially if the unit is under warranty. State recommends using their QR-based support tools to identify qualified service providers for your specific model.
