## Scotsman Ice Machine Error Code 5 — What It Means

Error Code 5 on a Scotsman ice machine signals a harvest assist fault — the harvest assist system (hot gas or mechanical assist that helps the ice slab release from the evaporator) failed to operate correctly, or harvest was not completed as expected. The control board monitors the harvest cycle; if the ice doesn't drop within the time limit and harvest assist doesn't respond, Code 5 is logged.

## Common Causes

- **Harvest assist solenoid not opening** — The hot gas valve that routes warm refrigerant to the evaporator during harvest may be stuck closed or its coil has failed.
- **Low refrigerant charge** — Insufficient refrigerant reduces the effectiveness of the hot gas harvest assist cycle.
- **Evaporator heavily scaled** — Scale buildup on the evaporator plates insulates them, making ice harder to release even when harvest assist is functioning.
- **Harvest assist timer miscalibration** — If the board's harvest timer has drifted or the model's firmware needs updating, the timing of harvest assist activation may be off.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Test the harvest assist solenoid** — During a harvest cycle, the solenoid should click and the suction line should warm up. Use a clamp meter or feel the line; if it stays cold, the solenoid coil is likely open. Test coil resistance — typically 10–30Ω.
2. **Descale the evaporator** — Run a full cleaning cycle with Scotsman Ice Machine Cleaner per the manual. Heavy scale requires a manual soak.
3. **Check refrigerant pressures** — Compare gauge readings to the unit's nameplate spec. Low suction during freeze and poor warm-up during harvest both point to low charge.
4. **Inspect the harvest assist wiring** — Trace the solenoid lead from the control board. Look for chafed insulation, a pulled terminal, or corrosion at the connector.
5. **Reset and monitor** — Power cycle and run a full freeze-harvest cycle. Observe whether the ice slab releases cleanly in under 3 minutes.
