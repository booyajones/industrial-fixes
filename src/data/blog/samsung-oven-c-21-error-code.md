## Samsung Oven C-21 Error Code — What It Means

The C-21 code indicates an over-temperature or overheating safety fault. Your oven's control board has detected that the cavity temperature climbed too high and has placed the unit into error mode to protect the appliance and your home. Field reports describe this code appearing when the oven continues heating past the set point and then trips on overheat. This is not simply a display error but a real overheating condition caused by a control, sensing, or heating problem.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board first when the real fault is a bad temperature sensor or blocked vent. Test the sensor resistance with a meter and inspect airflow paths before ordering expensive electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Failed temperature sensor** A sensor that reports incorrect values causes the control to misread cavity temperature and allows runaway heating.
- **Control board or relay failure** A stuck relay or logic fault keeps the bake circuit energized too long, letting heat build past the safe threshold.
- **Blocked air flow or vents** Debris, grease buildup, or obstructed rear and upper vents trap heat around the cavity and sensor area.
- **Stuck or shorted bake element** On electric models, a bake element that stays on when it should cycle off drives uncontrolled temperature rise.
- **Software or logic glitch** Smart models may misinterpret sensor input due to firmware bugs or corrupted calibration data.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut down the oven and cut power** at the circuit breaker if the unit is actively overheating or the cavity feels unsafe to touch.
2. **Let the oven cool completely** for at least one hour before opening the door or inspecting any components.
3. **Inspect all airflow paths** including rear vents, upper vent covers, and any slots around the cavity for grease, debris, or obstructions.
4. **Test the temperature sensor** by unplugging the oven, locating the sensor probe in the rear of the cavity, disconnecting the wire harness, and measuring resistance with a multimeter at room temperature.
5. **Check the bake element** for visible cracks, blistering, or signs of a short, and verify it cycles on and off during a normal bake rather than staying energized continuously.
6. **Inspect the control board and relay outputs** if the sensor tests within specification but the oven still overshoots and trips C-21.
7. **Replace the failed component** (sensor, element, or board), reassemble the oven, restore power, and run a test cycle to confirm normal temperature control without runaway heating.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with 240-volt circuits, if you cannot access the temperature sensor or element without disassembling cabinetry, or if the oven continues to trip C-21 after you have cleaned vents and verified the sensor resistance. A technician can safely test relay outputs, compare live sensor readings under heat, and trace wiring faults in the bake circuit. If your oven is still under warranty or part of a slide-in or wall installation, professional service protects both your investment and your kitchen.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150–350 depending on whether the sensor, element, or control board needs replacement.
