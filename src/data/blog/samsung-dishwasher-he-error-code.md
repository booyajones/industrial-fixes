## Samsung Dishwasher HE Error Code — What It Means

The HE code on a Samsung dishwasher stands for a heater or temperature detection fault. The control board has determined that water temperature is not rising as expected during the cycle, or the temperature sensor is not reading correctly. Samsung describes this as incorrect temperature detection. The dishwasher may stop mid-cycle or refuse to start properly because it cannot confirm the water is being heated to the programmed level.

## Common Causes

- **Failed heating element** The heater has lost continuity or resistance is out of specification, so it cannot warm the water.
- **Faulty thermistor or temperature sensor** The sensor is reading incorrectly or has drifted out of range, sending bad data to the control board.
- **Loose, corroded, or damaged wiring** Connectors to the heater or thermistor are not making solid contact or have been damaged by heat or moisture.
- **Very cold incoming water** Extremely low inlet water temperature can prevent the heater from raising the temperature fast enough, triggering the fault.
- **Control board or logic fault** The main control board is misinterpreting temperature data even when the heater and sensor test good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the dishwasher** by turning off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds, then restore power and run a normal empty cycle to confirm the HE code returns and is not a one-time glitch.
2. **Check incoming water temperature** at the supply line or another nearby faucet to confirm it is not unusually cold, which can overwork the heater and trip the code.
3. **Inspect the heater and thermistor wiring** by accessing the base or side panel, looking for loose plugs, corrosion, burn marks, or broken wires, and reseat all connectors firmly.
4. **Test the heating element for continuity** using a multimeter set to resistance mode, confirming it is not open and that resistance falls within the range specified in your model's service manual.
5. **Test the thermistor resistance** at room temperature and compare the reading to the service-manual value for your model, replacing the sensor if it is out of range or shows no continuity.
6. **Inspect the control board** for obvious signs of burn damage, moisture intrusion, or failed relay contacts only after confirming the heater, sensor, and wiring are all functioning correctly.
7. **Run a post-repair test cycle** with the dishwasher empty to verify that water heats properly and the HE code does not reappear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with electrical components or accessing the dishwasher base, or if you have tested the heater and thermistor and both are within specification but the code persists. Control board diagnosis and replacement often require service-mode testing and proper part programming. Also call for help if you find evidence of water damage inside the machine or if multiple error codes appear together, since that usually points to a more complex failure.
