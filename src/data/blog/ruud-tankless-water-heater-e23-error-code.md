## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E23 Error — What It Means

The E23 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a problem with internal communication between the control board and other system components, or a sensor reading that falls outside expected parameters. Because error codes can vary by model and firmware version, the exact meaning of E23 may differ across Ruud's product line. Always consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the unit for the specific definition for your model. In general, this code points to an electrical or sensor fault rather than a mechanical problem.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board when the real problem is a loose connector or failing sensor. Inspect all wiring harnesses and test sensor resistance first.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~35%)** Vibration, moisture, or poor initial installation can cause connectors between the control board and sensors to lose contact or corrode.
- **Failed temperature sensor (~25%)** Thermistors monitoring inlet, outlet, or heat-exchanger temperature can drift out of range or fail open, triggering communication or range errors.
- **Control board fault (~20%)** The main printed circuit board may develop a fault in its communication circuits or voltage regulation, preventing proper sensor data processing.
- **Low voltage or power supply issue (~10%)** Voltage drops, brownouts, or a failing transformer can cause intermittent communication errors as components lose stable power.
- **Water flow sensor problem (~10%)** A flow sensor that reads erratically or fails to send data can confuse the board and trigger a system error code.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the fasteners, typically located at the bottom and sides of the panel.
3. **Inspect all wire connectors** on the control board and sensors for looseness, corrosion, or burn marks, and reseat each one firmly.
4. **Consult your model's wiring diagram** (usually on the inside of the cover or in the manual) to identify the sensors associated with the E23 code.
5. **Test sensor resistance** using a multimeter set to ohms, comparing readings to the temperature-resistance table in your service manual.
6. **Replace the faulty sensor or control board** if testing confirms a failed component, following the wiring diagram for correct installation.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then run hot water to verify the error has cleared and the unit fires normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you are not comfortable working with gas appliances, if you lack a multimeter and diagnostic experience, or if the error persists after reseating connectors and power-cycling. Tankless water heaters involve gas combustion, high-voltage ignition, and precise sensor calibration. Misdiagnosis can lead to unsafe operation or expensive unnecessary part replacements. A qualified technician has the wiring diagrams, test equipment, and experience to pinpoint the fault quickly and safely.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
