## LG Washer PE Error Code — What It Means

The PE code on an LG washer indicates a water level or pressure sensor detection fault. The control system cannot correctly read the water level in the tub from the pressure-sensing circuit. LG's own support documentation states that the water level sensor is unable to correctly detect the water level.

This fault prevents the machine from knowing how much water is present, so the washer stops mid-cycle to protect against overfilling or underfilling. The pressure sensor reads air pressure in a small tube connected to the tub. When that sensing path is blocked, disconnected, or the sensor itself fails, the PE code appears. Excess moisture or humid installation conditions can also trigger PE on some LG front-load models.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the pressure sensor or control board first. Instead, inspect the pressure hose and connections carefully. Most PE faults are fixed by clearing a kink or reconnecting a loose hose, not by replacing electronic parts.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked, blocked, loose, or cracked pressure hose (~50%)** The small air tube running from the tub air dome to the pressure sensor can kink, accumulate debris, become disconnected, crack, or fill with water, preventing accurate pressure readings.
- **Failed pressure sensor or water level switch (~30%)** The pressure sensor itself can fail internally, giving erratic or no signal to the control board even when the hose and connections are intact.
- **Wiring or connector faults (~12%)** Loose terminals, corroded pins, or damaged wiring between the pressure sensor and the main control board interrupt the signal path.
- **Main control board failure (~5%)** In rare cases the control board's pressure-sensor input circuit can fail, though this is uncommon for PE codes.
- **Excess moisture or humid installation (~3%)** High humidity or moisture accumulation inside the washer cabinet can interfere with sensor readings on some LG front-load models.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** or turn off the circuit breaker and wait 5 minutes to reset the control board, then restore power and test the machine.
2. **Remove the outer cabinet or top panel** to access the pressure sensor and hose (consult your model's service manual for disassembly steps).
3. **Inspect the pressure hose** running from the air dome on the tub to the pressure sensor for kinks, cracks, blockages, water inside the tube, or loose connections at either end.
4. **Clear any blockages** by blowing gently through the hose or flushing with water, and straighten kinks. If the hose is cracked or split, replace it with a new hose and secure both ends with clamps.
5. **Check the pressure sensor wiring connector** for loose pins, corrosion, or damage. Disconnect and reconnect the harness to make sure a clean contact.
6. **Test or replace the pressure sensor** if the hose and wiring are intact. If you have a multimeter, check for continuity and switching behavior when air pressure is applied (consult the wiring diagram for terminal identification). If abnormal or if the fault persists, install a new sensor.
7. **Reassemble the washer**, restore power, and run a test cycle. If the PE code still appears and all hose, sensor, and wiring checks were normal, the main control board may need professional diagnostics or replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable disassembling the washer cabinet, if the pressure sensor and hose both test good but the code persists, or if control board diagnostics are required. Technicians have factory diagnostic modes and voltage testing equipment to isolate board-level faults. Also call a pro if the washer is still under warranty, as DIY repairs can void coverage. If the installation environment is very humid and the code recurs frequently, a technician can assess ventilation and recommend modifications to reduce moisture buildup.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $10-40 in parts (hose or sensor), 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-250 service call.
