## LG CH 26 Error Code — What It Means

The CH 26 fault code on your LG mini-split means the inverter compressor has seized up or is not providing rotation. LG's official error-code table defines this code with 2 blinks on the indoor LED. In practical terms, the outdoor unit is trying to command the compressor to start, but the compressor motor is not successfully turning or starting.

This is a serious compressor-drive issue that typically requires professional diagnosis and part replacement. The problem may be a mechanically seized compressor, a failed inverter drive board, or damaged wiring between the outdoor control board and the compressor motor.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Compressor mechanical seizure or internal failure** The compressor motor has locked up internally and cannot rotate when commanded by the inverter system.
- **Inverter drive or outdoor PCB problem** The outdoor control board or inverter power module is failing to deliver the proper drive signals to rotate the compressor.
- **Wiring or connector issues between outdoor PCB and compressor** Loose, corroded, or damaged harness connections prevent the compressor from receiving power or control signals.
- **Low-voltage or unstable power supply** Insufficient or fluctuating voltage at the outdoor unit prevents the compressor from starting reliably.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the CH 26 code** by checking the indoor unit LED for 2 blinks and verifying the display shows code 26.
2. **Verify power supply to the outdoor unit** and measure incoming voltage to rule out low or unstable supply conditions before deeper diagnostics.
3. **Inspect all wiring, connectors, and harnesses** between the outdoor PCB, compressor motor leads, and related power components for looseness, heat damage, corrosion, or opens.
4. **Check the inverter outdoor PCB and IPM drive section** for failure signs such as burn marks, blown components, or abnormal output when the compressor is commanded to start.
5. **Test the compressor mechanically and electrically** for seizure by checking winding resistance and attempting gentle manual rotation if accessible (outdoor unit must be de-energized and discharged).
6. **Repair or replace the failed component** based on findings: repair harness connections, replace the outdoor control or inverter board, or replace the seized compressor with evacuation and refrigerant recovery.
7. **After repair, evacuate and recharge the refrigerant system** if the compressor was replaced, then test operation and verify the CH 26 code clears and does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician immediately for CH 26. This code involves high-voltage inverter electronics, refrigerant recovery, and compressor replacement that require EPA certification, specialized tools, and training. Attempting compressor or inverter board work without proper credentials is unsafe and illegal in most jurisdictions. A qualified tech will perform electrical testing, identify the failed component, and handle refrigerant properly during any compressor or sealed-system repair.
