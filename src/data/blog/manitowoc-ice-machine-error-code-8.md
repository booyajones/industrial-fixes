## Manitowoc Ice Machine Error Code 8 — What It Means

Error code 8 on a Manitowoc ice machine (Indigo series, NEO series) indicates an ice thickness sensor fault. The ice thickness probe (a metal probe suspended in front of the evaporator) is used to detect when ice has grown to the correct thickness. When the ice bridge contacts the probe, the control board initiates the harvest cycle. Error code 8 means the probe circuit has an open circuit, short circuit, or the probe is so coated with scale that it cannot make proper electrical contact with the ice.

## Common Causes

- **Mineral scale on the thickness probe** — Calcium carbonate scale builds up on the probe in hard water areas, insulating it from the ice and preventing proper contact detection.
- **Broken probe wire** — The small wire leading to the probe can break from repeated freeze/thaw cycling or physical damage.
- **Probe connector corrosion** — The connector between the probe wiring and the control board corrodes, causing intermittent open-circuit readings.
- **Probe out of position** — If the probe arm has been bent or repositioned, the probe may be too far from or too close to the evaporator surface.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Clean the ice thickness probe** — Remove the probe from its bracket. Clean with white vinegar and a soft cloth or a scale remover (Manitowoc recommends nickel-safe scale remover). Rinse thoroughly.
2. **Inspect the probe wire and connector** — Trace the wire from the probe to the control board connector. Look for breaks, kinks, or corrosion at the connector pins. Clean connector pins with electronics contact cleaner.
3. **Test probe circuit resistance** — Disconnect the probe connector at the board and measure resistance of the probe wire with a multimeter. Continuity expected; open circuit (OL) = broken wire or probe. Replace probe assembly if wire is broken.
4. **Check probe position** — Refer to the Manitowoc installation guide for the correct probe position (typically 1/4" to 3/8" from the evaporator surface). Adjust the probe arm if it has been bent out of spec.
5. **Clean the ice machine and descale** — Run a full Manitowoc clean/sanitize cycle with the approved scale remover. Scale on the evaporator can affect not just the probe but overall ice production performance.

## When to Call a Pro

If code 8 persists after probe cleaning and wiring inspection, the control board's probe input circuit may have failed. A Manitowoc-certified technician should diagnose the board to avoid unnecessary part replacements.
