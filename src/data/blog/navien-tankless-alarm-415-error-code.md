## Navien Alarm 415 — What It Means

Alarm 415 on a Navien tankless water heater is a flow-related fault in the domestic hot water circuit. The control board is not receiving the expected signal from the flow sensor, which tells the unit how much water is moving through the heat exchanger. According to Navien, the problem is most often that the flow sensor inside the unit is not spinning properly, either because of debris, scale buildup, or a mechanical failure of the sensor itself.

The code prevents the heater from firing because the control cannot measure flow accurately. In some cases the water adjustment valve assembly (which works alongside the flow sensor to regulate flow) may also be involved. The fault can sometimes be cleared temporarily by cycling the pressure relief valve to flush out debris, but persistent codes usually require cleaning or replacing the flow sensor or addressing a restriction in the water path.

## Common Causes

- **Debris or scale in the flow sensor** Mineral scale, sediment, or small particles from the water supply can lodge in the flow sensor turbine and prevent it from spinning or sending a proper signal to the board.
- **Clogged inlet filter or strainer** A partially blocked inlet filter reduces flow to the point where the sensor cannot detect movement, triggering the alarm even though the sensor itself may be fine.
- **Failed flow sensor** The sensor's internal turbine or Hall-effect pickup can fail mechanically or electrically, giving no signal or an intermittent signal that the board reads as a fault.
- **Sticking or failed water adjustment valve** If the water adjustment valve does not open correctly or is jammed by debris, flow is restricted and the flow sensor cannot read normal movement.
- **Partially closed service valves** Incoming or outgoing isolation valves that are not fully open starve the unit of flow and produce the same fault as a clogged filter.
- **Air in the water lines** Air pockets after maintenance or a supply interruption can cause erratic flow-sensor readings until the system is purged.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and close** the inlet and outlet service valves to isolate the heater, then relieve pressure by opening a hot tap downstream until flow stops.
2. **Remove and inspect the inlet filter** (usually a fine mesh screen just inside the cold-water service valve) and clean or replace it if you find sediment, scale, or debris.
3. **Cycle the pressure relief valve** by lifting the lever for a few seconds to flush any debris near the flow sensor, as Navien recommends, then close it and check that it seats properly.
4. **Restore water supply and power**, open a hot-water fixture, and observe whether the heater fires and the code clears.
5. **If the code persists, access the flow sensor** by removing the front cover and locating the flow sensor assembly (consult your model's service manual for the exact position and procedure).
6. **Remove the flow sensor**, inspect the turbine for free rotation and clean any scale or debris from the housing and turbine blades using white vinegar or a descaling solution, then rinse thoroughly.
7. **Reassemble and test** by reinstalling the sensor with new O-rings if disturbed, restoring power and water, and running a hot-water draw to confirm the alarm does not return.
8. **If cleaning does not resolve the fault**, replace the flow sensor or water adjustment valve assembly as indicated by your diagnostic results and the parts available for your specific Navien model.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you are not comfortable working with water and electrical connections on a gas or electric tankless heater, if the code returns after cleaning the inlet filter and cycling the relief valve, or if you lack the tools to safely remove and test the flow sensor. A technician can verify the sensor's electrical output, check the water adjustment valve operation, and confirm that no other component (such as the control board or wiring harness) is contributing to the fault. Professional service is also recommended if your unit is still under warranty, because self-repair may void coverage.
