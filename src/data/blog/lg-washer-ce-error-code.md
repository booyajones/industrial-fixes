## LG Washer CE Error Code — What It Means

The CE error code on an LG washer indicates the control board has detected an over-current condition in the motor electrical circuit. This means abnormal current flow triggered a protective shutdown to prevent damage to the drive system or electronics. LG defines the fault as resulting from a short circuit, loose connection, ground fault, or power surge. The washer will not run until the underlying electrical fault is identified and corrected.

## Common Causes

- **Shorted or damaged drive motor** Internal winding faults or stator damage cause abnormal current draw and trigger the over-current protection.
- **Failed main control board or motor control board** Surge damage, failed relays, or burnt traces on the control electronics create erratic current conditions that register as over-current.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connections** Poor contact at motor, control, or harness connectors increases resistance and can cause intermittent current spikes.
- **Ground fault in motor circuit** Insulation breakdown or damaged harness allows current to leak to ground, triggering the fault detection.
- **Power surge or unstable supply** Voltage spikes from the home electrical system can damage motor-drive components or create transient over-current events.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** for at least 10 seconds, then restore power and attempt a test cycle to confirm whether the CE code returns after a reset.
2. **Inspect all wire harnesses and connectors** at the motor, main control board, and any motor-drive electronics for looseness, heat discoloration, corrosion, or damaged pins.
3. **Test the drive motor windings** with a multimeter for shorts to ground, open circuits, or abnormal resistance between terminals (consult your model's service manual for expected values).
4. **Examine the main control board and motor control board** for visible burn marks, damaged relays, scorched traces, or capacitor swelling that indicates surge or overcurrent damage.
5. **Check the home electrical supply** at the outlet for voltage stability and proper grounding, especially if power surges are suspected.
6. **Replace the failed component** (motor, control board, or damaged harness), then clear the code with a full power cycle and run a complete wash cycle to verify the repair.
7. **Document the failure mode** and monitor for recurrence, since intermittent faults may point to upstream electrical issues or marginal components.

## When to Call a Pro

LG recommends professional service if the CE code persists after a reset. This fault involves live electrical circuits, motor windings, and control-board diagnostics that require a multimeter and familiarity with washer disassembly. If you are not confident working with 120V AC wiring, testing motor resistance, or identifying control-board damage, contact an LG-authorized technician. Misdiagnosis can lead to repeated part replacements or create safety hazards. A qualified service provider will have model-specific resistance specs, the correct diagnostic sequence, and access to OEM parts.
