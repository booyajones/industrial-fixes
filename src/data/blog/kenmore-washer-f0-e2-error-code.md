## Kenmore Washer F0 E2 Error — What It Means

The F0 E2 code on a Kenmore washer indicates that the control has detected excessive suds during the wash or rinse cycle. When too much foam is present, the washer may extend rinse times, reduce spin speed, or leave your load wetter than normal to protect the pump and help clear the suds. This is almost always a detergent-use issue rather than a component failure. The machine is designed to sense foam levels, and when it finds too much it triggers F0 E2 and adjusts the cycle to compensate.

## Common Causes

- **Non-HE detergent in an HE washer** Regular detergent creates far too much foam for high-efficiency machines and is the most common trigger for F0 E2.
- **Too much HE detergent** Even HE formulas will over-suds if you pour more than the manufacturer's recommended dose for your load size and soil level.
- **Thickened or "no splash" liquid bleach** Easy-pour bleach with added thickeners can create unexpected suds and trip the sensor during the bleach dispense.
- **Residue from previous loads** Built-up detergent or fabric-softener film inside the drum and hoses can release foam when agitated, especially after switching products.
- **Use of fabric softener or other additives** Some liquid fabric softeners and boosters contain surfactants that combine with detergent residue to generate excess foam.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Stop the cycle** and let any visible foam settle for a few minutes, then run a Drain & Spin or Rinse & Spin cycle to clear the suds from the tub and drain system.
2. **Check your detergent bottle** for the HE symbol (a stylized swirl or "he" mark) and confirm you are using a high-efficiency formula, not regular detergent.
3. **Reduce the dose** to no more than two tablespoons of HE liquid (or the level 1 line in your dispenser) for a normal load, and even less for light soil or soft water.
4. **Run an empty cleaning cycle** with a washer cleaner tablet or one cup of liquid chlorine bleach (not thickened) to flush out any detergent buildup from the drum, hoses, and pump.
5. **Avoid thickened bleach** and skip fabric softener for the next few loads to confirm the code does not return.
6. **Retest with a small load** using the correct HE detergent at the reduced dose, and watch for normal rinse and spin performance without the F0 E2 fault.
7. **If the code persists** after detergent correction and cleaning, consult your model's tech sheet to rule out a stuck pressure sensor or control glitch, though these are rare for F0 E2.

## When to Call a Pro

F0 E2 is nearly always resolved by switching to HE detergent and cutting your dose in half. If the code returns after you have confirmed proper detergent use, run a cleaning cycle, and tested with a small load, a technician should inspect the pressure sensor and control board for a false suds-detection fault. Because this code does not point to a failed part in normal cases, most service calls end with detergent education rather than a repair. Call a pro only if you have verified detergent compliance and the washer still throws F0 E2 on every cycle.
