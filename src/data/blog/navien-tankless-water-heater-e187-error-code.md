## Navien E187 Error Code — What It Means

The E187 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates a communication failure between internal control boards or between the main control board and other components. This typically involves the printed circuit board (PCB) losing contact with sensors, sub-boards, or auxiliary modules. The heater will not operate until communication is restored. Because Navien systems rely on multiple boards talking to one another to manage ignition, flow, and temperature, any break in that chain triggers this safety lockout.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board when the real problem is a loose ribbon cable or corroded connector between boards. Inspect and reseat all visible harness plugs before buying any board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring harness connector (~55%)** Vibration, moisture, or age can cause plugs between the main board and sub-boards to lose contact or develop corrosion.
- **Failed main control board (~25%)** The primary PCB can develop solder cracks, capacitor failure, or component damage that breaks internal communication pathways.
- **Failed sub-board or auxiliary module (~12%)** Smaller daughterboards or interface modules can fail and stop responding to the main controller.
- **Damaged ribbon cable or internal harness (~5%)** Flat ribbon cables or twisted-pair harnesses inside the heater can fray, crack, or suffer pinched conductors.
- **Power supply voltage issue (~3%)** Low or unstable incoming voltage can cause intermittent communication failures as boards reset or lose stable reference voltages.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and turn off the gas supply to the heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by loosening the screws at the bottom and lifting the panel away.
3. **Locate all wiring harness connectors** on the main control board and any visible sub-boards or modules.
4. **Unplug and reseat each connector firmly**, listening and feeling for a click or positive engagement.
5. **Inspect connector pins and sockets** for corrosion, moisture, or bent contacts; clean with electrical contact cleaner if needed.
6. **Check ribbon cables and internal harnesses** for cracks, pinches, or fraying; replace any damaged cables.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit and observe whether the E187 code returns or the heater fires normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if reseating connectors and cleaning contacts does not clear the error, if you are uncomfortable working inside the heater near electrical components, or if diagnostic testing with a multimeter is required to pinpoint which board has failed. Gas-fired tankless water heaters involve both electrical and gas systems, and misdiagnosis can lead to expensive unnecessary part replacements. A qualified technician can perform board-level voltage checks, verify communication signals, and safely replace the correct module without trial and error.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-150 in parts if a connector or harness needs replacement, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-350 depending on whether the technician finds a wiring fault or needs to replace a board.
