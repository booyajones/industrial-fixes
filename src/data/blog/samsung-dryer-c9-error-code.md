## What this code means
The C9 or C90 error code on a Samsung dryer indicates that the unit has detected a severely restricted exhaust path. Samsung defines this code as approximately 90% of the vent system being clogged, which prevents proper airflow and can lead to overheating. The dryer groups C9, C90, CLg, Cg, C8, and C80 together as airflow restriction faults. This is primarily a venting problem, not a control board failure.

When the dryer's internal sensors detect that hot, moist air cannot escape through the exhaust duct, the machine stops the cycle and displays C9 to protect itself from overheating. Samsung's troubleshooting guidance also notes that in rare cases the code can result from abnormal thermistor resistance, which causes the control board to misread temperature and incorrectly flag a blockage.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board or thermistor when the real problem is simply lint buildup in the duct. Disconnect the exhaust hose and run a short Time Dry cycle to check airflow at the drum outlet before ordering any parts.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged lint filter or lint trap housing** A full or poorly seated lint screen reduces airflow and is the first item Samsung instructs you to clean when C9 appears.
- **Blocked exhaust vent duct** Lint accumulation inside the rigid or flexible duct between the dryer and the wall outlet restricts airflow and triggers the code.
- **Crushed or kinked exhaust hose** A flexible vent hose pinched behind the dryer or routed with sharp bends can reduce the duct cross-section enough to cause a 90% blockage reading.
- **Obstructed exterior vent termination** A vent hood or wall cap clogged with lint, bird nests, or debris prevents hot air from escaping to the outside and backs pressure into the dryer.
- **Abnormal thermistor resistance** Samsung lists faulty thermistor readings as a possible cause, since incorrect temperature sensing can make the control board think the vent is blocked when airflow is actually normal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the dryer** for 60 seconds to 2 minutes, then restart to clear any transient fault and see if the code returns immediately.
2. **Remove and clean the lint filter** thoroughly, and vacuum out the lint trap housing in the dryer door opening to remove any packed debris.
3. **Disconnect the exhaust vent hose** from the back of the dryer and pull the dryer away from the wall so you can access the full duct path.
4. **Inspect and clean the entire exhaust duct** from the dryer outlet to the exterior termination, removing lint buildup, straightening kinks, and replacing any crushed sections of flexible hose.
5. **Check the exterior vent hood** for blockages such as lint clogs, bird nests, or a stuck damper flap, and clear any obstructions.
6. **Run Samsung's Vent Blockage Test** with the drum empty and the dryer cool, following the procedure in your owner's manual, to confirm whether the code still appears after cleaning.
7. **If the code persists after vent cleaning**, disconnect the exhaust hose again and run a short Time Dry cycle to verify strong airflow at the dryer outlet, then test the thermistor for proper resistance or call a technician for component-level diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have cleaned the entire exhaust vent system from the dryer to the outside and the C9 code still appears, or if you lack the tools or access to safely inspect the duct run inside walls or the roof. A technician can disassemble the dryer cabinet to check for internal lint buildup around the blower wheel, test the thermistor and other temperature sensors with a multimeter, and verify that the blower motor is spinning at the correct speed. Professional vent cleaning services can also handle long, complex duct runs with multiple elbows or vertical risers that are difficult to reach with household tools.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-50 in cleaning tools or vent repair parts, 1-2 hours. A pro service call runs about $150-300 for vent cleaning or thermistor replacement.
