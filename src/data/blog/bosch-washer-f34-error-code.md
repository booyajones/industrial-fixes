## What this code means
The F34 error code on Bosch washing machines indicates the door is not locked properly. Bosch groups F34 together with E34 and F16 as the same door-lock fault class. The machine cannot start or continue the wash cycle until the door interlock confirms a secure closure.

The door must close completely with an audible click for the lock to engage. A full load, something jammed between the door and housing, or mechanical wear in the latch or interlock assembly can all prevent proper locking and trigger this code.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the door interlock assembly without first checking that the door handle, catch, or seal area is clean and aligned. Inspect the latch mechanism and test the door closure sequence before ordering any part.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully shut or misaligned (~40%)** The latch never engages because the door is not seated properly in the opening.
- **Something trapped at the door seal or latch area (~25%)** Laundry, threads, or debris wedged in the gasket or catch prevents complete closure.
- **Full or oversized load (~15%)** Clothes pushing against the door stop the latch from seating and the lock from engaging.
- **Faulty door interlock or door lock assembly (~15%)** The door closes correctly but the interlock switch is stuck, worn, or electrically failed.
- **Door handle or catch wear or breakage (~5%)** The plastic latch hook or handle is cracked, bent, or worn and no longer engages the lock mechanism.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn the program selector to off** and unplug the machine or switch off the circuit breaker.
2. **Close the door firmly** until you hear an audible click, then plug in and restart the program. If the error clears, the door was simply not latched.
3. **Open the door and inspect** the seal, latch area, and opening for trapped laundry, threads, or foreign objects. Remove any debris and wipe the surfaces clean.
4. **Check the load size**. If the drum is overfilled or bulky items are pressing on the door, remove enough laundry to allow free closure.
5. **Examine the door handle and catch**. Press the handle and watch the plastic latch hook move. If it is cracked, bent, or does not extend fully, replace the door handle assembly.
6. **Test the door interlock assembly**. Close the door until it clicks. If the error persists and the latch is engaging, the interlock switch is likely faulty. Remove the door panel screws, disconnect the interlock wiring harness, and install a new interlock assembly.
7. **Clear the fault code** using your model's reset procedure. One common Bosch/Siemens method is to turn the selector to off, then to the six o'clock position, hold the spin button while moving the dial one click to seven o'clock, hold for about three seconds, then return to off. Consult your service manual for the exact sequence for your model.
8. **Run a test cycle** to confirm the door locks and the machine starts normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if the door is stuck shut and the emergency release does not open it, if the fault returns after you have replaced the interlock and handle, or if you are uncomfortable working with the door wiring harness and control board reset procedures. A technician can verify the interlock's electrical signals, inspect the main control board for relay or connector faults, and perform the exact factory reset sequence for your platform. Also call if the machine is under warranty or if you need to open the door while water remains above the glass level, which requires pump-out and drain procedures.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30–80 in parts, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $120–250.
