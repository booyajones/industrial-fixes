## Samsung SUD Error Code — What It Means

The Samsung SUD, 5UD, Sd, 5d, SUdS, or 5Ud5 error code means the washer has detected an excessive suds (over-foaming) condition. The machine will pause the cycle briefly to allow foam to dissipate, then should resume automatically. Samsung states this is usually caused by incorrect detergent use, not a hardware failure.

If the code keeps returning after you switch to proper detergent and run empty rinse cycles, the issue is typically a drainage restriction or wash system fault preventing suds from clearing normally. In most cases, addressing detergent habits and checking the drain path resolves the problem without parts replacement.

## Common Causes

- **Too much detergent** Using more than the manufacturer's recommended dose creates excessive foam, especially in low-water front-load washers.
- **Non-HE detergent** Regular (non-HE) detergent or products not designed for high-efficiency washers produce far too many suds in low-water cycles.
- **Clogged drain pump filter** Lint, debris, or foreign objects in the filter restrict water and suds evacuation, causing foam to build up during the cycle.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose** A drain hose that is bent, crushed, or installed at the wrong height prevents proper drainage and traps suds inside the drum.
- **Drain pump restriction or failure** If the pump cannot evacuate water and suds effectively due to mechanical blockage or motor failure, foam accumulates and triggers the code.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify detergent use** by checking how much detergent you added and whether it is labeled HE (high-efficiency) for front-load or low-water washers.
2. **Run an empty cycle** with no detergent, fabric softener, or other additives and watch for visible suds during the rinse and spin phases.
3. **Repeat empty rinse cycles** until no foam appears in the drum or visible through the door glass, which may take two or three cycles if heavy suds are present.
4. **Inspect the drain hose** for kinks, blockages, or improper installation height, and straighten or reposition it according to your installation manual.
5. **Clean the drain pump filter** by powering off the washer, opening the filter access door (usually lower front), draining residual water into a shallow pan, and removing lint or debris from the filter housing.
6. **Check drain pump operation** by running a rinse cycle and listening for normal pump sound and observing that water evacuates fully.
7. **Reset and retest** by restoring power, running a short rinse or test cycle with proper HE detergent at the correct dose, and confirming the code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the SUD code persists after you have run multiple empty rinse cycles with no detergent, cleaned the drain filter, and verified the drain hose is clear and properly installed. Also seek service if you see the code repeatedly but no visible suds appear, as Samsung directs escalation in that scenario to check for underlying control or sensing faults. If you are uncomfortable accessing the drain pump filter or testing the pump, professional diagnosis will pinpoint whether a component replacement is required.
