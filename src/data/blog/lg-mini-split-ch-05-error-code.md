## LG Mini-Split CH05 Error — What It Means

The CH05 error code on an LG mini-split signals a communication failure between the indoor and outdoor units. The two halves of your system cannot exchange control signals, which prevents normal operation. This is an electrical fault in the control wiring, power supply, or circuit boards rather than a refrigerant or mechanical problem.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Tripped outdoor breaker** LG directs technicians to first confirm the outdoor unit's circuit breaker has not tripped, cutting power to the communication circuit.
- **Faulty interconnecting wire** Damaged, loose, or corroded control wiring between indoor and outdoor units interrupts the serial data link.
- **Loss of line or neutral power** An open neutral or missing hot leg at the outdoor unit prevents the board from powering the communication circuit.
- **Failed indoor control board** A defective PCB in the indoor unit can lose the ability to send or receive communication signals.
- **Failed outdoor control board** The outdoor inverter or main control board may fail at the communication circuit, power supply stage, or related components.
- **Loose or damaged electrical connections** Terminals that are not fully seated or that have arced will break the low-voltage communication path.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Cycle power on both units.** Turn off the outdoor breaker, then turn off the indoor breaker, wait five minutes, and restore both at the same time to reset the communication handshake.
2. **Verify the outdoor breaker is on and not tripped.** LG lists this as the first diagnostic check because lost outdoor power is the most common cause of CH05.
3. **Inspect all interconnecting control wiring** between indoor and outdoor units for cuts, burns, corrosion, or looseness at terminals.
4. **Check voltage at the outdoor board.** Confirm that line and neutral are present and stable to rule out an upstream power-supply fault.
5. **Test the communication signal at the control terminals** if your meter shows fluctuating DC voltage when the system is powered, but recognize that LG does not publish a universal spec for this reading.
6. **Isolate the defective board** by swapping or testing the indoor and outdoor PCBs if wiring and power are confirmed good.
7. **Call for service** if the code persists after power reset and basic wiring checks, since board-level diagnosis and repair require manufacturer training.

## When to Call a Pro

If the outdoor breaker is on, the wiring looks intact, and a power reset does not clear CH05, the fault lies in the control boards or requires signal-level testing that demands specialized tools and training. Board replacement on inverter mini-splits also involves refrigerant-system work and high-voltage connections. Call a qualified HVAC technician to diagnose the communication circuit, measure signal integrity, and replace the defective PCB safely.
