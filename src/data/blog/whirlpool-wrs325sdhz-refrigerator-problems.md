## Whirlpool WRS325SDHZ Refrigerator Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Whirlpool WRS325SDHZ is a side-by-side refrigerator that uses electronic controls and a filter-status indicator system. The red H2O light is the number-one service call on this model, and it's usually a simple filter replacement or reset rather than a sealed-system fault. Real error codes on the display (like sensor faults, communication codes, and over-temp alarms) point to thermistor failures, board communication problems, or airflow and defrost issues.

Most repairs involve the water filter, thermistors, the user interface, or the main control board. The side-by-side layout shares a common evaporator, so frost buildup or a failed evaporator fan can trigger temperature codes in both compartments. Whirlpool builds these units with modular harnesses and snap-in sensors, which makes diagnostics straightforward if you know where to look.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **Red H2O or filter light stays on** The water filter is expired, clogged, or not seated correctly, or the indicator was never reset after the last filter change.
- **E1 or E2 sensor fault code on display** A thermistor in the refrigerator or freezer compartment has failed, or the harness connection is loose or corroded at the evaporator or control board.
- **CF communication fault code** The wiring harness between the user interface and the main control board is loose, or the user interface or control board has failed.
- **Code 18 over-temperature alarm** The freezer temperature rose after a power outage, extended door opening, or a defrost fault that blocked airflow.
- **No water or slow flow at dispenser** Water supply is off, kinked, or low pressure (below 30 psi), or the filter is plugged and needs replacement.
- **Warm refrigerator or freezer compartment with sensor code** Frost buildup on the evaporator coil or a failed evaporator fan motor restricts airflow, or the thermistor reading is incorrect.
- **Temperature display flashing or blank** The user interface has lost power or communication, or the main control board is not sending signals to the display.
- **Ice buildup in freezer with poor cooling** Defrost heater or defrost thermostat has failed, or the defrost timer/control logic on the main board is not cycling defrost properly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you see refrigerant-system codes, compressor faults, or sealed-system issues like no cooling in both compartments with no obvious airflow or defrost cause. Sensor and board diagnostics require entering service mode and interpreting live sensor readings, so if you're not comfortable with that or if replacing the thermistor and resetting codes doesn't fix the problem, bring in a pro. Any work involving the sealed refrigerant system (compressor, evaporator, condenser, or refrigerant lines) requires EPA certification and specialized equipment. Water supply pressure below 30 psi or above 120 psi needs plumbing correction before you chase appliance parts.
