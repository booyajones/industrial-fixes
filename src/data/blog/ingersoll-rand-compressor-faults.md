## Ingersoll-Rand Compressor Fault Codes — What They Mean

Ingersoll-Rand rotary screw compressors (R-Series, UP6, SSR series) use the Intellisys or Xe-Series controllers to display fault and shutdown codes. Ingersoll-Rand is one of the most widely installed compressor brands in US industrial facilities.

| Code / Alarm | Meaning | Urgency |
|------|---------|---------|
| High discharge temperature | Discharge air temperature exceeded shutdown limit | Critical |
| High pressure | System pressure exceeded maximum | High |
| Low sump pressure | Sump pressure dropped abnormally | High |
| Oil separator pressure differential | Oil separator needs replacement | Medium |
| Sensor fault | Temperature or pressure sensor error | Medium |
| Motor overload / overtemperature | Drive motor protection tripped | High |
| E-stop | Emergency stop activated | High |

## Most Common Ingersoll-Rand Faults and Fixes {#fix}

### High Discharge Temperature
Check oil level immediately — low oil is the most common cause. Inspect the oil cooler and air cooler for fouling (compressed air facilities accumulate heavy dust and oil mist on coolers). Verify thermostatic mixing valve operation. Do not run the compressor until resolved.

### Oil Separator Pressure Differential
The oil separator element is plugged. Service life is typically 2,000-4,000 hours. Replace with Ingersoll-Rand OEM element to maintain proper oil carryover spec.

### Sensor Fault
The displayed sensor (discharge temperature, ambient temperature, sump pressure, etc.) is reading outside its valid range. Test the sensor against a reference instrument. Replace if failed.

### Motor Overload
Check supply voltage balance (all three phases within 1% of each other). Verify motor FLA on nameplate vs. actual running amps. Check for mechanical restriction.

## Parts Often Needed

| Part | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Oil separator element | Use Ingersoll-Rand OEM — aftermarket elements often don't meet carryover spec |
| Compressor oil (All Season Select / Ultra Coolant) | Ingersoll-Rand specific fluid |
| Oil filter | Replace at every oil change |
| Thermostatic mixing valve | Replace if high temperature persists after cooler service |

## When to Call a Pro

Discharge temperature faults that don't resolve with oil and cooler service require Ingersoll-Rand certified service for rotor clearance, bearing, and valve inspection.
