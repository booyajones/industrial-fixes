## Ingersoll-Rand Compressor Fault Codes — Quick Reference

Ingersoll-Rand SSR and Nirvana compressors report alarms for temperature, pressure, VSD status, and motor protection through the Xe-series controller or older Intellisys controller. The wording changes by controller version, but the failure patterns are consistent.

| Alarm | Meaning | Quick Fix |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| High Discharge Temp | Compressor too hot | Clean coolers; check oil level |
| Sensor Failure | Temp or pressure transducer issue | Check connector and sensor |
| VSD Fault | Variable speed drive trip | Review drive fault history |
| Motor Overload | Main motor current high | Check amperage and load |
| Emergency Stop | E-stop loop open | Reset and inspect circuit |
| Separator DP High | Separator restriction | Replace separator element |
| Low Sump Pressure | Inlet or lubrication issue | Check minimum pressure valve |
| Service Due | PM interval reached | Perform maintenance |

## Most Common Faults

### High Discharge Temperature
Dirty package coolers and low oil are the most common causes. In dusty plants, IR compressors can overheat even when the room feels cool because the coolers are packed internally. Clean both sides of the cooler bank and verify fan rotation.

### VSD Fault
On Nirvana compressors, the variable speed drive has its own fault history. Do not treat VSD Fault as a single root cause. Pull the actual drive trip, then work from that. Common underlying issues are input voltage imbalance, cooling airflow loss, and motor cable insulation problems.

### Low Sump Pressure
If sump pressure drops too low, the compressor may not maintain lubrication or separator function correctly. Check the minimum pressure/check valve, inlet valve operation, and whether the machine is trying to run unloaded under conditions it was not tuned for.

## When to Call a Pro
If a Nirvana or other VSD compressor faults repeatedly, get an IR tech or qualified compressor specialist involved. Misdiagnosing a drive-related trip wastes a lot of time and money.
