## Daikin C9 Error Code — What It Means

A C9 error on your Daikin heat pump indicates a fault with the indoor unit's suction air temperature sensor (thermistor). The indoor control board is reading an out-of-range resistance signal from this sensor, which tells the system the incoming air temperature. The fault is typically caused by a disconnected cable, short circuit, or a failed sensor element itself. When the board sees abnormal resistance—either open circuit, near-zero, or erratic—it throws C9 and stops operation to protect the system.

## Common Causes

- **Failed thermistor** The suction air temperature sensor inside the indoor unit has degraded or broken, producing out-of-spec resistance readings.
- **Disconnected or loose sensor connector** The plug between the thermistor leads and the indoor PCB has backed out, corroded, or lost contact.
- **Damaged or shorted sensor wiring** The sensor harness has been pinched, cut, or shorted against metal, creating an abnormal signal path.
- **Corroded terminals** Moisture or contaminants have corroded the connector pins or PCB socket, blocking signal continuity.
- **Indoor PCB input circuit fault** If the sensor and wiring test good, the board's input circuit may itself be damaged and misreading the signal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut off power** to the indoor unit at the disconnect or breaker, then wait two minutes before opening the cover.
2. **Remove the front panel and filter** to access the indoor coil area where the suction air thermistor is mounted near the return-air inlet.
3. **Inspect the sensor and its harness** for visible damage, loose connectors, pinched wires, or corrosion at the plug and terminals.
4. **Reseat the sensor connector** at the indoor PCB, ensuring the plug is fully seated and the locking tab clicks into place.
5. **Disconnect the sensor from the PCB and test resistance** with a multimeter across the thermistor leads at room temperature, then gently warm the sensor element and watch for smooth resistance change.
6. **Replace the thermistor** if the reading is open circuit, near-zero ohms, or does not change smoothly with temperature (consult your model's service manual for exact resistance values).
7. **Restore power and clear the fault**, then monitor the unit through a cooling cycle to confirm C9 does not return and suction temperature is displayed correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are uncomfortable working inside the indoor unit with power off, or if your multimeter test shows normal sensor resistance and intact wiring but the C9 code returns after reset, call a licensed HVAC technician. Diagnosing a board-level fault or verifying the exact sensor spec for your model requires service documentation and experience with Daikin control systems. Technicians also have access to resistance tables and can safely test live circuits to confirm the PCB input stage is functioning.
