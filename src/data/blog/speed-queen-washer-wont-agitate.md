## Speed Queen Washer Won't Agitate — What's Happening

A Speed Queen washer that won't agitate means the machine fills and enters the wash cycle, but the agitator or basket does not move as expected. Other functions like fill and drain may still work normally. This is a symptom rather than a specific fault code, and it points to a mechanical drive failure somewhere between the motor and the agitator assembly. In technician terms, it is a no-agitate condition that requires step-by-step isolation of the drive train and safety interlocks.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Worn or stripped agitator repair kit components** Internal agitator parts wear out over time and stop gripping the drive shaft, so the agitator spins freely or does not move at all even though the transmission shaft turns.
- **Damaged or stripped drive block** The drive block connects the agitator to the transmission shaft, and when it wears or breaks the agitator will not turn even if the shaft is moving.
- **Broken motor coupling** The coupling between the motor and transmission can crack or shear, stopping all drive motion while the motor still runs.
- **Faulty lid switch or lid strike misalignment** If the lid switch does not close or the strike does not engage properly, the control board will not allow the wash motor drive to run.
- **Broken or loose drive belt** A snapped or slipping belt prevents the motor from turning the transmission pulley, so agitation stops even though the motor spins.
- **Defective motor or seized motor bearings** A motor that hums, is noisy, or will not turn freely when spun by hand needs replacement because it cannot deliver torque to the drive system.
- **Worn or failed transmission or gearcase** When the transmission shaft does not turn during wash, internal gears or bearings have worn out and the entire transmission assembly requires replacement.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the symptom by running a wash cycle and listening to confirm whether the motor runs, hums, or stays silent while the agitator does not move.
2. Inspect the agitator assembly and drive block by removing the agitator cap and checking for stripped splines, cracks, or excessive play on the shaft.
3. Confirm the lid closes fully and that the lid strike engages the lid switch properly, then test the switch with a multimeter for continuity when the lid is closed.
4. Unplug the washer and access the drive system to check the motor coupling and drive belt for cracks, wear, or complete failure.
5. Verify the motor receives power during wash by measuring voltage at the motor terminals, and confirm the motor turns freely by hand when disconnected.
6. Remove the agitator or wash plate and observe the transmission shaft during a wash cycle to see if it rotates.
7. Check for mechanical binding by looking for objects jammed between the inner and outer tubs or a seized bearing in the transmission.
8. Replace the failed component identified by testing, whether agitator kit, drive block, coupling, belt, motor, control board, or transmission assembly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with electrical tests at the motor or control board, or if disassembly of the cabinet and transmission is required. Transmission replacement and bearing work involve specialized tools and alignment. If the agitator repair kit and drive block are intact and the motor receives power but still will not agitate, a qualified technician can isolate control board faults or internal transmission damage safely and order the correct OEM parts for your Speed Queen model.
