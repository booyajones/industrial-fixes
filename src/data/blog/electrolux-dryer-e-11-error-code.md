## Electrolux E11 Error Code — What It Means

The E11 error code appears on Electrolux front-load washers, not dryers. It means the control board did not detect the expected water level within the fill window during the wash cycle. When E11 displays, the unit locks the cycle for up to 6 minutes, and unplugging the machine restarts that timer.

This is a water fill timeout fault. The washer is signaling that it took too long to reach the correct water level. The code typically points to installation issues, household water supply problems, or restricted water flow rather than internal component failure.

## Common Causes

- **Reversed hot and cold supply lines** If the unit was recently installed or moved, the hot and cold hoses may be connected to the wrong inlet ports on the machine.
- **Insufficient household water supply** Low water pressure or partially closed supply valves restrict flow and prevent the washer from filling in the expected time.
- **Kinked or crushed inlet hoses** Bent or pinched hoses between the wall valves and the washer reduce water flow and trigger the timeout.
- **Clogged inlet screens or filters** Sediment, rust, or mineral deposits block the small mesh screens inside the hose couplings at the machine inlet valve.
- **Faulty water inlet valve** If supply and hoses test good but the machine still does not fill properly, the inlet valve solenoids or ports may be defective.
- **Pressure switch or hose issue** A blocked pressure hose or defective water-level sensor can prevent the control from recognizing that water has entered the tub.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and wait 6 minutes to clear the lockout timer, then restore power and attempt a new cycle to confirm the fault is persistent.
2. **Check both water supply valves** at the wall and turn them fully counterclockwise to the open position.
3. **Verify hot and cold hose connections** by comparing the markings on the valve ends with the inlet ports on the back of the washer, and swap hoses if they are reversed.
4. **Inspect inlet hoses** for kinks, crushing, or sharp bends, and straighten or reposition them as needed.
5. **Remove and clean inlet screens** by unscrewing the hoses from the washer inlet ports, using needle-nose pliers to pull out the small mesh filters, and rinsing them under running water to remove sediment.
6. **Test household water pressure** by disconnecting the hoses at the machine and holding the ends over a bucket while you open the supply valves briefly to observe flow volume.
7. **Examine the water inlet valve** by removing the top or rear panel, locating the valve where hoses attach, and checking for visible debris or testing coil resistance if the supply and hoses are confirmed good.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have confirmed that both supply valves are fully open, hoses are clear and correctly connected, inlet screens are clean, and household water pressure is adequate but the E11 code still appears. Persistent faults after these checks usually point to a defective inlet valve, pressure switch, or main control board. Professional diagnosis with a multimeter and service manual is needed to isolate the failing component and avoid replacing parts unnecessarily.
