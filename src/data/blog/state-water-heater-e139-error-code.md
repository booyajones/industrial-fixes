## State Water Heater E139 Error Code — What It Means

The E139 code on State tankless water heaters indicates an ignition or flame failure condition. This means the burner did not successfully ignite after a call for heat, or it lost flame during the start-up sequence. While State does not publish a detailed public explanation of E139 in most model documentation, field experience and service patterns show it behaves like other ignition-related faults on the A. O. Smith and State tankless platform. The control board expects to prove flame within a set window after opening the gas valve, and E139 appears when that does not happen.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake** Lint, dust, or debris on the intake screen restricts combustion air and prevents the burner from lighting reliably.
- **Low gas supply or pressure** Insufficient gas flow at the appliance, a closed manual shutoff, or pressure drop in shared piping stops ignition.
- **Clogged water inlet filter** Reduced water flow through a dirty inline filter prevents the unit from sensing adequate flow to enable firing.
- **Blocked or restricted exhaust vent** Obstructed vent termination, disconnected pipe, or improper slope causes combustion products to back up and interrupt ignition.
- **Dirty flame rod or sensor** Oxidation or soot on the flame-sensing electrode prevents the board from confirming ignition even when flame is present.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger** Hard-water deposits restrict flow or disrupt combustion on condensing tankless models, especially when codes persist after basic cleaning.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the unit** by turning off the electrical supply or unplugging for 30 seconds, then restore power and test for a call for heat to see if the fault clears.
2. **Inspect and clean the air intake screen** on the front or side of the heater, removing any lint, dust, or blockage that restricts combustion air.
3. **Check the exhaust vent** for obstructions, disconnections, or improper termination, and confirm the vent pipe is securely connected and slopes correctly.
4. **Verify the gas supply** by confirming the manual shutoff valve is fully open and checking that other gas appliances on the same line are operating normally.
5. **Remove and clean the water inlet filter** at the cold-water connection to restore full flow into the heater.
6. **Inspect the flame rod and burner area** for dirt, corrosion, or soot buildup, and carefully clean the flame-sensing electrode if accessible per your model's service instructions.
7. **If the code returns after these checks**, consult your model's service manual for gas-valve, igniter, and control-board diagnostics, or contact a licensed technician for component-level troubleshooting.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the E139 code persists after you have cleared the air intake, confirmed gas supply, cleaned the water filter, and inspected the exhaust vent. Diagnosis beyond that point requires gas-pressure measurement, flame-current testing, control-board evaluation, and potential heat-exchanger descaling or component replacement. Any work involving gas-valve adjustment, igniter replacement, or venting modification should be performed by a qualified professional to maintain safety and warranty coverage.
