## LG Microwave LOC Error Code — What It Means

LOC on an LG microwave is not an error code. It indicates the control lock or child lock feature is turned on. This safety function locks the keypad to prevent unintended operation, especially when small children are present. The display shows LOC whenever the lock is active, and the control panel will not respond to button presses until you disable it.

LG states that LOC is normal behavior and can be cleared by pressing and holding the STOP/CLEAR button until the display returns to normal. If the lock will not disengage using the standard unlock command, the issue is usually a stuck key on the touchpad or a contaminated keypad membrane. A failed control board can also prevent normal lock toggling, but that represents a hardware failure rather than the meaning of LOC itself.

## Common Causes

- **Lock feature was enabled intentionally** A user activated the child lock by pressing and holding STOP/CLEAR, or the microwave was left in lock mode after cleaning.
- **Stuck or jammed keypad button** A mechanically stuck STOP/CLEAR or other key continuously signals the lock command and prevents normal unlock.
- **Contaminated touchpad membrane** Spills, grease, or moisture under the keypad overlay create a short circuit that mimics a persistent lock state.
- **Failed display or user interface board** The control board cannot process the unlock command or update the display, leaving LOC visible even when the lock should be off.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the display shows LOC** and that the microwave otherwise has power and the interior light or clock is visible.
2. **Press and hold the STOP/CLEAR button** for three to five seconds until LOC disappears from the display, which is LG's official unlock method.
3. **Unplug the microwave for 60 seconds** if the code does not clear, then restore power and attempt the unlock command again to reset the control state.
4. **Inspect the touchpad for stuck buttons** by pressing each key firmly and listening for a click or tactile response, paying special attention to STOP/CLEAR.
5. **Clean the keypad surface** with a damp cloth and mild detergent, then dry thoroughly to remove any grease or residue that could short the membrane contacts.
6. **Check for immediate LOC return** after clearing, which suggests a shorted touchpad assembly or a button that remains mechanically depressed.
7. **Test the control board response** by attempting to start a cook cycle or change settings after unlock. If the panel remains unresponsive or LOC reappears instantly, replace the user interface board or main control board as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if LOC will not clear after you have held STOP/CLEAR, power-cycled the unit, and cleaned the keypad. Persistent LOC that returns immediately after clearing or a control panel that remains completely unresponsive indicates a failed touchpad assembly or control board. Board-level diagnosis and replacement require disassembly and proper handling of high-voltage components inside the microwave cabinet. If your microwave is under warranty, contact LG support before attempting any repair to preserve coverage.
