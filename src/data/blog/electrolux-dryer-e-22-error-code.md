## Electrolux Dryer E22 Error Code — What It Means

The E22 error code on Electrolux dryers typically indicates a problem with the drying process or moisture detection system. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the service panel. In general, this code appears when the dryer detects poor airflow, excessive drying time, or a failure in the moisture-sensing circuit. The control board expects the load to dry within a certain window, and when that does not happen the machine throws the fault and stops the cycle to prevent damage.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the moisture sensor bars or the control board first. Clean the lint filter, exhaust duct, and sensor bars with rubbing alcohol before ordering any parts.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked exhaust vent or lint filter (~50%)** Restricted airflow forces longer dry times and triggers the fault when the moisture sensors never see the load finish.
- **Dirty moisture sensor bars (~25%)** Fabric softener and detergent residue insulates the metal sensor strips so they cannot detect moisture accurately.
- **Failed moisture sensor assembly (~15%)** Corrosion or a broken wire in the sensor circuit prevents the control board from reading drum humidity.
- **Faulty control board (~10%)** The main electronic control may misinterpret sensor signals or have a failed relay that stops the cycle prematurely.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** by unplugging the dryer or switching off the dedicated breaker at the panel.
2. **Remove and clean the lint filter** under warm water with a soft brush to dissolve any waxy buildup, then let it dry completely.
3. **Pull the dryer away from the wall** and disconnect the exhaust vent hose from the back of the machine.
4. **Inspect the entire vent run** for kinks, crushing, or packed lint and use a vent brush or shop vacuum to clear any blockages all the way to the outside termination.
5. **Locate the moisture sensor bars** inside the drum, usually two metal strips near the lint filter or on the back wall, and wipe them with a cloth dampened in rubbing alcohol to remove film.
6. **Reconnect the vent hose** securely with a clamp and push the dryer back into position, leaving at least four inches of clearance behind for airflow.
7. **Restore power and run a test cycle** on an automatic setting with a small damp load to verify the error does not return and the load dries normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have cleaned the filter, cleared the entire exhaust vent, wiped the moisture sensors, and the E22 code still appears on every cycle. A pro can test the sensor circuit with a multimeter, check thermostats and the thermal fuse, and diagnose whether the control board has failed. Also call if you are uncomfortable working with the wiring harness or if your dryer uses a gas burner, since any gas appliance work requires proper tools and experience.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-50 in parts, 20-45 min. A pro service call runs about $120-220.
