## Whirlpool Microwave Buttons Not Working — What's Happening

When the buttons on your Whirlpool microwave stop responding, the control panel pads are disabled and the unit will not start. According to Whirlpool, this symptom most often points to Control Lock being turned on or a control logic glitch that needs a power reset.

If neither of those fixes restores operation, the fault is usually in the touch keypad assembly, the ribbon connection between the keypad and the main control board, or the electronic control board itself. Door switch problems can also prevent the microwave from starting even when the buttons light up or beep.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Control Lock enabled** Whirlpool's Control Lock feature disables all control panel pads so the microwave cannot be started accidentally.
- **Power interruption or control logic glitch** A temporary electrical event can freeze the control logic, which clears after a one-minute breaker reset.
- **Dirty or contaminated keypad surface** Grime, grease, or residue on the touchpad can interfere with the contact area and block button response.
- **Loose or poor keypad ribbon connection** The ribbon connector between the touch panel and the control board can work loose or become oxidized, breaking the signal path.
- **Failed touch keypad or control panel assembly** The touchpad itself can fail internally and stop registering presses even when the display is lit.
- **Failed electronic control board** The main control board can lose the ability to read keypad inputs, rendering all buttons unresponsive.
- **Stuck or damaged door switch** A faulty door interlock switch can prevent the microwave from starting even though the panel may still light or beep.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the microwave has proper power at the outlet and the circuit breaker is on.
2. Check your owner's manual and disable Control Lock if it is active (usually by holding a designated button for three to five seconds).
3. Turn off the circuit breaker or unplug the unit for one full minute, then restore power and test all buttons.
4. Inspect the door latch and switches by opening and closing the door firmly to confirm the interlock sequence engages.
5. If access is possible on your model, remove the front control bezel and locate the ribbon connector between the keypad and the control board.
6. Unplug the unit, reseat the ribbon connector by pressing it firmly into both ends, check for oxidation or contamination, then reassemble and retest.
7. If the keypad remains unresponsive, isolate whether the problem follows the touchpad assembly or the main control board by swapping the suspected part.
8. Replace only the confirmed failed component (keypad assembly or electronic control board) and verify all buttons respond before closing up the unit.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the one-minute breaker reset and Control Lock check do not restore button function, or if you are uncomfortable removing the control bezel and working near the high-voltage capacitor. A qualified appliance technician has the tools to safely isolate whether the touchpad, ribbon connector, door switches, or main control board has failed, and can source the correct model-specific parts for your Whirlpool microwave.
