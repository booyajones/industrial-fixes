## Bosch Oven ER6 Error Code — What It Means

The ER6 error code on a Bosch oven indicates a defective door lock or door lock circuit fault. This code appears when the control board cannot engage, release, or verify the status of the self-cleaning lock mechanism. The oven will not allow you to start a self-clean cycle, and in some cases it may prevent normal baking if the lock is stuck or the circuit is shorted. The door lock assembly contains a motor-driven latch and a switch that signals lock position back to the board. When either component fails or the wiring between them breaks, the board throws ER6 and halts operation.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock motor or latch** The lock motor burns out or the mechanical latch cracks and can no longer extend or retract fully.
- **Broken door lock switch contacts** The microswitch inside the lock assembly wears out and no longer closes to confirm the locked position.
- **Loose or damaged wiring at the lock connector** Vibration or heat causes the harness plug to back out or individual wires to fray where they enter the lock housing.
- **Control board relay or driver failure** The main board loses the ability to send power to the lock motor or read the switch signal, even when the lock itself is good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** at the circuit breaker or unplug the oven completely and wait two minutes for capacitors to discharge.
2. **Remove the oven door** by opening it fully, flipping the hinge locks forward, then lifting the door up and out at a 45-degree angle.
3. **Access the door lock assembly** by removing the screws along the top front panel or control console, depending on your model, and sliding the panel forward to expose the lock mounted near the top of the cavity.
4. **Inspect the lock mechanism** for visible cracks in the plastic housing, bent latch rods, or burn marks on the wire terminals.
5. **Disconnect the wiring harness** from the lock, pull the lock mounting screws, and remove the assembly.
6. **Test or replace the lock** by checking for smooth manual travel of the latch and audible clicks from the internal switch, or install a new assembly if any part is damaged.
7. **Reassemble and test** by reconnecting the harness, securing the front panel, reinstalling the door, restoring power, and starting a short self-clean cycle to verify the lock engages and ER6 clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the new lock assembly does not clear ER6, if you measure no voltage at the lock connector when a self-clean cycle is commanded, or if you are uncomfortable working inside a live high-voltage appliance. Door lock faults that persist after lock replacement usually trace to a failed relay or triac on the main control board, which requires board-level diagnosis and often a complete board swap. Professional help is also recommended if your oven is still under warranty, since self-repair may void coverage.
