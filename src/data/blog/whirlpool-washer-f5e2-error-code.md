## Whirlpool Washer F5E2 Error Code — What It Means

F5E2 means the washer cannot lock the lid or door before the cycle starts. The machine will not run until the lock confirms a closed and latched state. It shows on both top load and front load models.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Obstructed latch** Laundry or packaging caught in the latch keeps the lid or door from locking.
- **Misaligned door or lid** A door that sits slightly off cannot engage the strike and lock.
- **Failed lock motor** The small motor inside the lock assembly can wear out and stop latching.
- **Loose lock wiring** A loose harness at the lock breaks the signal that confirms locking.
- **Worn strike or latch** A broken strike or latch hook leaves the lock unable to catch.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer for several minutes to reset the control.
2. Check the latch area and remove any clothing or shipping material in the way.
3. Confirm the door or lid sits square and pushes firmly into the strike.
4. Plug back in and start a cycle to listen for the lock engaging.
5. If it still fails, unplug and inspect the lock wiring for loose or burnt connectors.
6. Test the lock assembly for continuity and confirm whether the motor still drives the latch.
7. Replace the lid lock or door lock assembly with an OEM part matched to your model.

## When to Call a Pro

If the latch path is clear and a new lock assembly does not clear F5E2, the control board may be driving the lock incorrectly. Call a technician if you cannot test the lock circuit safely.
