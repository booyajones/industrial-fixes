## Maytag F32 Error Code — What It Means

The F32 code on Maytag top-load HE and Bravos washers signals a drain problem. The control expects the tub to empty within a set time window during the drain portion of the cycle. When water drains too slowly or not at all, the machine stops and throws F32. The control monitors water level through a pressure transducer on the motor control board. If the pressure does not drop as expected, the fault is logged and the cycle halts.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain pump or debris in the impeller** Lint, coins, hairpins, or fabric obstruct the pump housing or jam the impeller blades, slowing flow and triggering the timeout.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose** A crushed, bent, or clogged hose restricts water flow between the pump and the standpipe or laundry sink.
- **Broken or weak drain pump** The pump motor runs but a cracked impeller blade or worn pump moves water too slowly to meet the drain window.
- **Pressure transducer or sensing hose fault** A disconnected, pinched, or clogged pressure hose prevents the control from detecting that the tub has drained, even when the pump clears water normally.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer and verify water remains in the tub after the error appears.
2. Inspect the drain hose from the back of the washer to the standpipe for kinks, crush points, and clogs. Straighten or replace if damaged.
3. Open the washer's lower access panel or front service area and locate the drain pump. Remove any lint trap or filter and clear visible debris.
4. Check the pump impeller by hand. Rotate it to feel for broken blades, looseness, or binding. If the impeller is damaged, replace the pump assembly.
5. Enter diagnostic or service test mode (consult your model's manual) and run the drain cycle. Watch whether the pump runs and whether water clears completely within the expected time.
6. Trace the pressure hose from the tub to the motor control board. make sure it is connected, not pinched, and free of water or debris. Blow through it gently to confirm it is open.
7. Reassemble the machine, plug in, and run a rinse-and-spin test cycle to confirm the tub drains fully and no code returns.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have cleared the pump and hose, confirmed the impeller spins freely, and checked the pressure hose but F32 still appears, the pressure transducer on the motor control board or the control itself may be faulty. Board-level diagnosis and replacement require safe handling of live circuits and calibrated test equipment. A qualified appliance technician can test the pressure sensing circuit, verify board operation in service mode, and source the correct control board for your model and serial number.
