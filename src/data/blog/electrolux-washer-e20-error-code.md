## Electrolux E20 Error Code — What It Means

The E20 code on an Electrolux washing machine indicates a draining or pumping fault. The appliance has detected that water is not leaving the tub properly. Electrolux groups E20, E21, and C2 together under drainage problems in their support documents. The machine may not be able to empty because of a blocked drain pipe, clogged filter, kinked hose, or a defective drain pump. In some Electrolux washer-dryer models, E20 or C2 is specifically described as a pumping problem.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the drain pump when the real problem is a blocked filter or a foreign object trapped in the sump path between the tub and pump. Always clean the filter and inspect the internal hoses and impeller for debris before ordering a new pump.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or dirty pump filter (~40%)** Lint, coins, hair, and debris accumulate in the fluff filter or sump filter, restricting water flow and triggering the E20 code.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose (~25%)** The hose may be twisted, crushed behind the machine, or clogged with detergent residue or lint.
- **Incorrect drain hose installation (~15%)** Electrolux specifies the hose must extend no more than 10 cm into the drain and the drain height must be between 60 cm and 100 cm above the base of the machine.
- **House drain or trap blockage (~10%)** The sink trap, siphon, or standpipe downstream of the machine may be clogged, preventing the washer from emptying.
- **Excess foam from too much detergent (~5%)** Overuse of detergent can create foam that blocks the level system and prevents proper drainage.
- **Defective drain pump (~5%)** The pump motor or impeller may be jammed, burned out, or unable to turn, though this is less common than blockages.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply.
2. **Open the filter access panel** at the front lower corner of the machine and place towels on the floor to catch residual water.
3. **Remove the pump filter** by turning it counterclockwise, then clean it thoroughly under running water and remove any lint, coins, or debris.
4. **Inspect the pump impeller** through the filter opening using a flashlight, and rotate the blade with a pencil to confirm it spins freely and is not obstructed.
5. **Pull the machine forward** and inspect the drain hose from the pump to the wall drain for kinks, twists, or blockages, and verify the hose height is between 60 cm and 100 cm above the base with no more than 10 cm inserted into the drain.
6. **Test the house drain** by placing the drain hose into a bucket or sink and running a rinse cycle, or by pouring water into the standpipe to confirm free flow.
7. **Reassemble the filter** and panel, plug the washer back in, and run a rinse or drain test cycle to verify the error clears and water flows freely.
8. **If the error persists**, switch the unit off for 1-2 hours to allow any overheated pump or protection device to reset, then retest.
9. **Replace the drain pump** if all hoses and filters are clear, the impeller will not turn, or the pump does not run during the drain cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have cleaned the filter, checked all hoses, verified correct installation height, and tested the house drain but the E20 code still appears. Also call if you are uncomfortable removing the machine's rear or lower access panels to reach the drain pump and internal hoses, or if the pump needs electrical testing or replacement and you lack the tools or experience. Electrolux recommends contacting authorized service if the above user checks do not restore drainage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300.
