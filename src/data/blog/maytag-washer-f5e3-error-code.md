## Maytag Washer F5E3 Error Code — What It Means

F5E3 is a door lock fault. The control commanded the lock but never read back a solid locked state, so the washer stops before it agitates or spins.

It can be a basic obstruction, but it often points to a failed lock component or a wiring problem at the latch.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Blocked latch area** Clothing, shipping foam, or debris keeps the lid or door from seating into the lock.
- **Failed lock component** The latch motor or sensor inside the lock wears out and cannot report locked.
- **Dirty lock sensor** Grime on the lock contacts breaks the electrical signal the control needs.
- **Damaged strike or hinge** A cracked, bent, or loose strike, latch, or hinge keeps the lock from sensing correctly.
- **Loose wiring connection** A connector backed out at the lock or control stops the locked signal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Remove obstructions. Clear anything around the latch and reduce the load if clothing presses on the door.
2. Close firmly. Shut the lid or door hard enough for the latch to seat into the lock.
3. Reset power. Unplug the washer for five minutes, plug it back in, and retry a cycle.
4. Inspect the hardware. Check the strike, latch, lid, door, and hinge for cracks, bends, or looseness.
5. Clean the lock contacts. Wipe the latch and sensor area so dirt is not breaking the signal.
6. Reseat the wiring. Check the connectors at the lock and the control for a firm fit.
7. Replace the door lock. If F5E3 returns after all checks, install a new lock assembly matched to your model and test a cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

If the latch is clear and aligned, the wiring is good, and a new lock does not clear F5E3, the control board may be at fault. That diagnosis is a good time to call an appliance technician, especially if the door stays locked and will not release.
