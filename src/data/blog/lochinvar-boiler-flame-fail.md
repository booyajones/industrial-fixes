## Lochinvar Boiler Flame Fail Error — What It Means

A **Flame Fail** lockout on a Lochinvar boiler (Knight, CREST, WHN, or commercial series) means the control board attempted to ignite the burner but the flame sensor (flame rod) did not detect a flame within the trial-for-ignition period — typically 4 seconds. After two or three failed ignition attempts, the boiler enters a hard lockout. The display shows "Flame Fail" or a corresponding fault code (E02 on some models). This is a safety lockout that requires manual reset after the root cause is corrected.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or corroded flame rod** — The flame rod detects combustion through ionization current. Carbon buildup or corrosion on the rod insulates it from the flame, preventing detection even when combustion occurs.
- **No gas supply or low gas pressure** — A closed shutoff valve, tripped gas regulator, or insufficient supply pressure prevents ignition from occurring at all.
- **Failed igniter** — The ignition electrode has cracked, corroded, or the gap has changed, preventing a strong enough spark to initiate combustion.
- **Air in the gas line** — After extended shutdown or new gas line work, air in the supply line prevents ignition on the first several attempts.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check gas supply** — Confirm the manual gas shutoff valve upstream of the boiler is fully open. Check that no other gas appliances have lost service (indicating a supply or regulator issue). Verify the gas meter is on.
2. **Clean the flame rod** — Shut down and lock out the boiler. Locate the flame sensor rod (a metal rod extending into the burner area with a ceramic insulator). Lightly clean the rod tip with fine steel wool or emery cloth. Do not bend or break the ceramic insulator.
3. **Inspect the ignition electrode** — Check the electrode for cracks in the ceramic insulator and verify the spark gap. Typical gap is 1/8" (3mm). A cracked insulator or wide gap means electrode replacement.
4. **Check the igniter cable and connections** — Inspect the high-voltage ignition wire from the ignition transformer to the electrode. Look for cracked insulation, loose connections, or arc marks indicating where the spark is escaping the cable.
5. **Reset and observe** — Press the manual RESET button on the boiler (hold for 5 seconds on most Lochinvar models). Watch and listen for the igniter spark and gas valve click. If gas doesn't ignite after 2 attempts, the gas valve or gas supply needs further investigation.

## When to Call a Pro

If the boiler sparks, gas is confirmed at the burner, and flame fail persists, the combustion controls may need adjustment — air-to-fuel ratio, gas valve pressure, or blower prover switch — which requires a licensed gas fitter or boiler technician.
