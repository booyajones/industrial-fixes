## Navien E005 Error Code — What It Means

The E005 code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates a flow sensor or flow-proving fault. The control board is not detecting valid water flow when it expects it, so the unit locks out to protect the heat exchanger and burner system. Navien states that the issue is most likely the flow sensor inside the unit is not spinning properly.

In practical terms, the flow sensor assembly may be obstructed by debris or scale, or the sensor itself has failed. The unit will not fire until the flow signal is restored and the code is cleared.

## Common Causes

- **Debris or scale blocking the flow sensor** Sediment, mineral buildup, or small particles prevent the sensor paddle from spinning freely, which is the most common cause according to Navien.
- **Failed flow sensor assembly** The sensor itself has mechanically failed or the electrical component inside the sensor no longer sends a valid signal to the control board.
- **Restricted inlet filter or strainer** A clogged inlet filter reduces flow velocity below the threshold needed to spin the sensor, even if the sensor itself is clean.
- **Air in the water lines** Trapped air pockets can interrupt flow detection during startup or after service work, causing the board to register a flow fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the error** by checking the unit's display panel while there is demand for hot water and verify the E005 code is present.
2. **Turn off power and close** the inlet and outlet isolation valves to safely work on the unit.
3. **Open and close the pressure relief valve** near the outlet connection a few times to try to flush any debris affecting the flow sensor, as recommended by Navien for this code.
4. **Remove and inspect the inlet filter** screen for sediment or scale buildup and clean it thoroughly with water or a descaling solution if needed.
5. **Access the flow sensor assembly** by removing the front cover and locating the sensor on the inlet waterway, then check for visible debris or buildup around the sensor paddle.
6. **Test the unit** by restoring power and water supply, then opening a hot water tap to see if the E005 clears and the burner fires normally.
7. **Replace the flow sensor** if the code persists after cleaning, as continued failure indicates the sensor mechanism or electronics have failed and the part must be swapped out.

## When to Call a Pro

Navien recommends service by the original installer, a licensed plumbing or heating contractor, or an authorized NSS technician if the fault persists after simple debris-clearing steps. If you are not comfortable removing the unit cover, working with water connections under pressure, or handling electrical components on a gas or electric water heater, call a professional. The flow sensor replacement requires disassembly of internal waterway components and verification of proper operation afterward, so professional diagnosis is the safest route if you have any doubt about the root cause.
