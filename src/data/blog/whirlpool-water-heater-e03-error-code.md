## What this code means
The E03 error code on Whirlpool Energy Smart electric water heaters indicates an upper thermistor sensor failure. This sensor is integrated into the Electronic Thermostat (ET) assembly and cannot be replaced separately. When the control board detects the sensor has failed or is reading outside normal parameters, it throws E03 and typically flashes an alert icon on the display. The upper thermistor monitors water temperature at the top of the tank so the control can manage heating cycles properly. When it fails, the system cannot regulate temperature safely and locks out heating.

## Common Causes

- **Failed upper thermistor sensor inside the ET** Whirlpool's service chart identifies E03 as upper thermistor sensor failure, and the sensor is part of the Electronic Thermostat assembly.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connections at the ET** Poor connections at the thermostat or element terminals can mimic a sensor failure and trigger E03.
- **Damaged wiring between the ET and control board** Pinched, frayed, or burned wires in the control circuit prevent the thermistor signal from reaching the board.
- **Overtemperature condition damaging the thermistor** If the tank was powered on before being fully filled with water, dry-fire overheating can burn out the upper thermistor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off electrical power** at the circuit breaker feeding the water heater and verify power is off with a non-contact voltage tester at the unit.
2. **Remove the access panel** covering the Electronic Thermostat assembly, usually located on the upper front of the tank.
3. **Inspect all wiring and connections** at the ET and upper element terminals for corrosion, looseness, or visible damage, and tighten or repair any suspect connections.
4. **Replace the Electronic Thermostat (ET)** if the code persists, because the upper thermistor sensor is integrated into the ET and cannot be serviced separately.
5. **Reconnect all wiring** to the new ET according to the wiring diagram on the tank label, matching wire colors and terminal positions exactly.
6. **Restore power** at the breaker and observe the display to confirm the E03 code clears and the unit begins a normal heating cycle.
7. **Monitor for code recurrence** over the next 24 hours, and if E03 returns after ET replacement, contact a qualified technician for deeper control-board diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are not comfortable working with 240-volt wiring or if the E03 code returns after you have replaced the Electronic Thermostat and verified all connections, call a licensed electrician or appliance service technician. Persistent codes after ET replacement suggest a control-board fault or a grounding issue that requires multimeter diagnostics and possibly additional parts. Also call a pro if you find evidence of water intrusion in the electrical compartment or if the tank was run dry, since those conditions can cause multiple component failures that need a full system evaluation.
