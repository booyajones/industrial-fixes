## Fujitsu E:08 Error Code — What It Means

The E:08 error code on a Fujitsu mini-split indicates a power source error or incorrect power supply fault. The unit is not receiving the expected electrical supply condition. This is not a refrigerant or sensor problem. It points to wrong voltage, voltage drop under load, loose connections, poor grounding, or damaged wiring feeding the indoor or outdoor unit.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Incorrect line voltage** The supply voltage at the unit does not match the nameplate requirements.
- **Voltage drop under load** Undersized wire or a long run causes voltage to sag when the compressor or fan starts.
- **Loose terminal connections** Connections at the terminal block, plugs, or wire connectors are not fully tightened.
- **Damaged wiring or poor ground** Pinched conductors, nicked insulation, or a missing or corroded ground wire interrupt the power path.
- **Unstable or interrupted power source** Breaker issues, utility fluctuations, or intermittent supply feed cause the fault to appear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker and wait three to five minutes, then restore power to see if the error clears after a full reset.
2. **Check incoming voltage** at the unit's terminal block with a multimeter and compare it to the nameplate voltage specification to confirm correct supply.
3. **Inspect and tighten all electrical connections** at the indoor and outdoor terminal blocks, plugs, and wire connectors.
4. **Examine wiring for damage** such as pinched or nicked conductors and verify the ground wire is securely connected at both ends.
5. **Measure voltage drop under load** by reading voltage at the unit while the compressor and fan are running to catch intermittent supply problems.
6. **Correct any supply-side issues** such as upgrading wire size, repairing damaged conductors, or fixing breaker or disconnect problems before replacing components.
7. **Replace the main control PCB** only after confirming that supply voltage, wiring, and grounding are all correct and the fault persists.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with line voltage or do not own a multimeter. Any work on mini-split electrical supply requires verifying correct voltage, load current, and grounding. If you have checked connections and the error returns, a technician will use load testing and PCB diagnostics to isolate the fault. Incorrect voltage or wiring repairs may also require an electrician to correct the building supply before the mini-split will operate without fault codes.
