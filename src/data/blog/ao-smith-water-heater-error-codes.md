# A.O. Smith Water Heater Error Codes: Complete Guide

A.O. Smith manufactures one of the widest lines of water heating equipment in North America, including the ProLine, Vertex (condensing), Voltex (heat pump), and Effex (high-efficiency gas) series. Error codes vary by model — this guide covers all major A.O. Smith fault codes.

## A.O. Smith Tankless Error Codes (ATI/ATH Series)

| [Code](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Code&tag=errorcodefixes-20) | Description | Common Cause |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Ignition failure | Gas supply, igniter, flame sensor |
| E02 | Flame signal lost during operation | Gas pressure, flame sensor |
| E03 | Overheat / high temperature limit | Scale, low flow, high setpoint |
| E04 | Cold water inlet sensor fault | Sensor failed or wiring |
| E05 | Hot water outlet sensor fault | Sensor failed or wiring |
| E06 | Flue gas temperature sensor fault | Flue sensor fault |
| E07 | Low water flow | Dirty inlet filter, low pressure |
| E08 | Combustion fan fault | Fan motor or speed sensor |
| E09 | Gas valve fault | Gas valve or wiring |
| E10 | Overheat — high limit trip | Second-level overheat protection |
| E11 | Scale detector alert | Scale buildup in heat exchanger |

## Most Common A.O. Smith Faults

### E03 — Overheat (Tankless)
Scale buildup is the leading cause in hard water areas:
1. Perform descaling with white vinegar or commercial descaler
2. Clean inlet filter screen
3. Lower setpoint — keep below 120°F for safe operation
4. Check minimum flow rate requirement (0.5 GPM for most models)

### 2 Flashes — Low Thermopile Voltage (Gas Storage)
1. Clean the thermopile with fine steel wool
2. Verify the pilot flame surrounds the thermopile tip
3. Check for draft problems — wind or negative pressure in the space
4. Replace thermopile if voltage stays below 350 mV

### E01 — Ignition Failure (Tankless)
1. Verify gas pressure at the unit (3.5 in. w.c. natural gas)
2. Check igniter spark — should be visible in dark conditions
3. Clean flame sensor
4. Check for blocked burner ports
