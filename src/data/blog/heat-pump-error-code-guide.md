## Heat Pump Error Codes: All Brands Guide

Heat pumps fail in a few predictable ways no matter whose badge is on the cabinet: high pressure, low pressure, defrost problems, sensor faults, communication failures, and compressor protection. Brand-specific codes change, but the physics do not.

## Common Heat Pump Error Codes Across Brands

| Symptom / Code | Common Meaning | Typical Brands |
|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| High pressure | Dirty coil, indoor airflow issue, or overcharge | Carrier, Trane, Goodman, Lennox |
| Low pressure | Low charge or restriction | Carrier, Goodman, Daikin, Rheem |
| Defrost fault | Sensor, board, or outdoor coil icing issue | Trane, Goodman, Lennox, Mitsubishi |
| Communication fault | Indoor and outdoor control issue | Communicating and inverter systems |
| Sensor fault | Ambient, coil, or discharge sensor failed | Most inverter systems |
| Compressor protection | Overcurrent, overtemp, or overload | Most brands |

## Cooling vs heating matters

A pressure fault in cooling points you toward the outdoor coil or refrigerant. The same style fault in heating may point toward the indoor coil, blower, or defrost performance.

## Defrost is a major differentiator

Straight AC systems do not have to manage frost. Heat pumps do. If a heat pump ices up outside and then shuts down, defrost sensors, defrost boards, or refrigerant charge become prime suspects.

## Inverter heat pumps protect themselves earlier

Variable-speed heat pumps will often throw discharge temp, sensor, or drive protection codes before they ever get to a hard mechanical failure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Pull the exact code** — Do not troubleshoot based on symptom alone.
2. **Check coils and airflow** — Indoor and outdoor airflow still solve a lot of heat pump problems.
3. **Consider operating mode** — Heating and cooling faults mean different things on the same machine.
4. **Review defrost behavior** — If the outdoor coil stays iced, the defrost system needs attention.
5. **Use gauges if qualified** — Pressure and charge faults need real measurements.
6. **Retest in both modes** — A true fix should survive both heating and cooling operation if seasonal conditions allow.

## When to Call a Pro

Heat pump codes that point to low refrigerant, compressor protection, or repeated defrost failure deserve a qualified tech. Heat pumps are harder to diagnose casually because the same circuit has to work correctly in two opposite modes.
