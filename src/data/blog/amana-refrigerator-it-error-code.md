## Amana Refrigerator IT Error Code — What It Means

The IT error code on an Amana refrigerator indicates a detected problem in the ice maker system that is affecting ice production or dispensing. This code is not published in official Amana documentation available to consumers, but field technician sources consistently describe it as an ice maker fault. When the code appears, the refrigerator has identified an issue that prevents the ice maker from operating normally, which may include mechanical blockages, electrical failures in the ice maker module, or water supply interruptions.

The fault can originate from several points in the ice making cycle, including the motor and harvesting mechanism, the water inlet path, the ice bin and ejector arm, or the control circuitry that manages ice production. Because the code is broad, diagnosis requires a step-by-step check of the entire ice maker system to isolate the specific component or condition responsible for the fault.

## Common Causes

- **Failed ice maker assembly or motor** The ice maker module or harvesting motor has stopped functioning and cannot complete the ice ejection or refill cycle.
- **Water supply interruption** The water line is disconnected, kinked, frozen, or the shutoff valve is closed, preventing water from reaching the ice maker.
- **Ice jam or chute blockage** Ice cubes or frost buildup have jammed the ejector arm, chute, or dispenser opening, preventing normal ice delivery and triggering a fault.
- **Ice maker turned off or bin not seated** The ice maker control is in the off position or the ice bin is not correctly installed, causing the system to register a fault.
- **Control board or circuit failure** The main board or ice maker control interface has a fault that prevents proper communication or power to the ice maker assembly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check the ice maker switch and bin.** Verify the ice maker is turned on (lift or press the control arm or button per your model) and confirm the ice bin is fully seated and not obstructing any sensors.
2. **Inspect the water supply.** Trace the cold water line from the shutoff valve to the refrigerator and confirm the valve is fully open, the line is not kinked or frozen, and water pressure is adequate.
3. **Clear any ice jams or frost.** Open the freezer and remove the ice bin, then inspect the ejector arm, ice chute, and surrounding area for stuck cubes or frost buildup and clear all obstructions.
4. **Run a manual ice maker cycle.** Locate the test button or lever on your ice maker assembly (consult your model's service sheet) and initiate a harvest cycle to observe whether the motor runs and ice ejects normally.
5. **Check for power and connectivity.** Unplug the refrigerator, remove the ice maker assembly cover, and inspect the wiring harness for loose connections, corrosion, or damage at the ice maker and control board.
6. **Test or replace the ice maker assembly.** If water supply and mechanical function are normal but the motor does not run or the unit fails the manual cycle, replace the ice maker assembly with an exact replacement part for your model number.
7. **Monitor after repair.** Restore power, allow 24 hours for a full ice production cycle, and verify the IT code clears and ice production resumes without further faults.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have verified the water supply and cleared all ice jams but the IT code persists, if the ice maker does not respond to a manual test cycle, or if you are uncomfortable working with refrigerator wiring and control boards. A technician can run factory diagnostic routines, measure voltages and continuity at the ice maker and control board, and identify whether the fault lies in the ice maker assembly itself or in upstream control circuitry. Professional help is also recommended if your unit is under warranty, since self-repair may void coverage, or if the fault involves refrigerant lines or sealed-system components near the ice maker.
