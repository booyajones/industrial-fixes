## Electrolux Washer E21 Error Code — What It Means

The E21 error code on an Electrolux front-load washer indicates the machine is taking too long to pump water out or is not draining fast enough. The control board did not see the washer empty within the allowed time window, so it flags a drain or pump-out performance fault.

This is not a fill error or door-lock issue. The machine detected water remaining in the tub longer than expected, which means something is blocking, restricting, or slowing the drain path, or the pump itself cannot move water adequately.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the drain pump immediately when the filter and hose are actually clogged. Clean the drain filter and inspect the hose for kinks or blockage before buying a pump.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked or restricted drain hose (~35%)** The drain hose is crushed, twisted, or improperly routed so water cannot exit the tub quickly enough.
- **Clogged drain filter or pump filter (~30%)** Lint, coins, fabric debris, or other objects block the filter housing or pump inlet and slow or stop water flow.
- **Improper drain hose installation (~15%)** The hose is inserted too far into the standpipe, routed above the recommended height, or otherwise installed incorrectly.
- **Debris in the drain path (~10%)** Obstructions inside the hose, pump chamber, or drain outlet restrict flow even when the filter appears clean.
- **Faulty drain pump (~10%)** The pump is weak, seized, noisy, or does not energize properly after the hose and filter have been confirmed clear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** from the wall outlet and turn off the water supply valves before opening any drain components.
2. **Inspect the drain hose** at the rear of the washer for kinks, crushing, or sharp bends, and verify it is routed correctly per the use-and-care manual without being inserted too far into the standpipe.
3. **Locate the drain filter access door** at the lower front of the washer, place a shallow pan or towels underneath, and slowly open the filter cap to catch any remaining water.
4. **Remove and clean the drain filter** thoroughly, checking the filter housing and pump inlet for lint, coins, fabric pieces, or other debris that could restrict flow.
5. **Flush the drain hose** by disconnecting it from the standpipe and running water through it into a bucket to confirm it is clear inside.
6. **Reinstall the filter and hose** securely, plug the washer back in, and run a drain or spin cycle to test if the E21 error clears and the tub empties normally.
7. **Test the drain pump** electrically and mechanically if the error returns after hose and filter checks are complete, and replace the pump if it is weak, seized, noisy, or does not energize when the machine attempts to drain.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the E21 code returns after you have cleaned the filter, verified the hose is clear and correctly installed, and flushed the drain path. A pro can test the drain pump electrically, check for wiring faults or pressure-switch issues, and replace the pump or other components safely. Also call if the washer was recently installed or moved and you are unsure whether the drain hose height, routing, or standpipe depth meets manufacturer specifications, since Electrolux explicitly identifies installation errors as a common cause of this fault.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-50 in parts (filter or hose if damaged), 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300 including service call and pump replacement if needed.
