## Electrolux Washer E21 Error Code — What It Means

E21 on an Electrolux front-load washer means the machine is taking too long to drain or water is not pumping out fast enough. The control board has timed out waiting for the tub to empty during a drain cycle. This is a pump-out time fault, not a total blockage in most cases. The washer will pause mid-cycle and display the code when drain performance falls below the expected threshold.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked or restricted drain hose** The most common issue flagged by Electrolux is a drain hose that is pinched, crushed, or routed improperly behind the washer.
- **Improper drain hose installation** A hose positioned too high, too low, or inserted too far into the standpipe will slow or restrict drainage.
- **Clogged pump filter or coin trap** Lint, coins, hairpins, and small debris collect in the front access filter and choke water flow to the pump.
- **Debris blockage in the drain path** Obstructions inside the drain hose, pump housing, or standpipe outlet prevent proper water evacuation.
- **Failed or weak drain pump** When the hose and filter are clear but the code persists, the pump impeller or motor may be worn or electrically faulty.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer from power before opening any access panels or removing the filter to avoid shock hazard.
2. Check the drain hose behind the washer for kinks, crushing, or tight bends and straighten any restrictions you find.
3. Verify the drain hose is correctly installed in the standpipe with proper height and insertion depth according to the installation manual.
4. Open the lower front access panel, place a towel or shallow tray beneath the filter, then unscrew and remove the drain pump filter to clean out lint, coins, and debris.
5. Disconnect the drain hose if accessible and flush it with water to clear any internal blockage.
6. Reassemble the filter and hose, then run a drain or spin cycle to test whether the E21 code clears.
7. If the code returns after clearing the drain path, inspect the drain pump assembly and related wiring or schedule a service call to test and replace the pump.

## When to Call a Pro

If the unit was installed or moved within the last 30 days, Electrolux recommends contacting your installer to verify proper drain hose setup. If you have cleared the hose, cleaned the filter, and flushed the drain path but E21 still returns, the pump or control board may be at fault and professional diagnosis with voltage and continuity testing is the next step. Any repair that requires disassembly of the pump housing, access to internal wiring, or testing of electrical components should be handled by a qualified appliance technician.
