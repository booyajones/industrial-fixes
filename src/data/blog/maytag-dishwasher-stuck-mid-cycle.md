## Maytag Dishwasher Stuck Mid Cycle — What's Happening

A Maytag dishwasher stuck mid cycle is not a single fault code. It means the unit stopped before finishing the program. Maytag's own troubleshooting treats this as a control, power, or interruption issue first, then as a drainage or error-code problem if present.

If you opened the door during the cycle, the machine paused on purpose. If power was interrupted or the control panel is flashing, you will need to restore power and reset the unit. If the dishwasher is not draining or is showing an error code, those issues must be addressed before the cycle can continue.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Door opened or latch not secure** Opening the door during a cycle pauses the program, and a weak or intermittent latch switch can stop the cycle even when the door appears closed.
- **Power interruption or tripped breaker** A momentary power loss or tripped circuit breaker will halt the cycle and require you to restore power and reset the control.
- **Drainage problem** Maytag's troubleshooting starts by checking whether the dishwasher is draining properly, because standing water can prevent cycle advancement.
- **Water fill problem or inlet valve failure** A clogged inlet valve screen or a failed valve can stop the dishwasher from filling and advancing through the cycle.
- **Control board glitch or electronic fault** A temporary control glitch or a deeper electronic failure can freeze the cycle, and Maytag includes control reset steps for this reason.
- **Heating-related stall on some models** Certain Maytag models pause cycle advancement until water reaches target temperature or a timeout occurs, and this is confirmed by checking the tech sheet for your exact unit.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify whether the door was opened, power was lost, or you pressed Cancel before the cycle finished.
2. Check the control panel for active error codes or flashing lights and note any displayed codes.
3. Close the door firmly and press Start to see if the cycle resumes.
4. If the cycle does not resume, turn off the dishwasher breaker for 30 to 60 seconds, restore power, and start a new cycle.
5. Confirm the dishwasher is draining by running a Cancel/Drain sequence and listening for the drain pump.
6. Inspect the door latch and door switch for proper operation by opening and closing the door and feeling for a solid click.
7. Check that the water supply valve is fully open and remove the inlet valve screen to look for debris or blockage.
8. Run a full test cycle after any repair to confirm the machine advances through wash, rinse, and heat stages without stopping.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a tech if the dishwasher continues to stop mid cycle after you have reset the power, checked the door latch, verified drainage, and inspected the water supply. If error codes appear on the panel or the control does not respond to resets, a technician with the model-specific service sheet can test the control board, thermistor, and heating circuit accurately. Heating-related stalls and control-board diagnosis require testing voltages and resistance values that vary by platform and are not safe to guess.
