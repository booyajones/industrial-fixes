## Mitsubishi PB Error Code — What It Means

The PB (or Pb) error code on a Mitsubishi mini-split signals an indoor unit fan motor error. The system has detected that the indoor fan is not operating normally, or the fan motor feedback circuit is reporting an abnormal condition. This fault appears in both Mr. Slim and City Multi systems and means the indoor blower cannot spin correctly or the control board cannot verify proper fan operation.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed indoor fan motor** The blower motor itself has burned out, seized, or lost electrical continuity and can no longer spin.
- **Bad indoor control board** The PCB that powers and monitors the fan motor has failed in its fan control circuit or feedback path.
- **Loose or disconnected fan motor connector** The harness plug at the motor or board has backed out, corroded, or lost contact with one or more pins.
- **Wiring damage or open circuit** A break, pinch, or rodent damage in the fan motor harness prevents the board from sending power or reading feedback.
- **Mechanical blockage or drag** Dirt, a broken blower wheel blade, or a seized bearing physically stops the fan from rotating even when power is present.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Pull the stored error history** from the wired controller or service menu to confirm PB/Pb is the active or recurring fault code.
2. **Power off the unit** at the disconnect and remove the indoor unit's front panel and filters to expose the blower assembly.
3. **Inspect the blower wheel and motor shaft** for dirt buildup, broken blades, binding, or anything that would prevent free rotation, then spin the wheel by hand to check for mechanical drag.
4. **Check the indoor fan motor connector** at both the motor and the indoor control board for looseness, corrosion, bent pins, or disconnected wires.
5. **Verify power and control signals** at the fan motor harness using a multimeter, consulting your model's service manual for expected voltage during fan call.
6. **Replace the indoor fan motor** if wiring and connectors are intact but the motor is stiff, noisy, or shows no continuity, or **replace the indoor control PCB** if the motor tests good and the fault persists.
7. **Clear the error history** through the service menu or by cycling power, then run the unit through all fan speeds to confirm stable operation and no return of the PB code.

## When to Call a Pro

A PB error requires opening the indoor unit, testing live circuits, and often replacing the fan motor or control board. If you are not comfortable working with line-voltage wiring, reading service menus, or handling refrigerant-system components, call a licensed HVAC technician. A pro will also have access to Mitsubishi's full fault history tools and genuine replacement parts matched to your exact model. If the blower spins freely by hand and all connectors look intact but the code returns, the root cause is likely in the control board or motor feedback circuit and professional diagnosis will save you from guessing at expensive parts.
