## Frigidaire Washer E20 Error Code — What It Means

The E20 code on a Frigidaire washer signals a drain fault. The control board has detected that water is not leaving the tub within the expected time window. This is a timeout condition during a drain cycle, not a wash or fill problem.

The fault points to something blocking the drain path, a restriction in the hose, or a failed drain pump. The washer is trying to pump water out but cannot complete the drain operation before the timer expires.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the drain pump immediately without checking for clogs. First inspect and clean the pump filter, coin trap, and hose for debris. A sock or hairpin can block flow without harming the pump motor.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged pump filter or coin trap (~45%)** Lint, hair, coins, socks, and small objects collect in the filter housing and block water flow to the pump impeller.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose (~25%)** The external drain hose may be crushed, kinked behind the washer, or obstructed by a sock or debris inside the hose itself.
- **Foreign object in pump impeller (~15%)** A coin, wire, or broken plastic piece lodges in the pump inlet or jams the impeller blades, preventing the pump from spinning freely.
- **Failed drain pump motor (~10%)** The pump motor runs weakly, makes noise without pumping, or does not run at all due to a burned winding or failed capacitor.
- **Collapsed internal sump hose or loose clamp (~5%)** The rubber hose or elbow between the tub sump and the pump inlet can collapse, crack, or come loose at a clamp, blocking flow before it reaches the pump.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves.
2. **Pull the washer away from the wall** and inspect the drain hose for kinks, crush points, and obvious blockages. Straighten or replace the hose if damaged.
3. **Open the lower front service panel** (usually a kick plate held by clips or screws) and locate the pump filter or coin trap, typically a round twist-off cap at the bottom front.
4. **Place a shallow pan or towels** under the filter, slowly unscrew the filter cap, and let residual water drain into the pan. Remove the filter and clean out lint, coins, hair, and any debris.
5. **Reach into the pump cavity** and check that the impeller blades spin freely by hand. Remove any objects wedged in the inlet or around the impeller.
6. **Reassemble the filter and service panel**, plug the washer back in, and run a drain or spin cycle to verify water pumps out normally and the E20 code does not return.
7. **If the code persists** after clearing all restrictions, test the drain pump electrically or replace the pump assembly with a new unit matching your model number.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have cleared the filter, hose, and pump cavity but the E20 code returns every cycle. A technician can test the pump motor electrically, check internal sump hoses and clamps that are hard to reach, and verify that the control board is sending the correct signals to the pump. Also call if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections or if the washer needs to be disassembled beyond the lower service panel to access internal components.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-250.
