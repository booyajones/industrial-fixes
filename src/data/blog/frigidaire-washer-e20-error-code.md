## Frigidaire E20 Error Code — What It Means

E20 on a Frigidaire washer is a drain fault. The machine is not detecting proper water evacuation during the drain portion of the cycle. In practical terms, this means the washer won't drain. The tub will still contain water and the cycle will stall or refuse to complete. The code points to a restriction or failure somewhere in the drain pump circuit, the drain hose path, or the pump itself.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain pump filter or trap** Lint, coins, fabric, hair, and small debris accumulate in the filter basket and block water flow.
- **Blocked or kinked drain hose** The external drain hose may be crushed, kinked, or internally clogged with debris.
- **Foreign object in the pump or impeller area** A sock, fabric scrap, or other item lodges in the pump inlet or impeller and jams the mechanism.
- **Faulty drain pump motor or assembly** When the drain path is clear but the unit still will not pump, the pump motor itself has failed.
- **Collapsed or constricted hose elbow** A hose section or elbow can collapse under suction during the drain cycle and limit flow.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer and shut off the water supply before opening any panels or accessing internal components.
2. Inspect the external drain hose for kinks, crushing, or blockage along its entire length and at the standpipe connection.
3. Access and remove the drain pump filter or trap, then clean out all lint, coins, debris, and foreign material.
4. Check the pump inlet, impeller, and hose elbow for lodged fabric or obstructions and clear any blockage.
5. Reassemble the filter and hoses, plug the machine back in, and run a drain or spin test cycle to confirm water evacuates normally.
6. If the code returns and the drain path is completely clear, test the drain pump electrically (consult your model's service sheet for test points) and replace the pump if it does not perform correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have cleaned the filter, cleared the hose, checked the impeller area, and the E20 code still appears after a drain test, the drain pump likely requires electrical testing and replacement. A technician has the tools to verify pump voltage, measure resistance, and safely install the new assembly. Call a pro if you are uncomfortable working around water and live electrical connections or if the pump is difficult to reach on your model.
