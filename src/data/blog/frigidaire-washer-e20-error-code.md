## Frigidaire Washer E20 Error Code — What It Means

The E20 code on a Frigidaire washer means water did not drain from the tub within the allowed drain period. The machine is reporting a drain-system fault. In the field, technicians treat it as a not-draining or slow-draining problem rather than a single failed component. The fault can be caused by a blockage anywhere in the path from the tub to the drain pump, a restriction in the drain hose, or less commonly an electrical issue with the pump motor or control board.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the drain pump when the real problem is a sock or coin stuck in the pump inlet or trap. Before ordering a pump, remove the trap cover and hose and check for debris by hand.

## Common Causes

- **Coin, sock, lint, or fabric obstruction (~50%)** Debris lodged in the pump trap, filter basket, tub-to-pump hose, or rubber elbow blocks water flow and triggers the drain timeout.
- **Drain pump impeller obstruction or jam (~20%)** A piece of wire, bobby pin, or hardened lint prevents the impeller from spinning freely even when the motor is energized.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose (~15%)** The flexible drain hose is pinched behind the machine, crushed, or clogged with detergent residue or lint.
- **Failed drain pump motor (~10%)** The pump motor does not turn or runs weakly because of electrical failure or worn brushes.
- **Control board or wiring issue (~5%)** A loose connector, corroded wire, or board fault prevents the pump from receiving power when the drain cycle starts.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and move it forward enough to access the rear panel or pump area.
2. **Open the pump trap or filter cover** at the front lower corner and place a shallow pan underneath to catch residual water.
3. **Remove and inspect the trap basket or filter** for coins, bobby pins, buttons, fabric, or lint and clean it thoroughly.
4. **Disconnect the drain hose** from the back of the machine and the standpipe, then check inside for kinks, crushing, or blockages.
5. **Remove the tub-to-pump hose or rubber elbow** by loosening the hose clamps and pulling it free, then look for trapped socks, dog hair, or lint wads.
6. **Inspect the drain pump impeller** by rotating it by hand to verify it spins freely and is not jammed by wire or debris.
7. **Reassemble all hoses and clamps**, plug the washer back in, and run a drain or spin cycle to verify water drains and the code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have cleared the trap, hoses, and pump and the E20 code still appears, or if you hear no sound from the pump during a drain cycle and are not comfortable testing wiring or replacing the control board. A technician can perform electrical diagnosis to confirm whether the pump is receiving the correct drive signal and whether the control board or wiring harness is at fault. Also call if the machine has leaked or if you see signs of water damage around the pump or base, since reassembly must be leak-tight.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts if a pump is needed, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-250.
