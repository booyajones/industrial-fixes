## Whirlpool Dishwasher F6E1 Error Code — What It Means

F6E1 sets when the control board does not detect water entering the tub within the expected time. The dishwasher tries to fill but the float never rises, so the cycle stalls.

On the Whirlpool Global HE platform this points first at the water inlet valve and its supply, then at the wiring between the valve and the control board.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Closed or low supply** The household water supply valve under the sink is shut off or barely open.
- **Kinked or blocked fill hose** A pinched fill line or a clogged inlet screen starves the tub of water.
- **Failed water inlet valve** The inlet valve solenoid is open electrically or stuck closed and no longer admits water.
- **Wiring or connector fault** A loose or corroded harness connector between the valve and the control board breaks the signal.
- **Weak home water pressure** Pressure below roughly 20 psi can stop the valve from filling in time.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for five minutes, then restore it and run a short cycle to see if the code clears.
2. Confirm the water supply valve under the sink is fully open.
3. Pull the dishwasher forward and inspect the fill hose for kinks or crushing.
4. Shut off water and power, then check the inlet screen at the valve for debris and clean it.
5. Test the inlet valve solenoid for continuity with a meter and replace it if the reading is open.
6. Reseat the harness connectors at the valve and at the control board.
7. If the valve and wiring test good, replace the water inlet valve assembly.

## When to Call a Pro

If the supply, hose, and valve all test good and the code returns, the fault is likely in the control board fill circuit. That diagnosis and board replacement is best handled by a qualified technician.
