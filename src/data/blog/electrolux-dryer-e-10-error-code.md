## Electrolux Dryer E10 Error Code — What It Means

E10 is not a verified Electrolux dryer fault code. According to manufacturer documentation, E10 appears on Electrolux washing machines and indicates a problem with water flow or filling. The appliance may not be filling with water or may be filling too slowly. If you are seeing E10 on a dryer display, you may be looking at a misread code, a model-specific fault not covered in standard literature, or the unit may actually be a washer-dryer combo. Confirm the exact model number from the rating plate and consult the model-specific service manual before proceeding with any repair.

If your appliance is actually a washing machine or washer-dryer combo, E10 means the water supply system is restricted or faulty. The machine cannot detect proper fill levels within the expected time. Common causes include closed taps, low pressure, clogged inlet filters, kinked hoses, or a failed inlet valve.

## Common Causes

- **Wrong appliance type or misread display** E10 is documented for Electrolux washers, not standalone dryers, so the code may be misidentified or the unit may be a combo model.
- **Water tap closed or low pressure (if washer/combo)** The supply tap is not fully open or household water pressure is too low to fill the machine in the expected time.
- **Clogged inlet hose filters** Fine mesh screens at the hose connection and valve inlet trap sediment and restrict water flow when blocked.
- **Kinked or twisted inlet hose** The supply hose is bent or crushed behind the machine, preventing adequate water flow.
- **Failed water inlet valve** The solenoid valve that controls water entry is stuck closed, partially blocked, or electrically failed.
- **Faulty pressure sensor or level circuit** The machine cannot accurately detect water level, causing fill errors even when water is entering normally.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the appliance type and model number** from the rating plate inside the door or on the back panel to confirm whether you have a dryer, washer, or combo unit.
2. **Check the water supply tap** (if washer or combo) is fully open and test flow by disconnecting the inlet hose and placing it in a bucket. It should fill about 10 liters in about 1 minute.
3. **Inspect and clean the inlet hose filters** at both ends of the supply hose. Remove the hose from the tap and the machine, pull out the fine mesh screens, rinse under running water, and reinstall.
4. **Examine the inlet hose for kinks, twists, or damage** along its length and behind the machine. Straighten or replace the hose if restricted.
5. **Clean the pump filter and check drain hose installation** (if washer or combo). The drain hose should extend no more than 10 cm into the standpipe and sit at a height of 60 to 100 cm.
6. **Test the water inlet valve** for blockage or electrical failure by disconnecting power, removing the top or rear panel, locating the valve at the fill hose connection, and checking for debris or coil resistance per your model's service data.
7. **Reset the machine** by unplugging for two minutes, then restore power and run a test cycle to see if the fault clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have confirmed your model number and E10 is a legitimate dryer code not covered in standard Electrolux literature, or if you lack the service manual for your specific model. Also call for help if you have cleaned all filters and hoses on a washer or combo unit but the fault persists, or if you are uncomfortable working with water connections and electrical components. A qualified appliance repair technician can access model-specific fault code tables, pressure switch diagnostics, and valve resistance specifications that are not published in consumer-facing documents.
