## State Water Heater E157 Error Code — What It Means

The E157 error code on State water heaters is not a universal fault across all models. State is part of the A. O. Smith support system, and error definitions vary by individual model and serial number. To determine exactly what E157 means for your unit, locate the rating plate on your water heater and use the QR code to access the correct manual and troubleshooting resources for that specific model.

Based on common fault patterns in State and A. O. Smith tankless water heaters, E157 is likely related to ignition or combustion failure. The most frequent causes in this product family are blocked air intake, insufficient gas supply, dirty flame sensors, or low water flow. These issues prevent the unit from lighting or maintaining a flame safely. Always confirm the exact fault definition from your model's manual before beginning repairs.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake or exhaust vent** Lint, debris, or snow blocking the air inlet or exhaust termination prevents proper combustion airflow and triggers ignition faults.
- **Insufficient gas supply or low gas pressure** Low gas pressure, a partially closed valve, or an undersized gas line starves the burner and prevents ignition.
- **Dirty or failed flame sensor** Carbon buildup or corrosion on the flame rod or thermocouple prevents the control board from detecting flame presence.
- **Clogged inlet water filter or low flow** A restricted inlet filter or low incoming flow rate prevents the unit from reaching minimum flow to initiate heating.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger** Mineral deposits in the heat exchanger reduce flow and heat transfer, causing overheating or flow-related faults on condensing models.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Identify your exact model and pull the manual.** Locate the rating plate on the side or front of the unit, scan the QR code, and access the official State support portal to confirm the exact meaning of E157 for your model.
2. **Inspect and clear the air intake and exhaust vent.** Remove any blockage from the intake screen and check the exhaust termination outside for obstructions like ice, leaves, or bird nests.
3. **Check gas supply and valve position.** Confirm the gas shutoff valve feeding the unit is fully open and that other gas appliances in the home are operating normally to rule out supply issues.
4. **Clean the inlet water filter.** Shut off the cold water inlet, remove the filter screen at the inlet connection, rinse it under running water, and reinstall it.
5. **Inspect and clean the flame sensor.** If your model uses an accessible flame rod or thermocouple, remove it per the manual, gently polish the metal tip with fine sandpaper or a soft cloth, and reinstall.
6. **Reset the unit and monitor.** Power cycle the water heater by turning off the power switch or breaker for 30 seconds, then restore power and observe whether the error returns.
7. **Check for recurring faults and call for service.** If E157 reappears after reset, the problem likely involves the gas valve, control board, or a sensor that requires model-specific diagnostic tools and parts replacement by a qualified technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if E157 persists after you have cleared the air intake, confirmed gas supply, and cleaned the inlet filter and flame sensor. Diagnosing control board faults, testing gas valve operation, or measuring inlet gas pressure requires specialized tools and training. Any work involving gas connections or internal combustion components should be performed by a technician familiar with State and A. O. Smith tankless systems to avoid safety hazards and warranty issues.
