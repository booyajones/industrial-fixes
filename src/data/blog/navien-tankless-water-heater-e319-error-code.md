## Navien Tankless Water Heater E319 Error — What It Means

The E319 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates a fault detected by the unit's control board. The exact meaning of E319 can vary by model and firmware version, so consult your owner's manual or the service label inside the unit for the specific definition. Generally, three-digit E3xx codes point to issues with internal sensors, communication between components, or control board operation.

Because Navien uses different code definitions across its product line, do not assume E319 means the same thing on every unit. Check your wiring diagram and service manual to identify which sensor or circuit the code references on your particular model before ordering parts.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the main control board when the real problem is a loose connector or a single failed sensor. Check all wiring harnesses and sensor continuity before swapping expensive boards.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded sensor connector (~30%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen plugs at temperature sensors, flow sensors, or gas valve connections, breaking communication with the control board.
- **Failed temperature sensor (~25%)** Inlet, outlet, or burner temperature sensors can drift out of range or fail open, triggering a fault code.
- **Control board glitch or firmware issue (~20%)** The main PCB may log a spurious error during startup or after a power surge, often cleared by a full power cycle.
- **Wiring harness damage (~15%)** Rodent damage, pinched wires during installation, or wear at flex points can create intermittent faults.
- **Faulty control board (~10%)** The main printed circuit board itself can develop solder cracks, failed relays, or component wear over time.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off electrical power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front service panel** by loosening the screws at the bottom and lifting the cover away from the unit.
3. **Photograph the control board** and all connected wiring harnesses so you have a reference for reassembly.
4. **Inspect every connector** on the control board and trace wiring to each sensor (inlet, outlet, burner, flow). Look for loose pins, corrosion, or burned contacts.
5. **Reseat any loose connectors** firmly and clean corroded pins with electrical contact cleaner or a pencil eraser.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit by pressing the reset button or cycling the power switch. Observe whether the E319 code reappears.
7. **If the code persists**, consult your model's service manual for the E319 definition and use a multimeter to test the resistance of the suspect sensor. Replace the sensor if readings are out of spec or if the manual identifies a specific failed component.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you are not comfortable working around gas connections, if the error code definition is unclear for your model, or if you have tested sensors and wiring without finding a fault. Tankless water heater diagnostics often require specialized tools and software to read internal fault logs and measure sensor signals in real time. A pro can also verify gas pressure, check for firmware updates, and replace the control board under warranty if applicable.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-450.
