## Navien E109 Error — What It Means

The E109 code indicates a motor fan problem or combustion air system fault. Your Navien has detected that the fan cannot spin normally, the air path is blocked, or the fan feedback signal is not reaching the control board. The unit shuts down to protect itself from unsafe combustion conditions. This is not a water temperature issue. It is an airflow or fan-rotation fault.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged air inlet filter or blocked intake path** Dirt, lint, or debris restricts airflow and prevents the fan from drawing combustion air properly.
- **Faulty or obstructed fan motor** The fan may be mechanically stuck, corroded, or damaged and cannot rotate at the required speed.
- **Loose or damaged fan wiring and PCB connection** A poor connection between the fan harness and the control board interrupts the feedback signal.
- **Water intrusion into the fan or gas valve area** Condensation or external water can enter the gas pipe path and damage the fan motor or electrical components.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and gas** at the circuit breaker and gas shutoff valve before opening any panels.
2. **Remove and inspect the air inlet filter** on the front or side of the unit. Clean it thoroughly with water and a soft brush or replace it if clogged or damaged.
3. **Open the front cover** and visually inspect the fan assembly for obstructions, corrosion, or standing water. Try rotating the fan blade by hand to confirm it spins freely without grinding or resistance.
4. **Check the fan wiring harness** where it plugs into the control board. Reseat the connector firmly and look for damaged pins, corroded terminals, or frayed wires. Repair or replace the harness if defects are found.
5. **Look for signs of water intrusion** around the gas valve, fan housing, and condensate drain. If moisture is present, identify and correct the source before proceeding.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit and observe the fan during startup. If the fan does not spin or the E109 returns immediately, replace the fan motor.
7. **Monitor the unit** through several heating cycles. If the code reappears, replace the control board or consult a technician for further diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are not comfortable working around gas appliances, call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician. Water intrusion cases often require investigation of the venting system, condensate trap, or building envelope, which may need professional assessment. If you have replaced the fan and harness and the E109 persists, the control board may need diagnosis with manufacturer-specific tools and firmware.
