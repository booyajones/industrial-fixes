## Navien E305 Error Code — What It Means

The E305 error code on Navien tankless water heaters typically signals an issue with the air pressure sensing system or exhaust ventilation. The unit detects that combustion air flow or exhaust venting is not operating within safe parameters. This safety feature prevents the heater from running when proper airflow cannot be confirmed.

The error may appear immediately on startup or after the unit has been running. Because proper venting is critical for safe combustion and carbon monoxide removal, the unit will lock out until the condition is resolved and the system is reset.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the air pressure switch first, but a blocked vent pipe or disconnected pressure sensing tube is more common. Inspect all vent pipes and hoses before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted vent pipe (~35%)** Ice buildup, bird nests, debris, or condensation pooling in horizontal vent runs prevents proper exhaust flow and triggers the pressure sensor.
- **Faulty air pressure switch (~25%)** The switch that monitors combustion air flow can fail electrically or become stuck, sending false signals to the control board.
- **Disconnected or cracked pressure sensing tube (~20%)** The small rubber or vinyl hose connecting the air pressure switch to the combustion chamber can become loose, cracked, or disconnected.
- **Incorrect vent pipe installation (~10%)** Vent pipes installed with improper slope, wrong diameter, excessive length, or too many elbows can create airflow problems that the sensor detects.
- **Fan motor or venturi blockage (~7%)** The combustion fan may run slowly due to bearing wear, or the venturi throat may be clogged with scale or debris, reducing airflow.
- **Control board misreading pressure signal (~3%)** The main control board may interpret normal pressure sensor readings incorrectly due to internal fault or corrosion on terminals.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker or service switch and turn off the gas supply valve.
2. **Inspect the exterior vent termination** for blockages such as ice, snow, leaves, or animal nests. Clear any obstructions and verify the vent cap is intact.
3. **Check all accessible vent pipe joints** inside and outside the home to confirm they are properly sealed, supported, and sloped according to the installation manual.
4. **Remove the front cover** of the unit and locate the air pressure switch, a small round or rectangular component with two or three wires and a sensing tube attached.
5. **Inspect the pressure sensing tube** running from the switch to the combustion chamber or venturi. Look for cracks, kinks, disconnections, or water inside the tube. Reconnect or replace if damaged.
6. **Test the air pressure switch** if you have a multimeter. With power off, disconnect the wires and check continuity. Consult your model's service manual for the correct resistance or switching behavior.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then restart the unit. If the E305 error persists after clearing blockages and verifying connections, call a qualified technician to test the fan motor, venturi, or replace the pressure switch or control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you cannot locate or clear a vent blockage, if the pressure sensing tube and switch appear intact but the error persists, or if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances. Technicians have manometers and diagnostic tools to measure actual air pressure, test the fan motor under load, and safely replace the air pressure switch or control board. Because improper venting can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, any uncertainty about vent pipe installation or combustion airflow should be evaluated by a licensed plumber or HVAC contractor familiar with Navien systems.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
