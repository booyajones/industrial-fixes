## KitchenAid F4E3 Error Code — What It Means

The F4E3 error on KitchenAid dishwashers signals a wash motor (circulation motor) fault. The dishwasher has detected that the circulation motor is not running as expected during the wash cycle. In most cases the machine will fill with water normally but will not circulate water for washing. KitchenAid's official Product Help page groups F4E3 under dishwasher error codes and instructs users to cycle power to reset the control, while technician sources identify the fault more specifically as a circulation or wash motor problem.

## Common Causes

- **Control glitch after power disruption** The control board has latched a fault and needs a power reset to clear the error and resume normal operation.
- **Failed circulation motor** The wash motor itself has failed internally and cannot run even when commanded by the control board.
- **Debris or impeller blockage** Foreign objects or buildup in the pump or motor area prevent the motor from rotating freely.
- **Loose or damaged wiring** Connectors at the motor or control are loose, corroded, or damaged, interrupting the electrical supply to the motor.
- **Motor not receiving proper voltage** The motor is not being supplied with line voltage during a wash command due to a harness or control issue.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power for one minute.** Unplug the dishwasher or turn off the circuit breaker for a full minute, then restore power to reset the control board.
2. **Run a test cycle.** Start a normal wash cycle and observe whether the machine fills and whether you hear the circulation motor run.
3. **Remove and inspect the pump area.** Pull out the lower spray arm, remove the filter assembly, and check for debris, broken glass, or any obstruction around the impeller and pump housing.
4. **Check the motor wiring and connectors.** Locate the circulation motor under the tub, inspect the wiring harness for damage, and verify that all connectors are fully seated and free of corrosion.
5. **Test for motor feed voltage.** With the dishwasher in a wash cycle (and power on), use a multimeter to verify that 120V is present at the motor terminals when the control commands a wash.
6. **Replace the circulation motor assembly.** If voltage is present but the motor does not run, or if the motor is mechanically seized, replace the wash motor and pump assembly.
7. **Clear any stored faults and retest.** After repairs, cycle power again and run a full wash cycle to confirm the error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the error returns after a power reset and you are not comfortable working with 120V electrical components or disassembling the lower pump area. Technicians have the tools to safely test motor voltage under load, diagnose control board faults, and replace the circulation motor assembly without damaging surrounding plumbing or wiring. If you see any signs of water damage, burnt wiring, or if the dishwasher is still under warranty, professional service is the safer choice.
