## Gree H6 Error Code — What It Means

The H6 error code on a Gree mini-split indicates an indoor fan motor or blower motor fault. The indoor unit's control board is not receiving the expected feedback signal from the blower motor, meaning the board cannot verify that the fan is running at the correct speed or running at all. This feedback typically comes from a pulse signal sent by the motor's internal sensor back to the microcontroller on the indoor PCB.

## Common Causes

- **Failed indoor blower motor** The motor windings, internal electronics, or feedback sensor inside the blower motor assembly have failed and no longer send a valid signal to the board.
- **Loose or disconnected wiring** The motor power or feedback connector at the motor or indoor PCB is loose, corroded, has damaged pins, or has become unplugged during service or vibration.
- **Jammed or stuck blower fan** Dirt, dust, debris, or bearing wear has caused the fan wheel to bind or resist rotation, preventing the motor from spinning and generating feedback pulses.
- **Faulty indoor control board** The motor and wiring test good but the indoor PCB's feedback input circuit or motor drive section is damaged and cannot interpret or power the motor correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the unit** at the breaker or disconnect switch and wait 60 seconds before opening the indoor unit cover.
2. **Inspect the blower fan** for free rotation by hand and check for any visible debris, dust buildup, or objects obstructing the fan wheel or housing.
3. **Check all motor connectors** at the blower motor and at the indoor control board for looseness, corrosion, burn marks, or damaged pins, and reseat them firmly.
4. **Test the motor electrically** with a multimeter to verify continuity in the motor windings and inspect the feedback sensor wiring for breaks or shorts, following your model's service manual for exact resistance values.
5. **Replace the indoor blower motor assembly** if the motor does not spin freely, shows abnormal resistance, or fails to produce feedback pulses when powered.
6. **Inspect or replace the indoor PCB** if a known-good motor still triggers the H6 code, indicating a board-level fault in the motor drive or feedback circuit.
7. **Restore power and run the unit** in cooling or fan mode for at least 10 minutes to confirm normal airflow and verify the H6 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are uncomfortable working with line-voltage electrical connections, if you do not have a multimeter and the skills to test motor windings and feedback signals safely, or if you have replaced the motor and checked all wiring but the H6 code continues to appear. Control board diagnosis and refrigerant-side work require specialized tools and EPA certification, so professional service is the safest choice for persistent faults or if you are unsure of the root cause.
