## Navien E314 Error Code — What It Means

The E314 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates an ignition failure. The unit has attempted to light the burner but has not detected flame within the allowed time window. This prevents the heater from producing hot water and is a safety feature to avoid gas buildup.

The code typically appears when the control board sends power to the igniter and opens the gas valve, but the flame sensor does not confirm ignition. Common triggers include interrupted gas flow, a weak or failed igniter, incorrect gas pressure, a dirty flame rod, or air in the gas line after a service shutoff. The unit will lock out and require a reset or correction before resuming normal operation.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flame rod sensor first when the real issue is low gas pressure or a gas valve that needs adjustment. Check that the gas supply is fully open and pressure is stable before replacing sensors.

## Common Causes

- **Gas supply interruption or low pressure (~35%)** The heater cannot ignite if the gas valve is partially closed, pressure is too low, or air has entered the line after a shutoff.
- **Failed or weak igniter (~25%)** The igniter may not generate enough spark to light the gas, or it may have cracked or corroded over time.
- **Dirty or corroded flame rod sensor (~20%)** A flame rod covered in soot or mineral deposits cannot sense the flame properly, causing the board to think ignition failed.
- **Gas valve malfunction (~12%)** The gas valve may stick closed, open too slowly, or have internal wear that prevents proper gas delivery during ignition.
- **Venting or combustion air blockage (~8%)** Blocked intake or exhaust pipes can starve the burner of oxygen or prevent exhaust gases from escaping, disrupting ignition.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve.
2. **Wait 30 seconds**, then restore power and open the gas valve fully.
3. **Attempt a reset** by pressing the reset button on the front panel or cycling power off and on.
4. **Inspect the flame rod** by removing the front cover (if accessible) and visually checking for soot, corrosion, or loose wiring.
5. **Check intake and exhaust vents** outside the home for blockages such as bird nests, ice, or debris.
6. **Call a licensed technician** if the error persists after these checks, as gas pressure testing and igniter or gas valve replacement require specialized tools and training.
7. **Document the error** by noting how many times it has occurred and under what conditions (cold start, heavy demand) to help the technician diagnose intermittent faults.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician for E314 errors because diagnosis and repair involve working with natural gas or propane under pressure, testing gas valve operation, measuring combustion air, and handling high-voltage ignition components. Improper work on gas systems can create carbon monoxide hazards or fire risks. A qualified technician will use a manometer to verify gas pressure, test igniter spark strength, inspect the flame sensor for proper grounding, and check the burner assembly for blockages. Many jurisdictions require a licensed professional to perform any work on gas-fired appliances.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
