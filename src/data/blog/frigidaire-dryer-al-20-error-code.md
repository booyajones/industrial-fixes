## Frigidaire Dryer AL-20 Error Code — What It Means

AL-20 is not a published code in Frigidaire's standard dryer fault lists. If your display shows AL-20, confirm the exact model number and check whether the display might read 'dr' instead. The common Frigidaire dryer door fault code is 'dr', which means the control does not see the door switch proving closed. This happens when the door is not latched fully, the door switch has failed, or the main control board is not reading the switch signal.

Until you verify the model-specific manual, treat AL-20 as a potential display misread or a variant code unique to your platform. The diagnostic steps below address the most common door-prove and start-interlock faults documented for Frigidaire dryers, which cover nearly all no-start conditions tied to the door circuit.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully latched** The door did not close squarely or the striker did not engage the latch mechanism completely.
- **Failed door switch** The door switch contacts are worn, stuck open, or the switch plunger is broken and no longer actuates when the door closes.
- **Misaligned door striker or catch** The striker plate or door catch is bent or loose, preventing the latch from seating and actuating the switch.
- **Control board not reading switch input** The main electronic control board has a failed input circuit or solder joint, so it does not see continuity even when the switch closes.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** from the wall outlet or turn off the circuit breaker to kill all power before any inspection or testing.
2. **Verify door closure** by opening and closing the door firmly, listening for a solid click and checking that the latch engages the striker with no wobble or gap.
3. **Access the door switch** by removing the screws that hold the top panel or front trim (location varies by model, consult your manual) and locate the small micro-switch mounted near the door latch.
4. **Test the door switch** with an ohmmeter across the common and normally-open terminals while you manually press the plunger in and out. You should see continuity only when the plunger is depressed and no continuity when released.
5. **Inspect the door striker and catch** for bends, cracks, or missing hardware. Tighten or realign any loose screws and replace the striker if it is deformed.
6. **Check wiring and connectors** from the door switch to the control board for loose push-on terminals, frayed wires, or corrosion. Clean and reseat all connections.
7. **Replace the control board** if the door switch tests good, the door closes properly, and all wiring is intact but the fault persists. A failed input circuit on the board is the final link in the door-prove chain.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you cannot locate a door switch on your model, if the wiring diagram is not available, or if you are uncomfortable working with the control board and its connectors. Also call if replacing the door switch does not clear the fault and you need help tracing the circuit or confirming the control board diagnosis. If your display truly shows AL-20 and is not a misread, a technician with access to Frigidaire service-mode diagnostics and model-specific bulletins can decode the exact fault definition and save you time.
