## What this code means
EC:25 on a Speed Queen washer indicates a card reader malfunction. This code appears when the machine's payment card reader assembly cannot communicate properly with the control board. The fault is tied directly to the reader hardware itself. Speed Queen's published fault code listing states that the card reader may need to be replaced when this code appears.

## Common Causes

- **Failed card reader assembly** The reader itself has failed internally and cannot process card transactions or communicate status to the control.
- **Contaminated or corroded reader contacts** Dirt, moisture, or corrosion on the card slot or internal contacts prevents proper electrical connection.
- **Loose or damaged connector** The harness plug between the reader and control board has backed out or has bent pins.
- **Physical damage to the reader housing** Impact or rough handling has cracked the reader case or broken internal circuit traces.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the display by powering the washer off and back on, then confirm EC:25 reappears rather than a different code.
2. Unplug the machine and remove the service panel to access the card reader assembly mounted near the coin box or control console.
3. Inspect the card reader connector for bent pins, corrosion, or a loose fit, and reseat the plug firmly into both the reader and control board sides.
4. Check the card slot opening for debris, moisture, or visible damage to the housing, and clean gently with dry compressed air if contamination is present.
5. If the connector and housing appear intact but the code persists, replace the card reader assembly with a genuine Speed Queen reader for your model.
6. Reconnect power and run a test cycle to confirm EC:25 does not return and the reader responds correctly.
7. If the fault remains after reader replacement, inspect the wiring harness between the reader and control board for cuts or continuity faults.

## When to Call a Pro

EC:25 typically requires card reader replacement, which involves working behind live control panels and validating payment-system communication. If you are not comfortable removing control panels or testing low-voltage connectors, call a qualified Speed Queen service technician. Also bring in a pro if the fault returns after you have replaced the reader, since that points to a control board or harness issue that requires factory diagnostic tools and schematic tracing.
