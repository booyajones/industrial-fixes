## LG Dishwasher LE Error Code — What It Means

The LE code (or CE on some models) is a motor error code on LG dishwashers. LG's support documentation defines it as a motor error and instructs you to power down the unit and restart. In practice, the code signals a problem in the wash or circulation motor system, its wiring, or the control board's ability to detect normal motor operation.

The error typically appears when the wash motor fails to start, runs abnormally, or is mechanically blocked. It can also result from loose or corroded wiring between the control board and motor, excessive suds interfering with motor operation, or a control board misreading motor feedback.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board first, but the motor or a blocked pump is usually the real problem. Always inspect the sump area for debris and check motor wiring before ordering a board.

## Common Causes

- **Wash motor failure (~40%)** The circulation motor has failed internally, seized, or will not start during the wash phase.
- **Mechanical blockage in pump or impeller (~25%)** Debris, broken glass, utensils, or other objects jam the pump impeller or sump area and prevent normal motor rotation.
- **Loose or corroded motor wiring (~20%)** Harness connectors between the control board and motor are loose, corroded, heat-damaged, or making intermittent contact.
- **Excessive suds (~10%)** Too much detergent or the wrong soap type creates foam that interferes with normal circulation and motor operation.
- **Control board misread or failure (~5%)** The main control board incorrectly detects a motor error or fails to drive the motor even when the motor and wiring are good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the dishwasher** by unplugging it or switching off the circuit breaker for at least two minutes, then restore power and start a new cycle to see if the LE code clears.
2. **Verify the code repeats** by running a wash cycle and watching for the LE error to return quickly, which confirms a real motor-system fault rather than a one-time glitch.
3. **Remove the lower spray arm and inspect the sump area** for debris, broken glass, utensils, or any obstruction that could jam the pump impeller or add resistance to the motor.
4. **Clean the filter and sump screen** thoroughly, then check that the impeller or pump rotor spins freely by hand without binding or grinding.
5. **Inspect the motor wiring harness and connectors** between the control board and wash motor for loose pins, corrosion, heat damage, or poor contact, and reseat all connections firmly.
6. **Test or replace the wash motor assembly** if the sump is clear and wiring is good but the motor will not start or runs abnormally during the wash phase.
7. **Evaluate the main control board** only after confirming the motor, harness, and mechanical components are intact, as a board misread or driver failure can also trigger the LE code.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable disassembling the lower dishwasher cabinet to access the sump and motor, if you lack a multimeter to test motor continuity and wiring, or if the motor and harness appear good but the LE code persists and points to a control-board fault. Technicians have the diagnostic tools to measure motor drive signals and determine whether the pump assembly or the board is at fault. Also call a pro if your household water pressure is below 20 PSI, as low pressure can cause related errors and may require plumbing work beyond appliance repair.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-400.
