## LG Dishwasher LE Error Code — What It Means

The LE code is a locked motor error on the water circulation pump. The pump motor stopped sending its feedback signal, so the control board halts the cycle. It often traces to a jammed impeller, a wiring fault, or a worn-out circulation pump motor.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Jammed impeller** Glass shards or food fragments lock the wash impeller so the motor cannot spin.
- **Loose wiring harness** A loose or corroded connector between the pump motor and board is the most common trigger.
- **Seized motor rotor** A worn bearing or failed oil seal adds drag until the rotor will not turn freely.
- **Overheated motor** A motor that ran hot can drop out until it cools, then trip LE again under load.
- **Failed control board** A bad main board can misread the motor feedback and post LE even with a good pump.
- **Broken impeller blade** A cracked blade unbalances the pump and can stall it mid-cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Power the dishwasher off at the breaker for 30 to 45 minutes to let the motor cool
2. Start a fresh cycle and see if LE clears after the cooldown
3. If it returns, remove the lower spray arm and filter to reach the pump area
4. Clear any glass or debris locking the wash impeller and spin it by hand to confirm it is free
5. Inspect and reseat the pump motor wiring harness, looking for burnt or corroded pins
6. Replace the circulation pump and motor assembly if the impeller is free and wiring is good
7. Run a cycle and verify the motor runs and LE stays clear

## When to Call a Pro

If the impeller spins freely and the wiring is solid but LE keeps returning, the pump motor or the control board has likely failed. A technician can read the motor winding resistance and confirm which part to replace before you order.
