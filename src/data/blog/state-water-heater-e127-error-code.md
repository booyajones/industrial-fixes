## State Water Heater E127 Error Code — What It Means

State water heaters are manufactured by A. O. Smith, and error codes on these units typically point to ignition problems, flame loss, venting restrictions, or flow-related faults. The specific meaning of E127 is not documented in widely available State or A. O. Smith technical literature, so the exact fault condition tied to this code depends on your model and controller generation. In general, three-digit error codes on State tankless and atmospheric models flag issues with combustion air supply, gas delivery, exhaust venting, or scale buildup that prevents proper burner operation.

Because E127 does not appear in standard fault-code lists, you should cross-reference the code against the diagnostic chart printed inside your unit's access panel or in the installation manual. If no chart is available, treat the fault as a general combustion or flow alarm and work through the most common failure points in sequence.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screen** Lint, dust, or debris restricts combustion air and triggers a fault when the burner cannot sustain ignition.
- **Insufficient gas supply or pressure** Low inlet pressure, a closed manual valve, or regulator failure prevents the burner from lighting or holding a flame.
- **Restricted exhaust vent** Blockage in the vent pipe or termination cap traps combustion products and causes a safety lockout.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger** Hard-water deposits reduce flow and raise outlet temperature beyond safe limits, prompting a sensor-driven shutdown.
- **Low water flow or clogged inlet filter** Insufficient gallons per minute through the unit prevents ignition or causes intermittent flame loss.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas shutoff valve before inspecting the unit.
2. **Remove and clean the air intake screen** at the front of the heater using a soft brush or vacuum to clear lint and dust.
3. **Check the inlet water filter** by shutting the cold-water isolation valve, removing the filter housing, and rinsing the screen under tap water.
4. **Inspect the exhaust vent termination** outside for nests, leaves, or ice dams, and verify that the vent pipe joints inside are tight and free of soot.
5. **Verify gas supply** by confirming that the manual valve is fully open and that other gas appliances in the home are operating normally.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit by pressing and holding the reset button on the control board for three seconds or cycling the power breaker.
7. **Observe the ignition sequence** through the sight glass; if the burner fails to light or the error returns immediately, consult your model's diagnostic chart or call a licensed plumber.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the E127 error persists after cleaning the air intake and inlet filter, if you smell gas, or if the unit fails to ignite at all. A professional will measure gas inlet pressure, test the flame sensor and igniter, inspect the heat exchanger for scale, and verify vent draft with a manometer. Because E127 is not a standard published code, the technician may also need to contact State technical support or consult factory service bulletins to decode the fault for your specific controller revision.
