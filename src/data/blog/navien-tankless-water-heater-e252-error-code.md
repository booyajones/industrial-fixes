## Navien E252 Error Code — What It Means

The E252 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically indicates a problem with the water pressure or flow sensing system. The unit's control board is not receiving the expected signal from the pressure sensor, which monitors incoming water flow and pressure. This can prevent the heater from firing or cause it to shut down as a safety measure.

The exact meaning can vary slightly across different Navien models, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit's cover for model-specific details. In most cases the code points to either a physical restriction in the water supply, a sensor that has failed or become disconnected, or a wiring fault between the sensor and the control board.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the pressure sensor itself when the real problem is a clogged inlet filter or a loose wire connector. Clean the filter and check all sensor plug connections before ordering a new sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged inlet filter (~40%)** Sediment and debris in the cold water supply accumulate on the mesh screen and reduce flow, preventing the sensor from reading correct pressure.
- **Loose or corroded sensor wiring (~25%)** The pressure sensor connector may work loose over time or develop corrosion that interrupts the signal to the control board.
- **Failed pressure sensor (~20%)** The sensor itself can fail due to age, mineral buildup on the diaphragm, or electrical component wear inside the sensor body.
- **Low incoming water pressure (~10%)** If supply pressure drops below the minimum required by the heater, the sensor will register a fault even when working correctly.
- **Control board fault (~5%)** A problem on the main control board may prevent it from interpreting the sensor signal correctly, though this is less common.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the cold water isolation valve.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the retaining screws to access the internal components and the inlet filter.
3. **Locate the inlet filter** on the cold water supply line entering the unit and unscrew the filter housing or remove the screen.
4. **Clean the filter** under running water with a soft brush to remove sediment and debris, or replace it if damaged.
5. **Inspect the pressure sensor** near the inlet manifold and check that the wire connector is firmly seated and free of corrosion or moisture.
6. **Reinstall the filter**, open the cold water valve slowly to purge air, and check for leaks at the filter housing.
7. **Restore power** at the breaker, open a hot tap to call for heat, and verify the error clears and the unit fires normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien technician if you are uncomfortable working with gas connections or if the error persists after cleaning the filter and checking sensor wiring. A professional can test the sensor output with a multimeter, check gas valve operation, and replace the control board if needed. Also call a pro if you discover active water leaks, smell gas, or if the unit displays multiple error codes at once, which may indicate a more complex system fault.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $180-350.
