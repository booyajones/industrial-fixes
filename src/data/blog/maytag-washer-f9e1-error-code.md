## Maytag Washer F9E1 Error Code — What It Means

F9E1 is a long-drain fault. The washer could not pump the water out within the time the control allows, usually around eight minutes, so it halts the cycle and leaves water in the drum.

This points to a blockage in the drain path or a weak or dead drain pump. Front-load models throw this code most often.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Clogged pump filter** Lint, coins, and small items collect in the drain pump filter and block flow.
- **Kinked or clogged drain hose** A pinched hose or a sock stuck inside it stops the water from leaving.
- **Drain hose pushed too far down the standpipe** A hose inserted deeper than about 4.5 inches can siphon and trip a drain fault.
- **Failed drain pump** A pump with a seized impeller or burned winding will hum or stay silent and move no water.
- **Object jammed in the pump** Debris caught in the impeller stops the pump from turning freely.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut the power. Unplug the washer before opening any panel since the drum still holds water.
2. Open the pump filter. On front-load models, open the lower front access door, set a shallow pan and towel under it, and twist the filter open to drain and clear it.
3. Check the drain hose. Pull the hose and look for kinks, crimps, or trapped items, and confirm it does not sit too far down the standpipe.
4. Inspect the pump inlet. Shine a light into the pump housing and remove any lint, coins, or fabric caught at the impeller.
5. Run a drain and spin. Plug back in and start a drain-and-spin to see if water now leaves the tub.
6. Listen to the pump. A loud hum with no draining points to a seized or failed pump that needs replacing.
7. Replace the drain pump. Match the pump to your model number, reconnect the hoses and harness, and run a test cycle to confirm a clean drain.

## When to Call a Pro

If the filter, hose, and pump are all clear and a new pump does not fix it, the fault can sit in the pressure sensor or the main control board. Those are good cases to hand off to an appliance technician.
