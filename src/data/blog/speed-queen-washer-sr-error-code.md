## Speed Queen SR Error Code — What It Means

On most Speed Queen front-load washers, "Sr" on the display is not an error code at all. It stands for cycle time remaining and shows how much longer the wash will run. If a technician has diagnosed an "SR error" or the machine is behaving abnormally while showing SR, third-party repair sources describe this as a stuck relay condition. This means a relay on the control board is not releasing properly, causing the washer to fail mid-cycle or not advance through the program.

Because Speed Queen's own documentation does not list SR as a fault code for most residential front-load models, always verify your exact model number and whether the display is actually indicating an error (such as flashing, beeping, or machine shutdown) rather than normal cycle time. If the washer is operating normally and SR appears, no repair is needed. If the machine is stopped and unresponsive, a control board relay or related wiring fault is the most common cause reported by field technicians.

## Common Causes

- **Control board relay failure** A relay on the main control board has welded contacts or internal failure, preventing it from opening or closing on command.
- **Wiring harness or connector damage** Loose pins, corrosion, or heat damage in the harness between the control board and load circuits can mimic a stuck relay condition.
- **Power surge or electrical event** A voltage spike or brownout can damage control board electronics and cause relay circuits to malfunction.
- **Main control board defect** The entire control board may have a manufacturing defect or component failure unrelated to the relay itself.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the display meaning** by checking your model's user manual to confirm whether SR is cycle time remaining or an actual fault code for your specific washer.
2. **Power-cycle the washer** by unplugging it for 60 seconds, then plug back in and attempt a new cycle to see if the condition clears.
3. **Inspect the control board and wiring harness** for visible signs of heat damage, burnt relays, loose connectors, or corrosion on the pins between the control board and load circuits.
4. **Check all connectors** to the main control board by unplugging and re-seating each one firmly, looking for bent or discolored pins.
5. **Test the machine in diagnostic mode** if your model supports it (consult your service manual for the key sequence) to isolate whether the fault is in the control board or a downstream load.
6. **Replace the main control board** if the fault recurs after power cycling and all wiring checks are clean, as stuck relay faults typically require board-level replacement.
7. **Document the exact behavior** and any beep patterns or flashing lights that accompany the SR display, then consult Speed Queen service literature for your model family before ordering parts.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical components or if the washer shows SR alongside other symptoms like burning smells, tripped breakers, or repeated fault codes. Control board diagnosis often requires a multimeter and familiarity with relay circuits. If your washer is under warranty or you cannot locate the exact control board part number for your model, professional service will save time and avoid incorrect part orders. A qualified appliance repair tech can also run factory diagnostics to confirm whether SR is truly a fault or a misunderstood status display.
