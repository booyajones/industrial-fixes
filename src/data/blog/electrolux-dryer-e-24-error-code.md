## Electrolux Dryer E24 Error Code — What It Means

The E24 error code on Electrolux dryers indicates a fault in the temperature sensing or heating circuit. The control board has detected that the dryer's temperature is outside normal operating parameters or that the thermistor (temperature sensor) is not reading correctly. This code will prevent the dryer from running a normal cycle because the machine cannot regulate heat safely.

The exact meaning can vary slightly between Electrolux models, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the back of the machine for model-specific information. In most cases, the issue relates to the thermistor, its wiring, or occasionally the control board itself.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board first, but a simple resistance check of the thermistor with a multimeter will identify the real culprit in most cases.

## Common Causes

- **Thermistor failure (~50%)** The temperature sensor has drifted out of range or failed open or short, sending incorrect readings to the control board.
- **Loose or corroded thermistor connector (~20%)** The plug connecting the thermistor to the wiring harness has become loose, corroded, or has a poor connection.
- **Damaged wiring harness (~15%)** The wires running from the thermistor to the control board are pinched, broken, or shorted.
- **Failed control board (~10%)** The main electronic control board has a faulty temperature-sensing circuit or relay.
- **Heating element issue (~5%)** A fault in the heating element circuit can trigger temperature-related error codes on some models.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** and pull it away from the wall to access the rear panel or open the cabinet.
2. **Locate the thermistor** on the blower housing or heating duct (consult your model's service diagram for exact location).
3. **Disconnect the thermistor wire connector** and inspect the terminals for corrosion or damage.
4. **Set a multimeter to resistance mode** and measure across the thermistor terminals at room temperature. Compare the reading to your model's specification table.
5. **If the reading is out of range or infinite**, replace the thermistor. If the reading is correct, inspect the wiring harness for breaks or shorts.
6. **Reconnect or replace parts** as needed, reassemble the dryer, and run a test cycle to confirm the error has cleared.
7. **If the error persists** after replacing the thermistor and verifying wiring, consider replacing the electronic control board or call a technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components or using a multimeter, or if you have replaced the thermistor and verified all wiring but the error persists. A technician can perform advanced diagnostics on the control board and heating circuit. Also call if the dryer is still under warranty, as DIY repairs may void coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $25-60 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-250.
