## Whirlpool Washer F20 Error Code — What It Means

F20, also shown as FH on some models, is a no fill or low fill error on Whirlpool front load washers. The control does not sense water entering within the expected time and stops the cycle.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Supply valves closed** Closed or partly open hot and cold valves keep water from reaching the washer.
- **Kinked or frozen hoses** A pinched or frozen fill hose blocks the water supply.
- **Clogged inlet screens** Sediment and mineral buildup on the valve screens restrict incoming water.
- **Defective water inlet valve** A valve that will not open electrically leaves the tub empty.
- **Pressure switch fault** A bad pressure switch or hose can falsely report no water.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Check that both supply valves are fully open and both fill hoses are connected.
2. Inspect the hoses for kinks, and in cold weather confirm none are frozen.
3. Turn off the water, remove the hoses, and clean the inlet screens with a soft brush.
4. Run water through each hose at a sink and replace any that flow poorly.
5. Reconnect, restore power, and start a cycle to watch for proper fill.
6. Test the water inlet valve coils for continuity if fill is still weak.
7. Replace the water inlet valve if it tests bad or stays shut under power.

## When to Call a Pro

If supply, hoses, and screens check out and a new inlet valve does not clear F20, the pressure switch or control board may be at fault. Call a technician if you are not set up to test electrical parts.
