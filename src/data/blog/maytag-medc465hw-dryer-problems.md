## Maytag MEDC465HW Dryer Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Maytag MEDC465HW is an electric dryer in Maytag's front-load lineup. Most service calls on this model trace back to airflow restrictions triggering the AF or F4E3 error code, followed by L2 voltage faults when the 240 V supply is incomplete. The control board displays F# E# codes for internal faults that require diagnostic mode access to identify the exact stored code. Power failures show as PF and typically clear on restart.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **AF or F4E3 restricted airflow error** Clogged lint screen, blocked exhaust vent, crushed or kinked vent hose, or an exterior flap stuck closed prevents proper airflow and the control throws AF or F4E3.
- **L2 low or no line voltage** One leg of the 240 V supply is missing due to a tripped breaker, blown fuse, loose power cord connection at the terminal block, or incorrect wiring, so the heater will not turn on.
- **PF power failure code** An outage, unplugged cord, or breaker trip interrupts the cycle and the control displays PF until you clear it or restart the dryer.
- **F# E# service codes** Any code starting with F and alternating with E means the control detected a fault that requires entering diagnostic mode to read the stored code pair and troubleshoot the specific component.
- **Dryer runs but no heat** L2 voltage fault, a failed heating element, a tripped thermal fuse on the blower housing, or a defective high-limit thermostat will prevent heat even if the drum turns.
- **Long dry times** Restricted airflow from a lint-packed screen or vent, a weak blower motor, or a heating element with open sections reduces performance before the AF code appears.
- **Will not start or respond** No 240 V supply, a faulty door switch that does not signal the control, or a failed main control board prevents the dryer from starting any cycle.
- **Drum does not turn** A stretched or broken drum belt, a seized idler pulley, or a failed drive motor keeps the drum from rotating even when the control and motor are energized.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician for any F# E# code after you record the exact pair in diagnostic mode, for persistent L2 voltage errors after verifying your home breaker and power cord connections, or for AF/F4E3 codes that return after you have cleaned the lint screen and confirmed the entire exhaust vent is clear. If the dryer will not start, has no heat after checking the breaker, or the drum does not turn, a pro can test the door switch, heating element, thermal fuses, drive motor, and control board with a meter and access the full service manual for this model.
