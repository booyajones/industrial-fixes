## Kenmore Range F033 Error Code — What It Means

The F033 error on your Kenmore range indicates a meat probe temperature fault. The control board is detecting an open circuit, short, or invalid signal from the meat probe input. This code is specifically tied to the probe you insert into meat during cooking, not the oven's main temperature sensor. A partially inserted probe jack is the most common trigger, because it can short the receptacle contacts and trip the fault.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Meat probe not fully inserted** A partially seated probe jack shorts the receptacle contacts and triggers the fault immediately.
- **Damaged meat probe or plug** Cuts, bent pins, or internal wire breaks in the probe assembly create an open or short circuit.
- **Faulty probe receptacle or jack** Contamination, corrosion, or bent contacts in the wall jack prevent proper electrical connection.
- **Wiring harness issue** Damaged wires or loose connections between the receptacle and the control board interrupt the probe circuit.
- **Control board input fault** If the probe and wiring test good, the electronic control board's input circuit may have failed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** to the range at the breaker or unplug the unit before any inspection or testing.
2. **Check the meat probe jack** for full insertion into the receptacle, then remove and reseat it firmly to see if the code clears.
3. **Inspect the probe plug and receptacle** visually for bent pins, contamination, corrosion, or damage that would prevent solid contact.
4. **Examine the probe cable and harness** for cuts, crushed insulation, or heat damage along its length, and verify all connectors are secure.
5. **Test the meat probe resistance** with a multimeter if you have the spec sheet for your model, or consult your service manual for the correct room-temperature ohm value.
6. **Check continuity of the wiring** from the probe receptacle back to the control board connector to rule out a harness break.
7. **Replace the control board** if the probe, receptacle, and wiring all test good and the fault persists after reconnecting power.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical testing or removing panels to access the control board. If you have replaced the probe and verified the receptacle is clean and undamaged, but the F033 code returns immediately on power-up, the fault is likely in the control board or internal harness. A qualified appliance technician can perform board-level diagnostics and confirm the correct replacement part for your specific Kenmore model number.
