## Daikin Mini Split A8 Error Code — What It Means

On Daikin mini splits, the A8 code signals a malfunction of the power supply or AC input overcurrent. The system has detected either unstable incoming voltage or excessive current draw, usually in the fan motor circuit or the control board's power section. Daikin's fault tree treats this as a protection event that shuts down operation to prevent component damage.

Because power-quality faults can stem from utility brownouts, internal board failures, or a failing motor, the repair always begins with confirming clean, stable voltage at the unit. Only after supply voltage is verified do you move to connectors, the fan motor itself, and finally the control board.

## Common Causes

- **Low or unstable supply voltage** Brownout, incorrect wiring, or undersized branch circuit prevents the unit from drawing clean power under load.
- **Failing fan motor** A motor with shorted windings or seized bearings draws excessive current and triggers the overcurrent protection.
- **Loose or corroded connectors** Oxidized terminals or partially seated plugs in the fan motor power path create intermittent faults or high resistance.
- **Defective power supply section or PCB** A fault in the board's inverter stage or power section prevents proper regulation even when incoming voltage is correct.
- **Shorted wiring or harness damage** Chafed insulation or pinched wires in the fan motor harness short to ground and pull excessive current.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live line voltage, if your multimeter readings do not match expected values and you cannot interpret the fault tree, or if the A8 code returns immediately after you replace the motor or board. Power-supply and overcurrent faults can damage expensive inverter components when misdiagnosed, so professional instruments and experience with Daikin's service software will save both time and parts. Also confirm that your specific mini-split model uses the A8 definition above, because Daikin publishes different code sets across product families and regions.
