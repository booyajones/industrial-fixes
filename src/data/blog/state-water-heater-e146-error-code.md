## State Water Heater E146 Error Code — What It Means

The E146 error code does not appear in State Water Heater's published troubleshooting resources or official fault code lists. State and its parent company A. O. Smith design their control systems to be model-specific, meaning the same code can reference different faults depending on the exact water heater series and control board. Without the manufacturer manual for your specific model, it is not safe to assume what E146 indicates.

To identify what E146 means on your unit, you need to locate the rating plate on the water heater, scan the QR code (if equipped), or look up the model number in the installation and operation manual that shipped with the unit. State directs all fault code lookups to these model-specific documents rather than publishing a universal code chart.

## Common Causes

- **Model-specific fault definition** E146 may refer to a sensor fault, flow issue, temperature limit, or communication error depending on the control board version installed in your model.
- **Incorrect manual lookup** Using a generic fault list or another model's guide will lead to misdiagnosis because State tankless and tank-type heaters use different code sets.
- **Intermittent wiring or connector issue** A loose connector or corroded wire at the control board can trigger fault codes that do not appear in older published lists.
- **Outdated firmware or control board revision** Some codes appear only on newer board revisions and are not documented in earlier printed manuals.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Locate the rating plate** on the side or front of the water heater and write down the complete model number and serial number.
2. **Scan the QR code** on the rating plate with your smartphone, or visit the State Water Heaters website and enter your model number to download the correct manual.
3. **Open the troubleshooting section** of your model-specific manual and look up E146 in the fault code table to see the exact definition and diagnostic steps.
4. **Power off the water heater** at the circuit breaker or gas valve, wait 30 seconds, then restore power to see if the code clears after a reboot.
5. **Inspect all wire harness connections** at the control board and any sensors listed in the manual's E146 entry for corrosion, moisture, or looseness.
6. **Follow the manufacturer's diagnostic flowchart** for E146 exactly as written, checking any specified resistance, voltage, or pressure values with a multimeter or manometer.
7. **Record the results** of each test and contact State technical support or a licensed technician if the manual's steps do not resolve the fault.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or water heater technician if you cannot locate your model manual, if the manual's diagnostic steps require gas pressure or electrical voltage tests you are not trained to perform safely, or if the fault returns immediately after following all published troubleshooting steps. Technicians have access to State's service hotline and updated technical bulletins that may cover recent code changes not yet in printed manuals. If your water heater is still under warranty, professional diagnosis is often required to preserve coverage.
