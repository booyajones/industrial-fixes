## LG Refrigerator EIU Error Code — What It Means

The EIU code on an LG refrigerator indicates a freezer compartment ice maker assembly failure. LG classifies this as an inspection code rather than a simple user alarm. The code appears on the display when you press and hold ICE PLUS and FREEZER for 2 seconds. According to LG Support, the code signals that the ice maker assembly is not operating correctly, usually because of a hardware defect in the ice maker itself or because ice and frost buildup is interfering with its operation.

LG does not publish a detailed field repair procedure for EIU. Instead, the manufacturer directs technicians to attempt a basic power reset and defrost cycle. If the code returns after these steps, LG recommends sending the unit to authorized service for inspection. The lack of published component-level diagnostics means most homeowners will need professional help if the reset and defrost do not clear the fault.

## Before You Replace Anything

Some technicians replace the entire ice maker assembly without first performing a full defrost cycle. LG explicitly recommends leaving the doors open for at least 8 hours to clear ice buildup before concluding the assembly has failed.

## Common Causes

- **Ice or frost buildup around the ice maker (~45%)** Frost can block the ice maker mechanism or sensors, and LG tells technicians to defrost the unit for at least 8 hours with doors open when EIU appears.
- **Failed ice maker assembly (~35%)** The ice maker assembly itself can fail due to internal motor, gear, or sensor faults, which LG defines as the root meaning of the EIU code.
- **Low water pressure or restricted supply line (~10%)** Inadequate water pressure prevents the ice maker from filling correctly, and LG support notes that low pressure should be corrected when this code is present.
- **Loose or corroded ice maker wiring harness (~7%)** Connector corrosion or a loose plug between the ice maker and the main control can trigger an assembly fault code.
- **Main control board communication fault (~3%)** Rarely, a glitch in the main board can misreport an ice maker failure even when the assembly is fine.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the refrigerator** from the wall outlet or turn off the circuit breaker, wait 2 minutes, then restore power to reset the main control board.
2. **Press and hold ICE PLUS and FREEZER** for 2 seconds to check if the EIU code still displays after the reset.
3. **Open both the refrigerator and freezer doors** and leave them open for at least 8 hours to allow complete defrost of any ice buildup around the ice maker assembly and evaporator.
4. **Inspect the ice maker compartment** for visible frost, ice dams, or blockages around the ice maker unit and the ice chute.
5. **Check the household water supply** by turning off the water valve, disconnecting the supply line at the valve, and observing flow when you turn the valve back on (flow should be strong and steady).
6. **Examine the ice maker wiring harness** at the back of the ice maker for loose connectors, corrosion, or damaged pins, and reseat any connectors you find.
7. **If the code persists** after reset, defrost, and supply checks, remove the ice maker assembly (consult your model's service manual for the release clips or screws) and test it for continuity on the motor and heater terminals, or contact LG service for a professional inspection.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the EIU code returns after you have completed a full power reset, an 8-hour defrost with doors open, and verified adequate water pressure. LG treats EIU as an inspection code and does not publish detailed component-level diagnostics for homeowners. A qualified technician will have access to LG service manuals, can test the ice maker assembly with specialized tools, and can order the correct OEM replacement part for your specific model. Professional help is also recommended if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors inside the freezer compartment or if your refrigerator is still under warranty, since DIY repairs may void coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $80-180 for ice maker assembly if replacement needed, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300 for service call and diagnosis.
