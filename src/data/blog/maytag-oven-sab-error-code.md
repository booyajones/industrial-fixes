## Maytag Oven SAB Error Code — What It Means

SAB, Sab, 5AB, or 5A6 on a Maytag oven or range means Sabbath Mode is activated. This is not a fault code. In Sabbath Mode, only the Bake cycle operates, all other cooking and cleaning cycles are disabled, tones do not sound, and the display will not show temperature changes. Opening or closing the door or operating the light switch will not immediately change the oven light or heating elements. The feature is designed to comply with religious observances and restricts normal controls intentionally.

## Before You Replace Anything

Some owners assume the control board or keypad has failed and replace it unnecessarily. Check your owner's manual for the Sabbath Mode exit sequence and try a power reset before ordering any parts.

## Common Causes

- **Sabbath Mode accidentally enabled (~85%)** A button combination was pressed during cleaning or cooking, activating the feature unintentionally.
- **User unfamiliar with the feature (~10%)** The mode was turned on by another household member or during a power outage, and the current user does not recognize the code.
- **Control board or keypad stuck in Sabbath state (~4%)** After a power surge or firmware glitch, the control may not respond to the normal exit sequence.
- **Incorrect exit procedure for the specific model (~1%)** Different Maytag oven models use different key sequences to exit Sabbath Mode, and the wrong procedure was attempted.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the display code** by checking that it reads SAB, Sab, 5AB, or 5A6 and that the oven otherwise appears to function in restricted mode (only Bake cycle available, no tones, no temperature display changes).
2. **Press and hold the Clock pad** for approximately 3 seconds to exit Sabbath Mode on most Maytag ovens. Some models may require a different button or combination, so refer to your owner's manual if this does not work.
3. **Check the display** to confirm the code has cleared and that normal operation has resumed (all cycles available, tones audible, temperature changes visible).
4. **If the code persists**, turn off power to the oven by switching off the circuit breaker or removing the fuse for one minute, then restore power and check the display again.
5. **Locate the exact exit procedure** for your model number in the owner's manual or on the Maytag product help website if the standard Clock-pad method and power reset do not clear Sabbath Mode.
6. **Test normal operation** by selecting a cooking cycle other than Bake (such as Broil or Convection) to verify that all functions are now enabled and the display responds as expected.
7. **If Sabbath Mode cannot be exited** after confirming the correct procedure and performing a power reset, suspect a control keypad or electronic control board issue and consult a technician or the service literature for further diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you have tried the correct Sabbath Mode exit sequence for your model, performed a power reset, and the code still will not clear. Also call if the control keypad does not respond to button presses or if you are uncertain which key combination applies to your specific Maytag oven model. A technician can verify the control board and keypad operation, check for firmware issues, and confirm that no other underlying fault is preventing the mode from disabling. For most cases, however, Sabbath Mode is simply a setting that can be turned off at home in under two minutes with no parts or service call required.
