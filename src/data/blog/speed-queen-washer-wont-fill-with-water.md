## Speed Queen Washer Won't Fill With Water — What's Happening

A Speed Queen washer that won't fill with water is a symptom, not a fault code. The machine is being told to start a wash cycle, but water is not entering the tub because something in the supply, inlet, control, or level-sensing logic is interrupting the fill step. Speed Queen troubleshooting guidance for "washer not filling properly" points first to water supply issues, inlet valve screens and valve condition, lid switch or lid lock status, water-level controls, and then the electronic control if power is not reaching the valve during fill.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Closed or restricted water supply** Hot or cold supply valves are turned off or household water pressure is too low for the inlet valve to operate correctly.
- **Kinked or crushed inlet hoses** Inlet hoses are pinched behind the washer or have loose, leaking connections that restrict flow.
- **Clogged inlet screens** Sediment has blocked the small mesh screens at the water inlet valve hose connection ports, preventing water from reaching the valve.
- **Defective water inlet valve** The valve receives power during the fill step but does not open properly or has a failed solenoid coil.
- **Defective lid switch or lid lock assembly** On models that interlock the cycle, a failed lid switch or lock prevents the washer from advancing into fill.
- **Control board not sending voltage to the valve** The electronic control is not outputting power to the inlet valve during the fill command.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify both hot and cold supply valves are fully open and that household water pressure is adequate.
2. Inspect inlet hoses for kinks, crushing, and secure attachment, and correct any hose issues before deeper testing.
3. Unplug the washer, shut off both water supply valves, remove the inlet hoses, and clean or replace the inlet screens at the valve ports.
4. Reconnect the hoses, restore power and water, and run a test cycle to see if fill resumes.
5. If fill is still slow or absent, test the lid switch or lid lock assembly for continuity using a multimeter and replace if it fails.
6. During an active fill command, check for incoming voltage at the water inlet valve terminals using the machine wiring diagram for your model.
7. If correct voltage is present at the valve but water still does not enter, replace the water inlet valve.
8. If no voltage is present at the valve during fill, inspect wiring for damage and replace the control board if it is not outputting power.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you are not comfortable working with live voltage testing during diagnostic cycles, if you lack the wiring diagram for your exact model, or if you have replaced the inlet valve and lid switch but the washer still does not fill. A qualified appliance tech can trace control board output, verify water-level switch and air-dome tube operation on older models, and safely diagnose electrical faults that prevent fill voltage from reaching the valve.
