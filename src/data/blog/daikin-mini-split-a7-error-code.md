## Daikin A7 Error Code — What It Means

The A7 error code on a Daikin mini split indicates a malfunction of the swing flap motor. This is the motor that controls the horizontal or vertical louvers (air discharge flaps) on your indoor unit. The indoor unit has detected that the motor is not responding correctly or that the flap mechanism itself is stuck, jammed, or unable to move through its normal range. This is not a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units. Daikin's self-diagnosis chart specifically identifies A7 as a swing flap motor or air outlet mechanism problem.

## Common Causes

- **Jammed or stuck louver assembly** Foreign objects, dust buildup, or broken linkage can physically block the flap from moving, triggering the fault even if the motor itself is good.
- **Defective swing flap motor** The motor that drives the louver has failed internally or its windings are open, so it cannot respond to control signals from the indoor board.
- **Stripped gears or broken flap linkage** Plastic gears or connecting rods in the louver mechanism can crack or strip over time, preventing the motor from moving the flap even though the motor runs.
- **Loose or corroded motor connector** The wiring harness plug at the swing motor can work loose or develop corrosion, cutting power or signal to the motor.
- **Indoor PCB drive circuit fault** If the motor and mechanism both test good, the indoor control board may have a failed driver or relay that supplies power to the swing motor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the disconnect or breaker and remove the front cover of the indoor unit to expose the air discharge flap and motor assembly.
2. **Inspect the louver mechanism** for any visible obstructions, broken plastic parts, or binding in the linkage, and manually move the flap through its full range to verify it is free.
3. **Clear any obstruction or debris** from the flap path, then restore power and use the remote to command a swing operation and observe whether the motor runs and the flap moves smoothly.
4. **Check the swing motor connector** on the indoor unit for loose pins, corrosion, or damaged wiring, and reseat the plug firmly onto the motor terminals.
5. **Replace the swing flap motor** if the mechanism is free but the motor does not run or makes abnormal noise when commanded, following your model's disassembly procedure to access the motor mount.
6. **Test the indoor PCB** by measuring for voltage at the motor connector during a swing command (consult your model's service manual for exact voltage), and replace the board only if the motor and wiring are proven good but no drive signal is present.
7. **Cycle power and clear the fault** after repair, then run a full swing cycle to verify normal operation and confirm the A7 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working inside the indoor unit or if you cannot locate the swing motor. If the fault returns after clearing obstructions and reseating connectors, a technician with the model-specific service manual can perform voltage and resistance tests on the motor and board to isolate the defective component. Refrigerant work is not required for A7, but accessing the motor often requires partial disassembly of the indoor unit and some models have fragile plastic parts that are easy to damage without proper experience.
