## Heatcraft Walk-In Cooler Error Codes — What They Mean

Heatcraft Refrigeration Products (brands: Bohn, Larkin, Climate Control, Inter-City) makes evaporator coils and unit coolers for walk-in coolers and freezers across food retail and foodservice. Heatcraft unit coolers with electronic controls (EC controllers) display alarm codes when faults are detected.

| Alarm | Meaning | Urgency |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | High temperature alarm | High |
| 2 | High discharge temperature | High |
| 3 | Low suction pressure | Medium |
| 4 | High suction pressure | Medium |
| 5 | Defrost termination fault | Medium |
| 6 | Evaporator fan motor fault | High |
| 7 | Temperature sensor fault | Medium |

## Most Common Heatcraft Walk-In Alarms and Fixes {#fix}

### Alarm 1 — High Temperature
Box temperature exceeded setpoint. Immediate action required if product is at risk. Check: evaporator fan motors running, coil not iced over, condensing unit operating, door seals intact.

### Alarm 2 — High Discharge Temperature
Discharge line temperature too high. Most common causes: dirty condenser on condensing unit, condenser fan failure, refrigerant overcharge, restricted liquid line.

### Alarm 3 — Low Suction Pressure
Low refrigerant charge is most common. Also check: dirty evaporator coil (restricted airflow), TXV malfunction (sticking closed), or liquid line filter-drier restriction.

### Alarm 5 — Defrost Termination Fault
Defrost failed to terminate properly. Check defrost termination thermostat and heater continuity. On air defrost systems, check fan operation and ambient conditions.

### Alarm 6 — Fan Motor Fault
Evaporator fan motor failure or overload. Check motor for seized bearings and correct rotation direction.

### Alarm 7 — Temperature Sensor Fault
Air temperature sensor open or shorted. Test sensor resistance and replace if out of spec.

## When to Call a Pro

Alarms 3 and 4 (pressure faults) indicate refrigerant circuit issues requiring an EPA 608-certified technician. All refrigerant work requires proper recovery and certification.
