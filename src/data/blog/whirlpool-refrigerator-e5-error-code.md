## Whirlpool Refrigerator E5 Error Code — What It Means

E5 signals an ice maker fault, usually a water fill problem or a bad temperature sensor inside the ice maker. The control cannot complete a normal ice cycle.

The most common symptom is no ice or very little ice while the rest of the fridge cools fine.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Faulty water inlet valve** A failed valve does not open to fill the ice mold even with good water pressure.
- **Ice maker thermistor fault** The ice maker temperature sensor reports an invalid value and stalls the cycle.
- **Clogged or frozen fill tube** A blocked or iced fill line stops water from reaching the mold.
- **Clogged water filter** An old filter drops flow below what the ice maker needs to fill.
- **Low water pressure** Supply pressure under about 20 psi keeps the valve from filling properly.
- **Failed ice maker assembly** Internal electronics in the ice maker have failed and need the whole unit replaced.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator for five minutes, then restore power to reset the ice maker.
2. Confirm the water supply valve is open and the line is not kinked.
3. Replace the water filter if it is older than six months or flow is weak.
4. Check the fill tube for ice and thaw it gently if frozen.
5. Test the water inlet valve coil for continuity and replace the valve if it is open or will not open.
6. If water fills but ice still fails, replace the ice maker assembly since the sensor is not separately serviced.
7. Restore power and run a test cycle to confirm E5 clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Filters, fill tubes, and inlet valves are common owner repairs. Call a technician if water pressure is adequate, the valve and filter check out, and E5 stays, since that points to a control or wiring fault behind the ice maker.
