## Manitowoc NEO Ice Machine Error Codes — What They Mean

Manitowoc NEO ice machines are the latest generation of Manitowoc commercial ice makers (N0300, N0400, N0503, N0654, N0804, N1005 and similar models). The NEO uses an updated control board with a 7-segment display and LED indicators that show fault codes when problems are detected. This guide covers the most common NEO error codes.

## NEO Error Code Summary

| Code | Meaning | Urgency |
|------|---------|---------|
| E01 | Long freeze cycle | Medium |
| E02 | Harvest timeout | High |
| E03 | Water fill timeout | Medium |
| E04 | High condenser temperature | High |
| E05 | High pressure cutout | High |
| E06 | Low pressure cutout | High |
| E08 | Ice thickness sensor fault | Medium |

## Common Causes

- **E01/E02 (cycle faults)** — Dirty condenser, low refrigerant, high ambient/water temperature, or harvest assist failure. These account for the majority of NEO service calls.
- **E03 (water fill)** — Low supply pressure, clogged inlet valve, or failed water inlet solenoid.
- **E04/E05 (high pressure/condenser)** — Dirty condenser or failed condenser fan are the primary causes.
- **E06 (low pressure)** — Low refrigerant charge is the most likely cause; confirm with manifold gauges.
- **E08 (thickness sensor)** — Scaled or failed ice thickness probe.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **For E01/E02/E04/E05** — Always start with condenser cleaning. Use a fin brush and blow from the clean side. Verify condenser fan runs. Check ambient temperature.
2. **For E03** — Verify water supply is fully open and at 20–80 PSI. Remove the inlet valve screen and clean. Test valve solenoid resistance.
3. **For E06** — Connect manifold gauges. Compare suction pressure to the NEO pressure-temperature chart. Low suction = low charge. Find and fix leak before adding refrigerant.
4. **For E08** — Inspect the ice thickness probe in the water curtain area. Clean with Manitowoc cleaner. Test probe per service manual. Replace if failed.
5. **Reset** — Hold the Off button for 3 seconds, then press On. Monitor the first complete cycle.
