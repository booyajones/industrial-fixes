## Rheem A008 Error Code — What It Means

A008 on a Rheem heat pump water heater indicates a detected dry fire condition. The controller has sensed that the heating elements tried to energize without enough water in the storage tank, so it disables heating to prevent damage. This is a protective measure that occurs when the unit believes the tank is not fully filled or contains trapped air.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Tank not fully filled after installation or service** The storage tank was not completely filled with water during initial setup, after a drain-down, or following maintenance work.
- **Air trapped in the tank or plumbing** Pockets of air remain inside the tank even after refilling, preventing proper water level detection and creating a false dry fire alarm.
- **Water supply issue preventing complete fill** A closed valve, insufficient pressure, or plumbing problem stops the tank from reaching its full capacity.
- **Condensate sensor fault after confirming tank is full** If the tank is verified full and air is purged but the alarm persists, the sensor circuit may be reading incorrectly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check the alarm code** on the EcoNet display or controller screen to confirm the unit is showing A008.
2. **Verify the water supply** is turned on and the tank inlet valve is fully open so the tank can fill completely.
3. **Fill the tank completely** by opening a hot water faucet in the house and letting it run until water flows steadily without sputtering or air bursts.
4. **Purge all trapped air** by continuing to run hot water at multiple fixtures until no more air bubbles appear and the flow is smooth and consistent.
5. **Reset the alarm** on the controller after confirming the tank is full and all air is expelled, then monitor to see if A008 returns.
6. **Repeat the fill and purge process** if the code reappears, as stubborn air pockets sometimes require multiple cycles to clear.
7. **Inspect the condensate line and sensor** if the alarm persists after confirming the tank is fully deaerated and the water supply is adequate, following the service manual troubleshooting path for sensor replacement if blockage is ruled out.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the A008 code returns after you have fully filled the tank and purged all air through multiple hot water fixtures. A pro can test the sensor circuit, inspect the condensate system, and verify the control board using manufacturer diagnostic procedures. Persistent dry fire alarms despite a full tank often require electrical testing and part replacement that go beyond basic homeowner troubleshooting. If your unit is under warranty, contact Rheem or your installer before replacing any parts to avoid voiding coverage.
