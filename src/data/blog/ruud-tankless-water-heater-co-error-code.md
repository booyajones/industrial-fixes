## Ruud CO Error Code — What It Means

The CO code on a Ruud tankless water heater is a scheduled cleaning reminder, not a combustion fault. It appears when the unit has logged enough operating time to trigger a maintenance interval. The code tells you the heat exchanger and water path need flushing to remove scale and mineral deposits. Once you complete the descaling service, the reminder can be reset and the unit will return to normal operation.

## Common Causes

- **Normal maintenance interval reached** The unit tracks run time and usage, then displays CO when service is due.
- **Scale and mineral buildup in the heat exchanger** Hard water leaves calcium and lime deposits in the water passages, reducing efficiency and triggering the cleaning reminder.
- **Restricted inlet water filter** Ruud units have an inlet filter that can clog with sediment, restricting flow and accelerating scale formation.
- **Insufficient flushing history** If the unit has never been descaled or maintenance was skipped, buildup accumulates and the code appears.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the code** on the display is CO and not an ignition, vent, or sensor fault code.
2. **Inspect and clean the cold-water inlet filter** by shutting off supply, removing the filter screen, rinsing debris, and reinstalling it.
3. **Isolate the water heater** using the service valves or by closing the inlet and outlet ball valves, then connect a descaling pump and hoses to the service ports.
4. **Flush the unit with a manufacturer-approved descaling solution** by circulating the cleaner through the heat exchanger for the time specified on the product label, typically 30 to 60 minutes.
5. **Rinse the system with clean water** by running fresh water through the flushing loop for several minutes to remove residual cleaner and dissolved scale.
6. **Restore water flow** and power, verify the unit heats properly, then reset or clear the CO reminder according to your model's control procedure.
7. **Record the service date** and schedule the next descaling interval based on your water hardness and the manufacturer's maintenance recommendations.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified plumber or water heater technician if the CO code does not clear after descaling, if you are uncomfortable working with service valves and chemical solutions, or if the unit shows poor heating performance or other fault codes after the flush. A technician can verify the heat exchanger is not damaged, inspect the overheat limiting wrap and venting, and make sure all service procedures are completed correctly. Professional help is also recommended if you have very hard water and want to install a water softener or filtration system to reduce future scale formation.
