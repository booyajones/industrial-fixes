## Hypertherm Plasma Cutter Fault 0-30 — What It Means

Fault 0-30 on a Hypertherm plasma cutter, including Powermax series units, indicates a shield cap or consumable fault. The machine's torch safety circuit detected that the retaining cap, shield, nozzle, or electrode stack is missing, loose, installed incorrectly, or not making proper electrical contact. Hypertherm uses this interlock to prevent accidental arc starts with an incomplete torch head assembly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Retaining cap not tightened fully** — The cap must be seated firmly to complete the torch interlock circuit. Even a partial turn loose can trigger 0-30.
- **Incorrect consumable stack order** — Installing the swirl ring, nozzle, electrode, or shield in the wrong sequence prevents proper contact and trips the fault.
- **Worn or damaged consumables** — A cracked nozzle, swollen swirl ring, or burned electrode can stop the torch head from seating correctly.
- **Contamination on torch contacts** — Metal dust, slag, or oxidation on the torch threads or contact surfaces interrupts the interlock signal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disassemble the torch head** — Turn off the plasma cutter and remove the retaining cap, shield, nozzle, electrode, and swirl ring. Lay them out in order.
2. **Inspect consumables for wear or damage** — Replace any cracked nozzle, pitted electrode, or deformed swirl ring. Hypertherm consumables should be replaced as a set if wear is uneven.
3. **Clean torch threads and contact surfaces** — Wipe the retaining cap threads and torch head contacts with a clean dry cloth. Remove slag, metal dust, or oxidation that could interrupt the safety circuit.
4. **Reassemble in the correct order** — Install electrode, swirl ring, nozzle, and shield exactly as shown in the Hypertherm consumables guide for the torch model. Tighten the retaining cap hand-tight only.
5. **Reset the system** — Power the machine back on and confirm the 0-30 fault clears. Perform a short test cut to verify the torch starts normally.

## Parts Often Needed

| Part | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Consumable kit (electrode/nozzle/shield) | Replace as a matched set for best torch performance |
| Swirl ring | Replace if cracked, warped, or melted |
| Retaining cap | Replace if threads are damaged or cap will not tighten fully |

## When to Call a Pro

If 0-30 remains after replacing consumables and cleaning the torch head, the torch lead or internal interlock circuit may be damaged. Hypertherm torch repairs involve high-voltage start circuits and should be handled by a qualified service technician.
