## GE Dishwasher C8 Error Code — What It Means

The C8 code shows the detergent cup is blocked from opening all the way during the cycle. The control flags that the soap may not release.

The most common cause is a tall item in the lower rack or hardened detergent jamming the cup. A failed dispenser actuator is the next step if the path is clear.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Item blocking the cup** A large dish or utensil leans against the door and traps the cup closed.
- **Caked detergent residue** Old hardened soap gums up the cup so it cannot swing open.
- **Debris in the cup track** Food bits lodge around the cup lid and stop its travel.
- **Failed dispenser actuator** The wax motor or solenoid that pops the cup open stops working.
- **Dispenser wiring fault** A loose connection at the dispenser keeps the actuator from firing.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Open the door and remove any item leaning against the detergent cup.
2. Clean caked detergent and food residue from the cup and its lid.
3. Check that the cup lid swings open and closed freely by hand.
4. Close and latch the door, then wait 30 seconds before restarting.
5. Run a cycle and watch that the cup releases the detergent.
6. If the cup stays shut with a clear path, plan to replace the dispenser.

## When to Call a Pro

If the cup is clean and free but still will not open on a cycle, the dispenser or its control signal has failed. A technician can confirm whether the dispenser or the board needs work.
