## Bosch E15 Error Code — What It Means

The E15 error code on Bosch dishwashers indicates that the leak protection system has detected water in the base pan beneath the machine. When water accumulates in the bottom area, it lifts a float or safety switch, which immediately puts the appliance into a protective shutdown state to prevent flooding. This code does not appear in Bosch refrigerator documentation, so if you see E15 on a refrigerator, consult your manual or contact Bosch support to confirm the meaning for your specific model.

## Common Causes

- **Worn or damaged door gasket** The rubber seal around the door can crack, tear, or lose its grip, allowing water to escape during the wash cycle and drip into the base pan.
- **Leaking water inlet valve** The valve that controls incoming water can develop seepage at the valve body or at its threaded connections, sending a slow drip into the base area.
- **Loose, cracked, or damaged hoses** Supply hoses, drain hoses, or internal circulation hoses and their clamps can fail or work loose, releasing water into the base pan during operation.
- **Failed sump or tub seal** Internal seals at the pump housing or tub joints can degrade over time, allowing wash water to leak past into the base pan.
- **Oversudsing or improper detergent** Using too much detergent or the wrong type creates excessive foam that can overflow into the base pan and trigger the leak sensor.
- **Drain backup or poor hose routing** A kinked, sagging, or improperly installed drain hose can cause water to back up or spill into the machine base during the drain cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut off power and water.** Turn off the circuit breaker for the dishwasher and close the water supply valve under the sink before you begin any inspection or repair work.
2. **Remove the lower access panel.** Take off the toe-kick or bottom trim panel at the front of the machine to expose the base pan and float assembly.
3. **Dry the base pan completely.** Use towels or a wet-dry vacuum to remove all standing water from the base pan, and look for any obvious pooling or water marks that show the leak path.
4. **Inspect all common leak points.** Check the door gasket for tears or gaps, examine the inlet valve and its connections for moisture, trace every visible hose and clamp for drips, and look at the sump area if accessible.
5. **Repair or replace the failed component.** Swap out a torn door seal, tighten or replace leaking hose clamps, install a new inlet valve if it is seeping, or replace any cracked hoses you find.
6. **Run a short test cycle with the panel off.** Start a rinse or short wash while watching the suspected area closely to confirm the leak is stopped and no new water enters the base pan.
7. **Reset the appliance and reinstall the panel.** Once the base is dry and the leak is fixed, power-cycle the machine or press and hold Start (consult your model's manual for the exact reset procedure), then replace the access panel.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you cannot locate the source of the leak after inspecting the door seal, inlet valve, and all visible hoses, or if the leak appears to come from inside the tub or sump area where disassembly is required. A pro should also handle the repair if you are uncomfortable working with water and electrical connections at the same time, or if the E15 code returns immediately after you dry the base pan and reset the machine. Additionally, if you see this code on a Bosch refrigerator rather than a dishwasher, contact Bosch or a technician to verify the correct meaning for your appliance model.
