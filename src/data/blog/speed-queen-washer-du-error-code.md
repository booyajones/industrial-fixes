## Speed Queen Washer dU Error Code — What It Means

The dU code is a door unlock fault. It appears when the control finished the wash cycle but did not detect that the door successfully unlocked. This is different from a dL or Er,DL code, which signals a problem locking the door during the cycle. The washer may have completed washing and spinning, but the door remains latched and the control cannot confirm the unlock condition.

## Common Causes

- **Sticking or failing door lock assembly** The latch mechanism inside the lock can bind or wear out, preventing the striker from releasing even when the control sends the unlock signal.
- **Worn or broken door strike** The plastic strike piece on the door frame can crack, warp, or break, stopping the lock from disengaging cleanly.
- **Door misalignment or sagging hinge** A hinge that has loosened or sagged over time shifts the door position so the lock cannot release the strike properly.
- **Water still in the tub** Some models will not unlock until they sense the drum is fully drained, so a failed drain or clogged pump can trigger the code.
- **Loose or corroded wiring at the lock** A poor connection at the lock harness interrupts the feedback signal that tells the control the door is unlocked.
- **Main control board lock-circuit fault** The driver or feedback path on the board can fail, though this is less common than mechanical lock problems.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Wait 2 to 5 minutes** after the cycle ends before you take any action, because some models delay the unlock while checking for residual water or motion.
2. **Cancel the cycle** by pressing the stop or pause button, then unplug the washer for 60 seconds and restore power to reset the control.
3. **Verify the tub is fully drained.** If water remains, run a drain-and-spin cycle or check the drain pump and hose for clogs before troubleshooting the lock.
4. **Unplug the washer and inspect the door strike and latch area.** Look for cracks, debris, or a loose strike screw, and check that the door sits square in the frame without sagging.
5. **Remove the top or front panel** to access the lock assembly, then disconnect the wiring harness and examine the terminals and wires for corrosion, looseness, or damage.
6. **Replace the door lock assembly** if the mechanical parts and wiring are sound but the code returns, since internal contact wear is the most frequent root cause.
7. **Test the new lock** by running a short cycle and confirming the door unlocks automatically at the end without repeating the dU fault.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the door will not unlock after you power-cycle the washer and you cannot safely access the lock from inside the drum, or if you replace the lock assembly and strike but the dU code returns. A repeating fault after new parts usually points to a control-board issue or a wiring problem deeper in the harness, and both require diagnostic tools and schematic knowledge to trace. Also call for help if the door is jammed shut and you need to retrieve laundry without damaging the cabinet or lock mechanism.
