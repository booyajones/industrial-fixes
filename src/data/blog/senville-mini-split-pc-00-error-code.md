## Senville PC 00 Error Code — What It Means

The PC 00 error code on a Senville mini-split indicates a compressor driver error or IPM (Intelligent Power Module) malfunction. The outdoor unit's IPM has detected an abnormal voltage signal going to the compressor, triggering IGBT over-strong protection. This fault typically shuts down the compressor to prevent damage.

Senville attributes PC 00 to wiring problems between the outdoor PCB and compressor, a failed IPM board or outdoor control board, outdoor fan or ventilation issues, low or abnormal refrigerant conditions after improper installation, or compressor winding and ground faults. The code requires a systematic diagnostic sequence to isolate the true cause.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or damaged wiring** Connections between the outdoor PCB, compressor terminals, and outdoor fan motor can work loose or corrode, creating voltage irregularities the IPM interprets as a fault.
- **Failed IPM module or outdoor PCB** The IPM board or main outdoor control board can fail, sending incorrect signals or misreading normal compressor current draw as an abnormal condition.
- **Outdoor fan failure or blocked airflow** A stopped fan motor or obstructed condenser coil causes the compressor to run hot and draw excessive current, tripping IPM protection.
- **Improper refrigerant charge or trapped air** Systems that were not properly evacuated or have service valves left partially closed run low on refrigerant and generate abnormal pressures that stress the compressor driver circuit.
- **Compressor winding short or ground fault** Internal compressor windings with uneven resistance or a winding shorted to the compressor shell will immediately trigger PC 00 protection at startup.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off system power** at the disconnect or breaker, wait two full minutes, then restore power and observe whether the PC 00 code clears and the unit runs normally.
2. **Inspect all wiring** between the outdoor control board, compressor terminals, and outdoor fan motor for loose connectors, burned terminals, or pinched insulation, and tighten or repair any damage found.
3. **Verify outdoor fan operation** by powering the unit and watching the outdoor fan spin freely, and clear any leaves, debris, or obstructions from the condenser coil fins.
4. **Check IPM terminal resistance** with a multimeter (power off and compressor leads disconnected), comparing each phase to the others for uniform readings, and replace the IPM board or outdoor PCB if resistance is uneven.
5. **Measure compressor winding resistance** across all three windings and from each winding to the compressor shell, replacing the compressor if any winding shows a short to ground or if resistances are significantly different.
6. **Confirm proper refrigerant commissioning** by verifying that both liquid and suction service valves are fully open and that the system was evacuated to proper vacuum before charging.
7. **Replace the outdoor control board** if all wiring, fan, IPM, and compressor checks pass but the PC 00 fault persists on every startup.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician immediately if you are not comfortable working with high-voltage wiring or do not own a multimeter and recovery equipment. Compressor diagnostics require isolating and measuring winding resistance with power disconnected, and replacing an IPM board or compressor involves refrigerant handling that is illegal without EPA certification. If the code returns after a simple power reset or you see any burned wires or smell electrical odors at the outdoor unit, shut down the system and schedule a service call rather than attempting further troubleshooting.
