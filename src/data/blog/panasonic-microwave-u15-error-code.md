## Panasonic U15 Error Code — What It Means

Panasonic's U15 error code appears on rice cookers and multi-cookers, not microwaves. The code tells you the appliance has detected a setup fault: the outer lid is open, the steam cap (taste catcher) is missing or incorrectly fitted, the inner pan is not properly seated, or there is foreign matter in the steam cap area. Some models also trigger U15 when the water vessel is not in position.

If you are troubleshooting a Panasonic microwave, U15 is not a documented microwave error code in manufacturer sources. Common Panasonic microwave codes include H97, H98, H95, U10, and U20. Double-check your display and consult your microwave's manual to confirm the exact code.

## Common Causes

- **Outer lid not fully closed** The lid latch is not engaged or the lid is sitting open, preventing the cooker from starting.
- **Steam cap (taste catcher) missing or loose** The steam cap is not installed, has fallen off, or is not snapped into place correctly in its mounting area.
- **Foreign matter in steam cap area** Food debris, oil, or dirt has accumulated around the steam cap seat, blocking proper contact or sensor detection.
- **Inner pan not seated properly** The inner cooking pan is tilted, raised, or misaligned in the base, breaking electrical contact or triggering a position sensor.
- **Water vessel not in position** On models with a separate water reservoir, the vessel is missing, empty, or not clicked into its correct slot.
- **Blocked vents or dirty exterior** Dust or grease buildup around intake and exhaust vents can cause overheating or sensor faults that mimic setup errors.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Press Cancel or Off** to clear the error and power down the appliance safely.
2. **Open the outer lid fully** and inspect the latch and hinge for damage or obstructions, then close it firmly until you hear or feel a click.
3. **Remove the steam cap** (taste catcher) from its mounting position on the lid or body and inspect it for cracks, warping, or missing tabs.
4. **Clean the steam cap seat** with a damp cloth to remove any food residue, oil, or debris, then wipe the cap itself and let both dry.
5. **Reinstall the steam cap** by aligning the tabs or grooves and pressing or twisting until it locks securely into place.
6. **Lift out the inner pan** and check that the bottom contact points and the pan's exterior are clean and dry, then reseat the pan flat in the base.
7. **Check the water vessel** (if your model has one) to confirm it is filled to the minimum line and clicked into its slot, then press Start to test.
8. **Unplug the unit and clean vents** with a soft brush if the error persists after reseating all components, allow the cooker to cool for 30 minutes, then retry.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if U15 returns after you have confirmed the lid is closed, the steam cap and inner pan are clean and seated, and all vents are clear. Persistent errors can indicate a failed lid switch, a faulty position sensor on the pan base, or a control board fault that requires meter diagnostics and soldering. If you see any signs of electrical arcing, burning smells, or if the outer case is cracked, unplug the unit immediately and arrange for authorized Panasonic service.
