## Bosch Washer F21 Error Code — What It Means

The F21 error code on a Bosch washer indicates a motor drive fault. The control board has detected that the motor is not rotating correctly or is not responding to drive commands. This is most often caused by worn carbon brushes in the motor, but can also point to motor wiring faults, connector problems, or a failed motor itself. The code typically appears when the washer attempts to spin but the motor cannot deliver the expected rotation or feedback signal to the control system.

## Common Causes

- **Worn carbon brushes** Carbon brushes on the motor are worn below serviceable length or making poor contact with the commutator, preventing proper motor operation.
- **Failed drive motor** The motor itself has failed internally due to winding shorts, bearing seizure, or commutator damage.
- **Loose or damaged motor wiring** Motor connector pins are loose, corroded, or overheated, interrupting power or feedback signals to the control board.
- **Mechanical binding in the drive system** The drum, belt, or motor pulley is jammed or binding, preventing the motor from turning freely and triggering a fault.
- **Control board or motor circuit fault** The drive electronics on the control board or the motor control circuit itself has failed and cannot command or sense motor rotation.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Disconnect mains power at the outlet or breaker, then shut off the water supply valves to make the washer safe to work on.
2. Access the rear panel or lower service area of the washer and check that the drum and drive belt turn freely by hand with no mechanical binding or foreign objects.
3. Remove the drive belt from the motor pulley, then unbolt the motor and disconnect the motor wiring connector and ground lead.
4. Inspect the carbon brushes for wear by removing the brush caps or housing. If the brushes are shorter than serviceable length or show poor contact, replace both brushes as a matched pair.
5. Check the motor connector and wiring harness for loose pins, corrosion, melted plastic, or damaged wires and repair or replace as needed.
6. Reinstall the motor, reconnect all leads, and refit the drive belt. Restore power and run the washer's diagnostic or reset sequence to clear the fault code.
7. Run a test spin cycle to confirm the motor operates correctly. If the F21 code returns after brush replacement, test or replace the motor or consult the control board diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable disconnecting mains power, accessing internal components, or working with motor wiring. If replacing the carbon brushes does not clear the F21 code, further diagnosis of the motor windings, control board drive circuit, or wiring harness typically requires a technician with a multimeter and model-specific service documentation. Also call a pro if the motor shows signs of mechanical seizure, burning, or if the fault returns immediately after a successful test cycle.
