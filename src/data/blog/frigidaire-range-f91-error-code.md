## Frigidaire Range F91 Error Code — What It Means

F91 is part of the F90/F91/F92/F93/F94 family of door lock issue codes on Frigidaire ranges. Frigidaire groups these codes together in owner documentation and treats them as the same fault category. The code signals that the control board cannot complete a lock or unlock cycle, either because the latch mechanism is jammed, the door lock switch is not reporting position correctly, the lock motor has failed, or wiring between the control board and the lock assembly is damaged or disconnected.

Frigidaire instructs owners to reset the range first by disconnecting power for up to 30 seconds, either by unplugging the unit or switching the breaker off and back on. If the code returns after the reset, the lock assembly or its wiring requires service.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board when the real fault is loose wiring or a broken door lock assembly. Inspect the harness between the control board and the lock assembly and test the lock motor circuit (should read around 2,000 ohms) before ordering any board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock assembly (~50%)** The latch motor or switch inside the lock assembly stops working and the control board cannot verify lock position.
- **Loose or damaged wiring (~25%)** Connections between the control board and the door lock assembly become loose, broken, or corroded and interrupt the signal.
- **Faulty door lock switch (~15%)** The switch that reports locked or unlocked position fails to change state or shows no continuity in either position.
- **Control board communication fault (~10%)** The main control board loses the ability to send or receive lock commands, though this is less common than mechanical lock failure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** by unplugging the range or switching the breaker off and leaving it off for at least 30 seconds, then restore power and check if F91 clears.
2. **Pull the range away from the wall** and remove the rear access panel or lift the cooktop to reach the control board area and door lock assembly wiring.
3. **Inspect all wiring** between the control board and the oven door lock assembly for loose, burned, or broken terminals and reconnect or repair any damaged connections.
4. **Test the door lock switch** by disconnecting the switch connector and checking continuity across its terminals in both locked and unlocked positions with a multimeter.
5. **Test the door lock motor circuit** by measuring resistance through the motor leads (expect around 2,000 ohms on typical Frigidaire ranges).
6. **Replace the door lock assembly** if the motor reads open (infinite resistance) or the switch shows no continuity change, and reassemble the range.
7. **Restore power and run a self-clean cycle** (or press the door lock button if your model has one) to verify the latch locks and unlocks smoothly and the F91 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable disconnecting power at the breaker, removing the rear panel, or using a multimeter to test switch continuity and motor resistance. Door lock repairs require access to the control area behind the range and involve live voltage when power is restored for final testing. If the wiring and lock assembly both test good but the code persists, the control board may need replacement and a technician can confirm the diagnosis and order the correct part for your model.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150–$300.
