## LG Washer SUD Error Code — What It Means

The SUD code on your LG washer means the machine has detected excessive suds or foam inside the drum. To protect itself, the washer automatically runs a suds-reduction routine that can add about 2 hours to your cycle time. If the foam level is still too high during the spin phase, the washer may stop spinning completely to help prevent leaking.

This code is not a mechanical failure. It is a protective response to too much detergent, the wrong type of detergent, or conditions like soft water that cause detergent to over-foam. Repeated oversudsing can leave residue buildup in the drum that makes the problem come back even when you think you have corrected the dose.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the drain pump thinking SUD is a drainage fault. Before buying any parts, simply reduce your detergent to the manufacturer's recommended amount and run a cleaning cycle to remove residue buildup.

## Common Causes

- **Too much detergent (~50%)** Using more than the manufacturer's recommended amount is the most common cause LG cites for excessive suds.
- **Non-HE detergent (~30%)** Regular (non-high-efficiency) detergent produces far more foam than HE washers can handle and will trigger SUD even at small doses.
- **Soft water (~10%)** Soft or treated water causes detergent to foam more aggressively, so even correct doses of HE detergent can over-suds.
- **Residue buildup in the drum (~8%)** Repeated oversudsing leaves detergent film inside the drum that continues to foam on future loads until it is cleaned out.
- **Restricted drain path (~2%)** A clogged drain pump filter or kinked drain hose can prevent suds from clearing, though this is a secondary factor rather than the root cause of SUD.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Pause the cycle and power-cycle the washer** by unplugging it for 30 seconds, then plug it back in and restart the cycle to see if the code clears.
2. **Verify you are using HE (high-efficiency) detergent.** Check the label on your detergent bottle. If it does not say HE, replace it immediately with an HE formula.
3. **Reduce your detergent dose** to no more than the manufacturer's recommended amount (typically 1-2 tablespoons for HE washers). If you have soft water, cut the dose in half again.
4. **Run an empty cleaning cycle** (also called Tub Clean on many LG models) with no detergent to flush out any residue buildup in the drum from past oversudsing.
5. **Inspect the drain pump filter** (usually behind a small access panel at the front lower corner of the washer). Remove any lint, coins, or debris and rinse the filter under running water.
6. **Check the drain hose** for kinks or clogs. Straighten any bends and run water through the hose to confirm it flows freely.
7. **Retest with a small load** using the corrected detergent type and dose. Monitor the cycle to confirm SUD does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the SUD code persists after you have confirmed you are using HE detergent at the correct dose, run a cleaning cycle to remove residue, and cleared the drain pump filter and hose. A pro can inspect the drain pump motor and pressure system to rule out a mechanical restriction that is trapping suds. Also call if the washer refuses to drain at all or if you see water pooling under the machine, since those symptoms point to a drain pump failure rather than a simple suds condition. For straightforward detergent correction and cleaning, this repair is well within DIY capability and requires no special tools or parts.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-15 for HE detergent if you need to replace non-HE soap, 10-20 min to adjust dose and run cleaning cycle. A pro service call runs about $100-200 if a service call is needed to inspect drain components when suds do not clear after detergent correction.
