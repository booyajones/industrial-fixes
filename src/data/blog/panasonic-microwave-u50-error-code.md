## What this code means
U50 on a Panasonic microwave means the oven cavity is overheated. Panasonic defines this code as appearing when internal temperatures exceed safe operating limits. The code should clear automatically once the unit cools down. If the display continues to show U50 after the appliance has been unplugged and allowed to cool for at least 30 minutes, the underlying temperature sensing or cooling system may have a fault requiring authorized service.

## Common Causes

- **Excessive back-to-back operation** Running the microwave repeatedly or for extended cycles without cooldown periods causes internal heat buildup that triggers the U50 code.
- **Blocked ventilation openings** Dust, debris, or objects obstructing the cooling vents prevent airflow and allow the cavity to overheat during normal use.
- **Insufficient clearance around the unit** Installing the microwave too close to walls or cabinets restricts cooling airflow and traps heat inside the chassis.
- **High ambient room temperature** Operating the microwave in a hot kitchen or environment above normal room temperature contributes to overheating conditions.
- **Cooling fan or sensor fault** If U50 returns immediately after cooldown under normal conditions, an internal cooling component or temperature sensor may have failed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the microwave immediately** and stop all use until the code clears to prevent damage to internal components.
2. **Allow at least 30 minutes of cooldown time** with the door open so the oven cavity and electronics can return to safe temperatures.
3. **Inspect all ventilation grilles** on the top, sides, and rear for dust, grease buildup, or blockages and clean them thoroughly with a soft brush or vacuum.
4. **Check installation clearances** against your model's manual and move the unit if it sits closer than the specified distance to walls or cabinetry.
5. **Restore power and test** by running a brief low-power cycle and watching for normal operation without the U50 code reappearing.
6. **Monitor room temperature** and avoid operating the microwave during the hottest part of the day if your kitchen runs warm.
7. **Contact an authorized Panasonic service center** if the U50 code returns immediately or if the microwave overheats again under normal use, as Panasonic directs persistent cases to professional diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call an authorized Panasonic service center if the U50 code does not clear after a full cooldown period, if it returns immediately when you restore power, or if the microwave overheats again during light normal use. Panasonic treats persistent error codes as diagnostic indicators of internal faults that require professional testing and genuine replacement parts. Do not attempt to disassemble the microwave yourself. High-voltage components inside present serious shock and burn hazards even when unplugged.
