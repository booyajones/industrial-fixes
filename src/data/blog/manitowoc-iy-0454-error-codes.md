## Manitowoc IY-0454 Error Codes — What They Mean

The Manitowoc IY-0454 is a 455 lb/day Indigo series half-dice ice machine commonly installed in hotel bars, restaurants, and healthcare facilities. It uses the standard Manitowoc Indigo fault code system.

| Code | Meaning | Urgency |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Long harvest | Medium |
| 2 | Harvest timeout | High |
| 3 | Long freeze | Medium |
| 5 | Safety switch fault | High |
| 6 | Water fill timeout | Medium |
| E04 | High condenser temperature | High |
| E05 | High pressure cutout | High |
| E06 | Low pressure cutout | High |

## IY-0454 Common Fixes {#fix}

**Codes 1, 3, E04:** The IY-0454 in a hot hotel environment accumulates condenser grease quickly. Clean condenser monthly in commercial kitchen proximity. Verify condenser fan runs.

**Code 6 (water fill):** The IY-0454's water inlet valve is accessible from the front panel. Remove and clean the valve screen annually. Check supply pressure (20-80 PSI).

**Code 5 (safety switch):** Check which safety has tripped using the service manual's LED indicator pattern. High pressure cutout is most common — trace back to condenser.
