## Samsung Oven E-OE Error Code — What It Means

E-OE is a door lock fault on Samsung ranges. The board told the motorized latch to move and it either did not move or did not confirm the new position. The code often turns up during or after a self clean cycle.

The board relies on a switch inside the lock to confirm locked or unlocked. When that confirmation never arrives, it posts E-OE and can leave the door held shut.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed lock motor** The motor that drives the latch has burnt out and cannot move the bolt.
- **Jammed latch** Burnt residue or debris around the latch blocks it from reaching its stop.
- **Bad latch switch** The position switch inside the lock has failed and never confirms the state.
- **Loose lock wiring** A disconnected or chafed harness between the lock and board breaks the signal.
- **Control board fault** Less often the board misreads a good lock and holds the error.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for a few minutes, then restore it to let the lock motor reset.
2. Confirm the door is pushed fully closed and flush.
3. Inspect the latch for charred residue or debris and clear anything blocking travel.
4. Pull the range and remove the top or back panel to reach the door lock assembly.
5. Check the wire harness between the lock and the control board and reseat any loose connector.
6. Test the lock motor and latch switch for continuity and replace the lock assembly if either fails.
7. Restore power and run a lock cycle to confirm the latch moves and clears the code.

## When to Call a Pro

If the door stays locked and a power cycle does not release it, do not force it open. A stuck self clean latch can break if pried. A tech can free the latch safely and replace the lock assembly without harming the door.
