## Bosch Commercial Dishwasher E-Codes — What They Mean

Bosch commercial dishwashers (undercounter, door-type, and conveyor models) display E-codes when faults occur. Bosch commercial equipment is common in hotel, restaurant, and healthcare food service operations.

## Common Fixes {#fix}

**E1 (water inlet):** Check supply valve is fully open. Verify supply pressure (20-80 PSI). Clean the inlet valve screen. Test solenoid coil resistance.

**E3/E6 (temperature faults):** The wash tank or final rinse isn't reaching required temperature. Check booster heater element continuity. Verify incoming hot water temperature is at least 140°F.

**E5 (wash pump fault):** Most urgent fault — no washing occurs. Check pump impeller for blockage. Test motor windings. Verify supply voltage.

**E4 (overflow):** Drain is blocked or drain pump has failed. Check drain line for blockage. Test drain pump operation.
