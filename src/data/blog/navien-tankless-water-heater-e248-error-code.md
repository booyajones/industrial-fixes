## Navien Tankless Water Heater E248 Error — What It Means

The E248 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates the unit is detecting an issue with water flow through the system. This code typically appears when the control board cannot verify proper flow rates through the heat exchanger, either because the flow sensor is not reading correctly or because insufficient water is moving through the unit. The heater will shut down to protect itself from operating without adequate flow, which could cause overheating or damage to internal components.

Because Navien tankless heaters rely on precise flow measurements to modulate burner output and maintain target temperatures, any disruption to the flow sensing system will trigger a protective shutdown. The E248 code is often related to physical obstructions in the water path or a failing flow sensor rather than a control board or gas valve problem.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flow sensor first, but many E248 codes are fixed by cleaning the inlet filter and flushing debris from the system. Always check and clean the filter and inspect for blockages before ordering a new sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged inlet filter (~45%)** Sediment and mineral buildup on the inlet filter restricts flow and prevents the sensor from reading correctly.
- **Debris in the flow sensor (~25%)** Small particles lodged in the turbine or Hall-effect sensor assembly interfere with flow measurement.
- **Failed flow sensor (~15%)** The flow sensor turbine or electronics fail and no longer send a valid signal to the control board.
- **Low incoming water pressure (~10%)** Inadequate supply pressure or partially closed isolation valves reduce flow below the minimum threshold.
- **Scale buildup in the heat exchanger (~5%)** Hard water deposits inside the heat exchanger restrict passages and reduce flow, especially in areas with high mineral content.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the cold water isolation valve.
2. **Close the hot water isolation valve** and open the drain valves to relieve pressure in the unit.
3. **Remove the inlet filter screen** from the cold water inlet and rinse it under running water to remove all sediment and debris.
4. **Locate the flow sensor** (consult your model's service manual for exact position, typically near the inlet manifold) and disconnect the wiring harness.
5. **Unscrew and remove the flow sensor** from its housing and inspect the turbine for debris, mineral buildup, or damage.
6. **Flush the sensor and housing** with clean water or a mild vinegar solution to remove any scale or particles, then reinstall or replace the sensor.
7. **Reassemble the unit**, close the drain valves, open the isolation valves, and restore power. Run a hot water tap to verify normal operation and confirm the error has cleared.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you are uncomfortable working with water supply plumbing, if the error persists after cleaning the filter and flow sensor, or if you suspect heavy scale buildup requiring a full descaling flush. A pro can also diagnose wiring issues between the flow sensor and control board, verify proper flow rates with calibrated instruments, and perform warranty-covered repairs if your unit is still under warranty.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $120-250.
