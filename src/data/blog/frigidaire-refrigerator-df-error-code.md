## Frigidaire Refrigerator dF Error Code — What It Means

dF means the control has detected no defrost circuit, in other words a defrost system fault. The freezer is not running a proper defrost cycle, so frost builds up on the evaporator coil and blocks airflow.

The usual signs are heavy frost in the freezer, a warming fresh food section, and weak cooling. The fault usually traces to the defrost heater, the defrost sensor, or the defrost control.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed defrost heater** An open defrost heater element cannot melt frost off the evaporator coil.
- **Bad defrost sensor or thermostat** A failed defrost bimetal or sensor stops the board from timing the cycle.
- **Faulty defrost control board** The board that triggers defrost can fail and skip the cycle entirely.
- **Broken defrost wiring** Open wires in the defrost loop break the circuit the control checks for.
- **Frozen over evaporator** A long missed defrost cycle leaves a block of ice that chokes airflow.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator and empty the freezer for safe access.
2. Remove the rear freezer panel to reach the evaporator coil and heater.
3. Manually defrost any heavy ice on the coil with the unit unplugged.
4. Test the defrost heater for continuity with a multimeter.
5. Test the defrost sensor or thermostat for continuity when cold.
6. Replace any defrost part that reads open on the meter.
7. Replace the defrost control board if the heater and sensor test good.

## When to Call a Pro

If the heater, sensor, and wiring all test good and dF returns, the defrost control board is the likely cause. Tracing the defrost circuit and confirming the board is a fair point to call a technician.
