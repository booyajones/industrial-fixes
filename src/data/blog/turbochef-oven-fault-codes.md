## TurboChef Oven Fault Codes — Quick Reference

TurboChef rapid cook ovens (Sota, Encore, i5, Bullet, and Tornado) combine microwave energy and impingement air for rapid cooking. The electronic controller displays fault codes when a condition is out of specification.

### E0 / E1 — Temperature Sensor Fault
TurboChef ovens use a Type K thermocouple to measure cavity temperature. A damaged or corroded connector is the most common cause. Disconnect the thermocouple connector at the control board. Measure thermocouple resistance — should read a few ohms (not open). If open, replace the thermocouple.

### E4 — Door Interlock Fault
TurboChef microwave-combination ovens require all door interlock switches to be closed before operating. An E4 fault means one or more interlocks is not engaged. Check:
1. Door closes fully and latches
2. Inspect door interlock switches (primary, secondary, monitor) for damage
3. Verify door hook not worn — worn hooks don't depress interlocks fully

Safety note: Never defeat or bypass door interlock switches on microwave-equipped ovens.

### E3 — Magnetron Fault
A magnetron fault on TurboChef combo units indicates microwave power generation failure. The magnetron requires replacement by a qualified technician — this is not a field serviceable item without specific training and equipment.

### E6 — Blower Motor Fault
The impingement blower provides hot air jets. If the blower fails, the oven cooks unevenly and can overheat. Check the blower motor connector, run capacitor (if applicable), and motor windings.

## Jump to Fix

- **E0/E1 sensor** → Check connector → Measure resistance → Replace thermocouple
- **E4 interlock** → Check door latch → Inspect interlock switches → Check door hooks
- **E6 blower** → Check motor connector → Verify power → Check for seized motor

## When to Call a Pro
TurboChef (a Welbilt brand) has authorized service agencies. Magnetron and high-voltage system work must be performed by certified technicians only.
