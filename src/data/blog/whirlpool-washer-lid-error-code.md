## Whirlpool Washer Lid Error Code — What It Means

Whirlpool washers display different lid-related codes depending on the model. On many top-load washers, a "LID" or "Lid opened" message simply means the washer detected the lid was left open. Closing the lid clears the display. If the lid stays open for more than about 10 minutes, the cycle resets and the washer drains out.

On other Whirlpool top-load HE models, a flashing Lid Lock light or the code F5/E3 indicates a lid-lock fault. This means the lock did not move into the locked position, the lock motor cannot be powered, or the lid is not fully closed due to interference. The washer will not start until the lid lock engages correctly.

## Common Causes

- **Lid is open or not fully closed** The most common cause is simply that the lid has not been pushed down firmly enough to engage the lock mechanism.
- **Debris blocking the lock or striker** Clothing, lint, detergent residue, or other debris can interfere with the lid lock striker and prevent proper engagement.
- **Misaligned lock striker** The lock striker on the lid may be bent, damaged, or out of alignment with the lid lock assembly in the frame.
- **Failed lid lock assembly** The lid lock mechanism itself can fail mechanically or electrically and will not latch even when the lid is fully closed.
- **Wiring or connector problem** Loose or corroded connections between the lid lock and the main control board can prevent the lock from receiving power.
- **Control board not commanding the lock** The main control board may not be sending power to the lid lock motor due to a control failure or incorrect cycle selection.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Close the lid completely** and make sure it latches firmly. On many Whirlpool models the "LID" message clears immediately when the lid is shut.
2. **Check for obstructions** around the lid lock and the striker on the underside of the lid. Remove any lint, detergent buildup, or fabric that may be blocking the lock mechanism.
3. **Inspect the lock striker** on the lid for alignment. If it is bent or out of position, adjust or replace it so it lines up with the lock assembly in the frame.
4. **Confirm the cycle selector** is set to a valid cycle and press and hold the Start button for up to 3 seconds. Some models require a long press to engage the lock.
5. **Perform a power reset** by unplugging the washer from the wall outlet for about 5 minutes, then reconnecting power. This clears temporary control faults.
6. **Check the lid lock harness** for loose or damaged connectors between the lid lock assembly and the main control board. Reseat or repair any connections.
7. **Test or replace the lid lock assembly** if the error persists with the lid fully closed, no obstructions present, and good wiring. Verify the lock motor receives power from the control before replacing the board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the lid lock light continues flashing or the error code returns after you have closed the lid, cleaned the lock area, performed a power reset, and verified the striker alignment. If you are not comfortable using a multimeter to check for power at the lid lock or if you suspect the main control board is not sending the correct signal, a qualified appliance technician can diagnose the electrical fault and replace the lid lock assembly or control board safely.
