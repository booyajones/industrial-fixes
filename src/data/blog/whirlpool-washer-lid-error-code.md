## Whirlpool Washer Lid Error Code — What It Means

The Whirlpool lid error indicates the washer has detected the lid is open. On basic models, the display will show a simple "Lid" or "Lid Open" message. On top-load HE models with a lid lock, related faults mean the lid lock did not move into the locked position, the lock motor cannot be powered, or the lid is not fully closed because of interference. Whirlpool washers will not fill or wash with the lid open. If the lid stays open for more than 10 minutes, the cycle resets and the washer drains automatically.

This code is a safety and operational interlock. The machine needs to confirm the lid is closed (and locked, on HE models) before starting the wash cycle. The fault can be as simple as the lid not being pushed down completely, or it can involve a mechanical or electrical problem with the lid lock assembly itself.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the lid lock assembly or control board when the real problem is detergent residue or a piece of lint blocking the striker. Clean the latch area thoroughly and power-cycle the washer before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Lid not fully closed (~50%)** Clothing, detergent buildup, lint, or another physical obstruction prevents the lid from seating completely or the striker from entering the lock.
- **Lid lock mechanism not engaging (~25%)** The lid lock motor or latch does not move into the locked position because of wear, misalignment, or a failed lock assembly.
- **Lid left open during cycle (~15%)** The lid was opened or not closed at the start, triggering the open-lid message.
- **Control or start input issue (~6%)** The Start button was not pressed and held long enough, or the cycle selector is not set correctly on models that require it.
- **Lock motor not being powered (~4%)** Wiring or connector issues prevent the control board from powering the lid lock motor, or the control board itself has failed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Close the lid firmly** and press and hold Start for up to 3 seconds if your model requires it. The code should clear if the lid was simply open.
2. **Inspect the lid striker and lock area** for visible obstructions, detergent residue, lint, or misalignment. Wipe the striker and the lock cavity with a damp cloth and remove anything blocking the latch path.
3. **Unplug the washer** for at least 5 minutes to reset the control board, then restore power and close the lid. Start a test cycle to see if the error returns.
4. **Confirm the tub has stopped moving** before expecting the lid to unlock. The lock will stay engaged until basket motion ends, so wait for the drum to come to a complete stop.
5. **Check the lid lock assembly** if the code persists. Open the lid, locate the lock mechanism on the washer top panel, and manually inspect the striker on the lid and the lock housing for damage or wear.
6. **Test the wiring and connectors** at the lid lock assembly. Look for loose or corroded pins on the harness plug. If you have a multimeter and model-specific service documentation, consult your model's wiring diagram for voltage and continuity checks at the lock motor.
7. **Replace the lid lock assembly** if cleaning, reset, and wiring checks do not resolve the fault. Install the new lock according to the service manual for your model and retest.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the lid lock assembly does not engage after you have cleaned the latch area, power-cycled the washer, and verified the lid closes fully. If you lack a multimeter or the service documentation to check the lock motor circuit, a pro can diagnose whether the fault is in the lock assembly, the wiring harness, or the main control board. Also call if the washer is still under warranty, because opening the cabinet may void coverage. Professional diagnosis typically costs the service call fee and will save you from replacing parts by trial and error.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–90 for a lid lock assembly, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–250 including service call and part.
