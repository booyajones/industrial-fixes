## Samsung Dryer C90 Error Code — What It Means

The C90 (or C9) error code on a Samsung dryer indicates the vent blockage test found more than 90% vent restriction. Samsung classifies this as a restricted airflow fault. The dryer is detecting abnormal airflow or high temperature caused by poor exhaust venting, not usually an electrical component failure inside the machine.

In practice, the dryer cannot exhaust properly, so heat and backpressure build up. The control logic flags this condition after running its internal vent blockage test. The code is telling you the vent system is over 90% clogged, and the dryer will not operate safely until airflow is restored.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the thermistor or control board first. Before buying any parts, clean the entire vent path from the lint screen to the outside hood and re-run the vent blockage test to see if the code clears.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged lint filter or screen** A heavily loaded lint screen restricts airflow at the drum exit and is the first place Samsung directs you to check.
- **Blocked or kinked flexible duct** A crushed, kinked, or collapsed transition hose between the dryer and the wall chokes exhaust flow and is a very common field cause.
- **Lint buildup inside the duct run** Lint accumulates over time in the rigid or flexible duct path, narrowing the passage and triggering the restriction test.
- **Blocked outside vent hood** Bird nests, debris, or a stuck flap at the exterior termination prevent air from escaping and create backpressure.
- **Long or poorly designed duct run** Excessively long duct, too many elbows, or undersized pipe can restrict airflow enough to cause the C90 code even when clean.
- **Abnormal thermistor resistance (rare)** If the vent is confirmed clean and the code persists, Samsung service guidance notes that abnormal thermistor behavior can contribute to overheating detection in the broader fault family.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off the dryer and unplug it** from the wall outlet to make sure safe access to the vent system.
2. **Remove and clean the lint filter** thoroughly under running water if needed, then dry it completely before reinstalling.
3. **Disconnect the flexible duct hose** at the back of the dryer and inspect it for kinks, crushing, or heavy lint buildup inside.
4. **Inspect the rigid duct run** from the wall connection to the outside hood, using a vent brush or vacuum to remove lint, and check for crushed or disconnected sections.
5. **Check the outside vent hood** for bird nests, debris, or a stuck flap, and clear any obstructions so the flap swings freely.
6. **Reconnect the duct**, plug in the dryer, and **run the vent blockage test** with the dryer cool and empty (run Air Fluff first if the drum is warm to bring it to room temperature).
7. **Verify the code clears** after the test. If C90 returns on a known-clean vent system, call a qualified technician to diagnose appliance-side temperature sensing or control faults.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the C90 code persists after you have thoroughly cleaned the entire vent path, verified strong airflow at the outside hood, and re-run the vent blockage test. A technician can inspect hidden duct sections inside walls, measure thermistor resistance against the service manual table for your model, and diagnose temperature-sensing or control faults. Also call a pro if you discover structural ductwork problems like disconnected sections inside a wall, excessively long runs that exceed code, or if you are uncomfortable working with the electrical connection or gas line (on gas models). Samsung recommends involving the installer if the dryer was recently set up and ductwork quality is in question.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $10-40 in vent cleaning supplies or replacement flexible duct, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $100-200 for vent cleaning service, higher if ductwork repair is needed.
