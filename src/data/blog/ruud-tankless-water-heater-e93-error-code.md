## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E93 Error — What It Means

The E93 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a communication fault within the system. This can mean the main control board is not receiving proper signals from a remote controller, sensor, or internal component. Because error code definitions can vary between models and firmware versions, consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit to confirm the exact meaning for your specific heater.

The fault may stem from loose or corroded connections, a failed remote controller, damaged wiring, or in some cases a control board issue. The heater will usually shut down or fail to ignite when this communication error is active.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board when the real problem is a loose connector or a failed remote controller. Inspect all wiring terminals and test the remote first before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~35%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen terminals at the control board, remote, or sensor connections, breaking communication.
- **Failed remote controller (~25%)** The optional wired remote can develop internal faults or water damage, sending corrupt signals to the main board.
- **Damaged or pinched wiring harness (~20%)** Wires between the board and remote or sensors can be pinched during installation or chewed by rodents, causing intermittent faults.
- **Control board firmware glitch (~10%)** Transient power surges or static discharge can corrupt board memory, requiring a power cycle or board replacement.
- **Main control board failure (~10%)** The board itself can fail due to age, moisture intrusion, or component wear, losing the ability to communicate with peripherals.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners to access the control board and wiring.
3. **Inspect all wire connections** at the control board, remote controller port, and sensor plugs. Look for looseness, corrosion, or burnt terminals.
4. **Disconnect the remote controller** (if installed) by unplugging its connector from the board. Power the unit back on and check if the E93 error clears.
5. **Clean any corroded terminals** with electrical contact cleaner and a small brush, then reseat all connectors firmly until they click or seat fully.
6. **Check the wiring harness** along its entire run for pinches, cuts, or signs of rodent damage. Repair or replace any damaged sections with the same gauge wire.
7. **Power cycle the heater** by turning the breaker off for 30 seconds, then back on. Observe the display for error codes and normal operation.
8. **Replace the control board** if all connections are secure and the error persists. Match the board part number exactly to your model and transfer wires one at a time.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical wiring, if the error persists after inspecting and reseating all connections, or if you suspect the control board needs replacement but want professional diagnostics first. Technicians have multimeters and manufacturer diagnostic tools to pinpoint communication faults quickly. Also call a pro if you find evidence of water intrusion inside the unit, as this can damage multiple components and may indicate a venting or installation problem that requires correction.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-150 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-400.
