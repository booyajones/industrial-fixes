## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E63 Error — What It Means

The E63 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a communication fault or control board issue. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or the unit's wiring diagram for your specific serial number. In many cases, the code appears when the main control board cannot communicate properly with other components, or when there is an internal electronics fault. The error may be intermittent or persistent depending on the underlying cause.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board immediately, but many E63 codes are caused by loose or corroded wiring harnesses. Inspect all connector plugs and ground wires before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring harness (~40%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen connectors between the control board and other components, interrupting communication signals.
- **Failed main control board (~30%)** The PCB may have a solder joint failure, capacitor failure, or damaged trace that prevents normal operation.
- **Power supply fluctuation or brownout (~15%)** Voltage dips or electrical noise on the incoming line can cause the microprocessor to fault and log an error.
- **Corroded ground connection (~10%)** A poor ground path can cause erratic behavior and communication faults in the control circuit.
- **Firmware glitch or static discharge (~5%)** Occasional static events or memory corruption may trigger a false error that clears after a power cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and verify with a non-contact voltage tester that the unit is de-energized.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners and lifting the cover away to expose the control board and wiring.
3. **Inspect all wiring connectors** on the control board, flame sensor, igniter, and flow sensor for loose seating, corrosion, or burn marks.
4. **Reseat each connector** by unplugging it, inspecting the pins, and pressing it back firmly into place until you hear or feel a click.
5. **Check the ground wire** attachment to the chassis and tighten if loose or corroded.
6. **Restore power** and observe the display. If the E63 persists immediately, note whether any other codes appear in the diagnostic menu.
7. **If the error remains**, consult a licensed technician to test the control board with a multimeter and evaluate whether board replacement or further electrical troubleshooting is required.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician if the error persists after checking all connections and performing a power reset. Gas-fired tankless water heaters involve live gas lines and high-voltage ignition circuits that require proper diagnostic equipment and licensing. A technician can test control board voltages, verify gas valve signals, and safely replace the board or other electronics. If you smell gas, hear unusual noises, or see sparking, shut off the gas supply and power immediately and call for service.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-450.
