## Whirlpool Refrigerator Door Bin Replacement — What This Part Does

The door shelf bin is a molded plastic tray that clips into the refrigerator door liner to hold bottles, jars, and condiments. It supports weight and keeps items from sliding or falling when you open and close the door. These bins fail from normal wear, impact when heavy bottles are set down hard, plastic fatigue over time, or using the wrong bin for your model which prevents the clips from seating properly.

Because door bins are model-specific, you must match the replacement to your refrigerator's exact model number. There are no electrical, pressure, or resistance specifications for a door bin since it is a passive plastic component. When the bin cracks, loosens, or breaks, the only fix is swapping in the correct OEM replacement part.

## Signs It Needs Replacing

- **Visible cracks or broken plastic** You see fractures in the bin body or mounting tabs, especially at stress points where bottles rest.
- **Bin sags or tilts forward** The bin no longer sits level because the rear clips or tabs have broken or pulled free from the door liner.
- **Bottles or jars fall out** Items slide forward and tip over or drop to the floor when you open the door.
- **Bin slides up or down in the door** The bin moves vertically instead of staying locked in position, even when you press it into place.
- **White stress marks or discoloration** The plastic shows cloudy stress fractures or brittleness before it breaks completely.
- **Bin missing or already removed** A previous owner discarded the broken bin and you need a replacement to restore door storage.

## How to Replace It {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator or turn off the circuit breaker to cut power during the repair.
2. Open the refrigerator door and remove all bottles, jars, and items from the damaged door bin.
3. Lift the front edge of the old bin upward to disengage the rear clips or tabs from the door liner slots.
4. Slide the bin forward and out of the door, inspecting the liner slots for any broken plastic fragments that need clearing.
5. Unpack the new OEM door bin and confirm the mounting clips or tabs match the slots in your door liner.
6. Align the rear clips or tabs of the new bin with the corresponding slots in the door liner.
7. Press the bin rearward and downward until the clips snap or slide fully into the slots and the bin sits level and secure.
8. Tug gently on the front edge of the bin to verify it does not lift or slide out of position.
9. Restore power to the refrigerator and reload the bin with bottles and jars to confirm it holds weight without sagging.
