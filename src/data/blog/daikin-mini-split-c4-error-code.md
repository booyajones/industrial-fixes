## Daikin C4 Error Code — What It Means

The C4 error code on a Daikin mini-split indicates a malfunction of the indoor heat exchanger temperature sensor, also called the liquid pipe thermistor. This sensor sits on the indoor coil and feeds temperature readings to the indoor PCB so the unit can regulate cooling, heating, and protection logic. When the indoor board cannot read the sensor correctly—because of a bad thermistor, loose connector, or PCB fault—it throws C4 and may shut down to protect itself.

This is a sensor circuit problem, not a refrigerant or compressor issue. In most field cases the thermistor itself has failed or the wiring connector has corroded. The code will stay on until the faulty component is identified and replaced.

## Common Causes

- **Defective thermistor** The heat exchanger thermistor itself has failed internally and no longer reports accurate resistance to the indoor board.
- **Loose or corroded connector** The plug between the sensor and indoor PCB has oxidized pins, dirt, or poor contact that breaks the circuit intermittently.
- **Damaged sensor wiring** The sensor harness has pinched, cut, or frayed insulation causing a short or open circuit.
- **Sensor not fully seated** The thermistor has slipped out of its mounting clip or pocket on the coil, giving incorrect or floating readings.
- **Faulty indoor PCB** When the sensor and wiring test correctly but the code persists, the indoor control board's sensor input circuit has failed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the C4 code** using the self-diagnosis function on your Daikin remote or controller, not just by observing symptoms.
2. **Isolate power** to the indoor unit at the breaker and wait two minutes before opening the front cover and filter panel.
3. **Locate the heat exchanger thermistor** on the indoor coil or liquid pipe and verify it is fully seated in its clip or mounting pocket.
4. **Inspect the thermistor connector** at the indoor PCB for corrosion, bent pins, loose fit, or broken wire insulation, and clean or reseat as needed.
5. **Measure the thermistor resistance** with a multimeter across the sensor terminals and compare the reading to your model's specification table, checking that it is not open, shorted, or stuck at a fixed value.
6. **Replace the thermistor** if the resistance is abnormal or does not change with coil temperature, then reassemble the unit and restore power.
7. **Test for PCB failure** by installing a known-good thermistor or swapping from another identical Daikin unit; if C4 remains with a good sensor, replace the indoor board.
8. **Clear the fault** by power-cycling the system and running a test cycle to confirm normal operation without the code reappearing.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working inside a live mini-split indoor unit or measuring resistance with a multimeter. A qualified tech has the exact Daikin sensor specifications, can safely test the PCB, and can verify refrigerant temperatures match the sensor readings after repair. If you replace the thermistor yourself and C4 returns immediately, the indoor board likely needs professional diagnosis or factory replacement.
