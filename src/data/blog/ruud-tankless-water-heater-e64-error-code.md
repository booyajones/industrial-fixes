## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E64 Error — What It Means

The E64 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a fault related to the combustion fan motor or its electrical circuit. The fan must spin at the correct speed to create proper airflow and vent combustion gases safely. When the control board does not detect the expected fan signal (either no rotation, incorrect speed, or a missing feedback signal), it throws the E64 code and shuts down the unit to prevent unsafe operation.

Because the exact definition can vary slightly between Ruud models and firmware versions, consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit for the precise meaning. In general, this code points to fan motor failure, a break in the fan wiring, a loose connector, or less commonly a control board that cannot read the fan signal.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board when the real problem is a failed fan motor or a loose connector. Always test power to the fan and check all connectors and harness continuity before replacing the board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed combustion fan motor (~50%)** The fan motor itself burns out, seizes, or loses its bearings and cannot spin to provide airflow.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~25%)** A connector on the fan harness pulls loose or corrodes, breaking the power or feedback signal path.
- **Faulty control board (~15%)** The main PCB fails to supply power to the fan or cannot read the tachometer signal even when the fan spins.
- **Blocked air intake or exhaust vent (~10%)** Debris, ice, or a bird nest obstructs the intake or vent, causing the fan to stall or draw excessive current.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off electrical power** at the breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** of the unit to expose the control board and combustion fan assembly.
3. **Locate the combustion fan** (usually a small centrifugal blower mounted near the burner) and trace its wiring harness to the control board.
4. **Inspect all connectors** along the harness for loose pins, corrosion, or burn marks; reseat each connector firmly.
5. **Use a multimeter** to measure voltage at the fan connector when the unit calls for heat (you may need to restore power briefly under observation); if voltage is present but the fan does not spin, the motor is faulty.
6. **Order and install a replacement fan motor** if the motor is confirmed dead, matching the model number on the fan label or consulting the parts diagram for your heater.
7. **Reassemble the cover**, restore power and gas, and run a test cycle to confirm the E64 code is gone and the fan spins normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician whenever you are uncomfortable working around gas appliances, high-voltage wiring, or combustion systems. Tankless water heaters combine gas, electricity, and venting in a tightly integrated package, and misdiagnosis can lead to unsafe operation or carbon monoxide hazards. A professional has the diagnostic tools to measure fan speed, verify gas pressure, and test control-board outputs accurately. If you have already replaced the fan motor and the E64 code persists, the control board or a wiring fault deeper in the system is likely, and a technician can trace circuits and update firmware if needed.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-$450.
