## Scotsman Ice Machine Error Code 6 — What It Means

Scotsman error code 6 indicates a **long harvest condition occurred multiple times** — the machine completed the freeze cycle but the harvest cycle (where ice is released from the evaporator) took longer than the maximum allowed duration more than once within a defined period. On Scotsman cube ice machines, the harvest cycle uses hot gas or water to release ice from the evaporator grid; if it takes too long (typically more than 3–4 minutes), the controller logs a long harvest event. Multiple occurrences trigger code 6 and place the machine in a safety shutdown.

## Common Causes

- **Scale or mineral buildup on the evaporator** — Mineral deposits on the ice-forming evaporator slow heat transfer during harvest, extending the time for ice release.
- **Insufficient hot gas during harvest** — Low refrigerant charge or a hot gas bypass valve issue reduces the heat available to release ice from the evaporator.
- **Water temperature too warm** — On water-harvest machines, warm incoming water slows harvest; on hot gas harvest machines, high ambient temperatures reduce the temperature differential driving harvest.
- **Dirty or restricted harvest water system** — Plugged water distributors or scale on the evaporator curtain slow water flow and extend harvest.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Clean the ice machine thoroughly** — Remove all ice from the bin. Run a full clean and sanitize cycle per the Scotsman procedure using Scotsman Scale Remover. This dissolves mineral deposits on the evaporator, water system, and curtain.
2. **Clean the condenser** — On air-cooled units, clean the condenser coil and fan. A dirty condenser raises head pressure and reduces hot gas temperature during harvest.
3. **Inspect the water system** — Remove and clean the water curtain, float valve, and water distributor holes. Blocked distributor holes cause uneven water flow during harvest.
4. **Check incoming water temperature** — Measure water temperature at the inlet. Water above 70°F (21°C) will slow harvest; consider a water filter or supply line change to reduce temperature.
5. **Check for refrigerant issues** — If cleaning doesn't resolve code 6, the refrigerant charge or hot gas valve may need service. This requires an EPA 608-certified technician.
6. **Reset the machine** — After cleaning, power cycle the machine and monitor the first several freeze/harvest cycles. Each harvest should complete in under 2–3 minutes.

## When to Call a Pro

If code 6 returns after thorough cleaning, a certified refrigeration technician should check refrigerant charge, hot gas valve operation, and evaporator surface condition. Repeated harvest problems with clean equipment indicate a refrigerant system issue requiring EPA 608 handling.
