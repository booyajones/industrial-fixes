## Frigidaire Refrigerator Water Dispenser Not Working — What's Happening

A Frigidaire refrigerator water dispenser that stops working is a symptom, not a single error code. Frigidaire treats this as a flow failure caused by supply setup issues or a component fault. If the refrigerator was just installed, the first checks are whether the water supply is connected, the house shutoff valve is open, the system has been primed to remove air, and no dispenser lock or shutoff condition is active.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Clogged or incorrectly installed water filter** A restricted or improperly seated filter is a common cause of weak or no water flow, and Frigidaire service guidance recommends inspecting the filter and replacing it if damaged or if it is not the OEM part specified in your manual.
- **Dispenser lock or control lock enabled** The dispenser lock can disable the water dispenser entirely, and one service guide notes holding the Control Lock button for 3 seconds to unlock.
- **Water supply not connected or valve closed** On a newly installed unit or after service, the refrigerator may not be connected to the household water line or the shutoff valve may be closed.
- **Kinked or frozen water line** A kinked tube behind the refrigerator or a frozen section in the door or cabinet can stop water flow completely.
- **Low household water pressure** Frigidaire service guidance cites concern when pressure is below 20 psi, as the inlet valve may not open properly with weak supply.
- **Defective water inlet valve** A failed inlet valve solenoid can block water flow even when pressure is adequate and the dispenser switch is working.
- **Faulty dispenser switch or paddle** If the dispenser switch does not close when the paddle is pressed, the control cannot energize the inlet valve.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the water supply line is connected to the refrigerator and the house shutoff valve is fully open.
2. Check for dispenser lock by holding the Control Lock button for 3 seconds to unlock, then retest the dispenser.
3. Remove the water filter and inspect it for proper seating, correct OEM part number, and any damage or restriction, then replace if suspect and retest.
4. Prime the system on a newly installed unit by dispensing water for several minutes to purge air from the lines per Frigidaire instructions.
5. Disconnect the supply line at the refrigerator and verify a strong, steady stream from the house line to confirm adequate pressure and flow before blaming internal components.
6. Trace the water line behind the refrigerator and through the door for kinks or crushed sections, and thaw any frozen tubing carefully if present.
7. Unplug the refrigerator, isolate the water inlet valve, and test the solenoid coil for continuity with a multimeter (typical field readings are about 100 to 500 ohms), then replace the valve if it shows no continuity or fails to energize.
8. Remove the dispenser trim and test the dispenser switch for continuity when the paddle is pressed, and trace wiring from the switch to the control board or door harness for open circuits or damaged connectors if the switch tests good but the valve does not receive power.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a tech if you have verified water supply and pressure, replaced the filter, and unlocked the dispenser but still have no flow. Tracing wiring through the door harness, testing the inlet valve with a multimeter, and accessing the dispenser switch assembly require disassembly and electrical diagnostics. A pro can isolate whether the fault is in the valve, switch, control board, or door wiring without unnecessary part replacement.
