## LG Dryer E13 Error Code — What It Means

E13 is a drain-system fault that only appears on LG hybrid condensing dryers. The code means the dryer is detecting that condensate water cannot leave the machine normally. LG states it is triggered when the drain hose is kinked, crushed, clogged, or when water in the sump has frozen.

Newer LG support content also ties E13 to the condensate water container being full or the drain pump being filled with air. The common thread is that condensate cannot flow out of the dryer through the normal drain path.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the drain pump when the real problem is a simple kink or twist in the drain hose. Always inspect the entire drain hose run for bends, sags, or blockages before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked, twisted, or crushed drain hose (~55%)** The drain hose is bent sharply, twisted, or pinched behind the dryer so condensate cannot flow out.
- **Clogged drain hose or outlet (~20%)** Lint, debris, or standing water has blocked the hose or the point where it drains.
- **Drain hose extended too long (~10%)** Adding extension hoses or using a non-standard hose creates extra resistance and prevents proper draining.
- **Frozen water in the sump (~8%)** If the dryer is in a very cold location, condensate water in the sump can freeze and block the drain path.
- **Water container full or drain pump filled with air (~7%)** On models with a removable condensate tank, the tank may be full or the pump may have trapped air preventing normal flow.

## Quick Diagnosis

Answer these to narrow it down fast.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** or flip its circuit breaker off and wait 60 seconds to reset the control.
2. **Restore power** and check whether the E13 code has cleared from the display.
3. **Pull the dryer away from the wall** so you can see the entire drain hose run from the dryer to the drain or standpipe.
4. **Inspect the drain hose** for kinks, twists, crushing, or sharp bends and straighten any you find.
5. **Check the drain outlet** for standing water or debris blocking the hose exit.
6. **Confirm the hose is the standard LG part** and not extended with additional hose or fittings that add resistance.
7. **If your model has a removable water container**, pull it out, empty it completely, and reseat it firmly before restarting the cycle.
8. **Press START/PAUSE** to resume the cycle and verify the E13 code does not return.
9. **If the code returns immediately or after one load**, call for service because the drain pump or internal condensate system needs repair.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the E13 code returns after you have confirmed the drain hose is straight, unobstructed, the correct LG part, and not frozen. Persistent E13 after hose correction means the drain pump may be air-locked, the internal condensate routing is blocked, or a sensor or pump assembly has failed. LG states that if the code reappears after basic troubleshooting, the unit requires service. A technician will test the drain pump, check for internal blockages in the condensate circuit, and verify the sump and sensor are functioning correctly.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-40 in parts (drain hose), 20-40 min. A pro service call runs about $120-220.
