## Bosch Washer E01 Error Code — What It Means

The E01 error code on Bosch front-load washers typically indicates a door lock or door latch failure. The machine cannot verify that the door is securely closed and locked, so it will not start a wash cycle. This code can also appear if the control board is not receiving proper signals from the door lock mechanism due to wiring issues or connector problems.

Because the exact meaning of error codes can vary by model and year, consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the cabinet for your specific washer. In general, E01 points to the door interlock system rather than water fill or drain problems.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many homeowners replace the control board when the real issue is a broken door latch striker or a loose wire connector on the lock assembly. Test the lock for continuity and inspect the door striker and harness plug first.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock assembly (~50%)** The electromechanical lock that secures the door can wear out or burn out, preventing the control from detecting a closed door.
- **Broken or misaligned door striker (~25%)** The plastic or metal catch on the door frame can crack, shift, or break off, so the latch cannot engage properly.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connector (~15%)** The harness plug at the door lock can work loose or develop corrosion, interrupting the signal to the control board.
- **Control board communication fault (~8%)** A failed relay or microprocessor on the main control board may not recognize the lock signal even when the lock is working.
- **Obstruction in the door latch (~2%)** Lint, small items, or debris can block the latch mechanism and prevent it from fully engaging.

## Quick Diagnosis

</details>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves to make sure safe working conditions.
2. **Open the door** and inspect the door striker on the inside of the door frame for cracks, breaks, or misalignment.
3. **Remove the top panel** by unscrewing the rear screws and sliding it back, then lift it off to access the door lock assembly on the right side of the front panel.
4. **Unplug the wiring harness** from the door lock and inspect the connector for corrosion, bent pins, or looseness. Clean with contact cleaner if needed.
5. **Test the door lock** with a multimeter set to continuity mode. Consult your model's wiring diagram for the correct terminals. Replace the lock if it shows no continuity.
6. **Install the new door lock assembly** by reversing the removal steps. Secure the mounting screws and reconnect the wiring harness firmly.
7. **Reassemble the washer**, plug it in, and run a test cycle to verify the door locks and the E01 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or if the error persists after replacing the door lock and striker. A technician can test the control board and trace wiring faults with specialized diagnostic tools. Also call a pro if the washer is still under warranty, as DIY repairs may void coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $50-120 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300.
