## Electrolux Dryer Mute Error Code — What It Means

The Mute indication on your Electrolux dryer does not signal a hardware failure. It means the unit's audible tones and alarm chimes have been turned off, and the dryer is currently in mute mode. Some models display an illuminated mute icon on the control panel when this setting is active. The dryer will still operate normally, but you will not hear any end-of-cycle alerts, button-press beeps, or other sounds until you restore the audio.

## Common Causes

- **Mute mode enabled by user** The most common reason is that the mute setting was activated intentionally or by accident through the control panel.
- **Accidental button press** A child or someone cleaning the panel may have pressed the mute button combination without realizing it.
- **Power cycle restoring saved setting** After a power outage or unplugging, the dryer may return to mute mode if that was the last saved state.
- **Control panel button stuck** Debris or moisture under the mute button can cause repeated toggling of the setting.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Locate the mute button or icon** on your dryer's control panel by consulting the owner's manual for your specific model number.
2. **Press and hold the designated button** (often labeled with a speaker icon or the word "Mute") for three to five seconds until the mute indicator turns off or you hear a confirmation beep.
3. **Try a common unmute sequence** if your manual is unavailable by pressing and holding the "Signal" or "Options" button for several seconds, as many Electrolux models use this approach.
4. **Verify the change** by starting a short cycle and checking that you hear normal button tones and end-of-cycle alerts.
5. **Clean the control panel** gently with a damp cloth if the setting keeps toggling back, removing any residue around the buttons.
6. **Power cycle the dryer** by unplugging it for one minute, then reconnecting and attempting the unmute sequence again if the display remains unresponsive.
7. **Inspect the user interface board** only if the dryer will not exit mute mode after all attempts and if the control panel shows other signs of malfunction such as unresponsive buttons or flickering display.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you cannot find the mute button sequence in your owner's manual and online resources for your exact model number do not provide guidance. Also seek help if the control panel will not respond to any button presses, if the display flickers or shows garbled characters, or if unmuting temporarily works but the dryer returns to mute mode within a few cycles. These symptoms may indicate a failing user interface board that requires diagnosis with the service manual for your specific model.
