## Bosch Washer F25 Error Code — What It Means

The F25 error code on a Bosch washer indicates a defect in the water level measurement system. This system uses a pressure sensor to monitor how much water is in the drum during wash and rinse cycles. When the sensor or its associated components fail to communicate proper water level data to the control board, the machine throws F25 and stops the cycle to prevent overfilling or underfilling.

Bosch official documentation describes this as a water level measurement fault, not a motor, tachometer, or turbidity sensor issue. The fault may be a one-time control glitch or a persistent hardware problem requiring service.

## Common Causes

- **Transient control fault** A temporary communication error between the pressure sensor and control board triggers the code but clears after a power reset.
- **Defective pressure sensor** The water level sensor itself has failed and no longer sends accurate pressure readings to the control board.
- **Blocked or kinked pressure hose** The air tube connecting the pressure sensor to the tub air trap is obstructed, preventing pressure changes from reaching the sensor.
- **Clogged air trap** Lint, detergent residue, or debris has built up in the small air chamber that transfers tub pressure to the sensor hose.
- **Failed control board** The main electronic control has a fault in the circuit that reads the pressure sensor signal, even though the sensor is good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off the washer** at the control panel and wait 10 seconds, then turn it back on to see if the error clears.
2. **Unplug the machine** from the wall outlet for at least 30 seconds to fully reset the control board, then plug it back in and test a cycle.
3. **Access the pressure sensor** by removing the top or rear panel (location varies by model) and locate the small round sensor with a thin air tube attached.
4. **Inspect the pressure hose** for kinks, cracks, or blockages and trace it from the sensor down to the air trap on the outer tub, replacing the hose if damaged.
5. **Remove and clean the air trap** by disconnecting the hose and flushing the trap chamber with warm water to clear any lint or detergent buildup.
6. **Test the pressure sensor** by gently blowing into the hose connection while monitoring for a click or checking continuity changes with a multimeter, or replace the sensor if no response occurs.
7. **Run a test cycle** after reassembly and monitor for the F25 code. If it returns after all checks, contact Bosch service for control board diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the F25 code returns after you have completed a full power reset and inspected the pressure hose and air trap. Bosch official guidance directs you to contact Bosch Customer Support or an authorized service technician when the error persists, since further diagnosis requires testing the pressure sensor with specialized tools and evaluating the control board for circuit faults. If you are uncomfortable removing panels or working near electrical components, schedule service rather than attempting the repair yourself.
