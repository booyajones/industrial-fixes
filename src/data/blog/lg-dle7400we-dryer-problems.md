## LG DLE7400WE Dryer Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The LG DLE7400WE is an electric dryer in LG's mid-range lineup, and it shares error code architecture with other models in the series. The most frequent service calls involve vent obstruction codes (d80, d90, d95) caused by lint buildup in the exhaust system, followed by power-related codes (nP, PS) tied to house wiring or terminal block connections. Steam-equipped variants may also display E1 or E4 codes related to the steam function, but the core reliability issues center on airflow and electrical supply.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **d80 / d90 / d95 or Flow Sense bars on display** These codes indicate a clogged or severely restricted exhaust duct at the percentage shown (80%, 90%, or 95%), and the fix is to remove and clean lint from the exhaust hose, house duct, termination cap, and blower housing, then check for kinked or crushed ducting.
- **nP code on display** LG says nP means no current is being detected at the heater due to a problem with home electrical wiring, and the fix is to inspect and tighten the terminal block connections, verify correct house receptacle wiring, and confirm proper incoming voltage.
- **PS code on display** LG states PS indicates improper voltage within the power cord and can be caused by the white and red wire being reversed on the terminal block, so the fix is to de-energize the dryer, correct the wire positions on the terminal block, and restore power.
- **PF code on display** PF is a power-failure code that appears after a voltage interruption, and the fix is to press the power button to clear the code and run a test cycle, or check for loose power connections if it recurs.
- **Door-open or door-not-closed error** The code appears when the door switch is open or the latch is not fully engaged, and the fix is to inspect the door catch, strike, and switch for wear or obstruction and replace the door switch assembly if it fails continuity testing.
- **E1 or E4 code (steam models only)** LG support pages state E1 and E4 apply only to models with the steam function and indicate a steam system fault, requiring inspection of the water supply connection, steam generator, and associated wiring.
- **Dryer runs but produces no heat** On electric models, this is typically caused by a blown thermal fuse on the blower housing due to restricted airflow, and the fix is to replace the thermal fuse and clean the entire exhaust path to prevent recurrence.
- **Long dry times with no error code** Partial vent restriction or a worn blower wheel reduces airflow without triggering a fault code, and the fix is to clean the vent system, inspect the blower wheel for lint buildup or damage, and verify the lint filter is clean.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a tech when you see nP or PS codes and are not comfortable working inside the dryer's electrical compartment or verifying house circuit wiring. Steam-related codes (E1, E4) require disassembly to access the steam generator and water supply. If you replace the thermal fuse and it blows again immediately, there is a deeper airflow or component fault that needs diagnosis. For gas variants (DLG7400WE), all work on the burner assembly, gas valve, or igniter should be handled by a qualified technician due to the risk of gas leak or carbon monoxide hazard.
