## LG Refrigerator IS Error Code — What It Means

The IS code (also shown as 1S or Er IS) means the board lost a good signal from the ice maker sensor, or the ice maker fan is not spinning freely. On many LG and LG built Kenmore units the in-door ice maker carries its own sensor wired through the door hinge.

Because the wiring runs through the door, a loose or damaged connector is a frequent cause, along with moisture in the circuit, frost on the fan, or a failed sensor inside the ice maker assembly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Loose door wiring** The sensor signal runs through the door hinge, so a loose connector there makes the board lose the sensor.
- **Moisture in the sensor circuit** Water near the ice maker can corrode the sensor or connector and break the signal.
- **Frost on the ice maker fan** Ice that blocks the fan can read as an abnormal condition and trip IS.
- **Failed ice maker sensor** A sensor that has shorted or opened inside the assembly gives the board a bad reading.
- **Damaged harness in the hinge** Wires flexed by the door over time can break where they pass through the hinge.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator or switch it off at the breaker, wait 5 minutes, then restore power and give it 10 to 15 minutes to settle.
2. Open the door and inspect the ice maker connector and the wiring at the hinge for looseness or corrosion.
3. Reseat the connector firmly and clean any corrosion you find on the pins.
4. Check the ice maker fan for frost or a stuck blade and thaw the area if ice is present.
5. Flex test the door harness gently to find any broken wire at the hinge and repair it.
6. Measure the ice maker sensor where accessible and compare the reading against spec.
7. Replace the in-door ice maker assembly if the built in sensor reads bad or the code persists.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if IS returns after you reseat the wiring and replace the ice maker assembly. A broken wire hidden inside the door hinge can be hard to find and may need a tech to trace and repair.
