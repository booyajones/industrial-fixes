## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E27 Error Code — What It Means

The E27 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a combustion-related fault. The unit has detected a problem with establishing or maintaining a stable flame during operation. This can stem from issues with gas delivery, air supply, venting, or sensor feedback.

The exact meaning can vary slightly across Ruud models, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the unit cover for model-specific details. In general, the heater's control board has shut down heating to prevent unsafe operation until the fault is cleared.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flame sensor or igniter when the real culprit is low gas pressure or a blocked vent. Check that the gas valve is fully open and the vent pipe is clear before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Gas supply interruption or low pressure (~35%)** A closed valve, regulator problem, or undersized gas line can starve the burner and prevent stable combustion.
- **Blocked or restricted venting (~25%)** Obstructions in the intake or exhaust vent pipe reduce airflow and disrupt the air-fuel mixture, triggering a fault.
- **Dirty or fouled burner assembly (~20%)** Dust, debris, or scale buildup on the burner can create uneven flames or prevent ignition altogether.
- **Faulty flame sensor or flame rod (~10%)** A corroded or misaligned sensor may fail to detect flame even when combustion is occurring, causing the board to shut down.
- **Igniter or spark electrode failure (~10%)** A cracked igniter or dirty electrode can prevent the initial spark needed to light the burner.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas valve to the water heater before beginning any inspection.
2. **Check the gas supply** by confirming the manual gas valve upstream of the heater is fully open and other gas appliances are operating.
3. **Inspect the vent pipes** outside the home for blockages in both the intake and exhaust terminations, and clear any debris or obstructions.
4. **Remove the front cover** of the heater and visually inspect the burner assembly for dust, corrosion, or foreign material.
5. **Power-cycle the unit** by turning the breaker back on and following the startup sequence in your owner's manual to see if the code clears.
6. **Call a licensed technician** if the error persists, as further diagnosis requires measuring gas inlet pressure, testing the flame sensor, and cleaning or replacing internal combustion components safely.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician for any E27 error that persists after you have verified gas supply and cleared vents. Combustion troubleshooting requires measuring gas pressure with a manometer, inspecting and cleaning the burner and heat exchanger, testing the flame sensor circuit, and ensuring proper venting airflow. Working with natural gas or propane carries serious safety risks, and improper repairs can lead to carbon monoxide exposure or fire. A qualified professional will have the tools and training to diagnose the fault accurately and restore safe operation.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
