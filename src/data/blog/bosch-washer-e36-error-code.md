## Bosch Washer E36 Error Code — What It Means

The E36 error on a Bosch washer signals a drainage fault. The machine has not emptied water within the expected time. Bosch groups this code with blocked drain pipes, clogged water outlet hoses, blocked pumps, and hoses that are kinked or installed too high. The machine stops mid-cycle to protect itself when it detects water still sitting in the tub after the drain cycle completes.

In Bosch service documentation the fault is described as the appliance being unable to empty water in the allocated time. The error typically appears during or after a drain or spin phase. Before assuming a hardware failure, check the entire drain path from the filter housing through the pump and out to the standpipe or drain.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked drain filter or coin trap** Lint, coins, hair ties, fabric scraps, and other small debris lodge in the filter housing and prevent water from reaching the pump.
- **Sump hose obstruction** The hose between the tub and the pump traps coins, buttons, or fabric, blocking the flow before it reaches the pump impeller.
- **Jammed or failed drain pump** Debris catches in the pump impeller and stops it from spinning, or the pump motor itself has burned out and no longer runs when powered.
- **Kinked or incorrectly installed drain hose** The outlet hose is kinked, routed too high above the permitted maximum of 39 inches, or blocked by lint buildup inside the corrugated tube.
- **Control board or wiring fault** After clearing all mechanical blockages, a failure to supply voltage to the pump points to a wiring connector issue or a fault in the main control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have cleared every blockage, confirmed the pump spins freely by hand and receives supply voltage, yet the machine still will not drain and the E36 code persists. That scenario points to a control-board fault, a wiring harness break between the board and pump, or a pressure-switch problem that prevents the controller from recognizing a successful drain. Also call for help if you are uncomfortable working with residual water, tilting the machine, or using a multimeter to check pump power. A qualified Bosch service technician has factory diagnostic software that can log fault history and verify sensor readings in real time.
