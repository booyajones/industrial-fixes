## Speed Queen Washer dL Error Code — What It Means

The dL (or E:dL) error code on a Speed Queen washer indicates a door lock failure. The machine either could not lock the door within 15 to 20 seconds after you started the cycle, or it could not unlock the door within about 3 minutes after the cycle finished. On some models, Er dL means the door unlocked unexpectedly during the wash cycle. The control board monitors the door lock circuit continuously and will halt operation when it does not see the correct lock signal within the expected window.

This fault protects you from running a cycle with an unsecured door and also prevents you from opening the door while water or motion is present. Most dL errors trace to mechanical misalignment, a worn latch, or a failed lock solenoid rather than the control board itself.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully closed or misaligned** The striker does not engage the latch completely because the door is ajar, sagging, or the hinges have shifted.
- **Failed door lock or latch assembly** The solenoid, strike mechanism, or microswitch inside the lock housing has worn out or broken and no longer signals a secure lock.
- **Broken or loose wiring harness** The wires or connectors running from the door lock to the control board are damaged, corroded, or disconnected.
- **Load jamming the door** A large or unbalanced load pushes fabric into the door seal and prevents the latch from seating fully.
- **Temporary control glitch** Static or a power surge can freeze the control logic, and a simple power cycle clears the fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the washer** by unplugging it for one minute, then restore power to clear any transient control fault before further diagnosis.
2. **Check door closure** by opening and firmly closing the door until you hear and feel a positive click, making sure no laundry is caught in the seal.
3. **Inspect the door latch and striker** for visible wear, cracks, or misalignment by opening the door and examining the metal or plastic catch on both the door and the tub frame.
4. **Test the lock mechanism** by starting a cycle and listening for the audible click or solenoid sound that confirms the lock is trying to engage.
5. **Examine the wiring harness** by unplugging the machine, removing the top or rear panel as needed, and tracing the wires from the door lock assembly to the control board for cuts, burns, or loose connectors.
6. **Replace the door lock assembly** if the harness and door alignment are good but the lock still does not signal properly during the test cycle.
7. **Verify the repair** by running a short test cycle and confirming the door locks within 15 to 20 seconds and unlocks cleanly at the end without throwing the code again.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have power-cycled the machine, confirmed the door closes fully, and inspected the visible latch hardware but the dL code returns immediately. Diagnosing the lock circuit with a multimeter and accessing the lock assembly on some models requires partial disassembly and familiarity with live voltage testing. If the lock assembly and wiring both test good yet the error persists, the control board input circuit may be at fault and requires board-level troubleshooting or replacement that is best left to a trained service provider.
