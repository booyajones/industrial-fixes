## Navien E338 Error Code — What It Means

The E338 error on Navien tankless water heaters signals an exhaust temperature problem. The unit has detected that exhaust gases are either too hot or the exhaust thermistor is reading abnormal values. This is a safety lockout that prevents dangerous operating conditions.

The code typically appears when the heater cannot properly vent combustion gases, when the exhaust path is blocked or restricted, or when the exhaust thermistor itself has failed. The unit will shut down until the problem is resolved and the system is reset.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the exhaust thermistor first, but most E338 codes result from venting problems. Inspect the entire vent run for restrictions, debris, and proper clearances before ordering any sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted exhaust vent (~45%)** Debris, ice, bird nests, or improperly installed vent pipe can restrict exhaust flow and cause abnormal temperature readings.
- **Exhaust thermistor failure (~25%)** The thermistor that monitors exhaust temperature can drift out of calibration or fail, sending false readings to the control board.
- **Incorrect venting materials or configuration (~15%)** Venting that does not meet manufacturer specifications, uses wrong materials, or has excessive length or too many elbows can cause poor draft and overheating.
- **Combustion air supply restriction (~10%)** Inadequate fresh air intake or a blocked intake vent can lead to incomplete combustion and elevated exhaust temperatures.
- **Control board fault (~5%)** A failing main control board may misread thermistor signals or incorrectly trigger the error even when exhaust temperature is normal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and turn off the gas supply valve.
2. **Inspect the outdoor exhaust termination** for any visible blockages, debris, ice buildup, or damage. Remove any obstructions you find.
3. **Check the entire vent run** from the heater to the termination. Look for disconnected joints, sagging horizontal runs, excessive elbows, or improper materials.
4. **Verify intake air supply** is not blocked and that the heater has adequate combustion air. Check any intake vent terminations for obstructions.
5. **Locate the exhaust thermistor** on the exhaust outlet of the heat exchanger. Disconnect the wiring harness and inspect for corrosion or loose pins.
6. **Test the thermistor resistance** with a multimeter if you have access to the model-specific resistance table in your service manual. Consult your model's table for correct values at room temperature.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit. Monitor for several heating cycles to confirm the error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician experienced with Navien tankless heaters if you cannot access or clear the venting, if the exhaust thermistor tests require technical knowledge, or if the error persists after clearing visible obstructions. Gas-fired appliances demand proper combustion and venting. A technician can perform a combustion analysis, measure vent draft, verify all code-compliant clearances, and safely test or replace the exhaust sensor or control board. Professional diagnosis prevents misdiagnosis and avoids the safety risks of improper venting or gas appliance work.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $180-400.
