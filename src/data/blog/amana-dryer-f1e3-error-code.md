## Amana Dryer F1E3 Error Code — What It Means

Amana dryer technical documentation does not list an F1E3 error code. The verified fault code in this family is F6E3, which indicates a voltage sensing or power supply problem. This means the dryer's control board has detected inconsistent or incorrect incoming voltage, typically pointing to an issue with the home electrical supply, wiring connections, or breaker rather than a dryer component failure. If your display shows F1E3, check your model number carefully and confirm the exact code, as it may be a misread or model-specific designation not covered in general Amana service literature.

## Common Causes

- **Incoming power supply interruption** Tripped breaker, loose breaker connection, or low line voltage from the utility can trigger voltage sensing faults.
- **Terminal block or power cord wiring** Loose, corroded, or burned connections at the dryer terminal block disrupt steady voltage delivery to the control.
- **Faulty control board voltage sensing circuit** The main electronic control board may have a defective voltage monitor or relay that falsely reports a power problem.
- **Intermittent outlet or home wiring fault** Worn receptacle contacts or backstabbed wire connections upstream of the dryer can cause voltage drops under load.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Turn off power at both breaker switches (dryers use two 120 V breakers for 240 V) and leave them off for five full minutes to reset the control board.
2. Inspect the breaker panel for any tripped or partially tripped breakers, reset them firmly, and check for signs of heat or burning at the breaker terminals.
3. Restore power and start a timed dry cycle (no load) for five minutes to see if the code returns immediately or clears.
4. Unplug the dryer or lock out the breaker, remove the rear access panel, and inspect the terminal block where the power cord attaches for loose screws, discolored wire, or corrosion.
5. Use a multimeter to verify 240 V at the terminal block (120 V from each hot leg to neutral) with power restored, confirming stable incoming voltage.
6. If voltage is stable and connections are tight but the code persists, the main control board voltage sensing circuit is likely faulty and the board should be replaced.
7. Record your full model number from the data plate and consult the manufacturer service sheet to confirm whether F1E3 applies to your specific dryer or if the code is F6E3.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you measure unstable voltage at the terminal block, if the breaker trips repeatedly when the dryer starts, or if you are uncomfortable working with 240-volt circuits. A licensed electrician should diagnose upstream wiring or panel issues. If the code appears immediately after a power reset and all connections are secure, the control board replacement requires live-voltage handling and proper grounding, so professional service is the safer choice for most homeowners.
