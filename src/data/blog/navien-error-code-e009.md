## Navien E009 Error Code — What It Means

E009 on a Navien tankless water heater or combi-boiler indicates a heat exchanger overtemperature fault. The heat exchanger outlet temperature sensor detected water temperature exceeding the safe upper limit. This is a thermal protection lockout — the unit shuts down to prevent damage to the copper heat exchanger, connecting hoses, and surrounding components.

## Common Causes

- **No water flow or very low flow through the unit** — Tankless heaters require a minimum flow rate (typically 0.5–0.75 GPM) to transfer heat from the heat exchanger into the water stream. Without adequate flow, the exchanger overheats rapidly.
- **Scaled heat exchanger** — Mineral scale (calcium carbonate) builds up on heat exchanger surfaces over time in hard water areas, reducing heat transfer efficiency and causing localized overheating.
- **Failed flow sensor** — If the flow sensor fails and reports zero flow when water is actually moving, the control board may restrict the gas valve but the burner may still overfire.
- **Faulty outlet temperature thermistor** — A thermistor drifting high will report false overtemperature; the unit shuts down but actual water temperature is normal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check for minimum flow** — Confirm the fixture demand is above the unit's minimum flow rate. Clean or replace aerators and showerheads that may be restricting flow below the activation threshold.
2. **Inspect the cold water inlet filter** — Navien units have a small in-line filter screen at the cold water inlet. A clogged filter screen reduces flow significantly — remove and clean it.
3. **Flush the heat exchanger** — If the unit is more than 2 years old in a hard water area (>7 grains/gallon), perform a vinegar descale flush through the service port valves. Use a circulation pump and at least 1 gallon of white vinegar for 30–60 minutes.
4. **Test the outlet thermistor** — With power off, disconnect the heat exchanger outlet thermistor and measure resistance. At 25°C, expect approximately 10 kΩ (NTC thermistor). A reading significantly different from the temperature-resistance curve in the service manual indicates a failed thermistor.
5. **Reset and test** — After repairs, press Power off/on or hold the Reset button. Run the unit at full demand and monitor for E009 recurrence.

## When to Call a Pro

If descaling and flow restoration do not resolve E009, the heat exchanger may have suffered damage from prolonged scale buildup. A licensed plumber should assess whether the heat exchanger needs replacement and whether a water softener or scale inhibitor should be added upstream of the unit.
