## Maytag Dishwasher F4E3 Error — What It Means

The F4E3 error code on a Maytag dishwasher signals a circulation or heating system fault, most commonly tied to the wash motor or circulation pump. On Whirlpool-platform dishwashers (which Maytag uses), this code typically means the control board is not seeing the expected performance from the wash motor during the cycle. The machine may fill with water but fail to spray or wash. Maytag's own support page only recommends a power reset, but field service data shows the fault usually points to a failed motor, a seized impeller, or a blockage in the sump or pump inlet preventing the motor from running.

## Common Causes

- **Failed circulation pump or wash motor** The motor windings burn out, the rotor locks, or the pump assembly mechanically fails and cannot turn water through the spray arms.
- **Obstruction in the sump, filter, or pump inlet** Debris, broken glass, food labels, or scale buildup jams the impeller or blocks water flow, preventing the motor from starting or running under load.
- **Loose or corroded wiring and connectors** Wire terminals at the motor or control board become loose, corroded, or heat-damaged, breaking communication or power delivery to the pump.
- **Control board failure or drive circuit fault** The main control cannot drive the motor correctly or does not receive feedback that the pump is running, triggering the fault even if the motor is mechanically sound.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Cycle power** by turning off the circuit breaker or unplugging the dishwasher for one full minute, then restore power and attempt a cycle to see if the code clears.
2. **Run a diagnostic cycle** (consult your model's service sheet) and observe whether the fault returns immediately or only when the wash motor should activate.
3. **Inspect the sump, filters, and spray arm path** for debris, broken items, or buildup by removing the lower rack and filter assembly and clearing any obstructions.
4. **Check the circulation pump motor** by listening and feeling for vibration during the wash portion. If the motor does not run and the tub is filled, suspect motor or blockage.
5. **Inspect the wire harness and connectors** at the circulation motor and control board for loose pins, corrosion, or heat marks, and reseat or replace damaged connectors.
6. **Test or replace the circulation pump assembly** if the motor is mechanically free, wiring is intact, and the pump still does not run when commanded by the control.
7. **Evaluate the control board** if the motor tests good and all wiring checks out, as the fault may originate from a failed motor-drive relay or feedback circuit on the board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the power reset does not clear the code, if you are not comfortable working with electrical connections under the dishwasher, or if you cannot determine whether the fault is in the motor or the control board. A technician has the diagnostic software, wiring diagrams, and test equipment to pinpoint whether the circulation pump, harness, or control is at fault. If your dishwasher is still under warranty, contact Maytag service directly before replacing any parts.
