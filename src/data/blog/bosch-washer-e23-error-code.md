## Bosch Washer E23 Error Code — What It Means

The E23 code on a Bosch washer indicates that the machine has detected water in the base pan or leak tray, which is part of the AquaStop leak-detection safety system. The control board is responding to a leak detection event rather than identifying the exact failed component. Water in the base pan can come from an actual leak in hoses or seals, a blocked drain that causes overflow, or leftover water from a previous spill or transport event. The safety system will not release until the base is dry and the source is addressed.

## Common Causes

- **Actual leak from hoses or seals** Inlet hoses, hose connections, door boot, detergent dispenser plumbing, tub-to-pump lines, or pump seals can develop leaks that drip into the base pan and trip the sensor.
- **Clogged drain pump or filter** Debris, lint, coins, or other objects blocking the pump or filter prevent proper drainage and cause water to back up and overflow into the base area.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose** A drain hose that is kinked, crushed, or obstructed at the standpipe or sink trap restricts water flow and can lead to spills into the base tray.
- **Water left in the base pan** Water from a prior overflow, tilt during transport, or previous leak event can remain in the base pan and keep the fault latched until the tray is completely dry.
- **Faulty leak sensor or float switch** A defective leak sensor, float switch, or related wiring in the base pan can falsely signal water presence even when the base is dry.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power and water supply** before opening any panels or handling plumbing to make sure safe working conditions.
2. **Tilt the washer back carefully** and remove the bottom front or rear access panel to expose the base pan area.
3. **Remove all water from the base pan** using towels or a shop vacuum, and dry the tray completely so the sensor can reset.
4. **Inspect all hoses and seals** for leaks by checking the inlet hoses, door boot, detergent drawer area, drain hose, and pump housing for moisture, mineral trails, or visible damage.
5. **Clean the drain pump filter** by unscrewing the access cover, removing the filter or coin trap, clearing out lint and debris, and verifying the impeller spins freely by hand.
6. **Check the drain hose routing** for kinks, blockage, or loose connections at the back of the machine and at the standpipe or sink drain.
7. **Reassemble and run a power cycle** by plugging the machine back in and running a short rinse or spin cycle to confirm the E23 code is cleared and no new leaks appear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the E23 code returns immediately after drying the base pan and no visible leak is found, or if you are uncomfortable tilting the machine and accessing internal plumbing. A technician can perform component-level diagnostics on the leak sensor wiring and control board inputs, and can identify hidden leaks in hard-to-reach areas like the tub-to-pump assembly or internal dispenser plumbing. If your washer is still under warranty or covered by a Bosch service plan, professional service may be included at no cost.
