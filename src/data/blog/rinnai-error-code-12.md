## Rinnai Error Code 12 — What It Means

Rinnai error code 12 indicates flame failure — the unit ignited but the flame was not detected within the required period, or was detected and then lost before the end of the heating call. Rinnai uses a flame rod (ionization sensor) to confirm combustion. If the rod doesn't detect adequate ionization current, the gas valve closes and the unit retries. After multiple failed attempts, it locks out with code 12. This is one of the most common Rinnai fault codes and is usually caused by gas supply issues, a fouled flame rod, or venting problems.

## Common Causes

- **Gas supply interruption** — Low line pressure, a partially closed shutoff, or an undersized gas supply line to the unit are frequent causes. At full firing rate, tankless units demand significant gas flow — often 150,000–199,000 BTU/hr.
- **Dirty flame rod** — The ionization rod must make electrical contact with the flame. Carbon or oxide buildup on the rod tip reduces the signal below the detection threshold.
- **Blocked or improper venting** — A blocked exhaust or intake vent prevents the combustion fan from proving pressure, which either prevents ignition attempts or creates combustion instability that extinguishes the flame.
- **Moisture ingress into igniter circuit** — Water from condensation or a leak can bridge the spark circuit, causing intermittent ignition and code 12 during cold weather or high-humidity conditions.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check gas supply** — At the unit, confirm the manual gas valve handle is parallel to the gas line (open). If other gas appliances in the home are working normally, your supply is likely adequate.
2. **Reset the unit** — Press the On/Off button twice to clear the code. Observe the startup: you should hear the fan spin up, then clicking (ignition), then the burner flame sound.
3. **Inspect the flame rod** — Access the burner section. The flame rod is a stainless probe positioned in the flame. Clean the rod tip with steel wool — remove all visible oxidation. Reinstall.
4. **Check vent terminations** — Inspect both intake and exhaust at the exterior. Clear any blockage. Verify that the exhaust and intake termination caps haven't been crushed or blocked by vegetation.
5. **Reset the system** — Run a hot water demand of at least 2 GPM. If the unit ignites and holds flame through the full draw without code 12, the fix worked.

## When to Call a Pro

Gas supply capacity testing and burner combustion analysis require a licensed gas technician. If code 12 appears only at full flow rates, the gas line may be undersized — a tech can verify with a manometer.
