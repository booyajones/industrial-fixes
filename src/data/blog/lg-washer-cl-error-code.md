## LG Washer CL Error Code — What It Means

On LG washers, CL is not a fault code. It stands for CHILD LOCK and means the control panel is intentionally locked during a cycle. When Child Lock is active, the washer will not accept most button inputs except power. This is a safety feature designed to prevent unintentional changes to cycle settings. The display may show 'CL' or a lock icon.

The control panel remains locked until you disable the Child Lock feature using the correct button combination for your model. This is a normal operating state, not a component failure.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes assume the control board or touchpad has failed when they see CL and the panel won't respond. Before replacing any parts, verify that Child Lock is simply active and follow the correct unlock procedure for your model.

## Common Causes

- **Child Lock was enabled (~85%)** The Child Lock or Control Lock feature was activated by pressing and holding the designated button or button pair for 3 to 5 seconds.
- **Accidental activation (~10%)** A household member or child accidentally held the lock buttons long enough to enable the feature without realizing it.
- **Sticky or stuck buttons (~3%)** Residue or debris around the control panel buttons caused them to register a long press and activate Child Lock.
- **Control board retaining lock state (~2%)** The main control board retained the Child Lock setting through a power interruption or cycle and did not reset automatically.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the display** shows CL or a lock icon and confirm the control panel is unresponsive to most buttons except power.
2. **Identify your model's unlock combination** by checking the control panel labeling or your owner's manual. Most LG washers use a dedicated Child Lock button or a pair of marked buttons.
3. **Press and hold the Child Lock button** (or the two designated buttons) for 3 to 5 seconds until the CL disappears from the display.
4. **Check the panel response** by pressing a cycle or option button to confirm the lock is disabled and the washer accepts input.
5. **Power-cycle the washer** if CL does not clear. Unplug the washer or switch off the circuit breaker, wait 30 seconds, restore power, and retry the unlock procedure.
6. **Recheck the lock state** after power is restored. If CL immediately returns, verify you are using the correct button combination and the washer is not in a cycle state that restricts lock changes.
7. **Inspect the control panel buttons** for sticky residue or debris that might cause accidental activation. Clean the panel gently with a damp cloth if needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have followed the correct unlock procedure for your model (pressing and holding the Child Lock button or button pair for 3 to 5 seconds), power-cycled the washer, and the CL code still will not clear or immediately returns. A technician can test the control panel button assembly and main control board with a multimeter to identify whether a component has failed. Also call if the control panel buttons are visibly damaged, cracked, or unresponsive in ways beyond the Child Lock feature, as this may require panel or board replacement.
