## Scotsman HID525 Error Codes — Quick Reference

The Scotsman HID525 is a hotel ice dispenser that combines ice making, storage, and dispensing in one compact unit. It uses a Scotsman electronic controller that tracks water fill, freeze cycle, harvest cycle, and bin control faults. Most service issues relate to water quality, scaling, and dispensing obstructions.

## Most Common Codes

### Fill Fault
The unit did not bring water to the correct level in time. Check the incoming water filter, inlet valve screen, and supply pressure.

### Freeze Fault
Freeze cycle exceeded the time limit. Dirty condenser coils and scale buildup are the two most common causes. HID units in hotels often go too long without preventive maintenance.

### Harvest Fault
Ice didn't release from the evaporator when the machine switched into harvest. Scale on the plate and weak hot gas valve performance are typical causes.

### Bin Full Fault
The dispenser thinks the bin is full or jammed. Remove the front panel and inspect for bridged ice or a stuck bin sensor.

### Dispense Fault
The auger or dispense motor is jammed or overloaded. Clear the ice chute and verify the dispense motor shaft turns freely.

## When to Call a Pro
If the HID525 is clean and still showing freeze or harvest faults, the sealed refrigeration system may have a weak compressor or low charge. That calls for a commercial ice machine tech.
