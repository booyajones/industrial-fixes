## Ice-O-Matic GEMU090 Error Codes — What It Means

The Ice-O-Matic GEMU090 is an undercounter pearl ice machine. It uses electronic controls to monitor water level, auger drive, condenser temperature, and harvest timing. Faults on this model usually trace to scaling, water quality, or auger load issues.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Low water / fill fault** — Water valve restricted or inlet screen clogged.
- **High temperature fault** — Dirty condenser or failed condenser fan.
- **Auger overload** — Mineral scale or ice jam causing the auger motor to work too hard.
- **Sensor fault** — Water level or temperature probe reading out of range.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Clean the condenser and verify airflow through the grille.
2. Inspect the water filter, inlet valve, and inlet screen.
3. Descale the evaporator and auger system using manufacturer-approved nickel-safe cleaner.
4. Check for ice bridging in the extrusion path.
5. Test thermistors and water level sensors per the service manual.
6. Restart the unit and monitor cycle timing.

## When to Call a Pro
Persistent high-temperature or auger-overload faults after cleaning usually point to motor wear or refrigeration issues. A pearl ice specialist is worth calling before the auger and evaporator are damaged.
