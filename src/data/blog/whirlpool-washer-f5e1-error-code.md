## Whirlpool Washer F5E1 Error Code — What It Means

F5E1 means the control cannot tell whether the lid or door is locked or unlocked. On top load washers it points to the lid lock or lid switch. On front load washers it points to the door lock assembly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Blocked lid or door** Clothing or an object in the path keeps the lid or door from closing fully.
- **Failed lid switch** A lid switch with no continuity cannot tell the control the lid is shut.
- **Worn lock assembly** A lid lock or door lock that no longer latches will not report a locked state.
- **Loose lock wiring** A frayed or loose harness between the lock and control board breaks the signal.
- **Control panel fault** In rarer cases the control board misreads the lock and shows F5E1.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer for at least one minute to attempt a reset.
2. Inspect the lid or door area and clear any clothing or object blocking the latch.
3. Plug back in and run a cycle to see whether the lock engages.
4. If the code returns, unplug again and disconnect the lid switch or lock from the board.
5. Set a meter to ohms and test for continuity with the lid open and then closed.
6. Inspect the wiring harness for frayed wires or corrosion and repair as needed.
7. Replace the lid lock or door lock assembly with an OEM part matched to your model.

## When to Call a Pro

If the lock tests good and a new assembly does not clear F5E1, the control board may be the cause. Call a technician if you are not comfortable testing switches or replacing a control board.
