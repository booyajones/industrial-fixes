## Samsung Dishwasher PE Error — What It Means

The PE error on Samsung dishwashers indicates a half-load or zone-wash diverter fault. The control board cannot verify that the water-routing diverter or dividing valve system is switching spray flow correctly during the wash cycle. This typically means the inner spray arm tied to the diverter is not responding as expected, or the system cannot detect the diverter's position. The dishwasher flags the function as failed and stops the cycle to prevent poor wash performance or component damage.

## Common Causes

- **Failed diverter motor or assembly** The motor that switches water flow between wash zones has burned out or become mechanically stuck, so the control cannot move it.
- **Mechanical jam in the diverter path** Food debris, broken plastic, or mineral buildup inside the diverter valve prevents it from moving fully between positions.
- **Position-sensing or feedback failure** The control board cannot confirm the diverter moved because the position sensor or feedback circuit is not working.
- **Loose or damaged wiring to the diverter** Harness connectors at the diverter motor or related assemblies are disconnected, corroded, or broken, blocking communication with the board.
- **Control board interpretation fault** The diverter and wiring test good, but the power board or main control misreads the feedback signal and sets PE anyway.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the dishwasher** by turning off the breaker for 60 seconds, then turn it back on and run a test cycle to see if PE returns.
2. **Inspect the sump, filter, and drain path** for blockage or restriction that could prevent proper water flow and diverter operation.
3. **Check the inner spray arm and diverter function** by manually rotating the arm if accessible and looking for jamming or unusual resistance during a cycle.
4. **Remove the lower access panel** and locate the diverter assembly and motor near the sump or pump housing.
5. **Inspect wiring and connectors** at the diverter motor for loose, corroded, or damaged terminals and push each connector firmly home.
6. **Test the diverter motor** electrically and mechanically, checking for continuity and free movement, and replace the diverter assembly if it fails.
7. **Replace the control or power board** if the diverter and wiring pass all checks but the PE error persists, since the board may be misreading feedback.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working inside the dishwasher base or if the PE error returns after you have cleared blockages and confirmed the diverter moves freely. Diagnosing the diverter motor and control board requires multimeter skills and access to the correct test points. A qualified appliance tech can quickly isolate whether the fault is in the diverter assembly, wiring, or control electronics and replace only the failed part.
