## LG Refrigerator dS Error Code — What It Means

The dS code on your LG refrigerator signals a defrost sensor error. The control board has detected either a short circuit or an open (disconnected) defrost sensor in one of the evaporator circuits. You may see it displayed as ER dS on single-evaporator models, F dS when the freezer evaporator sensor is faulty on dual-evaporator models, or r dS when the refrigerator evaporator sensor has failed. This is an electrical sensor-circuit fault, not a problem with the defrost heater or refrigerant system. LG specifies that this error requires professional service because it involves evaporator access and electrical diagnostics.

## Common Causes

- **Failed defrost sensor or thermistor** The sensor itself has developed an internal short or open circuit and no longer sends valid readings to the control board.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness** The connector at the defrost sensor has come unplugged, or the wiring has been pinched, cut, or corroded, breaking electrical continuity.
- **Moisture intrusion at the sensor connector** Water or condensation has entered the plug and corroded the terminals, creating an intermittent or permanent open circuit.
- **Ice or frost buildup around the evaporator** Excessive frost can physically damage the sensor or its wiring, though the code itself always indicates an electrical sensor fault.
- **Control board issue** In rare cases the main control board's sensor-input circuit has failed, though LG documentation centers on the sensor and its wiring as the primary suspects.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Identify the exact code variant** displayed: ER dS, F dS, or r dS, which tells you whether the fault is in the single evaporator, freezer evaporator, or refrigerator evaporator circuit.
2. **Disconnect power** at the wall or breaker, then remove the interior panels to access the evaporator compartment for the affected circuit.
3. **Inspect the defrost sensor and its connector** for physical damage, loose plugs, pinched wires, corrosion, or moisture intrusion.
4. **Test the defrost sensor resistance** with a multimeter and compare the reading to the resistance table in your model's service manual, looking for an open circuit (infinite resistance) or short (near-zero resistance) that confirms sensor failure.
5. **Replace the defrost sensor or repair the harness** if testing confirms an out-of-spec reading or visible damage to wiring or terminals.
6. **Reassemble the evaporator cover**, restore power, and monitor the display to confirm the code clears and the refrigerator resumes normal defrost cycles.
7. **Clear any accumulated frost or ice** from the evaporator area if present, since the faulty sensor may have allowed abnormal buildup during the fault period.

## When to Call a Pro

LG officially designates the dS code as a service-required error. The repair involves accessing sealed evaporator compartments, working with live electrical circuits, and interpreting model-specific resistance tables for the defrost sensor. Unless you have appliance-repair experience and a multimeter, call a qualified technician to diagnose the sensor circuit, replace the defrost thermistor if needed, and verify that the control board is reading correctly. Attempting the repair without proper access or testing can damage the evaporator cover, void warranties, or misdiagnose a wiring issue as a sensor failure.
