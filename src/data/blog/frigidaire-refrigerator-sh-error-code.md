## Frigidaire Refrigerator SH Error Code — What It Means

SH shows on the temperature display when a temperature sensor, also called a thermistor, has shorted out. It appears on the freezer display for the freezer sensor and on the fresh food display for the refrigerator sensor.

With a shorted sensor the control board gets a bad temperature reading and cannot hold the right temperature. The fix is checking the sensor wiring and the thermistor resistance.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Shorted thermistor** The temperature sensor has failed internally and reads outside its normal resistance range.
- **Pinched sensor wiring** A wire rubbing against metal can short the sensor circuit to ground.
- **Moisture in the connector** Water in the sensor plug can bridge the contacts and mimic a short.
- **Damaged wire harness** Crushed or melted insulation can short two sensor wires together.
- **Faulty control board input** If the sensor tests good, the board sensor input may be at fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator before touching any wiring.
2. Locate the sensor for the display showing SH, freezer or fresh food.
3. Disconnect the sensor harness and inspect it for pinched or wet wires.
4. Measure the thermistor resistance with a multimeter.
5. Compare the reading to spec, near 32,500 ohms at 32 degrees and 11,300 ohms at room temperature.
6. Replace the thermistor if the resistance is near zero or far off spec.
7. Dry and reseat the connector, then restore power and confirm the code is gone.

## When to Call a Pro

If both the sensor and its wiring test within spec but SH keeps coming back, the sensor input on the control board may be damaged. Board level testing is a reasonable point to call a technician.
