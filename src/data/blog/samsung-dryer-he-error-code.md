## Samsung Dryer hE Error Code — What It Means

The hE error code on a Samsung dryer indicates a heating fault. The machine has detected that it is not heating correctly, is heating too slowly, or is overheating during the cycle. Related codes like HC or HC4 also point to temperature or overheat issues tied to thermistor feedback or an actual over-temperature condition. In most cases, the dryer's control board is receiving abnormal temperature data or the unit is running hotter than safe limits allow. This error can appear on both electric and gas models, though the underlying heating components differ between the two.

## Common Causes

- **Restricted airflow** Clogged lint screen, blocked vent hose, crushed ducting, or a restricted exterior vent hood reduces exhaust and causes overheating.
- **Dirty lint filter with residue** Fabric softener and dryer sheet buildup on the screen blocks airflow even when the filter looks visually clean.
- **Failing thermistor or thermostat** A faulty temperature sensor sends incorrect data to the control board, making the dryer think it is overheating.
- **Failed thermal fuse or high-limit device** An open safety fuse from a previous overheat event can trigger the code or prevent proper heating.
- **Heating element fault (electric models)** A grounded, broken, or weak heating element produces abnormal heating behavior and code recurrence.
- **Gas valve or igniter issue (gas models)** A weak or failing gas valve assembly or igniter can cause improper heating when airflow and sensors test normal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Clear the code** by unplugging the dryer for 30 seconds, then run a short test cycle to confirm the fault repeats.
2. **Clean the lint screen thoroughly**, washing it with warm soapy water if you see any waxy residue or film, then rinse and dry completely.
3. **Disconnect and inspect the entire vent system**, checking for blockages, kinks, long restrictive runs, or a clogged exterior termination, and clean or replace as needed.
4. **Test the thermistor and thermostat** with a multimeter for correct resistance at room temperature (consult your model's service manual for the exact values) and inspect connectors for damage.
5. **Check continuity on all thermal fuses and high-limit devices** with power disconnected, replacing any that are open.
6. **Inspect the heating element** (electric) for continuity and for a short to ground, or inspect the igniter and gas valve circuit (gas) if airflow and sensors are good.
7. **Replace faulty components** one at a time, starting with the most likely culprit based on your tests, and retest the dryer after each repair.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the code returns after you have cleaned the entire vent system and lint filter, or if you are not comfortable using a multimeter to test electrical components with power disconnected. Gas dryer repairs involving the valve, burner assembly, or gas connections should always be handled by a qualified appliance technician or gas service professional. If you have replaced the thermistor, thermal fuse, and heating element (or gas valve) and the code persists, the issue may be in the control board or wiring harness and requires a diagnostic approach with the service manual and proper test equipment.
