## State E128 Error Code — What It Means

The E128 code on State (A. O. Smith family) tankless water heaters indicates an ignition failure, flame failure, or combustion interruption. The control board detects that the unit cannot light, hold flame, or properly sense combustion. Because State does not publish a universal E128 definition across all models, always pull the exact meaning from your unit's manual using the QR code on the rating plate. In general, this class of fault points to a problem in the combustion path rather than a water-side component.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted air intake** Lint, dust, nests, or debris covering the air inlet screen prevents adequate combustion air from reaching the burner.
- **Venting restriction or obstruction** Exhaust vent termination blocked by snow, leaves, or improper installation causes combustion gases to back up and shut down ignition.
- **Insufficient gas supply or low gas pressure** Partially closed gas valve, undersized line, or regulator fault delivers too little fuel for ignition or stable flame.
- **Dirty or failed flame sensor** Carbon buildup or corrosion on the flame rod prevents the board from confirming ignition, so the sequence aborts.
- **Faulty igniter or ignition transformer** Worn igniter electrode or failed transformer cannot create the spark needed to light the gas.
- **Scale buildup disrupting combustion** Mineral deposits inside the heat exchanger alter flame geometry enough to interfere with sensing or ignition stability.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the unit** at the circuit breaker and close the manual gas shutoff valve before beginning any inspection or service work.
2. **Inspect the air intake screen and exhaust vent termination** for blockage, remove any lint or debris, and confirm both pathways are clear and meet code clearances.
3. **Verify gas supply** by confirming the main and appliance shutoff valves are fully open, then check upstream appliances to rule out a building-wide low-pressure condition.
4. **Remove and clean the flame sensor** using fine-grit sandpaper or a soft cloth to remove soot and oxidation, taking care not to bend the probe.
5. **Check the igniter electrode** for cracks, heavy carbon, or gap misalignment, and clean or replace it if damaged.
6. **Measure inlet gas pressure** with a manometer while the unit attempts ignition, and compare the reading to the pressure table in your model manual.
7. **Restart the unit** and observe the ignition sequence; if the code returns, consult your model-specific manual via the rating-plate QR code or call a licensed technician to test the gas valve and control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you find no obvious blockage and the error persists after cleaning the flame sensor and verifying gas supply. Diagnosing gas-valve output, control-board signals, and combustion-chamber pressure requires a manometer, multimeter, and familiarity with your exact model's ignition sequence. Because State/A. O. Smith does not publish a single E128 definition for all tankless units, a pro can decode your specific model via the service manual and safely test live gas and electrical circuits. Any work involving gas-line pressure testing or control-board replacement should be performed by a qualified service provider.
