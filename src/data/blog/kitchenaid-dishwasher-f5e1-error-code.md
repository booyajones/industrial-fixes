## KitchenAid Dishwasher F5E1 Error Code — What It Means

F5E1 is a door switch fault. The dishwasher will not run a cycle until the control confirms the door is latched and the switch is closed.

A misaligned door, a failed latch switch, or a broken wire are the usual reasons the control never sees the door close.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Door not seating fully** A racked or overloaded lower basket keeps the door from closing all the way.
- **Failed door latch switch** The microswitch in the latch no longer closes when the door shuts.
- **Broken latch mechanism** A cracked latch hook does not engage the strike, so the switch never trips.
- **Loose switch wiring** A disconnected or corroded plug at the latch breaks the signal to the control.
- **Misaligned door or strike** A sagging door or shifted strike plate misses the latch engagement point.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Disconnect power before removing the inner door panel.
2. Confirm nothing in the racks blocks the door from closing fully.
3. Open and firmly close the door and listen for the latch to click into the strike.
4. Remove the inner door panel and inspect the latch assembly for cracks.
5. Reseat the latch switch wiring and check the harness for damage.
6. Test the latch switch for continuity as the latch engages and replace the latch assembly if it does not switch.
7. Reassemble, restore power, and run a short cycle to confirm the code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

If the latch engages and the switch tests good but F5E1 stays, the door switch input on the control board may have failed. A technician can confirm the board and check for a wiring fault inside the door.
