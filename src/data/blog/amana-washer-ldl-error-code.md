## Amana Ld Error Code — What It Means

The Ld code on Amana top-load washers stands for long drain. It means the control board detected that water is taking too long to leave the tub during a drain phase. Amana calls this a drain pump system problem, and the washer stops the cycle when the water level drops too slowly.

In most cases the issue is not the pump itself but the way the drain hose is installed. The code will repeat on every drain attempt until you fix the restriction or installation problem.

## Common Causes

- **Drain hose pushed too far into standpipe** Amana specifies the hose must not extend more than 4.5 in (113 mm) into the drainpipe or water cannot vent and flow slows.
- **Standpipe height too low** The top of the standpipe must be at least 39 in (99 cm) high to prevent siphoning during the wash cycle.
- **Drain hose kinked, crushed, or clogged** A physical restriction in the hose blocks flow and causes the tub to drain slowly.
- **Drain hose taped or sealed to standpipe** Amana requires a gap for airflow and specifies the U-shape drain hose form be used without taping the hose into the pipe.
- **Debris or blockage in pump inlet or impeller** Small objects, lint, or a broken impeller can restrict flow even when hose installation is correct.
- **Weak or failed drain pump** If the hose path is clear and installation meets spec, the pump motor itself may no longer move water fast enough.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have corrected the hose installation (depth, height, form, and no taping), cleared all visible blockages, and the Ld code still returns. At that point the pump or internal drain path likely needs disassembly and replacement. Also call if you are not comfortable disconnecting hoses or tipping the washer to access the pump, or if the washer is still under warranty and you want factory-authorized service.
