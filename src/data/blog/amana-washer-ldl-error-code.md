## Amana Ld Error Code — What It Means

On Amana top-load washers, Ld means a long-drain fault. The washer has detected that water is taking too long to drain from the tub. Amana's own help pages also list related drain-code variants such as drn, dLO, and LdL or LdU, so the exact display format can vary by model, but Ld itself is the long-drain fault on the washer platform.

This is not, by itself, a bad board code. It is a drain performance fault until the drain path and pump are verified. The machine is telling you to check the hose installation, the drain path, and the pump before moving to any control or sensor diagnostics.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board first. Check the drain hose installation and clear any blockage at the pump inlet before ordering any electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Drain hose installation wrong (~35%)** Hose pushed more than 4.5 inches into the standpipe or hose end below 36 inches from the floor, which interferes with drainage or causes siphoning.
- **Kinked or clogged drain hose (~25%)** Hose is crushed, pinched, or blocked inside, preventing water from leaving the tub.
- **Drain path blockage at pump inlet (~25%)** Lint, socks, coins, or other debris lodged in the pump inlet, outlet, or trap area, which is a very common service finding.
- **Failed drain pump (~12%)** Pump motor or impeller is bad and cannot move water even when the drain path is clear.
- **Control or level-sensing issue (~3%)** Main control or pressure switch fault, but only after the drain system and pump have been verified clear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and verify the symptom is a true drain fault, not a transient interruption or paused cycle.
2. **Inspect the drain hose installation.** Confirm the hose extends no more than 4.5 inches into the standpipe, the hose outlet is above 36 inches from the floor, and the hose is secured with the drain hose form and not taped over the drain opening.
3. **Check the hose for kinks, crushing, or clogs.** Disconnect the hose and flush it with water. Clear any obstruction and make sure the hose is not pinched behind the machine.
4. **Inspect the pump inlet, outlet, and trap area for debris.** Remove the access panel (usually front or rear lower), pull out any socks, lint, coins, or other blockages, and clean the pump screen if your model has one.
5. **Run the drain function again** after clearing the drain path. If the washer drains properly, reassemble and test a full cycle.
6. **Test or replace the drain pump** if the hose path is clear and the pump still does not move water. Listen for the pump motor running. If it hums or does not spin, replace the pump.
7. **Verify control and sensor operation** if the machine continues to show Ld after a verified clear drain path and pump check. Consult your model's service manual for diagnostic mode and pressure-switch tests.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have verified the drain hose installation is correct, cleared all blockages from the hose and pump, tested or replaced the pump, and the Ld code still returns. At that point the fault is likely in the control board or pressure-switch circuit and requires diagnostic mode testing and model-specific service procedures. Also call if you are not comfortable removing the washer panels, accessing the pump, or working around 120 V AC wiring.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts (if pump or hose needed), 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300 service call.
