## State Water Heater E117 Error Code — What It Means

The E117 error code does not appear in the accessible State water heater service documentation for residential electric models. State is manufactured by A. O. Smith, and their published fault tables for electric units list codes E01 through E09 but do not include E117. This suggests the code may be specific to a particular model line, a gas unit, or a commercial unit not covered in the residential electric troubleshooting guides available.

To identify what E117 means for your specific unit, locate the rating plate on your water heater and scan the QR code or note the full model and serial number. Use that information to download the correct manual from the manufacturer's website or contact State technical support directly. Without the model-specific documentation, attempting repairs based on general advice risks misdiagnosis and may void your warranty.

## Common Causes

- **Model-specific fault not in general documentation** The code may be unique to certain State models or product lines not covered in widely available residential electric manuals.
- **Gas or commercial unit code** E117 may apply to gas-fired or commercial water heaters that use different fault tables than residential electric units.
- **Thermistor or sensor fault** Many State electric codes reference thermistor or sensor issues, so E117 could indicate a temperature sensor problem if your model uses that numbering.
- **Electronic thermostat (ET) communication error** Some codes in the State line indicate communication or logic faults within the electronic thermostat board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Locate the rating plate** on the side of your water heater and write down the complete model number and serial number.
2. **Scan the QR code** on the rating plate with your smartphone to access the model-specific manual and troubleshooting guide directly from the manufacturer.
3. **Look up E117** in the fault code table of your model's manual to identify the exact component or condition triggering the error.
4. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker before inspecting any electrical components or sensors inside the water heater.
5. **Follow the model-specific diagnostic steps** in the manual, which may include checking thermistor resistance, inspecting wiring connections, or testing the electronic thermostat.
6. **Reset the unit** after repairs by restoring power and observing whether the code clears and the heater resumes normal operation.
7. **Contact State technical support** at the number in your manual if the code persists or if you cannot locate model-specific documentation for E117.
