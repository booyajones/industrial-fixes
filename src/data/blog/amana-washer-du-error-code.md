## What this code means
On Amana top-load HE washers, the dU error code (sometimes shown as LdU) means the lid cannot unlock. The washer's control has attempted to unlock the lid, but the lid-lock system did not report a successful unlock condition. Amana states the machine detects an obstruction on the lid or around the top that prevents unlocking, so the cycle stops with the dU fault.

This code tells you the lock mechanism either cannot physically release or cannot send the correct unlock signal back to the control board. The fault can be as simple as something sitting on top of the washer, or as involved as a failed lock assembly or wiring problem in the lid-lock circuit.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first, but the lid-lock assembly and its wiring are the most direct causes of a dU fault. Always inspect the lock mechanism, check the harness connections, and test the lock assembly before replacing the control.

## Common Causes

- **Failed lid-lock assembly (~50%)** The lock mechanism cannot release or report unlock status correctly, preventing the control from seeing a successful unlock.
- **Physical obstruction on the lid or top deck (~25%)** A laundry basket, detergent bottle, or other object resting on the washer lid prevents the lock from releasing.
- **Wiring or connector problem at the lid-lock harness (~15%)** Loose, corroded, or broken wires at the lock or control-board connector interrupt the unlock signal or actuator command.
- **Main control board fault (~10%)** The control's lock/unlock output or sense circuit fails, even though the lock assembly and wiring test good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Press START/PAUSE** to attempt to clear the dU code and restart the cycle, per Amana's system-error guidance.
2. **Remove any obstructions** from the top of the washer, including laundry baskets, detergent containers, or other items that may be pressing on the lid.
3. **Unplug the washer** and open the top panel or cabinet to access the lid-lock assembly at the top of the washer tub.
4. **Inspect the lid-lock mechanism and strike** for physical damage, debris, misalignment, or a broken actuator that would prevent the lock from releasing.
5. **Unplug and reseat the lid-lock harness** at both the lock assembly and the main control board, checking for broken wires, corrosion, or loose pins.
6. **Test the lid-lock assembly electrically** using a multimeter if you have the service manual's specs, or replace the lock assembly if the mechanism does not unlock reliably or feedback is inconsistent.
7. **If the new lock and wiring are good**, enter the washer's diagnostic mode to verify the control sees the lid-lock circuit correctly, then evaluate the main control board as the final suspect.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable working inside the washer cabinet, if you cannot access or identify the lid-lock assembly, or if you have replaced the lock and checked all wiring but the dU code persists. A qualified service tech can enter the washer's diagnostic mode, measure the lock circuit directly, and determine whether the main control board needs replacement. Professional diagnosis is also warranted if the washer is still under warranty or if you see signs of water damage or corrosion inside the cabinet that may indicate a larger problem.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–$90 for lid-lock assembly, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–$250 including service call and part.
