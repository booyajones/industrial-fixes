## State Water Heater E08 Error Code — What It Means

The E08 error code on State water heaters is not documented in State's standard residential tank-style manuals. If your unit is a tankless RV-style water heater sold under the State brand, E08 follows the same logic as similar RV tankless models and indicates an air pressure switch or exhaust vent fault. The heater is not proving that airflow or draft is correct before or during ignition, so it shuts down to prevent unsafe operation.

This code means the control board expects a signal from the air pressure switch confirming proper ventilation, but that signal is either missing or intermittent. The fault can be electrical (bad switch, open wire) or mechanical (blocked vent, kinked hose). Until the system proves safe airflow, the burner will not light.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty air pressure switch** The pressure switch itself has failed internally and no longer closes its contacts even when airflow is correct.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness** Connector terminals between the pressure switch and control board are corroded, loose, or the wiring has an open circuit.
- **Clogged or kinked pressure switch hose** The small tube that senses differential pressure is blocked by debris, pinched, or disconnected from the switch or venturi.
- **Blocked exhaust vent or air inlet** Lint, insect nests, or other obstructions in the vent pipe prevent proper draft and the pressure switch cannot prove airflow.
- **Control board not supplying voltage** The controller fails to send the expected signal or power to the pressure switch circuit, so the switch never energizes.
- **Poor 12 V supply or bad ground on RV units** Low battery voltage or a corroded ground connection prevents the control system from operating the pressure switch circuit correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and gas** at the water heater's disconnect switch and gas valve before any inspection or repair work.
2. **Verify supply voltage and ground** using a multimeter if your unit is a 12 V RV-style heater, checking for full voltage and a clean ground connection at the control board.
3. **Inspect the exhaust vent and air inlet** for blockage, nests, or debris, and clear any obstructions you find in the vent pipe or termination.
4. **Examine the pressure switch hose** for kinks, disconnections, or internal clogs, and blow through it gently to confirm it is open and properly attached at both ends.
5. **Check the wiring harness and connectors** between the pressure switch and control board for loose terminals, corrosion, or broken wires, and test continuity with a multimeter.
6. **Measure voltage at the pressure switch terminals** (or the controller's switch output pins if accessible) to confirm the board is sending the expected signal when the unit calls for heat.
7. **Replace the air pressure switch** if all wiring, hoses, and venting are intact and the switch does not close when the blower runs, or replace the control board if no output voltage is present.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are not comfortable working with gas appliances, low-voltage wiring, or venting systems. If the error persists after you have cleared the vent, checked all hoses and wiring, and replaced the pressure switch, the control board or a deeper combustion-air issue may be at fault and requires diagnostic equipment. A pro can also verify that your unit's make and model actually uses the E08 code in the way described here, since State's standard residential tank manuals do not list an E08 fault and mixing brands can lead to incorrect repairs.
