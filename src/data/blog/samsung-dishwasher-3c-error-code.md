## Samsung Dishwasher 3C Error Code — What It Means

The 3C code is a pump fault on the wash side. The circulation pump that drives water up to the spray arms is not turning as the control expects.

Debris in the pump is the easiest cause to rule out, but a 3C that returns after cleaning usually means the circulation pump motor itself has failed.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Debris in the pump impeller** Glass, seeds, or hard food jam the circulation impeller so it cannot spin.
- **Failed circulation pump motor** A burned-out or seized wash pump motor will not run even with a clear path.
- **Loose pump wiring** A loose or corroded connector at the pump drops power and trips the fault.
- **Clogged filter restricting flow** A packed filter starves the pump and makes it labor or stall.
- **Trapped air or low water** A pump running dry or air-bound cannot build flow and posts the fault.
- **Control board fault** A board that cannot drive or read the pump can report 3C with a good pump.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cancel the cycle, disconnect power, and remove the bottom rack and filter.
2. Clear standing water, then check the sump and circulation pump intake for glass or debris.
3. Spin the impeller by hand if you can reach it and confirm it turns freely.
4. Reseat the pump wiring connector and look for corrosion or burnt pins.
5. Reset the dishwasher and run a short cycle to listen for the wash pump running.
6. If the pump stays silent with power present, test the motor windings with a multimeter.
7. Replace the circulation pump if it reads open or shorted, then retest the cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

If the impeller is clear and the pump tests good but 3C keeps coming back, the control board may not be driving the pump correctly. A technician can confirm power to the pump and rule the board in or out before you buy a new pump.
