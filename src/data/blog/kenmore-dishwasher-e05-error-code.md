## Kenmore Dishwasher E05 Error Code — What It Means

The E05 code does not have a single universal meaning across all Kenmore dishwashers because Kenmore is a brand label applied to units built by different manufacturers. If your Kenmore dishwasher is Bosch-built, E05 indicates a wash arm diverter circuit fault in the main control board. If your model was made by another OEM, the code may mean something different. The first step is to identify the actual manufacturer behind your specific Kenmore model number before interpreting the fault or ordering parts.

On Bosch-built Kenmore units, the E05 diverter circuit fault typically points to a problem in the control board, the diverter motor assembly, or the wiring harness connecting them. The wash arm diverter directs water flow to different spray arms during the cycle, and a circuit fault prevents the control board from properly commanding or sensing the diverter position. Many Kenmore Elite models also include a service diagnostic mode that lets you retrieve all stored fault codes by pressing any three keys in sequence three times, which helps confirm whether E05 is the only issue or part of a larger pattern.

## Common Causes

- **Diverter circuit board fault (Bosch-built units)** The main control board cannot communicate with or control the wash arm diverter motor, triggering the E05 code on Bosch platforms.
- **Loose or corroded diverter wiring harness** Connectors between the diverter assembly and the control board may be loose, corroded, or damaged, interrupting the circuit.
- **Failed diverter motor or assembly** The diverter motor itself may have shorted or seized, preventing proper operation and causing the control board to log a circuit fault.
- **Transient power glitch or control board memory error** A brief power surge or brown-out can store a false E05 code in memory, which a hard reset will clear if no actual component is damaged.
- **Wrong OEM platform assumed** If the dishwasher is not Bosch-built, E05 may have an entirely different meaning, so confirming the manufacturer behind the model number is essential.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Identify the OEM platform** by locating the full Kenmore model number on the door edge or tub side, then search online to determine whether it is Bosch, Whirlpool, or another manufacturer.
2. **Enter service diagnostic mode** on Kenmore Elite models by pressing any three keys in sequence three times with no more than one second between presses, then record all displayed codes by opening and closing the door and pressing Start.
3. **Perform a hard reset** by switching off the circuit breaker or unplugging the dishwasher for 30 seconds, then restore power and check whether E05 reappears.
4. **Inspect the diverter wiring harness** by opening the lower access panel and checking every connector between the diverter motor assembly and the main control board for corrosion, looseness, or pinched wires.
5. **Test the diverter motor** by disconnecting its harness and using a multimeter to check for continuity and proper resistance, comparing readings to your model's service sheet.
6. **Replace the main control board** if the diverter motor and wiring test normal but the E05 code persists after reset, indicating a fault in the board's diverter-circuit driver.
7. **Run a test cycle** after any repair to confirm the E05 code is cleared and the diverter operates correctly during all phases of the wash program.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you cannot confirm the OEM platform behind your Kenmore model, if service diagnostic mode does not display codes as expected, or if you are uncomfortable working with live mains voltage during control-board diagnostics. Also call for help if the E05 code returns after you have completed a hard reset, verified all diverter wiring connections, and tested the diverter motor without finding an obvious fault. Control-board and diverter-motor replacement both require partial disassembly of the tub interior and careful handling of ribbon cables and connectors, so professional service is worthwhile if you lack prior dishwasher repair experience or if the unit is still under warranty.
