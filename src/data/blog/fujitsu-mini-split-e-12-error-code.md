## Fujitsu E:12 Error Code — What It Means

The E:12 error code on Fujitsu mini-split systems indicates an indoor fan failure or indoor fan abnormal condition. This means the indoor unit's control board is not detecting the expected operation or feedback signal from the indoor blower fan. The system has detected that the fan motor is not running properly, is not receiving correct power, or the feedback circuit is open. The unit will typically shut down or refuse to run until the fault is cleared.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Loose or disconnected wiring** Connectors at the indoor fan motor or control board have come loose, corroded, or developed an open circuit.
- **Failed indoor fan motor** The fan motor windings have failed, the motor electronics are damaged, or internal bearings have seized.
- **Mechanically stuck blower wheel** Debris, ice buildup, or a damaged fan wheel is preventing the motor from rotating freely.
- **Defective indoor control PCB** The main controller board inside the indoor unit is not supplying the correct drive signal or voltage to the fan motor.
- **Damaged fan motor harness** Wiring between the PCB and motor has been pinched, cut, or shorted during installation or service.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker or disconnect and wait two minutes before opening the indoor unit cover to safely access the blower compartment.
2. **Inspect the indoor blower wheel** for any visible debris, ice, or physical damage and look for anything blocking rotation or rubbing against the housing.
3. **Try rotating the fan wheel by hand** to confirm it spins freely without binding or scraping, then remove any obstructions you find.
4. **Check all wire connectors** at the indoor fan motor and the indoor control board for loose pins, corrosion, or disconnected plugs and reseat each connector firmly.
5. **Test the fan motor electrically** by measuring resistance or checking for supply voltage at the motor harness connector according to your model's service manual values.
6. **Replace the indoor fan motor** if it shows open or shorted windings, or if the PCB is supplying correct voltage but the motor does not run.
7. **Replace the indoor control PCB** if the motor and wiring test good but the board is not delivering the expected drive voltage or control signal, then cycle power and verify normal fan operation in all modes.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with line-voltage wiring or if basic inspection does not reveal an obvious blockage or loose connector. Diagnosing E:12 accurately requires a multimeter, the correct service manual voltage and resistance tables for your specific Fujitsu model, and the ability to safely disassemble the indoor unit. If you replace a motor or PCB and the fault persists, a technician can verify that communication between indoor and outdoor units is intact and that no secondary faults are present. Refrigerant-side work is not typically required for E:12, but improper fan replacement can damage other components if done incorrectly.
