## Navien E021 Error Code — What It Means

The E021 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates a cold water inlet thermistor open-circuit fault. The control board is not receiving a valid signal from the temperature sensor that monitors the cold water entering the unit. This usually means the thermistor itself has failed open or the wiring and connector between the sensor and the board are interrupted, corroded, or damaged.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed cold inlet thermistor** The sensor itself has an open circuit and no longer returns a valid resistance reading to the control board.
- **Loose or disconnected wiring** The harness connector at the thermistor or board has come loose, creating an intermittent or complete open circuit.
- **Corroded or damaged connector pins** Water intrusion, corrosion, or physical damage at the connector prevents electrical continuity to the sensor.
- **Chafed or broken wire in the harness** The thermistor wiring has been cut, pinched, or worn through somewhere between the sensor and the control board.
- **Control board input failure** The thermistor and harness test good but the board input circuit is damaged and cannot read the sensor signal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the unit at the breaker and shut off the gas supply before opening the cabinet or touching any internal components.
2. **Confirm the error code** on the display panel and note whether it returns immediately after a brief reset or only under flow conditions.
3. **Inspect the cold water inlet thermistor connector** for looseness, corrosion, bent pins, water damage, or debris, and reseat the connector firmly.
4. **Check thermistor resistance** with a digital multimeter by disconnecting the sensor and measuring across its terminals, then compare the reading to your model's service manual specification for that water temperature.
5. **Test harness continuity** from the thermistor connector all the way to the control board connector to locate any open wire or damaged section of the harness.
6. **Replace the cold inlet thermistor** if it reads open or out of specification, or repair and replace any damaged wiring or connectors if the sensor itself tests good.
7. **Restore power and test** the unit under normal flow conditions to verify the error does not return and the inlet temperature is being read correctly on the display.

## When to Call a Pro

E021 requires opening the unit, working with low-voltage wiring, and using a multimeter to diagnose open circuits. If you are not comfortable de-energizing the heater, accessing internal components, or interpreting resistance readings against manufacturer specifications, contact a Navien-certified technician. Navien technical support recommends that thermistor and sensor-related faults be handled by qualified service personnel who have access to model-specific service data and genuine replacement parts. Incorrect diagnosis or improper part replacement can leave the unit unable to regulate inlet temperature and may void your warranty.
