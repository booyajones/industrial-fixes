## LG Dishwasher OE Error Code — What It Means

The OE error code on an LG dishwasher means the unit has detected a drain issue. The dishwasher did not fully drain water within the expected time frame. This is almost always caused by a physical blockage or restriction somewhere in the drain path, not a pump or control board failure. LG's service documentation describes OE as a drain problem tied to water remaining in the tub after the drain cycle should have completed.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Clogged filter or food residue** The dishwasher filter is blocked with food particles, grease, or debris and water cannot pass through to the drain pump.
- **Kinked or pinched drain hose** The drain hose under the sink is bent, crimped, frozen, or physically obstructed and water cannot exit the appliance.
- **Blocked air gap** If your installation uses an air gap on the countertop, the internal passage is clogged with debris and prevents drainage.
- **Garbage disposal knockout plug not removed** The dishwasher inlet knockout plug inside the disposal was not punched out during installation, or plastic debris remains in the waste spigot.
- **Drain hose installation restriction** The drain hose exceeds the 12-foot maximum length or is routed in a way that restricts flow.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the dishwasher** and open the door, then remove any loose food debris or standing water from the bottom of the tub.
2. **Remove and clean the filter assembly** by twisting or lifting it out per your model's instructions, rinse away all food residue and grease, then reinstall it securely.
3. **Inspect the drain hose** under the sink for kinks, pinches, blockages, or freezing, straighten or reroute the hose as needed, and confirm total hose length does not exceed 12 feet.
4. **Check the air gap** if your installation has one by removing the chrome cap on the countertop, clearing any debris from the internal passages, and rinsing clean.
5. **Verify the garbage disposal knockout plug** was removed by reaching into the dishwasher inlet on the disposal and feeling for the plastic disc, remove any remaining plastic debris from the waste spigot.
6. **Clean the spray arms** by removing them and rinsing out any clogs in the spray holes, then reinstall.
7. **Run a drain test** by starting a cycle and observing the first 45 seconds (drain-only phase on many models) or use the CANCEL & DRAIN function if available, confirm water drains completely and the OE code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have cleaned the filter, cleared the drain hose and air gap, verified the disposal knockout plug was removed, and the OE error still appears after running a drain test cycle, the drain pump or a wiring fault may be the cause. At that point, contact an LG-authorized service technician for diagnosis. Also call for service if you are uncomfortable working under the sink or disconnecting plumbing, or if the dishwasher is still under warranty.
