## Navien Tankless Water Heater E249 Error — What It Means

The E249 error code on Navien tankless water heaters signals a detected fault within the system. Because Navien uses different code definitions across models and firmware versions, the exact meaning of E249 can vary. Some units use this code for communication errors between the control board and other components, while others may flag sensor issues or operational faults. Without the specific manual for your model and serial number, the safest approach is to consult the wiring diagram or technical documentation supplied with your unit.

In general, error codes in the E2xx range on Navien units often point to internal electronic or sensor communication problems. The fault may be temporary and resolve with a power cycle, or it may require inspection of wiring, connectors, and control boards. Before replacing any parts, verify that the unit has proper power, gas supply, and adequate combustion air and venting.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the main control board when the real issue is a loose wiring harness or a bad sensor connection. Check all connector pins and reseat harnesses before ordering a board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connector (~30%)** Vibration and humidity can loosen or corrode the plugs between sensors and the control board, interrupting communication and triggering fault codes.
- **Faulty temperature or flow sensor (~25%)** A degraded thermistor or flow sensor can send invalid signals to the board, causing the system to log an error and shut down.
- **Control board firmware glitch or temporary fault (~20%)** Transient power surges or internal memory errors can cause the board to register a fault that clears with a power cycle.
- **Failed main control board (~15%)** The PCB itself can develop solder cracks, capacitor failure, or component damage that generates persistent error codes.
- **Blocked or restricted venting (~10%)** Obstructed exhaust or intake pipes can cause abnormal operation and trigger safety codes on some Navien models.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners and lifting the cover away from the unit.
3. **Inspect all wiring connectors** on the main control board and each sensor (flow, temperature, pressure) for corrosion, moisture, or loose pins.
4. **Reseat each connector** firmly, and if you find corrosion, spray with electrical contact cleaner and let dry.
5. **Check the intake and exhaust vent pipes** outside for blockages, nests, ice, or heavy condensation, and clear any obstructions.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then press the reset button or power on the unit and observe whether the E249 code reappears.
7. **If the code persists**, use a multimeter to test resistance across each sensor per the wiring diagram, or call a qualified Navien technician to test the control board and sensors.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the error returns after reseating connectors and clearing vents, if you lack a multimeter or wiring diagram to test sensors, or if you are uncomfortable working around gas and 120V electrical components. Navien tankless units require specific diagnostic tools and fault-code readers that most homeowners do not own. A pro can quickly pinpoint whether the issue is a sensor, a board, or a gas-valve communication fault and can update firmware if a known bug is causing the code. Always use a Navien-certified service provider for warranty work.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-500.
