## Fujitsu Mini Split E:67 Error Code — What It Means

E:67 on Fujitsu mini-splits indicates a communication failure between the indoor evaporator and outdoor condenser units during startup. The two halves of your system are not able to exchange control signals, which prevents normal operation. This is a control and wiring fault rather than a refrigerant or sensor issue. The exact code format may vary slightly across different Fujitsu product families, but the root problem is the same: the system cannot establish proper communication between the indoor and outdoor controller boards.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or disconnected field wiring** The communication cable running between indoor and outdoor units has a loose terminal connection, corrosion, or physical damage that interrupts the signal path.
- **Miswired terminal blocks** Communication wires are crossed, reversed, or connected to the wrong terminals at either the indoor or outdoor unit.
- **Failed indoor controller PCB** The indoor unit's controller board has failed and can no longer send or receive communication signals to the outdoor unit.
- **Failed main PCB in outdoor unit** The outdoor unit's main control board has failed and cannot communicate with the indoor controller.
- **Poor connector seating on external I/O boards** Communication connectors on interface or expansion boards are not fully seated or have corrosion on the pins.
- **Low or unstable supply voltage** Voltage drop or grounding issues disrupt the low-voltage communication signals between units and trigger false faults.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the system** by turning off the breaker for 60 seconds, then restoring power to see if the fault clears or returns immediately.
2. **Verify supply voltage** at the outdoor unit is within the acceptable range of 187 to 253 volts AC under load to rule out power-quality issues.
3. **Inspect all communication wiring** from the indoor unit terminal block to the outdoor unit terminal block for loose screws, damaged insulation, or pinched wires.
4. **Reseat every connector** on the indoor controller board, outdoor main board, and any external interface boards by unplugging and firmly reconnecting each one.
5. **Check wiring correctness** by comparing your terminal connections to the installation manual wiring diagram and correct any crossed or reversed wires.
6. **Test or substitute the indoor controller PCB** if all wiring and connections are correct but the communication fault persists after power cycling.
7. **Test or substitute the outdoor main PCB** if the indoor controller checks good and all wiring is verified correct but the E:67 error remains.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with line voltage or if you lack a multimeter to verify wiring and voltage. Communication faults require methodical board-level troubleshooting and the technician will need to isolate whether the problem is in the field wiring, the indoor controller, or the outdoor main board. If power cycling and visual inspection of connections do not resolve the fault, a technician with board-substitution tools and model-specific wiring diagrams is the fastest path to repair.
