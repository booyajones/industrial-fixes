## LG Microwave DOOR Error — What It Means

The DOOR error code on an LG microwave indicates the unit is not detecting a proper closed-door condition. This is a door-interlock safety fault, not a cooking-performance issue. The microwave will not start until the control confirms the door is fully closed and all interlock switches are engaged.

The safety system uses mechanical latches and electrical switches to verify the door is secure before allowing the magnetron to energize. When any part of that chain fails to confirm closure, the DOOR code appears and the unit remains locked out.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the control board first, but most DOOR faults are mechanical. Inspect the door latch, switches, and alignment before ordering electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Foreign material or debris (~40%)** Packing material, food crumbs, grease, or spills can block the latch hook from fully engaging the interlock switches.
- **Door latch or interlock switch wear (~25%)** The latch hook, spring, or one of the door interlock switches can wear, crack, or fail so the door appears closed but the electrical circuit never confirms it.
- **Cooking rack out of position (~15%)** A misplaced or bent rack can prevent the door from closing completely and triggering the interlock.
- **Door misalignment or sagging (~15%)** Worn hinges or a sagging door cause the latch to miss the switch assembly, even when the door is pressed shut.
- **Control-side or wiring fault (~5%)** Less common, but a loose connector, damaged wire, or control-board issue in the door-interlock circuit can prevent the signal from reaching the main control.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the microwave** and leave it disconnected for at least one minute to reset the control and discharge residual voltage.
2. **Inspect the door area** for any packing material, food debris, crumbs, or grease around the door seal, latch, and hinge region, and remove anything blocking the door from closing fully.
3. **Check the cooking rack position** if your model uses one, and make sure it is seated correctly and not bent or interfering with the door.
4. **Close the door firmly** and listen for a solid click, then try to start the microwave to see if the DOOR code clears.
5. **Examine door alignment** by opening and closing the door several times and watching for sagging, rubbing, or a latch that does not line up with the catch on the frame.
6. **Remove the outer cabinet** only if you are trained and the basic checks have failed, then visually inspect the door latch hook, interlock switches, and wiring harness for cracks, wear, loose connectors, or misalignment.
7. **Replace the failed component** (latch, switch, or bracket) and reassemble, then test door closure and start function before restoring the unit to service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have cleaned the door area and verified no obstructions but the DOOR code persists, or if the door appears to close firmly but the microwave will not start. Internal diagnosis requires discharging the high-voltage capacitor, testing interlock switches with a multimeter, and working safely around magnetron circuits. Also call service if the door is visibly damaged, the latch is broken, or you see arcing or burnt wiring near the door assembly. LG directs persistent DOOR faults to authorized service after basic user checks have been completed.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-50 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $100-250.
