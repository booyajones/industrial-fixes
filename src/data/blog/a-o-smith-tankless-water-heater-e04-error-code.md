## A.O. Smith Tankless E04 Error — What It Means

The E04 error code on an A.O. Smith electric tankless water heater indicates that the unit has detected moisture or a leak inside the heater cabinet. This is a safety fault that protects the electronics and internal components from water damage. A.O. Smith's official troubleshooting guidance for E04 is to immediately turn off electrical power and water supply to the unit and contact Technical Support. Do not attempt to reset or continue operation until the source of moisture is identified and corrected.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Internal water leak** A leak at inlet or outlet connections, or from internal hydraulic components, has allowed water to reach the control or electronics compartment.
- **Condensation buildup** Moisture accumulation inside the cabinet can trigger the moisture detection circuit even without a true plumbing leak.
- **Water intrusion from outside** External water from flooding, plumbing work, or environmental conditions has entered the heater enclosure and wetted sensitive areas.
- **Failed moisture detection circuit** The sensor or wiring that monitors for leaks may have failed or developed a short, causing a false E04 alarm.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut off electrical power** at the branch circuit breakers supplying the tankless water heater.
2. **Shut off the water supply** to the heater at the isolation valves on the inlet and outlet lines.
3. **Inspect the unit for visible leaks** around all plumbing connections, fittings, and the cabinet interior where moisture could collect.
4. **Dry the interior** of the unit thoroughly using towels or a fan, and correct any identified leak source by tightening connections or replacing damaged components.
5. **Do not restore power** until the leak source is confirmed repaired and the interior is completely dry.
6. **Contact A.O. Smith Technical Support** as directed in the troubleshooting table, since the manual does not provide a field reset procedure for E04.
7. **If the fault persists** after the leak is corrected and the unit is dry, Technical Support may authorize control board evaluation or replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

A.O. Smith explicitly directs that E04 requires contacting Technical Support rather than field repair. If you are not comfortable working with 240-volt electrical circuits or cannot locate the source of the moisture, call a licensed plumber or electrician immediately. Even if you dry the unit and eliminate a visible leak, the fault may not clear without manufacturer-authorized diagnostic steps or component replacement. Because E04 is a safety interlock, do not bypass it or attempt to operate the heater until a qualified technician confirms the unit is safe.
