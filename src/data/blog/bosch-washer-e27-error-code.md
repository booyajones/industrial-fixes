## What this code means
The E27 error code on a Bosch washer indicates a door-lock or door-interlock fault. The control system cannot confirm that the door is locked or that the lock circuit is working correctly. The washer will not start a cycle until the door lock confirms a secure latch.

This code is specific to the door-lock mechanism and its electrical circuit. It is not related to water inlet or drainage. One source online describes E27 as a water-intake problem, but that information conflicts with Bosch washer-specific repair documentation and should be disregarded for washers.

## Before You Replace Anything

Some owners replace the main control board thinking it is a logic fault. Test the door lock assembly first by inspecting for power at the lock connector and checking whether the latch physically engages when the door is shut.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock assembly (~60%)** The internal solenoid, latch mechanism, or switch contacts in the door lock have failed and the control cannot read a locked state.
- **Door not closing or latching properly (~20%)** The door strike is bent, the hinge is loose, or an object blocks the door from seating fully against the lock.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~12%)** The harness plug at the door lock or control board is loose, corroded, or has a broken terminal that interrupts the lock signal.
- **Damaged wiring harness (~5%)** Wires between the control board and the door lock are pinched, cut, or heat-damaged and cannot carry the lock signal.
- **Control board lock circuit fault (~3%)** The control board itself has a failed relay or driver that cannot energize or read the door lock.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** by unplugging the washer or switching off the circuit breaker to prevent shock during disassembly.
2. **Remove the front panel or access the door lock** by taking out the retaining screws and lifting the panel away from the cabinet.
3. **Inspect the door strike and latch** to confirm the door closes fully and nothing obstructs the lock mechanism.
4. **Unplug the wiring harness** from the door lock assembly and examine the connector terminals for corrosion, burn marks, or looseness.
5. **Test the door lock** with a multimeter set to continuity mode across the lock switch terminals while manually actuating the latch, or consult your model's wiring diagram for the correct pins.
6. **Replace the door lock assembly** if continuity is absent or the lock does not actuate, using the part number listed in your washer's service manual or cross-reference (for example, part 648526 for model WFVC4400UC_29).
7. **Reassemble the front panel**, restore power, and run a short test cycle to verify the E27 fault is cleared and the door locks correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or removing the front panel of the washer. A technician should also diagnose the issue if replacing the door lock assembly does not clear the E27 code, since that points to a control-board fault or a deeper wiring problem that requires tracing circuits with a schematic. If the washer is still under warranty, contact Bosch service to avoid voiding coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $50-90 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-280.
