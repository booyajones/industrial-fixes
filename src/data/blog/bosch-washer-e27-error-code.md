## Bosch Washer E27 Error Code — What It Means

On Bosch washing machines, the E27 error code indicates a door-lock or door-interlock verification fault. The control system cannot confirm that the door is securely locked, so the machine stops the cycle to prevent water spillage or unsafe operation.

This code points to a problem in the door locking mechanism itself or the signal path from that lock back to the main control board. The machine may refuse to start, stop mid-cycle, or display E27 on the panel. Note that E27 on Bosch dishwashers refers to a mains-voltage or power-supply fault, so the appliance type matters when diagnosing this code.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first when E27 appears. Test the door lock assembly and inspect all wiring and connectors between the lock and control board before replacing the board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock assembly (~55%)** The electromechanical door lock or latch switch no longer reports a secure lock signal to the control board.
- **Damaged or loose wiring and connectors (~25%)** Broken conductors, corroded pins, or loose connectors between the door lock and the main control board interrupt the verification signal.
- **Misaligned or damaged door latch striker (~10%)** Physical wear, misalignment, or debris in the latch area prevents the door from engaging the lock fully.
- **Faulty main control board (~10%)** The control board itself fails to process the lock signal correctly even when the lock and wiring are sound.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power reset the washer** by unplugging it from the wall or switching off the dedicated circuit breaker for at least five minutes, then restore power and attempt a cycle to see if the E27 code clears.
2. **Verify the door closes and latches** by pushing it firmly shut and listening for a distinct click, then check that the door sits flush against the cabinet without gaps or play.
3. **Inspect the door latch striker and catch area** for cracks, wear, misalignment, or foreign objects, and clean any lint or debris that may prevent full engagement of the lock.
4. **Disconnect power and remove the top or front panel** to access the door lock assembly, then unplug the wiring connector from the lock and inspect both the connector pins and the lock terminals for corrosion, bent pins, or burn marks.
5. **Trace the wiring harness** from the door lock back to the main control board, checking for pinched, cut, or abraded insulation and ensuring all inline connectors are seated fully and free of corrosion.
6. **Test or replace the door lock assembly** by either measuring continuity and coil resistance (consult your model's wiring diagram for expected values) or installing a new lock if visual inspection or electrical testing indicates failure.
7. **If the lock and wiring test good**, suspect the main control board and verify that the board is receiving and processing the lock signal correctly, or arrange for board-level diagnosis or replacement by a qualified technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or disassembling the cabinet panels to access the door lock and control board. A technician should also handle any work that requires board-level diagnosis, signal tracing with a multimeter, or replacement of the main control board when the lock and wiring have already been ruled out. If the machine is still under warranty or a service plan, contact Bosch or an authorized service center before attempting any repair to preserve your coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–$120 in parts, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–$280.
