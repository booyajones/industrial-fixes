## Maytag Refrigerator dr Error Code — What It Means

The dr error code on Maytag refrigerators typically indicates a door-related fault. The control board is not detecting the door in the expected closed state, or there is a problem with the door switch or door-sensor circuit. While Maytag's published materials do not list dr as a standard code name across all models, in practice this code appears on Whirlpool-family units and points to a door open, door switch, or door state issue rather than a sealed-system or defrost problem.

Before you proceed with any repair, confirm the exact meaning of dr for your specific model by checking the tech sheet behind the grille or inside the refrigerator. On most units showing this code, the refrigerator may run continuously, refuse to enter energy-saving modes, or fail to maintain correct temperatures because the control thinks a door is open.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully closing** Obstructions from misloaded food, damaged door gaskets, or ice and debris buildup around the seal or hinge prevent the door from seating properly and triggering the switch.
- **Faulty door switch or reed switch** The door-position switch or sensor has failed or is stuck, causing the control to read an open state even when the door is closed.
- **Door alignment or leveling problem** The refrigerator is not level or the door has sagged on its hinges, preventing the switch from changing state normally when the door closes.
- **Pinched or damaged wiring harness** Wiring that runs through the hinge area or door pass-through has broken conductors, loose plugs, or intermittent opens caused by repeated door movement.
- **Main control board misreading input** The control board itself is failing to correctly read or drive the door-related circuit, even when the door hardware and wiring are intact.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the model and service sheet first.** Locate your exact model number (usually inside the fresh-food compartment or on the left wall) and pull the tech sheet from behind the lower grille or taped inside the unit to confirm the meaning of dr and any model-specific diagnostics.
2. **Inspect the door mechanically.** Open and close each door slowly, checking for obstructions, damaged bins or shelves, gasket gaps, hinge sag, and whether the door closes squarely and seals all the way around the perimeter.
3. **Power-cycle the refrigerator.** Unplug the unit for at least three minutes, then restore power to clear any temporary control glitch or memory fault that may be holding the code.
4. **Run service diagnostics if available.** Use the model-specific keypad sequence (refer to your tech sheet or service manual) to enter diagnostic mode and check door-input status or fault history.
5. **Test the door switch or sensor electrically.** With the refrigerator unplugged, locate the door switch (often near the top hinge or in the door frame) and use a multimeter to verify continuity change as you manually actuate the switch or close the door.
6. **Inspect the wiring harness at the hinge and door run.** Flex the harness gently while watching for breaks, look for loose connector plugs, and check for pinch points where the wires pass from the cabinet into the door.
7. **Replace the control board only after confirming input integrity.** If the door hardware, switch, and wiring all test good but the code persists, the main control board is likely misreading the door circuit and should be replaced.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have confirmed the doors close properly and seat fully but the dr code will not clear after a power cycle, if you are not comfortable using a multimeter to test switch continuity and trace wiring, or if the diagnostic mode on your model requires specialized tools or key sequences you cannot access. A technician has the service manual, wiring diagrams, and experience to quickly isolate whether the fault is in the door hardware, harness, or control board. Also call for help if your unit is still under warranty, since DIY board or switch replacement may void coverage.
