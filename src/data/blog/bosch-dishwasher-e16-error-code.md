## Bosch Dishwasher E16 Error — What It Means

The E16 error code on Bosch dishwashers typically indicates a water fill issue, though the exact meaning can vary slightly between models. The machine either cannot fill with water at all or is filling too slowly. The control board expects water to reach a certain level within a set time window, and when that does not happen the code appears and the cycle stops. This code protects the dishwasher from running without enough water, which would damage the wash pump and heating element. Always consult your specific model's documentation to confirm the exact definition, as some older or regional models may use E16 for a different fault.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the inlet valve when the real culprit is a clogged mesh screen inside the valve inlet or a partially closed shutoff valve. Clean the screen and verify full water pressure before ordering a new valve.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged inlet valve screen (~40%)** Hard water minerals, sediment, or rust particles block the fine mesh filter where the supply hose attaches to the valve body, slowing water flow below the minimum rate.
- **Kinked or pinched supply hose (~20%)** The braided or plastic hose behind the dishwasher is bent or compressed against the wall, restricting flow enough to trigger the timeout.
- **Low household water pressure (~15%)** Building supply pressure below about 20 psi or a partially closed shutoff valve means the inlet valve cannot deliver water fast enough even when working correctly.
- **Failed inlet valve solenoid (~15%)** The valve coil no longer opens fully or sticks partway, limiting flow even when power is applied and the screen is clean.
- **Faulty water level sensor or pressure switch (~7%)** The sensor or switch that tells the control board when enough water has entered fails to detect the rising level, so the board thinks no water arrived.
- **Control board communication fault (~3%)** A loose wire connection or board glitch prevents the control from correctly reading the fill status, throwing a false error.

## Quick Diagnosis

<details class="dtree"><summary>Does water flow freely and quickly when you disconnect the supply hose at the dishwasher and turn on the shutoff valve?</summary>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the dishwasher water shutoff valve under the sink or behind the unit.
2. **Pull the dishwasher forward** enough to access the rear panel and locate the braided or plastic supply hose connection at the inlet valve on the left side of the base.
3. **Place a shallow pan** under the connection and use pliers to loosen the hose coupling nut, then remove the hose and inspect the small mesh screen inside the valve inlet for debris.
4. **Rinse the screen** under running water or gently brush it with an old toothbrush to remove mineral scale and sediment, taking care not to tear the mesh.
5. **Reconnect the hose** hand-tight plus a quarter turn with pliers, open the shutoff valve fully, and restore power.
6. **Run a test cycle** and listen for the valve to click and water to flow steadily within the first minute.
7. **If the error persists** and water flow is confirmed good, disconnect power again and use a multimeter to measure resistance across the inlet valve coil terminals (typically 500-4000 ohms depending on model), then inspect the pressure switch and its air hose for blockage or damage.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have cleaned the screen and verified full water pressure but the error returns, or if multimeter testing shows the valve coil and pressure switch are within specification yet the machine still will not fill. Intermittent wiring faults and control board issues require diagnostic tools and experience to trace. Also call a pro if you are uncomfortable working around water connections or if the dishwasher is built-in and you cannot safely pull it forward without damaging cabinetry or flooring.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $120-220.
