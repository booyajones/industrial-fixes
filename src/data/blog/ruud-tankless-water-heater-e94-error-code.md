## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E94 Error Code — What It Means

The E94 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a flame-sensing issue. The unit has ignited but the control board is not receiving a proper flame signal from the flame rod sensor. This safety feature prevents the heater from operating when it cannot confirm combustion is occurring correctly.

The exact meaning can vary slightly by model, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit's cover for the precise definition. In most cases, the code points to either a dirty or failed flame rod, poor grounding, or a faulty ignition control board that cannot process the flame signal.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the ignition control board first, which can cost over two hundred dollars. Instead, remove and inspect the flame rod for carbon deposits or corrosion. Cleaning it with fine emery cloth or steel wool solves the problem in many cases.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or corroded flame rod (~55%)** Carbon deposits or corrosion on the flame rod insulator prevent it from detecting the flame current properly.
- **Poor grounding connection (~20%)** A loose or corroded ground wire at the chassis or burner assembly interrupts the flame-sensing circuit.
- **Failed flame rod sensor (~15%)** The flame rod itself can crack or the ceramic insulator can fail, requiring replacement of the sensor assembly.
- **Faulty ignition control board (~8%)** The control board may fail to process the flame signal even when the rod is clean and properly grounded.
- **Intermittent burner flame (~2%)** Blocked burner ports or low gas pressure can cause unstable flame that the sensor cannot reliably detect.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the tankless water heater at the breaker or disconnect switch and turn off the gas supply valve.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the retaining screws and carefully lifting the panel away from the unit.
3. **Locate the flame rod** near the burner assembly. It is a small metal rod with a ceramic insulator, usually positioned to sense the flame directly.
4. **Disconnect the flame rod wire** by gently pulling the spade connector or unthreading the screw terminal, noting its position for reassembly.
5. **Remove the flame rod** by unscrewing the mounting bracket or nut that secures it to the burner housing.
6. **Clean the flame rod** with fine-grit emery cloth or steel wool, removing all carbon deposits and corrosion from the metal rod and ceramic insulator. Do not use harsh chemicals.
7. **Reinstall the flame rod** in the same position, reconnect the wire, and verify that the ground wire at the unit chassis is tight and free of corrosion.
8. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit by pressing the reset button or power-cycling. Monitor the display to confirm the E94 error has cleared.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are uncomfortable working around gas appliances, if the error persists after cleaning the flame rod and verifying grounding, or if you suspect the ignition control board has failed. Professionals have diagnostic tools to measure flame current and can safely test gas pressure and combustion performance. If the unit is still under warranty, professional service may be required to preserve coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-50 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-275.
