## Frigidaire Washer Won't Turn On — What's Happening

When a Frigidaire washer won't turn on, there is no single fault code behind it. The problem is most often in the power path, the door lock circuit, or the control board's ability to detect that conditions are safe to start. Frigidaire service guidance for no-start conditions begins with verifying incoming power and confirming the door is fully closed and latched.

Front-load models may display E41 if the control does not detect the door as locked, or E42 if the washer believes the door is still locked after a cycle should be complete. Top-load units typically have no display at all when power or lock circuits fail. In both cases the washer is either not receiving power or is being held in a safety interlock that prevents the control from waking up.

## Most Likely Causes

- **No power at the outlet or tripped breaker** The most common cause is loss of incoming power from a tripped circuit breaker, blown fuse, or faulty wall outlet.
- **Failed door latch or lock assembly** If the door lock does not engage or the control cannot detect the lock signal, the washer will refuse to start.
- **Water still in the tub from incomplete drain** Many models will not start a new cycle if the drain pump failed to empty the tub or if the drain path is clogged.
- **Clogged drain pump or blocked filter** A jammed pump impeller or debris in the coin trap can prevent the washer from completing its pre-start checks and lock out the control.
- **Kinked or clogged drain hose** If the drain hose is pinched behind the washer or clogged with lint, the unit may sense standing water and refuse to power on.
- **Door switch or lock mechanism stuck** Physical binding in the door strike or a worn switch contact can prevent the lock circuit from closing even when the door appears shut.
- **Failed main control board or wiring fault** If all power and lock checks pass, the control board itself or a broken wire harness may be preventing startup.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the wall outlet has power by plugging in a lamp or testing with a multimeter, and confirm the circuit breaker is not tripped.
2. Check that the washer power cord is fully seated at both the wall and the back of the machine.
3. Open and firmly close the door, listening and feeling for the latch to click and the lock to engage.
4. If the washer is a front-load model, look for error codes E41 or E42 on the display and treat as a door-lock fault path.
5. Inspect the drain hose for kinks or clogs and remove the drain pump filter or coin trap to clear any debris or standing water.
6. Run the washer's built-in diagnostic mode or error-code retrieval sequence per the service manual to identify stored faults.
7. Test the door lock assembly for continuity and correct engagement, and replace it if the lock does not click or signal the control.
8. If all checks pass and the washer still will not turn on, inspect wiring connectors at the control board and door lock, then consider control board replacement only after verifying all other causes.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with live 120 V power, if the diagnostic mode reveals communication faults you cannot interpret, or if you have replaced the door lock and verified all drain components but the washer still will not start. Control board diagnosis requires a multimeter and familiarity with Frigidaire wiring harnesses, and misdiagnosis can lead to unnecessary part replacement.
