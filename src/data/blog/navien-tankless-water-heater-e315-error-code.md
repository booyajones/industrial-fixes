## Navien E315 Error Code — What It Means

The E315 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates a problem with water flow through the unit. The heater's control board has detected that flow is insufficient, restricted, or erratic, preventing the unit from operating safely. This code typically appears when the system cannot maintain the minimum flow rate needed to activate the burner or when sensors detect inconsistent flow patterns.

Because tankless heaters require a steady minimum flow to operate, any restriction in the water path will trigger protective shutdowns. The code protects the heat exchanger from dry-firing or overheating. In most cases the issue is mechanical rather than electronic, involving scale deposits, debris, or a failing component in the water path.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the flow sensor when the real problem is a clogged inlet filter or scale in the heat exchanger. Pull and inspect the filter and check for scale deposits before ordering a sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged inlet filter (~40%)** Sediment and mineral deposits accumulate on the mesh screen at the cold water inlet, restricting flow into the unit.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger (~30%)** Hard water leaves calcium and lime deposits inside the heat exchanger coils, narrowing the passages and reducing flow.
- **Failing flow sensor (~15%)** The turbine or Hall-effect sensor that measures flow rate becomes fouled or mechanically worn, sending incorrect signals to the control board.
- **Low incoming water pressure (~10%)** Municipal supply pressure below the minimum required threshold or a partially closed service valve upstream prevents adequate flow.
- **Defective circulation pump (if recirculation is installed) (~5%)** A weak or failed pump in recirculation systems causes erratic flow patterns that confuse the control board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the isolation valves on the cold and hot water lines to the heater.
2. **Locate the inlet filter** on the cold water service valve (usually a brass fitting with a removable cap at the bottom of the unit).
3. **Remove the filter screen** by unscrewing the cap or plug with a wrench, then pull out the mesh screen.
4. **Clean the filter** under running water with a soft brush, removing all sediment and mineral deposits.
5. **Reinstall the filter** and service-valve cap, then open the isolation valves and restore power.
6. **Run hot water** at a tap for two minutes and observe whether the error clears and the unit fires normally.
7. **If the error persists**, perform a descaling flush by connecting a pump and bucket with white vinegar or descaling solution to the service ports and circulating for 45 minutes (consult your manual for port locations).
8. **If the code remains after descaling**, test or replace the flow sensor by disconnecting the wiring harness, inspecting the turbine for debris, and swapping in a new sensor if needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if descaling and filter cleaning do not resolve the E315 code, if you are uncomfortable working with plumbing connections and pump equipment, or if the flow sensor replacement does not clear the error. A pro can test water pressure accurately, inspect internal components for hidden blockages, and diagnose control-board or wiring issues that are beyond typical DIY scope. Also call a pro if you have never descaled a tankless heater before and want to avoid damaging the heat exchanger with incorrect chemical concentration or procedure.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-350.
