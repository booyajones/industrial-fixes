## Daikin U2 Error Code — What It Means

U2 on a Daikin mini-split or multi-zone system indicates a power supply undervoltage fault. The outdoor unit's control board detected that the supply voltage dropped below the minimum threshold for safe compressor and fan motor operation. Daikin systems typically require 208–240VAC (±10%) on single-phase units. U2 is a protective shutdown — the system locks out to prevent compressor damage from low voltage.

## Common Causes

- **Low utility supply voltage** — Grid voltage sags during peak demand periods, during storms, or on long branch circuits can drop below the unit's minimum operating voltage.
- **Undersized or deteriorated wiring** — Excessive voltage drop across a long or undersized wire run causes the voltage at the unit to be significantly lower than at the panel.
- **Loose connection at disconnect or contactor** — A corroded or loose terminal in the outdoor unit's disconnect box or at the compressor contactor creates resistance and voltage drop under load.
- **Failing capacitor or compressor drawing excess current** — A weak start or run capacitor causes the compressor to draw high inrush current, which drops voltage on the supply circuit temporarily.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Measure supply voltage at the outdoor unit** — With the system attempting to run, use a true-RMS multimeter at the disconnect box or on the unit's terminal block. Compare to the nameplate voltage range. If voltage is below the minimum (typically 187VAC for a 208/230V unit), the issue is upstream.
2. **Check utility voltage at the main panel** — Measure voltage at the breaker. If low voltage is present at the panel, contact the utility company — this is a supply problem, not a unit problem.
3. **Inspect wiring for undersizing** — If panel voltage is correct but voltage drops significantly at the unit, calculate voltage drop for the wire run. Upgrade wire gauge if drop exceeds 3%.
4. **Tighten all connections** — With power off, inspect and tighten terminals at the disconnect, contactor, and terminal block inside the outdoor unit. Look for signs of arcing or heat discoloration.
5. **Clear the fault and test** — After correcting voltage, power cycle the outdoor unit (off for 3 minutes to allow the compressor to equalize). Restart and verify voltage under load.

## When to Call a Pro

Low utility voltage and wiring upgrades require a licensed electrician. A U2 that recurs after tightening connections and confirming wire sizing needs an electrical load analysis to identify the root cause.
