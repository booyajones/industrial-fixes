## LG Washer Cd Error Code — What It Means

Cd stands for Cool Down and appears on LG washer-dryer combo units after the drying cycle finishes. It is not a fault code. The machine tumbles the load without heat to reduce wrinkles and help cool the drum so the load is safe to remove. LG states this cool-down operation can continue for up to 4 hours if you do not remove the clothes or clear the display. The door remains locked during this period to protect you from the hot drum and load.

## Common Causes

- **Normal end-of-dry cool down** The drying cycle just completed and the unit is running its automatic cool-down routine to protect the load and reduce wrinkles.
- **Laundry left in the drum** You did not remove the load or press a button to clear the display, so the machine continues the anti-wrinkle cool-down cycle.
- **Model confusion** Cd only appears on washer-dryer combo units with a drying function, not on standard wash-only models.
- **Display not clearing** The unit may not be exiting cool-down mode correctly due to a control issue or a stuck cycle state.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify your model** has a drying function by checking the control panel for dry-cycle settings, because Cd only appears on washer-dryer combo units.
2. **Press START/PAUSE or any non-cycle button** to clear the cool-down display and allow the door-lock mechanism to release.
3. **Wait about 2 minutes** after clearing the display for the door lock to disengage, then open the door and remove your load.
4. **Remove all items from the drum** promptly after each drying cycle to prevent the cool-down routine from running for the full 4-hour period.
5. **Power the unit down** by unplugging or turning off the circuit breaker for 60 seconds if the Cd display persists unusually long, then restore power and retest.
6. **Inspect the drum and door seal** for any trapped objects or items caught in the tub seam that might prevent the machine from recognizing the cycle is complete.
7. **Run a short test dry cycle** with a small load to confirm the machine exits cool-down normally and the door unlocks after you press START/PAUSE.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the Cd display does not clear after pressing START/PAUSE and waiting 2 minutes, if the door remains locked even after a full power reset, or if the machine enters cool-down mode without completing a drying cycle. Also call if you notice unusual noises, burning smells, or error codes other than Cd appearing during or after the cool-down period. A professional can test the door-lock assembly, control board, and cycle-transition logic to identify any component failure that prevents normal operation.
