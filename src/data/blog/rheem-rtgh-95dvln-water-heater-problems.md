## Rheem RTGH-95DVLN Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Rheem RTGH-95DVLN is a high-efficiency condensing tankless water heater with electronic diagnostics that displays numbered error codes on the control board. The unit uses a flame rod sensor, hot surface igniter, gas valve, and flow sensor that all communicate faults through the diagnostic system. Most service calls trace back to ignition problems (code 11), flame sensing issues (code 12), flow sensor faults (code 24), or scale buildup affecting heat exchange and sensor readings.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **Code 11 ignition failure** Gas supply shut off, air in the line after interruption, fouled igniter, or failed ignition rod prevents startup and requires gas line purging, igniter cleaning, or component replacement.
- **Code 12 flame loss or no flame detection** Flame rod sensor fouled with carbon buildup or poor electrical connection causes the unit to shut down during operation and needs flame rod cleaning or connector inspection.
- **Code 13 combustion abnormality or gas valve problem** Insufficient gas pressure, clogged burner, or failed gas valve prevents proper combustion and requires gas pressure verification and possible gas valve replacement.
- **Code 24 flow sensor or water flow issue** Inlet filter clogged with sediment or failed flow sensor stops the unit from firing and needs filter cleaning or flow sensor replacement.
- **Code 14 thermal fuse or overheat shutoff** Blocked vent, restricted airflow, or scale buildup in heat exchanger triggers the overheat safety and requires vent inspection, flushing, or thermal fuse reset.
- **Code 16 outlet temperature sensor fault** Failed thermistor or wiring issue at the outlet temperature sensor causes erratic temperature control and requires sensor replacement.
- **Code 15 inlet temperature sensor fault** Inlet thermistor failure or connector corrosion prevents the unit from reading incoming water temperature and needs sensor or wiring repair.
- **Code 31 inlet water filter maintenance alert** Sediment buildup in the inlet filter restricts flow and triggers the service reminder, requiring filter removal and cleaning.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician for any gas supply work, gas valve replacement, or venting issues. Professionals have combustion analyzers to verify proper gas pressure and air-fuel mix. If you see repeated ignition codes after cleaning the flame rod and igniter, or if the unit trips thermal safeties, the problem usually involves gas pressure, venting, or internal scale that needs professional flushing and component testing. Any work inside the combustion chamber or on gas connections requires a qualified gas technician. For gas line, burner, or igniter work, or if you ever smell gas, stop and call a licensed technician.
