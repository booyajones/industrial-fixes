## MRCOOL Mini Split F1 Error Code — What It Means

On MRCOOL mini splits, the F1 error code indicates that the outdoor ambient temperature sensor, designated T4, is either open or shorted. This is the manufacturer-defined fault for this code. The outdoor control board cannot read a valid signal from the T4 sensor circuit, which may be caused by a failed sensor, damaged wiring, loose connectors, or a faulty board input. The system will not operate normally until the sensor circuit is restored.

## Common Causes

- **Failed outdoor ambient thermistor (T4)** The T4 sensor itself has failed internally, causing an open or short circuit that the board detects as an F1 fault.
- **Loose or disconnected sensor connector** The plug or terminals at the sensor or board end have come loose, creating an open circuit the board reads as F1.
- **Corroded or damaged sensor wiring** Water intrusion, rubbed insulation, or corrosion in the harness creates an intermittent or permanent short or open.
- **Miswired or unplugged sensor leads** During installation or prior service, the T4 leads were connected incorrectly or left unplugged from the outdoor board.
- **Outdoor control board input failure** The PCB input circuit that reads the T4 sensor is damaged, so even a good sensor will produce the F1 code.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the unit** at the breaker or disconnect switch before touching any wiring or sensors in the outdoor section.
2. **Open the outdoor unit service panel** and locate the outdoor control board and the T4 ambient temperature sensor, which is usually mounted on or near the board or the coil.
3. **Inspect the T4 sensor harness and connector** for loose pins, corrosion, water damage, rubbed insulation, or disconnection at the board or sensor end.
4. **Measure the sensor circuit resistance** with a multimeter at the board connector or directly at the thermistor leads to check for open (infinite resistance) or short (zero resistance) conditions.
5. **Compare the reading to manufacturer service data** if available, or swap in a known-good sensor to test. If the reading is implausible or the sensor is damaged, replace the T4 sensor and reconnect it securely.
6. **If the sensor and wiring test good**, inspect the outdoor PCB connector pins and input circuit for damage. If the board does not read a known-good sensor correctly, replace the outdoor control board.
7. **Restore power and run the unit** to confirm the F1 code clears and the system operates normally. Monitor for a few cycles to verify the repair.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical circuits, if you do not have a multimeter or refrigeration tools, or if the F1 code persists after replacing the sensor and inspecting the wiring. A pro can verify the board input circuit, check refrigerant pressures if other faults are present, and make sure the system is safe and compliant with local codes. If your unit is under warranty, contact MRCOOL or your installer before opening the unit to avoid voiding coverage.
