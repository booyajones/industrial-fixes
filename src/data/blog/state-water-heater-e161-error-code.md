## State Water Heater E161 Error Code — What It Means

The E161 error code on State water heaters is not universally defined across all models, so the exact meaning depends on your specific unit. On tankless water heaters, codes in this range typically indicate an ignition, combustion, or airflow problem. The heater is detecting that it cannot establish or maintain proper combustion conditions, which can be caused by gas supply issues, blocked air pathways, restricted water flow, or fan operation faults.

Before troubleshooting, locate the rating plate on your unit and use the QR code to download the exact manual for your model from State's support system. The manual will confirm the precise definition of E161 for your heater and provide model-specific diagnostic steps. Generic troubleshooting may help identify common causes, but the factory guide is the authoritative source for your unit.

## Common Causes

- **Interrupted or inadequate gas supply** The gas shutoff valve may be closed, the supply pressure may be too low, or air may be in the gas line after recent service or an outage.
- **Clogged inlet water filter or low flow** Sediment or debris in the inlet screen restricts water flow below the minimum needed to trigger ignition or maintain operation.
- **Blocked air intake or exhaust vent** Obstructions in the combustion air intake, exhaust termination, or vent piping prevent the heater from drawing in fresh air or expelling exhaust gases.
- **Fan or blower malfunction** The combustion air fan may not be spinning at the correct speed, or the control board may not be sensing proper airflow through the heater.
- **Ignition component or gas valve fault** A failed igniter, flame sensor, or gas valve prevents the burner from lighting or staying lit after gas and airflow are established.
- **Combustion or airflow sensor failure** A faulty pressure switch, airflow sensor, or related control input incorrectly signals unsafe conditions even when gas and air paths are clear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Identify your exact model number** from the rating plate on the water heater and scan the QR code to download the official manual and troubleshooting chart for E161.
2. **Check the gas supply** by confirming the gas shutoff valve is fully open, other gas appliances are working, and the heater's manual shutoff is in the on position.
3. **Inspect and clean the inlet water filter** by shutting off the cold water supply, removing the inlet screen or filter, rinsing it under running water, and reinstalling it securely.
4. **Examine the air intake and exhaust vent** for blockages, nests, debris, or ice buildup, and verify the vent termination meets the clearance and configuration shown in the installation manual.
5. **Power-cycle the heater** by switching off the unit at the control panel or circuit breaker for thirty seconds, then restore power and observe whether the error clears during the next call for hot water.
6. **Listen for fan operation** during startup to confirm the combustion blower is running, and check for unusual noises that suggest a bearing problem or obstruction.
7. **Consult the manual's diagnostic section** for any additional tests, such as gas pressure checks or control board LED patterns, and contact a licensed contractor if the code persists after basic corrections.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the E161 code returns after you have verified gas supply, cleaned the inlet filter, and confirmed clear venting. Diagnosing ignition faults, measuring gas pressure, testing combustion airflow sensors, and replacing control boards or gas valves require specialized tools and training. Any work involving gas connections, combustion components, or electrical control circuits should be performed by a qualified contractor to maintain warranty coverage and make sure safe operation.
