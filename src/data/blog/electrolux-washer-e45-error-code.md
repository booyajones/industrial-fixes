## Electrolux Washer E45 Error Code — What It Means

E45 is part of the E40–E45 family of door-lock and door-interlock faults on Electrolux washers. The machine is not seeing the door as properly locked, or it cannot confirm that the lock-state changes during the cycle. On some models, E45 specifically indicates a communication or control-circuit problem between the main control board and the door lock assembly, meaning the lock signal path may be bad even if the latch looks mechanically fine.

Because Electrolux publishes codes by product family, the exact sub-fault can vary by model, but practical service diagnosis always centers on the door lock system. The washer will not start a cycle or will stop mid-cycle when it cannot verify the door is securely locked.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first when E45 persists, but a simple continuity and fit check of the door-lock wiring harness and connectors often reveals the real problem and costs nothing.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty door lock assembly or interlock (~50%)** The electrical coil or latch-switch inside the lock module fails, so the washer cannot confirm the door is locked.
- **Door not fully closed or gasket obstruction (~20%)** Clothes, the rubber boot, or a damaged gasket prevent the latch from engaging the strike cleanly.
- **Wiring harness or connector issue (~15%)** A loose pin, broken conductor, or corroded connector between the door lock and control board interrupts the lock signal.
- **Main control board or lock-driver fault (~10%)** The PCB is not driving the lock properly or fails to read the lock-state feedback, even when the lock hardware tests good.
- **Mechanical damage to strike, latch, or hinge alignment (~5%)** Worn or bent door hardware keeps the lock from closing reliably, triggering the interlock fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** from the wall outlet to kill all power before opening the door or accessing any internal components.
2. **Open the door and inspect the strike and latch** for foreign objects, clothing caught in the gasket, tears in the rubber boot, or visible damage to the hinge alignment.
3. **Close the door firmly** and listen for a click. If the door does not latch cleanly, adjust the hinge screws or replace the worn strike or gasket before proceeding.
4. **Plug the washer back in and run a power reset** by unplugging for 60 seconds, then plugging in and restarting the cycle to see if the code clears.
5. **Access the door lock assembly** by removing the front panel or opening the inner door boot clamp (consult your model's service manual for the exact fastener locations).
6. **Inspect the door-lock wiring connector** for loose pins, corrosion, heat damage, or broken wires. Clean or repair any connector issues and retest.
7. **Test or replace the door lock assembly** if the wiring is intact but the E45 persists. Disconnect the lock harness, remove the mounting screws, install the new lock, reconnect, and reassemble.
8. **Clear any stored fault codes** using the service diagnostic mode for your model (consult the manual for the keypad sequence), then run a full wash cycle to verify lock engagement, fill, tumble, and door release.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have replaced the door lock assembly and inspected all wiring but the E45 code returns, which points to a main control board fault that requires board-level diagnosis and possibly a service-mode readout. Also call a pro if you are uncomfortable removing the front panel, working around the door boot clamp, or handling the live wiring harness connections. A technician can run the factory diagnostic sequence, measure lock-driver output on the PCB, and confirm whether the board or a subtle wiring fault is at fault. If your washer is still under warranty, any door-lock work should be done by an authorized Electrolux service center to preserve coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–80 in parts, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–250.
