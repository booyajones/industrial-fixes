## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E31 Error — What It Means

The E31 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a flame failure or ignition problem. The unit detects that it cannot establish or maintain a proper flame during the heating cycle. This code means the control board has shut down the burner for safety reasons.

The exact definition can vary slightly between Ruud tankless models, so always consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit for the precise meaning. Common triggers include insufficient gas flow, venting problems that starve combustion air, a fouled flame rod, or a failing igniter.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the igniter or flame sensor first, but these codes are often caused by low gas pressure or a partially closed gas valve. Check that the gas line valve is fully open and that other gas appliances are working normally before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Insufficient gas supply or pressure (~35%)** Low gas pressure or a partially closed valve prevents the burner from igniting or maintaining a stable flame.
- **Blocked or restricted venting (~25%)** Obstructed intake or exhaust pipes reduce combustion air and cause the flame to extinguish or fail to ignite.
- **Dirty or failed flame sensor (~20%)** A fouled flame rod cannot detect the flame signal, causing the control board to shut down the burner as a safety measure.
- **Faulty igniter (~12%)** A weak or broken igniter will not produce a spark strong enough to light the gas, triggering the error.
- **Gas valve failure (~8%)** An internal valve defect or electrical fault in the gas valve assembly can prevent gas from reaching the burner.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the breaker or disconnect switch.
2. **Check the gas supply valve** on the water heater line and confirm it is fully open.
3. **Inspect the intake and exhaust vents** outdoors for blockages, bird nests, or debris and clear any obstructions.
4. **Restore power** and attempt to run hot water to see if the unit fires and the error clears.
5. **If the code persists**, turn off power again and call a licensed technician to test gas pressure at the unit and inspect the flame sensor and igniter.
6. **Do not attempt to disassemble** the burner chamber or gas valve without proper training, as this involves combustible gas and requires specialized tools.
7. **Document the error history** by noting how often the code appears and under what conditions, then share this information with your service technician for faster diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician for any E31 error that does not clear after confirming full gas supply and clear venting. Diagnosis requires a manometer to measure gas pressure at the inlet, and repair often involves working with live gas lines, high-voltage ignition circuits, and sealed combustion components. Incorrect work on gas appliances poses serious safety risks including fire, explosion, and carbon monoxide hazards. A qualified technician will also verify that venting is installed to code and that the unit is receiving the correct gas type and pressure for safe operation.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
