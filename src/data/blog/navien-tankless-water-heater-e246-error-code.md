## Navien E246 Error Code — What It Means

The E246 error code on Navien tankless water heaters generally signals a communication or sensor fault within the unit. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the front panel for your specific unit. In most cases, the code points to a problem with the flow sensor circuit, a wiring connection, or the main control board itself. The unit may refuse to fire or deliver hot water until the fault is cleared.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the flow sensor or control board before checking wiring harness connections and performing a power reset. Inspect all connectors for corrosion or loose pins, and reset the unit by cutting power for two minutes before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~35%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen connector pins at the flow sensor or control board, interrupting the signal.
- **Failed flow sensor (~30%)** The flow sensor may develop internal faults or debris buildup that prevents accurate flow measurement.
- **Control board fault (~20%)** The main PCB can develop solder cracks or component failures that produce spurious error codes.
- **Low incoming water flow (~10%)** Restricted inlet supply or a clogged inlet filter can cause the sensor to read erratically and flag an error.
- **Software glitch or transient voltage spike (~5%)** Electrical noise or a momentary power event can confuse the board until it is reset.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker or disconnect switch and close the gas shutoff valve to the unit.
2. **Wait two minutes**, then restore power and gas and observe whether the E246 code clears on startup.
3. **Remove the front cover** by lifting or unscrewing the retaining fasteners to access the internal components.
4. **Inspect all wire harness connectors** at the flow sensor (usually near the water inlet) and the main control board for loose pins, corrosion, or moisture.
5. **Clean any corroded terminals** with electrical contact cleaner and a small brush, then firmly reseat each connector.
6. **Check the inlet filter screen** by closing the cold-water supply valve, relieving pressure, and unscrewing the inlet service port. Rinse debris from the filter and reinstall.
7. **Test the unit** by opening a hot-water tap and verifying that the heater fires without the error code returning; if the code persists, contact a qualified technician to test the flow sensor and control board with a multimeter or replace as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if the error persists after wiring checks and a power reset, if you are uncomfortable working with 120-volt or gas connections, or if diagnostic testing with a multimeter is required. Navien tankless heaters use both electrical controls and gas valves that demand proper training to service safely. A technician can perform voltage and resistance tests on the flow sensor and control board, interpret advanced fault logs, and order factory-supported parts under warranty if applicable.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
