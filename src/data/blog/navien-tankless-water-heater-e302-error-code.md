## Navien E302 Error Code — What It Means

The E302 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates a flame detection failure. The control board expects to sense flame when the burner fires, but the flame rod sensor is not confirming ignition. This safety feature shuts down the unit to prevent unburned gas from accumulating. The code can appear during startup or while the unit is running, and the heater will lock out until the problem is resolved and the error is cleared.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the gas valve or igniter first, but checking and cleaning the flame rod sensor and its wiring connection takes minutes and solves most E302 errors.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or corroded flame rod sensor (~50%)** Carbon buildup or corrosion on the flame rod prevents it from detecting the flame signal, triggering the error even when the burner ignites normally.
- **Failed flame rod sensor (~20%)** The flame rod itself can crack or break internally, losing its ability to send a signal to the control board.
- **Loose or corroded flame rod wiring (~15%)** A loose connection or corroded terminal at the flame rod or control board interrupts the signal path.
- **Faulty gas valve or low gas pressure (~10%)** If the gas valve does not open fully or supply pressure is too low, the flame may be weak or unstable and not reliably detected by the sensor.
- **Control board failure (~5%)** The control board's flame detection circuit can fail, causing false E302 codes even when the flame rod and flame are both present and correct.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and gas** to the water heater at the breaker and the manual gas shut-off valve, then allow the unit to cool for at least 10 minutes.
2. **Remove the front cover panel** by unscrewing the access screws and carefully setting the panel aside.
3. **Locate the flame rod sensor** near the burner assembly. It is a thin metal rod extending into the combustion chamber, usually with a single wire connected to its base.
4. **Disconnect the flame rod wire** at the connector, then unscrew the flame rod from its mounting bracket.
5. **Clean the flame rod** using fine-grit sandpaper or a soft wire brush to remove carbon deposits and corrosion, then wipe it clean with a dry cloth.
6. **Inspect the flame rod for cracks or damage**. If the ceramic insulator is cracked or the rod is bent, replace the flame rod with a new one.
7. **Reinstall the flame rod** and reconnect the wire, ensuring the connection is snug and the rod tip is positioned correctly in the flame path according to your model's installation diagram.
8. **Restore gas and power**, then reset the error code by pressing the reset button on the control panel or cycling power. Monitor the unit through a full heating cycle to confirm the error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you are uncomfortable working around gas appliances, if the error persists after cleaning or replacing the flame rod, or if you suspect a control board or gas valve problem. Tankless water heaters operate under gas pressure and high voltage, and incorrect repairs can create safety hazards. A technician can test gas pressure, verify control board signals, and perform combustion analysis to pinpoint the root cause.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
