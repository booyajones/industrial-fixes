## Fujitsu E:43 Error Code — What It Means

E:43 on a Fujitsu mini split is a communication error. The indoor unit, outdoor unit, and controller PCB are not talking to each other properly. This is not a refrigerant or temperature problem. It means the control system cannot see the expected signal path between components. The exact meaning can vary slightly by model and controller type, so always verify with your specific model manual, but the core issue is always communication failure at startup or during operation.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or miswired interconnect wiring** The field wiring between indoor and outdoor units is open, partially connected, pinched, or wired incorrectly.
- **Loose or corroded connector** A plug at the controller PCB, external I/O board, or unit harness is not fully seated or has corroded pins.
- **Failed controller or main PCB** The controller board or main control PCB has failed and can no longer send or receive communication signals.
- **Power supply or grounding issue** Voltage drop, poor ground, or electrical interference from another load on the same circuit disrupts communication.
- **Damaged filter or transistor PCB** On models with intermediate boards, a fault in the filter PCB or transistor PCB breaks the communication chain.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset power** by turning off the breaker or disconnect for at least five minutes, then restore power and check if the error clears.
2. **Inspect all interconnect wiring** between the indoor and outdoor units for breaks, pinches, loose terminations, or incorrect wire placement.
3. **Check every connector** at the indoor PCB, outdoor PCB, controller PCB, and any external I/O or filter boards for full insertion, corrosion, or bent pins.
4. **Verify supply voltage and grounding** at the unit to confirm no voltage drop, poor ground connection, or interference from other equipment on the circuit.
5. **Test wiring continuity** by isolating the field wiring path and checking for open circuits or shorts as directed by your model's service manual.
6. **Replace the controller PCB** if wiring and power check out but the fault persists, and reset any remote address settings after installation.
7. **Clear power again and retest** after any repair to confirm the communication fault does not return on startup.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with line voltage, if the error returns after a power reset, or if you cannot locate loose wiring or connectors. Communication faults often require DC voltage testing and board-level diagnosis that is difficult without a service manual and multimeter experience. If you have already checked wiring and connectors and the fault persists, a technician will need to isolate the failed PCB and verify proper communication protocol between units.
