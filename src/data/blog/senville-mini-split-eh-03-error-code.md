## Senville Mini Split EH 03 Error Code — What It Means

The EH 03 code (sometimes displayed as E3) signals that your Senville mini split's indoor fan is not spinning at the correct speed or responding properly to the control board. The system detects that the fan motor is operating outside its normal range, which triggers a shutdown to prevent damage. This is an indoor-unit problem, not related to the outdoor compressor or refrigerant.

## Common Causes

- **Obstruction in the blower wheel** Dust, debris, or an object blocking the fan prevents it from turning freely or reaching normal speed.
- **Loose or corroded motor wiring** The connector between the indoor fan motor and control board may be loose, damaged, or corroded, interrupting the signal.
- **Failed indoor fan motor** Worn bearings or a burnt winding inside the blower motor stop it from spinning smoothly or reaching target RPM.
- **Faulty indoor control board (PCB)** The main circuit board may not be delivering the correct voltage to the motor, even when the motor itself is healthy.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power at the breaker** and wait two full minutes, then restore power to see if the code clears on its own.
2. **Remove the front cover and filters** from the indoor unit and visually inspect the blower wheel for dust buildup, objects, or anything binding the fan.
3. **Spin the fan blade by hand** with power off. It should rotate smoothly without resistance. If it binds, scrapes, or feels rough, the motor bearings are likely worn.
4. **Check the motor wiring connector** on the indoor control board. Look for loose pins, corrosion, or burn marks and reseat the connector firmly.
5. **Measure the voltage** at the indoor fan motor connector with a multimeter while the unit is in standby or test mode. On 208-240 V models, voltage below 100 V indicates a bad board. On 115 V models, below 50 V means replace the board.
6. **Replace the indoor fan motor** if the wheel does not spin freely or if voltage is correct but the code persists after reconnecting the motor.
7. **Replace the indoor control board** if the motor spins freely, connections are clean, and measured voltage is out of spec or the error returns after a new motor is installed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working with live voltage measurements or if the error persists after you have cleared obstructions and checked connections. Replacing the fan motor or control board requires removing the indoor unit cover, disconnecting refrigerant lines in some cases, and handling sensitive electronics. If your unit is still under warranty, contact Senville support before opening the unit to avoid voiding coverage.
