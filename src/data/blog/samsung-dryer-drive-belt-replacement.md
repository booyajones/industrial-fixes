## Samsung Dryer Drive Belt Replacement — What This Part Does

The drive belt wraps around the dryer drum and connects it to the motor pulley and idler pulley so the motor can rotate the drum during a cycle. The belt is under constant tension and friction, so over time it will crack, fray, glaze, stretch, or break completely.

When the belt fails or slips off, the motor runs but the drum stays still. Samsung does not flag this as an electronic fault code. You diagnose it by opening the cabinet and inspecting the belt, idler pulley, and drum support rollers for wear or damage.

## Signs It Needs Replacing

- **Drum does not spin but motor runs** The motor hums or runs normally but the drum stays completely still, meaning the belt is broken or has slipped off the pulleys.
- **Squealing or slipping noise during operation** A glazed or worn belt can slip on the pulleys and produce high-pitched squealing or rubbing sounds.
- **Burning smell from the dryer** Belt slip or friction against a seized idler pulley can create a burning rubber odor.
- **Visible cracked, frayed, or broken belt** Inspecting the belt through the cabinet shows cracks, thinning, fraying, or a complete break in the rubber.
- **Drum spins too freely by hand** When the belt is off or broken, the drum rotates with almost no resistance instead of the normal firm drag.

## How to Replace It {#fix}

1. Unplug the dryer from the wall outlet and if it is a gas dryer, shut off the gas supply valve before beginning service.
2. Remove the dryer top and front panels per your model's cabinet design to expose the drum, belt path, motor assembly, and idler pulley.
3. Inspect the old belt for cracking, fraying, glazing, or breakage and check the idler pulley for free rotation, bearing wear, or physical damage.
4. Check the drum support rollers and belt path for burrs, sharp edges, or other mechanical interference that could damage the new belt.
5. Remove the old belt from around the drum, motor pulley, and idler pulley, noting the correct routing and ribbed-side orientation.
6. Install the new belt around the drum with the ribbed side facing the drum and route it over the motor pulley and under the spring-loaded idler pulley to establish proper tension.
7. Rotate the drum by hand to confirm the belt tracks cleanly on all pulleys and does not slip or rub against the cabinet or drum edges.
8. Reassemble the front and top panels, reconnect any harness plugs or door switch connections that were removed, and restore power.
9. Run a short test cycle to verify the drum rotates normally, the belt stays in place, and there are no unusual noises or slip.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable disassembling the cabinet panels or routing the belt around multiple pulleys under spring tension. If the drum support rollers are worn or noisy, they are often replaced at the same time as the belt to prevent future drum damage or uneven rotation. For gas dryers, any work involving the burner assembly, gas connections, or igniter should be handled by a qualified service professional to prevent gas leaks or fire hazards.
