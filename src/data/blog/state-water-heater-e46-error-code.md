## State Water Heater E46 Error Code — What It Means

E46 on State tankless water heaters indicates a flame-sensor or combustion-failure condition during the burner ignition sequence. The control board detected that the burner did not light properly, or the flame sensor could not prove the flame after ignition. This is an ignition and flame-proving fault in the A. O. Smith and State tankless code family, not a temperature or pressure alarm.

Because State error codes vary by platform (atmospheric, power-vent, tankless), always verify your exact model and serial number from the rating plate or QR code to pull the correct manual. The E46 fault typically locks out the burner until the underlying issue is resolved and the unit is reset.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake** Lint, dust, insect nests, or other debris blocking the combustion air inlet prevents proper airflow for ignition.
- **Low gas pressure or closed supply valve** Inadequate gas flow or partially closed valve stops the burner from lighting or causes intermittent flame loss.
- **Dirty flame rod or sensor** Scale, carbon, or corrosion buildup on the flame-sensing rod prevents the control board from detecting the flame.
- **Restricted water flow or clogged inlet filter** Low water flow through a dirty filter can prevent the unit from initiating the ignition sequence on some tankless models.
- **Obstructed venting or exhaust** Blocked, kinked, or improperly terminated vent pipe restricts exhaust flow and causes combustion failure.
- **Failed ignition assembly or gas valve** A faulty igniter, control board, or gas valve prevents burner lighting even when air, gas, and water flow are correct.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and gas** at the shutoff valves before performing any inspection or cleaning.
2. **Locate your model and serial number** from the rating plate or QR code and download the correct service manual from the State website for your specific unit.
3. **Inspect and clean the air intake screen** for lint, dust, or blockage, and verify nothing is obstructing the combustion air inlet.
4. **Check venting and exhaust** for blockage, proper termination, and correct slope, and clear any obstruction in the vent pipe.
5. **Remove and clean the flame rod** with fine sandpaper or a soft cloth to remove scale, carbon, or corrosion, then reinstall securely.
6. **Verify gas supply** is fully open and delivering adequate pressure, and check the inlet water filter for restriction or debris.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit according to your manual. If E46 returns immediately, test the gas valve, ignition assembly, and control board with a multimeter or call a technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the E46 code returns after cleaning the air intake, flame rod, and inlet filter, or if you are not comfortable working with gas connections and electrical testing. A pro should also diagnose and replace the gas valve, ignition control board, or wiring harness when component-level testing is required. If your unit is still under warranty, contact State or your installer before replacing any parts to avoid voiding coverage.
