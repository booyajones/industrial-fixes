## What this code means
E1 on a Hydrotherm boiler indicates ignition failure. The boiler control attempted to light the burner and did not detect a stable flame within the allotted trial-for-ignition period. Hydrotherm condensing and standard atmospheric boilers use this code to signal that the ignition sequence completed without establishing combustion. The boiler locks out and will not retry until manually reset.

## Common Causes

- **Failed or contaminated flame sensor** — A dirty or oxidized flame sensor rod cannot generate the microamp flame rectification signal required to confirm ignition to the control board.
- **Gas supply problem** — The manual gas shutoff valve is closed or gas pressure is too low. A failed gas valve that does not open during the trial period also prevents ignition.
- **Faulty spark igniter** — The spark electrode gap is incorrect, the insulator is cracked, or the ignition module is not generating a spark during the ignition window.
- **Blocked combustion air or exhaust vent** — A restricted intake or exhaust vent prevents proper air-fuel mixture and combustion, causing the flame to fail to establish or be immediately extinguished.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset the boiler** — Locate the reset button on the Hydrotherm control and press it firmly. The boiler will initiate a new ignition sequence. Observe the sequence to identify where it fails.
2. **Clean the flame sensor** — Remove the flame sensor rod from the burner assembly and gently clean the rod tip with fine steel wool. Reinstall and reset. A clean sensor resolves the majority of E1 faults.
3. **Verify gas supply** — Confirm the manual shutoff valve upstream of the boiler is fully open. Check that other gas appliances in the building have normal pressure. If you suspect low gas pressure, contact the gas utility.
4. **Check the spark electrode** — Inspect the spark electrode for correct gap (typically 1/8") and look for cracks in the ceramic insulator. Replace if the insulator is damaged or the electrode tip is heavily worn.
5. **Inspect the combustion air intake and exhaust vent** — Check both the intake and exhaust vent pipes for obstructions: bird nests, ice, debris at the termination cap. Ensure the vent pipe joints are intact and not allowing recirculation.
6. **Test the gas valve** — Listen for the gas valve to click open during the ignition sequence. If you hear no click, measure voltage at the gas valve terminals during the trial (should be 24V AC or the rated control voltage). No voltage indicates a control board issue; voltage present with no valve operation indicates a failed valve.
7. **Reset and verify** — After repairs, reset the boiler and confirm it completes a full ignition and heating cycle without re-locking on E1.

## When to Call a Pro

Gas valve replacement and combustion air system diagnosis require a licensed technician. Repeated E1 faults without a clear cause should be investigated by a Hydrotherm-certified service technician to avoid gas accumulation hazards.
