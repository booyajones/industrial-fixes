## Navien Tankless Water Heater E167 Error — What It Means

The E167 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically indicates an internal communication or control board problem. The exact meaning can vary slightly between models, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the unit for model-specific definitions. In general, this code suggests the main control board is not receiving proper signals from one of the internal components, or there is a fault in the board itself. Because tankless water heaters rely on multiple sensors, switches, and circuit boards working together, an E167 often points to a wiring issue, a faulty connector, or a control board that needs replacement.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the main control board without checking connector pins and harness continuity first. Inspect all plug connections at the board and use a multimeter to verify wiring integrity before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~40%)** Vibration or moisture can cause harness plugs at the control board to work loose or develop corrosion, interrupting communication signals.
- **Failed main control board (~30%)** Internal component failure on the printed circuit board can generate spurious error codes when the board can no longer process signals correctly.
- **Intermittent sensor fault (~15%)** A temperature sensor or flow sensor with an intermittent open circuit may send erratic data that the board interprets as a communication error.
- **Electrical noise or power surge (~10%)** Voltage spikes from the utility or a nearby appliance can scramble microprocessor memory and trigger a communication fault code.
- **Faulty wiring harness (~5%)** A damaged wire or poor crimp inside the harness can create an open or high-resistance path that disrupts board-to-sensor communication.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off electrical power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas shut-off valve to the water heater.
2. **Wait 30 seconds**, then restore power to perform a hard reset of the control board and see if the error clears.
3. **Remove the front cover** of the heater by unscrewing the panel fasteners (consult your model's manual for exact locations).
4. **Inspect every wiring harness connector** at the main control board for snug fit, corrosion, or burn marks on the pins.
5. **Reseat each connector** firmly, ensuring you hear or feel a positive click, and check that no wires are pinched or frayed.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then run a hot-water tap to initiate a heating cycle and observe whether the E167 code reappears.
7. **If the code persists**, document which connectors you checked and call a licensed technician to perform continuity tests on the harness and board outputs.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician whenever you are uncomfortable working around gas lines, high-voltage wiring, or internal control boards. Tankless water heaters integrate gas valves, flame rods, and microprocessor controls that require specialized diagnostic equipment to test safely. If free checks and connector reseating do not clear the E167 code, a professional can measure voltage and continuity at each sensor, verify board outputs, and replace the control board or harness under proper safety protocols. Attempting board-level repairs without training risks electric shock, gas leaks, and voiding your warranty.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-500.
