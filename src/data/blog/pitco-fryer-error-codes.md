# Pitco Commercial Fryer Error Codes Guide

Pitco fryers use either a mechanical thermostat, a solid-state controller, or a digital computer such as the Intellifry or Solstice control. Error messages vary by controller, but most service calls come from ignition problems, probe faults, high-limit trips, and filtration issues.

## Most Common Pitco Fryer Faults

### E7 — Ignition Lockout
Check gas first. Verify manual gas cock is open and inlet pressure matches Pitco spec. Then inspect the igniter, spark wire, and flame sensor. A dirty burner rack or poor ground path often causes repeated ignition lockouts.

### E6 — High Limit Open
If the high-limit opens, the fryer overheated. Let the fryer cool before resetting. Then check the operating probe, controller output, and gas valve. If the fryer overshoots setpoint by a wide margin, replace the probe or controller before returning the unit to service.

### FILTER Message
On units with built-in filtration, the controller expects a filter cycle after a certain number of cooks. Check the filter pan position, pump motor, and all quick disconnects. If the pump hums but does not move oil, inspect the filter screen and pump impeller.

### Probe Faults (E4/E5)
Pitco controllers rely on accurate probe feedback. Check resistance against the temperature chart from the service manual. If the reading is unstable, inspect the harness where it routes near the frypot, because insulation damage from heat is common.

> **Pro tip:** Watch the fryer through a full heat cycle after any repair. Pitco faults often clear after reset, but the real problem shows up when the fryer heats, idles, and recovers under load.
