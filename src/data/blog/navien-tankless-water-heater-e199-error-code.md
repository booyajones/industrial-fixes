## Navien E199 Error Code — What It Means

The E199 error code on Navien tankless water heaters typically signals a communication problem between the main control board and other internal components, or a general system fault that the unit cannot categorize more specifically. Because Navien uses different code meanings across model lines, the exact definition of E199 can vary. Consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the front cover to confirm what E199 means for your specific model.

In most cases, this code appears after a power interruption, loose wiring connection, or voltage irregularity rather than a failed part. The unit may display E199 when it detects an out-of-range signal or fails to receive expected feedback from a sensor or relay.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main PCB board when E199 appears, but a loose connector or dirty terminal is far more common. Reseat every plug at the board and check for corrosion before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connector (~40%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen plugs at the main board or sensors, breaking communication paths.
- **Transient voltage spike or power interruption (~20%)** A brief power loss or surge can leave the microprocessor in a fault state that persists until reset.
- **Failing main control board (PCB) (~15%)** Capacitors or solder joints on the board can fail over time, causing intermittent communication errors.
- **Faulty sensor or relay (~15%)** A temperature sensor or flow sensor sending garbled data can trigger a system-wide fault code.
- **Low or unstable supply voltage (~10%)** Incoming voltage below the unit's threshold can prevent stable operation and trigger fault codes.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off electrical power** at the breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by loosening the screws at the bottom and lifting the panel off its hooks.
3. **Photograph all wiring** at the main control board so you have a reference for reassembly.
4. **Reseat every connector** by pulling each plug straight out and firmly pressing it back into its socket on the board.
5. **Inspect terminals and pins** for any green corrosion, burn marks, or bent contacts and clean with electronics contact cleaner if needed.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit by pressing the power button or cycling the breaker.
7. **Observe the display** for 2-3 minutes to see if E199 reappears or if the unit returns to standby mode without error.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are uncomfortable working with 120V wiring, if the error persists after reseating connectors and resetting the unit, or if you measure supply voltage outside the range printed on the rating plate. A pro can use Navien diagnostic software to read fault history and pinpoint which sensor or relay is sending bad data. Also call a pro if you see any signs of water intrusion on the board or if the unit has been exposed to flooding, since internal damage may require multiple parts and a safety inspection.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-150 in parts if a connector or relay is replaced, 20-40 min. A pro service call runs about $150-350 including diagnostics and parts.
