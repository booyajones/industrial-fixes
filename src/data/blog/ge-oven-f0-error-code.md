## GE Oven F0 Error Code — What It Means

F0 tells you the electronic oven control sees a key that reads as pressed when no one is touching it, or a short across the touch panel. The board reads that constant signal as a held button and stops normal operation.

On most GE ranges and wall ovens the keypad and front control panel are one assembly, so a true F0 usually points at that panel or the connection between it and the board.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Stuck or shorted key** A membrane key has shorted or is physically jammed and the board reads it as held down.
- **Loose ribbon cable** The flat ribbon between the touch panel and the control has worked loose or corroded at the connector.
- **Moisture or grease intrusion** Steam or cooking residue behind the panel bridges contacts and creates a false key signal.
- **Failed touch panel** The membrane keypad itself has degraded and now sends a bad signal even after a reset.
- **Failed control board** The electronic oven control has faulted and misreads the keypad input.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for 2 to 3 minutes to force a full control reset.
2. Restore power and watch whether F0 returns within a minute.
3. If it returns, kill power again and remove the rear or under-panel screws to reach the control.
4. Locate the flat ribbon cable from the keypad to the control and unplug it, then firmly reseat it.
5. Inspect the panel and connector for moisture, grease, or burnt traces and clean gently.
6. Restore power and test. If F0 stays, replace the touch panel or control panel assembly.
7. Match the replacement panel to the full model number and transfer any brackets and screws.

## When to Call a Pro

If F0 stays after a reset and a panel swap, or you find scorched traces on the board, bring in an appliance tech. Live control wiring behind the panel carries line voltage and is not worth a guess.
