## Samsung Refrigerator 25E Error Code — What It Means

The 25E code signals the ice making system is not working as expected. The board may see a stuck ice maker, a frozen line, or no water reaching the tray.

A weak or failed water inlet valve is one of the most common roots, since the valve must open and pass enough water to fill the ice mold.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed water inlet valve** The valve will not open or passes too little water so the mold never fills.
- **Low water pressure** Supply below about 20 psi keeps the valve from delivering a full fill.
- **Frozen fill tube** Ice in the water line or fill tube blocks water to the tray.
- **Jammed ice maker** A stuck rake or frozen mold stalls the harvest cycle.
- **Faulty ice maker sensor** A bad sensor in the assembly reports the wrong state to the board.
- **Freezer too warm** If the freezer is not cold enough the ice cycle will not complete.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Run a forced ice maker test or reset using the test button on the assembly.
2. Check that the household supply and saddle valve are fully open with good pressure.
3. Thaw any frozen fill tube by unplugging and letting the area warm, then dry it.
4. Unplug the fridge and check the water inlet valve for power and continuity with a multimeter.
5. Confirm supply pressure at the valve is at least 20 psi.
6. Replace the water inlet valve if it has power and pressure but still will not fill.
7. Restore power, run a fresh ice cycle, and confirm the code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

If water reaches the tray and the valve checks out but 25E persists, the ice maker control or main board may be involved. Bring in a technician for board level ice maker diagnosis.
