## Fujitsu E:15 Error Code — What It Means

The E:15 code on a Fujitsu mini-split signals a high discharge temperature fault. The outdoor unit has detected that the compressor discharge line is running too hot, and the system has stopped or limited operation to protect the compressor from damage. This is a refrigeration-cycle protection mechanism, not a communication or sensor-only error.

In practical terms, something is causing the compressor to work harder than it should or preventing it from rejecting heat properly. The control board monitors the temperature at the compressor discharge line, and when it climbs beyond safe limits, it trips this fault and shuts down to prevent permanent damage.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or blocked outdoor coil** Dust, cottonwood, leaves, or other debris restricting airflow across the condenser coil is the most common trigger for high discharge temperature.
- **Outdoor fan failure or reduced speed** A failed fan motor, loose connector, or mechanically jammed fan blade prevents air from moving across the coil, causing discharge temperature to spike.
- **Low refrigerant charge or refrigerant restriction** Undercharge or a blockage in the refrigeration cycle forces the compressor to run hotter and drives discharge temperature up.
- **Faulty discharge temperature sensor or wiring** An open, shorted, or out-of-range thermistor or damaged sensor harness can report false high temperature and trigger the fault.
- **Overcharged refrigerant system** Too much refrigerant increases head pressure and compressor discharge temperature, especially under high ambient load.
- **Compressor or reversing valve issue** Internal compressor wear or a stuck valve can create abnormal discharge conditions that elevate temperature beyond the safe threshold.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker and wait two minutes, then power back on to see if the fault was transient or if it reappears under load.
2. **Inspect the outdoor unit** for visible obstructions, debris buildup on the coil, or blocked discharge air pathways, and clean the coil thoroughly with coil cleaner and a soft brush if dirty.
3. **Verify outdoor fan operation** by powering the unit on and confirming the fan spins freely, reaches full speed, and is not making unusual noise or failing to start.
4. **Check the discharge temperature sensor and wiring** at the outdoor unit by inspecting the sensor connector for looseness, corrosion, or damage, and measure thermistor resistance against the model's thermistor table in the service manual.
5. **Measure refrigerant pressures** on both the high and low side while the system runs, and compare to the model's target values to identify overcharge, undercharge, or restriction in the cycle.
6. **Test the outdoor fan motor and circuit** by confirming DC voltage between red and black wires (150 to 380 V) and black and white wires (15 V) per Fujitsu service guidance, and replace the motor if readings are absent or the motor does not respond.
7. **Replace the identified faulty component** (sensor, fan motor, or PCB), restore power, run the system under load for at least 20 minutes, and confirm the E:15 fault does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live 240 V power, recovering and recharging refrigerant, or interpreting pressure and temperature diagnostics. The E:15 fault requires refrigeration-cycle analysis, and misdiagnosis can lead to compressor damage or refrigerant loss. If you have cleaned the coil, confirmed the fan runs, and the fault persists, a technician with gauges, a thermistor table, and Fujitsu service software is needed to isolate whether the root cause is electrical, mechanical, or refrigerant-side.
