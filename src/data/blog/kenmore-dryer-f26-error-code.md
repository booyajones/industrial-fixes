## Kenmore Dryer F26 Error Code — What It Means

The F26 error code on Kenmore HE-style dryers indicates a door switch fault. The control board has detected that the door switch circuit is not functioning correctly, which prevents the dryer from starting or continuing a cycle. This fault can be caused by a defective door switch, a door that is not closing completely, or damaged wiring in the door switch circuit. Some technician sources mention motor control errors, but manufacturer documentation and field experience point to the door switch and its circuit as the primary issue.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Defective door switch** The switch itself has failed internally and no longer signals proper door closure to the control board.
- **Door not closing completely** The door latch or strike is misaligned, broken, or obstructed so the door switch is never engaged.
- **Broken or loose wiring** Wiring in the door switch circuit has become disconnected, corroded, or damaged, breaking continuity to the control.
- **Faulty door latch assembly** The mechanical latch or its mounting hardware is worn or broken and does not activate the switch properly.
- **Control board detection issue** The machine control electronics may have a fault in reading the door switch circuit, though this is less common.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** or turn off the circuit breaker to make sure no power is present before you begin any inspection or testing.
2. **Inspect the door latch and strike** for proper alignment, damage, or obstructions, and verify the door closes firmly and engages the switch with a distinct click.
3. **Access the door switch** by removing the top or front panel as required for your model, then disconnect the switch wiring harness.
4. **Test the door switch with an ohmmeter** by placing probes across the switch terminals and manually actuating the switch; a properly functioning switch should show 0 to 2 ohms of resistance when closed.
5. **Inspect all wiring and connectors** in the door switch circuit for breaks, corrosion, or loose pins, and repair or replace any damaged harness sections.
6. **Replace the door switch** if it fails the continuity test or shows intermittent operation, then reconnect all wiring and reassemble panels.
7. **Restore power and run a timed dry cycle** to verify the F26 code does not return; if the code reappears after switch replacement and wiring checks, consult your model's tech sheet for control board diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the F26 code persists after you have verified door closure, replaced the door switch, and checked all wiring for continuity. Intermittent faults that only appear during certain cycles or after the drum has been running may indicate a control board issue that requires diagnostic equipment and access to the manufacturer's tech sheet. If you are uncomfortable working with electrical testing or disassembling panels, a professional can complete the diagnosis and repair safely.
