## Bosch Washer E07 Error Code — What It Means

The E07 error code on many Bosch washers typically indicates a problem with the door-locking mechanism. The washer control expects confirmation that the door is securely closed and locked before the cycle can start or continue. When the lock does not engage, does not report its status, or fails to release, the machine halts and displays E07.

Because error-code meanings can vary across Bosch washer model lines, always cross-check your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the service panel. Some Bosch models use different code schemes, so confirming the exact definition for your serial number is the safest first step.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board when E07 appears, but testing continuity and voltage at the door-lock connector will pinpoint whether the lock itself or its wiring is at fault before spending money on electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door-lock assembly (~50%)** The solenoid or relay inside the lock wears out and no longer signals the control board that the door is secure.
- **Misaligned door striker (~20%)** The plastic or metal catch on the door edge has shifted or broken, so the latch cannot engage the lock body on the frame.
- **Broken wiring or loose connector (~15%)** Vibration or a previous repair left the harness plug partially unseated or a wire fractured near the lock terminals.
- **Control-board relay fault (~10%)** The main board's lock-drive relay has failed and cannot send power to actuate the lock solenoid.
- **Foreign object in latch slot (~5%)** A piece of fabric, detergent buildup, or debris prevents the striker from fully entering the lock cavity.

## Quick Diagnosis

Answer these to narrow it down fast.

<details class="dtree"><summary>Does the door close smoothly and click into place without force?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The striker and hinge alignment are probably fine; proceed to electrical tests of the lock assembly.<br><strong>No:</strong> Inspect the door hinges for sag and the striker for cracks or offset; realign or replace the striker before testing electrical components.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>With the machine unplugged, does the lock assembly connector show corrosion or a loose fit?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> Clean the terminals with electrical contact cleaner, reseat firmly, and retest; a poor connection often causes intermittent E07.<br><strong>No:</strong> The connector is sound, so test the lock itself for continuity or replace it if the resistance is infinite across the coil terminals.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>After replacing the door lock, does E07 still appear?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The control board may have a failed relay or the wiring harness has a break; consult a technician to trace the circuit.<br><strong>No:</strong> The new lock has resolved the fault; run a test cycle to confirm normal operation.</div>
</details>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and shut off the water supply valves to work safely.
2. **Open the door** and inspect the striker plate on the door edge for cracks, looseness, or misalignment; tighten or replace if damaged.
3. **Remove the screws** securing the door-lock assembly to the front panel (usually accessed from inside the door opening) and pull the lock body forward to expose the wire connector.
4. **Disconnect the harness plug** and use a multimeter set to ohms to measure continuity across the lock coil terminals; consult your model's service manual for expected resistance values.
5. **Install a new door-lock assembly** if the reading is open or infinite, routing the connector back through the panel and securing the lock with the original screws.
6. **Reconnect power and water**, close the door firmly, and start a short rinse cycle to verify the lock engages and E07 does not return.
7. **Check for leaks** around the door seal and confirm the drum spins without error before returning the machine to service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have replaced both the door lock and the striker yet E07 persists, if you measure correct voltage arriving at the lock connector but the lock still will not actuate, or if tracing wiring through the cabinet reveals a break you cannot safely repair. A technician has the service manual, harness diagrams, and board-level test equipment to isolate control-board faults or intermittent wiring shorts that are difficult to diagnose at home.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–80 in parts, 30–45 min. A pro service call runs about $150–250.
