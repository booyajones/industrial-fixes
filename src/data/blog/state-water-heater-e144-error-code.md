## State Water Heater E144 Error Code — What It Means

The E144 error code is not documented in available State Water Heaters manufacturer materials, so its exact meaning depends on your specific model. State directs users to locate the model number and serial on the rating plate, then access model-specific troubleshooting through the QR code support system on the unit. Without the exact model manual, this code cannot be definitively interpreted.

Based on typical tankless water heater fault behavior, an unconfirmed error like E144 often points to ignition failure, combustion problems, or system lockout. Common triggers include gas supply interruption, blocked air intake or exhaust venting, clogged inlet filter screens, dirty flame sensors, or poor combustion conditions. Before attempting any repair, you must retrieve your model's documentation to confirm what E144 means for your unit.

## Common Causes

- **Gas supply interruption or low pressure** Insufficient gas delivery or closed supply valves prevent the burner from lighting or maintaining stable combustion.
- **Blocked air intake or exhaust vent** Obstructions in the intake or exhaust pathways disrupt combustion air flow and trigger safety lockouts.
- **Clogged cold-water inlet filter screen** Sediment buildup in the inlet filter restricts flow, causing the unit to fail startup or trigger flow-related protection.
- **Dirty or failing flame sensor** Contamination or corrosion on the flame-sensing rod prevents the control from detecting flame, resulting in ignition lockout.
- **Burner contamination or poor combustion quality** Dirt or debris in the burner assembly or combustion chamber degrades flame quality and prevents reliable startup.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Locate your model number** on the rating plate and scan the QR code or visit State support online to download the correct manual and confirm the official E144 definition for your unit.
2. **Shut off power and gas** to the water heater, wait one minute, then restore both and attempt to clear the fault by resetting the unit per the manual.
3. **Inspect the air intake and exhaust** vent terminations outside for blockages, nests, or debris, then clear any obstructions and check the vent piping for proper slope and secure connections.
4. **Remove and clean the cold-water inlet filter screen** by shutting off the cold supply valve, unscrewing the inlet union, pulling the screen, rinsing under water, and reinstalling it.
5. **Check gas supply** by confirming the manual gas shutoff valve upstream of the heater is fully open and verifying gas flow at another appliance on the same line.
6. **Inspect and clean the flame sensor** by removing the burner access cover, locating the flame rod near the burner, gently cleaning it with fine-grit sandpaper or a soft cloth, and reseating all connectors.
7. **Test the unit** by calling for hot water and observing the ignition sequence. If the fault persists, consult the model-specific diagnostic chart in your manual or contact a licensed technician for combustion analysis and gas-train testing.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the fault returns after clearing vents and filters, if you cannot locate your model manual to confirm the E144 definition, if you smell gas or observe yellow flames, or if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances. Combustion diagnostics, gas pressure measurement, and control board replacement require specialized tools and training. Any persistent ignition or lockout fault that does not resolve with basic cleaning and airflow correction should be evaluated by a professional to prevent unsafe operation or further damage.
