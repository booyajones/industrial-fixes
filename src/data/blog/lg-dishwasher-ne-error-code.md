## LG Dishwasher nE Error Code — What It Means

The nE code on LG dishwashers indicates the unit has detected a possible issue with the vario motor, which controls the dishwasher's spray function. This motor manages water distribution between spray paths during the wash cycle.

The error appears when the control board cannot operate the vario motor correctly. The fault may be in the motor itself, the wiring harness that connects it, or the main control board that drives the motor. The dishwasher will not complete a normal cycle until the fault is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first, but the actual problem is usually the vario motor or a loose connector. Test the motor electrically and inspect all connectors before buying any parts.

## Common Causes

- **Failed vario motor (~55%)** The motor windings are open, shorted, or the motor is mechanically seized and cannot turn.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness (~25%)** The connector at the motor or underneath the unit is loose, corroded, has burnt terminals, or damaged insulation that interrupts the circuit.
- **Faulty main control board (~15%)** The board is not supplying the correct voltage or signal to drive the motor even though the motor and wiring are good.
- **Mechanical obstruction or binding (~5%)** Food debris, a broken diverter, or a jammed spray-path component prevents the motor from moving normally and triggers the fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker to the dishwasher before opening any panels or touching wiring.
2. **Reset the dishwasher** by leaving power off for two minutes, then restore power and run a short cycle to confirm the nE code reappears and is not a random fault.
3. **Access the vario motor area** by removing the lower kickplate and possibly the side panel to reach the motor and its wiring harness underneath the tub.
4. **Inspect all wiring and connectors** at the motor and along the harness for looseness, burnt pins, corrosion, or damaged insulation, and reseat or repair any faults you find.
5. **Test the vario motor resistance** using a multimeter set to ohms (consult your model's service manual for the correct range, as published values vary by series and are not universal across all LG models).
6. **Check the control board output** to the motor during a diagnostic cycle if your model supports test mode, or measure voltage at the motor connector during operation to verify the board is sending power.
7. **Replace the failed component** (motor, harness, or control board) based on your test results, reassemble the dishwasher, and run a full cycle to confirm the code is cleared.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with the dishwasher's electrical system, testing components with a multimeter, or interpreting voltage and resistance readings. The vario motor is located under the tub and requires panel removal and precise connector work. If you have already inspected the wiring and the fault persists, a technician can perform model-specific diagnostic tests, verify control board output, and replace the correct component without trial and error. Professional diagnosis is especially important if you lack the service manual with the exact electrical specifications for your model.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $80-180 in parts, 1-2 hours. A pro service call runs about $180-350.
