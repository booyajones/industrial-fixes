## Bosch E22 Error Code — What It Means

E22 on Bosch dishwashers signals a drainage restriction, typically caused by clogged filters or debris blocking the pump inlet. Bosch support describes this code as "filters blocked," meaning the machine detects that water cannot leave the sump normally because the filter assembly is dirty, not seated correctly, or the drain path is obstructed. This is a flow problem, not a heating or electrical fault. In most cases the dishwasher will not complete its cycle and may leave standing water in the bottom of the tub.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged or dirty filter assembly** Food particles, grease, and debris accumulate in the cylindrical filter and fine screen, blocking water flow and triggering the error.
- **Debris in the sump or pump inlet** Seeds, broken glass, labels, or other objects lodge around the drain pump impeller and prevent the pump from moving water.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose** The hose from the pump to the sink or disposal is crushed, looped too tightly, or internally blocked by buildup.
- **Filter not fully locked in place** After cleaning, the filter cap or cylinder was not rotated fully clockwise, leaving a gap that the machine reads as a blockage.
- **Failed or jammed drain pump** The pump motor is seized, the impeller is broken, or internal wear prevents the pump from creating enough flow to satisfy the drain sensor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** at the breaker or unplug the dishwasher completely before opening any panels or touching internal parts.
2. **Remove the lower dish rack** and locate the cylindrical filter assembly in the center of the tub floor.
3. **Unlock and remove the filter** by turning the outer cap counterclockwise, then lift out the cylindrical screen and fine mesh filter, rinse all pieces under warm running water, scrub away grease and particles with a soft brush, and inspect the sump opening below for any debris.
4. **Check the drain pump area** by removing any visible food, glass, or foreign objects from the sump cavity and around the impeller blades if accessible.
5. **Inspect the drain hose** from the pump connection to the sink or disposal for kinks, crushing, or blockage, straighten or clear as needed, and confirm the hose end is not submerged in standing water.
6. **Reinstall the filter assembly** by placing the fine screen and cylinder back into the sump, rotating the cap fully clockwise until it locks, and confirming no gap remains between the cap and the tub floor.
7. **Restore power and run a rinse or drain cycle** to verify the tub empties completely and the E22 code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the E22 code returns after you have cleaned the filter, cleared the sump, and inspected the drain hose. A persistent fault may indicate a failed drain pump, a wiring issue between the pump and control board, or a pressure sensor problem that requires diagnostic tools and part-specific testing. Also call for help if you find water leaking from the pump housing or if the dishwasher will not power on after reassembly.
