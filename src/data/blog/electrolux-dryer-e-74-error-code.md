## Electrolux Dryer E74 Error Code — What It Means

E74 on an Electrolux dryer indicates a drive motor relay circuit failure. The control board is not correctly energizing or sensing the relay circuit that powers the drive motor. This code is distinct from door, sensor, or ventilation blockage codes and points specifically to the motor relay circuit rather than heating or temperature components.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed main control board or relay** The relay circuitry on the electronic control board has failed and cannot energize the motor circuit.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness** Connectors or wires between the control board and motor circuit are burned, loose, or have damaged pins.
- **Motor circuit fault** The drive motor itself has developed a fault that prevents the relay circuit from behaving normally.
- **Mechanical load issue** On some models, blower wheel problems or mechanical binding can trigger relay circuit faults in the 71-74 code family.
- **Incorrect line voltage** Supply voltage issues at the outlet can cause the control board to flag a relay circuit failure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Disconnect power at the breaker and unplug the dryer before any diagnostic work.
2. Verify incoming line voltage at the wall outlet to confirm the dryer is receiving proper power before moving to internal checks.
3. Remove the dryer's access panels and inspect the wiring harness from the main control board to the motor circuit for loose terminals, burns, pin damage, or broken conductors.
4. Check the drive motor and blower wheel for mechanical binding or other load issues that could affect the relay circuit.
5. Test the motor circuit connections at the control board and motor terminals for continuity and proper seating.
6. Replace the main electronic control board if wiring and motor checks pass, since the relay circuit on the board is the most common failure point.
7. Reassemble the dryer, restore power, and run a test cycle to confirm the E74 code clears and the motor starts and stops normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable working with live voltage testing, if you lack a multimeter and wiring diagrams for your model, or if the code persists after you have checked all connectors and replaced the control board. Relay circuit diagnosis requires tracing low-voltage signals and confirming board output, which is easier with service documentation and experience.
