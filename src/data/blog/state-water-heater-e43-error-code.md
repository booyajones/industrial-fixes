## State Water Heater E43 Error Code — What It Means

E43 is an ignition or combustion fault on State tankless water heaters. It means the control board attempted to light the burner but did not receive a stable flame signal, so the unit locked out for safety. This is a flame-proving failure rather than a simple temperature or flow alarm.

On State and A. O. Smith family tankless products, this fault is most commonly triggered by blocked combustion air intake, insufficient gas supply or pressure, or low water flow caused by a clogged inlet filter. The unit will not resume normal operation until the underlying cause is corrected and the system is reset.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake** Lint, debris, insect nests, or restricted combustion-air screens prevent proper airflow and are the most common cause reported for this fault.
- **Low gas supply or pressure** Insufficient gas flow or pressure at the inlet prevents the burner from establishing flame during ignition.
- **Dirty inlet water filter** A clogged cold-water inlet screen reduces flow below the threshold needed for safe ignition and operation.
- **Fouled flame rod or sensor** Carbon buildup or corrosion on the flame-sensing probe prevents the board from detecting combustion even when flame is present.
- **Blocked or restricted venting** Obstructed exhaust vent or intake piping disrupts combustion airflow and exhaust gas evacuation, causing unsafe flame conditions.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the E43 code persists after you have cleaned the air intake, verified gas supply, and flushed the inlet water filter. Diagnosing ignition faults beyond these steps requires measuring gas pressure at the valve, testing the flame-sensor circuit for correct resistance and microamp signals, inspecting the igniter and gas-valve operation under live conditions, and verifying control-board inputs. Work on gas appliances and combustion systems carries safety and code-compliance risks that require proper training, tools, and licensing. If you see repeated lockouts, smell gas, or notice unusual combustion behavior, shut off the gas supply and call for service immediately.
