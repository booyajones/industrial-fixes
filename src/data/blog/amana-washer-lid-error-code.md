## Amana Washer Lid Error Code — What It Means

Amana top-load HE washers display LdL when the control does not see the lid lock engage, LdU when something prevents the lid from unlocking, or 'lid' when the lid is simply left open. LdL and LdU are true fault codes that stop the cycle, while 'lid' is a reminder message. The exact code and behavior depend on your model platform, so confirm what appears on the console.

These codes protect the washer from spinning with the lid open or from forcing a locked lid. The lock stays engaged whenever the basket is moving and releases only after the drum comes to a complete stop. If the code persists after the basket stops and the lid is fully closed, the lock mechanism or its circuit needs attention.

## Common Causes

- **Clothing or basket obstructing the latch** An item in the load is physically keeping the lid from locking into the strike.
- **Detergent buildup or lint around the lock** Residue in the lid-lock area prevents proper engagement or release of the latch.
- **Object on top of the washer blocking lid movement** Something resting on the lid keeps it from seating fully or releasing after unlock.
- **Basket still spinning** The lock remains engaged until the tub fully stops, so the unlock fault clears once motion ceases.
- **Failed lid-lock assembly** The actuator or switch inside the lock is defective and does not report status to the control.
- **Damaged lid strike or latch mate** The plastic receiver on the lid or cabinet is cracked or misaligned so the lock cannot engage.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Note the exact code** displayed on the console (LdL, LdU, or 'lid') and write down the full model number from the washer label so you can reference the correct platform.
2. **Close the lid completely** and check that no clothing, detergent bottles, or other items are preventing the lid from seating flat against the cabinet.
3. **Inspect the lid-lock area** for lint, detergent residue, or broken plastic around the strike and receiver, then wipe the area clean with a damp cloth.
4. **Wait for the basket to stop** if you hear the drum still moving, because the lock will not release until motion ceases.
5. **Unplug the washer** for five minutes to reset the control, then restore power and try a new cycle to see if the code clears.
6. **Run the service diagnostic** mode for your model if the error persists, and follow the on-screen tests to check the lid-lock assembly, wiring harness, and control response.
7. **Replace the failed component** (lid lock, strike, or harness connector) if the lock does not actuate, does not report status, or shows physical damage after obstruction and reset checks.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the code returns after you have cleared all obstructions, cleaned the lock area, and completed a full power cycle. A pro can run the factory diagnostic sequence, measure the lock circuit with a meter, and determine whether the fault is in the actuator, harness, or control board. If your washer is still under warranty or if you are uncomfortable working around the lid-lock mechanism and electrical connections, professional service is the safest path.
