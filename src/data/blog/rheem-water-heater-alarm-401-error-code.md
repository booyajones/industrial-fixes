## Rheem 401 Alarm — What It Means

Alarm 401 on Rheem tankless water heaters is a maintenance notification, not a breakdown. The unit has accumulated a high number of combustion hours and requires service. Unlike ignition or flame faults, this code tells you the system needs cleaning and inspection to continue running efficiently. The water heater may still operate, but addressing the alarm now prevents future performance issues and potential safety concerns.

## Common Causes

- **Normal combustion-hour accumulation** The unit has reached its programmed maintenance interval based on total burn time, triggering the reminder automatically.
- **Dirty or clogged burner assembly** Carbon buildup, dust, or debris on the combustion chamber and burner surfaces reduces efficiency and triggers the maintenance flag.
- **Restricted air intake or venting** Blocked air filters, intake screens, or vent terminations reduce airflow and force the unit to work harder, accelerating service needs.
- **Condensate drain blockage** On condensing models, a clogged condensate path can affect combustion performance and prompt the maintenance alarm.
- **Neglected periodic service** Skipping annual or manufacturer-recommended maintenance allows minor issues to compound until the alarm activates.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the model number and confirm the displayed code** on the unit's control panel or digital interface, then check the alarm history to confirm it is a 401 maintenance notification rather than a different fault.
2. **Turn off power and gas supply** to the water heater at the service disconnect and gas shutoff valve before opening any access panels or working on internal components.
3. **Inspect and clean the burner and combustion chamber**, removing dust, carbon deposits, and debris from the burner surfaces, flame rods, and any accessible combustion pathway using a soft brush or vacuum.
4. **Check the air intake screen or filter** for blockages and clean or replace it, then verify that vent terminations are clear and properly installed with no restrictions or damage.
5. **Inspect the condensate drain** (if your model has one) for clogs or buildup, flush the drain line with water, and confirm that condensate flows freely from the unit.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then clear the maintenance alarm using the procedure in your model's service manual (often a button sequence or control-board reset), and run the unit through a complete heating cycle to verify normal operation.
7. **Record the service date and reset the maintenance timer** if your model allows, and schedule the next service according to Rheem's recommended interval for your installation conditions.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the alarm will not clear after you complete maintenance, if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances, or if you find damaged combustion components, wiring issues, or error codes that appear alongside the 401. A qualified Rheem service provider has the model-specific diagnostic tools, combustion analyzer, and access to manufacturer bulletins needed to verify safe operation and address any underlying issues that a maintenance cleaning alone cannot resolve.
