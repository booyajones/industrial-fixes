## Hobart Commercial Dishwasher Error Codes — What They Mean

Hobart commercial dishwashers (AM15, CLeN, UW50, and FOSS series undercounters, door-types, and flight machines) use electronic controls that display fault codes when problems are detected. Hobart is the dominant commercial dishwasher brand in US foodservice.

## Most Common Hobart Dishwasher Codes and Fixes {#fix}

### E1 / E2 — Temperature Faults
The wash tank or final rinse isn't reaching required temperature. Check: booster heater element continuity, thermostats, incoming hot water temperature (should be 140°F minimum for final rinse on low-temp units). High-temp units also need the tank heater.

### E3 — Low Water Level / Fill Fault
Machine isn't filling to the required level. Check: water supply pressure (at least 20 PSI), inlet valve strainer for scale, water valve solenoid coil resistance, float switch operation.

### E4 — Drain Fault
Machine not draining completely. Check: drain pump operation, drain hose for kinks or blockage, check valve in drain line.

### E5 — Wash Pump Motor
Wash pump motor failure or overload. Check pump motor for seized impeller, burned windings, or tripped overload. Wash pump is the primary cleaning mechanism — this is urgent.

### E7 — Door Switch
Door not closed or door interlock switch fault. Verify door closes fully and latch engages. Check door switch continuity.

## How to troubleshoot Hobart Dishwasher

Hobart's fault behavior differs by product family, so identify your series first: LXi/LXiG and AM Select undercounters show short codes (E0/E2/E3/E4 fill, E5 drain, E6 service-required drain/fill fault, CL/Pr overfill-leak or water-level-probe fault, P1-P3 temperature probe). LXe undercounters show plain-text alerts (FILL ERROR, DRAIN ERROR, WASH ARM ALERT, LOW RINSE TEMP). CL/CLe conveyor and flight machines show text prompts (Clear Conveyor Jam, Probe Err - TANK). Newer LXn/LXGn and Ecomax units use numeric codes. A code that means "fill" on one family can be an unrelated number on another, so never map a code across series.

Start with the cheap, common causes before assuming a board or sensor failure. The majority of fill, overfill, and level-sensor faults (E0-E5, CL/Pr, Probe Err) trace back to two things: a clogged inlet strainer or low incoming water pressure, and scale or food soil on the water-level probes. Confirm the water supply is on with adequate pressure, clean the inlet/hose strainer, and clean the level probes. Regular deliming prevents most repeat sensor faults in hard-water areas; many machines will even prompt to delime on their own.

For temperature and sanitizing problems (low final-rinse temp, P-codes, LOW RINSE TEMP), verify the incoming hot water temperature and that the booster heater and its thermostat are working. Critical safety point: if final rinse never reaches the sanitizing setpoint, dishes are not sanitized. Pull them and re-sanitize rather than trusting a warm-looking cycle, and treat a persistent low-rinse-temp alert as a food-safety issue, not a nuisance. A P1/P2/P3 fault shuts off the affected heater while the machine keeps washing, so the load can run cold - verify sanitizing temperatures until it is repaired.

A soft reset clears many transient faults: put the machine in standby, wait about 60 seconds, then power it back up. Call an authorized Hobart service agent when a code returns immediately after a reset, when the fault involves the control board or temperature sensors that need replacement (E6 and P-codes are routed to service), or for any high-voltage booster/heater element work. Element and sealed-electrical repairs are pro-only.


## Frequently asked questions

### My Hobart shows E0, E2, E3, or E4 and won't run. What's wrong?

On LXi and AM Select undercounters these are all fill faults, meaning the machine isn't filling or isn't reaching the proper level. Check that the water supply valve is open with adequate pressure, clean the inlet strainer, make sure the drain valve isn't stuck open, and clean scale off the water-level probes. The unit stays locked out until it fills correctly.

### What does a CL or Pr code mean on my Hobart dishwasher?

CL/Pr indicates overfill or a leak, typically that the machine is filling too high or water is escaping the door. The usual causes are a unit that isn't level, dirty water-level probes, or a leaking or faulty inlet valve. Level the machine, clean the probes, and inspect or replace the fill valve.

### The final rinse isn't hitting temperature (low rinse temp / P-code). Are my dishes safe?

No, not automatically. If final rinse doesn't reach the sanitizing setpoint the load is not sanitized, so re-sanitize those dishes before use. Then check the incoming hot water temperature and the booster heater and thermostat. A P1/P2/P3 code points to a failed temperature sensor, which should be replaced.

### How do I reset a Hobart dishwasher?

Place the machine in standby, wait about 60 seconds, then turn it back on. This clears many transient faults, including a 'Reset Required' message. If the same code reappears right after the reset, stop and have it serviced rather than repeatedly power-cycling it.

### How often should I delime my Hobart, and why do sensor faults keep coming back?

In hard-water areas, limescale on the water-level probes and heaters is the leading cause of repeat fill, overfill, and sensor faults. Delime on the schedule your manual specifies (the machine may prompt you when a delime is due). Regular deliming and probe cleaning prevents most recurring E-series and probe errors.
