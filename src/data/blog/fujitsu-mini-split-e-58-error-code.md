## Fujitsu E:58 Error Code — What It Means

Error code E:58 on a Fujitsu mini-split indicates an intake grille position error. The indoor unit's control system detects that the front intake grille or panel is not in the expected closed and locked position, or the position feedback signal from the grille sensor is not being received correctly. This fault prevents normal operation because the unit cannot verify that airflow paths are properly configured.

In most cases, the grille is either not fully seated, a latch tab is broken or worn, or the wiring harness for the position switch has come loose. The error will persist until the unit confirms the grille is secure and the position signal is valid.

## Common Causes

- **Grille or front panel not fully closed** The intake grille is misaligned, warped, or blocked by an obstruction and cannot seat properly into the latch mechanism.
- **Broken or worn latch tabs** Plastic clips or hinge tabs on the grille assembly are cracked, stretched, or missing and no longer hold the panel securely.
- **Loose or disconnected wiring connector** The plug for the grille position switch has come loose, is not fully seated, or wiring in the harness is open or miswired.
- **Defective position switch or sensor** The grille position sensor itself has failed and does not send a valid closed signal even when the panel is latched.
- **Faulty indoor controller PCB** The main control board on the indoor unit is not correctly reading the grille position input due to a circuit fault or component failure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker or disconnect switch before opening or inspecting the indoor unit.
2. **Remove and reseat the intake grille or front panel**, ensuring it clicks fully into place and all latch tabs engage without resistance or misalignment.
3. **Inspect the grille latch tabs and hinges** for cracks, breaks, warping, or wear that would prevent a secure fit, and check for any obstructions along the seating edges.
4. **Locate and check the grille position switch connector** on the indoor unit harness, confirming it is fully plugged in, free of corrosion, and that no wires are pinched or broken.
5. **Restore power and test** the unit to see if the error clears after reseating the grille and verifying connections.
6. **Test the position switch circuit** (if you have a multimeter and wiring diagram) by checking continuity or voltage at the connector when the grille is open versus closed, or consult your model's service manual for resistance values.
7. **Replace the indoor controller PCB** if the grille closes correctly, all wiring tests normal, and the error persists, indicating a fault in the signal-processing circuit on the board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if the grille appears to close properly and all visible connections are secure but the E:58 code returns immediately. Diagnosing position-switch circuits and PCB signal faults requires a wiring diagram, multimeter skills, and familiarity with the specific model's control architecture. A technician can also source and install the correct OEM controller board or sensor assembly and verify refrigerant-side integrity if the unit was opened for extended periods during troubleshooting.
