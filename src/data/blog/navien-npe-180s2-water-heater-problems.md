## Navien NPE-180S2 Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Navien NPE-180S2 is a condensing tankless water heater in the NPE series. The most commonly reported problems are ignition and flame faults, venting and airflow faults, thermistor sensor failures, and flow or scale-related faults that trigger error codes on the display. Gas supply issues, blocked intake or exhaust piping, dirty filters, scale buildup restricting flow or heat transfer, and failed sensors or corroded connectors are the real-world triggers technicians see most often in the field.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **E003 ignition failure code** Gas valve closed, low gas pressure, dirty or failed igniter, or blocked venting prevents the burner from lighting.
- **E012 flame loss during operation** Flame dropout is caused by gas supply or pressure problems, intake air restriction, dirty flame rod, or poor grounding connections.
- **E010 air pressure or venting fault** Blocked vent or intake piping, dirty air filter, condensate drain issues, or improper termination trigger the air-pressure fault.
- **E002 low water pressure or flow** Restricted inlet filter, low incoming water pressure, or closed shutoff valve prevents adequate flow through the heat exchanger.
- **E001 overheating or boiling in heat exchanger** Scale buildup or blocked inlet filter restricts flow and causes water to overheat inside the heat exchanger.
- **E006 or E008 thermistor short fault** Water intrusion, damaged wiring, or a failed temperature sensor creates a short-circuit condition at the inlet or outlet thermistor.
- **E007 or E005 thermistor open fault** Loose connector, broken wire, or open temperature sensor causes an open-circuit reading at the inlet or outlet thermistor.
- **No hot water or low temperature output** Scale buildup in the heat exchanger, incorrect gas pressure, or restricted flow reduces heating capacity and water temperature.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician for any gas supply, gas pressure, or gas valve work on this unit. Thermistor replacement, igniter or flame rod service, venting inspection, and heat exchanger descaling or flushing should also be handled by a trained pro familiar with Navien NPE-series diagnostics and fault-code workflows. If you see repeating ignition or flame codes after clearing the fault, or if the unit locks out, a tech with a manometer and combustion analyzer is needed to verify gas pressure, airflow, and burner operation safely. For gas line, burner, or igniter work, or if you ever smell gas, stop and call a licensed technician.
