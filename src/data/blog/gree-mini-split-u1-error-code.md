## Gree U1 Error Code — What It Means

The U1 error code on a Gree mini split signals a compressor current-circuit malfunction. This means the outdoor unit's inverter board cannot correctly read the electrical current flowing through the compressor's phase wires, or the circuit responsible for sensing that current has failed. When the board detects this condition, the system will stop operating to prevent damage. Gree classifies U1 as an outdoor-unit protection fault that requires professional attention to diagnose and repair the specific component failure.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty current-sensing circuit on outdoor board** Components on the drive board that measure compressor current have failed or the analog-to-digital input is damaged.
- **Loose or damaged compressor phase wiring** Connections between the compressor terminals and the outdoor board are corroded, burned, loose, or open.
- **Miswired or reversed compressor leads** Phase wiring was installed incorrectly or became disconnected, preventing the board from reading valid current feedback.
- **Failed compressor** Internal damage to the compressor causes abnormal current draw or prevents normal operation, triggering the fault.
- **Outdoor PCB or IPM module damage** The drive board's inverter power module or current-sampling traces have burned or degraded, blocking accurate readings.
- **Outdoor power supply problems** Incoming power issues or wiring faults upstream prevent the board from establishing proper current sensing.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the disconnect and breaker before opening the outdoor unit, since U1 indicates a board or high-current fault.
2. **Inspect outdoor wiring and terminals** for burn marks, corrosion, loose connectors, or damaged insulation on the compressor phase leads and power harness.
3. **Check compressor phase connections** at both the board and compressor terminals to confirm they are tight, correctly phased, and free of damage.
4. **Examine the outdoor control board** for visible signs of failure such as burned traces, damaged current-sensing resistors, or failed components near the compressor connections.
5. **Test the compressor** for internal faults if wiring and board appear intact, following your model's service manual for resistance and insulation checks.
6. **Replace the failed component**, whether damaged wiring, the outdoor PCB, or the compressor, based on the inspection results.
7. **Restore power and monitor** the system through a full cooling cycle to confirm the U1 fault does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician for any U1 fault. Gree's service documentation directs professional diagnosis and repair because the fault involves high-voltage inverter circuitry, precise current measurements, and potential compressor replacement. Misdiagnosis or incorrect wiring during repair can damage the new board or create a safety hazard. A qualified tech has the metering tools and model-specific service data to isolate whether the fault lies in the wiring, the board's sensing circuit, or the compressor itself, and can safely replace the correct component under warranty if applicable.
