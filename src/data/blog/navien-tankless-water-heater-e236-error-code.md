## Navien Tankless Water Heater E236 Error — What It Means

Error code E236 on a Navien tankless water heater typically signals an issue with water flow, temperature sensing, or internal heating components. The exact definition can vary slightly by model, so consult your owner's manual for the precise meaning. In general, the unit is detecting conditions that prevent it from heating water safely or efficiently, such as insufficient flow through the heat exchanger or a sensor reading outside expected ranges. The code protects the system from damage by shutting down operation until the problem is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flow sensor first, but scale on the heat exchanger or a clogged inlet filter is often the real culprit. Flush the system and clean filters before swapping sensors.

## Common Causes

- **Scale buildup on heat exchanger (~35%)** Mineral deposits restrict water flow and insulate the temperature sensor, causing erratic readings and triggering the error.
- **Low inlet water pressure (~25%)** Insufficient supply pressure prevents the unit from reaching minimum flow requirements to activate heating.
- **Clogged inlet filter (~15%)** Sediment or debris in the cold water inlet screen reduces flow and can mimic a flow sensor fault.
- **Faulty flow sensor (~15%)** A worn or failed flow sensor sends incorrect data to the control board, preventing normal operation.
- **Temperature sensor drift (~10%)** An aging thermistor reports inaccurate water temperature, causing the board to shut down for safety.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the inlet cold water isolation valve.
2. **Remove and clean the inlet filter screen** located on the cold water service valve. Rinse under running water and reinstall.
3. **Check static water pressure** at a nearby tap with a gauge. If below 20 psi, address the building supply or pressure regulator.
4. **Perform a vinegar or descaling flush** by connecting a pump and hoses to the service valves, circulating descaler through the heat exchanger for 45 minutes, then flushing with clean water. Consult your owner's manual for the procedure.
5. **Inspect the flow sensor wiring** for loose connectors or corrosion. Reseat all connections firmly.
6. **Test the flow sensor resistance** with a multimeter if you have the model's service manual. Replace the sensor if readings are out of range.
7. **Restore power and water** and run a hot water tap. Monitor for the error. If it persists after cleaning and flushing, call a qualified technician to test temperature sensors and the control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien service technician if you are uncomfortable working with gas connections, if descaling the heat exchanger does not clear the error, or if you lack the tools to test sensors and circuit boards. A professional can isolate faulty components quickly with diagnostic software and pressure-test the system after repairs. Persistent E236 codes after basic cleaning often point to sensor replacement or control board issues that require specialized knowledge and warranty considerations.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
