## Maytag Refrigerator Light Not Working — What's Happening

A Maytag refrigerator light that won't turn on is not a fault code problem. It's a component issue involving the LED light assembly or bulb, the door switch that signals the light to turn on, the wiring harness under the top hinge, or the control board. The interior light is controlled by the door switch, and on many newer models the light will also shut off automatically after the doors have been open for 10 minutes. Closing all doors resets this timeout.

Most cases trace back to a burned-out LED module, a door switch that isn't being pressed when the door closes, or a loose wiring connector at the top hinge after the unit was moved or the doors were removed. If both the refrigerator and freezer compartment lights are out at the same time, the control board becomes a stronger suspect because door switches signal the board to turn the lights on and off.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Burned-out LED assembly or bulb** The most common cause is a failed LED module on newer models or a burned-out appliance bulb on older units with a replaceable socket-based lamp.
- **Failed or misadjusted door switch** The door switch may not be physically engaged when the door closes due to switch failure, the refrigerator being out of level, or door misalignment.
- **Loose or unplugged wiring harness** The wiring connector under the top hinge cover can become loose or unplugged if the unit was recently moved or the doors were removed for service.
- **Bad light socket** On models with a serviceable bulb, the socket may show discoloration, loose wires, or a cracked socket that prevents the bulb from making contact.
- **Automatic timeout active** The light turns off after the door has been open longer than 10 minutes and may need all doors closed to reset the control logic.
- **Magnetic or electronic interference** Magnets or electronic devices within 3 inches of the door switch can interfere with proper switch operation.
- **Failed control board** If both refrigerator and freezer compartment lights are out and door switches test good, the control board that receives the switch signals may have failed.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Close all doors and wait 30 seconds to clear the automatic 10-minute timeout condition, then reopen the door and check if the light comes back on.
2. Inspect the door switch to verify it is physically being pressed when the door closes and released when the door opens.
3. Check that the refrigerator is level and the doors are aligned properly so the door switch makes full contact when closed.
4. Unplug the refrigerator and remove the top hinge cover to inspect the wiring harness connector for a loose or unplugged connection.
5. On models with a replaceable bulb, tighten the bulb in the socket and replace it with an appliance bulb of the same size, shape, and wattage (40 watts maximum) if it is burned out.
6. On LED models, check the LED module connection and replace the entire LED assembly if the module is dark or flickering.
7. Inspect the light socket on bulb-based models for discoloration, loose wires, or cracks, and replace the socket if damaged.
8. If both refrigerator and freezer lights are out and all wiring and switch checks pass, test the control board or have a technician evaluate it for replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you've replaced the LED assembly or bulb and checked the door switch and wiring but the light still won't turn on. Also call for help if both the refrigerator and freezer compartment lights are out at the same time and you're not comfortable testing the control board. A pro can trace the wiring harness, test switch continuity, and verify whether the control board is sending power to the light circuit.
