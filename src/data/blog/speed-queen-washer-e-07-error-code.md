## What this code means
The E:07 (or E7) code on a Speed Queen washer indicates an inoperative control or control communication fault. The washer's electronic control system has detected that a required control function or communication path between boards is not working correctly. This is not a heater fault. The issue typically involves loose or corroded wiring connections, damaged harness conductors, or a failed control board. In many cases, the fault can be cleared by reseating connectors or cycling power to reset the control.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or disconnected harness plugs** Wiring connectors at the front-end control board or drive board can work loose over time or fail to seat fully during previous service.
- **Oxidized or corroded terminals** Moisture or age causes corrosion on connector pins, breaking the electrical path between control modules.
- **Damaged harness conductors or burnt pins** Physical damage, pinching, or heat can break wires or burn connector terminals in the harness between controls.
- **Control lockup or power interruption** A brief power spike or brownout can freeze the control processor, causing a communication fault that clears with a power cycle.
- **Failed front-end or drive control board** If all connections and wiring are verified good, one of the electronic control boards has failed internally and must be replaced.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and wait 60 seconds to de-energize the control system and allow capacitors to discharge.
2. **Remove the top panel** by releasing the clips or screws at the rear, then sliding it back and lifting it off to access the control area.
3. **Inspect all wiring harness connectors** at the front-end control board and drive board, looking for loose plugs, bent pins, corrosion, or burnt terminals.
4. **Reseat every connector** firmly, and repair or replace any damaged harness segments or terminals you find.
5. **Restore power** and run a short test cycle to check whether the E:07 code clears and the washer operates normally.
6. **If the fault returns** after verifying all connections and harness integrity, replace the front-end control board first, then the drive board if needed.
7. **Consult the wiring diagram** for your model (located inside the cabinet or in the service manual) to trace communication paths if the problem persists.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical components, if you cannot access or identify the control boards, or if the fault returns after you have reseated all connections and cycled power. A technician has the wiring diagrams, multimeter skills, and board-level diagnostic tools to isolate communication faults quickly. If the washer is still under warranty, contact Speed Queen or an authorized service center before opening the cabinet, since DIY repairs may void coverage.
