## Panasonic Microwave U12 Error Code — What It Means

The U12 error code on a Panasonic microwave indicates the control board has detected a self-diagnostic malfunction. Panasonic does not publish a specific definition for U12 in their public microwave troubleshooting guides, so the exact fault depends on your model. Unlike user-resettable messages, this code means the appliance requires professional diagnosis. Stop using the microwave immediately and unplug it to prevent further damage or safety hazards.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Door interlock or latch fault** A failed door switch or misaligned latch may prevent the control from confirming the door is securely closed.
- **Control board communication error** The main control may lose signal from a sensor or relay, triggering a diagnostic fault code.
- **Magnetron or high-voltage component failure** A fault in the magnetron, capacitor, or diode can cause the control to halt operation and display an error.
- **Wiring or connector damage** Loose, corroded, or burned connectors between the control board and other components interrupt signal paths.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the microwave** from the wall outlet and leave it disconnected for at least five minutes to clear temporary faults and discharge high-voltage capacitors.
2. **Restore power** and check if the U12 code clears on its own after the reset period.
3. **Inspect the door** for visible damage, misalignment, or foreign objects around the latch and striker, then open and close it firmly to test the interlock.
4. **Note your exact model number** from the rating label inside the door frame or on the back panel, as the U12 fault definition varies by model.
5. **Contact an authorized Panasonic servicenter** with your model number, since Panasonic advises professional service for all self-diagnostic error codes.
6. **Do not operate the microwave** until a qualified technician diagnoses and repairs the fault to avoid electrical shock or fire hazards.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional immediately for any persistent U12 code on a Panasonic microwave. Panasonic states that self-diagnostic error codes require service by an authorized technician. Microwaves contain high-voltage capacitors that can deliver lethal shocks even when unplugged, and only trained technicians have the tools and schematics to safely diagnose model-specific faults. Attempting DIY repairs on microwave high-voltage circuits risks serious injury and voids your warranty.
