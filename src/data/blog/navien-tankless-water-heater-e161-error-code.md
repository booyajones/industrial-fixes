## Navien E161 Error Code — What It Means

The E161 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates a communication fault between internal control boards or components. This code typically appears when signals cannot pass properly between the main PCB and other system modules. The exact meaning can vary slightly by model, so consult your owner's manual or the unit's wiring diagram for model-specific details.

Because Navien units rely on integrated electronic control systems to manage ignition, gas flow, and temperature, any interruption in communication can prevent normal operation. The unit will often shut down as a safety measure when this error is detected.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board when the real problem is a loose connector or failed ribbon cable. Inspect all wiring harnesses and connectors for corrosion or looseness before ordering boards.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wire connection (~40%)** Vibration, moisture, or age can loosen connectors between the main board and sensors or ignition modules, breaking the signal path.
- **Failed ribbon cable or internal harness (~25%)** Flex cables that link circuit boards can crack or develop hairline breaks, especially in high-heat environments.
- **Main PCB failure (~20%)** The primary control board can develop solder joint failures or component faults that disrupt communication with other modules.
- **Low or unstable incoming voltage (~10%)** Voltage sags, brownouts, or loose connections at the breaker can prevent the control system from maintaining stable communication.
- **Grounding issue (~5%)** Poor grounding can introduce electrical noise that corrupts digital signals between boards.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and wait two minutes to allow the control system to fully discharge.
2. **Restore power** and observe whether the E161 code returns immediately or after attempting to fire the unit.
3. **Remove the front cover** of the heater according to your model's instructions, exposing the main control board and wiring.
4. **Inspect all connectors** on the control board for signs of looseness, corrosion, or burn marks, and gently reseat each plug.
5. **Check the ribbon cables** or internal harnesses that link multiple boards, looking for cracks, kinks, or discoloration.
6. **Verify incoming voltage** at the breaker and measure at the unit's terminals if you have a multimeter (consult your model's voltage specifications).
7. **Document your findings** and contact a qualified Navien technician if the error persists after reseating connections and confirming stable power.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the error returns after reseating all visible connectors and power cycling the unit, or if you lack experience working with line-voltage wiring and control boards. Navien tankless heaters integrate gas, water, and electronic controls, and misdiagnosis can lead to unsafe operation or expensive part replacements. A qualified technician will have diagnostic tools to measure board communication, test voltage stability, and isolate the exact point of failure. Always call a pro if you see burn marks, smell unusual odors, or if the unit has been exposed to water intrusion or power surges.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
