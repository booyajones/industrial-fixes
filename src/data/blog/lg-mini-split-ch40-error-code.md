## LG Mini Split CH40 Error Code — What It Means

The CH40 code on an LG mini split indicates an inverter AC current abnormal condition in the outdoor unit. The control board has detected that the compressor or inverter circuit is drawing current that is too high, unstable, or inconsistent with normal operation. This is an electrical fault in the outdoor unit's inverter system, not a sensor error indoors. The unit shuts down to protect the compressor and inverter components from damage.

## Common Causes

- **Compressor winding fault** Unequal winding resistance between phases, an open or shorted winding, or insulation breakdown to ground will all trigger abnormal current sensing.
- **Compressor mechanical failure** A seized rotor, hard-starting compressor, or internal mechanical damage causes the motor to draw excessive starting or running current.
- **Inverter PCB or power module fault** The outdoor board's current-sensing circuit or drive output stage can fail, reporting incorrect current readings or delivering abnormal power to the compressor.
- **Wiring or connector problem** Loose, corroded, or damaged connections between the inverter board and compressor create intermittent high resistance and current spikes.
- **Supply voltage issue** Unstable or incorrect incoming voltage to the outdoor unit can contribute to abnormal compressor current draw and inverter operation.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the breaker and disconnect, then remove the outdoor unit service panel to access the compressor and inverter board.
2. **Check incoming power supply** at the outdoor disconnect and at the board terminals to confirm stable voltage within the unit's nameplate range.
3. **Inspect all wiring and connectors** from the inverter PCB to the compressor for loose pins, burn marks, corrosion, or damaged insulation.
4. **Measure compressor winding resistances** between each pair of terminals using an ohmmeter. All three windings should read equal and fall between 1 and 4 ohms.
5. **Test insulation to earth** by measuring resistance from each compressor terminal to the compressor case or ground. You should see a minimum of 50 megohms on each.
6. **Check inverter board outputs and run current** if you have the tools and model-specific voltage tables. Look for abnormal waveforms or current spikes during compressor startup.
7. **Replace the faulty component**: if winding resistance is unequal, open, or insulation is below 50 megohms, replace the compressor. If windings and wiring are good but the code persists, replace the inverter PCB.

## When to Call a Pro

CH40 requires both electrical diagnostics and refrigerant-circuit work. If you are not EPA-certified and equipped with an insulation tester (megohmmeter), quality multimeter, and the ability to safely recover refrigerant and replace a sealed-system component, call a licensed HVAC technician. Compressor and inverter board replacement both involve high-voltage AC, refrigerant handling, brazing, evacuation, and recharge. Misdiagnosis can lead to expensive part swaps, and improper refrigerant work is illegal and unsafe. A qualified tech can quickly isolate whether the fault is in the compressor windings, the inverter drive circuit, or the wiring, and complete the repair correctly the first time.
