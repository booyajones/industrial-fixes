## Maytag Oven LOC Error Code — What It Means

On a Maytag oven or range, LOC is not a failure code. It indicates the Control Lock feature has been activated, which disables the keypad and control inputs to prevent unintended use. On some models the oven door may also lock depending on the operating state. The display shows LOC whenever you press any button while the control is locked. Maytag instructs you to repeat the lock/unlock procedure to clear it, and the display will return to normal once unlocked.

## Common Causes

- **Accidental activation of Control Lock** Holding the wrong key or a lock-related key for the required time activates the feature unintentionally.
- **Button press while already locked** Pressing any control pad when the lock is on causes LOC to reappear on the display.
- **Temporary electronic or display glitch** A minor control hiccup can be cleared by a full power reset at the breaker.
- **Feature state, not a component fault** LOC reflects a user-activated control state rather than a failed sensor, relay, or door switch.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the symptom** by confirming the display shows LOC, Lock, or Locked and the oven does not respond to normal keypad input.
2. **Check whether Control Lock is engaged** by making sure the oven and timer are off, then touch and hold START/ENTER for 5 seconds (a single tone sounds and LOC appears when locking).
3. **Repeat the same action** (hold START/ENTER for 5 seconds) to unlock the control and remove LOC from the display.
4. **Retest keypad response** after unlock to confirm LOC has disappeared and the oven accepts commands.
5. **Perform a power reset** if the lock will not clear normally by turning off the circuit breaker(s) for 1 minute, then restoring power and observing for 1 minute to confirm the code does not return.
6. **Verify operation** by running a short cycle or pressing keys to confirm the control is fully responsive.
7. **Inspect the control interface** (user interface panel or electronic control board) only if the unit still behaves as locked after proper unlock and power reset, consulting model-specific service literature before ordering parts.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the control will not unlock after you hold START/ENTER for 5 seconds and perform a full 1-minute breaker reset. If the display continues to show LOC or the keypad stays unresponsive after these steps, the user interface panel or electronic control board may need diagnosis. Because LOC is normally a user-activated feature rather than a hardware fault, persistent lock behavior after proper unlock procedure indicates a control electronics issue that requires service literature and testing equipment to pinpoint.
