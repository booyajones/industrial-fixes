## Navien E324 Error Code — What It Means

The E324 error code on Navien tankless water heaters typically indicates an exhaust overheat condition. The unit has detected that exhaust gas temperatures have exceeded safe operating limits. This triggers a safety shutdown to prevent damage to the heat exchanger or venting system.

The error is part of the unit's self-protection system. When the exhaust thermistor or high-limit sensor detects temperatures above normal range, the control board stops operation and displays E324. Common causes include restricted airflow through the venting system, a failing exhaust thermistor, scaling or buildup in the heat exchanger reducing efficiency, or incorrect gas pressure causing incomplete combustion.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the exhaust thermistor when the real problem is scale buildup in the heat exchanger or a blocked vent. Inspect venting and check for error history patterns before ordering sensors.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted exhaust vent (~35%)** Debris, bird nests, ice, or disconnected vent sections prevent hot exhaust gases from escaping, causing temperature buildup.
- **Failed exhaust thermistor (~25%)** The sensor that monitors exhaust temperature has drifted out of calibration or failed, sending false overheat signals to the control board.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger (~20%)** Hard water mineral deposits coat the heat exchanger tubes, reducing heat transfer efficiency and causing localized overheating.
- **Incorrect gas pressure (~15%)** Gas supply pressure too high or gas valve out of adjustment causes overfiring and excessive exhaust temperatures.
- **Blocked intake air supply (~5%)** Restricted combustion air intake starves the burner of oxygen, resulting in incomplete combustion and high exhaust temperatures.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power and gas** to the water heater at the service shutoffs and wait five minutes for the unit to cool.
2. **Inspect the exhaust vent** termination outside for visible blockages, disconnected sections, or damage. Clear any obstructions and verify all joints are secure.
3. **Check the intake air filter** (if your model has one) and clean or replace it if clogged with dust or lint.
4. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit according to your owner's manual (usually by pressing and holding the reset button or cycling the power switch).
5. **Monitor the unit** through a full heating cycle. If the E324 error returns, the problem is internal and requires further diagnosis.
6. **Call a qualified technician** to test exhaust temperature with a combustion analyzer, measure gas pressure, inspect the heat exchanger for scale, and test the exhaust thermistor with a multimeter.
7. **If the technician confirms** a failed thermistor, have it replaced. If scale is found, arrange for a professional flush or heat exchanger service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the error persists after clearing visible vent blockages and resetting the unit. Diagnosing E324 requires testing exhaust temperatures, verifying gas pressure with a manometer, inspecting the heat exchanger for scale, and testing thermistor resistance. Working on gas appliances and combustion systems carries safety risks. A technician will also check for proper venting configuration and combustion air supply, which are critical to safe operation and often require specialized tools.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $180-400.
