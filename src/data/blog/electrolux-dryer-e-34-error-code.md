## Electrolux Dryer E34 Error Code — What It Means

The E34 error code on an Electrolux dryer indicates a system fault detected by the control board. The exact meaning of E34 varies by model and series, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the dryer cabinet for the specific definition for your unit. In many cases, error codes in this range point to sensor or wiring issues that prevent the dryer from operating safely.

Because Electrolux uses different control systems across its product lines, E34 may relate to temperature sensing, motor control, or another subsystem depending on the model year and configuration. Always verify the code definition for your specific dryer before ordering parts.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board first when seeing an error code, but most E-series faults stem from a failed sensor or loose wiring harness. Test sensors with a multimeter and inspect all connectors before buying a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed temperature sensor or thermistor (~40%)** A thermistor or temperature sensor with an open circuit or out-of-range reading will trigger a fault code and prevent the dryer from starting.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connector (~25%)** Vibration or moisture can cause harness connectors at the control board or sensors to work loose or corrode, sending false signals.
- **Faulty control board (~20%)** The electronic control board may have a failed relay, bad solder joint, or software glitch that generates the error code incorrectly.
- **Damaged wire harness (~10%)** A pinched, chafed, or broken wire between the control board and sensors can cause intermittent or permanent fault codes.
- **Defective motor control component (~5%)** If E34 relates to motor feedback on your model, a worn motor relay or failed tachometer sensor may be the root cause.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** from the wall outlet and wait two minutes to reset the control board, then restore power and test.
2. **Locate the wiring diagram** on the inside of the dryer cabinet or download the service manual using your model number from the Electrolux website.
3. **Identify the component** associated with E34 on the diagram (it may be labeled as a thermistor, sensor, or motor relay depending on your model).
4. **Remove the dryer's top or rear panel** using a screwdriver or nut driver to access the control board and sensor connectors.
5. **Inspect all wire harness connectors** for loose pins, corrosion, or damage; clean contacts with electronic contact cleaner and press connectors fully into place.
6. **Test the suspected sensor** with a multimeter set to resistance mode; compare the reading to the expected range in the service manual or wiring diagram.
7. **Replace the faulty sensor or control board** if testing confirms a failure, routing new wiring carefully and securing all connectors before reassembling the panels.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you cannot locate the wiring diagram for your model, if multimeter testing is unfamiliar, or if the error persists after replacing the identified sensor. Technicians have access to service manuals and diagnostic software that can pinpoint the exact fault quickly. Also call a pro if the dryer uses a high-voltage (240V) control board and you are not comfortable working with live electrical connections, or if the repair requires disassembly of gas connections on a gas dryer.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-90 in parts, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $120-250.
