## Whirlpool F4E3 Error Code — What It Means

The F4E3 error code on a Whirlpool dishwasher indicates a wash or circulation pump communication fault. The control board is not receiving the expected feedback from the circulation pump motor, meaning the pump cannot start, maintain speed, or communicate properly. This can stem from a pump motor failure, a wiring problem, or a restriction that prevents normal water movement through the wash system.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed circulation pump or motor assembly** The wash motor cannot start, maintain speed, or communicate with the control board due to internal motor or pump failure.
- **Loose or damaged wiring and connectors** Corroded terminals, broken conductors, or loose harness connections between the control board and circulation pump interrupt the signal.
- **Restricted water flow or blocked sump** Clogged filters, blocked spray arms, or debris in the sump area prevent the pump from moving water normally and trigger a performance fault.
- **Insufficient water fill** The dishwasher does not fill to the proper level, starving the circulation pump and causing it to fail feedback tests.
- **Control board fault** The main control board fails to send or interpret the pump motor signal correctly, though this is less common than pump or wiring issues.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power for one minute** by unplugging the unit or switching off the circuit breaker, then restore power and run a cycle to confirm the error repeats.
2. **Inspect the filter and sump area** by removing the lower dish rack and filter assembly, clearing any food debris, broken glass, or objects that could block the pump impeller.
3. **Check spray arm rotation** by spinning each spray arm by hand to confirm it moves freely and is not clogged with mineral deposits or debris.
4. **Verify proper water fill** by starting a cycle and observing that water enters the tub to the expected level before the wash phase begins.
5. **Examine pump wiring and connectors** at both the circulation pump and the control board for loose plugs, corrosion, heat damage, or broken wires.
6. **Test the circulation pump motor** by listening for startup noise during the wash phase and checking for continuous operation or stalling sounds that indicate motor failure.
7. **Replace the circulation pump assembly** if diagnostics confirm the motor does not run, runs intermittently, or the dishwasher fills but does not spray water during the wash cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the power reset does not clear the code and you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or removing the pump assembly. A technician can measure signals at the control board and pump, isolate wiring faults, and confirm whether the circulation motor or control board is responsible. If your dishwasher is under warranty or you lack a multimeter and service manual for your specific model, professional diagnosis will save time and avoid unnecessary part replacement.
