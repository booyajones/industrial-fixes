## What this code means
The F4E3 error code on Whirlpool dishwashers signals that the control board is not detecting proper wash motor operation. The circulation pump, which sprays water through the spray arms during the wash cycle, is either not running, not being sensed correctly, or not being driven by the control. Whirlpool's dishwasher product support lists F4E3 as a dishwasher error and recommends a power reset as the first troubleshooting step.

This fault means wash circulation is not occurring. The dishwasher may fill normally but then stop or fail to spray water. The code may appear immediately when the wash cycle should begin, or it may set after the control attempts to start the motor and does not see the expected feedback. The underlying problem lies in the circulation pump itself, the electrical path to it, or the control board's ability to command and monitor it.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first. Instead, remove the lower access panel and check the circulation pump for mechanical binding, debris in the impeller, or a burned-out motor before spending money on electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Seized or failing circulation pump / wash motor (~40%)** Food debris, hard water scale, or bearing wear can lock the impeller or burn out the motor windings so the pump will not spin.
- **Restricted impeller or pump blockage (~25%)** Bits of broken glass, food particles, or plastic can lodge in the sump, filter, or pump inlet and prevent the impeller from turning freely.
- **Poor electrical connection at the motor or control (~15%)** Corroded terminals, a loose wire harness connector, or heat-damaged wiring can interrupt the power or feedback signal between the control board and the wash motor.
- **Control board output failure (~10%)** A failed relay or driver circuit on the main control board can prevent the board from sending voltage to the circulation motor even when commanded.
- **Low water fill or inlet restriction (~7%)** If the dishwasher does not fill to the correct level, the circulation pump may run dry or cavitate and the control will not see normal operation.
- **Temporary control lockup (~3%)** A software glitch or power surge can freeze the control board's microprocessor and set a false fault that clears with a hard reset.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset power** by unplugging the dishwasher or switching off the circuit breaker for a full minute, then restore power and run a test cycle to see if the fault was transient.
2. **Remove the lower access panel** (usually the kick plate at the front bottom) and locate the circulation pump and sump assembly beneath the tub.
3. **Inspect the sump, filter, and spray-arm feed** for food debris, broken glass, or foreign objects that could block the impeller or restrict water flow.
4. **Check the circulation motor for mechanical binding** by trying to spin the impeller by hand (power off and disconnected). If it does not turn freely or makes grinding noises, the motor bearings or impeller are seized.
5. **Inspect all wiring and connectors** between the main control board and the circulation motor for loose pins, corrosion, or heat damage. Reseat connectors firmly.
6. **Test motor winding resistance** with a multimeter if you have access to the motor terminals. Compare the reading to your model's service data. An open (infinite resistance) or very low reading indicates a failed motor.
7. **Replace the circulation pump / wash motor assembly** if the motor is seized, electrically open, or does not run when the control commands it and wiring is intact. If the motor is good and wiring is verified, replace the main control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with 120-volt AC wiring, if you cannot access the circulation pump through the lower panel, or if you have tested the motor and wiring and still cannot isolate the fault. A technician has the model-specific service manual, voltage and resistance specifications, and the tools to safely measure live circuits while the dishwasher runs. Also call if the pump is integrated into a complex sump assembly that requires special disassembly or if your model requires a software reset or control reprogramming after board replacement.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $100–$200 in parts (circulation pump), 1–2 hours. A pro service call runs about $200–$350 service call and pump replacement.
