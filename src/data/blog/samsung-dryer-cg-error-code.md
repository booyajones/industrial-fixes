## Samsung Dryer Cg Error Code — What It Means

The Cg code on a Samsung dryer is a high-temperature or overheating protection fault. Samsung groups this code with hE, hC, hC1, hC2, C8, C80, C9, and C90, all of which indicate the dryer has detected that its internal temperature is too high. This is not a single failed part but a condition triggered when the machine cannot cool itself properly.

The code usually points to a restricted venting system or an abnormal temperature-sensing condition. In most cases the real cause is poor airflow from a clogged lint filter, blocked exhaust duct, kinked flex hose, or obstructed outdoor vent hood. When air cannot escape, cabinet temperature rises and the overheating protection circuit trips.

## Common Causes

- **Restricted exhaust venting** Crushed flex duct, disconnected vent pipe, or heavy lint buildup in the exhaust path prevents hot air from leaving the dryer cabinet.
- **Clogged lint filter or heat exchanger area** Lint accumulation on the filter screen or inside the heat exchanger cover assembly blocks airflow and raises internal temperature.
- **Blocked outdoor vent hood** Bird nests, debris, or a stuck flapper damper at the outside termination stop exhaust air from escaping.
- **Kinked or improperly installed vent hose** A sharp bend or compression behind the dryer restricts the air path and traps heat inside.
- **Faulty temperature-sensing or overheat-protection circuit** If airflow is confirmed good but the code persists, an open, short, or intermittent fault in the temperature-sensing circuit may be triggering the code.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** and wait at least 30 seconds to clear any temporary faults and safely reset the control board.
2. **Remove and clean the lint filter** thoroughly, checking both the screen and the filter housing cavity for any buildup or blockage.
3. **Inspect the entire vent system** from the dryer connection to the outdoor hood, looking for kinks, crushed sections, disconnected joints, or heavy lint accumulation inside the duct.
4. **Check the outdoor vent termination** to confirm the flapper opens freely and no debris, nests, or ice block the opening.
5. **Clean the heat exchanger area** if your Samsung model has a ventless or heat-pump design, following the instructions in your user manual for accessing and cleaning the internal heat exchanger cover assembly.
6. **Restore power and run a test cycle** to see if the code clears after airflow improvements have been made.
7. **Contact Samsung service** if the code returns after confirming all venting and airflow paths are clear, as the fault is likely in the temperature-sensing or overheat-protection circuit and requires electrical diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the Cg code persists after you have cleaned the lint filter, inspected and cleared the entire vent system, and confirmed the outdoor hood is open and unobstructed. At that point the fault is likely in the temperature-sensing circuit, overheat protection device, wiring harness, or control board inputs, which require electrical testing and part-level diagnosis. Also call for service if your dryer uses a sealed heat-pump system and you cannot access the internal heat exchanger yourself, or if you are uncomfortable working with 240-volt dryer power connections.
