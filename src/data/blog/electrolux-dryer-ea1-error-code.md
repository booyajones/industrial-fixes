## Electrolux Dryer EA1 Error Code — What It Means

EA1 is a power-frequency supply fault. Your Electrolux dryer's control board has detected that the incoming line frequency is out of range (it expects 60 Hz in North America). In practical terms, this means the dryer is either not receiving the electrical service it expects, or the control board is misreading that service. This code often appears when the dryer is plugged into an incorrect power source or when there is a problem with the control electronics rather than a mechanical component failure.

## Common Causes

- **Incorrect power source** Dryer is plugged into an extension cord, off-grid inverter, generator, or moved between regions with different electrical standards.
- **Supply frequency out of specification** The incoming AC power at your outlet is genuinely unstable or does not meet the required 60 Hz standard.
- **Voltage surge or unstable power** A recent power event or brownout has triggered a fault condition in the control board.
- **Faulty electronic control board** The power electronic board or main control is misreading normal supply power and generating a false EA1 code.
- **Installation or wiring issue** Incorrect wiring at the outlet or dryer terminal block causes the control to interpret the power as abnormal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the dryer is plugged directly into a dedicated wall outlet** rated for your model, not through any extension cord or adapter.
2. **Measure the incoming power at the receptacle** with a multimeter or outlet tester to confirm voltage and that your home is supplying correct AC frequency (60 Hz in North America).
3. **Check for recent power events** such as outages, surges, or generator use that might have corrupted the control board's fault memory.
4. **Power-cycle the dryer** by unplugging it (or flipping the breaker) for at least two minutes, then restore power and attempt a new cycle to see if EA1 clears.
5. **Inspect the terminal block and house wiring** at the back of the dryer for loose connections, reversed neutrals, or signs of overheating that could cause a false power-supply reading.
6. **Replace the electronic control board or power board** if supply power is confirmed correct and EA1 persists after power-cycling, since the board is likely misreading the incoming frequency.
7. **Reassemble, restore power, and run a test cycle** to verify the dryer operates normally without the EA1 code returning.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed electrician if you are not confident measuring outlet voltage and frequency, or if your outlet tester shows incorrect wiring or voltage outside the nameplate range. Call an appliance technician if the supply power is confirmed correct but EA1 returns after power-cycling, since control board replacement requires disassembly and often soldered connectors. If your dryer is still under warranty, contact Electrolux service before replacing any boards to avoid voiding coverage.
