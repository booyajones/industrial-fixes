## Frigidaire Dishwasher LO Error Code — What It Means

The LO code on a Frigidaire dishwasher indicates that the rinse aid dispenser is low or empty. The dishwasher's electronic diagnostics monitor the rinse aid level and display this reminder when the reservoir needs refilling. This is not a drain fault or wash-motor issue. The code is designed to alert you before the dispenser runs completely dry, which can affect drying performance and leave spots on dishes.

In most cases the dispenser is actually empty or low. Less common causes include a defective rinse aid dispenser assembly that does not properly detect the level, a wiring problem between the dispenser and the main control board, or a faulty control board itself. The first step is always to verify the rinse aid level and refill the reservoir.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the main control board when the LO code persists, but the dispenser assembly or its wiring is usually the real culprit. Fill the rinse aid reservoir, reset the power, and inspect the dispenser cap and harness connections before ordering a board.

## Common Causes

- **Empty or low rinse aid reservoir (~70%)** The dispenser has run out of rinse aid or fallen below the sensor threshold, triggering the LO reminder.
- **Defective rinse aid dispenser assembly (~15%)** A stuck float, damaged cap, or faulty level sensor inside the dispenser causes a false low reading even when filled.
- **Loose or corroded wiring at the dispenser (~10%)** The harness connector between the rinse aid dispenser and the main control board is loose, corroded, or damaged.
- **Faulty main control board (~5%)** The control board misreads the dispenser signal or has a failed rinse-aid monitoring circuit.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Open the dishwasher door and locate the rinse aid dispenser** on the inside of the door, usually next to the detergent cup.
2. **Unscrew or flip open the rinse aid cap** and check the reservoir level through the sight glass or opening.
3. **Fill the reservoir with liquid rinse aid** until it reaches the fill line, then close and tighten the cap securely to prevent leaks.
4. **Reset the dishwasher** by pressing and holding Start/Cancel for several seconds, or by switching off the circuit breaker for two to three minutes and restoring power.
5. **Run a short rinse cycle** and observe whether the LO code clears from the display.
6. **Inspect the dispenser cap and housing** for cracks, stuck mechanisms, residue buildup, or signs of leakage if the code persists after refilling.
7. **Check the wiring harness** at the back of the dispenser assembly for loose connectors, corrosion, or damaged wires, and reseat or clean connections as needed.
8. **Replace the rinse aid dispenser assembly** if the code remains after wiring checks and the dispenser does not retain rinse aid or detect the level correctly.
9. **Test or replace the main control board** only after confirming the dispenser and all wiring are intact and functioning properly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have refilled the rinse aid, reset power, and inspected the dispenser cap but the LO code still appears. A pro can test the wiring harness for continuity, verify the dispenser sensor signal at the control board, and determine whether the dispenser assembly or the board itself has failed. Also call if you are uncomfortable removing the inner door panel to access the dispenser mounting screws and electrical connectors, or if other error codes appear alongside LO that suggest a broader control-board issue.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-40 in parts (dispenser assembly if needed), 20-45 min. A pro service call runs about $120-220 service call.
