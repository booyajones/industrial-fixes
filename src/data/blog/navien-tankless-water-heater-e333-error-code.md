## Navien Tankless Water Heater E333 Error — What It Means

The E333 error code on a Navien tankless water heater signals a fault with the air pressure sensor system or the venting circuit. The unit uses an air pressure sensor to verify proper combustion airflow and safe venting. When the sensor detects pressure outside its normal range or cannot confirm proper airflow, the heater shuts down and displays this code to prevent unsafe operation.

This code can result from blocked vent pipes, a failed air pressure sensor, improper vent installation, or issues with the sensor tubing. The system is designed to stop heating if combustion air supply or exhaust venting is compromised. The exact meaning and response can vary slightly between Navien models, so consult your owner's manual for model-specific guidance.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many owners replace the air pressure sensor when the real problem is a blocked vent pipe, wasp nest in the vent termination, or kinked sensor tube. Inspect all venting and sensor tubing before ordering a sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted vent pipe (~40%)** Debris, bird or wasp nests, ice buildup, or improper vent termination can restrict airflow and trigger the air pressure sensor.
- **Failed air pressure sensor (~25%)** The sensor itself can fail from age, moisture, or electrical issues and send false signals to the control board.
- **Kinked or disconnected sensor tubing (~15%)** The small rubber or plastic tube connecting the sensor to the combustion chamber can become pinched, cracked, or pulled loose.
- **Improper vent installation (~10%)** Vent pipes that are too long, have too many elbows, or lack proper slope can cause pressure readings outside normal range.
- **Control board or wiring fault (~10%)** Corroded connectors or a failing control board can misread sensor signals and produce false error codes.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve.
2. **Inspect the outdoor vent termination** for blockages, wasp nests, bird nests, lint, or ice buildup and clear any obstructions you find.
3. **Remove the front cover** of the unit (typically held by screws at the bottom) to access the internal components.
4. **Locate the air pressure sensor** (a small round or rectangular device with electrical connectors and a rubber or plastic tube attached) and trace the sensor tube to the combustion chamber.
5. **Check the sensor tubing** for kinks, cracks, disconnections, or blockages. Replace the tube if damaged or reconnect if loose.
6. **Inspect the vent pipes** inside the unit and verify they are properly assembled, free of debris, and sloped correctly per the installation manual.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit and observe whether the error clears. If the E333 persists, the air pressure sensor or control board likely needs replacement by a qualified technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician if you cannot safely access the vent termination, if the error persists after clearing blockages and checking tubing, or if you suspect the air pressure sensor or control board has failed. Tankless water heaters involve gas combustion and precise venting requirements. Incorrect repairs can create carbon monoxide hazards or void warranties. A qualified technician has the tools to test sensor voltage, verify proper vent drafting, and replace components safely while maintaining code compliance.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
