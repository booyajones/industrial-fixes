## Bosch Dishwasher E05 Error Code — What It Means

The E05 code on a Bosch dishwasher signals a wash arm diverter circuit fault in the main control board. Bosch also lists E05 when water cannot enter the dishwasher properly or there is an issue with the filling system. The error can point to either an electronic fault in the diverter circuit on the control board or a mechanical problem in the water inlet path. Because the same code covers multiple systems, diagnosis starts with a power reset and moves through the fill components before addressing the control board or diverter assembly.

## Common Causes

- **Temporary electronic lockup** The control board may freeze and trigger E05 until you fully disconnect power.
- **Faulty wash arm diverter circuit** A failed circuit on the main control board that commands the wash arm diverter will log E05.
- **Kinked or damaged inlet hose** Bosch lists filling-system issues under E05, and a restricted inlet hose blocks water flow.
- **Blocked inlet filter or flow meter** Debris in the inlet filter or around the flow meter can prevent proper fill detection.
- **Defective control board components** Failed triacs or relays on the main control board can disrupt the diverter circuit and cause E05.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset power at the breaker** for at least 30 seconds to clear any temporary electronic fault, then restore power and run a test cycle.
2. **Inspect the inlet hose** for kinks, twists, or damage that would restrict water flow into the dishwasher.
3. **Check the inlet filter** at the water supply connection inside the machine, remove any debris, and confirm water can enter freely.
4. **Verify that water is filling** by starting a cycle and listening for inlet flow, and check that the machine reaches the correct water level.
5. **Examine the wash arm diverter** and its wiring harness for loose connectors or visible damage if the error returns after fill checks.
6. **Test or replace the main control board** if all mechanical and inlet components pass inspection, because the diverter circuit fault is board-level.
7. **Contact Bosch Customer Support** if the error persists after power reset and basic checks, as Bosch directs for unresolved E05 faults.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the error returns after a power reset and you have verified that water is entering the machine normally. Diagnosing a control board diverter circuit requires model-specific wiring diagrams and multimeter testing that most homeowners do not have. If you are uncomfortable removing panels, tracing low-voltage circuits, or handling the main control board, professional service is the safest route. Bosch also recommends contacting their customer support when basic troubleshooting does not resolve E05.
