## Electrolux Washer E01 Error Code — What It Means

The E01 error code on Electrolux washing machines typically relates to a door or lid lock failure, though the exact meaning can vary by model. The control board is signaling that it cannot verify the door is properly secured, or the lock mechanism is not engaging. Some Electrolux models may use this code for different faults, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram on the machine for the precise definition.

When the washer detects a door-lock issue, it will not start the wash cycle as a safety measure to prevent water leaks or injury. The error may appear at startup or mid-cycle if the lock disengages unexpectedly.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board when the real problem is a worn door latch or a loose wire at the lock connector. Test continuity across the lock's terminals and inspect the latch striker before buying electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Failed door lock assembly (~50%)** The electric door lock solenoid or switch inside the lock housing fails and cannot signal the control that the door is secure.
- **Damaged door striker or latch (~25%)** The plastic striker on the door or the catch on the cabinet is cracked, worn, or misaligned so the lock cannot engage fully.
- **Loose or corroded wiring at the lock connector (~15%)** Vibration or moisture causes the harness plug at the door lock to work loose or develop corrosion, breaking the signal circuit.
- **Control board fault (~10%)** The main control board or user-interface board fails to read the lock signal correctly, though this is less common than a mechanical lock failure.

## Quick Diagnosis

Answer these to narrow it down fast.

<details class="dtree"><summary>Does the door click audibly when you close it, and does it feel firmly latched?</summary>

Proceed to test the lock.<br><strong>No:</strong> The striker or latch is damaged or misaligned. Inspect both parts for cracks or buildup and clean or replace as needed.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>With the washer unplugged and the door open, can you hear or feel the lock solenoid click when you plug back in and press Start?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The lock receives power but may not be completing its circuit. Check wiring continuity and the lock's internal switch.<br><strong>No:</strong> The lock is not receiving power, or the solenoid coil is open. Test for voltage at the lock connector and replace the lock if voltage is present.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>After unplugging for one minute and restarting, does the error clear?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> A transient glitch or stuck relay may have caused the fault. Monitor for recurrence before replacing parts.<br><strong>No:</strong> The fault is persistent. Focus on the lock assembly, striker, and wiring as the root cause.</div>
</details>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and pull it away from the wall to access the front panel or top, depending on your model's service access.
2. **Remove the top panel or front panel** by unscrewing the retaining screws (usually along the back edge or inside the door opening) and lifting or tilting it clear.
3. **Locate the door lock assembly** on the door opening, typically on the right side of the cabinet front, and disconnect the wire harness.
4. **Inspect the striker** on the door itself for cracks, wear, or detergent buildup, and clean or replace if damaged.
5. **Test the lock** by using a multimeter set to continuity mode and checking across the lock's switch terminals (consult your model's wiring diagram for pin-out), then replace the lock if it shows no continuity when manually engaged.
6. **Install the new door lock** by snapping or screwing it into the cabinet bracket, reconnecting the harness, and ensuring the striker aligns properly.
7. **Reassemble the panels**, plug the washer back in, close the door, and run a short cycle to verify the error has cleared and the lock engages.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or disassembling the cabinet panels. Also call if you have replaced the door lock and striker but the error persists, as the control board or wiring harness may need diagnosis with specialized tools. If your washer is still under warranty, contact Electrolux service to avoid voiding coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40-90 in parts, 20-45 min. A pro service call runs about $120-250.
