## Samsung Microwave E-X1 Error — What It Means

Samsung defines E-X1 as an **additional open error** on the microwave display. The control system has detected an open-circuit condition in an additional monitored circuit. Samsung does not publish a more specific component-level definition for E-X1 beyond that label, and the code usually requires technician verification rather than user repair.

Samsung's published guidance is to unplug the microwave or switch it off at the breaker, wait about 60 seconds (or let it dry if moisture is involved), then restore power. If the code returns after this reset, request service. The direct cause is an open circuit in the monitored path, which can be a fault in the sensor, communication harness, or control board connection. Moisture, steam, or a temporary electronic glitch can sometimes trigger the code.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the main control board before checking for a loose connector or corroded wire terminal. Inspect all harness plugs and continuity-test the monitored circuit first to isolate the open before replacing any board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded harness connector (~40%)** An open connection at a plug or terminal creates the open-circuit condition Samsung's control detects.
- **Damaged or broken wire in the monitored circuit (~25%)** A break in the harness or a wire that has pulled out of a connector opens the circuit path.
- **Moisture or steam contamination in the control area (~15%)** Samsung specifically mentions moisture can trigger information codes and recommends drying the unit before retrying.
- **Failed sensor or monitored module (~10%)** If a sensor has failed open or a module has lost its circuit path, the control sees an additional open error.
- **Main control or electronics board fault (~10%)** An open trace or failed component on the board side of the monitored circuit can cause E-X1.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the microwave** or turn off the breaker, then wait 60 seconds to reset the control.
2. **Restore power** and check whether E-X1 returns immediately or during a test heating cycle.
3. **Inspect for moisture** around the control panel, vent, and steam sensor areas. If present, dry thoroughly and wait overnight before retrying.
4. **Remove the outer cabinet** (with power disconnected) and examine every harness connector for loose engagement, corrosion, or pushed-back pins.
5. **Continuity-test the monitored circuit** using a multimeter on the harness and sensor terminals to locate the open path.
6. **Repair or replace** the affected harness, connector, sensor, or control board once the open is isolated.
7. **Reassemble and retest** the microwave. If E-X1 persists after basic inspection, request Samsung service as recommended in the support article.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified technician whenever E-X1 returns after a 60-second power reset and basic drying. Samsung groups this code with information codes that require technician verification, and the exact circuit being monitored is not defined in consumer-facing support documents. Diagnosing an open circuit safely requires disassembly, continuity testing, and knowledge of the control architecture. Microwaves also contain a high-voltage capacitor that can store a lethal charge even when unplugged, so internal work carries serious shock risk. A trained service provider can trace the open, replace the faulty harness or sensor, and verify the repair without risking injury or further damage to the control board.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150–300.
