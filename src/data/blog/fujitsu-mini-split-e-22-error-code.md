## Fujitsu E:22 Error Code — What It Means

The E:22 code (or E2 on some Fujitsu platforms) typically indicates a return-air thermistor open circuit. This means the indoor unit is not receiving a valid temperature signal from the room-air sensor. The fault points to a problem in the sensor itself, the wiring harness, a loose connector, or the indoor control board. It is not a refrigerant or compressor issue. Because Fujitsu uses different code definitions across product families, always verify the exact meaning for your specific indoor and outdoor model numbers in the service manual before replacing parts.

## Common Causes

- **Open or failed return-air thermistor** The temperature sensor has failed internally or is reading out of range, breaking the circuit to the indoor board.
- **Loose or corroded sensor connector** The plug between the thermistor and the indoor control board has backed out, corroded, or lost contact.
- **Damaged sensor harness** The wire between the sensor and the board is cut, pinched, or has an intermittent break.
- **Blocked air filter or intake** Restricted airflow can cause abnormal sensor behavior and trigger fault codes on some platforms.
- **Indoor control board fault** The main PCB is not reading a valid signal even when the sensor and wiring are intact.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm your exact model numbers** (indoor and outdoor) and look up E:22 in the Fujitsu service manual for that platform to verify the code definition.
2. **Power off the unit** at the breaker or disconnect for 30 seconds, then restore power to see if the fault clears as a transient error.
3. **Remove and clean the indoor air filter** and inspect the intake grille for blockages that could affect airflow and sensor readings.
4. **Access the indoor unit** following normal service procedures and locate the return-air thermistor, usually clipped near the evaporator coil inlet or mounted on the return-air path.
5. **Inspect the sensor connector** for looseness, corrosion, or damage, then unplug and firmly reseat it at the indoor board.
6. **Test thermistor resistance** with an ohmmeter and compare the reading to the resistance chart in your model's service documentation (the correct value depends on ambient temperature and platform).
7. **Check harness continuity** from the sensor plug to the indoor board connector for opens or intermittent breaks, and replace the harness if damaged.
8. **Replace the thermistor** if it reads open or out of range, or replace the indoor control board if the sensor and wiring both test correctly but the fault persists.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working inside the indoor unit, if you do not have an ohmmeter and cannot verify sensor resistance against the Fujitsu chart, or if the fault returns after you have reseated connectors and confirmed wiring continuity. Also call a pro if the indoor board needs replacement, since proper refrigerant handling and system checkout are required after PCB work. If your unit is under warranty, contact Fujitsu or an authorized dealer before opening the unit to avoid voiding coverage.
