## Speed Queen Washer EC:09 — What It Means

The EC:09 code on your Speed Queen washer indicates a water-level control or overflow fault. The machine has detected either an unsafe high water level or an inability to lower the water level by draining within the allowed time (typically 5 minutes). This fault can also appear when the pressure-sensing system, which monitors water level through a pressure hose and sensor, is reading incorrectly due to a blocked, kinked, or leaking hose.

In practice, the washer's control has decided that either too much water is present, water is entering when it should not be, or the drain cycle is not removing water as expected. The code protects the machine from flooding and requires you to find and fix the component that is allowing overfill, preventing drain, or sending false level signals.

## Common Causes

- **Inlet valve leaking internally** A water valve that does not fully close allows water to creep into the tub even when the washer is off, causing a slow overfill that triggers the fault.
- **Blocked or kinked pressure hose** The small hose connecting the tub to the pressure sensor can become obstructed, pinched, or disconnected, making the control read the water level incorrectly.
- **Air leak in the pressure system** A split, cracked, or loose-fitting pressure hose allows air into the sensing circuit and prevents accurate water-level detection.
- **Drain restriction or pump issue** A clogged drain hose, kinked standpipe, or failing drain pump keeps the water level from dropping within the allowed time, raising the overflow fault.
- **Corroded or loose wiring connectors** Damaged pins, corrosion, or loose connections in the water-level sensing circuit can send false signals to the control board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut off the water supply** and unplug the washer if the tub is actively filling or near overflow to prevent flooding.
2. **Power-cycle the control** by unplugging the machine (or opening the breaker) for 60 seconds, then restore power to clear the code and reset the system.
3. **Observe a fill and drain cycle** to determine whether the washer is actually overfilling, failing to drain, or simply reading the water level incorrectly.
4. **Check the inlet valve for leaks** by watching for water entering the tub with the washer powered off and the supply valves open. Replace the valve if water seeps in.
5. **Inspect the pressure hose** from the tub to the pressure sensor for kinks, blockages, splits, or loose fittings. Clean or replace the hose as needed.
6. **Verify the drain system** by checking the drain hose routing, standpipe height, and pump filter or impeller for clogs or restrictions that prevent normal draining.
7. **Examine all harness connectors** in the water-level circuit for corrosion, burnt pins, or loose contact, and repair or replace damaged wiring or connectors.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the fault returns after you have replaced the inlet valve and pressure hose, if you are not comfortable working with water supply plumbing or electrical connectors, or if the control board itself is suspect. A professional can perform pressure-switch tests, measure circuit voltages, and access the full service literature for your specific Speed Queen model to pinpoint faults in the sensing circuit or control logic. If the washer is still under warranty, contact Speed Queen or an authorized service center before opening the cabinet or replacing any parts.
