## Whirlpool Washer F02 Error Code — What It Means

F02 is a long drain error common on Whirlpool Duet front load washers. The machine could not empty the tub within the expected time. On many units a Sud warning shows first, then F02 follows a few minutes later.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain pump filter** Debris in the coin trap blocks flow and is the most common cause of F02.
- **Blocked or kinked drain hose** A pinched or clogged hose stops water from leaving the tub.
- **Excess suds** Too much detergent or non HE detergent creates foam the pump cannot move.
- **Weak or jammed drain pump** A failing pump cannot push water out in time and trips the code.
- **Loose pump connection** A loose harness between the pump and control board breaks the drain circuit.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer before you open any panel or remove the filter.
2. Remove the lower front service panel and set a pan and towel under the filter housing.
3. Turn the filter counterclockwise, let residual water drain, and pull out all debris.
4. Inspect the full length of the drain hose for kinks or clogs and clear them.
5. Switch to HE detergent and limit it to about one tablespoon to stop suds buildup.
6. Restore power and run a Rinse and Spin to confirm the tub empties.
7. If it still fails, test the drain pump and its wiring, then replace the pump if defective.

## When to Call a Pro

If the filter, hose, and detergent are all correct and a new pump does not clear F02, the control board or wiring may be at fault. Bring in a technician if multimeter testing is outside your comfort zone.
