## York Furnace Error Code E3 — What It Means

York Furnace Error Code E3 indicates a pressure switch stuck closed fault — the draft inducer pressure switch is signaling closed (pressure detected) when it should be open at the start of the ignition sequence. York's control board checks the pressure switch at the beginning of every cycle; if it's already closed before the inducer starts, the board interprets this as a stuck switch or a wiring short and locks out for safety.

## Common Causes

- **Pressure switch contacts welded closed** — A switch that has repeatedly arced or been exposed to moisture can weld its contacts in the closed position, making it always report pressure.
- **Wiring short in the pressure switch circuit** — A pinched or melted wire between the switch and the control board can mimic a closed switch signal.
- **Restricted or kinked pressure tubing** — The small rubber hose connecting the pressure switch to the inducer housing can kink or block, trapping residual pressure that keeps the switch closed.
- **Inducer housing condensate backup** — Water pooling in the inducer housing creates false pressure at the switch port, holding the switch closed even when the inducer is off.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off and inspect the pressure switch** — Locate the pressure switch (typically mounted near the inducer motor). Disconnect the two wires and test continuity across the switch terminals. If it reads closed with the inducer off, the switch is failed.
2. **Inspect the pressure tubing** — Check the small rubber tubing from the switch to the inducer housing for kinks, cracks, or blockages. Blow through it gently — if airflow is restricted, clean or replace.
3. **Check for water in the inducer housing** — Look for condensate backup in the bottom of the inducer assembly. Drain if present and clear the condensate drain line.
4. **Inspect the wiring** — Check both switch wires for pinch points, burned insulation, or contact with metal edges. A shorted wire reads exactly like a stuck-closed switch.
5. **Replace the switch and reset** — Install a replacement York pressure switch (match to model and WC rating). Restore power and run a heating cycle to confirm E3 clears.

## When to Call a Pro

If the switch, wiring, and tubing all check out and E3 persists, the control board may be misreading the switch input. Board diagnostics and replacement require a licensed HVAC technician.
