## KitchenAid AB Error Code — What It Means

KitchenAid does not publish 'AB' as a distinct fault code in its current error-code charts. The closest official match is SAB, Sab, 5AB, or 5A6, which indicates Sabbath Mode is activated on your oven. This is a feature state, not a component failure. Sabbath Mode disables certain controls and display updates to comply with religious observance, and it must be turned on intentionally through the control panel. If your display shows 'AB' without the leading 'S', it may be a model-specific display variant, but KitchenAid's documentation does not confirm a separate 'AB' fault code.

Because this is not an equipment malfunction, no parts are defective and no repair is needed. Your oven is simply in a programmed mode. The solution is to exit Sabbath Mode using the procedure in your owner's manual, or perform a brief power reset if the display is unclear. If a different code appears after reset, consult KitchenAid's fault-code chart for the actual diagnostic path.

## Common Causes

- **Sabbath Mode was intentionally enabled** Someone pressed the key sequence to activate Sabbath Mode, and the oven is operating as programmed.
- **Power interruption left the mode active** A brief power loss or surge may have caused the control to resume in the last saved state, which was Sabbath Mode.
- **Accidental button sequence** Holding or pressing certain control combinations can activate Sabbath Mode without the user realizing it.
- **Control panel retained prior setting** The oven was used in Sabbath Mode previously and was never returned to normal operation mode.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Write down the exact code** displayed, including any letters, numbers, or spaces, and note your oven's full model number from the data plate inside the door or on the frame.
2. **Locate your owner's manual** (or download it from KitchenAid's support site using your model number) and turn to the section on Sabbath Mode or special features.
3. **Follow the exit procedure** in the manual, which typically involves pressing and holding a specific button or combination (often 'Clock' or 'Options') for several seconds until the display changes.
4. **If the manual is unavailable or unclear**, turn off power at the circuit breaker, wait one full minute, then switch the breaker back on and observe the display for one minute.
5. **Check that the display returns to normal** (time of day or 'off') and that all oven functions respond to button presses as expected.
6. **If a different error code appears** after reset (such as F1, F2, F3, F4, or F9), write it down and look it up in KitchenAid's fault-code chart, because that indicates a real component fault.
7. **Test the oven** by setting a bake cycle at a moderate temperature to confirm normal heating and display behavior before returning it to service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the power reset and manual procedures do not clear the display, if the oven shows a different fault code after reset, or if the control panel becomes unresponsive to all button presses. Also seek professional help if you are uncomfortable working with your home's circuit breaker or if the oven exhibits other symptoms such as failure to heat, unusual sounds, or burnt odors. A certified tech can retrieve internal diagnostic logs from the electronic control and verify whether the board itself has failed or whether wiring or sensor issues are causing a persistent error state.
