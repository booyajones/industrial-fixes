## Amana Washer PF Error Code — What It Means

PF stands for Power Failure on Amana washers. The control board detected that the wash cycle was interrupted by a loss or disruption of electrical power. This is not a mechanical fault with the motor, drain, or water level. The code simply tells you the washer lost power partway through the cycle and needs instruction on how to proceed. Amana uses the same PF code on its dryers, confirming it is a general power-interruption indicator across the platform.

## Common Causes

- **Utility outage or brownout** A brief loss of line power during the cycle will trigger PF and stop the washer mid-program.
- **Tripped circuit breaker** If the branch breaker opened and then reset, the washer will flag PF when power returns.
- **Loose power cord or unreliable outlet** A plug that is not fully seated or an outlet with worn contacts can cause intermittent power interruption.
- **Power-line noise or unstable supply** Voltage sags or electrical noise on the house wiring can be interpreted by the control as a power failure event.
- **Premature button press after power-up** Pressing a key before the control finishes its startup delay can trigger PF on related Amana platforms.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check the circuit breaker** at your panel to confirm it has not tripped and is fully on.
2. **Inspect the power cord and outlet.** Push the plug firmly into the receptacle and look for any scorching or loose contacts.
3. **Clear the code** by pressing and holding the START button to restart the cycle, or press POWER to clear the display entirely.
4. **Run a test load** and monitor whether PF reappears during normal operation without any known power event.
5. **Reset the breaker for five minutes** if you suspect a recent outage, then restore power and wait five minutes before starting a new cycle, following Amana's dryer-platform guidance for PF behavior.
6. **Verify stable household voltage** using a multimeter at the outlet if PF recurs with no external power loss; consult an electrician if readings fluctuate.
7. **Consider the electronic control board** only after confirming the supply path is clean and the code still returns with no real power interruption.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the PF code returns repeatedly with no actual power outage and after you have verified the breaker, cord, and outlet are all in good condition. A pro will test the control board's power-supply circuits and check for voltage irregularities that a basic multimeter may not catch. Also call if you are uncomfortable working inside your electrical panel or testing live circuits.
