## Electrolux Dryer H2 / B2 Error Code — What It Means

The H2, EH2, or ExH2 code on Electrolux compact and condensing dryers signals a line-voltage problem. The dryer has detected that the incoming electrical supply is not within the expected range for that model. Depending on the platform, this can mean under-voltage, over-voltage, or unstable power delivery at the outlet. The exact acceptable voltage window varies by model and region, so always consult your dryer's technical sheet or rating plate.

In the field, the two most common outcomes are a genuine power-supply issue at the receptacle or a failed electronic control board after the supply checks out clean. Some Electrolux platforms expect 208 to 240 V, while others flag a problem when the outlet exceeds 130 V. Because error-code definitions differ across Electrolux dryer families, verify your exact model number before replacing parts.

## Common Causes

- **Incorrect or unstable supply voltage** The outlet is delivering power outside the dryer's required range, either too high or too low for that model.
- **Extension cord or poor wiring** An extension cord, loose terminal block, or undersized circuit can cause voltage drop under load and trigger the code.
- **Incompatible power source** The dryer is plugged into the wrong electrical system or used in a different-country application without the correct supply.
- **Faulty electronic control board** After verifying incoming power is correct, a failed main control board is the most common component cause.
- **Damaged power cord or harness** Frayed wires, burnt terminals, or corroded connections between the outlet and the board can disrupt voltage delivery.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Locate your exact model number** on the door frame or rating label and pull the technical sheet to confirm the required supply-voltage range for your dryer.
2. **Measure the outlet voltage** with a multimeter while the dryer is attempting to run, and compare the reading to the spec on your model's rating plate or tech sheet.
3. **Remove any extension cord** and plug the dryer directly into a dedicated outlet rated for the appliance, then retest the code.
4. **Unplug the dryer for two minutes** to perform a hard reset, reconnect power, and run a test cycle to see if the code clears.
5. **Inspect the power cord and terminal block** for burn marks, loose screws, or frayed wires, and repair or replace the cord if damage is found.
6. **Check the electronic control board** for visible burn marks, swollen capacitors, or corrosion on the connector pins, and replace the board if the incoming voltage is confirmed good and the code persists.
7. **Test the door switch, thermal fuses, and heater continuity** if your model's service manual calls for process-of-elimination checks before condemning the board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working with live voltage or if measuring the outlet shows an out-of-spec supply and you need a licensed electrician to correct the circuit. Also call for help if you have verified the incoming power is correct, performed a reset, and the code still returns, since that points to a control-board replacement and you want a professional to confirm the diagnosis and match the correct board to your exact model and serial number before ordering the part.
