## Bosch E21 Error Code — What It Means

On most Bosch washer platforms, E21 (sometimes shown as F21) indicates the control board has detected a problem in the motor circuit or lost normal motor rotation feedback. The machine stops because it cannot confirm the drum is spinning as commanded. The exact meaning can vary by model, and some older or regional variants use E21 for drain system faults or excess suds instead. Always confirm your model's specific fault table before diving into repairs, but the most technically grounded interpretation treats E21 as a motor-drive issue involving the motor itself, its wiring, the tachogenerator signal, or the drive circuit on the control module.

## Common Causes

- **Worn or damaged motor carbon brushes** Brushes wear down over time and lose contact with the commutator, breaking the motor circuit and triggering the fault.
- **Failed triac or relay on the control module** The power-switching component or its solder joints can fail, preventing the board from driving the motor correctly.
- **Lost tachogenerator signal** The tacho sensor or its wiring sends rotation feedback to the control board, and any open circuit or bad connection will show as a motor fault.
- **Loose or broken wiring harness between board and motor** Vibration and heat can crack connectors or break wires, opening the motor circuit intermittently or permanently.
- **Blocked drain or excess suds (model-dependent)** On certain Bosch platforms E21 is tied to drainage or foam detection, so a clogged filter or too much detergent can trigger the code.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are uncomfortable working with live mains-voltage components or if you lack a multimeter and the skills to trace motor circuits safely. If you have replaced brushes and checked all wiring but the code returns, the fault may lie in the tachogenerator, the motor itself, or complex control-board logic that requires factory service documentation and specialized test equipment. Professional help is also the right choice when your model's E21 definition is unclear and you want to avoid unnecessary parts replacement.
