## Amana Refrigerator CI Error Code — What It Means

The CI error code is not officially documented in Amana's published troubleshooting guides, but repair technicians interpret it as an ice maker circuit fault. The code typically appears when the control board detects a problem communicating with the ice maker assembly, when the ice maker motor cannot complete a cycle, or when a sensor or shutoff mechanism is not reporting correctly. Because Amana uses different control platforms across model families, the exact meaning can vary by refrigerator model.

Before assuming a hardware failure, confirm your ice maker is turned on, the ice bin is installed correctly, and the water supply is connected and flowing. Many apparent CI faults clear after a simple power reset, similar to Amana's guidance for other display codes.

## Common Causes

- **Ice maker not enabled or bin not seated** The ice maker switch may be off, or the ice storage bin may not be installed correctly so the shutoff arm cannot move freely.
- **Water supply restriction** A clogged or incorrectly installed water filter, kinked supply line, or closed shutoff valve prevents water from reaching the ice maker.
- **Ice maker motor or sensor failure** The ice maker motor may be jammed or burned out, or the internal position sensor cannot send status signals to the main control board.
- **Control board communication glitch** A temporary software fault in the user interface or main board can trigger the code even when hardware is functioning normally.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-reset the refrigerator** by turning off the circuit breaker or unplugging the unit for one full minute, then restore power and observe whether the code clears.
2. **Verify the ice maker is turned on** using the dispenser panel or the mechanical switch on the ice maker itself, and confirm ice production is selected if your model has separate crushed/cubed/ice-off buttons.
3. **Remove and reinstall the ice bin**, making sure it clicks fully into place and that the wire shutoff arm or optical sensor can move without obstruction from loose ice or frost buildup.
4. **Inspect the water filter** by removing it and checking the date code or condition, then install a new filter if it is more than six months old or if water flow is restricted.
5. **Check the water supply line** behind the refrigerator to confirm the saddle valve or quarter-turn shutoff is fully open, the tubing is not kinked, and water pressure is adequate when you disconnect the line briefly.
6. **Listen for ice maker motor noise** during a manual harvest cycle (consult your model's instructions to trigger a test), and if the motor hums but does not turn or makes no sound at all, the ice maker assembly likely needs replacement.
7. **Call a technician** if the code returns immediately after reset and you have confirmed water supply, bin installation, and ice maker power, since control-board output or wiring faults require component-level diagnostics and a model-specific service manual.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the CI code reappears after a power reset and you have verified the ice maker is on, the bin is seated, and water flows freely to the refrigerator. Diagnosing control-board output, testing the ice maker motor winding, and tracing the wiring harness require a multimeter and the factory service manual for your specific model. Because Amana does not publish a universal definition for CI, a qualified technician will confirm the exact fault using the diagnostic mode built into your refrigerator's control system and will have access to the correct resistance and voltage specifications for your platform.
