## Bosch Oven E6200 Error Code — What It Means

E6200 is a fan-related fault on Bosch and BSH built-in ovens. The error appears when the internal cooling or circulation fan motor is faulty, stalled, or not being detected correctly by the control electronics. The oven may stop heating, sound an alarm, or refuse to continue operation when the fan is not rotating properly. Because Bosch's public error-code documentation does not list E6200, this identification comes from user reports and repair documentation across the BSH family of appliances.

## Common Causes

- **Fan motor failure** The fan motor itself has failed or worn bearings cause it to stall or run intermittently.
- **Mechanical obstruction or binding** The fan wheel or blade is blocked, or a loose or missing fixing has allowed the assembly to shift and bind.
- **Loose or damaged wiring** Wiring between the fan motor and control board is disconnected, heat-damaged, or making intermittent contact.
- **Control board fault** The main PCB fails to supply power to the fan or cannot interpret the fan feedback signal correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Isolate power** at the wall or circuit breaker and wait for stored charge to dissipate before opening any panel.
2. **Confirm the symptom** by observing whether the fan is silent, noisy, or stops after warm-up, and note whether the fault returns after a reset.
3. **Inspect the fan mechanically** by removing the rear panel or internal cover, checking for obstructions, loose hardware, or damage, and verifying the blade spins freely by hand.
4. **Check all wiring and connectors** from the fan motor back to the control board for looseness, corrosion, heat damage, or open circuits.
5. **Test the fan motor** by disconnecting it and measuring continuity or substituting a known-good motor to determine whether the motor or the control circuit is at fault.
6. **Replace the control PCB** if the motor and wiring test good but the fault persists, indicating the board is not driving or reading the fan correctly.
7. **Reassemble and test** by restoring power, running the oven through a full cycle, and verifying the fan starts promptly and the error does not reappear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified technician if you are not comfortable working with mains voltage or disassembling the oven cabinet. Replacing the fan motor or control board requires safe access to live circuits and accurate part identification by exact model number. If you have substituted the motor and checked all wiring but the fault returns, diagnosis of the control board or internal signaling may require service documentation and test equipment that are not widely available to homeowners.
