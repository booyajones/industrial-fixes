## Electrolux Washer E04 Error — What It Means

The E04 error code on many Electrolux washers signals a drain system fault. The machine detects that water is not leaving the tub within the expected time, or the pressure sensor is not seeing the expected drop in water level. This can happen when the drain path is blocked, the pump is failing, or the pressure switch system has a problem.

The exact meaning can vary slightly by model, so check your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the cabinet for confirmation. In most cases, the washer will stop mid-cycle and refuse to advance until the drain issue is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the drain pump when the real culprit is a clogged filter or a sock blocking the impeller. Pull the filter and spin the pump by hand to check for obstructions before buying a new pump.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain pump filter (~40%)** Lint, coins, hair, and small objects collect in the filter and slow or stop water flow, triggering the error.
- **Failed drain pump (~25%)** The pump motor burns out or the impeller breaks, so it cannot move water even when the path is clear.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose (~20%)** A hose bent behind the machine or a partial clog in the standpipe prevents proper drainage.
- **Pressure switch or air-trap hose fault (~10%)** A stuck pressure switch or a clogged air-trap hose keeps the control board from detecting that the tub is empty.
- **Wiring or connector problem (~5%)** Loose or corroded connectors at the pump or pressure switch interrupt the signal to the control board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves.
2. **Locate the drain pump filter** access door, usually at the front lower corner of the cabinet, and open it.
3. **Place a shallow pan** under the filter opening and slowly unscrew the filter cap to drain residual water.
4. **Pull out the filter** and rinse it under running water, removing lint, coins, and any debris.
5. **Reach into the pump housing** and spin the impeller by hand to check for obstructions or rough bearings.
6. **Inspect the drain hose** from the pump to the standpipe for kinks, clogs, or improper height.
7. **Reconnect everything**, plug in the washer, and run a drain or spin cycle to verify the error is gone; if the code persists, test the drain pump for continuity and replace if faulty.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have cleaned the filter and hose, verified the pump spins freely, and the E04 code still appears. A pro can test the pressure switch, trace wiring faults, and access the control board diagnostics. Also call if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections or tilting the machine to access internal components. If the washer is still under warranty, professional service may be required to preserve coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $120-220.
