## Navien E782 Error Code — What It Means

The E782 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates an abnormal operation fault with main panel communication. This means the display panel and the main control board (PCB) are not exchanging data properly. The fault is internal to the unit's control system and is not caused by water flow, gas supply, or combustion problems. The error will prevent normal operation until the communication link is restored.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed main control board (PCB)** The primary PCB has malfunctioned and can no longer communicate with the panel, requiring replacement.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connectors** Harness connections between the display panel and PCB are not seated or have corrosion blocking signal transfer.
- **Damaged communication wiring** The wire harness linking the main board and panel has been pinched, cut, or abraded.
- **Unstable or interrupted power supply** Voltage fluctuations or a weak power source prevent stable board-to-panel communication.
- **Contamination or moisture on PCB** Water intrusion or dust buildup on the board creates shorts or signal interference.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the unit** at the breaker and wait 60 seconds to allow the board to reset.
2. **Verify the error code** on the display panel and confirm it reads exactly E782.
3. **Open the front cover** and visually inspect the main PCB for burn marks, corrosion, moisture, or loose connectors.
4. **Reseat all harness connectors** between the display panel and the main board, checking for bent pins or corrosion on the contacts.
5. **Check the power supply** at the unit and confirm stable voltage at the inlet terminals (consult your model's specification sheet).
6. **Replace the main PCB** if connectors and power are verified good and the error persists after reseating and a power cycle.
7. **Restore power and test** the unit through a full hot water cycle to confirm the E782 code clears and normal operation resumes.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified Navien technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical components or if the error persists after reseating connectors and cycling power. PCB replacement requires proper handling of control boards, matching your exact unit model, and verifying calibration settings after installation. A technician can also perform voltage and continuity tests on the communication circuit to isolate intermittent faults that are not obvious during visual inspection.
