## Navien NPE-240A2 Water Heater Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Navien NPE-240A2 is a condensing tankless gas water heater in the NPE-A series. It uses a stainless steel heat exchanger, internal recirculation pump, and electronic ignition. Most problems on this model come from dirty air filters, gas supply issues, flow sensor faults, and venting restrictions. Error codes on the display point you toward the system at fault, but the exact meaning and repair steps need to come from the Navien installer and service manual for the NPE-240A2, not generic third-party lists. The unit will lock out and refuse to fire if it detects ignition failure, flame loss, overheating, or abnormal flow.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **Ignition failure or no flame (common error codes)** Gas supply valve closed, inlet gas pressure low, dirty intake air filter blocking combustion air, or failed igniter prevents the burner from lighting.
- **Flow sensor error or no hot water** Clogged inlet water strainer, failed flow sensor, or intermittent water flow causes the unit to not recognize demand and refuse to fire.
- **Overheating or high-limit fault** Restricted heat exchanger from scale buildup, failed high-limit switch, or pump circulation fault causes the unit to shut down on thermal protection.
- **Flame loss during operation** Burner contamination, flame rod sensing failure, grounding issues, or venting blockage causes the unit to lose flame and lock out mid-cycle.
- **Vent blockage or exhaust fault** Obstructed or improperly installed vent piping, failed fan motor, or air pressure switch fault prevents safe combustion and triggers a lockout.
- **Thermistor or temperature sensor error** Failed inlet or outlet thermistor, corroded connector, or wiring fault sends incorrect temperature data to the PCB and prevents normal operation.
- **Internal pump fault (NPE-A models)** The internal recirculation pump fails, loses prime, or has a wiring issue, preventing hot-water delivery or triggering a pump error code.
- **PCB or control board failure** Power surges, moisture intrusion, or component wear on the main circuit board cause intermittent faults, erratic behavior, or total loss of function.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician for any work involving gas supply, gas valve testing, or burner inspection. Gas pressure verification, igniter replacement, flow-sensor diagnostics, heat-exchanger flushing, and PCB replacement all require specific tools and knowledge of the NPE-240A2 service procedures. If the unit locks out repeatedly, shows an error code you cannot clear, or you smell gas, shut off the manual gas valve and call for service immediately. Venting work must meet local code and Navien installation requirements, so improper DIY changes can create unsafe combustion conditions. For gas line, burner, or igniter work, or if you ever smell gas, stop and call a licensed technician.
