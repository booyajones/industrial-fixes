## Bosch ER2 Oven Error — What It Means

The ER2 error code on a Bosch oven indicates an electrical network connection failure. When this code appears, the oven will not heat and you will hear a continuous alarm buzzer. The fault points to a problem with the power supply reaching the appliance, either from the incoming line, the breaker panel, the appliance connection itself, or the control electronics that monitor the supply. Bosch does not list ER2 on all its official oven error-code pages, but third-party appliance lookup tools and field experience consistently identify it as a power or network connection fault.

## Common Causes

- **Tripped breaker or blown fuse** A transient power interruption or overload can trip the circuit breaker or blow the fuse, cutting stable power to the oven and triggering the ER2 code.
- **Incorrect or unstable incoming voltage** Line voltage outside the appliance's expected range or fluctuating supply from the utility or panel will cause the control to flag a network fault.
- **Loose or miswired appliance connection** Loose conductors at the terminal block, corroded terminals, or incorrect wiring during installation create an unstable connection that the control detects as a network failure.
- **Damaged supply wiring or harness** Heat damage, pinched wires, or corrosion in the supply cable or internal harness between the terminal block and control can interrupt or degrade the electrical path.
- **Control board or electronics fault** If the control board or power-monitoring circuit is damaged, it may falsely report a network failure even when the supply is correct, especially if the error persists after a full reset.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Switch off the breaker or fuse** supplying the oven and leave it off for at least 30 seconds to fully reset the control electronics, then re-energize the circuit.
2. **Observe whether the code returns.** If the ER2 clears and does not come back, the fault was likely transient. If it reappears immediately or within a few cycles, continue troubleshooting.
3. **Verify incoming line voltage** at the breaker panel or wall outlet with a multimeter to confirm it is within the appliance's rated range and stable. If voltage is incorrect or fluctuating, contact an electrician to inspect the panel and incoming service.
4. **Inspect the appliance terminal block and supply wiring.** Turn off the breaker, remove the rear access panel, and check for loose, burned, or corroded connections at the terminal block and any inline connectors. Re-seat or replace damaged terminals and conductors as needed.
5. **Check internal harness connections** between the terminal block and the control board for pinched, heat-damaged, or loose wires. Repair or replace any damaged harness sections.
6. **Test the oven again** after securing all connections and restoring power. If the ER2 code persists and the supply and wiring are confirmed good, the control board or power-monitoring circuit is the likely cause.
7. **Replace the control board** if all power-supply and connection checks pass and the error remains. Use the exact replacement board for your Bosch E-Nr model number and transfer any non-volatile settings or configuration as directed in the service manual.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician or electrician if you are not comfortable working inside a live electrical panel or high-voltage appliance. If the breaker reset does not clear the code and you see no obvious loose or damaged connections, professional diagnostics with a multimeter and wiring diagram are the safest path. Persistent ER2 errors after verifying correct incoming voltage and secure connections almost always require control-board replacement, which involves high-voltage work and proper part programming. An electrician should also be called if incoming line voltage is incorrect or unstable, as that points to a panel or utility issue beyond the appliance itself.
