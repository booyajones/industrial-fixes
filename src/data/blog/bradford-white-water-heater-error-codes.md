# Bradford White Water Heater Error Codes: Complete Guide

Bradford White manufactures residential and commercial water heaters. Their Infiniti series (tankless), eF series (electric), and Defender series (gas) models use LED flash codes and digital display codes to communicate faults. This guide covers all Bradford White error codes.

## Bradford White Electric Water Heater Error Codes

Electric models use a simple reset system:
- **High-limit trip (red button pops out):** Overtemperature — reset by pressing the red button. If it trips again: failed thermostat or shorted heating element
- **No hot water, element checks good:** Check thermostats for continuity at correct temperature

## Most Common Bradford White Faults

### E03 — Overheat / High Limit (Infiniti Tankless)
1. Descale the heat exchanger — scale is the most common cause in hard water areas
2. Check inlet filter screen — clean if clogged
3. Lower temperature setpoint if set above 140°F
4. Check water flow rate — minimum 0.5 GPM

### E01 — Ignition Failure (Infiniti Tankless)
1. Check gas supply pressure at the unit (3.5 in. w.c. natural gas)
2. Inspect igniter for carbon deposits — clean with fine sandpaper
3. Check flame sensor (thermopile) voltage — should be above 350 mV when lit
4. Inspect burner for blocked ports

### 2 Flashes — Low Thermopile Voltage (Defender Series)
1. Clean the thermocouple with fine steel wool
2. Check draft — poor draft reduces flame and thermopile output
3. If voltage stays below 350 mV after cleaning: replace thermopile
