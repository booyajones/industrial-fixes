## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E36 Error — What It Means

The E36 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters indicates a fault detected by the unit's control board. Because Ruud uses different code definitions across models and generations, the exact meaning of E36 can vary. Your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit will list what E36 means for your specific model.

Common issues flagged by error codes in this range include sensor failures, flame problems, or communication errors between components. Before attempting repairs, consult your model's documentation to confirm what the code represents on your particular unit.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace expensive control boards when the real problem is a failed sensor or loose wiring. Check all sensor connections and test sensor resistance before replacing any control components.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty temperature sensor (~35%)** A thermistor or temperature probe can drift out of range or fail, sending incorrect readings to the control board and triggering an error.
- **Loose or corroded wiring (~25%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen connectors or corrode terminals, breaking communication between sensors and the control board.
- **Failed flame rod or ionization sensor (~20%)** If the unit cannot confirm flame presence, it may shut down and display an error code to prevent unsafe operation.
- **Control board failure (~15%)** The main circuit board can develop failed relays or memory corruption, causing false errors or inability to read sensors correctly.
- **Incorrect gas pressure or air supply (~5%)** Combustion problems from blocked vents or wrong gas pressure can create conditions the control interprets as a fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off** electrical power and gas supply to the water heater at the breaker and shut-off valve.
2. **Locate your model number** on the rating plate and find the owner's manual online or the wiring diagram inside the unit's cover.
3. **Look up E36** in the troubleshooting table to identify which sensor or component your model associates with this code.
4. **Remove the front cover** and inspect all wire connectors at the control board, sensors, and any noted components for looseness or corrosion.
5. **Test sensor resistance** with a multimeter if the code points to a thermistor or flame rod, comparing readings to the values in your manual.
6. **Replace the identified component** (sensor, flame rod, or board) if testing confirms it is out of specification or the manual directs replacement.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then run hot water to verify the heater fires without error and the code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you cannot locate the exact meaning of E36 for your model, if you are uncomfortable working with gas connections, or if the unit continues to fault after resetting and checking wiring. Tankless water heaters involve combustion, gas pressure, and electrical controls that require proper training to diagnose safely. A technician can use diagnostic software to read additional fault history, measure gas pressure, and test high-voltage ignition components that are not safe for DIY troubleshooting.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $180-400.
