## Frigidaire Dryer AL-26 Error Code — What It Means

Frigidaire dryers do not use a standard AL-26 code in manufacturer documentation. You likely have an E61, E63, or E64 error, which all relate to heating circuit problems. E61 indicates a heater relay fault on the control board. E63 signals a ground fault in the heating circuit. E64 means an open circuit in the heating path. All three codes prevent the dryer from heating properly and require inspection of the heater assembly, wiring, and control board. Confirm your exact code and model number to identify the right repair path.

## Common Causes

- **Failed heater relay on the control board** The relay that switches power to the heating element can stick or burn out, triggering E61 or false fault reports.
- **Shorted or grounded heating element** The element coil can crack and touch the metal housing, creating a ground fault that trips E63.
- **Open heating element or thermal cutoff** A broken heater coil or blown thermal safety device creates an open circuit that shows as E64.
- **Wiring harness damage or chassis contact** Loose, frayed, or pinched wires in the heater circuit can cause intermittent opens or grounds.
- **Defective main control board** The control board itself can misread the heating circuit and falsely report a fault even when components test normal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** and turn off the gas supply if applicable, then remove the rear access panel or lower front panel to reach the heating element assembly.
2. **Inspect the heating element and wiring** for visible damage, burn marks, or any wire touching the metal frame or housing.
3. **Test the heating element for continuity** using a multimeter with the power off; you should see resistance in the range of 20 to 30 ohms between the heater terminals and infinite resistance from each terminal to ground.
4. **Check each thermal cutoff and safety thermostat** in series with the heater for continuity; replace any open device.
5. **Examine the wiring harness** from the heater to the control board for breaks, corrosion, or pinch points, and repair or replace damaged sections.
6. **Test the control board heater relay** by checking for proper switching when the dryer calls for heat; if the relay does not close or the board continues to report the fault with a known-good heater circuit, replace the control board.
7. **Reassemble the dryer**, restore power, and run a heat cycle to verify the code clears and the unit heats normally.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are not comfortable working with electrical circuits or using a multimeter to test for continuity and grounds, call a qualified appliance technician. Heating circuit diagnostics require safe access to live and powered-off components, and misdiagnosis can lead to repeated part replacement without fixing the root cause. A technician can also confirm whether your display actually shows AL-26 or a different code, since AL-26 does not appear in standard Frigidaire fault tables. If your dryer is still under warranty or you lack the tools to test relays and control boards, professional service is the faster and safer choice.
