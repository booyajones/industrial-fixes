## Kenmore Dishwasher Stuck Mid Cycle — What's Happening

A Kenmore dishwasher stuck mid-cycle is a symptom, not a specific error code. It points to one of several faults depending on where the cycle stops. If the dishwasher stops or restarts when you touch the door, the door latch or door switch is the likely problem because the control sees an open-door condition and halts everything. If it stops with standing water in the tub, you're looking at a drain blockage or pump issue. If the machine goes completely dead mid-cycle, the thermal fuse or a power interruption is often the cause.

When the dishwasher stops during fill or never fills correctly, the water inlet valve or low supply pressure is usually responsible. If it stops after the wash motor tries to start, the circulation pump or motor may be stalled or weak. Control board or timer failures can also corrupt the cycle state, but mechanical causes are more common.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Door latch, door switch, or door lock assembly failure** The most common cause when the dishwasher stops or restarts if the door is touched or moved, because the control sees an open-door signal and halts the cycle.
- **Drain system restriction or drain pump failure** Clogged sump filter, drain hose kink, blocked air gap, or a failed drain pump will leave standing water and stop the cycle from advancing.
- **Water inlet valve blockage or failure** A restricted inlet screen, failed valve solenoid, or low household water pressure prevents proper fill and stalls the cycle during or after the fill stage.
- **Thermal fuse open or power interruption** If the dishwasher loses all power mid-cycle and becomes unresponsive, the thermal fuse has likely blown or there is a circuit or wiring fault.
- **Circulation pump or wash motor stall** A seized, weak, or failing wash motor will start then stop, causing the cycle to hang after fill or during the wash phase.
- **Control board or timer malfunction** Failed relays, burnt traces, or corrupted cycle memory in the timer or control board can freeze the cycle at any point, though mechanical faults are more common.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Run a rinse or quick cycle and watch where it stops: during fill, wash, drain, or dry.
2. Check the door latch and strike for proper engagement, alignment, and wear, and see if moving the door causes the cycle to stop or restart.
3. Confirm the dishwasher has incoming power at the plug, breaker, and junction box, and test the thermal fuse if the unit is completely dead.
4. Listen for water fill, inspect the inlet valve screen for debris, and verify household water supply and pressure are normal.
5. Look for standing water in the sump, clean the filter and drain area, check the drain hose and air gap for blockages, and verify the drain pump runs and moves water.
6. If fill and drain are working, test the circulation pump and wash motor by listening for startup and checking for stall or weak operation.
7. Inspect the control board or timer for visible damage, burnt relays, or loose connectors, and attempt a hard reset by cutting power for two minutes.
8. Replace the failed component, restore all connections, and run a complete cycle to confirm the dishwasher progresses through fill, wash, drain, and dry without stopping.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you've checked the door latch, cleaned the filter and drain, and confirmed incoming water and power but the dishwasher still stops mid-cycle. Control board diagnosis, circulation motor testing, and thermal fuse replacement often require a multimeter and service documentation. If the machine is completely dead or you see burnt wiring or board damage, a qualified technician should handle the electrical troubleshooting and part replacement safely.
