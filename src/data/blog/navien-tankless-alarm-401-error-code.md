## Navien Alarm 401 Error Code — What It Means

Navien does not publish an official "Alarm 401" code in manufacturer documentation. If your display shows this fault, it most likely points to a flow-sensing or water-proving problem. Navien's own troubleshooting guidance states that flow-related issues usually mean the flow sensor inside the unit is not spinning properly or the system is not detecting water movement correctly.

Because the code is not defined in Navien's published lists, confirm the exact model number and the precise text on your display before ordering parts. The steps below address the most common flow-sensor and water-flow faults seen across Navien tankless units.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Flow sensor not spinning freely** Mineral buildup, debris, or a failed bearing can stop the turbine, preventing the unit from detecting water flow.
- **Restricted inlet filter or low water pressure** A clogged filter or insufficient supply pressure reduces flow below the sensor's threshold.
- **Air trapped in the water line** Air pockets prevent the sensor from spinning or registering steady flow, especially after recent service or installation.
- **Faulty sensor wiring or connection** Loose, corroded, or damaged connectors can interrupt the signal between the sensor and the control board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm the exact code and model.** Write down the full display text and your Navien model number to verify the fault before any repair.
2. **Turn off power and water** to the unit at the breaker and the service valves.
3. **Remove and clean the inlet filter screen.** Unscrew the inlet service valve, pull the mesh filter, rinse under water, and reinstall.
4. **Open and close the pressure relief valve** slowly two or three times to flush any debris or air through the flow sensor.
5. **Restore water and power, then test.** Open a hot tap and watch the display to see if flow is detected and the error clears.
6. **Inspect the flow sensor assembly.** If the fault persists, locate the sensor on the inlet pipe, disconnect the wiring harness, and check for physical obstruction or a broken turbine.
7. **Replace the flow sensor or repair wiring** as needed. If the sensor spins freely and connections are clean but the error remains, contact a technician to test the control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the error returns after cleaning the filter and cycling the relief valve, if you see error codes cycling on the display, or if you are not comfortable disconnecting gas or electrical connections. A pro can test the flow sensor's output voltage, inspect the control board for fault logging, and verify proper venting and gas pressure. Because "Alarm 401" is not a published Navien code, professional diagnostics will confirm whether the fault is sensor-related or points to a deeper control or wiring issue.
