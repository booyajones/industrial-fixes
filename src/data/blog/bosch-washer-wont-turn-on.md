## Bosch Washer Won't Turn On — What's Happening

A Bosch washer that won't turn on is typically not showing a fault code at all. The control panel stays dark or completely unresponsive. This symptom points to a power delivery problem, a control board that can't boot, or a failed user interface. It sits upstream of any wash cycle, so the machine never gets far enough to log an error.

If the display lights up but the washer won't start a cycle, the issue shifts to door lock, child lock, water supply, or drain blockage. Bosch support materials emphasize checking mains power, door closure, water taps, and hoses before assuming an internal failure. When no specific fault code appears, the path is power and reset troubleshooting, not code interpretation.

## Most Likely Causes

- **No incoming power** Dead outlet, tripped breaker or RCD, unplugged cord, or damaged plug are the most common reasons the control stays dark.
- **Damaged power cord or plug** A frayed cord or cracked plug can interrupt mains supply even when the outlet and breaker are good.
- **Failed control board or user interface** The main control module or display board may fail and prevent the washer from powering on at all.
- **Door not latched or door lock fault** If the door switch or lock assembly fails, some models will not power the display or will refuse to start (often shown as E16/F16 when the board does boot).
- **Child lock enabled** Child lock can prevent operation and may not show a clear indicator if the display is dim or partially lit.
- **Control software glitch or transient fault** A temporary lock-up can leave the board unresponsive until power is cycled for at least 30 seconds.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Test the outlet with a known-good appliance or plug-in tester to confirm mains power is present.
2. Check the home breaker panel or RCD for a tripped circuit and reset any tripped breaker.
3. Inspect the washer power cord and plug for visible damage, burn marks, or loose prongs.
4. Confirm the washer is fully plugged in at both the wall and the machine inlet (if applicable on your model).
5. Close the door firmly and listen or feel for the latch engaging, then check for any child-lock icon or indicator and cancel it per your model instructions.
6. Unplug the washer for at least 30 seconds to two minutes to reset the control, then reconnect and attempt to power on.
7. If the display lights but the cycle won't start, verify the water tap is fully open and delivers about 2.5 gallons per minute (10 liters per minute), and check that the drain hose and pump filter are clear.
8. If all external checks pass and the washer still will not power on, measure voltage at the control board connector and test the door lock and wiring harness for continuity before replacing any boards.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the outlet and breaker test good, the cord is intact, and a 30-second unplug reset does not restore power. Diagnosing a failed control board, user interface, or door lock requires multimeter work and familiarity with Bosch wiring harnesses. Bosch support directs users to contact service when troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, especially when no fault code appears to guide the next step.
