## GE Water Heater F81 Error Code — What It Means

The F81 error code on a GE GeoSpring hybrid heat pump water heater indicates that the leak sensor battery is running low. GE's published fault-code table for the GeoSpring platform explicitly defines F81 as a low battery condition in the leak sensor module. This sensor monitors for water leaks around the base of the unit and relies on its own battery power. When the battery voltage drops below the threshold, the control board triggers F81 to alert you before the sensor stops functioning. The water heater will typically continue to operate, but the leak-detection feature may be compromised until you replace the battery.

## Common Causes

- **Depleted leak sensor battery** The battery inside the leak sensor module has reached the end of its service life and no longer holds enough charge to power the sensor.
- **Poorly seated battery contact** The battery is present but not making solid electrical contact due to corrosion, dirt, or improper installation in the leak sensor housing.
- **Intermittent connection to leak sensor** A loose or corroded wire connection between the leak sensor module and the main control board can mimic a low-battery condition.
- **Environmental moisture intrusion** Humidity or condensation inside the leak sensor enclosure can cause voltage readings to drop and trigger the low-battery fault prematurely.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the model and fault code** by confirming you have a GE GeoSpring heat pump water heater and that the display reads F81, not a similar code like F78 or F96.
2. **Locate the leak sensor** near the base of the water heater, typically a small plastic module with a wire lead running to the control board and a battery compartment on the side or bottom.
3. **Open the battery compartment** by sliding or unclipping the cover, then remove the old battery and note its type and orientation.
4. **Install a fresh battery** of the same type, making sure the positive and negative terminals align correctly and that the contacts are clean and free of corrosion.
5. **Close the battery compartment** securely and wait 10 to 15 seconds for the leak sensor to power up and re-establish communication with the control board.
6. **Clear the fault code** by cycling power to the water heater at the breaker or by entering the diagnostic menu (consult your owner's manual for the button sequence) and selecting the fault-reset option.
7. **Monitor the display** for 24 hours to confirm F81 does not reappear, and check that the leak sensor LED or indicator shows normal operation if your model has one.

## When to Call a Pro

If F81 returns after you replace the battery and verify all connections are clean and secure, or if you cannot locate the leak sensor on your model, contact GE Appliances Water Heater Service or an authorized technician. Because GE no longer manufactures the GeoSpring line and directs support inquiries to Bradford White for these units, a professional familiar with hybrid heat pump platforms can trace wiring, test control-board voltage outputs, and access service documentation that is not publicly available. Also call a pro if you see related fault codes such as F96 (disconnected or low-battery CTA module) appearing alongside F81, since that points to a broader electrical or communication issue in the sensor network.
