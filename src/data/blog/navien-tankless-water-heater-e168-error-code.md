## Navien Tankless Water Heater E168 Error Code — What It Means

The E168 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically indicates a communication fault or control board malfunction. This code appears when internal components fail to communicate properly or when the main control board detects an internal fault. The exact meaning can vary slightly between models, so consult your owner's manual for model-specific details.

This error often results from electrical issues such as loose connections, voltage fluctuations, or control board failure. Because the code is less common than sensor or flow errors, it usually points to deeper control system problems rather than routine maintenance issues.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace flow sensors or other external components when the actual problem is a failed main control board or loose internal wire harness. Always reset the unit and check for visible wiring issues before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Main control board failure (~50%)** The primary circuit board can fail due to voltage surges, age, or moisture intrusion, disrupting communication between components.
- **Loose or corroded internal wiring (~25%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen wire harness connectors or cause corrosion at connection points inside the unit.
- **Power supply voltage issues (~15%)** Incoming voltage outside the rated range or brownout conditions can trigger control system faults.
- **Failed internal communication module (~10%)** A separate communication board or module may fail, preventing proper signal exchange within the control system.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and shut off the gas supply valve for safety.
2. **Wait 60 seconds**, then restore power to perform a full reset and observe whether the E168 code reappears.
3. **Remove the front cover panel** (usually held by screws at the bottom and sides) to access the internal wiring and control board.
4. **Inspect all wire harness connectors** on the main control board and any secondary boards for looseness, corrosion, or burn marks, and reseat any suspect connections.
5. **Check for visible damage** on the control board itself, such as burned components, swollen capacitors, or moisture stains.
6. **If the code persists**, contact a qualified Navien service technician to test the control board with diagnostic equipment and replace it if necessary.
7. **Reassemble the front panel** securely and restore gas supply once repairs are complete, then test hot water flow to verify normal operation.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the E168 error persists after a power reset or if you find any burned wiring or damaged components. Control board diagnosis requires specialized testing equipment and knowledge of the unit's internal communication protocols. Technicians can also check incoming voltage, test individual board components, and correctly match replacement parts to your specific Navien model. Gas appliance work and electrical troubleshooting at this level carry safety risks and are best handled by trained service personnel. If your unit is under warranty, professional service may be required to preserve coverage.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $300-600.
