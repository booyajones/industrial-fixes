## Kenmore Dryer F01 Error Code — What It Means

The F01 error code on a Kenmore dryer indicates that the electronic machine control board has failed or is not reading correctly. This fault can be triggered by a board defect, a logic glitch, or a power disturbance. The code points directly to the control board itself, not to sensors or other components.

While some online sources claim F01 means a thermistor failure, the better-supported evidence for Kenmore and Whirlpool-style dryers ties F01 specifically to the main control board. The failure may result from a damaged relay, cracked solder joint, burned trace on the board, or a temporary corruption from a power surge. In some cases a loose wiring connection or neutral issue can also trigger the code, but the board is the primary suspect.

## Before You Replace Anything

Some people replace the thermal fuse or thermistor first, but F01 on Kenmore dryers points to the control board itself. Power-cycle the dryer and inspect the board and connectors before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Failed control board or relay issue (~60%)** The electronic machine control board has a defective relay, cracked solder joint, or burned component that causes the F01 fault.
- **Power surge or electrical glitch (~20%)** A voltage spike or brownout corrupted the board's logic or damaged onboard components, triggering the code.
- **Loose or poor wiring connections (~15%)** A loose harness connector at the control board or a poor main power or neutral connection can cause the board to fault.
- **Thermal fuse or safety component in the fault path (~5%)** Although F01 points to the control board, some techs check the thermal fuse during diagnosis as part of the overall circuit.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** by unplugging the dryer or switching off the breaker.
2. **Wait at least 5 minutes** to allow the board to fully discharge and reset any temporary fault.
3. **Plug the dryer back in** and run a short test cycle to see if F01 returns.
4. **Remove the control panel** by taking out the screws at the top rear or sides (consult your model's service manual for exact location).
5. **Inspect the main control board** for burned components, cracked solder joints, failed relays, or heat damage.
6. **Reseat all wiring harness connectors** on the control board, pressing firmly until they click into place.
7. **Replace the control board** if damage is visible or the code persists, using the correct part number for your Kenmore model.
8. **Reassemble the panel**, restore power, and run a full test cycle to confirm the repair.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with 240-volt dryer wiring, if the board replacement does not clear the code, or if you see evidence of arcing or burned wiring that suggests a deeper electrical fault. A technician can verify the main power and neutral supply, test the integrity of the circuit, and confirm that no other components are drawing excessive current or causing the board to fail. If the dryer is still under warranty or a service plan, contact Kenmore or your retailer before opening the unit.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $100–200 for a control board, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–300.
