## Samsung ME21DG6500SR Microwave Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Samsung ME21DG6500SR is an over-the-range microwave with electronic touch controls and a temperature sensor circuit. The most common field complaint on this model and its series is the SE or -SE- key short error, which Samsung defines as a touch key pressed for more than 10 seconds or contamination on the button area. Other reported codes include C-F0/C-F2 for communication faults between the control board and touch panel, C-20 through C-22 for temperature sensor open or short conditions, and C-A1 for damper sensing faults when the unit cannot detect damper movement within one minute. Real-world reliability problems center on the membrane touch panel getting stuck or shorted by moisture, the control board losing communication with the touch interface, and temperature sensor or damper motor failures in units with heavy use or poor ventilation.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **SE or -SE- key error on display** Moisture, grease film, or a stuck button on the membrane touch panel is causing a false key-short condition, or the touch panel itself has failed and needs replacement.
- **C-F0, C-F1, C-F2, or E-83 communication error** The ribbon cable between the control board and touch panel has a bad connection, or one of the two boards has failed and requires replacement.
- **C-20, C-21, C-22, E-21, or E-22 sensor fault** The temperature sensor (thermistor) in the cavity is open, shorted, or has a bad connection at the control board.
- **C-d0, C-d1, or C-d2 door or key error** Samsung lists this as a door or key fault, caused by a stuck touch key, a failed door switch, or a door latch problem.
- **C-A1 or E-A1 damper error** The microwave cannot sense the convection damper opening or closing within one minute, pointing to a failed damper motor or position sensor.
- **C-21 or E-24 over-temperature or fire sense** The temperature sensor detected excessive heat in the cavity, often from blocked ventilation, continuous cooking, or actual overheating in the exhaust path.
- **Microwave turns on by itself or random key presses** A shorted or moisture-damaged touch panel is sending false signals to the control board, and the panel or control board needs replacement.
- **Dead unit with no display or response** Check the household breaker and the internal thermal fuse on the control board or magnetron, as Samsung microwaves will go completely dead if the thermal fuse opens.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro for any repair that requires removing the outer cabinet and working around the high-voltage magnetron, capacitor, and transformer. The capacitor holds a lethal charge even when the unit is unplugged, and technicians discharge it with an insulated tool before touching any internal part. If you see arcing, smell burning plastic inside the cavity, or the unit trips the household breaker, stop using it and call for service. Samsung directs that most error codes require a service call after a 60-second power cycle fails to clear the fault. If you are comfortable cleaning the touch panel with a damp cloth and that fixes an SE error, you can avoid a service call, but any board-level repair or sensor replacement on this model is a professional job.
