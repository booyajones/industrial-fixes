## Daikin A6 Error Code — What It Means

The A6 error code on a Daikin mini split indicates a fan motor fault in the indoor unit. This means the system has detected that the indoor blower motor is not running properly, has stopped responding, or is not sending correct feedback signals to the control board.

The fault typically originates from one of three places: a mechanical obstruction that prevents the fan wheel from spinning freely, a failed motor with burned windings or bad internal sensors, or faulty wiring and connectors between the motor and the indoor circuit board. Less commonly, the indoor PCB itself may be failing to send the correct drive voltage to the motor.

## Common Causes

- **Jammed or obstructed blower wheel** Dust buildup, debris, or foreign objects wedged in the cross-flow fan prevent the motor from spinning.
- **Failed fan motor windings or Hall sensor** The motor's internal coils or feedback sensor have burned out or developed an open circuit.
- **Loose, burned, or corroded wiring** Connectors between the fan motor and indoor PCB are oxidized, loose, or have burned terminals that interrupt power or signal.
- **Faulty indoor control board** The PCB is not sending the correct drive voltage or PWM signal to power the fan motor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the system** by turning off the breaker for 60 seconds, then restore power and check if the A6 code returns after startup.
2. **Inspect the indoor blower wheel** by removing the front cover and air filters, then look for dust clumps, debris, or any obstruction that prevents the fan from turning freely by hand.
3. **Check the fan motor connector** at the indoor PCB and at the motor itself for loose plugs, burned pins, or corroded terminals, and reseat any suspect connections.
4. **Measure motor supply voltage** at the fan motor terminals while the unit is powered on and calling for airflow, and verify the PCB is sending drive voltage (consult your model's service manual for expected DC voltage range).
5. **Test motor winding continuity** with a multimeter by disconnecting the motor plug and measuring resistance across the motor terminals (refer to your model's service documentation for resistance specs).
6. **Replace the fan motor** if continuity is open, resistance is far outside normal range, or the motor does not spin even when correct voltage is present.
7. **Replace the indoor PCB** if the motor tests good but the board is not outputting drive voltage or the code persists after motor replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live voltage, cannot safely access the blower assembly, or if the fault persists after cleaning the fan and checking visible wiring. Diagnosing drive voltage and motor feedback signals requires a multimeter and familiarity with DC motor circuits. If both the motor and PCB appear functional but the A6 code continues, the issue may involve communication errors or sensor feedback that require factory service software to diagnose.
