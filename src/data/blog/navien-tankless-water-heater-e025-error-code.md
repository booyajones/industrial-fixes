## Navien E025 Error Code — What It Means

The E025 error code on Navien tankless water heaters typically indicates a flow sensor or water circulation issue. The heater detects either insufficient water flow through the unit or a problem reading flow data, which prevents it from heating properly or causes it to shut down as a safety measure. Because the exact meaning can vary slightly by model, consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit cover for the precise definition on your appliance. The error often appears when the system cannot confirm adequate water movement through the heat exchanger, triggering a fault to protect internal components from overheating or dry-fire conditions.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flow sensor when the real problem is a clogged inlet filter or closed isolation valve. Clean the filter and verify full valve position before ordering a new sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged inlet filter (~40%)** Sediment and scale build up on the cold-water inlet screen, reducing flow below the heater's minimum activation threshold.
- **Faulty flow sensor (~30%)** The flow sensor fails to send accurate readings to the control board, even when water is moving correctly through the unit.
- **Closed or partially closed isolation valve (~15%)** A service valve left partly closed or a ball valve not fully turned restricts water flow into the heater.
- **Low system pressure or demand (~10%)** Incoming water pressure is too low or the open fixture does not draw enough gallons per minute to activate the heater.
- **Air in the lines (~5%)** Air pockets after a repair or water-service interruption confuse the flow sensor until the system is purged.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the heater at the circuit breaker and close both the cold-water inlet and hot-water outlet isolation valves.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners and setting the cover aside.
3. **Locate the inlet filter** on the cold-water service valve (usually the right-hand valve when facing the unit) and unscrew the filter housing or cap.
4. **Pull out the filter screen** and rinse it under running water, scrubbing away sediment with a soft brush or soaking it in white vinegar for 15 minutes if scale is present.
5. **Reinstall the clean filter** and tighten the housing hand-tight, then open the isolation valves fully and check for leaks.
6. **Restore power** and open a hot-water tap to test operation; if the E025 persists, access the service menu (consult your manual) to check flow-sensor readings in real time.
7. **Test the flow sensor** by disconnecting its wiring harness and measuring resistance with a multimeter; compare the value to your model's specification table and replace the sensor if out of range.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you are uncomfortable working with plumbing connections, if the error returns immediately after cleaning the filter and verifying valve positions, or if you lack a multimeter to test the flow sensor. A professional can also flush the heat exchanger for scale buildup, update firmware if a software bug is suspected, and verify that incoming water pressure meets the heater's minimum requirement. If the control board itself is logging multiple error codes or the unit has a history of intermittent faults, a technician can perform a full system diagnostic and check for grounding or wiring issues that are not obvious to a homeowner.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-350.
