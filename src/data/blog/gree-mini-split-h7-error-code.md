## Gree Mini Split H7 Error Code — What It Means

The H7 error on a Gree mini split indicates a compressor-related fault. The outdoor control board is not detecting the compressor or the compressor is not operating correctly. This is a drive-side issue, not an indoor sensor problem. In most cases the compressor itself is still good and the real culprit is damaged wiring, loose connections, or a failed outdoor control board.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Loose or burned compressor wiring** The wiring harness or connectors between the outdoor PCB and compressor terminals have worked loose, overheated, or corroded.
- **Damaged compressor connector** The plug or terminals at the compressor have burned, cracked, or pulled apart from vibration.
- **Failed outdoor control board** The outdoor PCB or inverter drive stage has failed and can no longer sense or energize the compressor.
- **Compressor electrical failure** The compressor windings are open, shorted, or grounded, preventing normal operation.
- **Phase or drive-stage fault** On inverter models, incorrect phase wiring or a damaged IPM module in the drive board can trigger a compressor fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the disconnect and wait at least five minutes for capacitors to discharge before opening the outdoor unit.
2. **Check supply voltage** at the outdoor unit to confirm proper incoming power is reaching the board.
3. **Inspect the compressor terminals and connectors** for signs of overheating, corrosion, loose pins, or burn marks on the plug or wiring.
4. **Trace the compressor wiring harness** back to the outdoor PCB and check every connection point for broken strands, loose crimp terminals, or burned insulation.
5. **Test the compressor electrically** if all wiring looks intact. Check for continuity across windings and resistance to ground to rule out an internal compressor fault.
6. **Examine the outdoor control board** for visible damage such as burned traces, bulging capacitors, or scorched components near the compressor drive section.
7. **Replace the outdoor PCB** if the compressor and all wiring test normally, since the board is no longer detecting or driving the compressor correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are not comfortable working with live 240 V power, high-voltage inverter circuits, or refrigerant-containing systems, call a licensed HVAC technician. Compressor diagnostics require a multimeter and knowledge of three-phase inverter drives. Replacing a compressor involves recovering refrigerant, brazing, evacuation, and recharge, all of which require EPA certification and specialized tools. Most H7 faults are wiring or board issues that a tech can resolve in one visit without replacing the compressor.
