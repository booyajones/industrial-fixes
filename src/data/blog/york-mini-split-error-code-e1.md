## York Mini Split Error Code E1 — What It Means

E1 on a York mini split indicates a communication error between the indoor and outdoor units. York ductless systems (part of the Johnson Controls family, sharing engineering with Coleman and Luxaire ductless products) use a single signal wire to carry digital communication between the two control boards. When this signal is lost, the system shuts the compressor down and shows E1 on the indoor display. This fault can appear immediately after installation (wiring error) or develop gradually as connections corrode or wiring degrades.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or missing S-wire connection** — The most common cause. A wire left loose at the outdoor unit terminal block, or accidentally omitted during installation, causes instant E1.
- **Corroded outdoor terminal block** — Outdoor units in coastal, industrial, or high-humidity environments develop corrosion at the terminal block faster than indoor units.
- **Chafed or damaged line set wiring** — The communication wire bundled with the refrigerant lines can be chafed by a sharp edge, a wire tie overtightened, or a staple during the rough-in.
- **Surge-damaged outdoor PCB** — The communication IC on the outdoor board is particularly vulnerable to voltage spikes. A damaged IC leaves the outdoor board unable to respond, so the indoor unit reports E1.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power at the breaker** — Wait 2 full minutes before opening the outdoor or indoor units.
2. **Check the outdoor unit terminal block** — Open the wiring compartment and locate the S (or 3) terminal. Tighten any loose connections and clean corrosion with contact cleaner.
3. **Check the indoor unit terminal block** — Verify the S wire is properly terminated at the indoor side as well.
4. **Test wire continuity** — Disconnect the communication wire at both ends. Measure resistance end-to-end; should be less than 5 Ω for a good wire. Check for shorts to ground.
5. **Restore power and test** — Reconnect, power on, and set a cooling or heating call. E1 should clear within 30–60 seconds with a good wire.
6. **Replace outdoor PCB if wiring is confirmed good** — If E1 persists, the outdoor board's communication circuit has failed and requires replacement with a York OEM board.

## When to Call a Pro

York mini split service and warranty parts are distributed through Johnson Controls dealers. If the unit is under warranty, call a York-authorized HVAC contractor — DIY PCB replacement may void coverage.
