## Frigidaire Oven F11 Error Code — What It Means

The F11 code on a Frigidaire oven or range signals a keypad or touchpad short circuit error. The electronic control has detected that a button appears to be continuously pressed or that the keypad membrane circuit is shorted internally. In practical terms, the oven believes a key is stuck down even when you are not touching the panel.

This fault can be a genuine stuck button, moisture or debris under the membrane, a failed keypad assembly, or occasionally a problem with the wiring connector between the keypad and the main control board. The oven will typically be locked out and unable to start a bake or broil cycle until the short is cleared.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the electronic oven control board first when F11 appears. Before buying a new board, unplug the oven and inspect the keypad for stuck buttons, moisture, and check the ribbon connector for corrosion or looseness.

## Common Causes

- **Failed keypad or touchpad membrane (~50%)** Worn or shorted internal contacts in the membrane switch assembly make the control see a phantom button press.
- **Stuck key from grease or debris (~20%)** Food residue, grease, or a mechanically jammed button keeps one key continuously closed.
- **Moisture intrusion behind the panel (~15%)** Spills, steam, or cleaning liquids seep under the touchpad and create a short across the keypad circuits.
- **Loose or corroded ribbon connector (~10%)** The harness or ribbon cable between the keypad and control board has loose pins, corrosion, or damage that mimics a shorted key.
- **Electronic control board fault (~5%)** The control board itself has a damaged input circuit or internal short, though this is less common than keypad issues.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** by unplugging the range or switching off the circuit breaker, then wait at least five minutes for residual charge to dissipate.
2. **Restore power and observe** whether the F11 code clears on its own and whether you can start a bake cycle normally.
3. **Inspect the keypad surface** for any button that feels stuck, visible grease, moisture, or physical damage to the membrane.
4. **Remove the control panel** by taking out the screws along the top or sides of the console (consult your model's service manual for the exact fastener locations).
5. **Check the ribbon connector** where the keypad plugs into the electronic control board for loose fit, corrosion, bent pins, or damage to the cable.
6. **Clean the connector** with a small amount of isopropyl alcohol on a swab if you see residue or corrosion, then let it dry completely and reseat the connector firmly.
7. **Replace the keypad assembly** if the fault returns immediately after reset and inspection, ensuring the new part matches your exact Frigidaire model number.
8. **Replace the electronic control board** only if a new keypad does not resolve the F11 code and you have confirmed the connector and wiring are intact.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working inside the control panel, if the oven is still under warranty, or if you have replaced both the keypad and the control board and the F11 code persists. A technician can perform model-specific continuity tests on the keypad matrix and control board inputs to isolate the fault with certainty. Also call a pro if you see burn marks, unusual odors, or sparking from the control area, as these indicate a more serious electrical fault that requires immediate expert attention.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $50–150 in parts, 30–90 min. A pro service call runs about $150–350.
