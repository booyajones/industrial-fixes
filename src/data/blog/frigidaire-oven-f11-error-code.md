## Frigidaire Oven F11 Error Code — What It Means

F11 is a shorted keypad fault. The electronic oven control detects constant button input when nothing is actually being pressed, so it stops the cooking function and beeps.

Heat and moisture from cooking are the usual cause. Debris or liquid behind the membrane bridges the contacts and shorts the keys.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Shorted keypad membrane** Moisture or debris behind the touch panel bridges the contacts and reads as a held key.
- **Cleaner sprayed on the console** Liquid sprayed at the display wicks behind the panel and shorts the keypad.
- **Corroded ribbon connection** A dirty or loose ribbon cable between keypad and control mimics a stuck key.
- **Failed electronic oven control** The EOC can fail internally and report a keypad short that is not real.
- **Physical keypad damage** A cracked or worn overlay lets contacts touch and trip the fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the range or flip the breaker off for two to three minutes, then restore power and watch the display.
2. Dry the keypad and console with a soft cloth and confirm no button is stuck down.
3. Remove the rear console panel to reach the back of the keypad and the control board.
4. Inspect the keypad membrane and ribbon cable for moisture, debris, or cracks.
5. Reseat the ribbon cable into the control and check for corrosion on the pins.
6. Replace the keypad overlay if the membrane is damaged or shorting.
7. Replace the electronic oven control as a last step if the keypad checks out.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if F11 stays after a reset, a dry-out, and a keypad swap, or if you would rather not open the console and work around the line voltage behind it.
