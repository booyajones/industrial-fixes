## Bosch A3 Error Code — What It Means

The A3 error code on a Bosch tankless water heater signals a wire harness defect detected by the control board. The fault typically involves a loose connection or a broken wire inside the harness, most commonly near the overmolding where the plastic coating protects the wires. The control board detects an open circuit or abnormal resistance and shuts down the unit to prevent unsafe operation.

This is a wiring fault, not a sensor or component failure. The harness connects the control board to various sensors and safety devices throughout the heater. When a wire breaks internally or a connector works loose, the control board loses the signal it expects and flags the A3 code. Water damage from rain entering the exhaust or vibration over time can also cause this fault.

## Before You Replace Anything

Technicians sometimes replace the control board first when the wire harness is actually at fault. Always measure the harness resistance at the overmolding (should be 115 to 120 ohms) before replacing the board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or broken wire at overmolding (~50%)** The wire breaks or disconnects internally near the overmolding where the plastic coating joins the connector, creating an open circuit the control board detects.
- **Poor connector attachment (~25%)** The wire harness connector is not fully seated on the control board or the component it interfaces with, causing intermittent or lost signal.
- **Physical damage to the harness (~15%)** The harness is pinched, cut, or damaged by vibration, heat, or installation error, breaking the circuit.
- **Corrosion at connection points (~7%)** Moisture enters the connector or harness and corrodes the terminals, raising resistance or breaking continuity.
- **Faulty control board input (~3%)** The control board connector or input circuit is damaged, causing the A3 code even when the harness resistance is within spec.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the breaker and disconnect the unit to work safely.
2. **Remove the front cover** to access the control board and wire harness connections inside the unit.
3. **Inspect and reseat** the wire harness connector on the control board, pressing it firmly into place and checking for visible damage or corrosion.
4. **Set a multimeter to ohms** and measure the resistance of the wire harness near the overmolding (where the wires meet the plastic coating).
5. **Compare the reading** to the specification of 115 to 120 ohms. An infinite reading means an open circuit, and a near-zero reading means a short.
6. **Replace the wire harness** if the resistance is out of range, or replace the control board if the resistance is correct but the error persists after reseating connectors.
7. **Restore power** and run the unit through a heating cycle to confirm the A3 error code has cleared and the heater operates normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Bosch-certified technician for the A3 error. Diagnosing this fault requires a multimeter and knowledge of the harness routing and overmolding location, which varies by model. Replacing the wire harness or control board involves working inside the unit's electrical compartment and may require disconnecting gas lines or venting components. If you see water damage inside the unit or if rain has entered the exhaust, do not attempt to repair it. Bosch recommends replacing water-damaged units entirely for safety. Contact Bosch Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904 or consult your model's service manual for harness specs and connector locations before proceeding.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
