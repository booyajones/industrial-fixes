## MRCOOL Mini Split EH 04 Error — What It Means

The EH 04 error code on MRCOOL mini split systems indicates a problem with the electric heating circuit. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or installation guide for the specific definition. Generally, this code appears when the control board detects an issue with the auxiliary electric heater, its wiring, or related safety sensors. The system may continue to provide cooling but heating performance will be limited or disabled until the fault is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board assuming it has failed when the actual problem is a loose wire connection at the heater terminal block or a tripped thermal cutout that can be reset. Always inspect all heater wiring and test the heater element for continuity before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded heater wiring (~30%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen connections at the heater terminal block, causing intermittent faults that trigger the error.
- **Failed thermal cutout or overheat sensor (~25%)** A safety sensor may trip or fail if the heater runs too hot due to restricted airflow or a sensor defect.
- **Defective electric heater element (~20%)** The heating element itself can develop an open circuit or short, preventing proper operation and setting the fault code.
- **Control board communication error (~15%)** The board may lose signal from the heater circuit due to a bad connector or internal board fault.
- **Faulty relay or contactor for heater power (~10%)** The relay that energizes the heater can stick or fail, causing the control to detect no heater current flow.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the breaker and verify with a non-contact voltage tester that the indoor unit is de-energized.
2. **Remove the front cover** of the indoor unit according to your model's service instructions to access the heater compartment.
3. **Inspect all wire connections** at the heater terminal block and the control board for looseness, corrosion, or burn marks.
4. **Test the heater element** for continuity using a multimeter (it should show a low resistance reading, typically a few ohms).
5. **Check the thermal cutout** or overheat sensor by testing continuity across its terminals (it should be closed when cool).
6. **Reconnect or replace** any damaged wiring, tighten loose terminals, and replace failed sensors or the heater element as needed.
7. **Reassemble the unit**, restore power, and run a heating cycle to verify the error code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician for any EH 04 error that does not clear with a simple power reset or filter cleaning. Diagnosing heater faults requires working with line voltage, testing live circuits, and accessing internal components that can be hazardous. A technician has the tools to measure heater current draw, test relay operation, and safely replace elements or boards. If you see any signs of burnt wiring or smell burning plastic near the indoor unit, do not attempt further troubleshooting and contact a professional immediately.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
