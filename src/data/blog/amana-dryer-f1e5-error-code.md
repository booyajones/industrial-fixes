## Amana Dryer F1E5 Error Code — What It Means

The F1E5 code does not appear in Amana's published dryer error-code documentation. The manufacturer's help resources list F3E5 as the moisture sensor fault for Amana dryers. F3E5 means the dryer has completed five consecutive loads without receiving any moisture sensor data, which prevents the automatic cycle from detecting when clothes are dry. If your display shows F1E5, double-check the screen. If it truly reads F1E5 and not F3E5, the code is not verified in Amana's consumer materials and may require service-manual lookup for your specific model.

For the documented F3E5 code, the dryer has not registered contact between wet laundry and the moisture-sensor strips inside the drum. This typically happens when loads are too small, the dryer is not level, or the sensors are dirty or damaged. The control board tracks sensor readings across cycles and throws the code after five loads fail to register moisture.

## Common Causes

- **Misread or mistyped code** The display shows F3E5 but was read as F1E5, since Amana does not document F1E5 for dryers.
- **Load too small or single item** One towel or a few lightweight items do not make consistent contact with the moisture sensors during tumbling.
- **Dryer not level** A tilted drum causes clothes to bunch in one area and miss the sensor strips mounted on the drum wall.
- **Moisture sensors dirty or coated** Fabric softener residue or lint buildup on the sensor bars blocks conductivity between wet fabric and the electrodes.
- **Timed Dry cycle used repeatedly** Timed cycles bypass the moisture sensors entirely, so five timed loads in a row will trigger F3E5 even if the sensors work.
- **Faulty moisture sensor or wiring** Broken sensor strips or a loose wire harness prevent the control board from receiving any moisture data.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the displayed code** by checking the control panel carefully. Confirm whether it reads F1E5 or F3E5, since Amana documents only F3E5 for dryer moisture sensor faults.
2. **Clear the code** by pressing the Power button to turn the dryer off, then wait ten seconds before restarting.
3. **Check dryer level** using a bubble level across the top of the cabinet front to back and side to side. Adjust the leveling feet until all four corners sit flat and the bubble centers.
4. **Run a larger load on Automatic** instead of Timed Dry. Use at least six towels or a full basket of mixed laundry so fabric contacts the moisture sensors throughout the cycle.
5. **Clean the moisture sensors** by locating the two metal strips inside the drum near the lint filter and wiping them with a cloth dampened in white vinegar to remove any residue.
6. **Perform a power cycle** by switching off the dryer circuit breaker for one full minute, restoring power, then running a one-minute Timed Dry cycle to confirm the code does not return.
7. **Inspect sensor wiring** by unplugging the dryer and removing the top or front panel to trace the wires from the drum sensor bars to the control board, checking for loose connectors or broken strands.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the code persists after leveling the dryer, cleaning the sensors, and running a full automatic cycle with a large load. A pro can test continuity at the sensor terminals and check for correct voltage at the control board. If your display truly shows F1E5 rather than F3E5, a service tech with access to your model's wiring diagram can identify whether that code exists in the internal fault table or if the control board itself has failed and is displaying an invalid code.
