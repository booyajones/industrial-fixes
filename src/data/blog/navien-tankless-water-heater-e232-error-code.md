## Navien E232 Error Code — What It Means

The E232 error code on Navien tankless water heaters typically indicates an abnormal exhaust temperature reading. The unit's control board has detected that exhaust gases are either too hot or the sensor monitoring those gases is not functioning correctly. This safety feature prevents operation when combustion or venting conditions fall outside normal parameters.

Because exhaust temperature codes can stem from blocked vents, incomplete combustion, or sensor failure, the exact meaning and corrective action may vary by model. Always consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit's front cover to confirm the code definition and manufacturer-recommended steps for your specific Navien model.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the exhaust thermistor or flame rod sensor before checking for vent blockages or verifying proper combustion air supply. Inspect the vent pipe for obstructions and confirm adequate clearance around the termination before ordering sensors.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted exhaust vent (~40%)** Ice buildup, bird nests, lint, or improper termination spacing can restrict exhaust flow and spike temperature readings.
- **Faulty exhaust thermistor (~25%)** The temperature sensor in the exhaust path may drift out of calibration or fail, sending incorrect readings to the control board.
- **Dirty or fouled flame rod (~15%)** Carbon deposits on the flame-sensing rod can disrupt flame detection and cause irregular combustion that raises exhaust temperature.
- **Insufficient combustion air supply (~10%)** Blocked or undersized intake piping starves the burner of air, leading to incomplete combustion and higher exhaust temperatures.
- **Heat exchanger scale or fouling (~10%)** Mineral buildup inside the heat exchanger restricts water flow and can overheat the combustion chamber and exhaust gases.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve to the heater before beginning any inspection.
2. **Inspect the exhaust vent** outside for blockages, ice, bird nests, or improper clearances. Remove any obstructions and confirm the termination meets manufacturer spacing requirements.
3. **Check the intake air pipe** for kinks, condensate pooling, or debris. make sure the intake termination is clear and meets code for separation from the exhaust.
4. **Remove the front cover** and locate the flame rod inside the combustion chamber. Gently clean the rod with fine-grit sandpaper or a soft cloth to remove carbon deposits.
5. **Inspect the exhaust thermistor** mounted in the exhaust path. Look for loose or corroded connectors and verify the sensor probe is secure and free of soot buildup.
6. **Restore gas and power**, then initiate a hot-water call. Observe the flame through the inspection window and listen for abnormal sounds during ignition.
7. **Monitor the display** for code recurrence. If E232 returns after clearing blockages and cleaning sensors, call a licensed technician to test thermistor resistance and check gas pressure settings.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if free checks and sensor cleaning do not resolve the E232 code. Adjusting gas pressure, verifying proper combustion air ratios, testing thermistor resistance with a multimeter, and performing a heat-exchanger descale all require specialized tools and knowledge of combustion appliance safety. Any work involving gas connections, sealed combustion chambers, or electronic control diagnostics should be left to a qualified service provider familiar with Navien tankless systems.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
