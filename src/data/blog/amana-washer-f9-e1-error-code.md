## Amana Washer F9 E1 Error Code — What It Means

F9 E1 on an Amana washer signals a long drain fault. The washer is taking longer than expected to pump water out, typically over eight minutes on front-load models. The control board has detected that the tub is not emptying fast enough for the cycle to continue normally. This code appears when water is still present after the drain pump has run for the maximum allowed time.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Drain hose installed incorrectly** The hose is pushed too far down into the standpipe beyond the elbow, causing siphoning or water draining back into the tub.
- **Kinked or clogged drain hose** The hose is bent, collapsed, or has a restriction that slows water flow.
- **Blocked household drain** The standpipe, drainpipe, or utility sink is partially or fully clogged.
- **Excessive suds** Too much detergent or non-HE detergent was used, delaying the drain on front-load models.
- **Failing drain pump** If all drain path checks pass, the pump assembly itself may be weak or faulty.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check drain hose installation** by pulling it back from the standpipe so it does not extend past the elbow and confirm the hose form is not altered or taped to the pipe.
2. **Inspect the drain hose** along its entire length for kinks, collapse, or clogs, then straighten and clear any restrictions.
3. **Verify the household drain path** by running water into the standpipe or utility sink to confirm it flows freely and is not blocked.
4. **Look for excessive suds** inside the drum and switch to HE detergent if you have been using regular detergent on a front-load model.
5. **Clear the error code** by pressing Pause or Cancel twice and Power once, or disconnect power for about five minutes, then restart the cycle.
6. **Retest with a drain or spin cycle** to see if the code returns after addressing hose and drain issues.
7. **Schedule service** if the code reappears after all external checks, as internal pump testing and replacement require access to the pump assembly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the F9 E1 code returns after you have verified correct drain hose installation, cleared any kinks or clogs, confirmed the household drain is open, and switched to HE detergent. At that point the pump assembly or internal wiring is the likely cause, and diagnosing it requires disassembly and electrical testing. Also call if you are uncomfortable working with water connections or accessing the pump area on your model.
