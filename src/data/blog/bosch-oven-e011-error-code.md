## Bosch Oven E011 Error Code — What It Means

The Bosch E011 error code signals that the oven has detected one or more control panel buttons being held down, jammed, or shorted. The control electronics scan the keypad constantly and throw this fault when a button fails to return to its normal open state. The problem is almost always in the button or interface area rather than heating or door-lock hardware.

Common triggers include a physically stuck key, food or liquid residue under the buttons, debris behind the keypad membrane, moisture intrusion into the control panel, or a damaged button membrane or user-interface board. Less often the fault lies in the wiring or electronic scan circuit on the control board itself.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main electronic control board when E011 appears. Before ordering an expensive board, inspect and clean every button on the control panel and confirm each springs back normally. Most E011 faults are mechanical sticking or contamination, not electronics failure.

## Common Causes

- **Stuck or jammed button (~50%)** A control-panel button is physically wedged, stuck down, or fails to spring back after being pressed.
- **Food, liquid, or grease contamination (~25%)** Spills, grease, or sticky residue have worked under the keypad membrane and bridge the button contacts.
- **Moisture intrusion (~12%)** Water or steam has entered the control panel and is causing intermittent short circuits across button circuits.
- **Damaged button membrane or interface assembly (~8%)** The flexible keypad membrane or its carrier has cracked, torn, or deformed so one or more buttons no longer open cleanly.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connector (~3%)** The harness connector between the control panel and main board is loose, corroded, or intermittently shorted.
- **Failed user-interface control board (~2%)** The electronic scan circuit on the control or interface board has failed and falsely reports a button press.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** at the circuit breaker and wait five minutes to clear the control electronics.
2. **Press every button** on the control panel firmly and confirm each springs back freely. Note any that feel stuck, mushy, or do not return.
3. **Clean the control panel surface** with a damp cloth and a drop of mild dish detergent. Wipe around and between all buttons to remove grease, food residue, or sticky buildup. Dry thoroughly.
4. **Remove the control-panel overlay or front cover** if your model allows access without voiding the warranty. Check the button membrane, the wiring connectors behind the panel, and the interface board for visible damage, moisture, or loose connections.
5. **Reseat all wiring connectors** between the control panel and the main control board. Look for corrosion or bent pins.
6. **Reconnect power** and observe whether the E011 code appears immediately. If it does, and no button is stuck, replace the keypad membrane or user-interface board assembly.
7. **Test each button** after reassembly to confirm normal operation and verify the error has cleared.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional appliance technician if you have freed all stuck buttons, cleaned the control panel thoroughly, and reseated every connector but the E011 error persists. A technician has the service documentation and tools to isolate whether the fault is in the button-scan circuit on the control board, a damaged membrane that is not visible from the outside, or an intermittent short in the wiring harness. Also call a pro if you are uncomfortable removing the control-panel cover or working around line-voltage wiring inside the oven. Most Bosch ovens require partial cabinet disassembly to access the control-panel back, and mishandling connectors can cause additional faults.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–150 for a keypad membrane or control-panel assembly, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–300 including service call and part.
