## Carrier E19 Error Code — What It Means

E19 is a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units of your Carrier heat pump. The indoor unit control board is specifically identified in Carrier documentation as the fault source. The control system cannot exchange valid signals between the indoor and outdoor sections, so the unit may lock out or run erratically until the fault is cleared. Because the code points to communication, the likely problem is in the signal path or one of the boards, not a simple refrigerant or airflow issue.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Loose or disconnected interconnecting wiring** Field wiring between indoor and outdoor units is loose, disconnected, or has damaged insulation that breaks the communication circuit.
- **Faulty indoor control board** The indoor control board has failed and can no longer send or receive communication signals to the outdoor unit.
- **Poor terminal connections at board or splice points** Corroded, oxidized, or loose terminal connections at the board connectors or field wiring splice points interrupt the communication path.
- **Failed outdoor main board or power board** The outdoor board has stopped responding to communication signals from the indoor unit, depending on which side fails the test.
- **Power-supply issues or electrical noise** Unstable voltage or electrical interference prevents the boards from establishing stable communication.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with low-voltage control wiring or printed circuit boards. Diagnosing communication faults requires a multimeter and familiarity with your unit's service manual and wiring diagram. Misdiagnosis or incorrect board replacement can be expensive. A qualified tech can isolate the fault to wiring or a specific board, perform safe voltage checks, and verify repair through a full heat and cool cycle.
