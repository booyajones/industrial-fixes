## Fujitsu E:35 Error — What It Means

The E:35 error code on a Fujitsu mini split indicates a Manual Auto Switch error. The indoor unit has detected that the local manual auto switch has been continuously ON for 60 seconds or longer. This push switch is a physical button on the indoor unit control board used for manual operation.

In practice, this error almost always means the manual auto switch is stuck, contaminated, or electrically shorted so the unit thinks it is being pressed continuously. It can also mean the indoor unit controller PCB is defective and falsely reading the switch as active. Field technicians often find damage or corrosion on the switch board itself rather than the main control board.

## Common Causes

- **Manual auto switch stuck or contaminated** The physical push switch on the indoor unit control board is mechanically stuck, misaligned, or contaminated with dust or moisture, causing it to remain in the pressed position.
- **Switch board damage or corrosion** The switch PCB assembly has failed components, electrolyte leakage, or corrosion that creates a false closed-switch signal.
- **Switch held down by cover or actuator** The indoor unit cover, a broken actuator, or a misaligned part is physically pressing and holding the manual auto switch button down.
- **Loose or damaged wiring and connectors** Connectors or harness wiring between the switch board and the controller PCB are loose, corroded, or miswired, causing the controller to read a continuous switch signal.
- **Defective indoor unit controller PCB** The main PCB or controller board has a faulty input stage that incorrectly reads the switch as continuously active even when the physical switch is working correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Reset power to the system by turning off the circuit breaker for at least 60 seconds, then restore power and check if the E:35 error returns during normal operation.
2. Remove the indoor unit cover and locate the manual auto switch assembly on the control board, then visually inspect the switch for physical sticking, contamination, debris, or mechanical damage.
3. Check that the switch is not being held down by the cover, housing, or any broken actuator parts, and press the switch manually to confirm it moves freely and returns to the off position.
4. Inspect all connectors and wiring between the switch board and the indoor unit controller PCB for loose connections, corrosion, or visible damage, and reseat or clean connectors as needed.
5. Examine the switch board PCB and main controller PCB for visible signs of damage such as discolored components, electrolyte leakage, or burned traces, paying close attention to the area around the switch input circuitry.
6. If the switch board shows damage or the switch itself is defective, replace the switch board assembly, then verify the error clears and operation is normal.
7. If the switch and wiring are intact but the error persists, replace the indoor unit controller PCB or main PCB, then confirm the remote control address settings if required by your model and recheck operation.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical components or removing the indoor unit cover. This repair requires identifying the correct switch board or controller PCB for your specific Fujitsu model, diagnosing the electrical fault with multimeter testing, and potentially reprogramming or addressing the remote control after board replacement. If the error returns after resetting power or you see visible board damage, a professional can safely diagnose whether the switch assembly or main PCB needs replacement and source the correct part.
