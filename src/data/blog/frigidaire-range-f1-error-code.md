## Frigidaire Range F1 Error Code — What It Means

F1 on a Frigidaire range is the manufacturer-listed code for a shorted keypad. The electronic oven control (EOC) sees a stuck key signal or invalid input from the touchpad. Frigidaire's current error-code documentation separates F1 (shorted keypad) from F10 (thermal runaway), so F1 is not an overheating fault. The problem lies in the keypad assembly, the ribbon cable and connector between the keypad and control board, or the control board itself misreading the keypad.

## Common Causes

- **Shorted or failed touchpad membrane** Internal membrane shorts or wear cause the control to see a permanent key-press signal.
- **Stuck key from contamination or moisture** Spills, grease, or humidity on the console can bridge contacts and trigger F1.
- **Loose or corroded ribbon cable or connector** The harness between the keypad and EOC may have damaged pins, corrosion, or poor seating.
- **Electronic oven control (EOC) failure** The main control board falsely reads a keypad short even when the keypad is good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Power-cycle the range by unplugging it or flipping the breaker off for two minutes, then restore power and check if F1 returns.
2. Press each key on the control panel one by one and watch for a key that feels stuck, unresponsive, or triggers the error immediately.
3. Inspect the touchpad surface and console for spills, grease, or moisture and wipe clean with a dry cloth or slightly damp cloth safe for the surface.
4. Unplug power, remove the control console cover, and inspect the ribbon cable connector between the keypad and EOC for loose fit, bent pins, or corrosion.
5. If the ribbon and connector are intact and the keypad shows no stuck keys, disconnect the keypad ribbon from the EOC and check if F1 clears when you power on (the display may blank or show a different code if the keypad is unplugged, indicating the EOC is working).
6. Replace the keypad assembly if a key is stuck or the touchpad is damaged, and verify the new part clears the code.
7. Replace the electronic oven control board if the keypad and wiring test normal but F1 persists, since the EOC is falsely detecting a short.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable working behind live 240 V connections or removing the control console. If you replaced the keypad and F1 returns immediately, the EOC likely needs replacement and proper diagnosis with a multimeter to confirm the keypad circuit before ordering a board will save time and cost. Technicians also carry model-specific wiring diagrams and can verify connector pin integrity and ribbon continuity that are difficult to test at home.
