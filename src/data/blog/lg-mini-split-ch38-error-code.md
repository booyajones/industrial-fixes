## LG Mini-Split CH38 Error Code — What It Means

The LG CH38 error code indicates an **outdoor inverter compressor fault** — the outdoor unit's inverter drive detected a problem with the compressor's operating current or inverter output. CH38 is specific to LG's dual inverter and multi-zone systems and typically means the inverter detected an abnormal current pattern during compressor operation, which can indicate a compressor winding fault, a failing inverter module, or a refrigerant charge issue causing the compressor to operate outside normal parameters.

## Common Causes

- **Low refrigerant charge** — Undercharge causes the compressor to run at high speed with low suction pressure, generating abnormal current patterns detected by the inverter.
- **Compressor winding fault** — Winding breakdown or unbalanced resistance between phases causes the inverter to see an asymmetric load.
- **Failing inverter PCB** — The outdoor inverter module's current sensing or output transistors fail, triggering false CH38 faults.
- **Dirty outdoor coil** — High head pressure from a fouled condenser loads the compressor and can push current beyond the inverter's normal operating window.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Clean the outdoor coil** — Turn off the system and rinse the outdoor coil with a garden hose from the inside out. Heavy fouling is a common cause of high-load faults. Allow to dry and retest.
2. **Check for low refrigerant symptoms** — In cooling mode, check supply air temperature at the indoor unit (should be 15–20°F below room temp). Frost on the indoor coil or low suction pressure suggests undercharge.
3. **Power-cycle the outdoor unit** — Turn off the breaker for 5 minutes to allow the inverter capacitors to discharge. Power back on; if CH38 clears and doesn't return, the fault may have been transient.
4. **Inspect wiring at the outdoor unit** — Check compressor terminals and inverter PCB connectors for loose connections or corrosion. Tighten and clean as needed.
5. **Measure compressor winding resistance** — With power off and capacitors discharged, check resistance across all three compressor terminals. Significant imbalance (>10% difference) or near-zero/open readings indicate a failed compressor.
6. **Document and call for refrigerant check** — CH38 related to refrigerant issues requires pressure testing and refrigerant handling by an EPA 608-certified technician.

## When to Call a Pro

CH38 with a compressor or refrigerant root cause requires certified refrigerant handling and specialized inverter diagnostics. LG's inverter diagnostics require the LG LGMV service tool for full fault data. Contact an LG-authorized service provider if the basic checks above don't resolve the fault.
