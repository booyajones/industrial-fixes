## Frigidaire Refrigerator Freezing Food — What's Happening

When your Frigidaire refrigerator is freezing food in the fresh-food compartment, you are dealing with either too much cold air entering that section or uneven airflow creating localized cold spots. This is different from a high-temperature alarm. Frigidaire's HI or H display means the cabinet is too warm, often after a door was left open or a power interruption. If food is actually freezing, the problem is overcooling or air distribution, not a high-temp fault.

The most common cause is the air damper that controls how much freezer air flows into the fresh-food section. Frigidaire guidance specifically points to freezer air entering through the damper as a first-line cause of crisper freezing. Blocked vents from overpacked shelves, incorrect temperature settings, low crisper humidity, faulty thermistors, and door gasket leaks can all contribute to the same symptom.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Damper allowing too much freezer air into the fresh-food section** Frigidaire notes that freezer air moving through the damper can freeze crisper contents, and a stuck-open or failed damper is the most common mechanical cause of this symptom.
- **Food blocking supply vents or packed too tightly** Items placed directly in front of air outlets or overpacked against the rear wall create uneven airflow and localized freezing.
- **Temperature setting too cold in the refrigerator compartment** If the fresh-food setpoint is dialed too low, the entire section will overcool and freeze items near the vents or in the crispers.
- **Crisper humidity control set incorrectly or low humidity** Frigidaire says the humidity-control adjustment may need correction if crisper freezing occurs, especially in low-humidity conditions.
- **Faulty thermistor or sensor reading inaccurate temperature** A sensor that reads warmer than actual can tell the control to keep cooling, causing the fresh-food compartment to drop below freezing.
- **Door gasket leakage or poor seal** Air infiltration around a torn or misaligned gasket drives abnormal cycling and temperature swings that can freeze food near vents.
- **Defrost or airflow problem routing cold air incorrectly** Ice buildup or a blocked evaporator fan can change airflow patterns and send excess cold air into the fresh-food section.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Measure actual fresh-food temperatures at multiple locations, especially near the crisper drawers and near the evaporator air outlet, to confirm whether the problem is localized or whole-compartment overcooling.
2. Check the refrigerator temperature setpoint and turn it up one notch if it is set colder than the midpoint, then adjust the crisper humidity control per Frigidaire guidance.
3. Inspect the interior for blocked vents or overpacked shelves and remove any items placed directly in front of air supply openings or the rear wall.
4. Check door gasket contact around the entire perimeter, door alignment, and whether bins or shelves prevent full closure.
5. Locate the air damper assembly (usually in the fresh-food ceiling or rear wall) and verify it is not stuck open or overfeeding cold air to the section.
6. Remove the rear freezer evaporator cover and inspect for frost buildup, evaporator fan operation, and blocked air passages, correcting any defrost failure before condemning the damper or control.
7. Test the fresh-food thermistor by comparing measured resistance to the manufacturer chart for your exact model, if available, to rule out sensor drift.
8. Unplug the unit for two to three minutes to reset the control board only after completing all mechanical and loading checks, then monitor temperatures for 24 hours.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have verified the temperature setting, cleared blocked vents, and reset the control but the fresh-food section still freezes food. Damper replacement, thermistor testing with factory resistance charts, and control-board diagnosis all require model-specific service manuals and parts look-up. If you see heavy frost on the evaporator or the evaporator fan does not run, the repair extends into the sealed refrigeration system or defrost circuit, and professional diagnosis will save time and prevent incorrect part replacement.
