## Bosch Dishwasher Stuck Mid Cycle — What's Happening

When a Bosch dishwasher stops mid-cycle, it is not displaying a specific fault code. Instead, the machine is being interrupted by a door or latch issue, a water-fill problem, a drainage restriction, or a control interlock. Bosch's own troubleshooting guide treats this as a symptom and directs technicians to check door closure, power, water supply, vent blockage, leveling, and the door latch first.

The cycle can stop when the door is not fully latched, when there is a power or water interruption, when the vent is clogged, or when the unit is not level and the door latch or door seal is affected. If the dishwasher drains and then cannot refill, the machine can appear to stop mid-cycle because the inlet valve is not allowing the next fill step.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Door latch or door closure fault** Bosch lists a faulty door latch and improper closure as a key cause of mid-cycle stopping.
- **Water inlet valve or fill restriction** A bad water inlet valve is the most likely cause when the dishwasher drains and then cannot refill.
- **Power interruption or tripped breaker** Bosch and service literature note cycle interruption from a power issue or tripped breaker.
- **Blocked or malfunctioning vent** A clogged vent can build steam pressure and pop the door open enough to interrupt the cycle.
- **Improper installation or not level** Bosch says uneven installation can affect door latch operation and cause stopping.
- **Drainage restriction** Common service guidance includes drainage blockage as a likely cause, especially when the unit cannot progress normally.
- **Control board or electronic glitch** A defective control board is among possible causes, though Bosch emphasizes basic power, closure, and supply checks first.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Confirm the symptom and perform a power cycle by disconnecting power for at least 5 seconds, then reconnect and retry the cycle.
2. Check door closure and verify the latch engages fully. Inspect the latch for wear, damage, or misalignment and confirm the unit is level.
3. Verify the dishwasher is plugged in and the outlet or breaker is supplying power. On hardwired units, check the branch circuit and junction connections.
4. Confirm the water supply valve is fully open and check the inlet screen or strainer for debris. If the machine stops after draining or before wash, test the water inlet valve electrically and mechanically.
5. Inspect the vent for clogs or obstructions and verify dishes are not blocking the door or rack movement.
6. Check filters, drain hose, and drain path for blockage if the unit stalls with water remaining or cannot advance after a drain phase.
7. If all mechanical and supply checks pass, evaluate the control board and associated safety switches or interlocks.

## When to Call a Pro

If the power cycle, door latch inspection, and water supply checks do not resolve the stopping, call a technician. Control board diagnosis requires test equipment and experience with Bosch interlocks. If the unit is still under warranty or if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components or water connections, professional service is recommended.
