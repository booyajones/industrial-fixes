## What this code means
The C-24 code appears when Samsung's temperature sensor detects that your oven cavity is heating faster than the normal rate during operation. Samsung classifies this as a temperature-related information code tied to abnormal heat rise. The oven recognizes that internal temperature is climbing more quickly than the control algorithm expects, which typically points to trapped heat rather than a failed heating element or runaway thermostat.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes assume the temperature sensor itself has failed and order a replacement probe, but Samsung documents that C-24 almost always traces to airflow restriction. Clear the vent and reset power before buying any parts.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or partially blocked vent (~70%)** A pan, foil, or other object covering the vent at the back of the cooktop traps heat inside the cavity and causes the sensor to register an abnormal temperature rise.
- **Restricted venting path (~15%)** Dust, grease buildup, or cabinet clearance issues can choke airflow through the vent channel and prevent hot air from escaping during cooking.
- **Cookware placement over vent openings (~10%)** Large pots or griddles sitting directly over the vent slots on the cooktop force exhaust back into the oven compartment.
- **Control board glitch or temporary sensor spike (~5%)** Occasionally a brief electrical transient or corrupted reading triggers the code even when airflow is normal, and a power cycle clears it.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off the oven** and let it cool completely with the door open for about one minute to release trapped heat.
2. **Inspect the vent openings** at the back of the cooktop and remove any pans, foil, or cookware that might be covering the slots.
3. **Check the vent grille** for dust, grease, or debris and wipe it clean with a degreaser and a soft cloth.
4. **Open and close the oven door** a few times for about three seconds each to push warm air out through the vent, as Samsung recommends in its support guidance.
5. **Power cycle the range** by switching off the dedicated circuit breaker for 30 seconds, then restore power and start a test bake cycle at 350 °F.
6. **Monitor the display** during the first 10 minutes of preheating to see if C-24 reappears; if it does not, the issue is resolved.
7. **Request service** through Samsung Support if the code returns after clearing the vent and resetting power, since further diagnosis may involve checking sensor wiring or the control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the C-24 code persists after you have cleared all vent obstructions, cleaned the grille, and performed a full power reset at the breaker. Samsung's published guidance escalates to service at that point because the fault may involve sensor calibration, wiring continuity, or a control-board issue that requires diagnostic software and replacement parts matched to your specific model. Professional diagnosis typically costs between $100 and $200, and any needed sensor or board replacement will add parts and labor on top of the trip charge.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-15 in cleaning supplies, 10-20 min. A pro service call runs about $100-200 service call if airflow checks do not resolve it.
