## Navien Tankless Water Heater E222 Error — What It Means

The E222 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically indicates a problem with water flow detection or circulation. The unit is not sensing adequate water movement through the system, which can stem from a malfunctioning flow sensor, circulation pump failure, air trapped in the lines, or a blocked filter. The heater shuts down to protect itself when it cannot confirm proper flow.

Because the exact meaning of error codes can vary between Navien model series and firmware versions, always cross-reference your specific model's manual or the diagnostic chart inside the front panel. The E222 code often points to internal circulation issues rather than incoming cold-water supply problems, but the root cause may range from a simple air lock to a failed electronic component.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the flow sensor when the real problem is air in the line or a failing circulation pump. Purging air and checking pump operation cost nothing and should be done first.

## Common Causes

- **Air trapped in circulation loop (~35%)** Air pockets in the internal recirculation line prevent the flow sensor from reading correctly and trigger a flow fault.
- **Flow sensor failure (~25%)** The turbine or hall-effect flow sensor becomes fouled with scale or fails electronically, sending false readings to the control board.
- **Circulation pump not running (~20%)** The internal recirculation pump loses power or seizes, stopping water movement through the heat exchanger loop.
- **Clogged inlet filter or heat exchanger (~15%)** Sediment buildup in the inlet screen or heat exchanger restricts flow enough to fall below the minimum threshold the sensor expects.
- **Wiring or connector issue (~5%)** Loose or corroded connectors at the flow sensor or pump interrupt the signal and cause intermittent faults.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the breaker or service switch and close the isolation valves on the cold inlet and hot outlet.
2. **Remove and clean the inlet filter screen** by unscrewing the cold-water service valve cap and rinsing the mesh screen under running water.
3. **Purge air from the system** by opening the isolation valves slightly and running hot water at multiple fixtures for five minutes to clear any trapped air pockets.
4. **Power the unit back on** and observe the display. If the E222 code reappears immediately, proceed to sensor and pump diagnostics.
5. **Check circulation pump operation** by listening for motor noise near the pump housing when the unit fires. Silence indicates pump failure or a wiring fault.
6. **Test the flow sensor** using a multimeter to verify it produces a pulsing voltage signal when water flows. Consult your model's service manual for the correct pin-out and voltage range.
7. **Replace the faulty component** (flow sensor or circulation pump) with an OEM Navien part, reconnect all wiring harnesses, and test the heater under normal demand.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you cannot clear the error after cleaning the filter and purging air, if you are uncomfortable working with 120V wiring and water connections, or if the circulation pump needs replacement. Gas-fired tankless heaters involve combustion controls and venting that require professional diagnostics. A technician has the tools to measure flow-sensor output, test pump voltage, and inspect the heat exchanger for scale buildup that may not be visible from outside the unit.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-450.
