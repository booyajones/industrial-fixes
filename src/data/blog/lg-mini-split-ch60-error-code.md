## LG Mini Split CH60 Error — What It Means

CH60 on LG mini split systems indicates an outdoor unit PCB EEPROM failure. The outdoor control board cannot successfully read its nonvolatile memory (EEPROM), which stores board and program data. LG service references identify this as a problem with the EEPROM chip itself or the outdoor main PCB assembly.

The fault typically requires either reseating a removable EEPROM chip to restore contact or replacing the entire outdoor PCB if the EEPROM is integrated or if reseating does not clear the error.

## Common Causes

- **Poor EEPROM chip contact** A removable EEPROM chip may have shifted or oxidized contacts, preventing the outdoor PCB from reading stored data.
- **Defective outdoor PCB** The outdoor control board itself has failed, either from age, power surge, or component-level failure of the integrated EEPROM.
- **Moisture or corrosion on the board** Water intrusion or condensation in the outdoor unit can corrode the PCB traces or EEPROM socket, disrupting memory communication.
- **Electrical transient or power surge** A voltage spike can damage the EEPROM or corrupt its stored data, triggering the CH60 fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the system** at the disconnect and the breaker, then wait three minutes for all capacitors to discharge before opening the outdoor unit service panel.
2. **Inspect the outdoor control board** for visible damage, burn marks, corrosion, moisture, or loose connectors, and document any findings.
3. **Locate the EEPROM chip** on the outdoor PCB (it may be a removable IC in a socket or soldered directly to the board).
4. **If the EEPROM is removable, carefully pull it from its socket**, clean the chip pins and socket contacts with contact cleaner or isopropyl alcohol, and firmly reseat the chip ensuring all pins align and insert fully.
5. **Restore power and test** the system by running a cooling cycle for ten minutes, watching for the CH60 code to clear.
6. **If the error persists or the EEPROM is not removable, replace the outdoor unit PCB** with the correct part number for your outdoor model.
7. **After installing the new PCB, perform a system test** and verify normal operation across all modes before returning the unit to service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are uncomfortable working with live electrical components or if the error returns after reseating the EEPROM. Replacing the outdoor PCB requires proper refrigerant handling, accurate wire mapping, and often board configuration or parameter transfer. A technician will also have access to model-specific LG service bulletins and the correct part numbers for your outdoor unit.
