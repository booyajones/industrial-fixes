## Weil-McLain Boiler A48 Error — What It Means

The A48 error on a Weil-McLain boiler is an air-signal or air-proving fault. It appears when the boiler's air pressure transducer does not receive the expected minimum signal within 25 seconds during the startup sequence. The control board cannot confirm that combustion air and venting airflow are present, so it locks out to prevent unsafe operation. This fault is tied to diagnostic code F05 in the control logic and is formally described as 'Air signal protection activation.' The boiler will not fire until the air-proving circuit confirms proper airflow and the fault is cleared.

## Before You Replace Anything

Technicians sometimes replace the air pressure transducer first, but incorrect wiring or a blocked vent can produce the same A48 code. Verify wiring against the diagram and inspect the flue before ordering a new transducer.

## Common Causes

- **Incorrect air pressure transducer wiring (~40%)** Loose, reversed, or incorrectly terminated wires prevent the transducer from sending a valid signal to the control board during the proving window.
- **Blocked or restricted flue or vent (~30%)** Obstructions, dirt, ice, or incorrect vent sizing stop airflow and prevent the pressure differential required to prove combustion air.
- **Failed air pressure transducer (~20%)** The transducer itself can fail internally and no longer respond to pressure changes, even when airflow is present.
- **Dirty or contaminated vent pipe interior (~10%)** Soot, corrosion, or condensate buildup inside the vent restricts airflow and prevents the transducer from seeing the minimum signal in time.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the boiler at the service switch and allow the unit to cool.
2. **Locate the air pressure transducer**, typically mounted on or near the blower or vent collar. Consult your model's installation manual for the exact location.
3. **Verify wiring** by comparing the transducer connector to the wiring diagram in the manual. Check that each wire is fully seated, not corroded, and connected to the correct terminal.
4. **Inspect the flue and vent system** from the boiler outlet to the termination. Look for blockages, improper slope, excessive length, or debris inside the pipe. Clean or correct as needed.
5. **Check vent sizing** against the installation manual's tables. An undersized or incorrectly configured vent will not develop sufficient pressure in the proving window.
6. **Test the air pressure transducer** if wiring and venting are correct. Use a manometer or multimeter (consult the service manual for voltage or resistance specs) to confirm the transducer responds to airflow when the blower runs.
7. **Replace the air pressure transducer** if it shows no response or fails the test. Clear the fault code, restore power, and observe a complete startup cycle to confirm the A48 does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed boiler technician for any A48 fault. This code involves the air-proving circuit, which is a safety interlock tied to combustion and venting. Incorrect diagnosis or wiring can lead to unsafe operation, carbon monoxide risk, or damage to the heat exchanger. A qualified technician has the tools to measure pressure differentials, verify transducer output, and confirm vent sizing against manufacturer tables. If the fault returns after a transducer replacement, the problem may be in the control board, blower motor, or vent design, all of which require professional troubleshooting.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150–350.
