## Maytag Washer D4 Error Code — What It Means

The D4 error code on Maytag washers indicates a lid or door sensing circuit failure on the main control board. On top-load models, D4 specifically means a lid switch circuit fault. On certain front-load models, the same D4 code is used for a door sense error. In both cases, the control board is not receiving the proper signal that the lid or door is securely closed and locked.

This code prevents the wash cycle from starting or continuing because the machine cannot confirm the lid or door is safe to operate. The fault may be in the physical switch or lock assembly, the wiring harness that carries the signal, or the input circuit on the control board itself. Always verify your model family (top-load or front-load) before beginning diagnosis, because the specific component involved differs between the two.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty lid switch or door lock assembly** The mechanical switch or lock that signals closure has failed internally and no longer sends the correct status to the control board.
- **Loose or damaged wiring connectors** Vibration or wear has loosened the connector at the switch, lock, or control board, breaking the circuit.
- **Broken or pinched wire harness** The wiring between the lid or door assembly and the control board has been cut, pinched, or shorted, interrupting the sense signal.
- **Misaligned or obstructed lid or door** The lid or door is not closing fully due to a bent hinge, worn latch, or object blocking the closure, preventing the switch from actuating.
- **Failed control board input circuit** The control board's lid or door sense input has failed and cannot read the switch signal even when the switch and wiring are intact.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are uncomfortable working with the electrical connectors and control board, if the wiring harness runs through areas you cannot easily access, or if the fault persists after replacing the lid or door assembly. A technician has the diagnostic software and multimeter skills to isolate control board input circuit faults and can source the correct model-specific parts quickly. If your washer is still under warranty, contact Maytag service directly before attempting any repair.
