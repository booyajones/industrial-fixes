## Panasonic Microwave U14 Error Code — What It Means

The U14 error code on a Panasonic microwave indicates that the water tank is empty on models equipped with steam-cooking or water-tank operation. This is a self-diagnostic fault that tells you the unit cannot detect water in the tank during a steam cooking cycle. Panasonic's official troubleshooting instructions state that U14 means the water tank is empty and requires a refill, proper reinstallation, and a reset to clear the code.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Water tank is actually empty** The tank ran out of water during a steam cooking cycle or was not filled before starting the function.
- **Tank not seated correctly** The water tank was removed or reinserted improperly after refilling so the microwave cannot detect the water supply.
- **Tank removed during operation** The water tank was pulled out mid-cycle, interrupting the steam system and triggering the fault.
- **Water supply path interrupted** A blockage or disconnection in the water supply path prevents the system from detecting water flow even when the tank is full.
- **Failed water-level detection component** A sensor or detection switch in the tank assembly or steam system is faulty and cannot confirm water is present.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Stop the microwave** and cancel the current cycle by pressing Stop/Reset on the control panel.
2. **Remove the water tank** from the microwave and inspect it to confirm it is empty or low on water.
3. **Fill the tank** to the proper level with clean tap water according to the fill line marked on the tank.
4. **Reinstall the water tank** by sliding it fully into the correct position in the oven cavity until it seats firmly and you feel or hear it lock in place.
5. **Press Stop/Reset** on the control panel to clear the U14 code and attempt to resume steam cooking.
6. **Test a steam cycle** by running a short steam cook function to verify the error does not return and water flows correctly.
7. **Unplug the microwave** and contact an authorized Panasonic service center if the U14 code reappears after refill and reset, as this indicates a detection component or tank assembly fault requiring service-level troubleshooting.

## When to Call a Pro

Call an authorized Panasonic service center if the U14 code returns after you have refilled the tank, reseated it properly, and pressed Stop/Reset. Panasonic states that unresolved error codes may indicate a malfunction requiring professional diagnosis and repair. A technician will inspect the water tank assembly, the mounting interface, and any water-level or water-supply detection components in the steam system. Do not attempt to disassemble the microwave cabinet or steam system yourself, as this can void your warranty and expose you to high voltage hazards.
