## Bosch Washer E26 Error Code — What It Means

The E26 (or F26) error code on a Bosch washer indicates an analogue pressure sensor fault in the water-level sensing system. The pressure sensor measures how much water is in the tub and sends that signal to the control board so the machine knows when to fill, when to stop filling, and when the tub is empty. When this sensor fails or the control board cannot read its signal, the washer will not continue operation safely and will display E26.

This is not a drain pump problem or a clog in the drain hose. It is specifically a problem with the pressure-sensing circuit that monitors water level inside the drum. Bosch directs owners to service when this code appears because reliable water-level feedback is required for safe wash cycles.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the control board first when the real problem is a cracked or disconnected pressure-sensor hose. Always inspect the hose and its connections before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed analogue pressure sensor (~50%)** The sensor itself has failed and no longer sends a valid water-level signal to the control board.
- **Pressure-sensor hose cracked, kinked, or disconnected (~30%)** The small air hose that connects the tub to the sensor has a hole, kink, or loose connection so pressure cannot reach the sensor correctly.
- **Blockage in the pressure-sensor hose (~10%)** Detergent residue, lint, or debris has clogged the hose and prevents accurate pressure transmission.
- **Faulty wiring or connector at the pressure sensor (~7%)** The harness plug or wires between the sensor and the control board are loose, corroded, or broken.
- **Control board interpretation issue (~3%)** The control board itself cannot correctly read the sensor signal even when the sensor and hose are intact.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves before you begin any inspection or repair work.
2. **Pull the washer away from the wall** so you can access the rear panel or service panel, depending on your Bosch model.
3. **Locate the analogue pressure sensor**, which is usually a small round or square component mounted near the top or side of the tub with a thin air hose attached.
4. **Inspect the pressure-sensor hose** from the tub connection to the sensor for cracks, kinks, disconnections, or blockages, and reconnect or replace it if damaged.
5. **Test or replace the pressure sensor** by disconnecting the wiring harness and removing the mounting screws, then installing a new sensor if the hose and connections are intact.
6. **Reassemble the panels**, plug the washer back in, turn on the water, and run a short test cycle to verify that the E26 code does not return.
7. **If the error persists** after sensor replacement and hose checks, schedule a service appointment for control-board or wiring-harness diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional technician if you are not comfortable opening the washer cabinet or disconnecting electrical connectors. Also call service if the E26 code returns after you have replaced the pressure sensor and verified that the hose and connections are sound. At that point the fault is likely in the wiring harness or the control board, and a technician with a schematic and multimeter can trace the signal path and perform board-level diagnosis. Bosch directs customers to authorized service for this error code when the simple checks do not resolve it.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $150-300.
