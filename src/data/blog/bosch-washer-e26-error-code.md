## Bosch Washer E26 Error Code — What It Means

The E26 (or F26) error code on Bosch washing machines indicates a fault with the analogue pressure sensor or its water-level sensing circuit. According to Bosch's own support documentation, this code specifically flags a problem with the pressure sensor that tells the control board how much water is in the tub. While some third-party sources describe E26 as a drain-related error, Bosch's manufacturer guidance defines it as "analogue pressure sensor faulty." The sensor relies on air pressure changes transmitted through a small hose from the tub to detect water level. When that path is blocked, kinked, or the sensor itself fails, the board cannot accurately read fill or empty status and throws the code. Most often the root cause is a clogged or damaged pressure hose, contamination in the pressure chamber, or a failed sensor unit.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty analogue pressure sensor** The pressure sensor itself has failed and no longer provides accurate readings to the control board.
- **Blocked or kinked pressure hose** The air tube running from the tub pressure chamber to the sensor is obstructed, cracked, or pinched, preventing correct pressure transmission.
- **Clogged pressure chamber or air trap** Detergent sludge, lint, or scale has built up in the chamber where pressure sensing begins, blocking the air path.
- **Loose or corroded sensor connector** The wiring harness or pins at the sensor have corroded, backed out, or been damaged, interrupting the signal.
- **Control board sensing circuit fault** The main control board's input circuit for the pressure sensor is not reading or processing the signal correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and shut off water supply valves before opening any panels or disconnecting parts.
2. **Remove the top or rear panel** to gain access to the pressure sensor, typically mounted high on the side or rear of the tub.
3. **Inspect the pressure hose** from the tub's pressure chamber to the sensor for kinks, cracks, blockage, or disconnection, and clear or replace it if damaged.
4. **Check the pressure chamber** (air trap) at the bottom of the tub for detergent buildup or lint, and flush it clean if obstructed.
5. **Examine the sensor's electrical connector** for corrosion, loose pins, or damaged wiring, and clean or re-seat the connection.
6. **Replace the analogue pressure sensor** if the hose and chamber are clear and the code persists, as the sensor is the most common failing component.
7. **Run a fill and drain test cycle** to confirm the machine detects water level correctly and completes a full wash without repeating the error.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are uncomfortable working inside the washer cabinet, unplugging high-voltage connectors, or tracing wiring harnesses, call a qualified appliance technician. If you have replaced both the pressure hose and the sensor yet the E26 code returns, the fault may lie in the main control board's sensing circuit or require model-specific diagnostics and a service manual. Professional help is also recommended if the pressure chamber is difficult to access or if your washer is still under warranty, since self-repair can void coverage.
