## Electrolux Washer Won't Drain — What's Happening

When your Electrolux washer won't drain, the machine is unable to pump water out at the end of a cycle. You may see standing water in the drum, and the display often shows error codes E20, E21, or C2. These codes all point to a drainage problem rather than a single failed component.

Electrolux lists the root cause as a blocked drain path, dirty filter, or defective drain pump. The machine either cannot move water through the hose and pump assembly, or the control board is not detecting proper water evacuation. Most cases trace back to a physical blockage or incorrect hose installation rather than electrical failure.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Clogged pump filter or fluff filter** Lint, coins, and small objects collect in the filter housing and block water flow to the pump impeller.
- **Kinked, twisted, or incorrectly routed drain hose** The hose may be pinched behind the machine, extended more than 10 cm into the standpipe, or installed at the wrong height.
- **Blocked household drain or sink trap** The standpipe or sink connection may be clogged, preventing discharged water from leaving even if the pump runs.
- **Obstructed or damaged pump impeller** Debris can jam the impeller blades inside the pump body, or the blades may be cracked and unable to move water.
- **Failed drain pump** The pump motor may be electrically open, shorted, or mechanically seized so it cannot spin.
- **Excess suds from too much detergent** Electrolux notes that oversudsing can interfere with proper drainage and trigger drain-related error codes.
- **Faulty wiring or control board output** If the pump tests good but receives no voltage, the harness connector or main control board may have failed.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Note any error code on the display, then unplug the washer and move it forward for access to the rear and base.
2. Inspect the drain hose from the pump outlet to the standpipe or sink connection for kinks, twists, and proper routing; confirm the hose extends no more than 10 cm into the drain.
3. Test the household drain by running water into the sink or temporarily routing the washer hose into a bucket to rule out a clogged standpipe or trap.
4. Open the pump filter access door at the front lower corner, place towels to catch residual water, and unscrew the filter; clean all debris and rotate the visible pump blade by hand to check for obstructions or binding.
5. If the impeller spins freely and the filter is clear, disconnect power and remove the pump from its housing; inspect the impeller and volute for cracks, foreign objects, or damage.
6. Use a multimeter to check pump resistance across the motor terminals if the pump appears intact; be aware that Electrolux pumps vary by model and published values range from around 13 to 170 ohms depending on platform.
7. Check the wiring harness and connector at the pump for corrosion, loose pins, or broken wires if the pump itself tests within spec.
8. If all mechanical and electrical checks pass but the washer still will not drain, suspect a control board fault and consult model-specific diagnostics or replace the main PCB.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have cleaned the filter, confirmed the hose is routed correctly, and verified the household drain is clear but the washer still will not pump out, the issue is likely electrical. Testing the pump motor with a multimeter and tracing voltage at the control board requires live diagnostics and knowledge of model-specific resistance ranges. A technician can also access service mode to run forced drain cycles and read board-level fault history that is not visible on the user display.
