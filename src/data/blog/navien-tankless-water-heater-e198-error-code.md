## Navien Tankless Water Heater E198 Error — What It Means

The E198 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates a system fault or communication problem within the unit. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or wiring diagram for the specific definition. In general, this code points to an internal control issue, a sensor communication failure, or a circuit-board anomaly that prevents normal operation.

Because Navien uses proprietary diagnostics and the code can flag several different internal faults depending on the model year and firmware, you should start with basic power checks and a reset. If the error persists, professional diagnosis is usually needed to pinpoint whether the fault lies in wiring, a sensor, or the main control board.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace sensors or the igniter when E198 appears, but this code typically originates at the control board or internal communication bus. Have a technician test board signals and connectors before swapping expensive parts.

## Common Causes

- **Main control board fault (~50%)** A failing or glitched PCB triggers the E198 error when it cannot complete its self-check or communicate with sensors.
- **Loose or corroded internal wiring (~25%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen connector pins inside the cabinet, breaking communication between the board and components.
- **Sensor communication failure (~15%)** A temperature or flow sensor with a damaged cable or connector sends garbled data that the board flags as a system fault.
- **Power supply fluctuation (~10%)** Voltage sags or surges can corrupt the board's memory or cause a transient fault that registers as E198.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and at the unit's service switch if present.
2. **Wait 30 seconds**, then restore power to reset the control board and clear any transient fault.
3. **Observe the display** to see if E198 reappears immediately or if the unit begins a normal startup sequence.
4. **Remove the front cover** (typically four screws) and inspect all wire harness connectors and ribbon cables for looseness or corrosion.
5. **Reseat any loose connectors** by unplugging and firmly pressing them back into their sockets.
6. **Replace the cover**, restore full power, and test hot water flow at a fixture to confirm normal operation.
7. **If the error persists**, record the exact model number and serial number from the unit's rating plate and contact a licensed Navien technician for board-level diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional whenever E198 does not clear after a power reset or when you find no obvious loose connectors. Diagnosing control-board faults requires a multimeter, wiring diagrams, and familiarity with Navien's proprietary communication protocol. Technicians can test sensor signals, check board voltage rails, and safely replace the control board or wiring harness. Because tankless water heaters also involve gas lines and venting, any repair beyond a simple reset or connector reseat should be handled by a licensed plumber or HVAC technician trained on Navien units.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-500.
