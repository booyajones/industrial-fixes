## GE Dishwasher C1 Error Code — What It Means

The C1 code shows the dishwasher tried to pump out but the drain took longer than the control allows. GE flags this when the drain time runs past about two minutes.

This points to a partial restriction in the drain path rather than a dead pump. Water still moves, just too slowly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Clogged filter or sump** Food debris packs the bottom filter and slows the drain to a crawl.
- **Blocked air gap** The countertop air gap fills with gunk and chokes the outflow.
- **Kinked drain hose** A bent or crushed drain hose behind the unit restricts the pump out.
- **Clogged disposer inlet** A new disposer with the knockout plug still in place blocks the drain line.
- **Partial drain pump obstruction** A small piece of debris in the pump slows the impeller without fully jamming it.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker and pull out the lower rack.
2. Remove and rinse the bottom filter and clear any debris in the sump.
3. Clean the countertop air gap if your setup has one.
4. Check the drain hose for kinks and straighten any sharp bends.
5. If the unit drains into a disposer, confirm the knockout plug was removed.
6. Restore power, run a rinse cycle, and watch that the water clears fully.

## When to Call a Pro

If the filter, air gap, and hose are clear and C1 keeps returning, the drain pump may be weakening. A technician can test the pump and check the control before you spend on parts.
