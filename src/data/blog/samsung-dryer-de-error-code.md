## Samsung dE Error Code — What It Means

The dE error code on a Samsung washer indicates a door or door-lock fault. The machine is detecting that the door is not fully closed, not latched, or not being recognized as locked by the door interlock system. The washer will not start or continue the cycle until it confirms the door is securely locked.

In practical service terms, the usual failure path is the door latch, door lock assembly, or interlock circuit rather than a generic control-board error. The code can appear if a garment is trapped in the door opening, if debris or detergent buildup prevents full closure, if the door lock mechanism has failed, or if wiring to the lock is loose or damaged.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board thinking it is a sensor or software glitch. Instead, check that the door closes firmly and test or replace the door lock assembly first, since the lock hardware is the primary failure point for this code.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully closed or garment trapped** A piece of clothing caught in the door opening or gasket prevents the door from shutting completely and engaging the lock.
- **Debris, lint, or detergent buildup in the latch area** Residue or foreign objects in the door seal or striker prevent the latch from seating properly and triggering the interlock.
- **Faulty door lock or interlock assembly** The lock mechanism itself has failed mechanically or electrically and no longer actuates or signals the control that the door is locked.
- **Loose, corroded, or broken wiring to the lock** The harness connector to the door lock is loose, burnt, corroded, or has broken conductors that interrupt the signal to the control board.
- **Worn or misaligned door latch or handle** The plastic striker on the door or the handle assembly is cracked, worn, or misaligned so the door does not engage the lock properly.
- **Damaged or deformed door gasket** A torn, swollen, or misshapen door seal prevents the door from closing flush against the front of the tub and engaging the lock.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** from the wall outlet to kill power before you touch any internal components or wiring.
2. **Open and close the door firmly** to confirm it latches with a clear click and that no clothing, coins, or other objects are trapped in the gasket or door opening.
3. **Inspect the door seal and latch striker** for lint, detergent buildup, cracks, tears, or deformation that would prevent full closure, and clean or replace the gasket if damaged.
4. **Remove the top or front panel** as required by your model to access the door lock assembly (usually mounted on the front tub ring near the door opening).
5. **Disconnect the wiring harness** from the door lock and inspect the connector and terminals for corrosion, burn marks, loose pins, or broken wires.
6. **Test or replace the door lock assembly** by checking whether it physically latches when you press the striker into it and whether it shows continuity or resistance across its terminals (consult your model's wiring diagram for expected values), or simply replace the lock if it shows any mechanical or electrical fault.
7. **Reassemble the washer**, reconnect all wiring securely, restore power, and run a short rinse or drain cycle to verify the dE code clears and the door locks properly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional appliance technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connectors or disassembling the front panel of the washer. A pro is also the right choice if you have replaced the door lock assembly and inspected all wiring and the dE code still appears, since that may point to a control-board fault or a more complex interlock-circuit problem. If your washer is still under warranty, contact Samsung service or an authorized repair center before opening the machine yourself.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30–80 in parts, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–250.
