## Bosch Refrigerator Freezer Not Freezing — What's Happening

When your Bosch refrigerator's freezer compartment stops freezing, the control system is not managing cooling properly. The compressor may run but fail to maintain cold temperatures, or the unit may not run cooling cycles at all. On some models you may see an E2 fault code on the display, which signals a defective freezer compartment temperature sensor or thermostat issue. Even without a code, the same underlying problems can cause the freezer to stay warm.

The temperature sensor tells the control board how cold the freezer is so it can regulate compressor run time and defrost cycles. If the sensor fails or its wiring is damaged, the board gets no signal or a bad signal and cannot control freezing. Other basic issues like incorrect thermostat settings, no power to the unit, or a poor door seal can also prevent proper freezing.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Failed freezer temperature sensor** The thermistor in the freezer compartment has failed open, shorted, or is reading out of range, so the control board cannot measure freezer temperature and will not run proper cooling cycles.
- **Loose, damaged, or corroded sensor wiring** The harness or connector between the freezer sensor and control board has a broken wire, corroded terminal, or poor pin fit that interrupts the temperature signal.
- **Wrong thermostat setting or no power** The temperature control is set too warm, the unit is unplugged, or the outlet has no power, so the compressor never starts or runs only briefly.
- **Ice buildup or blocked airflow from defrost failure** Excessive frost on the evaporator coils or air ducts restricts cold air from circulating into the freezer, even though the compressor is running.
- **Faulty door gasket or door not closing fully** Warm air leaks into the freezer continuously because the door seal is torn or the door is misaligned, overwhelming the cooling system.
- **Defective control board or display module** The main control board has failed and cannot interpret sensor readings or command the compressor and fan relays, even when the sensor and wiring are good.
- **Ice maker module fault on some 500/800 series models** On certain Bosch models the ice maker control can interfere with normal freezer temperature management when it fails or reports an error.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify that the unit is plugged in, the outlet has power, and the circuit breaker is not tripped.
2. Check the freezer temperature setting on the control panel and confirm it is set to the recommended cold position (usually 0°F or lower).
3. Inspect the door gasket all the way around the freezer door for tears, gaps, or deformation, and confirm the door closes fully and latches without obstruction.
4. Unplug the refrigerator for 5 to 10 minutes, then restore power to reset the control board and clear any transient faults.
5. If an E2 code appears or the freezer stays warm, locate the freezer temperature sensor behind the rear panel in the freezer compartment and inspect the connector and wiring for corrosion, loose pins, or physical damage.
6. Disconnect the sensor harness and measure the sensor resistance with a multimeter, then compare the reading to the manufacturer's thermistor specification for your exact model (specifications are model-specific and not provided in general troubleshooting).
7. If the sensor reads open, shorted, or far out of range, replace the freezer temperature sensor.
8. If the sensor and wiring both test good but the fault persists, the control board or ice maker module may need replacement and professional diagnosis is recommended.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable working with live electrical connections, if you cannot locate or access the freezer temperature sensor, or if the sensor and wiring both test normal but the freezer still will not freeze. Sealed-system problems such as refrigerant leaks or compressor failure also require a licensed technician with refrigerant recovery equipment. If excessive frost or ice buildup persists even after confirming the door seal is good, the defrost system may need professional diagnosis and repair.
