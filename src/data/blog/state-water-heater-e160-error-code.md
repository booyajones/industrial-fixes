## State Water Heater E160 Error Code — What It Means

The E160 error code is not officially documented in available State Water Heater manufacturer materials, so its exact meaning may vary by model. Based on common fault patterns in State and A. O. Smith tankless units (State is part of the A. O. Smith family), codes in this range often relate to ignition failure caused by restricted airflow, insufficient gas supply, or inadequate water flow. The most frequently reported issue on State tankless systems is a blocked or dirty air intake screen, which prevents proper combustion air from reaching the burner. Without manufacturer-specific documentation for E160, always confirm the code definition in your unit's manual or service label before proceeding with repairs.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screen** Lint, dust, or debris on the intake screen restricts combustion air and triggers ignition faults.
- **Low or interrupted gas supply** A closed valve, empty propane tank, or kinked gas line prevents the burner from lighting.
- **Dirty inlet water filter** A clogged filter reduces flow below the minimum required for ignition, causing the unit to fault out.
- **Restricted venting or exhaust** Obstructions in the vent pipe or exhaust termination block combustion gases and stop the unit from firing.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the breaker and close the gas supply valve before inspecting any components.
2. **Locate and inspect the air intake screen** on the front or side of the unit and remove any visible lint, dust, or debris with a soft brush or vacuum.
3. **Check the gas supply** by confirming the manual valve is fully open, the propane tank has fuel (if applicable), and there are no kinks or damage in the gas line.
4. **Remove and clean the inlet water filter** at the cold water inlet connection, rinsing it under running water and clearing any sediment or debris.
5. **Inspect the vent and exhaust termination** outside the building for blockages such as bird nests, ice, or snow, and clear any obstructions.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then attempt to run hot water and observe whether the unit fires normally and the error clears.
7. **Record the exact model number** and consult your owner's manual or call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the code persists, since E160 definitions vary by model.

## When to Call a Pro

If cleaning the air intake, verifying gas supply, and clearing the inlet filter do not resolve the E160 code, or if you smell gas at any point, shut off the gas valve and call a licensed plumber or gas technician immediately. Because E160 is not standardized across all State models, a professional with access to manufacturer service bulletins can confirm the exact fault definition and test components such as the igniter, flame sensor, gas valve, and control board safely. Any work involving gas lines, venting modifications, or electrical diagnostics on tankless units should always be performed by a qualified technician.
