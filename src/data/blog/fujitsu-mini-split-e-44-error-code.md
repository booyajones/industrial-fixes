## Fujitsu E:44 Error Code — What It Means

The E:44 code on your Fujitsu mini-split indicates a fault in the indoor unit's sensing or control circuit. Fujitsu error codes are model-dependent, but this E4x-series fault typically points to a temperature sensor (thermistor) problem, a wiring issue, or a control board communication error. The indoor unit has detected that a sensor circuit is open, shorted, or not responding correctly.

This is not a refrigerant or compressor fault. The system has shut down to protect itself because it cannot accurately read the temperature or control inputs it needs to operate safely. The fault may be as simple as a loose connector or as involved as a failed controller board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or unplugged sensor connector** The thermistor harness plug at the indoor controller board has vibrated loose, was not fully seated during installation, or has corroded pins.
- **Open or shorted thermistor circuit** The temperature sensor itself has failed internally (open or shorted resistance) or its wiring has been damaged or pinched.
- **Damaged sensor wiring or harness break** The cable running from the sensor to the controller board has a break, cut, or internal wire failure that interrupts the signal.
- **Defective indoor controller PCB** The main control board has failed and cannot correctly read the sensor circuit even when the sensor and wiring test good.
- **Poor grounding or electrical interference** Voltage drop, inadequate grounding, or external electrical noise on the circuit disrupts sensor readings and communication.
- **System power interruption or reset needed** A transient electrical event or power surge has caused a fault flag that requires a full power cycle to clear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical connections, do not own a multimeter, or cannot locate your model's thermistor resistance specifications. Also call a pro if the fault returns after you have reseated connectors and power-cycled the unit, or if you need to replace the controller board and are unfamiliar with refrigerant-system electrical work. Fujitsu mini-splits require model-specific diagnostic charts and parts, and a qualified tech will have access to the factory service manual, correct replacement boards, and the tools to verify refrigerant charge and system operation after the repair.
