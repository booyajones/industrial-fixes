## LG Refrigerator FS Error Code — What It Means

The FS code (shown as Er FS) means the freezer temperature sensor, also called the freezer thermistor, is reading as a short or an open circuit. The main board relies on this sensor to know the freezer temperature, so a bad reading throws FS and cooling control suffers.

The usual causes are a failed thermistor, a damaged wiring connector, or moisture in the sensor circuit. The control board is the last item to check.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed freezer thermistor** A sensor that has shorted or opened internally gives the board an out of range reading.
- **Loose sensor connector** A connector that backed out or corroded breaks the circuit between the sensor and the board.
- **Moisture or water damage** Water reaching the sensor or its wiring corrodes pins and causes a false short or open.
- **Pinched or chafed wiring** A wire pinched during a prior repair can break the sensor circuit over time.
- **Main control board fault** A board that misreads the sensor input can post FS even with a good thermistor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator or trip the breaker for about 30 seconds, then restore power to see if FS clears.
2. Locate the freezer thermistor and inspect its connector for looseness, corrosion, or moisture.
3. Reseat the connector firmly and clean any corrosion you find on the pins.
4. Check the wiring along its run for pinch points or chafing and repair any damage.
5. Measure the thermistor resistance with a multimeter and compare it against the value for the freezer temperature.
6. Replace the freezer thermistor if the resistance is out of range or reads open or shorted.
7. If a new sensor and clean wiring still show FS, replace the main control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if FS returns after a new thermistor and verified wiring. A code that survives a good sensor usually points to a board or harness fault that needs careful meter work to confirm.
