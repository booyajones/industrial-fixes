## Samsung Dryer HC2 Error Code — What It Means

Samsung identifies HC2 as a gas ignition error code on gas dryers. The dryer has detected a failure in the gas-heating or ignition sequence, meaning the burner is not lighting properly or the control cannot verify ignition. This is not a general overheating warning. It is a specific fault tied to the gas ignition circuit.

Samsung's support documentation lists HC2 among error codes that should be addressed first by power-cycling the dryer, then checking the manual and calling for service if the code returns. While airflow restrictions like a clogged lint screen or vent are the most common cause of Samsung dryer error codes in general, HC2 points directly to a problem in the ignition path rather than airflow alone.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the gas valve assembly first, but a failed igniter or flame sensor is more common and cheaper. Test the igniter for continuity and observe whether it glows before replacing the valve.

## Common Causes

- **Gas ignition circuit failure (~50%)** A failed igniter, gas valve coil, or flame sensor prevents the burner from lighting and triggers HC2.
- **Clogged lint screen or exhaust vent (~25%)** Restricted airflow causes trapped heat and can interfere with burner operation, leading to repeated error conditions.
- **Gas supply interruption (~15%)** A closed shutoff valve or empty propane tank stops gas flow to the burner and prevents ignition.
- **Control board or wiring fault (~10%)** A failed connection or control board issue prevents the dryer from powering or sensing the ignition circuit correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the dryer** by unplugging it or switching off the breaker, wait 2–3 minutes, then restore power and restart a cycle to reset the control.
2. **Remove and clean the lint filter** thoroughly, and inspect the rear exhaust vent for blockages or kinks that restrict airflow.
3. **Verify the gas supply** by checking that the shutoff valve at the wall or floor is turned fully to the open position, and confirm that a propane tank is not empty if applicable.
4. **Access the burner assembly** by removing the lower front or rear access panel (consult your model's service manual for panel location and fastener type).
5. **Inspect the igniter** for cracks or breaks, then use a multimeter to test continuity. A good igniter will show continuity and should glow orange when the dryer calls for heat.
6. **Check the flame sensor** for corrosion or soot buildup, and test continuity. Clean the sensor with fine sandpaper if dirty.
7. **Test the gas valve coils** for continuity. If the igniter glows but the valve does not open, replace the gas valve assembly or the coils if sold separately.
8. **If HC2 returns after these checks**, stop further disassembly and call a Samsung-qualified technician for control board or wiring harness diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional immediately if you smell unburned gas at any point, or if HC2 returns after power-cycling, cleaning the lint system, and confirming gas supply. Gas ignition faults require diagnosis of the igniter, flame sensor, gas valve, and control board wiring. A qualified appliance technician has the tools to measure igniter draw, test valve coil operation, and verify flame sensing circuits safely. Samsung recommends service if the error persists after basic user-level troubleshooting. Do not attempt to disassemble the burner or gas valve without shutting off the gas supply and unplugging the dryer.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150–300.
