## Carrier Error Code 29 — What It Means

Error code 29 does not have a universal meaning across all Carrier HVAC equipment. Carrier furnaces and air handlers use two-digit status codes, but the specific definition of code 29 varies by model and control board generation. Without your exact model number, the code cannot be definitively interpreted.

If you see code 29 on a different brand of condensing equipment (such as certain tankless water heaters), it typically indicates a condensate drainage or neutralizer failure. Always verify the fault code against your unit's specific service manual or the label inside the blower compartment door.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked condensate drain line** Algae, mineral deposits, or debris can clog the condensate trap or drain tubing and trigger a drainage fault on condensing equipment.
- **Incorrect drain piping slope** Drain lines installed with upward slope or insufficient fall prevent gravity drainage and cause water to back up into the unit.
- **Trapped or kinked drain tubing** Improper pipe routing creates low spots where condensate accumulates and blocks flow.
- **Clogged neutralizer cartridge** On high-efficiency equipment that uses a condensate neutralizer, a spent or plugged cartridge restricts drainage.
- **Faulty condensate sensor or switch** A stuck float switch or failed sensor can report a drainage fault even when the drain is clear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you cannot locate a fault-code table for your specific Carrier model, if the error persists after clearing the drain and verifying slope, or if you are not comfortable working with electrical connections and pressure switches. A technician has the service literature and diagnostic tools to identify the exact cause of code 29 on your unit and can test condensate sensors, measure flue pressures, and verify control-board operation. Professional help is also recommended if your unit requires a neutralizer kit installation or if the fault involves the heat exchanger or flue system.
