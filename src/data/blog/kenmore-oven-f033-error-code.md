## Kenmore Oven F033 Error Code — What It Means

The F033 error code on Kenmore ovens typically indicates a problem with the touch-pad interface or the main electronic control board. The exact meaning can vary by model, so check your owner's manual or wiring diagram for your specific unit. In most cases, the control board is detecting that the touch-pad membrane is either stuck, sending continuous signals, or has lost communication with the board. This code often appears after years of use when the membrane contacts degrade or when moisture gets behind the control panel.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the entire electronic control board when only the touch-pad overlay or membrane is at fault. Disconnect and reseat the ribbon cable between the touch panel and control board first, and inspect the membrane for tears or moisture before ordering expensive electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Touch-pad membrane failure (~50%)** The flexible membrane overlay degrades over time, causing stuck buttons or poor contact with the control board.
- **Loose or corroded ribbon cable (~20%)** The flat ribbon cable connecting the touch panel to the control board can work loose or develop corrosion at the connector.
- **Electronic control board fault (~20%)** The main control board may have failed circuitry in the touch-pad interface section.
- **Moisture intrusion (~10%)** Water or steam seeping behind the control panel can short contacts and trigger false signals.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and unplug the oven if possible to make sure safe work.
2. **Remove the control panel** by taking out the screws along the front edge or sides of the panel, then gently pull it forward.
3. **Disconnect the ribbon cable** from the back of the touch panel and the control board by releasing the locking tab or clip and sliding the cable out.
4. **Inspect the ribbon cable and connectors** for corrosion, damage, or poor seating. Clean contacts with isopropyl alcohol if needed and reseat firmly.
5. **Examine the touch-pad membrane** for tears, stuck buttons, or moisture. If damaged, order a replacement membrane or touch-panel assembly for your model.
6. **Reconnect the ribbon cable** and reassemble the control panel, then restore power and test. If the error returns, replace the touch panel or control board as indicated by your diagnostics.
7. **Run a test cycle** to confirm the oven operates normally and the F033 code does not reappear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections inside the oven, if the error persists after reseating cables and replacing the touch panel, or if your oven requires accessing high-voltage wiring behind the control board. A pro can test the control board's output signals and verify proper grounding, and they will have the tools to diagnose intermittent faults that are hard to catch at home. If your oven is still under warranty or is a built-in model requiring cabinet removal, professional service is the safer choice.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40-120 in parts, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-280.
