## Bosch E07 Error Code — What It Means

Bosch E07 indicates a problem in the dryer fan, ventilation, or Zeolite/CrystalDry drying system. The control board has detected that the drying fan assembly or vent path is not operating correctly. This is not a drain fault. The error appears when the fan motor fails to run, when airflow is blocked by debris or moisture, when wiring connections are loose or damaged, or when water has intruded into the drying chamber from a cracked or melted component.

The code will appear on the display panel during or after a cycle when the dishwasher attempts to activate the drying system. Some models use a Zeolite mineral chamber with a fan to recirculate heated air. Others use a vent door and fan assembly. In either case, the control expects to see the fan running and air moving. When that feedback signal is absent or incorrect, E07is triggered and the cycle may stop or complete without proper drying.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the control board first, thinking E07 is an electronic fault. Inspect the fan area for blockages, check the wiring harness for looseness or corrosion, and test the fan motor electrically before ordering any board.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty fan motor in the CrystalDry or Zeolite drying system (~40%)** The motor may have failed electrically, seized, or developed a short, preventing the control from seeing the expected feedback signal.
- **Blocked fan or vent path (~25%)** Debris, food particles, moisture buildup, or a physical obstruction in the airflow path stops the fan from spinning or reduces airflow below the threshold the control expects.
- **Loose, damaged, or disconnected wiring at the fan assembly (~20%)** Vibration, heat, or corrosion can cause harness connectors to pull away or wires to fray, breaking the signal circuit between the fan and the control board.
- **Water intrusion into the Zeolite chamber or drying assembly (~10%)** A cracked tube, melted spray-arm supply line, or failed seal can allow wash water to flood the drying system, shorting the fan or triggering a moisture-related fault.
- **Faulty vent door or vent mechanism (~5%)** On models with a door-mounted vent, a stuck or broken vent door prevents air circulation and the control sees the drying cycle as incomplete.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and wait 60 seconds, then restore power and run a short cycle to see if the code clears or returns.
2. **Remove the dishwasher door panel** or vent grille as required for your model to access the drying-fan assembly, following the service manual for your specific series.
3. **Inspect the fan and vent path** for debris, moisture, or physical blockages, and clean any obstructions from the fan blades, housing, and air ducts.
4. **Check all wiring connectors** at the fan motor and trace the harness back to the control board, looking for loose pins, corrosion, frayed insulation, or heat damage.
5. **Test the fan motor electrically** with a multimeter set to resistance if you have access to the motor terminals, and compare the reading to any service documentation for your model.
6. **Inspect for water intrusion** by examining the Zeolite chamber, tubing, and spray-arm supply lines for cracks, melting, or signs of leakage that would flood the drying system.
7. **Replace the fan motor or complete fan assembly** if the motor fails electrical testing, is physically seized, or shows visible damage, and reassemble the door panel and vent grille.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable removing the dishwasher door panel, working with electrical connectors, or using a multimeter to test motor circuits. The drying-fan assembly sits behind interior panels and requires partial disassembly of the door or chassis. If water intrusion is present and you cannot identify the cracked or melted component, a technician can trace the leak source and repair the underlying plumbing fault. If the fan motor tests good and the wiring is intact but the code persists, the control board may need replacement or reflashing, which is best handled by a Bosch-certified service center with access to diagnostic software and updated firmware.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350 depending on part and labor.
