## Bosch E07 Error Code — What It Means

The E07 error code on Bosch dishwashers with the Zeolite or CrystalDry drying system indicates a fault in the Zeolite drying fan motor circuit. Bosch identifies this as a defective Zeolite (Crystal Dry) fan motor. However, field experience shows the fault is frequently triggered by water intrusion into the Zeolite drying assembly rather than a failed motor alone. The error prevents the drying fan from operating correctly, which impacts the final drying phase of the cycle.

## Common Causes

- **Cracked fill tube or water supply tube leaking onto the Zeolite system** This is the most commonly reported field cause, where a crack allows water to spray directly into the drying fan assembly and flood the duct.
- **Standing water in the Zeolite fan duct or reservoir** Water accumulation in the fan housing or Zeolite chamber triggers the fault and may clear temporarily after drying but returns until the leak source is repaired.
- **Failed Zeolite drying fan motor** The motor itself can fail electrically or mechanically, and replacement is required when the unit does not respond after drying and leak repair.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connection at the fan** The connector or harness to the fan motor can become loose, corroded, or damaged, breaking the circuit and triggering the code.
- **Blocked vent or debris in the drying air path** Obstructions in the vent or drying system can prevent proper fan operation and airflow, causing a fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset power at the breaker** for at least 30 seconds to clear any transient fault, then restore power and test the dishwasher.
2. **Remove the lower access panel or kick plate** to reach the Zeolite drying fan assembly, typically located at the front lower area of built-in models.
3. **Inspect for standing water** around the fan duct, Zeolite reservoir, and nearby areas, and use a wet/dry vacuum to remove all moisture and dry the assembly thoroughly.
4. **Check the fill tube and water supply tubes** for visible cracks, especially anywhere near the drying system, and replace any damaged tubing immediately as this is the most common root cause.
5. **Examine the fan motor connector and wiring** for looseness, corrosion, or physical damage, and reseat or repair connections as needed.
6. **Test the fan motor with a multimeter** across its terminals to verify electrical continuity, keeping in mind resistance values vary by model and are not universally specified.
7. **Replace the Zeolite fan assembly** if the motor fails testing, if water damage persists, or if the fault returns after all leak sources have been corrected and the unit dried.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components, if you cannot safely access the lower interior of the dishwasher, or if the error persists after you have dried the system and inspected for visible leaks. Technicians have the tools to test the fan motor circuit accurately, identify hidden water intrusion points, and replace the Zeolite assembly correctly. If your dishwasher is under warranty, contact Bosch or an authorized service center before opening the unit to avoid voiding coverage.
