## LG Dishwasher CE Error Code — What It Means

LG states that the CE (or LE) display message is a motor error on dishwashers. It may indicate an issue with the motor itself or the wiring harness between the motor and the control board. The code is tied specifically to the wash motor or circulation motor circuit, not a general communication fault.

When the control board loses proper signal from the motor or detects an abnormal load condition, it halts the cycle and displays CE. The fault can stem from a failed motor assembly, a loose or corroded connector, or damage along the motor harness.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many owners replace the main control board first, assuming an electronics fault. Inspect and test the motor wiring harness and motor assembly connector integrity before ordering a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed wash motor or circulation motor (~50%)** The motor windings have burned out, the impeller is seized, or the motor cannot generate the required speed signal for the control board.
- **Loose or corroded motor wiring harness connector (~25%)** The plug between the motor and control board is not seated, has corroded pins, or vibration has worked it free during operation.
- **Damaged motor wiring harness (~15%)** The wire bundle is pinched under the tub, chafed against a sharp edge, or has internal breaks that interrupt the motor signal.
- **Mechanical bind or overload in the wash system (~7%)** A foreign object is jamming the wash impeller, spray arm bearings are seized, or the pump housing is cracked and causing drag on the motor shaft.
- **Faulty main control board motor-drive circuit (~3%)** The control board's motor relay or driver has failed and cannot energize or read the motor correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **De-energize the dishwasher** by unplugging it or switching off the circuit breaker at the panel before any inspection or repair work.
2. **Reset the control** by restoring power, turning the dishwasher on, and starting a new cycle to see if the CE code clears.
3. **Access the motor and wiring** by removing the dishwasher's lower front panel or tipping the unit and removing the base cover, depending on your model.
4. **Inspect the motor wiring harness** for pinched, chafed, or broken wires and check that the connector between the motor and control board is fully seated and free of corrosion.
5. **Check the motor assembly** by attempting to spin the impeller by hand. It should rotate smoothly with no grinding, seizing, or foreign objects in the housing.
6. **Replace the motor or circulation pump assembly** if the impeller is jammed, the motor does not hum or spin when power is applied during a test cycle, or resistance testing (if you have a multimeter) shows an open or shorted winding.
7. **Reassemble and test** by securing all panels, restoring power, and running a full wash cycle to confirm the CE error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections, if the dishwasher is built-in and difficult to access, or if the error persists after you have verified wiring integrity and replaced the motor assembly. A technician can perform resistance and voltage tests on the motor windings and control board outputs, confirm that household water pressure is above 20 PSI (which LG specifies for proper operation), and diagnose any interaction between the door latch sensor and motor circuit that might contribute to the fault.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $100–250 in parts (motor assembly), 1–2 hours. A pro service call runs about $200–400 for diagnosis and motor replacement.
