## What this code means
Ed22 (sometimes displayed as Ed,22) on a Speed Queen washer means the drive motor has overheated or the control board believes motor temperature is too high. The washer shuts down to protect the drive system from damage. This code usually points to something forcing the motor to work harder than normal, which raises current draw and heat. Common triggers include overloaded drums, mechanical drag in the basket or bearings, loose or damaged motor wiring, or a failing motor that can no longer handle normal loads without overheating.

## Common Causes

- **Overloaded washer** A drum packed too full or with heavy items forces the motor to draw excessive current and overheat.
- **Mechanical drag or resistance** Worn bearings, tight seals, or foreign objects create friction that prevents the drum from spinning freely and overloads the motor.
- **Loose or damaged motor harness** A connector that is not fully seated or a chafed wire between the motor and drive board can cause erratic operation and false overtemp signals.
- **Failed drive motor** An aging motor with worn windings or internal faults may run hot even under normal load conditions.
- **Temporary control glitch** Occasionally the board misreads motor temperature and a simple power reset clears the fault if no underlying problem exists.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and wait 1 to 3 minutes to reset the control board, then restore power and test a short cycle to see if Ed22 returns.
2. **Check the load** in the drum to confirm you did not exceed the rated capacity or pack clothes so tightly that the motor had to strain during agitation or spin.
3. **Manually rotate the drum** with the washer empty and power off to verify the basket spins smoothly without roughness, binding, or unusual noise that would indicate bearing drag or mechanical interference.
4. **Inspect the motor wiring harness** at both the motor and the drive board, pressing each connector fully home and looking for chafed insulation, heat marks, or broken wires.
5. **If the drum does not spin freely,** diagnose and repair the source of mechanical drag (bearings, stuck objects, or damaged seals) before replacing any electrical components.
6. **If wiring is intact and the drum spins freely,** but Ed22 persists under normal loads, test the motor for winding resistance per your model's service sheet or plan to replace the drive motor.
7. **Reassemble and run a test cycle** with a small balanced load to confirm normal operation and verify that motor temperature stays in the safe range.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you do not feel comfortable unplugging the machine and manually rotating the drum, if you find the drum difficult to turn and suspect bearing work, or if the motor harness and load conditions check out but Ed22 keeps coming back. Motor and bearing replacement on Speed Queen washers involves disassembling the cabinet and drive train, and the repair is faster and safer when you have the correct service literature and torque specs for your model. A qualified appliance tech can also measure motor winding resistance and verify control-board signals to pinpoint whether the fault is thermal, mechanical, or electronic before ordering parts.
