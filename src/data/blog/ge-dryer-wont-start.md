## GE Dryer Won't Start — What's Happening

A GE dryer that won't start is a symptom, not a single fault code. The machine is not entering the run state, and the cause can be anything from a simple power interruption to a failed safety switch or a control board personality issue on newer electronic models. GE's factory guidance starts with checking whether the dryer has power by opening the door and looking for the drum light. If the light works, you can rule out a basic power problem and move to other checks.

On some newer GE electronic dryers (2019 and later), a failed start condition after a power interruption can require a personality reset. Electric models use selection 1 and gas models use selection 2 during that reset procedure. Beyond that, the most common culprits are the door switch, start switch, thermal fuse, or drive belt system.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Tripped or blown breaker** No power to the dryer is the most frequent cause, often from a tripped breaker, unplugged cord, or missing line voltage.
- **Failed door switch** The door switch must close the circuit when the door is shut, and a stuck or broken switch will prevent the dryer from starting.
- **Blown thermal fuse** A blown thermal fuse or other safety fuse opens the start circuit and stops all operation.
- **Bad push-to-start switch** The start switch can wear out or fail to close the circuit when pressed, blocking the run command.
- **Broken drive belt or belt switch** If the belt is off or the belt-safety interlock is open, some dryers will not start as a precaution.
- **Control board personality reset needed** Some 2019+ GE electronic dryers may fail to start after a power loss until you perform a stored-configuration personality reset.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Open the dryer door and check whether the drum light comes on to confirm the unit has power.
2. If the light does not illuminate, verify outlet power and reset the breaker at the panel.
3. Measure supply voltage at the terminal block on electric dryers (you should see about 120 V on each hot leg to neutral and at least 208 V across the two hot legs).
4. Test the door switch by confirming it clicks and changes continuity when you open and close the door, or bypass it temporarily to see if the dryer runs.
5. Test the push-to-start switch with a meter in resistance or continuity mode and replace it if it does not close when pressed.
6. Test the thermal fuse and any high-limit safety fuses for continuity (a blown fuse will read open).
7. Inspect the drive belt path and belt-safety switch if the drum or belt interlock is suspect.
8. On newer electronic GE dryers, perform the personality reset procedure if the unit powers but will not run after a power loss (use selection 1 for electric, selection 2 for gas).

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you are not comfortable working with 240 V electric circuits or if you have verified all the common safety switches and fuses and the dryer still will not start. Control-board diagnosis and personality resets on newer GE electronic models can be tricky without the service manual, and a qualified technician can run the board's internal diagnostics and check for stored fault codes you cannot see from the user interface.
