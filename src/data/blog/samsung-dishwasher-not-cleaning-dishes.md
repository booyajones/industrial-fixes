## Samsung Dishwasher Not Cleaning Dishes — What's Happening

When your Samsung dishwasher doesn't clean well, the cycle completes but dishes come out dirty, greasy, or covered in food particles. This is a performance symptom with multiple possible causes, not a single fault code. Samsung ties poor cleaning to water delivery problems, clogged filters or spray arms, detergent dispenser issues, improper loading, and water quality. In some cases, dirty water can recirculate if the drain hose lacks a proper high loop to prevent backflow from the drainpipe.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Most Likely Causes

- **Clogged filter assembly** Food debris trapped in the filter recirculates onto dishes during the wash cycle instead of draining away.
- **Clogged spray arm nozzles** Mineral deposits or food particles block the jets in the middle or lower spray arms, reducing water coverage on dishes.
- **Detergent dispenser malfunction** A clogged spring, damaged door, or sticky dispenser prevents detergent from releasing properly during the cycle.
- **Low water supply pressure** Insufficient water pressure or a partially closed supply valve limits the amount of water entering the dishwasher.
- **Improper loading or overloading** Dishes packed too tightly or positioned incorrectly block spray arms and prevent water and detergent from reaching all surfaces.
- **Hard water or mineral buildup** Mineral deposits reduce cleaning performance and can clog components over time if the water softener salt is not refilled.
- **Drain hose backflow** A drain hose installed without a high loop allows dirty water to siphon back into the dishwasher and contaminate clean dishes.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Confirm that detergent and rinse aid are loaded and that an appropriate wash cycle was selected for the soil level.
2. Check that the water supply valve under the sink is fully open and verify adequate supply pressure (Samsung specifies 20 PSI to 120 PSI).
3. Remove and clean the filter assembly by rinsing it under warm water and scrubbing away debris with a soft brush, then replace if damaged.
4. Remove the middle spray arm by holding the nut and rotating clockwise, and pull the lower spray arm straight up to remove it, then wash both in warm soapy water and clear clogged jets with a soft brush or toothpick.
5. Inspect the detergent dispenser for clogs or damage, clean the spring and door with a small brush, and replace the dispenser if the spring or door is broken.
6. Check the drain hose installation and confirm it has a proper high loop to prevent backflow of dirty water from the drainpipe.
7. If hard water is present and your model uses a softener, refill dishwasher salt and adjust regeneration settings per the user manual.
8. Run a test cycle with properly loaded dishes after cleaning and corrections to verify improved performance.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if cleaning and maintenance steps do not restore performance, if the water inlet valve needs replacement, or if you suspect an internal circulation pump or wash motor issue. A pro can test water flow rates, measure inlet valve function, and diagnose electronic control problems that affect wash cycle performance. If your dishwasher is still under warranty, contact Samsung support before attempting component replacement.
