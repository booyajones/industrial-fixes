## GE Oven FC Error Code — What It Means

FC means the control found a door lock system fault, much like F9. It commonly appears during or after self clean when the lock motor fails to engage or release the latch on cue.

The usual culprits are debris in the latch path, a lock switch that reports the wrong state, or a lock motor that hums but will not move. Wiring and the control round out the list.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Debris in the latch** Crumbs or buildup block the latch so it cannot reach the locked or unlocked position.
- **Failed lock switch** A lock sensing switch sends the wrong state and the control flags FC.
- **Failed lock motor** The door lock motor hums but cannot drive the latch through its travel.
- **Damaged lock wiring** A frayed or pinched wire in the lock circuit breaks continuity to the control.
- **Failed control board** The control misreads a working lock circuit and reports FC.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for about 5 minutes to reset the control.
2. Restore power and inspect the latch area for debris, then clear anything blocking the path.
3. If the door is stuck shut, find the door jamb switch on the face and press it until the cavity light goes off.
4. If FC persists, kill power and reach the lock mechanism behind the console.
5. Test the lock switch for continuity and rotate the cam to confirm it moves freely.
6. Replace the lock switch or the full door lock motor assembly if it binds or tests bad.
7. Restore power and run a short self clean to confirm the latch locks and releases.

## When to Call a Pro

If the latch will not move and the door stays locked on a hot oven, or you see burnt lock wiring, call a tech. Forcing a hot self clean door can damage the latch and the door frame.
