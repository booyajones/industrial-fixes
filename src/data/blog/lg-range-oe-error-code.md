## LG OE Error Code — What It Means

The OE error code on LG washers (both front-load and top-load) indicates a drain failure. The machine has detected that water is not being expelled from the tub within the expected time. This is LG's signal that something in the drain path is blocked, kinked, or malfunctioning. The washer will typically stop mid-cycle and display OE on the control panel, often leaving standing water in the drum. LG's own support materials point to three primary areas: the drain hose, the drain pump filter, and the drain pump itself.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked or clogged drain hose** A crushed, twisted, or internally blocked drain hose restricts water flow and prevents the washer from draining properly.
- **Clogged drain pump filter** Lint, coins, buttons, and other debris accumulate in the filter at the lower front access panel and obstruct the pump inlet.
- **Failed or obstructed drain pump** The drain pump itself may be jammed by foreign objects, or its motor may have failed and cannot expel water even when the hose and filter are clear.
- **Excessive suds** Using too much detergent or the wrong type creates foam that the pump cannot remove effectively, triggering a drain-related error.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the washer from the wall outlet to make the unit safe before beginning any inspection or repair work.
2. Inspect the drain hose at the rear of the washer for kinks, crushed sections, or clogs. Straighten or clear any restrictions, then run a spin-only cycle to test drainage.
3. Locate and open the drain pump filter access panel at the lower front of the machine. Place towels on the floor, drain any standing water into a shallow pan, then remove and clean the filter of all lint, coins, and debris.
4. Run a spin-only cycle and listen for the drain pump to hum or run when the cycle starts. If the pump does not engage or makes unusual noise, the pump or its connected hoses may be obstructed or faulty.
5. If the pump does not run or the error returns after cleaning the hose and filter, pull the washer forward and inspect the drain pump and its attached hoses for blockages or damage. Replace the pump if it fails to operate correctly.
6. If you have been using excessive detergent, reduce the amount per load and run a tub-clean cycle to clear residual suds that may interfere with drain sensing.
7. Run a full test cycle after clearing the fault to confirm the washer drains normally and the OE code does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have cleaned the drain hose and filter, verified the pump runs during a spin cycle, and the OE error still appears, the issue may involve internal wiring, a faulty pressure switch, or a control-board fault that requires diagnostic tools and model-specific service procedures. A qualified appliance technician can measure pump voltage, test the pressure sensor, and access service-mode diagnostics to pinpoint the root cause. Also call a pro if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections, moving a heavy washer, or accessing internal components behind panels.
