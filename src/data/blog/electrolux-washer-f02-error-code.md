## Electrolux Washer F02 Error Code — What It Means

The F02 error code on many Electrolux washer platforms indicates the machine is detecting a drain cycle that is taking longer than expected. The washer attempted to pump water out but did not see the water level drop within the programmed time window. This is typically a mechanical obstruction or pump problem, not an electronics fault. Note that Electrolux error codes can vary by model family, so always cross-check your specific model number against the service sheet if F02 does not match a drain-related symptom.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked or clogged drain hose** The flexible drain hose is pinched, routed too low, or blocked internally by lint and debris.
- **Dirty drain pump filter** The pump filter or coin trap is packed with lint, coins, or small objects and restricts water flow out of the tub.
- **Blocked drainpipe or utility sink** The standpipe or sink the hose drains into is clogged or the air gap is sealed, preventing water from leaving the machine.
- **Jammed or failed drain pump** The pump impeller is jammed by debris or the pump motor has failed and cannot move water even when powered.
- **Excessive suds from non-HE detergent** Too much detergent or regular detergent in an HE machine creates foam that slows drainage and can fool the pressure sensor.
- **Faulty pump wiring or control signal** Broken wires, loose connectors, or a control board fault prevent the pump from receiving power during the drain command.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off and reset the washer.** Unplug the machine or turn off the circuit breaker, wait five minutes, then restore power and try running a drain or spin cycle to see if the fault clears on its own.
2. **Inspect the drain hose routing.** Pull the washer forward and check the entire length of the drain hose for kinks, crushing, or low loops that trap water, and confirm the standpipe or sink connection is not blocked.
3. **Clean the drain pump filter.** Unplug the washer, locate the filter access door at the front lower corner, place towels and a shallow pan to catch residual water, unscrew or pull the filter, and remove all lint, coins, and debris from the filter housing and impeller area.
4. **Test the drain pump for obstructions.** With the filter removed, shine a light into the pump chamber and rotate the impeller by hand to confirm it spins freely and is not jammed by fabric, wire, or hard objects.
5. **Run a manual drain cycle.** Reinstall the filter, restore power, and select drain or spin-only mode to listen for pump operation. A humming noise without water movement suggests a jammed or weak pump motor.
6. **Check pump electrical connections.** If the pump is silent, verify the wire harness is fully seated on the pump terminals and inspect for visible damage or corrosion on the connector and wiring.
7. **Replace the drain pump if defective.** If the pump receives power but does not run or runs weakly, order the correct replacement pump assembly for your model, disconnect the old unit, transfer hoses and clamps, and install the new pump following the service manual torque and clamp specifications.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the drain pump runs but the code returns, if you find water remaining in the tub after a full drain cycle with a clean filter and clear hose, or if you are not comfortable working with 120-volt AC wiring and water connections. A pro can perform model-specific diagnostics to test the pressure switch, control board drain output, and wiring continuity that require service mode and a multimeter. Also call for help if the washer is still under warranty, since DIY pump replacement may void coverage.
