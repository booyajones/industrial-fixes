## Samsung Washer UD5 Error — What It Means

The UD5 error code on Samsung front-load and top-load washers indicates an unbalanced load condition. The washer's sensors detect that the weight distribution inside the drum is uneven, preventing the machine from spinning safely at high speed. Samsung washers attempt to redistribute the load automatically by stopping and restarting the spin several times, but if the imbalance persists the machine will halt and display UD5.

This code protects the suspension and bearings from damage caused by excessive vibration. It appears most often with bulky items like comforters, a single heavy towel, or a small mixed load that clumps to one side during the spin cycle. In some cases the problem is not the load itself but worn suspension components or a washer that is not sitting level on the floor.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board or drum sensors when the real cause is worn suspension rods or shock absorbers. Check that the drum moves freely and returns to center when pushed, and verify the washer is level on all four feet before ordering electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Unbalanced or bulky load (~50%)** Heavy items like comforters or a single towel clump to one side of the drum during spin, triggering the sensor.
- **Worn suspension rods or shock absorbers (~20%)** Weakened suspension components no longer dampen drum movement, causing excessive swing and imbalance detection.
- **Washer not level (~15%)** One or more leveling feet are not properly adjusted or the floor is uneven, allowing the drum to tilt during high-speed spin.
- **Failed hall sensor or control board (~10%)** The sensor that monitors drum speed and balance may send incorrect signals, or the control board may interpret normal motion as an imbalance.
- **Loose or broken shipping bolts (~5%)** If shipping bolts were not removed during installation or cabinet mounts have loosened, the drum cannot move freely on its suspension.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves.
2. **Open the door** and redistribute the wet laundry evenly around the drum, separating any clumped items.
3. **Check all four leveling feet** by pushing down on each corner of the washer to make sure it does not rock; adjust any foot that does not touch the floor by turning it clockwise to extend or counterclockwise to retract.
4. **Place a bubble level** on top of the washer front to back and side to side, and adjust the rear self-leveling feet or front feet until the bubble is centered in both directions.
5. **Inspect the drum suspension** by reaching inside and pushing the drum firmly to each side; it should move smoothly and return to center without binding or excessive play.
6. **Remove the rear or top access panel** (depending on your model) and visually inspect the suspension rods or shock absorbers for leaks, cracks, or detachment; replace any damaged component.
7. **Reconnect power and water**, run a short spin cycle with a balanced mixed load, and verify the code does not reappear.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if redistributing the load and leveling the washer do not resolve the UD5 code, especially if the drum wobbles excessively or you hear clunking during spin. Professionals have diagnostic tools to test the hall sensor and control board signals, and they can safely disassemble the cabinet to replace suspension components or check for loose mounting hardware. If the washer is still under warranty, professional service may be covered. Also call if you are uncomfortable working around the drum assembly or if the machine shows other error codes in combination with UD5.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $120-250.
