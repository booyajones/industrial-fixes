## Navien E322 Error Code — What It Means

The E322 error on Navien tankless water heaters typically indicates a problem with combustion air pressure sensing. The unit has detected that air pressure conditions are not within safe operating limits, which prevents the burner from firing. This code is related to the air intake and exhaust system that supplies combustion air and removes flue gases. The heater will lock out and refuse to operate until the condition is resolved. The exact threshold and components involved can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual for model-specific details.

Most commonly, this code appears when airflow is restricted due to blockages in the venting system or when the air pressure switch fails to close properly. Because proper combustion air is essential for safe operation, the heater shuts down immediately when it cannot verify adequate airflow.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the air pressure switch when the real problem is a blocked vent or condensate drain line. Always inspect and clear venting and drain lines before replacing any switch.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted vents (~45%)** Ice, snow, debris, or improper vent installation can obstruct airflow to the combustion chamber or prevent exhaust gases from leaving.
- **Clogged condensate drain (~20%)** A plugged drain line or trap can create back pressure that interferes with the air pressure switch operation.
- **Faulty air pressure switch (~15%)** The switch may fail to close or open correctly even when airflow is adequate, sending a false signal to the control board.
- **Disconnected or damaged pressure sensing hose (~10%)** The rubber or vinyl hose connecting the pressure switch to the burner assembly can crack, split, or pop off.
- **Venturi or burner assembly obstruction (~7%)** Dust, lint, or corrosion inside the burner or air intake venturi can reduce airflow below the safe threshold.
- **Wind or back-drafting (~3%)** Strong wind conditions or poor vent termination placement can create negative pressure that affects combustion air sensing.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker or service disconnect to make sure safe work conditions.
2. **Inspect outdoor vent terminals** for blockages such as snow, ice, leaves, bird nests, or other debris. Clear any obstructions and make sure both intake and exhaust pipes are open.
3. **Check the condensate drain line** for clogs. Disconnect the drain and flush with water or use a wet-dry vacuum to clear any blockage. Clean the condensate trap if your model has one.
4. **Remove the front service panel** and locate the air pressure switch, typically a small round or square device with a rubber hose attached.
5. **Inspect the pressure sensing hose** for cracks, splits, or loose connections. Reconnect or replace the hose if damaged. make sure the hose is clear of obstructions.
6. **Test or replace the air pressure switch** if all hoses and vents are clear. Use a multimeter to check for continuity when the switch is activated, or install a new switch if testing indicates failure.
7. **Reassemble the unit**, restore power, and run a test cycle. Monitor for the E322 code and verify hot water flows normally without errors.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances, if the error persists after clearing vents and drains, or if you suspect a problem with the combustion blower or control board. Professionals have the tools to measure combustion air pressure, test switches under operating conditions, and diagnose complex control board faults. Any work involving gas connections, burner disassembly, or electrical diagnostics beyond basic switch replacement is best left to a trained service technician. If the unit is under warranty, contact Navien or an authorized service provider to avoid voiding coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $50-120 in parts, 1-2 hours. A pro service call runs about $150-350.
