## State Water Heater E17 Error Code — What It Means

The E17 error code is not verified in available State Water Heater or A. O. Smith technical documentation. State is part of the A. O. Smith family, and their tankless units typically display codes related to ignition failure, flame loss, or venting problems. Without manufacturer confirmation of E17, you should cross-reference your exact model number and serial number on the rating plate with the manual or contact State support directly. General troubleshooting for recurring error codes on State tankless heaters focuses on air intake blockage, gas supply issues, and vent restrictions, which are the most common root causes across the product line.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screen** Lint, dust, or debris on the combustion air inlet is the most frequent trigger for error codes on State tankless models.
- **Insufficient gas pressure** Low incoming gas pressure or a partially closed supply valve prevents proper ignition and flame sensing.
- **Restricted vent termination** Ice, snow, or bird nests at the exhaust vent can cause pressure-switch faults and repeating codes.
- **Dirty or clogged inlet filter** Sediment in the cold-water inlet filter reduces flow and can trip flow or pressure sensors.
- **Undocumented or transient code** The display may show a code not listed in older manuals if firmware has been updated or a sensor fault is intermittent.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Write down your complete model and serial number** from the rating plate on the side or front of the heater so you can look up the correct manual online.
2. **Power off the unit** by unplugging it or switching the dedicated circuit breaker, then wait two minutes before restoring power to clear transient faults.
3. **Inspect and clean the combustion air intake screen** on the front or side of the unit using a soft brush or vacuum to remove any dust, lint, or blockage.
4. **Check that the gas supply valve** is fully open and that other gas appliances in the home are operating normally to confirm adequate pressure.
5. **Remove and rinse the cold-water inlet filter** by closing the isolation valve, relieving pressure, unscrewing the filter housing, and flushing the screen under running water.
6. **Verify the exhaust vent termination** outside is clear of snow, ice, leaves, or nests and that the vent pipe has no sags or disconnections.
7. **Contact State customer support or a licensed technician** with your model number and the E17 code to confirm whether the code is valid for your platform and what diagnostics are required.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if the code persists after cleaning the air intake and inlet filter, if you smell gas, or if you cannot locate E17 in your printed manual. Because this code is not confirmed in published State documentation, a professional can connect diagnostic software to read live sensor data and verify whether the display is showing a valid fault or a controller issue. Any work involving gas valves, flame rods, or pressure switches should be handled by a qualified technician to maintain warranty coverage and make sure safe operation.
