## Navien E023 Error Code — What It Means

The E023 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates that exhaust temperatures have exceeded safe operating limits. The unit detects abnormally high temperatures in the exhaust system and shuts down to prevent damage to internal components. This is a safety feature that protects the heat exchanger and venting system.

The error typically points to restricted airflow, either through the combustion chamber or the exhaust vent pipe. When exhaust gases cannot leave the unit efficiently, heat builds up and triggers the temperature sensor. Less commonly, the sensor itself may be faulty or the venting configuration may not meet installation requirements.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the exhaust temperature sensor first, but the real issue is usually scale buildup or blocked venting. Inspect the heat exchanger and vent pipes for obstructions before ordering any sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Heat exchanger scale or soot buildup (~50%)** Mineral deposits or combustion residue restrict exhaust gas flow, trapping heat inside the unit.
- **Blocked or improperly installed exhaust vent (~25%)** Obstructions, excessive length, too many elbows, or incorrect pipe slope prevent exhaust from venting properly.
- **Combustion air inlet blockage (~10%)** Restricted intake air starves the burner, causing incomplete combustion and elevated exhaust temperatures.
- **Faulty exhaust temperature sensor (~10%)** The sensor reads incorrectly high or has drifted out of calibration, triggering the code even when temperatures are normal.
- **Burner or gas valve issue (~5%)** Improper gas pressure or a dirty burner creates inefficient combustion and excess heat.

</details>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve.
2. **Inspect the outdoor vent termination** for nests, ice, leaves, or other blockages and remove any obstructions.
3. **Check all intake and exhaust PVC runs** for proper slope, sagging sections, or disconnected joints; correct any issues per installation manual.
4. **Remove the front cover** and visually inspect the heat exchanger fins for heavy scale, corrosion, or soot deposits.
5. **Flush the heat exchanger** using a descaling pump and manufacturer-approved solution, following the service manual procedure, or call a technician if you lack the equipment.
6. **Test the exhaust temperature sensor** with a multimeter for continuity and compare resistance values to the service table in your manual.
7. **Restore power and gas**, run a hot water tap, and monitor the unit; if the E023 returns, contact a qualified Navien service technician for combustion analysis and advanced diagnostics.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you lack a descaling pump, are uncomfortable working with gas appliances, or if the error persists after clearing obvious blockages. Professionals have combustion analyzers to measure flue gas temperatures and oxygen levels, tools essential for diagnosing burner efficiency and gas pressure issues. If the heat exchanger is severely scaled or corroded, replacement requires specialized knowledge and may be covered under warranty when performed by an authorized service provider.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
