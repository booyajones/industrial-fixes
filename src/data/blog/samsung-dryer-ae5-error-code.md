## Samsung Dryer AE5 Error Code — What It Means

Samsung groups AE5 with other AE-family codes (AE, AE3, AE4, AC, Et, EEE, E3, 1 AC) under internal component communication failures. This means the dryer's control boards are not exchanging data correctly. AE5 is not an airflow or venting problem. The electronics cannot talk to each other, usually because the main control board, user interface board, or the wiring harness connecting them has failed. In practice, this points to a control-side fault rather than a mechanical or sensor issue.

## Common Causes

- **Main control board failure** The central board that coordinates all dryer functions has stopped communicating and must be replaced.
- **User interface or display board fault** The panel board cannot send or receive signals to the main control, breaking the internal network.
- **Loose, damaged, or corroded wiring harness or connector** Ribbon cables or connectors between boards have come loose, suffered pin damage, or developed corrosion.
- **Power interruption or unstable supply** A brief outage or voltage dip can scramble electronics and trigger a temporary communication fault.
- **Another internal component blocking communication** Less commonly, a separate module in the control network has failed and stops data exchange.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power-cycle the dryer.** Unplug the dryer for at least one minute, then reconnect and restart to clear any temporary electronics glitch.
2. **Verify incoming power.** Check that the outlet is live, the cord is secure at both ends, and the circuit breaker has not tripped, because power events can cause communication codes.
3. **Note related symptoms.** If the panel flickers, the dryer shuts off unexpectedly, or AE5 returns immediately after restart, suspect a persistent control fault rather than a one-time glitch.
4. **Open the dryer and inspect internal harnesses.** Pull the top or rear panel depending on your model and examine every connector, ribbon cable, and harness route for looseness, bent pins, corrosion, or heat damage.
5. **Reseat all board connectors.** Disconnect and firmly reconnect every plug between the main control board, user interface board, and any intermediate harnesses.
6. **Isolate the failed board.** If the code persists and harnesses are clean and tight, the main control board is the most common culprit and should be tested or substituted.
7. **Replace the defective board or harness.** Install the new main control board, UI board, or board-to-board cable as indicated by your model and retest the dryer in a normal cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if AE5 returns after a full power cycle and harness inspection, if you are not comfortable opening the dryer cabinet and working around live 240 V wiring, or if you cannot identify which board has failed. Samsung's official guidance is to contact service when communication codes persist after restart. Board-level diagnosis often requires a multimeter and knowledge of your model's control architecture, and reversing board connections or installing the wrong part can damage expensive electronics. A qualified service tech will isolate the failed component, confirm the correct replacement part number for your exact model, and install it safely.
