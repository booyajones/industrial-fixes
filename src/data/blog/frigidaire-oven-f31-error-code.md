## Frigidaire Oven F31 Error Code — What It Means

F31 means a shorted probe. The control sees the RTD oven temperature sensor reading far too low, often under 500 ohms, which signals a short in the sensor or its wiring.

A failed sensor is the common cause, but a wire shorted to the metal cabinet will produce the same code. The oven will not regulate temperature correctly until it is fixed.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Shorted RTD sensor** The probe has failed internally and reads well below its normal resistance.
- **Wire grounded to cabinet** A sensor wire touching the metal frame shorts the circuit and trips F31.
- **Pinched sensor wiring** A wire crushed against a sharp edge can short the two conductors together.
- **Corroded or wet connector** Moisture in the sensor plug bridges the pins and lowers the reading.
- **Failed electronic oven control** A bad EOC input can report a shorted probe even with a healthy sensor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for 30 seconds, then restore it to see if F31 clears.
2. Pull the range out and remove the rear panel to reach the sensor harness.
3. Unplug the RTD sensor and measure its resistance with a multimeter at room temperature.
4. A reading under about 500 ohms or far from 1080 ohms means the sensor is shorted.
5. Check each sensor wire for a short to the metal cabinet and for pinched or melted insulation.
6. Replace the RTD temperature sensor if it reads shorted or out of spec.
7. Replace the electronic oven control if the sensor and wiring both test good.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you cannot find the short and the code stays after a sensor swap. Tracing a grounded wire inside the cabinet can be slow and a pro has the tools to isolate it fast.
