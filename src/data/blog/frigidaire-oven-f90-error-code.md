## Frigidaire Oven F90 Error Code — What It Means

F90 means the maximum oven door unlock time was exceeded. The control told the door latch to unlock but the mechanism did not finish within the allowed time, usually after a self-clean cycle.

The most common cause is a failing door latch motor or a binding lock cam. A latch switch that does not change state on cue can also stall the unlock and trip the code.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Failed door latch motor** The lock motor will not drive the cam to the unlock position in time.
- **Binding lock cam or rod** A jammed cam, rod, or spring stops the latch from completing its travel.
- **Faulty latch switches** A lock switch that does not open or close on cue leaves the control waiting.
- **Damaged wiring to the latch** A broken wire between the control and the latch motor stalls the unlock.
- **Failed electronic oven control** The EOC can mismanage the lock cycle and report a timeout even with a good latch.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Cut power at the breaker for 30 seconds, then restore it. Sometimes the latch finishes its cycle on restart.
2. Open the oven door if it will open and look at the lock mechanism along the top front of the cavity.
3. Pull the range out and remove the rear or top access panel to reach the latch assembly.
4. Watch the latch motor cycle and feel for binding in the cam, rod, and spring.
5. Check the latch switches and wiring for continuity as the lock moves.
6. Replace the motor door latch assembly if the motor stalls or the switches fail.
7. Replace the electronic oven control if the latch and switches all test good.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the door stays locked and you cannot reach the latch, or if the latch tests good and the code stays. A pro can force the lock open safely and confirm the control.
