## Frigidaire Washer E10 Error Code — What It Means

E10 on a Frigidaire washer indicates a water fill or water supply problem. The control board is telling you the machine did not detect enough water entering the tub within the expected time. This fault triggers when the inlet path is restricted, the water supply is inadequate, or the inlet valve is not operating correctly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Closed or partially closed water supply valve** The shut-off valve at the wall is not fully open or has been accidentally turned down, restricting flow into the washer.
- **Clogged inlet screen or filter** Sediment, scale, or debris has collected in the screen at the water valve or hose connection, blocking water flow.
- **Faulty water inlet valve** The solenoid valve does not open fully or at all, preventing the washer from filling even when supply pressure is good.
- **Kinked or restricted fill hose** The inlet hose is bent, crushed, or internally collapsed, reducing water flow to the valve.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** for at least three minutes to reset the control board, then plug it back in and try a fill cycle to see if the error clears.
2. **Check both water supply valves** at the wall and turn them fully counterclockwise to the open position, then inspect the fill hoses for kinks or damage.
3. **Remove the inlet hoses** from the back of the washer and examine the inlet screens inside the water valve ports for sediment or mineral buildup.
4. **Clean the screens** with a soft brush under running water, rinse thoroughly, and reinstall the hoses with new rubber washers if the old ones are cracked.
5. **Run a fill test** by starting a normal wash cycle and watching the water enter the tub to confirm the flow rate is strong and the error does not reappear.
6. **Test the water inlet valve** for electrical continuity with a multimeter if the error persists after cleaning, looking for resistance within normal range (consult your model's service sheet for the exact specification).
7. **Replace the inlet valve** if it fails the resistance test, does not click when energized, or allows only weak flow even with clean screens and full supply pressure.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the error returns after you have verified full water supply pressure, cleaned all inlet screens, and confirmed the hoses are not restricted. A technician can test the control board's fill circuit, check for intermittent wiring faults, and perform voltage and pressure diagnostics that require specialized meters and service documentation. If you are not comfortable working with a multimeter or removing the inlet valve, a qualified appliance repair technician can complete the diagnosis and replacement safely.
