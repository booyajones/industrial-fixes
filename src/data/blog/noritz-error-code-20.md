# Noritz Error Code 20 — Low Water Flow

**Error Code 20** on Noritz tankless water heaters (NR, NC, NRC series) means the unit has detected insufficient water flow to safely operate. Noritz units require a minimum flow rate to activate the burner — without adequate flow, the heat exchanger can overheat. Error 20 prevents this.

## Minimum Flow Requirements

- **Noritz NR98-OD / NR83-OD:** Minimum flow = 0.26 GPM
- **Noritz NRCP199-OD (condensing):** Minimum flow = 0.26 GPM
- Below this flow rate, Error 20 is triggered and the burner will not ignite

## Common Causes {#most-likely-cause}

| Cause | Likelihood |
|---|---|
| Clogged inlet filter screen | Very High |
| Partially closed cold water shutoff valve | High |
| Low water pressure at the unit | High |
| Scale or debris blocking the flow sensor | Medium |
| Failed flow sensor | Medium |
| Scale in hot water piping or fixtures | Low |

## Step-by-Step Diagnosis {#diagnosis}

**Step 1 — Clean the inlet filter screen**
This is the most common fix:
1. Shut off cold water supply to the unit
2. Disconnect the cold water inlet connection
3. Remove the stainless mesh filter screen
4. Rinse under running water and clean with a soft brush
5. Reinstall and test

**Step 2 — Check cold water supply pressure**
- Supply pressure should be 15–150 psi at the unit
- Use a pressure gauge on the cold inlet service port if available
- Check for partially closed shutoff valves at the unit, water meter, or pressure regulator

**Step 3 — Inspect the flow sensor**
- The flow sensor (turbine-type) is inside the unit on the cold water inlet piping
- Remove and inspect for scale deposits, sediment, or debris in the turbine chamber
- Spin the turbine wheel — it should rotate freely
- Flush with water — do not use tools in the turbine housing

**Step 4 — Test the flow sensor**
- Reassemble and measure flow with a bucket and stopwatch at the hot water outlet
- If flow is adequate (>0.26 GPM) but Error 20 persists: flow sensor is failed — replace

**Step 5 — Check entire hot water distribution**
- In heavily scaled systems, scale inside pipes reduces fixture flow below the minimum
- Disconnect the hot outlet and test flow directly from the unit
- If unit flow is fine but fixtures are slow: problem is downstream scale

## Reset Procedure

Noritz units reset automatically when flow is restored:
1. Fix the root cause (clean filter, open valve)
2. Open a hot water fixture
3. Error 20 should clear as soon as flow exceeds minimum
4. If Error 20 doesn't clear with adequate flow: flow sensor is failed
