## What this code means
Noritz error code 10 means abnormal combustion — the unit's flame sensor detected an irregular or unstable flame pattern that doesn't match expected combustion parameters. Unlike a simple ignition failure code, code 10 means the burner lit but the quality of combustion was poor enough that the control board flagged it as abnormal and shut the unit down. This can be caused by gas supply issues, a dirty burner, incorrect gas type configuration, or inadequate combustion air. It often appears intermittently before becoming a persistent fault.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or clogged burner** — Mineral deposits, dust, or scale on the burner ports distort the flame pattern. A distorted flame doesn't produce the clean ionization signal the sensor expects.
- **Gas supply fluctuation** — If line pressure varies significantly during operation (undersized gas line, multiple appliances competing for gas), the flame quality oscillates and triggers code 10.
- **Wrong gas type setting** — Noritz units must be configured for either natural gas or LP. A unit set to LP while running on natural gas (or vice versa) produces abnormal combustion.
- **Blocked combustion air intake** — Insufficient fresh air to the combustion chamber causes incomplete combustion with an abnormal flame pattern.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset the unit** — Press the On/Off or reset button on the unit. Observe the ignition sequence. If code 10 appears after only a few seconds of burning, the burner or gas supply is the likely issue.
2. **Inspect the burner** — Remove the front cover and burner access panel. Look at the burner ports for debris, scale, or discoloration indicating hot spots. Clean with a soft brush — do not use wire brushes that could enlarge ports.
3. **Verify gas type setting** — On Noritz units, the gas type (NG vs. LP) is set via a DIP switch on the control board or a dedicated gas conversion kit. Verify the setting matches your actual gas supply.
4. **Check combustion air** — Confirm the unit's installation has adequate combustion air per Noritz specifications. In small closets or tight spaces, add a combustion air duct.
5. **Reset the system** — After addressing the root cause, power cycle and run a 2-gallon hot water demand. Monitor for recurrence.

## When to Call a Pro

Gas pressure measurement and burner combustion analysis require a licensed tech with a manometer and combustion analyzer. Persistent code 10 after cleaning the burner is a combustion efficiency issue that needs professional diagnosis.
