## Amana Dryer No Power — What's Happening

When your Amana dryer shows no power, it means the unit is not receiving usable electrical power or cannot complete a start condition. This is a symptom, not a fault code. If the display shows PF, Amana says that means a power failure interrupted the drying cycle. If it shows L2, that means low or no line voltage from your home supply, often preventing the heater from turning on. If the dryer is completely dead with no display at all, Amana's troubleshooting guidance treats that as a power-supply or startup problem at the source, not an internal control fault.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Tripped breaker or blown fuse** Amana states electric dryers use two household fuses or breakers, and both must be intact for proper operation.
- **Control Lock enabled** Amana says the Controls Locked light can prevent operation on some models, and it must be cleared before the dryer will start.
- **Improper power cord or terminal block connection** Amana specifically instructs checking that the power cord is properly installed at the dryer connection point.
- **Loose or failed incoming power supply (L2 code)** Amana's L2 guidance points to low or no line voltage from the home supply, which requires checking the electrical panel and outlet.
- **Power interruption event (PF code)** For PF, the cycle was interrupted by a power failure and the display needs to be cleared before restarting.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Confirm whether the unit is dead with no display, shows PF, or shows L2 on the control panel.
2. Check the double-pole breaker at your electrical panel and verify both legs of supply are present and fully reset.
3. Check if the Controls Locked status is on and clear it by holding Cycle Signal for 3 seconds on most models.
4. Inspect the power cord connection at the terminal block on the back of the dryer and confirm it is properly installed per the installation instructions.
5. If the display showed PF, press and hold START to restart the cycle or press POWER to clear the display, then test operation.
6. If the display showed L2, restart the dryer using a Timed Dry heated cycle after restoring power at the panel.
7. Test the dryer with a full heated cycle to confirm it completes without interruption.
8. If L2 persists after supply checks, Amana says to consult a qualified electrician for the home wiring.

## When to Call a Pro

If both breakers are intact and fully reset, the power cord is properly installed, Control Lock is cleared, and the dryer still shows no power or displays L2 after these checks, Amana recommends consulting a qualified electrician to inspect your home wiring and outlet. If the dryer powers on but fails to complete cycles or shows other fault codes, contact an appliance technician to diagnose internal control or component failures.
