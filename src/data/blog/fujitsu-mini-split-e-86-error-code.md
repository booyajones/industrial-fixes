## Fujitsu E:86 Error Code — What It Means

Error code E:86 on a Fujitsu mini-split indicates an outdoor unit pressure sensor error. The outdoor control board is detecting an abnormal signal from the pressure sensor circuit. This can mean the sensor itself has failed, the wiring or connector is loose or damaged, the outdoor PCB is not reading the sensor correctly, or the refrigerant system pressure is outside the expected range and triggering the fault. The unit will typically not run or will shut down to protect the compressor until the fault is cleared.

## Common Causes

- **Failed outdoor pressure sensor** The pressure sensor in the outdoor unit has failed internally and no longer sends a valid signal to the control board.
- **Loose, open, or damaged sensor wiring or connector** The sensor harness connector is loose, corroded, or the wiring has an open circuit or damage that interrupts the sensor signal.
- **Outdoor PCB (main control board) failure** The outdoor unit control board has failed in the sensor input circuit and misreads or cannot interpret the pressure sensor signal even when the sensor is good.
- **Incorrect refrigerant charge or abnormal system pressure** The refrigerant charge is too low or too high, or a system fault causes pressures that fall outside the sensor's expected range and trigger the error.
- **Connector pin corrosion or poor contact** Moisture or environmental exposure has corroded the sensor connector pins, causing intermittent or no contact between the sensor and the board.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system completely** at the breaker or disconnect, wait 60 seconds, then restore power and observe if the E:86 code returns after the unit restarts.
2. **Inspect the outdoor unit sensor connectors and wiring harness** for loose plugs, visible damage, corrosion, or breaks in the wire insulation, especially at the pressure sensor location on the outdoor coil or service valve.
3. **Test the pressure sensor circuit** using a multimeter to check continuity and connector integrity, and verify the sensor connector is fully seated and pins are clean and making good contact.
4. **Check system operating pressures** with refrigerant gauges while the unit attempts to run (if safe to do so), and verify refrigerant charge is correct per the unit nameplate and service manual specifications.
5. **Inspect and test the outdoor PCB** for visible damage, burn marks, or failed components near the sensor input terminals, and verify incoming power voltage is within the acceptable range for the unit.
6. **Replace the identified failed component** after testing confirms the fault source: pressure sensor if the circuit is open or readings are out of range, wiring harness if connectors or wires are damaged, or outdoor PCB if sensor and wiring test normal but the fault persists.
7. **Clear the fault code** per the service manual procedure (usually by power cycling or using the service remote), then run the unit through a full cooling and heating cycle to verify the repair and confirm the E:86 does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician for E:86 faults. This error requires refrigerant system diagnostics, pressure testing, sensor circuit troubleshooting, and potentially brazing or refrigerant recovery work that must be performed by an EPA-certified technician. Misdiagnosis can lead to compressor damage or refrigerant loss. A qualified tech will have the gauges, meter, and Fujitsu service documentation to isolate the sensor, wiring, or board fault safely and make the correct repair while maintaining system integrity and warranty coverage.
