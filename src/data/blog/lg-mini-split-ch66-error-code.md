## LG Mini Split CH66 Error Code — What It Means

CH66 does not appear in LG's published fault code tables for standard mini split systems. LG uses different code sets across product families, so CH66 may be specific to certain chassis or Multi V equipment. Without the exact model number and service manual, the code cannot be accurately defined. Based on LG's general inverter troubleshooting protocols, a CH-prefix code typically points to outdoor unit faults related to the inverter, DC power stage, compressor circuit, or main PCB. Do not attempt repairs until you confirm the exact meaning in your model's service documentation.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Model-specific code not in public tables** CH66 does not match standard LG residential mini split codes, so it may apply only to your chassis or commercial equipment.
- **Inverter or DC-link power stage fault** LG inverter troubleshooting focuses on DC voltage regulation, reactor circuits, and power module defects when outdoor faults occur.
- **Outdoor main PCB or inverter PCB failure** Control board defects, damaged connectors, or carbonized traces can trigger unlisted fault codes.
- **Compressor winding or insulation breakdown** LG protocols test compressor resistance and insulation above 10 MΩ to rule out motor faults versus inverter defects.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness** Outdoor unit harness connections to the inverter, compressor, or EEV can cause intermittent power faults.
- **Incorrect power supply or breaker issue** LG troubleshooting starts with verifying incoming voltage and breaker integrity before diagnosing inverter circuits.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Record the exact model and serial number from the outdoor unit nameplate and locate the service manual or fault code table for your specific chassis before proceeding.
2. Check incoming power at the outdoor unit disconnect and verify the breaker is sized correctly and not tripped or weak.
3. Inspect all wiring harnesses between indoor and outdoor units and at the outdoor PCB for loose, corroded, or burned connectors.
4. Consult your model's service manual to confirm whether CH66 points to inverter, compressor, or power-stage diagnostics.
5. If the manual directs you to inverter checks, measure DC-link voltage at the test points specified (LG inverter troubleshooting references approximately 310 V DC for single-phase and 540 V DC for three-phase in related faults).
6. Test compressor winding resistance and insulation resistance only if the service manual calls for it (LG guidance requires insulation above 10 MΩ to rule out compressor defects).
7. Contact LG technical support or a certified technician with your model number and code to confirm the fault definition and parts list before ordering components.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a certified LG technician immediately if CH66 appears and you cannot locate the code in your service manual. Inverter and DC power-stage diagnostics require specialized meters, knowledge of high-voltage DC circuits, and access to model-specific fault trees. Misdiagnosis can lead to expensive part swaps or safety hazards. A qualified tech can retrieve the exact code definition from LG's internal database, perform the correct voltage and resistance tests, and order the right board or compressor for your chassis.
