## Navien E255 Error Code — What It Means

The E255 error code on a Navien tankless water heater signals a communication fault between the main control board and another internal component or sensor. This typically means the control board is unable to send or receive signals properly, often due to loose wiring, damaged connectors, or a failed circuit board. The exact component involved can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or the unit's wiring diagram for specifics.

Because Navien tankless units rely on precise communication between sensors, relays, and the control board to modulate gas and water flow, any interruption in that signal chain will trigger a fault code and shut down the heater. The E255 code is generally a catch-all for internal communication errors, so diagnosis requires methodical inspection of connectors and boards rather than a single obvious part swap.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board before checking that every wiring harness and connector is seated and free of corrosion. Inspect and reseat all connectors first, as a loose plug is far cheaper than a new board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring harness connection (~40%)** Vibration, humidity, or improper installation can cause connectors to back out or corrode, breaking the signal path between boards.
- **Failed main control board (~30%)** Power surges, component failure, or manufacturing defects can damage the microcontroller or communication circuits on the main PCB.
- **Failed component board or sensor module (~20%)** The downstream board or sensor that the control board is trying to communicate with may have failed, preventing proper signal acknowledgment.
- **Water intrusion or condensation in the control panel (~10%)** Moisture can short out traces, corrode solder joints, or create intermittent faults in the communication circuit.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** of the unit to access the control panel and internal wiring.
3. **Inspect all wiring harness connectors** on the main control board and any component boards, looking for looseness, bent pins, or corrosion.
4. **Reseat every connector** by unplugging and firmly reseating each plug, ensuring a snug fit.
5. **Check for moisture or water stains** inside the control panel enclosure; if present, dry the area and investigate the source of water intrusion.
6. **Restore power and gas**, then monitor the display to see if the E255 error returns.
7. **If the error persists**, use a multimeter to check voltage at the connectors and consult the wiring diagram to identify the affected communication circuit, or call a qualified technician to test and replace the control board or component board as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components, if the error persists after reseating all connectors and power-cycling the unit, or if you lack a multimeter and wiring diagram to trace the communication fault. Tankless water heaters combine gas, water, and complex electronics, so misdiagnosis can lead to expensive parts purchases or unsafe conditions. A trained technician can test voltage, read diagnostic fault logs, and pinpoint whether the main control board, a component board, or a sensor is at fault.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
