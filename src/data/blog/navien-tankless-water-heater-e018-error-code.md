## Navien Tankless Water Heater E018 Error — What It Means

The E018 error code on Navien tankless water heaters indicates the exhaust temperature has exceeded safe limits. The unit detects excessive heat in the flue or exhaust system and shuts down to prevent damage. This condition typically results from restricted airflow, a dirty heat exchanger, or combustion problems that cause incomplete burning and higher exhaust temperatures.

The error appears when the control board receives a signal from the exhaust thermistor that the temperature has crossed the upper threshold. The unit will lock out until the condition is corrected and the system is reset. This code protects the internal components and venting system from thermal damage.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the exhaust thermistor or fan first, but testing the thermistor resistance and checking for blocked vents or a scaled heat exchanger will reveal the real cause in most cases.

## Common Causes

- **Scaled or dirty heat exchanger (~50%)** Mineral deposits and sediment coat the heat exchanger fins, restricting heat transfer and causing exhaust temperatures to climb.
- **Blocked or restricted exhaust vent (~20%)** Debris, ice, or improper vent termination outside prevents hot gases from escaping, raising exhaust temperature.
- **Defective exhaust fan or fan speed too low (~15%)** A failing fan or incorrect speed setting reduces airflow through the combustion chamber, trapping heat.
- **Failed exhaust thermistor (~10%)** The sensor that monitors exhaust temperature sends a false high-temperature signal to the control board.
- **Improper gas pressure or air-fuel mixture (~5%)** Too much gas or too little combustion air leads to incomplete burning and hotter exhaust gases.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the gas shutoff valve.
2. **Inspect the exhaust vent** outside for blockages, snow, ice, bird nests, or any obstruction. Remove any debris found.
3. **Check the intake air vent** as well to confirm airflow into the unit is not restricted.
4. **Reset the unit** by restoring power and opening the gas valve. Observe whether the error clears and the unit fires normally.
5. **If the error returns**, call a qualified technician to descale the heat exchanger, test the exhaust thermistor, and measure combustion airflow and gas pressure.
6. **During the service visit**, have the technician flush the heat exchanger with a descaling solution to remove mineral buildup.
7. **After cleaning**, the technician should verify exhaust fan operation, check thermistor resistance, and adjust the air-fuel mixture if needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if the E018 error returns after clearing vents and resetting the unit. Descaling the heat exchanger requires a flushing pump, descaling solution, and knowledge of the isolation valves and internal plumbing. Gas-fired appliances also demand proper testing of combustion, gas pressure, and airflow to prevent unsafe operation. A technician will measure exhaust temperature, verify thermistor readings, inspect the fan, and adjust the gas valve as needed. Attempting to disassemble the combustion chamber or adjust gas settings without training can create carbon monoxide hazards and void your warranty.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
