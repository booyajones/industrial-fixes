## State Water Heater E92 Error Code — What It Means

The E92 code on State (A. O. Smith platform) tankless water heaters indicates an ignition failure. The control board attempted to light the burner but could not establish or verify flame. This family of codes points to one of three system failures: restricted combustion air supply, inadequate gas flow or pressure, or dirty or failed flame-sensing hardware. The unit will lock out and require a reset before attempting another ignition cycle.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screens** Lint, dust, or debris clogging the combustion air intake prevents the unit from drawing enough fresh air to complete ignition.
- **Low gas pressure or closed shutoff** Insufficient gas supply pressure or a partially closed valve starves the burner and prevents flame establishment.
- **Dirty or contaminated flame sensor** Carbon buildup or oxidation on the flame rod prevents the control from detecting flame even when the burner lights.
- **Restricted vent termination** Blockage at the exhaust vent outlet, including bird nests or ice, prevents proper exhaust flow and triggers a safety lockout.
- **Clogged inlet water filter** Low water flow from a dirty filter can interrupt the ignition sequence on tankless heaters that require minimum flow to fire.
- **Failed igniter or gas valve** When cleaning and supply checks pass, the igniter assembly or gas valve itself may have failed and require replacement.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Reset the unit** at the control panel and observe whether the code returns immediately or only after a hot-water demand cycle.
2. **Inspect and clean the combustion air intake screens.** Remove any lint, dust, or debris blocking the intake grilles or filter.
3. **Check the gas supply.** Confirm the manual shutoff valve is fully open and that other gas appliances in the home are operating normally to rule out supply pressure issues.
4. **Inspect the vent termination** outdoors for blockages, nests, ice, or restriction and clear any obstruction.
5. **Remove and inspect the flame sensor.** If the rod shows carbon buildup or oxidation, clean it gently with fine abrasive cloth or replace it per manufacturer guidance.
6. **Check the inlet water filter** at the cold-water connection and clean or replace if clogged.
7. **Run a test ignition cycle** and watch for proper pre-purge, igniter glow, gas valve opening, and flame establishment. If the code returns and all supply and airflow checks pass, replace the igniter assembly or gas valve as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you are uncomfortable working with gas connections, if you lack a manometer to verify gas supply pressure, or if the code persists after you have cleaned the air intake, vent, flame sensor, and inlet filter. Igniter and gas valve replacement require disassembly of sealed combustion components and safe handling of gas fittings. A technician can measure flame signal current, verify gas pressure against the rating plate, and confirm that the control board is commanding the ignition sequence correctly.
