## Mitsubishi E5 Error Code — What It Means

For Mitsubishi Heavy Industries heat pumps, the E5 error code indicates an outdoor unit microcomputer failure or a communication error between the indoor and outdoor units. This code appears when the outdoor control board cannot process signals correctly or when the two units cannot talk to each other over the interconnect wiring. The fault is electronic rather than refrigerant-side, meaning the problem lies in the control circuit, power supply, or communication wiring rather than sensors or pressures.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Outdoor control PCB or microcomputer failure** The outdoor unit's main control board or microcomputer has failed and cannot process or send signals.
- **Communication wiring faults** Indoor-to-outdoor communication wires are loose, reversed, broken, or have poor terminal connections.
- **Power supply issues to the outdoor unit** The outdoor unit is not receiving correct supply voltage at the incoming terminals, causing the microcomputer to fail.
- **Blown outdoor unit fuse** A failed fuse in the outdoor unit protection circuit mimics or triggers communication and control faults.
- **Damaged or open conductors** Physical damage to the interconnect cable between indoor and outdoor units has interrupted the control signal path.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the system** at the breaker and lock out the disconnect to work safely on both indoor and outdoor units.
2. **Verify supply voltage** at the outdoor unit incoming terminals to confirm the unit is receiving the correct power from the main supply.
3. **Inspect the communication wiring** between indoor and outdoor units for loose, reversed, broken, or damaged conductors and confirm all terminals match the wiring diagram exactly.
4. **Check the outdoor unit fuse** and any other protection devices on the outdoor control board for open or blown components.
5. **Examine the outdoor control board** for visible damage, burned components, scorch marks, or signs of microcomputer failure such as no LED activity or erratic behavior.
6. **Reset the system** by restoring power and observe whether the E5 code returns immediately or after a few minutes of attempted communication.
7. **Replace the outdoor control PCB** if wiring and supply voltage are correct and the code persists, because the fault definition points to the outdoor microcomputer or board as the primary suspect.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with live electrical circuits or if the fault persists after wiring and terminal checks. Because E5 points to control-board failure or communication faults, diagnosis requires multimeter skills, wiring-diagram interpretation, and safe handling of both line voltage and low-voltage control circuits. A technician will verify supply power, trace the communication path, and replace the outdoor PCB or damaged wiring as needed. If your unit is still under warranty, contact an authorized Mitsubishi service center before opening the outdoor unit to preserve coverage.
