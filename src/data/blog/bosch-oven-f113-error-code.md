## Bosch Oven F113 Error Code — What It Means

F113 on a Bosch oven indicates an overheating fault during the self-cleaning cycle. The control board detected that cavity temperature exceeded safe limits and shut down the cycle. This code is not listed in Bosch's published owner-support documentation, but independent repair sources tie it specifically to self-clean thermal runaway. The fault usually means the oven lost feedback from the temperature sensor, the sensor failed, or a heating element stayed energized when it should have shut off. Left unchecked, continued overheating can damage wiring, connectors, and the control board itself.

## Common Causes

- **Failed or open temperature sensor** The oven probe or sensor lost signal or drifted out of range, so the control could not monitor cavity temperature during the high-heat self-clean cycle.
- **Stuck or welded heating element relay** The bake or broil element remained energized because the relay on the control board fused closed, driving runaway heat.
- **Damaged sensor wiring or connectors** Burned, melted, or loose harness connections interrupted the temperature feedback loop, causing the control to lose visibility.
- **Blocked cooling vent or inadequate airflow** Obstructed vents or a failed cooling fan prevented heat from escaping during self-clean, pushing cavity temperature beyond limits.
- **Control board logic or software fault** Corrupted firmware or a failed output stage on the electronic control prevented proper regulation of the self-clean cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Cut power at the breaker** for at least three minutes, then restore power and check whether the F113 code clears on its own.
2. **Record the full model and serial number** from the oven rating plate so you can verify code definitions and service literature specific to your platform.
3. **Inspect the cavity and wiring** for evidence of overheating, including discolored terminals, melted harness insulation, or burn marks around the temperature sensor.
4. **Test the temperature sensor resistance** using a multimeter at room temperature and compare the reading to your model's specification table, if the oven is cool and safe to access.
5. **Check whether the bake or broil element is energized** when the oven is off and cold, indicating a stuck relay or shorted control output.
6. **Examine cooling vents and the rear cavity fan** for blockages, dust buildup, or failed rotation that could have contributed to thermal runaway during self-clean.
7. **Replace the confirmed faulty component**, whether sensor, harness, relay board section, or electronic control, and run a brief test bake cycle before attempting another self-clean.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the code returns after a power reset, if you find evidence of severe overheating or charred wiring, or if multimeter tests fall outside normal ranges and you are not comfortable working with line voltage. Because Bosch does not publish F113 in its owner documentation, diagnosis may require model-specific service literature and board-level testing that a professional can access. If the oven recently completed or attempted a self-clean cycle and now will not operate at all, professional service is the safest route to prevent further damage or unsafe operation.
