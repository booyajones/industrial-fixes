## Samsung Washer Won't Turn On — What's Happening

A Samsung washer that won't turn on is a power or start failure symptom, not a fault code. Samsung attributes this problem primarily to the power cord or the electrical connection to your home supply. The washer is either not receiving power at all or is not processing it correctly due to an interlock, setting, or internal component failure.

This symptom covers cases where the machine stays completely dark, turns off by itself, stops unexpectedly, or won't respond when you press the power button. The root cause is almost always in the power path, the door latch system, or the control electronics.

## Most Likely Causes

- **No power at the outlet** A tripped breaker, blown fuse, or unplugged cord is the most common reason the washer receives no power at all.
- **Door not fully closed or defective door lock** The washer will not start if the door or lid is not latched, the strike is misaligned, or the lock assembly has failed.
- **Loose or damaged power cord** A frayed, burnt, or loose connection at the plug or cabinet entry interrupts power delivery.
- **Child lock or delay start active** Many Samsung models will not respond to button presses if child lock is engaged or a delayed cycle is running.
- **Failed control board** Burnt components, insect contamination, or shorted traces on the main PCB prevent the washer from powering on or processing commands.
- **Defective start switch** A worn or broken start button assembly can fail to send the signal to the control board.
- **Damaged internal wiring or connectors** Pinched, chewed, or corroded harness wires between the board and power supply or door lock interrupt the circuit.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify power at the outlet by plugging in a lamp or other known-good device, or measure outlet voltage with a multimeter.
2. Check the breaker or GFCI feeding the laundry circuit and reset it if tripped, or replace the fuse if blown.
3. Inspect the entire power cord from plug to cabinet entry for cuts, burn marks, or loose connections at either end.
4. Confirm the door or lid closes completely and latches with an audible click, then check the strike and latch for damage or debris.
5. Cancel child lock by holding the appropriate button combination per your model's manual, and press Cancel or Pause to clear any delay start.
6. Unplug the washer for 60 seconds to reset the control board, then plug it back in and attempt to power on.
7. Remove the console or top panel and inspect the control board for visibly burnt spots, broken solder joints, or insect nests, and check all harness connectors for tightness.
8. Test the door lock, start switch, and control board supply circuits with a multimeter according to the wiring diagram if all visible checks pass and the washer still will not start.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you are not comfortable working with live 120 V or 240 V circuits, if the outlet or breaker panel requires repair, or if internal diagnosis requires removing the control panel and testing live circuits with a multimeter. Also call if the control board shows visible damage but you are unsure which replacement part matches your model, or if you have replaced the door lock and power cord but the washer still will not start.
