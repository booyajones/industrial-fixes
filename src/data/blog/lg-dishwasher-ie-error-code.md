## What this code means
The IE error on an LG dishwasher means the unit detected insufficient water fill. The tub did not reach the expected water level within the allowed fill time, typically between 30 seconds and 10 minutes depending on your model. LG describes this as the dishwasher not receiving adequate water or the correct water level not being reached within the expected time frame.

When the control board sees too little water after the fill cycle, it stops the wash and displays IE to prevent the pump and spray arms from running dry. The error points to a problem somewhere in the water supply path, from the house shutoff valve all the way to the inlet valve inside the dishwasher.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the water inlet valve first, but more than half of IE codes are caused by a closed shutoff valve, kinked hose, or clogged inlet screen. Check the supply and clean the screen before buying a new valve.

## Common Causes

- **Water supply valve closed or partially open (~35%)** The shutoff valve under the sink or behind the dishwasher is not turned all the way open, restricting flow.
- **Kinked or pinched inlet hose (~25%)** The water supply hose is bent, crimped, frozen, or torn, blocking or slowing the flow into the dishwasher.
- **Clogged inlet screen or filter (~20%)** Sediment or debris has accumulated in the small mesh screen at the water inlet connection, restricting flow.
- **Faulty water inlet valve (~15%)** The solenoid valve that controls water entry is stuck, partially blocked, or electrically failed and will not open properly.
- **Low household water pressure (~5%)** House water pressure is below 20 PSI, preventing the dishwasher from filling quickly enough to satisfy the timer.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dishwasher** or switch off the dedicated circuit breaker to de-energize the appliance before any service work.
2. **Verify the water supply valve** under the sink or behind the dishwasher is turned fully open, then confirm water is available by running a nearby faucet.
3. **Inspect the inlet hose** from the shutoff to the dishwasher for kinks, pinches, sharp bends, tears, or frozen sections, and straighten or replace the hose if damaged.
4. **Remove the inlet connection** at the back of the dishwasher and pull out the small mesh inlet screen, then rinse it under running water and scrub away any sediment or debris with a soft brush.
5. **Check household water pressure** if you suspect low flow. LG requires above 20 PSI for proper operation. If pressure is low, correct the supply problem before replacing parts.
6. **Test the water inlet valve** by reconnecting the clean screen and hose, restoring power, and starting a wash cycle. If the dishwasher still fills slowly or not at all, replace the inlet valve.
7. **Reassemble and run a test cycle** to confirm the dishwasher fills to the correct level and the IE error clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you have checked the supply valve, cleaned the inlet screen, inspected the hose, and replaced the water inlet valve but the IE code persists. At that point the problem may involve wiring, the control board, or an internal pressure sensor that requires diagnostic tools and schematic knowledge. Also call for service if your household water pressure is consistently below 20 PSI and you need a plumber to install a booster pump or correct supply-line restrictions. If you are uncomfortable working under the sink, disconnecting water lines, or accessing the back of a built-in dishwasher, a technician can perform the entire diagnostic and repair sequence safely in a single visit.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-80 in parts, 30-90 min. A pro service call runs about $120-250.
