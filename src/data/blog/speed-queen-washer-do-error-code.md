## Speed Queen Washer DO Error Code — What It Means

The DO error code on a Speed Queen washer indicates that the control has detected an open door or lid condition during an active cycle, or that the latch and lock status was not confirmed. The machine will stop operation because it sees an unsafe condition. This fault can appear if the door is actually open, if something is preventing it from closing fully, if the latch mechanism is obstructed or misaligned, or if the door lock assembly or switch has failed.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully closed or obstructed** Clothing caught in the door opening or a load pressing against the door can prevent the latch from engaging fully.
- **Lint or detergent buildup at the latch** Residue, lint, or debris in the strike area or around the latch can block the lock mechanism from seating properly.
- **Misaligned door strike or keeper** If the door strike or keeper has shifted, the latch may not click into the locked position even when the door is pushed closed.
- **Failed door lock or latch assembly** A worn or broken door lock assembly will not send the proper closed-and-locked signal to the control board.
- **Faulty door switch or sensor** A defective lid switch or door sensor can incorrectly report an open condition even when the door is securely latched.
- **Wiring or control board fault** Damaged wiring, loose connectors, or a control board input failure can cause the machine to lose door-status feedback.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Stop the cycle** and open the door or lid, then remove any clothing or objects caught in the opening.
2. **Firmly re-close the door** and listen for a distinct click to confirm the latch has engaged fully.
3. **Inspect the latch and strike area** for lint, detergent residue, or visible debris, and clean any buildup with a damp cloth.
4. **Check door alignment** by examining the gap around the closed door and verifying that the strike plate or keeper sits squarely in line with the latch.
5. **Power reset the washer** by unplugging it for at least two minutes, then plug it back in and restart the cycle.
6. **Test the door lock assembly and switch circuit** if the error returns, checking connector fit and wiring continuity from the lock to the control board.
7. **Evaluate the main control board** for failed door-status input processing if all mechanical and wiring checks pass, and consider professional diagnostics or board replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have re-closed the door, cleaned the latch area, confirmed alignment, and completed a power reset but the DO code still appears on the next cycle. A professional can safely test the door lock circuit with a multimeter, verify control-board door-status inputs, and replace the lock assembly, switch, or wiring harness as needed. Also call if you are uncomfortable working with electrical connections or if your washer is still under warranty, since DIY repairs may void coverage.
