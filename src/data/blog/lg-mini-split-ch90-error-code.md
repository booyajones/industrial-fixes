## LG Mini Split CH90 Error Code — What It Means

CH90 is reported as an outdoor fan problem or outdoor fan motor fault on LG mini-split systems. The exact definition varies by model family, so always verify against your unit's service manual. In general, the code means the outdoor unit's PCB has detected that the fan motor is not running correctly or not responding to drive signals. This can be caused by a mechanical jam, electrical disconnection, motor failure, or a faulty outdoor control board.

## Common Causes

- **Seized or jammed outdoor fan** Debris, broken blades, or bearing failure can prevent the fan from spinning freely.
- **Disconnected or loose motor wiring** The fan motor connector may be unplugged or partially unseated at the outdoor unit.
- **Failed outdoor fan motor** Internal motor windings or bearings can fail, preventing the motor from starting even when power is present.
- **Faulty outdoor PCB or inverter board** If the motor is good but the board does not supply the correct drive voltage, the PCB is at fault.
- **Rotation-sensing or feedback circuit fault** Some models use rotation feedback signals, and a break in this circuit can trigger the error even if the fan spins.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the system** at the breaker and confirm voltage is zero before opening panels or touching components.
2. **Remove the outdoor unit's top grille** and visually inspect the fan for broken blades, debris, or any visible mechanical obstruction.
3. **Rotate the fan blade by hand** to check for binding or seized bearings. It should spin freely with no grinding or hard stops.
4. **Locate the fan motor connector** on the outdoor PCB and verify it is fully seated. Check the wiring harness for cuts, burns, or pinched insulation.
5. **Measure the AC voltage supplied to the fan motor** with power restored. Your service manual may specify a range (for example, 120 V AC at low speed up to 170 V AC at high speed on some platforms). If voltage is present and the motor does not run, the motor is faulty. If no voltage is present, the outdoor PCB is faulty.
6. **Replace the outdoor fan motor** if it is mechanically seized or electrically open, or **replace the outdoor PCB** if the motor is good but drive signal is missing.
7. **Restore power and run a full cooling cycle** to verify the fan operates normally and the CH90 code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are uncomfortable working with line voltage, cannot safely access the outdoor unit, or do not have a multimeter and service documentation for your specific model. Outdoor PCB diagnosis requires measuring AC drive signals and interpreting model-specific fault charts. If the fan motor is physically accessible and you confirm it is seized or the connector is loose, those are straightforward homeowner checks. If you replace the motor and the fault persists, the outdoor board or a feedback circuit is involved and professional tools are needed.
