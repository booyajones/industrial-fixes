## Navien Tankless Water Heater E229 Error — What It Means

The E229 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically signals an internal communication problem or a flow sensor abnormality. The control board is not receiving expected signals from one or more sensors, which can prevent the unit from heating water properly or cause it to shut down as a safety measure.

This code often appears after power surges, during system startups, or when sensor wiring becomes loose or corroded. Because Navien units rely on precise flow and temperature data to modulate the gas valve and burner, any interruption in sensor communication triggers protective error codes like E229.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flow sensor when the real problem is a loose connector or a temporary glitch in the control board. Always inspect all wiring connections and perform a full power reset before ordering sensor parts.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded sensor wiring (~40%)** Vibration or moisture can cause sensor plugs to back out or corrode, interrupting communication between the flow sensor and the main board.
- **Failed flow sensor (~30%)** The flow sensor itself can develop internal faults or debris buildup that prevents it from sending accurate signals to the control board.
- **Control board glitch or firmware issue (~15%)** Temporary software errors or power surges can cause the board to misread sensor data or enter a fault state that requires a reset.
- **Water flow interruption or air in lines (~10%)** Low inlet pressure, closed service valves, or trapped air can cause erratic flow readings that the board interprets as a sensor fault.
- **Ground or voltage supply problem (~5%)** Inadequate grounding or unstable AC voltage can introduce electrical noise that disrupts low-voltage sensor signals.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas supply valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners to expose the internal components and wiring.
3. **Inspect all sensor connectors** on the main control board and flow sensor, looking for loose plugs, bent pins, or green corrosion.
4. **Disconnect and clean** any corroded connectors with electrical contact cleaner, then reseat them firmly until they click.
5. **Restore power** for two minutes, then turn the unit back on and check whether the error code clears from the display.
6. **Test water flow** by opening a hot tap and observing the heater's response; listen for the burner to ignite and check for steady flow.
7. **Replace the flow sensor** if the error persists after wiring checks and resets, following the unit's service manual for the correct part and installation procedure.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you are uncomfortable working inside the unit, if the error returns after a sensor replacement, or if you suspect a control board fault. Gas-fired tankless heaters require precise diagnostics and safe handling of both gas and electrical systems. A technician can perform voltage tests, check ground continuity, and verify that the new sensor communicates correctly with the board. Professional service also preserves your warranty and complies with local codes.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
