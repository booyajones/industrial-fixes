## Frigidaire Washer E23 Error Code — What It Means

E23 on many Frigidaire washers indicates a failure in the drain pump relay or drain pump control circuit on the main control board. The board detects a problem in the circuit that powers the drain pump, so the washer may fail to drain or stop mid-cycle with water still in the tub.

Because the fault lies in the control circuit rather than always in the pump itself, technicians verify the pump, harness, and pressure switch before replacing the board. The code is commonly reported as a drain pump relay failure on the main control, though manufacturer service documentation for this specific code is limited in public sources.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first, but a shorted or seized drain pump will trigger the same code. Always meter the pump for continuity and check for mechanical binding before buying a board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed relay on the main control board (~50%)** The drain pump relay or driver circuit on the electronic control board has failed and cannot energize the pump.
- **Failed or shorted drain pump (~30%)** The pump is open, shorted, or mechanically seized, drawing abnormal current or no current at all and triggering the relay fault.
- **Loose, damaged, or corroded wiring (~12%)** Connectors between the control board and drain pump are corroded, water-damaged, or have poor contact.
- **Pressure switch or hose fault (~8%)** An incorrect water-level signal from the pressure switch can complicate drain logic and contribute to the code when combined with other issues.

## Quick Diagnosis

Answer these to narrow it down fast.

<details class="dtree"><summary>Does the washer still have standing water in the tub?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The drain path may be blocked or the pump is not running; check for kinks in the drain hose and listen for pump hum during a Drain/Spin cycle.<br><strong>No:</strong> The pump may have drained earlier but the control board logged a relay fault; power-cycle the washer and retest to see if the code returns.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>Can you hear the drain pump motor trying to run during a Drain cycle?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The pump is receiving voltage but may be blocked by debris or mechanically bound; remove and inspect the pump impeller and housing.<br><strong>No:</strong> The pump is silent, so either the control board is not commanding it (relay fault) or the pump is electrically dead; test pump continuity with a meter.</div>
</details>

<details class="dtree"><summary>Does the pump measure normal continuity (typically a few tens of ohms across its terminals)?</summary>
<div class="dtree-body"><strong>Yes:</strong> The pump coil is intact; inspect the wiring harness and connectors for damage, then suspect the control board relay if wiring is good.<br><strong>No:</strong> The pump is open or shorted; replace the drain pump assembly and clear the code before condemning the board.</div>
</details>

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply, then move the unit away from the wall to access the service panels.
2. **Remove the lower front panel or rear access cover** as required by your model to reach the drain pump and control board area.
3. **Inspect the drain hose and pump filter** for kinks, blockages, or debris that would prevent drainage, and clear any obstructions.
4. **Disconnect the drain pump harness** and measure resistance across the pump terminals with a multimeter; consult your model's table for normal values or look for obvious opens or shorts.
5. **Check the wiring and connectors** between the pump and control board for corrosion, moisture, or loose pins, and repair or replace any damaged sections.
6. **If the pump tests bad, replace the drain pump assembly**, transferring any mounting brackets or discharge fittings, then reconnect the harness.
7. **If the pump and wiring are good, replace the main control board** since the relay circuit is integral to that board and cannot be serviced separately.
8. **Reassemble the washer, restore power, and run a Drain/Spin test cycle** to confirm the pump operates and the E23 code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are uncomfortable working around 120 V wiring, if you cannot access the pump or control board on your washer model, or if the code persists after you have replaced both the pump and the board. A pro can also verify the pressure switch and water-level sensor circuits that influence drain behavior and may contribute to intermittent faults. If your washer is still under warranty or a service plan, contact Frigidaire or your retailer before opening the cabinet.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $80–180 for drain pump or $150–250 for control board, 1–2 hours. A pro service call runs about $200–400 including service call and parts.
