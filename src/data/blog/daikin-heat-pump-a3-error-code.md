## Daikin A3 Error Code — What It Means

The A3 error code on a Daikin heat pump signals a drain level control system abnormality. Your indoor unit has detected that condensate water is not draining correctly or that the drain-level safety circuit is open or faulty. This is not a refrigerant or compressor problem. Instead, the indoor unit's drain pan, float switch, or drain pump circuit is reporting trouble.

The fault typically appears when the drain line is clogged, the drain piping is sloped incorrectly, the drain pump has failed, or the float switch is stuck or disconnected. In some cases, a faulty indoor control board can prevent the drain circuit from operating properly. The unit will usually stop to prevent water overflow and damage.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked condensate drain line** Dirt, sludge, algae, or debris in the drain pan or drain line prevents water from flowing out and triggers the float switch.
- **Improper drain piping slope or routing** An upward slope, kink, or incorrect piping installation prevents gravity drainage and causes water to back up in the pan.
- **Failed drain pump** The lift pump that removes condensate is not running or is unable to move water out of the unit.
- **Stuck or faulty float switch** The float is jammed by debris, the switch itself has failed, or the connector is shorted or disconnected.
- **Indoor PCB drain-circuit fault** The control board's drain-pump output or float-switch sensing circuit is defective and cannot operate the drain system correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the unit** at the breaker or disconnect switch to work safely on the indoor unit and drain components.
2. **Inspect the drain pan and drain line** for standing water, clogs, sludge, or algae buildup and clear any blockages by flushing or cleaning.
3. **Check the drain piping slope and routing** to confirm it slopes downward or that the lift pump is installed correctly without upward rises or kinks.
4. **Test the float switch mechanically** by lifting and lowering it to confirm it moves freely and is not jammed by debris in the pan.
5. **Verify the float-switch connector and wiring** are fully seated and not corroded, shorted, or disconnected from the indoor board.
6. **Test the drain pump operation** by observing whether it runs and moves water when the float switch signals high water level or by manually triggering it.
7. **Clear the error code** and restore power, then run the unit under normal operating conditions to confirm the fault does not return and drainage works correctly.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you cannot safely access the indoor unit, if the drain pan or piping requires disassembly or modification, or if you've cleared blockages and tested the float switch and pump but the A3 code returns. A technician can perform electrical diagnostics on the float-switch circuit and indoor PCB, verify drain-pump output signals, and consult the model-specific service manual for resistance and voltage specifications. Indoor board replacement and refrigerant-circuit work must be done by a certified professional.
