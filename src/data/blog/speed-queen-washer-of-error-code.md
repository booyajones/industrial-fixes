## Speed Queen Washer OF Error Code — What It Means

The OF code on a Speed Queen washer indicates the machine has detected an overfill condition or excessive suds in the tub. The washer stops the cycle to prevent water from overflowing or to protect components from foam interference. This fault typically means either too much water has entered the tub, or heavy suds are confusing the water-level sensor. The machine is designed to flag this condition before damage occurs, so the code itself is protective rather than a sign of catastrophic failure.

Most often the problem is detergent related, not a hardware fault. High-efficiency washers require low-sudsing HE detergent in small amounts. Regular detergent or too much HE detergent will create foam that blocks drain flow and fools the level sensor into thinking the tub is overfull. If suds are not the issue, the next suspects are a water inlet valve that leaks past closed, a clogged or kinked drain path, or a pressure sensor that cannot read tub level correctly.

## Common Causes

- **Excessive detergent or high-sudsing detergent** Foam interferes with water-level sensing and drain performance, triggering the overfill fault even when the actual water level is normal.
- **Water inlet valve stuck open or leaking** The valve continues to allow water into the tub after the control has stopped calling for fill, causing true overfill.
- **Faulty water-level sensor or pressure switch** The sensor cannot accurately detect tub level, so the control incorrectly believes the machine is overfilled.
- **Clogged or kinked pressure hose** The small air hose that connects the tub to the pressure sensor is blocked, pinched, or filled with water, preventing accurate level measurement.
- **Restricted drain hose or drain path** Water cannot exit the tub fast enough, so the level remains high and the control flags an overfill condition.
- **Incoming water pressure too high** Excessive supply pressure can force water past the valve faster than the control expects, leading to an overfill reading.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the washer** for 60 seconds, then restart the cycle to reset the control and clear any transient fault.
2. **Open the lid or door** and inspect the tub for visible suds or foam. If heavy suds are present, run an extra rinse or drain cycle to clear them, then reduce detergent quantity on the next load.
3. **Check both water supply valves** at the wall to confirm they are fully open, then inspect inlet hoses for kinks and remove each hose to clean the inlet screens at the back of the washer.
4. **Inspect the drain hose** for kinks, clogs, or improper installation. The hose should not be pushed more than eight inches into the standpipe and must not be sealed airtight.
5. **Locate and examine the pressure hose** that runs from the tub air dome to the pressure switch or control board. Look for cracks, disconnections, or water inside the tube, and replace if damaged.
6. **Test the water inlet valve** by disconnecting power, removing the fill hoses, and checking whether water continues to seep through the valve ports. Replace the valve if it leaks when de-energized.
7. **Run any onboard diagnostics** available for your model to verify fill, drain, and level sensor inputs. Consult your service manual for the procedure, as diagnostic mode varies by Speed Queen platform.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the OF code returns after you have verified correct detergent use, confirmed the drain path is clear, and checked that inlet screens and hoses are clean. Pressure-switch replacement and inlet-valve testing require multimeter skills and access to wiring diagrams. If you are not comfortable removing cabinet panels, working near live voltage, or interpreting diagnostic test results, professional service is the safer choice. A qualified tech can run model-specific diagnostics to pinpoint whether the fault lies in the sensor circuit, the valve, or the main control board, and will have the correct replacement parts on the truck.
