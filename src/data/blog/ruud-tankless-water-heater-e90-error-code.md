## Ruud Tankless Water Heater E90 Error — What It Means

The E90 error code on Ruud tankless water heaters typically indicates a communication fault or control board issue. This means the main control board is either not receiving signals from one or more sensors, experiencing internal errors, or has lost communication with components like the ignition system or flame sensor. The exact meaning can vary slightly by model, so consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit cover for model-specific details.

Because this code involves electronic communication rather than a simple sensor reading, it often points to wiring problems, loose connections, voltage irregularities, or a failing control board. Before assuming the board itself has failed, check all physical connections and power supply issues, as these account for a significant share of E90 faults.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many homeowners replace the main control board without first checking wiring connections and sensor harnesses. Inspecting and reseating all connectors and testing for voltage at the board can save hundreds of dollars.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connections (~35%)** Vibration, moisture, or age can loosen connectors between the control board and sensors, interrupting communication and triggering the E90 code.
- **Low or unstable incoming voltage (~25%)** If the unit is not receiving stable voltage from the breaker or the home's electrical service, the control board may report a communication fault.
- **Failed main control board (~20%)** Internal components on the control board can fail due to power surges, heat, or age, preventing normal operation and communication.
- **Faulty flame sensor or ignition assembly (~12%)** A sensor or ignition component that fails to send the correct signal back to the board can be interpreted as a communication error.
- **Damaged or pinched wiring harness (~8%)** Physical damage to the internal wiring harness from installation, rodents, or service work can interrupt signal paths.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and turn off the gas supply to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the fasteners and setting the panel aside.
3. **Inspect all wire connectors** on the control board and sensor harnesses for looseness, corrosion, or signs of arcing.
4. **Reseat each connector** firmly, and use electrical contact cleaner if corrosion is present.
5. **Check incoming voltage** at the breaker and at the main power terminals on the unit with a multimeter to confirm stable supply.
6. **Restore power** and reset the unit by turning the breaker back on and following the manufacturer's reset procedure.
7. **Monitor the display** for the E90 code. If it returns immediately, test communication between the control board and sensors or replace the board if all connections and voltage are correct.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you are not comfortable working with gas appliances, testing electrical voltage, or replacing control boards. Tankless water heaters combine gas, electricity, and water in a compact space, and improper work can create safety hazards or void warranties. A professional can diagnose communication faults with specialized tools, test voltage under load, and safely replace the control board or sensor components. If the unit is still under warranty, contact Ruud or an authorized service provider to avoid voiding coverage.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
