## Frymaster Commercial Fryer Error Codes — What They Mean

Frymaster (a Welbilt brand) manufactures commercial deep fryers for quick-service restaurants, fast food chains, and high-volume foodservice operations. The M2000 and MJ Series controllers display alphanumeric fault codes.

| Code | Meaning | Urgency |
|------|---------|---------|
| F1 | High temperature limit exceeded | Critical |
| F2 | Temperature sensor fault | High |
| F3 | Ignition failure | High |
| F4 | High-limit thermostat tripped | Critical |
| F7 | Setpoint temperature not reached | Medium |
| F9 | Drain valve fault | Medium |

## Most Common Frymaster Fault Codes and Fixes {#fix}

### F1 / F4 — High Temperature / High Limit
Oil temperature exceeded 410°F (F1) or the high-limit safety device tripped (F4). Immediate shutdown. Check: thermostat calibration, gas valve not stuck open, heat exchanger scale buildup. F4 requires manual high-limit reset after the fryer cools.

### F2 — Temperature Sensor
The cavity temperature probe has failed. Test probe resistance (typically 1kΩ RTD at 32°F). Replace if out of spec.

### F3 — Ignition Failure
No flame established during ignition trial. Check gas supply pressure (typically 3.5-5" WC natural gas), pilot flame if applicable, igniter spark, and gas valve operation.

### F7 — Setpoint Not Reached
Fryer can't reach the set oil temperature. Check burner operation, gas pressure, and heat exchanger cleanliness. A scaled heat exchanger dramatically reduces heating efficiency.

## When to Call a Pro

Gas valve, high-limit thermostat, and heat exchanger work requires a certified commercial kitchen equipment technician. Never operate after F1/F4 without identifying the root cause.
