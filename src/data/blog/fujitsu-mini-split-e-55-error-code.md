## Fujitsu Mini Split E:55 Error Code — What It Means

The E:55 code on your Fujitsu mini split indicates a communication fault between the indoor and outdoor units. The system is reporting that the evaporator and condenser cannot talk to each other at startup. This is not a refrigerant or airflow problem. It is a control-system issue involving the wiring, connectors, or circuit boards that link the two halves of your split system.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or miswired interconnect cable** The communication wires running between indoor and outdoor units are loose at a terminal, open, or connected incorrectly.
- **Failed control board** The indoor controller PCB or outdoor inverter PCB has failed and can no longer send or receive communication signals.
- **Bad PCB connector or plug** A connector at the indoor or outdoor control board is loose, corroded, heat-damaged, or has a pinched conductor.
- **Incorrect unit pairing or addressing** On multi-split or linked systems, the indoor unit type or address setting does not match the outdoor unit configuration.
- **Power quality issue** Voltage drop or poor grounding at the unit can disrupt the communication circuit and trigger the fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Cycle power at the breaker** and let the system restart. Confirm the E:55 code returns before moving forward with diagnosis.
2. **Check the exact fault display** on your wired controller or indoor unit. Note any additional address or unit numbers shown, especially if you have a multi-zone system.
3. **Inspect all interconnect wiring** from the indoor to outdoor unit. Look for loose terminals, damaged insulation, pinched cable, or miswiring at both ends.
4. **Reseat all PCB connectors** on the indoor controller board and outdoor control or inverter board. Check for burned or corroded terminals and clean or replace as needed.
5. **Verify unit pairing and addressing** if your system is part of a multi-split installation. Consult your installation manual for correct configuration settings.
6. **Test the control boards** by substitution if wiring and connectors are intact. Replace the indoor controller PCB or outdoor control PCB one at a time and retest.
7. **Check supply voltage and grounding** at both units. Measure for voltage drop under load and confirm a solid ground connection at the outdoor disconnect and indoor unit.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working inside live electrical panels or handling low-voltage control boards. Communication faults require methodical testing of wiring continuity, board voltage, and system configuration. If you have already inspected connectors and wiring and the fault persists, a tech with a meter and manufacturer fault guides will isolate the failed board quickly. On multi-split or zoned systems, incorrect addressing or pairing can only be corrected by someone familiar with Fujitsu setup procedures.
