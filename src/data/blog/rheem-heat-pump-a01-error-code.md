## Rheem A01 Error Code — What It Means

The A01 code on Rheem equipment means the controller failed to prove main burner flame after repeated ignition attempts. This code is documented for Rheem gas water heaters, not heat pumps. If you have a Rheem heat pump and see A01, verify you are reading the display correctly or check your specific model's service literature, because heat pump units use a different fault code family.

When A01 appears on a gas water heater, the ignition sequence started but the control board could not detect flame during the allowed tries. The appliance will lock out and stop firing until you reset the code and correct the underlying problem.

## Common Causes

- **No gas supply or closed valve** The manual gas shutoff valve is closed, the inlet pressure is too low, or the meter or supply line is interrupted.
- **Failed igniter or poor connection** The hot surface igniter is cracked, burned out, or the wiring connector is loose or corroded so it cannot heat properly.
- **Flame sensor contamination or grounding fault** The flame sensor rod is coated with soot or scale, or a poor ground path prevents the rectification circuit from proving flame.
- **Burner orifice or combustion-air restriction** Debris blocks the burner ports or orifice, or venting and air supply issues prevent a stable flame from establishing.
- **Control board ignition-circuit fault** The board does not drive the igniter or sense flame correctly due to internal component failure or circuit damage.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are not comfortable working with gas appliances, if you smell gas at any point, or if the unit still faults after you have confirmed gas supply and replaced the igniter and flame sensor. A professional can perform combustion analysis, measure gas pressure at the valve, and safely diagnose grounding or control-board faults that require specialized test equipment. If your unit is actually a heat pump and you see A01, a technician can decode the correct fault family and prevent misdiagnosis.
