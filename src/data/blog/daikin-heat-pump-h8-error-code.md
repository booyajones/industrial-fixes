## Daikin H8 Error Code — What It Means

The H8 error code on Daikin heat pumps indicates a malfunction of the compressor input (CT) system. This is an outdoor-unit inverter or compressor-input fault, not a simple communication or thermostat issue. The code points specifically to problems in the inverter power section or the current-sensing and input path used to drive the compressor.

Daikin's official fault tables list four main causes: defective power transistor, defective reactor, faulty inverter-system wiring, or a defective outdoor-unit PCB. In field practice, the outdoor inverter board or power module is the most frequent culprit, followed by damaged wiring or a failed reactor in the inverter circuit.

## Common Causes

- **Defective outdoor inverter PCB** The outdoor unit's circuit board or inverter power section has failed, preventing proper compressor input.
- **Failed power transistor or inverter power module** The semiconductor switching device in the inverter has shorted or opened, blocking compressor drive signals.
- **Defective reactor** The line reactor or choke in the inverter circuit is open, shorted, or heat-damaged.
- **Faulty inverter-system wiring or connectors** Loose, corroded, or burnt wiring between the inverter board and compressor causes input faults.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the error code** by checking the unit's diagnostic display or using Daikin's error-code check procedure to confirm it is truly H8 and not a related inverter fault.
2. **Shut off all power** to the heat pump at the breaker and the outdoor disconnect before opening any panels or touching electrical components.
3. **Inspect the outdoor unit** for obvious physical damage, including loose connectors, burnt terminals, damaged wire harnesses, signs of water ingress, or visible PCB burn marks or component failure.
4. **Check the inverter PCB and power section** for failed transistors, blown fuses, or damaged traces, since Daikin lists power transistor and PCB defects as primary causes of H8.
5. **Inspect the reactor** for electrical continuity and signs of overheating or insulation breakdown, as a defective reactor is explicitly named in Daikin's fault list.
6. **Replace the failed component** (outdoor PCB, inverter power module, reactor, or damaged wiring) based on your testing, then reassemble the unit securely.
7. **Restore power and run a test cycle** to confirm the H8 code clears and the compressor starts normally without re-triggering the fault.

## When to Call a Pro

H8 is an inverter-level electrical fault that requires disassembly of the outdoor unit, live voltage diagnosis, and often surface-mount soldering or board-level replacement. If you do not have an HVAC multimeter, DC bus measurement tools, or experience handling inverter boards and high-voltage components, call a licensed Daikin technician. Misdiagnosis can destroy expensive inverter parts, and working inside the outdoor unit while power is on carries shock and arc-flash risk. Always call a pro if you are uncomfortable with live electrical troubleshooting or if the reactor or power module requires replacement and you lack soldering or board-swap experience.
