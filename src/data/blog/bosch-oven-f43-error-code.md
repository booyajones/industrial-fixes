## Bosch Oven F43 Error Code — What It Means

The F43 error code appears on Bosch washing machines when the control board detects that the drum or motor is not turning or the motor speed feedback signal is invalid. This fault indicates the control cannot verify proper drum rotation during a cycle.

Although this guide addresses F43 as documented for Bosch washers (the code is not officially listed for Bosch ovens in available sources), the fault halts normal operation and typically points to a motor, mechanical obstruction, or control communication issue.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Worn or damaged carbon brushes** Carbon brushes on the motor wear down over time and lose electrical contact, preventing the motor from spinning.
- **Seized or jammed motor** The motor can seize due to bearing failure, overheating, or internal damage, blocking drum rotation.
- **Foreign object or mechanical obstruction** An item lodged between the drum and tub or tangled in the belt prevents free rotation and triggers the fault.
- **Faulty tachogenerator or speed sensor** The tachogenerator sends rotation feedback to the control board, and if it fails the board cannot confirm motor speed.
- **Motor control module or wiring fault** Loose connections, damaged wiring, or a failed motor control board can interrupt power or feedback signals to the motor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Disconnect power at the outlet or breaker and wait at least five minutes to allow the control board to fully reset.
2. Open the door and rotate the drum by hand in both directions to confirm it spins freely without binding or resistance.
3. Inspect the drum, door seal, and visible areas for foreign objects, clothing items, or debris that may obstruct rotation.
4. Remove the rear access panel and locate the motor, then inspect the carbon brushes for length (if they appear less than half original length or heavily worn, they need replacement).
5. Check motor wiring harness and connectors for loose pins, corrosion, or damage, and verify the motor shaft rotates by hand with no mechanical seizure.
6. If brushes are worn, install new carbon brushes and reassemble; if the motor is seized or the tachogenerator is defective, replace the motor assembly.
7. Restore power, clear any residual fault codes per the user manual, and run a short spin cycle to verify normal drum rotation and confirm the error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are uncomfortable accessing internal motor components, if the drum is mechanically jammed and you cannot locate the obstruction, or if replacing carbon brushes does not resolve the fault. Professional diagnosis is also recommended when the error returns after motor replacement, since the issue may lie in the motor control board, wiring harness, or speed-feedback circuitry that requires multimeter testing and board-level repair.
