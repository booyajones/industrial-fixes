## State Water Heater E148 Error Code — What It Means

The E148 error code on State tankless water heaters indicates an ignition or combustion fault, typically related to airflow or combustion sensing. While State and A. O. Smith do not publish an official definition for E148 in widely available service documentation, the code behaves like other ignition lockout faults in the State/A. O. Smith tankless family. These units rely on precise air and gas mixing for safe combustion, and any blockage or restriction in the air intake, venting, or gas supply can prevent ignition and trigger a lockout.

The most common real-world cause is blocked or restricted air intake screens. Lint, dust, pet hair, and other debris accumulate on the intake filter over time, starving the burner of combustion air. Other causes include vent blockage, low gas pressure, dirty flame sensors, or scale buildup in the heat exchanger that disrupts combustion sensing.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake screen** Lint, dust, and debris clog the intake filter and starve the burner of combustion air, preventing ignition.
- **Vent or exhaust restriction** Crushed pipe, improper termination, or blockage in the exhaust vent creates back pressure and combustion failure.
- **Low or interrupted gas supply** Closed manual valves, inadequate inlet gas pressure, or gas line issues prevent the burner from lighting.
- **Dirty flame sensor or ignition assembly** Carbon buildup or corrosion on the flame rod or igniter prevents the board from confirming ignition.
- **Scale buildup in heat exchanger** Hard water deposits restrict water flow and interfere with combustion sensing, triggering repeated faults.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check the exact model and fault history** by reading the model number on the unit label and noting how many times the code has appeared, then power off the unit at the circuit breaker or disconnect.
2. **Inspect and clean the air intake screen** by removing the front cover or access panel, locating the intake filter or screen, and vacuuming or washing away all lint, dust, and debris to restore free airflow.
3. **Inspect the exhaust vent and termination** by tracing the vent pipe from the heater to the exterior termination, checking for crushed sections, blockage, or improper clearances that could restrict exhaust flow.
4. **Verify gas supply and pressure** by confirming all manual gas valves upstream are fully open, then checking that inlet gas pressure matches the rating plate specifications if you have a manometer or call a technician if gas delivery is suspect.
5. **Inspect the flame sensor and ignition components** by locating the flame rod inside the burner chamber and gently cleaning it with fine emery cloth or a soft brush if accessible, following any model-specific service instructions.
6. **Check for scale or flow issues** by inspecting the heat exchanger inlet filter screen and flushing the unit if you see reduced water flow or have hard water, or schedule a descaling service if scale is severe.
7. **Reset the unit and test** by restoring power, resetting the fault through the control panel or by power cycling, then running a hot water tap to confirm ignition and normal operation without the code returning.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or water heater technician if the code returns after cleaning the intake and checking venting, if you cannot safely access gas connections or combustion components, or if you lack tools to measure gas pressure. Professionals have manometers, combustion analyzers, and access to model-specific service bulletins that clarify the exact meaning of E148 for your unit. Also call for help if you suspect scale buildup in the heat exchanger, since descaling tankless heaters requires pumps and chemical knowledge, or if the fault involves the gas valve or control board replacement.
