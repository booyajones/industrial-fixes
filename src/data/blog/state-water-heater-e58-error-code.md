## State Water Heater E58 Error Code — What It Means

E58 is not a universally published State-specific fault code in official documentation. However, on State-branded tankless water heaters that share the A. O. Smith platform, E58 may indicate an ignition lockout or a control communication fault. Some third-party service sources list E58 as a no-response error from the internal control module. Without the exact model number and technical manual, treat E58 as a probable ignition or combustion fault, or a communication failure between the control board and other modules. The heater will not fire or will shut down after repeated ignition attempts.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked air intake or venting** Restricted combustion air screens or obstructed vent pipes prevent proper airflow and cause ignition lockout.
- **Low or interrupted gas supply** Insufficient gas pressure or a closed gas valve will prevent the burner from lighting and trigger a fault.
- **Dirty flame rod or ignition sensor** Contamination or scale on the flame rod stops the control from detecting flame, leading to lockout.
- **Low water flow or clogged inlet filter** Restricted water flow from debris in the inlet filter or supply line prevents the unit from starting.
- **Control module communication failure** Loose wiring, damaged connectors, or a failed internal control module can interrupt signals and generate the code.
- **Scale buildup on heat exchanger or combustion components** Mineral deposits restrict heat transfer and airflow, affecting combustion performance and sensor readings.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the exact model number** from the rating plate on the water heater and download the matching State or A. O. Smith technical service manual to confirm the meaning of E58 for your unit.
2. **Inspect and clean the air intake screens** on the front or side of the unit and check the vent piping for blockages, nests, or restrictions.
3. **Confirm gas supply** by verifying the manual gas valve is fully open and checking that other gas appliances in the home are operating normally.
4. **Remove and clean the inlet water filter** at the cold water connection and flush any debris from the supply line.
5. **Access the flame rod and ignition assembly** per the service manual, remove the flame rod, and clean it gently with fine sandpaper or a soft cloth to remove scale or soot.
6. **Check all wiring harnesses and connectors** between the control board and ignition components for loose connections, corrosion, or damage and reseat or repair as needed.
7. **Perform a descaling flush** if the unit has been in service for more than a year or if hard water is present, following the manufacturer's flushing procedure and using a compatible descaling solution.
8. **Reset the unit** by turning off power at the breaker or disconnect for 30 seconds, then restore power and observe the startup sequence and any new or repeated error codes.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you cannot locate the exact meaning of E58 for your model, if the code persists after cleaning air intake and flame rod, or if you are not comfortable working with gas connections and combustion components. A technician will verify gas pressure with a manometer, test flame-detection circuits, and diagnose control-board communication faults with proper diagnostic equipment. If the unit requires descaling, venting modification, or replacement of internal control modules, professional service is the safest and most reliable option.
