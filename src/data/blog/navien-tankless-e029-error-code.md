## Navien E029 Error Code — What It Means

The E029 code on a Navien tankless water heater signals an air pressure sensor or air pressure switch abnormality. The control board is not receiving the expected signal from the combustion air pressure circuit. This is typically a sensor, wiring, or venting issue rather than a simple "no hot water" fault. Because Navien uses different error codes across models, confirm the code definition in your specific model's manual before starting any repair.

## Common Causes

- **Faulty air pressure sensor or switch** The sensor itself has failed or drifted out of spec, so it cannot send a valid signal to the control board.
- **Loose, corroded, or damaged sensor wiring** Connectors at the air pressure sensor or on the harness are loose, corroded, or broken, interrupting the signal path.
- **Blocked intake or exhaust venting** Debris, ice, or other obstructions in the vent pipes create abnormal pressure conditions that the sensor cannot reconcile.
- **Dirty or clogged air filter** A restricted air filter changes the airflow and pressure profile enough to trigger a sensor fault on some models.
- **Control board input circuit fault** The PCB is not reading or interpreting the sensor signal correctly, even when the sensor and wiring are sound.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the code** in your model's manual, since Navien assigns different meanings across product lines.
2. **Inspect intake and exhaust vent pipes** for blockages, bird nests, ice, or debris and clear any obstructions you find.
3. **Check and clean the air filter** if your model uses one, making sure airflow is unrestricted.
4. **Examine the air pressure sensor wiring** and connectors for looseness, corrosion, or physical damage and reseat or repair as needed.
5. **Test the air pressure sensor** by disconnecting it and checking continuity or resistance per the service manual, or simply replace it if you lack test values.
6. **Replace the air pressure sensor** if the venting is clear and the wiring is intact but the fault persists.
7. **Evaluate the main control board** if a known-good sensor still throws E029, since the PCB input circuit may be at fault.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are not comfortable working with gas appliances, if you cannot locate or access the air pressure sensor, or if replacing the sensor and clearing vents does not resolve the code. Tankless water heaters involve combustion air systems and control logic that require proper testing equipment and gas-safety training. A pro can perform pressure tests, read live sensor values on the board, and make sure the unit meets local code after any repair.
