## Amana Washer F5 E2 Error Code — What It Means

F5 E2 on Amana washers is a lid-lock or door-lock fault. The control is requesting a locked state but cannot confirm the lock has engaged. On Amana front-load machines, F5 E2 means the door is not locking properly. On Amana top-load machines, the same code indicates the lid lock cannot engage. In both cases, the washer will not start the wash cycle because it has not received the expected locked confirmation signal from the lock assembly.

This code appears when the machine is attempting to secure the door or lid at the start of a cycle or during a spin phase. The control board is monitoring the lock circuit continuously. If the lock mechanism does not actuate, if the door or lid is not fully closed, or if the wiring between the lock and the control is faulty, the F5 E2 code will display and the cycle will halt.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board when they see a fault code, but F5 E2 almost always points to the lock assembly or its wiring. Test the lock and inspect the harness connector before buying a control board.

## Common Causes

- **Failed lid lock or door lock assembly (~50%)** The lock mechanism itself has worn out, the solenoid coil is open, or the internal switch contacts no longer close to signal a locked state.
- **Door or lid not fully closed (~25%)** The door or lid is not seated tightly enough for the lock to engage, often because laundry is caught in the opening or the door is not pushed firmly shut.
- **Foreign object or obstruction in the lock area (~10%)** Debris, lint, or a small item is blocking the lock striker from entering the lock housing, preventing engagement.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connector at the lock (~10%)** The harness plug at the lock assembly is loose, has corroded pins, or a wire has broken near the connector, interrupting the lock signal to the control.
- **Misaligned lid strike or door strike (~5%)** The plastic or metal strike that mates with the lock is bent, worn, or out of position so the lock cannot fully engage even when the door or lid is closed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** from the wall outlet or switch off the circuit breaker to remove all power before you begin any inspection or repair work.
2. **Open and close the door or lid** several times and check for clothing, small objects, or debris caught in the opening or around the lock area that would prevent full closure.
3. **Inspect the lock strike** (the plastic or metal tab that enters the lock housing) for cracks, wear, or misalignment, and press the door or lid firmly to confirm it seats completely.
4. **Perform a control reset** by leaving the washer unplugged for at least 5 minutes, then restore power and attempt a test cycle to see if the code clears.
5. **Access the lock assembly** (consult your model's service manual for panel-removal steps) and inspect the wiring harness connector at the lock for loose pins, corrosion, or visible wire damage.
6. **Test or replace the lock assembly** if the harness and strike are intact but the code persists, because the lock solenoid or internal switch is the most common failure point.
7. **Reassemble the washer**, restore power, and run a short test cycle to confirm the F5 E2 code does not return and the door or lid locks audibly during the cycle start.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are not comfortable removing the washer's access panels to reach the lock assembly, if you find damaged wiring inside the cabinet that requires harness repair or splicing, or if replacing the lock assembly does not clear the F5 E2 code and you suspect a control-board fault. A technician has the diagnostic tools to measure lock-coil resistance, verify control-board outputs, and access the full wiring diagram for your specific model. Also call for service if your washer is still under warranty, because attempting your own repair may void coverage.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $40–90 for a lock assembly, 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $150–280 for service call and lock replacement.
