## Samsung Dishwasher Buttons Not Working — What's Happening

When your Samsung dishwasher buttons stop responding, you are seeing a symptom rather than a specific fault code. The touchpad is not registering your presses, or the dishwasher is being held in a locked or safety state that blocks the start sequence. This can happen because control lock is turned on, the door is not fully latched, the touchpad assembly itself has failed, or the main control board is not sending power to the user interface. RepairClinic's troubleshooting guide identifies the touchpad and control panel as the most common failure point, followed by main control board issues that prevent voltage from reaching the touchpad.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Control lock or child lock is active** On most Samsung models, holding the Heated Dry or Sanitize button for about 3 seconds will unlock the panel and restore button function.
- **Failed or moisture-damaged touchpad and control panel** RepairClinic identifies the touchpad assembly as the most common failure, especially when multiple buttons stop working or respond intermittently.
- **Door latch or door switch not engaging** If the door does not fully close or the latch switch is defective, the control will not accept start commands even though the display may light up.
- **Main control board not sending power to the touchpad** The main control board can fail to supply voltage to the user interface, though this is less common and often misdiagnosed according to RepairClinic.
- **Loose or damaged wiring harness under the door** Connectors and wires that flex with the door can break or corrode over time, interrupting communication between the touchpad and the main board.
- **Leak sensor or float-related safety shutdown** Some field reports describe units held in a protection state by the leak sensor, though this is less directly covered in manufacturer documentation.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Confirm the dishwasher has power at the outlet and that the circuit breaker has not tripped.
2. Check if control lock or child lock is enabled by looking for a lock icon on the display, then hold Heated Dry or Sanitize for 3 seconds to release it.
3. Open and close the door firmly to verify it fully latches and that nothing blocks the door striker or switch.
4. Inspect the touchpad and control panel for moisture, stuck buttons, or nonresponsive zones, especially if several buttons fail together.
5. Turn off power at the breaker, remove the door panel or control cover, and check all wiring connectors at the user interface, door harness, and junction box for looseness or corrosion.
6. Test the door latch switch and wiring if the unit still will not start after the door is closed.
7. If the touchpad wiring and door interlock are intact, test the main control board outputs to the user interface, replacing the board only after ruling out the more common touchpad and wiring failures.
8. Reassemble the door panel, restore power, and verify that each button responds and that the dishwasher completes a full start cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

If you have verified that control lock is off and the door latches fully but buttons still do not work, the repair will require opening the control area and testing touchpad connectors, wiring continuity, and board outputs with a multimeter. Because the door panel on many Samsung dishwashers must be disassembled to access the user interface and main board, and because misdiagnosing a board can lead to unnecessary expense, call a qualified appliance technician if you are not comfortable with electrical testing or if you lack the service manual with test points for your exact model.
