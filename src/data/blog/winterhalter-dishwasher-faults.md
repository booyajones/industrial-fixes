# Winterhalter Dishwasher Fault Codes

Winterhalter dishwashers use digital controls that display faults for filling, draining, heating, detergent dosing, and water treatment. Code lists vary by model family, but the service approach is consistent across UC undercounter, PT passthrough, and rack systems.

## Most Common Winterhalter Faults

### F01 — Fill Fault
Check supply pressure first. Winterhalter machines are sensitive to low incoming pressure and dirty strainers. Pull the inlet screen, clean it, and verify the machine receives the required pressure while another fixture in the kitchen is running.

### F04 — Rinse Heating Fault
The machine must hit rinse temperature to sanitize properly. Check booster heater continuity, contactor operation, and the temperature probe. Heavy scale on the heater or tank walls slows heat transfer and causes repeated rinse heat faults.

### F07 — Detergent Dosing Fault
Low chemical, air in the dosing line, or a bad peristaltic pump all trigger this fault. Re-prime the chemical line, inspect the squeeze tube, and verify the controller calls for the dosing pump during the wash cycle.

### F09 — Wash Pump Fault
A blocked impeller is common in busy kitchens. Power the machine down, remove debris from the pump housing, and check motor amps. If the pump trips again under load, test the motor capacitor or replace the pump assembly.

> **Pro tip:** Run the machine long enough to see wash, rinse, drain, and refill. Winterhalter faults often move from one stage to the next because one weak component, like a scaled heater or weak pump, affects several parts of the cycle.
