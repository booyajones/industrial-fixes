## Kenmore Washer E24 Error Code — What It Means

The E24 error code is not a standard Kenmore washer fault code in manufacturer documentation. E24 is a dishwasher drain fault code used by Bosch and Thermador, indicating the machine is not draining properly. If your appliance is truly a Kenmore washer, the model number matters because Kenmore codes are platform-specific and E24 may not apply to washers.

For dishwashers displaying E24, the verified manufacturer definition is that drainage is impaired or blocked. The code triggers when the appliance cannot evacuate water from the sump during or after a cycle. Bosch and Thermador both identify a blocked drain filter as the primary condition associated with this fault. Check your appliance data tag or door frame for the full model number to confirm the correct code definition for your specific machine.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked drain filter or pump area** Bosch and Thermador both identify a clogged filter in the sump as the main condition that triggers E24, trapping debris and preventing water evacuation.
- **Kinked, damaged, or blocked drain hose** A drain hose that is pinched, crushed, or internally obstructed will prevent proper water flow and cause the appliance to fault during drain cycles.
- **Garbage disposal knockout plug not removed** If the dishwasher drains into a new garbage disposal and the inlet plug was not knocked out during installation, water cannot exit the appliance.
- **Loose or improperly seated pump cover** The pump cover beneath the filter must be fully locked in place or the system will not drain correctly and may trip the fault code.
- **Blocked internal drain path or pump impeller obstruction** Glass shards, food debris, or foreign objects lodged in the sump or around the pump impeller can jam the pump and block water flow.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the complaint and reset the appliance by cycling power off for two minutes, then run a drain cycle to confirm the E24 error returns.
2. Remove and clean the drain filter located in the floor of the tub, rinsing it under running water to clear all food particles and debris.
3. Inspect the pump cover beneath the filter for proper seating and check the pump cavity for any glass, plastic, or foreign objects that could block the impeller.
4. Check the drain hose from the appliance to the sink or disposal for kinks, damage, or blockage, and verify the hose has a proper high loop or air gap per installation guidelines.
5. Inspect the dishwasher-to-disposal connection if applicable and confirm the garbage disposal inlet knockout plug has been removed to allow water to exit.
6. Test the drain pump by running a drain cycle and listening for motor noise, then check that the impeller rotates freely by hand with power disconnected.
7. If the hydraulic path is clear and the pump runs but the error persists, consult your model's service manual for control board or sensor diagnostics and consider professional service.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have verified the drain filter, pump area, hose, and disposal connection are all clear yet the E24 code returns during drain cycles. A professional can test the drain pump electrically, inspect the pressure sensor and control board, and verify the appliance is correctly installed per manufacturer specifications. Also seek service if you are uncomfortable working with water connections, disassembling sump components, or if your appliance is under warranty and you want to preserve coverage.
