## State Water Heater E67 Error Code — What It Means

E67 does not appear in available State Water Heater manufacturer documentation. The only verified State error in current sources is Error Code 021 (RE3), not E67. However, E67 is a documented fault code for Noritz tankless water heaters, where it indicates an Air-Gas Mixer (AGM) abnormality. Some State models may use Noritz components or control boards, so if your State unit displays E67 and is a tankless model, it may share Noritz diagnostics.

Before proceeding, verify your exact model number and consult your State installation or service manual. If no State-specific E67 definition exists for your model, contact State technical support or check whether your unit is a rebadged or OEM Noritz platform. Without manufacturer confirmation, following Noritz E67 procedures may not apply to your heater.

## Common Causes

- **Failed Air-Gas Mixer assembly** The AGM component itself has failed internally and cannot regulate the air-fuel ratio for combustion.
- **Electrical connection fault at the AGM** Loose, corroded, or damaged wiring between the control board and the Air-Gas Mixer prevents proper signal communication.
- **Control board misread or transient fault** A temporary voltage spike or board glitch triggers the E67 code even though the AGM is functioning normally.
- **Combustion blockage affecting AGM operation** Debris, lint, or scale in the burner or venturi area interferes with airflow and causes the mixer to report an abnormality.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker or disconnect switch and wait at least 30 seconds to allow the control board to reset fully.
2. **Restore power** and observe the display; if E67 does not reappear and the heater fires normally, the fault was transient and no further action is needed.
3. **If E67 returns immediately**, turn power off again and remove the front cover to access the combustion chamber and AGM assembly per your service manual.
4. **Inspect the AGM wiring harness** for loose connectors, pinched wires, or corrosion; clean contacts with electronic cleaner and reseat all plugs firmly.
5. **Check the AGM mounting** and surrounding burner area for debris, insect nests, or scale buildup; clear any obstructions and verify the mixer moves freely if it has mechanical linkage.
6. **If the code persists after cleaning and reseating connections**, replace the Air-Gas Mixer assembly with the correct OEM part number for your model; consult your parts diagram or contact State support for the exact AGM part.
7. **After installing a new AGM**, cycle power, run a hot-water draw, and confirm stable combustion and no return of E67; record the repair and monitor for 24 hours.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or gas technician if you cannot locate a State-specific definition for E67 in your manual, if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances, or if the error returns after you have reset power and inspected wiring. Gas-fired water heaters require proper combustion analysis and venting checks that only qualified technicians can perform safely. A pro will also confirm whether your State unit shares Noritz components and can source the correct AGM or control board. If your heater is still under warranty, professional service may be required to preserve coverage.
