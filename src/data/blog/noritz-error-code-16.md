## Noritz Error Code 16 — What It Means

Error code 16 on a Noritz tankless water heater indicates an over-temperature shutdown — the outgoing water temperature exceeded the unit's maximum safe limit. The control board detected that the heat exchanger outlet temperature reached an unsafe level (typically above 85°C / 185°F for most residential models) and cut the burner to protect the heat exchanger, plumbing, and users from scalding.

## Common Causes

- **Mineral scale in the heat exchanger** — Calcium and magnesium scale insulates heat exchanger walls, causing the water channel to overheat before the burner responds. Common in hard water areas.
- **Very low flow rate** — When demand falls below the minimum flow rate (typically 0.5 GPM), the unit may not modulate down fast enough and the standing water in the exchanger overheats.
- **Faulty outlet thermistor** — A temperature sensor reporting higher than actual temperatures causes the board to register over-temperature when the water is still within safe limits.
- **Gas pressure too high** — Excess manifold pressure causes the burner to fire at higher than rated capacity, producing more heat than the water flow can absorb.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check and clean the inlet filter screen** — A partially clogged cold water inlet filter reduces flow and can cause temperature spikes. Remove, rinse, and reinstall.
2. **Flush the heat exchanger** — For units 2+ years old in hard water areas, perform a vinegar descale: connect a small pump to the service isolation valves, circulate white vinegar (1 gallon) through the unit for 45–60 minutes.
3. **Test the outlet thermistor** — Disconnect the sensor and measure resistance. Compare to the Noritz NRC/NRCR temperature-resistance table. A reading more than 15% off at room temperature indicates a drifting sensor — replace it.
4. **Lower the thermostat set point temporarily** — If the unit is set to the maximum output temperature, reduce the set point and see if code 16 clears. This is not a fix, but it confirms temperature-setting vs. sensor issues.
5. **Reset and test** — Power cycle the unit (hold ON/OFF for 3 seconds or switch breaker). Run a full demand cycle at normal flow and confirm code 16 does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Gas pressure measurement and adjustment require a licensed gas technician. Persistent code 16 after descaling and thermistor replacement may indicate a heat exchanger that has been damaged by scale and needs replacement — a licensed plumber should assess.
