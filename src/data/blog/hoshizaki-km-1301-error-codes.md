## Hoshizaki KM-1301 Error Codes — What It Means

The Hoshizaki KM-1301 is a large-capacity crescent cuber used in hotels, restaurants, and institutional kitchens. It uses the Hoshizaki control board with diagnostic LEDs and fault memory rather than a text-heavy display. Most service techs interpret KM-series faults by the alarm pattern, board LEDs, and cycle timing behavior.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Long freeze cycle** — Dirty condenser, weak water supply, scale on evaporator plates, or low refrigerant.
- **Long harvest cycle** — Hot gas valve issue, stuck cube release, or water valve leaking.
- **Bin control problem** — Faulty bin thermostat or mechanical lever switch.
- **Thermistor fault** — Evaporator or discharge thermistor out of range.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Inspect and clean the condenser coil and air filter.
2. Verify water supply pressure and clean the inlet screen.
3. Check freeze and harvest cycle times against the Hoshizaki service manual.
4. Test the thermistors for proper resistance at known temperature.
5. Inspect the hot gas valve during harvest and listen for proper actuation.
6. Check the float switch and water reservoir for scale buildup.

## When to Call a Pro
If the KM-1301 is running long cycles with a clean condenser and good water flow, the issue may be refrigerant charge, a restricted TXV, or a hot gas valve problem. Those are refrigeration service jobs.
