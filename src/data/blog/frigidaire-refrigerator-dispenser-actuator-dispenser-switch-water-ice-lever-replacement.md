## Frigidaire Refrigerator Dispenser Actuator/Switch Replacement — What This Part Does

The dispenser actuator is the front paddle or lever assembly you press to request water or ice. When you push it, the paddle mechanically presses a switch or actuator inside the dispenser door, which tells the control board to energize the water valve or ice chute motor. Frigidaire uses separate water actuators and ice actuators on many models, and some units have a complete dispenser module that includes both.

Failure occurs when the plastic lever breaks off, the retaining tabs or springs crack, or the switch mechanism behind the paddle wears out. Broken pedals are a very common failure mode. The actuator is retained by tabs, plates, screws, and springs inside the dispenser frame, and these mounting points can lose alignment or break over time. If the actuator or paddle is damaged, the dispenser will not respond even though the refrigerator and water supply are otherwise working normally.

## Signs It Needs Replacing

- **Broken or missing dispenser paddle** The plastic lever has cracked, snapped off, or fallen out of the front of the dispenser.
- **Loose or floppy lever feel** The paddle wobbles, does not return to its resting position, or feels disconnected when you press it.
- **No water dispensing when paddle is pressed** Pressing the water paddle does not trigger any sound or flow, but ice may still work (or vice versa).
- **No ice dispensing when paddle is pressed** Pressing the ice paddle produces no auger motor sound or ice drop, but water may still work (or vice versa).
- **Paddle stuck in or out** The lever will not move freely or is jammed in the pressed or released position.
- **Clicking or rattling inside the dispenser** You hear loose plastic parts or a clicking noise when the paddle is pressed, indicating a broken tab or spring.

## How to Replace It {#fix}

1. Unplug the refrigerator from the wall outlet and shut off the water supply line to the fridge.
2. Remove the door handle or trim pieces as needed to access the dispenser control panel or front frame (models vary).
3. Carefully pull the dispenser panel or control board forward and disconnect the wire harness connectors behind it.
4. Remove the screws or retainers holding the dispenser module or actuator bracket in place.
5. Release the old actuator from its mounting tabs, pins, plates, or bracket and lift it out of the dispenser frame.
6. Align the new actuator paddle with the chute or bracket and press or snap it into the correct grooves, tabs, or clamps until it seats fully.
7. Reinstall the dispenser module or bracket with all mounting screws, reconnect the wire harness, and reposition any water tubing that was moved.
8. Restore the dispenser panel, trim, and door handle, then turn on the water supply and plug the refrigerator back in.
9. Test both water and ice dispensing by pressing each paddle and verifying proper lever feel and actual flow or ice drop.

## When to Call a Pro

If the new actuator does not restore operation, the failure may be in the water inlet valve, control board, or wiring harness rather than the paddle itself. If you are uncomfortable removing the dispenser panel or disconnecting wire connectors, or if your model requires removal of the entire refrigerator door to access the actuator assembly, contact a qualified appliance technician. If you see water leaking from behind the dispenser after reassembly, call a pro to inspect the water tube connections and seals.
