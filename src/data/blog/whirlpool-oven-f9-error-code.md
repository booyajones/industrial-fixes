## Whirlpool Oven F9 Error Code — What It Means

F9 means the oven cannot confirm its door latch reached the position it was told to reach. The control commanded a lock or unlock and the latch did not report back correctly, so it shuts down as a safety step.

It shows up most after a high-heat self-clean cycle, when grime or heat can bind the latch. The usual culprits are a dirty or worn latch assembly, its switches, or the wiring to the control.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Common Causes

- **Jammed door latch** Baked-on grease and food debris bind the latch so it cannot finish its travel, common after self-clean.
- **Failed latch motor** The motor that drives the latch can burn out and leave the door stuck locked or unlocked.
- **Bad latch switch** A switch that never confirms the locked or unlocked position keeps the control from clearing the cycle.
- **Damaged latch wiring** Loose or frayed wires between the latch and control interrupt the lock signal.
- **Control board glitch** A power event can leave the control thinking the door is in the wrong state until it is reset.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Shut off the oven breaker for about 10 minutes to clear the control memory.
2. Restore power and see if F9 clears and the door releases.
3. If the door is stuck locked, start a self-clean cycle, wait 60 seconds, then cancel it to cycle the latch.
4. Find the manual latch release inside the top of the door opening and free the latch if needed.
5. Clean grease and food debris from the latch mechanism so it moves freely.
6. Test the latch motor and switches for continuity and replace the assembly if either has failed.
7. Inspect the latch wiring, repair any damage, then restore power and confirm normal locking.

## When to Call a Pro

If cleaning and a new latch assembly do not clear F9, the control board may be misreading the door state. A technician can confirm the board and free a door that stays locked safely.
