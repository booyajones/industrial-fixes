## What this code means
On Whirlpool electric ovens and ranges, the F9 or F9 E0 error code indicates the control has detected a problem with the electrical supply or wiring. Specifically, it means the outlet or appliance connection may be miswired, or the control is seeing incorrect voltage or power-line noise during or after a power interruption. Whirlpool ties this code to incoming power and wiring issues during startup, not to a temperature sensor or heating element fault.

If the code appears after a recent installation, the electrical supply should be verified by a qualified electrician. If it follows a power outage, the control may have detected unexpected line noise or a voltage irregularity. On some models, pressing keys too early during the startup sequence can also trigger the code. When F9 returns after a reset, Whirlpool recommends treating it as an electrical supply or wiring issue and having the home supply verified before diagnosing internal appliance components.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the electronic control board when the real problem is miswiring at the outlet or terminal block. Always verify the home electrical supply and appliance connections with a qualified electrician before ordering a control board.

## Common Causes

- **Miswired outlet or appliance connection** The range or oven outlet was wired incorrectly during installation, or the appliance connection at the terminal block is reversed or loose.
- **Incorrect voltage or power-line noise** The control detected an unexpected voltage level or electrical noise on the supply line during or after a power outage.
- **Keys pressed too early during startup** On some models, pressing buttons before the control completes its power-up sequence can trigger the F9 code.
- **Loose or corroded terminal-block connections** The wire terminals at the back of the range are loose, corroded, or making intermittent contact, causing voltage irregularities.
- **Faulty control board power input circuitry** After ruling out external wiring and supply issues, the control board's power-input or filtering circuitry may be damaged.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker or fuse box supplying the range or oven and wait a full 1 minute to allow the control to reset.
2. **Restore power** at the breaker and do not touch any buttons for the first minute, allowing the control to complete its startup sequence.
3. **Monitor the display** for 1 minute. If the F9 or F9 E0 code does not return, test normal oven operation by setting a bake cycle.
4. **If the code returns immediately**, turn off power at the breaker and contact a qualified electrician to verify that the home outlet or hardwired connection is supplying the correct voltage and is wired according to the appliance nameplate and local electrical codes.
5. **Inspect the terminal block** at the back of the range (with power off) for loose, corroded, or reversed wire connections. Look for signs of arcing or burnt insulation.
6. **If the electrician confirms correct supply voltage and wiring**, and the code persists, the fault is internal. The control board's power-input circuitry or a related harness connection may be damaged and should be diagnosed by a qualified appliance technician.
7. **Record the full model number** from the appliance nameplate before ordering any parts, because Whirlpool does not publish a universal replacement part for F9 and the correct control or harness depends on your specific model.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified electrician immediately if the F9 code appears after installation or if it returns after a reset. Whirlpool explicitly recommends verifying the home electrical supply and appliance wiring before attempting any internal repairs. Working inside a range involves high voltage (typically 240 VAC in North America) at the terminal block and control circuitry, which can cause fatal shock if handled incorrectly. If the electrician confirms correct supply voltage and wiring, contact an appliance service technician to diagnose the control board and internal power connections. Do not operate the range or oven until the fault is resolved, because miswiring can damage internal components or create a fire or shock hazard.
