## Amana Dryer F31 Error Code — What It Means

F31 on an Amana dryer is a power-supply fault. Whirlpool (Amana's parent manufacturer) defines F31 as L2 Low or No Line Voltage, meaning the control is not seeing proper power on the second leg of the 240 V supply and the heater cannot energize correctly. This is not the same as F30, which Amana defines as restricted airflow from a lint screen or vent blockage.

The code appears when one leg of the 240 V household supply is missing or weak. Electric dryers use two breakers or fuses, and if one trips or opens the dryer will power on but cannot heat. The control detects the missing L2 voltage and displays F31.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the control board or heating element when F31 appears, but the real cause is almost always a household electrical supply problem. Check both breakers and measure voltage at the outlet before ordering any dryer parts.

## Common Causes

- **One household breaker tripped or one fuse open (~60%)** Electric dryers use two breakers or fuses, and if one trips the dryer gets only 120 V instead of 240 V so the heater cannot turn on.
- **Loose or incorrect power cord installation (~20%)** The dryer power cord may be incorrectly installed or loose at the terminal block, breaking the connection to L2.
- **Home outlet or branch wiring problem (~15%)** A worn receptacle, loose wire in the breaker panel, or damaged branch circuit can prevent full line voltage from reaching the dryer.
- **Intermittent low-voltage condition after installation or power interruption (~5%)** A recent installation, power outage, or disturbed cord connection can cause a temporary loss of L2 voltage that the control detects.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off both breaker switches** at your electrical panel that supply the dryer circuit, wait one full minute, then turn both breakers fully back on to reset the circuit.
2. **Check that both breaker handles are firmly in the ON position** and have not tripped partway off. Electric dryers always use two breakers or two fuses, and both must be closed.
3. **Unplug the dryer and inspect the power cord connection** at the terminal block on the back of the dryer. Remove the access cover and confirm that all three or four wires are tight and installed according to the dryer installation instructions.
4. **Plug the dryer back in and start a Timed Dry cycle** and let it run for about one minute. Watch the display to see if F31 returns.
5. **If the code returns, measure the voltage at the wall outlet** using a multimeter. You should see approximately 240 V across the two hot terminals (L1 and L2) and 120 V from each hot to neutral.
6. **If voltage is missing or low**, the problem is in your home electrical supply (receptacle, breaker, or branch wiring) and you will need an electrician to diagnose and repair the circuit.
7. **If voltage is correct and the code still appears**, the dryer control board may have a fault detecting L2 voltage, but this is rare. Contact a service technician for further diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed electrician if both breakers are on and the dryer cord connections are tight but you still see F31, or if you are uncomfortable working inside your electrical panel. An electrician can test the receptacle, measure voltage at the outlet and panel, and trace branch-circuit wiring faults. Call an appliance technician if the electrician confirms full 240 V at the outlet but the dryer still displays F31, because the fault may then be in the dryer control board or internal wiring.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0 if a tripped breaker, $15-40 for a replacement power cord if needed, 10-30 min. A pro service call runs about $100-200 service call for electrical diagnosis and breaker or receptacle work.
