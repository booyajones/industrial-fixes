## LG Mini Split CH29 Error Code — What It Means

The CH29 error code on LG mini split systems signals an outdoor unit inverter compressor drive fault. This means the compressor drive circuit has detected a problem supplying or controlling the inverter compressor. LG groups CH29 with related codes like CH21, CH26, and CH27, and directs service attention to the compressor, inverter PCB, converter board, wiring, and DC link voltage checks.

In real-world service calls, the most frequent causes are a failed inverter board, a failed compressor, or damage in the converter board and diode section that feeds the inverter stage. The repair will depend on which component has failed during electrical testing.

## Common Causes

- **Failed inverter PCB** The outdoor unit's inverter board has failed or sustained damage to its drive circuit, IPM section, or heat sink connections.
- **Compressor failure** The compressor itself has failed, showing abnormal phase-to-phase resistance or low insulation resistance to ground.
- **Converter board or diode damage** Diodes or the converter section feeding the inverter stage have failed, causing abnormal DC link voltage.
- **Compressor harness carbonization** The compressor connection harness or connector joints have burned or carbonized, creating poor electrical contact.
- **Low insulation resistance** One or more compressor windings show insulation breakdown to the piping or unit ground, indicating internal compressor damage.
- **Poor inverter PCB screw joints** Loose or corroded screw joints and connectors on the inverter PCB have increased resistance and caused the drive circuit to fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the system** and turn off the breaker, then visually inspect the outdoor unit for obvious signs of burning, carbonization, or damaged wiring at the compressor terminals and inverter PCB.
2. **Check input voltage** at the outdoor unit power supply and confirm it matches LG's specification of approximately 220 V ±10% for single-phase systems before proceeding.
3. **Verify airflow and installation conditions** by confirming the outdoor coil is clean, fans spin freely, and there are no obstructions causing abnormal load or restricted airflow.
4. **Measure compressor phase-to-phase resistance** across all three compressor terminals using a multimeter and confirm all three readings are equal (if they do not match, the compressor is defective).
5. **Test insulation resistance** from each compressor phase terminal to the outdoor unit piping using a megohmmeter, and verify readings are above 10 MΩ (lower values indicate compressor insulation failure).
6. **Check DC link voltage** between the P and N terminals on the inverter PCB with power on, looking for approximately 310 V DC on single-phase systems or 540 V DC on three-phase systems.
7. **Inspect diodes and the inverter PCB** by checking diodes between the P terminal and each compressor phase, and examining the IPM, screw joints, and connectors for carbonization, burning, or loose connections before deciding on inverter PCB or compressor replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

CH29 requires advanced electrical diagnostics including DC voltage measurement, insulation testing with a megohmmeter, and interpretation of inverter PCB signals. The repair involves working with high-voltage DC circuits (over 300 volts), refrigerant recovery, and potential compressor or inverter board replacement. Unless you are EPA-certified and experienced with inverter mini split systems, call a licensed HVAC technician. Misdiagnosis can lead to replacing expensive parts unnecessarily, and working on these boards without proper safety precautions can cause serious injury or further damage to the system.
