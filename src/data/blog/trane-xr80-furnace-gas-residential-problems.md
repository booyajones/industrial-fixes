## Trane XR80 Furnace Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Trane XR80 is a residential gas furnace that uses an LED flash pattern on the control board to signal different fault categories. The most common issues trace back to airflow restrictions and venting problems rather than outright component failure. Trane's own guidance directs homeowners to note the exact blink pattern and have a technician decode it, because the same symptom can come from multiple root causes. The XR80 control board will lock out after repeated ignition or proving failures, and resetting power without fixing the underlying problem just cycles the fault.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **Pressure switch fault code (blinking LED)** Blocked intake or exhaust vent, water pooled in vent pipe, or clogged secondary heat exchanger prevents the pressure switch from closing and allowing ignition to proceed.
- **High limit or rollout switch trip** Dirty filter, plugged blower wheel, restricted evaporator coil, or crushed ductwork starve airflow and cause the high-limit safety switch to open and lock out the furnace.
- **Ignition failure code** Cracked or weak hot surface igniter fails to reach temperature or breaks during heat-up, preventing burner light-off.
- **Flame sensor lockout** Corroded or coated flame sensor rod fails to prove flame current to the control board, shutting down the gas valve after a few seconds of burner operation.
- **Inducer motor runs but burners do not light** Pressure switch hose disconnected, cracked, or blocked with debris prevents pressure proving even though the draft inducer is running.
- **Flame sensed when none should be present** Faulty flame sensor grounding, leaking gas valve, or control board fault incorrectly signals flame and locks out the system.
- **No diagnostic LED at all** Loss of 24V transformer output, blown fuse on the control board, or complete board failure cuts power to the status indicator and control logic.
- **Furnace cycles on high limit repeatedly** Plugged secondary heat exchanger, restricted return duct, or undersized ductwork causes heat buildup and repeated safety trips even with a clean filter.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified HVAC technician for any fault code that returns after you have replaced the filter and verified vents are clear. All gas valve work, pressure switch diagnosis, flame sensor testing, igniter replacement, and combustion analysis require proper tools and training. If the furnace locks out on a code you cannot decode from the flash pattern, or if you smell gas at any time, shut off the gas supply at the manual valve and call for service immediately. Control board faults and intermittent wiring problems need a technician with a multimeter and the correct Trane service literature for your specific XR80 submodel.
