## Bosch Oven ER3 Error Code — What It Means

The ER3 error code on a Bosch oven signals that the appliance has overheated and triggered its built-in overheat protection. This is not a sensor fault code but a safety response to actual high temperature inside the cabinet. The code typically clears on its own once the oven cools down to a safe operating range.

Bosch appliances are designed to shut off automatically when internal temperatures exceed safe limits. If the oven was running a self-clean cycle, cooking at maximum temperature for an extended period, or operating in a hot ambient environment, ER3 may appear as a normal protective measure. The code should disappear after the unit has cooled for 30 to 60 minutes.

## Common Causes

- **Cooling fan not running or running slowly** The fan that exhausts heat from the oven cavity may have failed or become obstructed, preventing normal heat dissipation during or after cooking.
- **Blocked or restricted airflow vents** Dust, grease buildup, or objects placed too close to the oven's ventilation grilles can trap heat inside the cabinet and trigger the overheat cutoff.
- **Extended high-heat cooking or self-clean cycle** Running the oven at maximum temperature for long periods, especially during self-cleaning, can push internal temperatures above the threshold and activate ER3.
- **Improper installation clearances** If the oven is installed too close to adjacent cabinets or walls, heat cannot escape properly and accumulates around the control electronics.
- **Faulty temperature sensor or control board** A failing sensor may send incorrect high-temperature readings, or the control board itself may falsely detect an overheat condition even when the oven is cool.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off the oven** and let it cool completely for at least 30 to 60 minutes, then check whether the ER3 code clears on its own.
2. **Listen for the cooling fan** during and after cooking to confirm it runs at normal speed and does not stop prematurely or make grinding noises.
3. **Inspect all ventilation openings** on the top, sides, and rear of the oven for dust, grease, or obstructions, and clean or remove any blockages you find.
4. **Verify installation clearances** around the oven match the manufacturer's specifications, ensuring at least the minimum required spacing from walls and adjacent cabinets.
5. **Power-cycle the oven** by switching off the circuit breaker for 30 seconds, then switching it back on to reset the control board if the code persists after cooling.
6. **Test the oven at a moderate temperature** (350°F) for a short bake cycle to see if ER3 returns, which points to a recurring fault rather than a one-time overheat event.
7. **Contact Bosch service or a qualified technician** if the error reappears after cooling and reset, since persistent ER3 indicates a component failure that requires diagnostic tools and replacement parts.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if ER3 returns after the oven has cooled, you have cleared all vents, and you have performed a full power reset. Diagnosing whether the cooling fan motor, temperature sensor, or control board is at fault requires a multimeter and knowledge of your model's wiring. Bosch recommends contacting their service network if any error code persists after basic troubleshooting, and attempting to replace the control board or sensor without proper training can damage the appliance or void your warranty.
