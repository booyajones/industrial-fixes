## Navien Tankless Water Heater E177 Error — What It Means

The E177 error code on Navien tankless water heaters signals an internal fault detected by the control system. The exact meaning can vary by model, so consult your owner's manual or service diagram for the precise component referenced. Generally, this code points to a communication error, sensor malfunction, or control board issue. The unit may shut down or fail to heat water until the fault is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the control board immediately, but loose or corroded sensor connectors often trigger this code. Inspect and reseat all wiring harness connections before ordering expensive boards.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~40%)** Vibration, moisture, or age can loosen harness plugs or corrode terminals, interrupting signal flow between sensors and the control board.
- **Failed temperature sensor (~25%)** Thermistors or temperature sensors can drift out of range or fail completely, sending invalid data to the control board.
- **Control board fault (~20%)** Internal component failure or power surge damage to the main PCB can trigger diagnostic codes.
- **Water flow sensor malfunction (~10%)** A clogged or failing flow sensor may report erratic readings that the system interprets as a fault.
- **Flame rod or ignition sensor issue (~5%)** Carbon buildup or a failing flame rod can prevent proper flame sensing, leading to error codes.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and turn off the gas supply valve to the unit.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the mounting screws and carefully setting it aside.
3. **Inspect all wiring harness connectors** on the control board, sensors, and flow assembly for looseness, corrosion, or moisture. Reseat each connector firmly.
4. **Locate the temperature sensors** (inlet and outlet) and check their wiring. Look for signs of damage, burns, or pinched wires.
5. **Check the flow sensor** for debris or buildup. Clean the sensor housing and make sure the turbine spins freely if accessible.
6. **If all connections are secure**, use a multimeter to test sensor resistance values according to your model's service manual. Replace any sensor reading outside the specified range.
7. **Reassemble the unit**, restore power and gas, then run a test cycle and monitor for the error code.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are uncomfortable working with gas appliances, if the error persists after reseating connectors and testing sensors, or if you lack a multimeter and service manual to perform resistance checks. Professional diagnostics can isolate the exact failing component and prevent unnecessary part replacement. Technicians also have access to proprietary service codes and software updates that may resolve intermittent faults.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $30-120 in parts, 45-90 min. A pro service call runs about $180-350.
