## MAN Energy Solutions Compressor Fault Codes — Quick Reference

MAN Energy Solutions large reciprocating and centrifugal compressors are used in refineries, chemical plants, and gas processing. Their control systems (PLC-based, often Siemens or ABB) generate alarms and shutdown signals tied to process, mechanical, and safety conditions.

| Fault Category | Typical Alarm | Quick Fix |
|---------------|--------------|-----------|
| High Discharge Temp | Discharge gas temperature high | Check cooling water flow and coolers |
| Low Lube Oil Pressure | Oil system fault | Check oil pump, filter, and level |
| High Vibration | Mechanical issue detected | Stop and inspect bearings, couplings |
| High Differential Pressure | Valve or piston ring issue (recip) | Inspect suction/discharge valves |
| Rod Drop | Piston rod wear detected | Measure rod drop, replace packing |
| High Coolant Temp | Cooling water outlet too hot | Check cooling water flow and fouling |
| High Gas Temp (inter-stage) | Intercooler performance low | Clean intercooler |
| ESD Activated | Emergency shutdown | Investigate shutdown cause before restart |

## Most Common Faults

### High Discharge Temperature
In large recips, high discharge temp usually signals a valve problem — worn or broken suction or discharge valves reduce volumetric efficiency and raise discharge temperature. Also check cooling water flow rate and temperature to the aftercooler.

### High Vibration
MAN compressors use vibration probes on crossheads and bearings. A vibration trip requires immediate shutdown. Root causes include loose connections, worn bearings, piston ring failure, or liquid carry-over into the cylinder. Never run through a vibration alarm on a large recip.

### Rod Drop / Rider Ring Wear
Rider rings support the piston and rod in horizontal cylinder compressors. As they wear, the piston drops and the rod drop sensor triggers. Schedule a planned shutdown to replace rider rings before they allow metal-to-metal contact.

## Jump to Fix

- **High discharge temp** → Check cooling water → Inspect valves → Check intercooler
- **High vibration** → Stop immediately → Inspect bearings and crosshead → Check for liquid
- **Rod drop** → Measure and record → Schedule rider ring replacement
