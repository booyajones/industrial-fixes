## Amana Refrigerator PO Error Code — What It Means

The PO code on your Amana refrigerator stands for Power Outage. The control has detected that power was interrupted and the freezer temperature rose to 18°F (-8°C) or higher while power was off. This is usually an alert message, not a component failure. The display shows "PO," sounds an alert tone three times, and "Confirm" blinks on the panel.

In most cases, the code indicates a real utility outage or brief power interruption that caused temporary warming. Once you acknowledge the alert by pressing MEASURED FILL under Confirm, the PO message disappears and the display returns to the home screen. The refrigerator should resume normal cooling automatically.

## Common Causes

- **Utility power outage or brief interruption** A storm, grid issue, or short loss of household power allowed the freezer to warm above the 18°F threshold.
- **Circuit breaker trip or fuse event** The breaker feeding the refrigerator tripped or a fuse blew, cutting power long enough to trigger the alert.
- **Accidental unplugging during cleaning or moving** The power cord was disconnected when pulling the unit out for service or floor cleaning.
- **Loose or poor plug and outlet connection** A partially seated plug or worn receptacle can cause brief micro-interruptions that the control interprets as a power outage.
- **Damaged power cord or supply circuit** Frayed insulation, internal breaks, or poor upstream wiring can create intermittent power loss.
- **False detection by the electronic control** If PO appears repeatedly with stable incoming power, the control board's power-monitoring circuit may be faulty.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify that a power interruption actually occurred.** Check whether your home experienced an outage, a breaker trip, or whether the refrigerator was unplugged recently.
2. **Acknowledge the alert on the control panel.** Press the MEASURED FILL button located under the blinking "Confirm" label to clear the PO message and return the display to the home screen.
3. **Confirm cooling is resuming normally.** Listen for the compressor to start and verify that cold air begins flowing in both the freezer and fresh-food compartments within 15 to 30 minutes.
4. **Inspect the power supply path if PO returns.** Check that the plug is fully seated in the outlet, examine the power cord for kinks or damage, and verify the breaker is firmly on and not overloaded.
5. **Reset the refrigerator if the code will not clear.** Disconnect power by unplugging the unit or turning off the breaker for 60 seconds, then restore power and monitor for normal startup.
6. **Test the outlet and breaker for stability.** Plug a lamp or multimeter into the same outlet to confirm steady voltage, and listen for any clicking or buzzing at the breaker that might indicate a loose connection.
7. **Escalate to control-system diagnostics if false PO alerts persist.** If the refrigerator shows PO repeatedly without actual power loss, the electronic control board or its power-monitoring circuitry likely requires professional evaluation or replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the PO alert returns multiple times even after you have confirmed stable household power, replaced the outlet, and verified a tight plug connection. Repeated false power-outage codes with no actual interruption point to a failing electronic control board or internal wiring fault that requires diagnostic tools and safe disassembly. Also call for service if the refrigerator does not resume cooling after you clear the alert, or if you are uncomfortable working near live household circuits or accessing internal control components.
