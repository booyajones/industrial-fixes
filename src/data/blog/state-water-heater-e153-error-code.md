## State Water Heater E153 Error Code — What It Means

The E153 error code indicates that the safety float switch in the condensate collection system has been activated. This normally closed switch opens when water in the drain pan reaches a high level, signaling a drainage problem or switch failure. The code protects your equipment by shutting down operation when water cannot drain properly from the condensate tray.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked condensate drain line** The most common cause is an obstruction in the drain line that prevents water from leaving the pan, triggering the float switch when the pan fills.
- **Failed condensate pump motor** If your system uses a pump to remove condensate, a non-functioning motor will allow water to accumulate in the pan until the float switch trips.
- **Faulty float switch** The switch itself can fail mechanically or electrically, remaining open and reporting a false high-water condition even when the pan is completely dry.
- **Stuck or jammed float mechanism** Sediment, debris, or corrosion can prevent the float from moving freely, causing it to stay in the high-water position regardless of actual water level.
- **Clogged drain pan outlet** The drain bung or pan outlet can become blocked with mineral deposits or algae, preventing water from entering the drain line even when the line itself is clear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the unit** at the circuit breaker or main disconnect before beginning any inspection or repair work.
2. **Inspect the drain pan** for standing water to determine if the float switch is responding to an actual high-water condition or a false signal.
3. **Clear any drain blockage** by disconnecting the drain line and flushing it with water, checking both the line and the pan outlet for obstructions or buildup.
4. **Test the condensate pump** (if equipped) by verifying that the motor runs and that it produces adequate flow when water is present in the reservoir.
5. **Check the float switch** with a multimeter if the pan is empty but the code persists, verifying that it switches from closed to open as the float rises and returns closed when lowered.
6. **Replace the faulty component** (switch, pump, or cleared drain line) and clean the pan thoroughly before reassembling all connections.
7. **Restore power and monitor** the system through a complete cycle to confirm the error does not return and that condensate drains properly during operation.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you cannot locate the condensate pan or float switch assembly, if the error returns immediately after clearing the drain and resetting the system, or if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components and water together. A professional can also verify that the drain system is properly sized and sloped for your installation, and can trace wiring faults that may cause false float-switch signals.
