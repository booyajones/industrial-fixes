## Amana Dishwasher F8E5 Error Code — What It Means

The F8E5 code on an Amana dishwasher signals an overfill condition or flood-protection fault. The control has detected either too much water in the tub or water entering the base pan where it should not be. When this code appears, the dishwasher immediately begins a drain sequence and locks out normal operation until the condition is corrected. The machine will refuse to start a cycle even after the code is cleared if the underlying problem is still present.

## Common Causes

- **Water inlet valve stuck open or leaking internally** The valve continues to allow water into the tub even when the control signals it to close, causing the unit to overfill.
- **Float switch stuck or blocked** The overfill float inside the tub cannot move freely due to debris, detergent buildup, or a misplaced dish, preventing proper level sensing.
- **Leak in hoses, sump, or drain connections** Water escapes from the main tub circuit and collects in the base pan, triggering the flood-protection sensor.
- **Failed float switch or sensor** The electrical component that detects float position has failed and sends a false overfill signal to the control.
- **Control board energizing valve incorrectly** The main control continues to power the inlet valve when it should be off, though this is less common than mechanical valve or float failures.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Shut off the water supply** to the dishwasher immediately, since the code indicates a possible flood condition.
2. **Remove the lower dish rack** and inspect the float area at the bottom of the tub to verify the float moves up and down freely without obstruction.
3. **Pull the dishwasher forward** and check the base pan underneath for standing water, then trace all water supply lines, drain hose connections, and the sump area for visible leaks.
4. **Disconnect power and water**, then remove the lower front access panel and examine the water inlet valve for signs of leaking or mineral buildup that would prevent it from closing fully.
5. **Test the inlet valve** by checking whether water continues to enter with the valve unpowered (valve failed closed is the problem) or whether the valve is being powered when the machine is idle (control or wiring fault).
6. **Dry the base pan completely** if water is present, repair or replace the leaking component (valve, hose, seal, or float switch), then restore power and water and run a short test cycle.
7. **Monitor the first few minutes** of the test cycle with the lower panel off to confirm no new water enters the base and the float returns to its rest position when the tub is empty.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you find water in the base pan but cannot locate the leak source, if the float mechanism and valve both test normal but the code returns immediately, or if you are uncomfortable working with water and electrical connections simultaneously. A technician can perform wiring-harness continuity checks, measure float-switch resistance at the control connector, and determine whether the board is misreading the overfill input. If your dishwasher is still under warranty, contact Amana service before replacing any parts to preserve coverage.
