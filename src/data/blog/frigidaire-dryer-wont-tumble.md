## Frigidaire Dryer Won't Tumble — What's Happening

A Frigidaire dryer that won't tumble is typically either a mechanical drive problem or an interlock issue. If the display shows "dr," the dryer thinks the door is open and will not start the drum. The control sees no valid closed-door signal, so it blocks operation. If there is no code and the drum simply does not turn, the issue is more often a broken drive belt, seized idler pulley, worn drum rollers, failed motor, open thermal fuse or limiter, or a power supply fault.

Most no-tumble calls trace back to a door that isn't fully latched, a failed door switch, or a snapped belt. Less often the control board itself loses the door circuit even when the switch is good. Overheating can also trip a thermal limiter on the motor and stop rotation until the part cools or is replaced.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Door not fully latched or misaligned** The door must click closed and engage the switch completely or the dryer will refuse to start the drum.
- **Failed door switch** A bad switch will not signal continuity to the control, causing a "dr" code or silent refusal to tumble.
- **Broken or slipped drive belt** The belt connects the motor to the drum, and a snap or slip means the drum cannot rotate even if the motor runs.
- **Seized idler pulley or worn drum rollers** A locked idler or rough rollers add enough drag to stall the drum or prevent the motor from turning it.
- **Motor overheated or thermal limiter open** If the motor runs too hot the thermal limiter can trip and stop operation until it cools, or the limiter may need replacement if it has opened permanently.
- **Failed electronic control board** When the door and switch both test good but "dr" persists, the control board is the next suspect for a false open-door signal.
- **Power supply issue** A loose plug, tripped breaker, or incorrect voltage (electric models need 240 V confirmed by a qualified electrician) can prevent normal operation.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify whether the display shows "dr" or if the dryer is silent with no code, since the two paths differ.
2. Check that the door closes firmly and the latch engages fully with no visible misalignment.
3. Unplug the dryer and test the door switch with a meter for continuity between COM and NO terminals with the door closed (a good switch will read about 0.1 ohms closed and OL when open or bad).
4. Inspect the wiring from the door switch to the control board for breaks or loose connectors if the switch tests good but "dr" remains.
5. If there is no door code and the drum will not rotate, remove the front or top panel and check the drive belt for breakage or slipping off the pulleys.
6. Spin the idler pulley by hand and roll each drum roller to confirm they turn freely without roughness, flat spots, or seizure.
7. Test the thermal fuse and any thermal limiter on the motor or blower housing for continuity and replace any that are open.
8. Check that the exhaust duct and vent hood are clean and not blocked, since restricted airflow can overheat components and trigger shutdowns.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you are not comfortable working inside the cabinet or testing live voltage. A qualified electrician should verify that electric models receive the correct 240 V supply at the outlet. If you have replaced the door switch and checked wiring but the "dr" code remains, control-board diagnosis and replacement are best handled by a service pro. Any motor or thermal-limiter work also benefits from professional tools and experience to avoid repeat failures.
