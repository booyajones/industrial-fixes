## Samsung HC4 Dryer Error — What It Means

The HC4 error code on your Samsung dryer indicates an overheating condition. Samsung groups HC4 together with HC, hE, and 1 HC as heating errors that all mean the dryer has detected excessive heat. This fault triggers when the dryer's internal temperature exceeds safe operating limits, and the control board stops the cycle to prevent damage or fire risk.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged lint screen** A blocked lint filter restricts airflow through the drum, trapping heat and triggering the overheating fault.
- **Blocked exhaust vent or ductwork** Lint buildup, crushed ducts, or obstructed exterior vent hoods prevent hot air from escaping, causing temperature spikes inside the dryer.
- **Low voltage supply (electric models)** Inadequate voltage at the outlet can cause erratic heating behavior and Samsung identifies this as a potential cause of heating errors.
- **Shorted heating element** A heating element touching the chassis or housing can create uncontrolled heat and trip the overheating sensor.
- **Failed thermistor or temperature sensor** A sensor reading out of range or sending incorrect signals may cause the control board to misinterpret normal heat as an overheat condition.
- **Control board fault** If all mechanical and electrical components test normal, the main control board itself may be misreading temperature data or failing to regulate the heating cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Stop the dryer and allow it to cool completely** before beginning any inspection or testing, then unplug the unit from power.
2. **Remove and clean the lint screen thoroughly** with warm water and a soft brush to clear any film or buildup that reduces airflow.
3. **Disconnect the exhaust duct from the back of the dryer** and inspect the entire vent path from the dryer to the exterior hood for lint accumulation, crushed sections, or blockages, then clean or replace any restricted ductwork.
4. **Verify the dryer is plugged into its own dedicated circuit** without any extension cord or adapter, and on electric models confirm the outlet is delivering the correct voltage by having an electrician test if cleaning did not resolve the code.
5. **Access the heating element** by removing the front or rear panel (model-dependent) and use a multimeter to test for continuity between each terminal and the metal chassis, looking for any measurable resistance that indicates a short to ground.
6. **Locate the thermistor or temperature sensor** on the blower housing or heating duct and measure its resistance at room temperature, comparing the reading to your model's specification sheet to verify it is within range.
7. **Restart the dryer on a timed dry cycle** and monitor for the HC4 code to return; if the error persists after airflow, voltage, element, and sensor checks all pass, the control board is the likely remaining fault and requires replacement or professional diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

If cleaning the lint screen and exhaust vent does not clear the HC4 code, or if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components and multimeter testing, contact a Samsung service center or qualified appliance technician. Samsung specifically recommends professional service if the error remains after you have checked airflow and verified proper voltage supply. Electric dryer voltage faults and control board diagnostics require an electrician or certified repair technician to avoid shock hazard and to make sure warranty coverage remains intact.
