## Whirlpool Microwave E63 Error Code — What It Means

The E63 error on Whirlpool over-the-range microwaves means the oven sensor circuit reported a shorted condition to the control board. Whirlpool's official description states that the control board is seeing the sensor input electrically out of range. This fault points to a problem with the sensor itself, the appliance control board, or the wiring that connects them. A short in the circuit can happen when the sensor fails internally, when wires are damaged or pinched, or less commonly when the control board has a fault. The error prevents normal operation because the control board relies on accurate sensor feedback to regulate cooking functions.

## Common Causes

- **Failed oven sensor** The thermistor has developed an internal electrical short, sending out-of-range signals to the control board.
- **Damaged or pinched wiring** Harness insulation has been cut, crushed, or melted, causing a short to chassis or between conductors.
- **Loose or corroded connector** The sensor plug at either the control board or sensor end has backed out or developed corrosion that creates intermittent shorts.
- **Faulty appliance control board** The control board itself has a circuit fault in the sensor input stage, reading a short condition even when the sensor is good.
- **Temporary glitch after power event** A surge or brownout can cause the control to latch a false fault that clears after a full power cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power at the breaker** for a full 60 seconds to reset the control board and clear any transient faults.
2. **Restore power and observe** for at least one minute to see if the E63 code returns immediately or during a test cycle.
3. **Access the sensor circuit** by removing the outer cabinet panels to expose the control board and sensor location, keeping power off during inspection.
4. **Inspect all wiring** in the oven sensor circuit for pinched insulation, melted spots, loose connectors, or signs of arcing to the chassis.
5. **Test the oven sensor electrically** by disconnecting it and measuring resistance, then compare the reading to your model's service data (Whirlpool does not publish consumer specs for E63, so consult the technical sheet or service manual for your specific model number).
6. **Check the sensor connector** at both the control board and the sensor end for secure seating, bent pins, and any corrosion or moisture that could cause a short path.
7. **Replace the defective component** (sensor, wiring, or control board) based on test results, then run a full cook cycle to verify the fault is cleared and the microwave operates normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the code returns after a power reset and you are not comfortable working with live 120V circuits or disassembling cabinetry around a mounted microwave. Diagnosing E63 requires access to the sensor circuit, accurate resistance testing against model-specific service data, and safe handling of control board connections. If you replace the sensor and the fault persists, the control board is the next likely candidate, and board-level diagnosis is best left to a technician with the correct service literature and test equipment for your model.
