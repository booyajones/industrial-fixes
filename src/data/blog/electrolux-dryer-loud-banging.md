## Electrolux Dryer Loud Banging — What's Happening

A loud banging noise in an Electrolux dryer is not an error code. It is a mechanical noise symptom. Electrolux classifies banging, rattling, and clanking under dryer noise conditions caused by loose objects, foreign items in the drum, an unlevel machine, or shipping bolts still in place on new installations.

In practical repair terms, banging means a physical impact or movement problem inside the dryer cabinet. It can be as simple as coins hitting the drum or as serious as worn drum support components creating impact noise during rotation. The sound comes from metal-on-metal contact or components moving out of position.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Loose or foreign objects in the drum** Coins, belt buckles, or other hard items in clothing pockets create clanking and rattling noises as they tumble against the drum.
- **Worn drum rollers** Drum rollers develop flat spots or binding over time, causing thumping or rumbling that sounds like banging as the drum turns.
- **Dryer not leveled** An unlevel unit causes excessive vibration and movement, producing banging or rattling during operation.
- **Worn drum bearing or rear support** A worn rear drum bearing creates grinding, squealing, or impact-like noises as the drum rotates on a damaged surface.
- **Shipping bolts not removed** On newly installed dryers, shipping bolts left in place cause severe banging and movement restriction.
- **Blower wheel obstruction or damage** Lint buildup, cracks, or wobble in the blower wheel produce rumbling or noisy operation that can sound like banging.
- **Worn or damaged drive belt** A frayed or glazed belt can contribute to abnormal noise as it slips or contacts the drum incorrectly during rotation.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Run the dryer empty to confirm the noise comes from the machine and not from items in the load.
2. Check the drum and all clothing pockets for coins, fasteners, or other hard foreign objects.
3. Verify the dryer is level by placing a level on top of the unit and adjust the leveling legs as needed.
4. Confirm all shipping bolts were removed if the dryer was recently installed.
5. Inspect the drive belt for fraying, glazing, or damage by accessing the drum area and replace if worn.
6. Inspect the drum rollers and axles for flat spots, wobble, or binding by rotating the drum by hand and replace all rollers together if one is worn.
7. Check the drum bearing and rear support by removing the belt and turning the drum manually. Replace if it feels rough, noisy, or loose.
8. Inspect the blower wheel for lint buildup, wobble, or cracks and clean or replace as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the noise persists after checking for foreign objects and leveling the unit, or if you are not comfortable disassembling the dryer cabinet to access internal components. Drum roller, bearing, and blower wheel replacement requires removing panels, disconnecting the belt, and handling parts under spring tension. A pro can also diagnose less common causes like drive motor bearing failure and has the tools to test components safely.
