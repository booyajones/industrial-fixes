## Rheem A004 Error Code — What It Means

The A004 code on a Rheem hybrid heat pump water heater indicates a compressor shutdown due to a discharge-suction protection trip. The controller has detected unsafe operating conditions in the compressor circuit and has stopped heat pump operation. The unit will typically shift to resistance heating elements to continue providing hot water, but the heat pump function is disabled until the fault is cleared. This is a compressor protection measure, not just a sensor reading error.

## Common Causes

- **Restricted airflow across the evaporator** A clogged or dirty filter screen blocks air movement and causes abnormal refrigerant temperatures that trigger the protection trip.
- **Failed or weak evaporator fan** If the fan does not run or runs too slowly while the compressor is energized, pressure and temperature conditions go out of range.
- **Faulty suction thermistor** The suction thermistor provides temperature feedback to the controller, and when it fails the controller sees false temperature data and shuts down the compressor.
- **Bad compressor capacitor** On models that use a capacitor, a weak or failed capacitor prevents proper compressor start and run behavior.
- **Refrigerant leak or sealed-system damage** Loss of refrigerant charge or internal compressor failure creates conditions the controller interprets as a discharge-suction fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check and clean the evaporator filter and screen.** Remove any dust, lint, or debris blocking airflow through the heat pump section and verify nothing is obstructing the air intake or discharge.
2. **Verify the evaporator fan runs when the compressor is commanded on.** Listen and look for fan rotation during a heat cycle, and inspect the fan motor and wiring if it does not run.
3. **Access the service menu and check thermistor readings.** Step through the diagnostics display to view suction and discharge temperature values and compare them to normal operating ranges for your model.
4. **Inspect for oil residue around the compressor and refrigerant tubing.** Visible oil suggests a refrigerant leak or sealed-system problem that will require professional repair or unit replacement.
5. **Test the compressor capacitor if your model uses one.** Use a multimeter with capacitance function to verify the rated microfarad value and replace the capacitor if it tests low or open.
6. **Replace the suction thermistor if diagnostics show out-of-range readings.** This is the most common part replacement for A004 faults and is often the fix after airflow and fan operation are confirmed normal.
7. **Reset the unit and monitor for code recurrence.** Clear the fault from the controller, run a full heat cycle, and check the fault history to confirm the repair resolved the trip condition.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you see oil residue around the compressor or tubing, if thermistor and fan checks do not resolve the code, or if you are not comfortable working with electrical diagnostics and refrigerant systems. Sealed-system repairs and compressor replacement are not typical field repairs and may require unit replacement. A qualified HVAC or water heater technician can perform refrigerant and pressure testing, diagnose control board issues, and determine whether repair or replacement is the better option for your situation.
