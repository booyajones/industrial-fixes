## LG Refrigerator Running Constantly — What's Happening

When an LG refrigerator runs constantly, the compressor is staying on because the unit has not reached its set temperature or because cold air is escaping. LG states that the refrigerator may continue operating if cold air is lost through a poorly sealed door gasket, if the temperature is set to Medium or higher, or if the cabinet is still warm after a recent move, service, repair, or power interruption.

Once the set temperature is reached, the compressor should normally cycle on and off. Continuous running means the system is still working to recover temperature or is losing cooling capacity faster than it can replace it.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Door not closing properly or weakened magnetic gasket** Warm air infiltrates through a weak, dirty, warped, or damaged door seal, preventing the cabinet from reaching setpoint.
- **Recent move, service, or power outage** LG notes the refrigerator may run continuously for a day or longer while it pulls down temperature after relocation, repair, or a power interruption that warmed the cabinet.
- **Temperature setting too warm** Setting left at Medium or higher keeps the compressor running longer to maintain target temperature.
- **Food or items blocking door closure or internal vents** Obstructions prevent full closure or block airflow inside the cabinet, slowing cooling.
- **Dirty condenser coils** Dust or debris on the coils reduces heat rejection and forces longer compressor run time to achieve setpoint.
- **Failed condenser fan or poor airflow** A fan that does not run or is blocked prevents proper heat exchange and extends compressor operation.
- **Sealed system or compressor failure** If temperature does not recover after basic corrections, the compressor, inverter control board, or sealed system refrigerant circuit may have failed.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the complaint by checking actual cabinet temperatures and confirming the compressor is truly running without cycling off.
2. Confirm the door fully closes and inspect the magnetic gasket for weak adhesion, dirt, warping, tears, or gaps.
3. Perform a seal check and confirm no food or items are blocking closure or internal vents.
4. Check the temperature setting and lower it one step if it is at Medium or above.
5. If the unit was recently moved, serviced, or lost power, allow 24 to 48 hours for temperature stabilization before replacing parts.
6. Check condenser coil cleanliness and airflow around the cabinet, cleaning coils if dusty or obstructed.
7. Inspect the condenser fan and evaporator air movement for proper operation.
8. If temperature is not recovering after basic corrections, check compressor operation, inverter control board output voltage with a meter, and evaluate the sealed system for refrigerant or compressor failure.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if the refrigerator continues running constantly after you have cleaned the door seal, confirmed proper closure, adjusted the temperature setting, and waited 24 to 48 hours for recovery. A technician will use a meter to check inverter board output, test the compressor and sealed system, and diagnose refrigerant or control failures that require EPA-certified refrigerant handling and board-level diagnostics.
