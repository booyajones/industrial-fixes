EVAPCO cooling towers and evaporative condensers with PMC (Packaged Mechanical Component) control panels or VFD systems generate fault conditions from motor protection, temperature monitoring, and water management systems.

## Most Common Faults

### Fan Motor Fault
EVAPCO towers use axial or centrifugal fans. Motor overloads are caused by: ice buildup on fan blades in winter, worn motor bearings increasing load, blade pitch set incorrectly, and belt slippage on belt-drive models. Check motor amperage and compare to nameplate. Inspect bearings for roughness or excessive play.

### High Leaving Water Temperature
If the tower is not maintaining approach temperature, check: fan speed (VFD may be in low-speed or manual mode), drift eliminators and distribution basins for scale buildup, and whether the heat load has increased beyond the tower's rated capacity.

### Freeze Alarm
Cold weather tower operation is critical. EVAPCO towers use basin heaters to prevent freeze-up. Check the heater element with an ohmmeter. Verify the freeze protection thermostat setpoint. EVAPCO recommends maintaining basin water temperature above 35°F.

## EVAPCO Closed-Circuit Coolers (LSTE)

EVAPCO LSTE closed-circuit evaporative coolers add additional monitoring:
- **Tube Bundle Fouling** — external fouling reduces heat transfer; schedule cleaning
- **Blowdown Fault** — blowdown valve not responding; check valve and conductivity controller
- **High Conductivity** — water quality issue; increase blowdown rate

## Jump to Fix

- **Fan motor fault** → Check amps → Inspect bearings → Check for ice or blade damage
- **High leaving water temp** → Verify fan speed → Check for scale → Evaluate load
- **Freeze alarm** → Check basin heater → Verify thermostat → Increase circulation
