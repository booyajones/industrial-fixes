## Navien Tankless Water Heater E307 Error — What It Means

The E307 error on a Navien tankless water heater indicates the control board is not receiving a proper flame-sense signal from the flame rod. The flame rod acts as a sensor that confirms the burner has ignited and remains lit. When the rod cannot detect the flame, the unit shuts down to prevent unsafe operation. This code can appear even when the burner lights briefly or appears normal, because the sensing circuit requires a clean electrical path through the flame itself.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the flame rod or igniter assembly when the real issue is simply carbon buildup on the rod tip. Pull the rod and inspect it closely under good light before ordering parts.

## Common Causes

- **Dirty or corroded flame rod tip (~55%)** Carbon and mineral deposits coat the sensing surface and block the electrical signal needed to confirm flame presence.
- **Loose or corroded flame-rod connector (~20%)** The wire terminal at the rod or control board can oxidize or pull away, interrupting the sense circuit.
- **Worn or cracked flame rod (~15%)** Over time the ceramic insulator can crack or the metal tip can erode, preventing reliable flame detection.
- **Grounding issue in the burner assembly (~7%)** If the flame rod ground path is compromised by rust or a loose burner mounting bolt, the sense current cannot complete its circuit.
- **Failed control board flame-sense circuit (~3%)** Rarely, the input stage on the control board itself fails and cannot read the microamp signal from the rod.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off** electrical power at the breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** and locate the flame rod, a thin metal probe extending into the burner chamber, usually held by one or two screws.
3. **Disconnect the wire** from the flame rod terminal and unscrew the mounting hardware to pull the rod free.
4. **Clean the sensing tip** with fine-grit sandpaper or steel wool until the metal is shiny and smooth, removing all carbon and oxidation.
5. **Inspect the ceramic insulator** along the rod shaft for cracks or burn marks; replace the rod if damaged.
6. **Check the wire connector** for corrosion and spray with electrical contact cleaner if needed, then reconnect firmly.
7. **Reinstall the flame rod**, tighten the mounting screws, and confirm the rod is centered in the burner with adequate clearance from metal surfaces.
8. **Restore gas and power**, initiate a hot-water call, and verify the unit fires and the E307 error does not return.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed technician if you are uncomfortable working around gas appliances, if the flame rod and connections appear clean but the error persists, or if you suspect a failed control board. A pro can perform a microamp flame-current test to confirm the sensing circuit is functioning and can safely verify gas pressure and combustion settings. Also call if the burner will not ignite at all, since that points to ignition components or gas-supply issues beyond simple flame-rod maintenance.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15-50 in parts if replacement needed, 30-60 min. A pro service call runs about $150-280 service call.
