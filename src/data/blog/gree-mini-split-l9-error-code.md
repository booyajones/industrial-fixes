## Gree Mini Split L9 Error Code — What It Means

L9 indicates that your Gree mini split has entered power protection mode. The control system has detected abnormal incoming power or a power-related fault condition and has locked out operation to protect the inverter and electronics. According to Gree's published troubleshooting guidance, this code is triggered by a significant power surge or similar supply disturbance.

Because L9 is a protection-based lockout rather than a direct sensor error, the fault may be temporary (a momentary spike that has passed) or ongoing (unstable line voltage or damaged components). The system will remain off until the condition is cleared and the unit is reset.

## Common Causes

- **Utility surge or lightning strike** A major power surge from the grid or a nearby lightning event can trip the power protection circuit and lock out the unit.
- **Unstable line voltage** Voltage sags, brownouts, or fluctuations outside the unit's operating range will activate protection mode.
- **Loose or damaged wiring connections** Poor terminations at the disconnect, outdoor unit, or branch circuit can create intermittent power faults.
- **Branch circuit or disconnect issue** A weak breaker, corroded disconnect, or undersized wire can produce abnormal supply conditions that trigger L9.
- **Control board or inverter damage from a previous event** If a surge has already damaged internal electronics, the board may latch into protection mode even after power is restored.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **De-energize the system** by turning off the disconnect or breaker, wait at least five minutes, then restore power to attempt a reset of the protection lockout.
2. **Check the incoming voltage** at the outdoor unit's power terminals with a multimeter to confirm the supply is stable and within the required range for your model.
3. **Inspect all wiring and terminations** at the disconnect, branch circuit, and unit itself for loose connections, corrosion, or signs of heat or surge damage.
4. **Verify the branch circuit and disconnect** are properly sized and functioning, and that the breaker has not weakened or the disconnect contacts degraded.
5. **Power the unit back on** and monitor for several minutes to see if L9 returns immediately or under load, which may indicate a persistent fault in the control or power circuitry.
6. **Consult the model-specific service manual** for your exact Gree unit, as L9 definitions and diagnostics can vary by product family and region.
7. **If the code persists or returns**, contact Gree technical support or a qualified technician to perform board-level diagnostics and determine whether the main control board or inverter electronics require replacement.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if the L9 code returns after a power reset, if you measure unstable or out-of-range voltage at the unit, or if you lack the tools to safely test live high-voltage circuits. Because L9 is a protection lockout that can result from either supply problems or internal board damage, professional diagnostics are often required to pinpoint the root cause and avoid unnecessary part replacement. Always use Gree-approved service procedures and parts for your specific model to maintain warranty coverage and system safety.
