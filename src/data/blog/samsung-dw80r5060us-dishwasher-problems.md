## Samsung DW80R5060US Dishwasher Problems & Error Codes — Overview

The Samsung DW80R5060US is a 24-inch built-in dishwasher in Samsung's mid-range lineup. Field experience shows the most documented failure on this specific model is a nonresponsive or intermittent touchpad, traced to a failed touchpad control board rather than drain or water-supply faults. Like other Samsung dishwashers, it shares common error codes including 1E/IE for clogged filters, 3C for pump failure, and PC for Zone Wash malfunctions.

Most service calls stem from user-interface failures, clogged filters causing fill or drain errors, kinked drain hoses, and wet-base-pan conditions that trigger leak-protection shutdowns. Samsung does not publish a DW80R5060US-specific service bulletin in publicly available materials, so technicians rely on the brand's general dishwasher error-code guide and model-line repair patterns. The touchpad repair requires door disassembly and careful handling of the cosmetic outer skin during touch-panel replacement.

## Most Common Problems on This Model {#fix}

- **Buttons not responding or intermittent touchpad** The touchpad control board fails and stops registering user inputs, requiring replacement of the touch panel assembly after removing the upper door panel screws and carefully separating the cosmetic skin.
- **1E or IE error code** A clogged filter or debris blocking water flow through the filter basket triggers this code, and the fix is cleaning or replacing the main filter and checking the sump area for obstructions.
- **Drain-related faults or standing water** Kinked drain hose, loose drain connections, or obstructions in the drain path prevent proper pump-out, so inspect and straighten the hose, tighten fittings, and clear any blockages in the drain pump or air gap.
- **Leak-protection shutdown or wet base pan** Water staining or mineral deposits around hoses and fittings indicate a slow leak that fills the base pan and trips the leak sensor, requiring you to dry the pan thoroughly, find and repair the leak source, then rerun a cycle to confirm the fault clears.
- **3C error code** Pump failure or a blocked impeller causes this code, and the repair involves checking for foreign objects in the pump housing, testing pump electrical continuity, and replacing the circulation or drain pump if defective.
- **No fill or weak fill** A closed water-supply valve, clogged inlet screen, or kinked fill hose restricts water entry, so verify the valve is fully open, remove and clean the inlet screen at the valve connection, and inspect the fill hose for kinks or damage.
- **PC error code** A malfunction in the Zone Wash function triggers this code, typically caused by a failed spray-arm motor or control-board communication issue that requires component-level diagnosis and replacement of the affected actuator or board.
- **Codes persist after attempted fixes** Residual fault memory or incomplete resets keep error codes displayed even after repair, and running a hard reset by unplugging the unit for 10 minutes or entering Samsung's diagnostic test mode clears stored faults and confirms the actual system state.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician when error codes persist after you have cleaned the filter, checked the drain hose, and completed a 10-minute hard reset, or when the touchpad requires door disassembly and you are uncomfortable working with the cosmetic skin and control-board connectors. Any work involving the main control board, pump replacement, or leak diagnosis in the base pan benefits from a technician's test equipment and access to Samsung's diagnostic mode. If you see water staining, mineral deposits, or recurring leak-protection shutdowns and cannot locate the source, a pro can pressure-test hoses, inspect door-gasket integrity, and replace the leak sensor or related components safely.
