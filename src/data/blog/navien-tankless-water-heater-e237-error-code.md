## Navien Tankless Water Heater E237 Error — What It Means

The E237 error on Navien tankless water heaters typically indicates an air pressure sensing issue. The unit's air pressure sensor (APS) monitors the pressure differential in the combustion and exhaust system to confirm safe venting. When the sensor detects abnormal pressure or fails to receive the expected signal, the heater will display E237 and shut down to prevent unsafe operation.

This code can stem from physical blockages in the intake or exhaust venting, a failing APS itself, or wiring problems between the sensor and the control board. Because proper venting is tied directly to combustion safety, the unit will not operate until the condition is resolved.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the APS without checking venting first. Inspect all intake and exhaust pipes for blockages, ice, or bird nests before ordering a new sensor.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted venting (~50%)** Debris, ice, or condensation in the intake or exhaust pipe prevents normal airflow and triggers the pressure sensor fault.
- **Failed air pressure sensor (APS) (~25%)** The sensor itself can wear out or develop internal faults, sending incorrect signals to the control board.
- **Loose or corroded APS wiring (~15%)** Poor electrical connections at the sensor or board interrupt the signal and generate the error.
- **Improper vent pipe installation or length (~10%)** Venting that is too long, has too many elbows, or is undersized creates excessive back pressure.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker and close the main gas supply valve.
2. **Inspect the outdoor vent terminations** for visible blockages such as ice buildup, bird nests, leaves, or snow covering the intake or exhaust opening.
3. **Remove any obstructions** from the vent pipes and make sure both intake and exhaust are clear and unobstructed.
4. **Check indoor vent piping** for loose joints, condensate pooling, or disconnected sections that could affect airflow.
5. **Locate the air pressure sensor** (a small round device with a rubber hose attached, usually mounted near the burner assembly) and verify that the tubing is not kinked, cracked, or disconnected.
6. **Inspect the sensor wiring harness** for corrosion, loose pins, or damaged connectors, and reseat the connector firmly.
7. **Restore power and gas**, then reset the unit by pressing the reset button or cycling power, and observe whether the error clears and the heater fires normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien-certified technician if you find no obvious vent blockages and the error persists. Diagnosing a faulty APS requires a multimeter to test pressure switch continuity and may involve inspecting the combustion chamber and gas valve. Because tankless water heaters involve gas and combustion safety, any work beyond clearing vents or reseating connectors should be handled by a professional. A technician can also verify that the venting was installed to code and that no exhaust gas spillage is occurring.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-350.
