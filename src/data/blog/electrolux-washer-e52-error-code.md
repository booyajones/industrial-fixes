## Electrolux Washer E52 Error — What It Means

The E52 error code on Electrolux washers indicates a motor tachometer or motor feedback fault. The control board is not receiving the expected motor-speed signal, so it cannot verify that the motor is turning correctly. In practical terms, the machine thinks the motor should be running, but the speed signal is missing, incorrect, or inconsistent.

This fault points to a problem with the tachometer (tacho) sensor on the motor, the wiring that carries the signal to the control board, or the control board itself. It can also appear when mechanical drag or a worn motor prevents normal rotation, which produces a false feedback fault.

## Before You Replace Anything

Many people replace the main control board first. Always test the motor tacho circuit and motor windings with a meter and inspect the motor harness connectors before replacing the board, because the fault usually follows the motor or wiring.

## Common Causes

- **Failed motor tachometer or speed sensor (~40%)** The tacho coil or sensor inside the motor assembly is open or delivering an abnormal signal to the control board.
- **Worn motor brushes (~25%)** Brushes that are short or making poor contact prevent the motor from starting or turning normally, which can produce a false feedback fault.
- **Loose, damaged, or oxidized motor wiring or connectors (~20%)** Corrosion, heat damage, or loose pins at the motor plug or control-board connector interrupt the tacho signal path.
- **Mechanical binding or obstruction (~10%)** A seized bearing, foreign object, or drum drag prevents the tub from rotating freely, making the motor appear faulty.
- **Main control board fault (~5%)** The electronic control board fails to read a valid tacho signal even when the motor and wiring test correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** at the wall outlet or circuit breaker and confirm the machine is fully isolated before opening any panels.
2. **Check for mechanical binding** by rotating the drum by hand with the machine empty. Look for any obstruction, foreign object, or rough bearing noise that would prevent free rotation.
3. **Access the motor** by removing the rear or bottom panel (consult your model's service manual) and locate the motor harness connector at the motor and at the main control board.
4. **Inspect both connectors** for corrosion, bent pins, heat damage, or looseness. Clean with contact cleaner and reseat firmly if needed.
5. **Test the motor tacho and windings** with a multimeter set to resistance (ohms). One Electrolux repair video reports tacho readings of 171–196 ohms and motor winding readings of 2.5–3.5 and 1–2.5 ohms on certain terminals, but consult your model's technical sheet for correct values because these are not universal.
6. **Test continuity through the harness** from the motor plug to the control-board plug to confirm the wiring path is intact and matches the motor readings.
7. **Replace the motor assembly** if the tacho or motor windings are out of spec, or **replace the main control board** if the motor and wiring test correctly but the fault persists.
8. **Clear the fault** by cycling power, then run a test wash cycle and confirm stable motor start and feedback under load.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are not comfortable working with electrical components or using a multimeter to test motor windings and tacho circuits. The repair requires safe isolation of the machine, access to specific resistance values for your model, and the ability to distinguish between a motor fault, wiring fault, and control-board fault. A technician can test the tacho signal path, compare readings against factory specifications, and replace only the failed component. Also call a pro if the drum does not turn freely, because bearing or drive-system work often requires specialized tools and disassembly.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200–450 depending on whether the motor, control board, or wiring is at fault.
