## Maytag Washer Won't Start — What's Happening

When a Maytag washer won't start, the machine is being blocked from entering a cycle by one of the basic start conditions. The manufacturer-documented first checks are Control Lock being on, the lid or door not fully closed and latched, not holding the Start button long enough, or a control that needs a reset by unplugging for five minutes.

Because "won't start" is a symptom rather than a single fault code, the exact meaning depends on the model and whether the machine is top-load, front-load, or an older mechanical design. If you have a model number or an actual displayed code, the diagnosis can be narrowed to the exact fault definition.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Control Lock enabled** The controls will not respond to any touch commands when the lock feature is active.
- **Lid or door not fully closed or latched** The washer will not operate until the lid is closed, and some models also require the lid lock to engage before starting.
- **Start button not held long enough** Some models require holding Start for up to three seconds rather than a quick tap.
- **Control glitch needing a reset** Maytag recommends unplugging the unit for five minutes and plugging it back in to clear a frozen control.
- **No incoming power or tripped breaker** Field troubleshooting shows the outlet, breaker, or fuse may have failed, cutting power to the washer.
- **Lid switch or lid lock assembly failure** A faulty safety interlock prevents the machine from accepting a start command even when the lid appears closed.
- **Wire harness damage or corroded contacts** Field reports show some units are restored by cleaning oxidized user-interface contacts or repairing harness damage at connectors.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify supply power at the outlet with a tester or plug in another appliance, then check the breaker or fuse if needed.
2. Confirm the washer is fully plugged in and inspect the power cord and plug prongs for damage or bent pins.
3. Check if Control Lock is on (usually shown by an indicator light or icon) and turn it off per your model's instructions.
4. Close the lid or door firmly and listen or feel for a positive latch or lock action.
5. Press and hold the Start button for up to three seconds rather than tapping it once.
6. Unplug the washer for five minutes to reset the control, then plug it back in and retry.
7. If still dead, enter diagnostics mode if your model supports it and test the lid switch or door lock, user-interface-to-main-board communication, and wire harness continuity.
8. If tests point to a failed component, replace that part rather than the control board first, since field sources describe the control board as rarely at fault.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have verified power at the outlet, turned off Control Lock, latched the lid, held Start for three seconds, and reset the control by unplugging for five minutes but the washer still will not respond. A pro can enter diagnostics mode, measure continuity and voltage at the lid switch or lock assembly, and trace harness or control-board faults that require disassembly and meter work.
