## Frigidaire Dryer AL-15 Error Code — What It Means

Frigidaire does not publish a clearly documented AL-15 error code in available manufacturer support material, so the exact meaning cannot be confirmed from official sources. Based on Frigidaire and Electrolux-family dryer code patterns, AL-15 is often a user transcription or display misread of a door or door-switch type alarm. If this is a door-related fault, it means the control does not detect the door as securely closed or the door-switch circuit is not being made.

If the code was misread or varies by model platform, the underlying fault family could involve the door switch, door strike or latch, thermal switch, heating circuit, or main control board relay. Always verify the exact code and model number from the product sticker before ordering parts, since code meanings can vary across Frigidaire platforms.

## Common Causes

- **Door not fully closed or obstructed** Lint buildup, trapped clothing, or failure to push the door firmly can prevent the latch from engaging and the switch from closing.
- **Worn, cracked, or misaligned door strike or latch** Physical damage or wear to the strike or latch hardware prevents a secure mechanical connection that activates the door switch.
- **Failed or intermittent door switch** The switch itself may no longer change state reliably when the door closes, breaking the circuit the control expects.
- **Door sag or alignment issue** A sagging or misaligned door creates uneven gaps and poor switch engagement, causing the fault to appear or clear unpredictably.
- **Loose wiring or connector at door switch or control** A good switch with a broken wire, loose pin, or corroded connector can mimic a failed switch to the control board.
- **Main control board input or relay fault** If the door circuit is mechanically and electrically sound but the fault persists, the control board input or relay logic may be at fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the exact code and model number** by reading the full display carefully and locating the model sticker on the dryer frame, since code meanings can vary by platform and a misread code leads to misdiagnosis.
2. **Disconnect power at the breaker or plug** before inspecting or testing any components to prevent shock or accidental start.
3. **Check the door mechanically** by opening and closing it firmly, listening for a positive latch click, and inspecting for trapped clothing, lint buildup around the strike, cracks in the latch, or loose mounting hardware.
4. **Inspect door alignment and sag** by looking for uneven gaps around the door perimeter, then gently lifting or pressing the door inward while closing to see if engagement improves, which points to hinge or strike adjustment needs.
5. **Test the door switch circuit with a multimeter** by locating the switch behind the door strike area, disconnecting its leads, and checking for a clean state change (open to closed) when the door closes, replacing the switch if it does not toggle consistently.
6. **Check wiring and connectors** at both the door switch and the control board for loose pins, broken or pinched conductors, and corrosion, since a good switch with bad wiring produces the same symptom as a failed switch.
7. **Reset and functional test** by removing power for several minutes, restoring power, closing the door firmly, and running a short timed-dry cycle to confirm the fault clears and the dryer starts reliably without stopping when the door is touched.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you are uncomfortable working with electrical circuits or removing panels to access the door switch and wiring. Also call if the door latch and switch test good, wiring is intact, and the fault persists after a power reset, since that points to a control board issue that requires model-specific diagnosis. If the dryer is still under warranty, contact Frigidaire or an authorized service center before replacing any parts to avoid voiding coverage.
