## Daikin UH Error Code — What It Means

The UH code on Daikin heat pumps indicates a system malfunction caused by improper connection of the transmission wiring. This is not a sensor or refrigeration fault. The unit is unable to establish proper communication between the indoor and outdoor units, or between multiple heads in a multi-split system, because the wiring topology or signal does not match what the system expects.

In practical terms, the outdoor unit and indoor unit(s) cannot talk to each other correctly. This is almost always a wiring issue at the communication terminals, not a failed component. The system is designed to shut down and throw UH when it detects that the transmission circuit is miswired, reversed, or incomplete.

## Common Causes

- **Miswired transmission circuit** Communication wires between indoor and outdoor units are landed on the wrong terminals or crossed.
- **Reversed or swapped comm connections** Polarity or indoor unit order is incorrect on multi-split or VRV systems that require specific sequence.
- **Loose or disconnected transmission wiring** Terminals are not tight, wires have backed out, or connections are intermittent due to vibration or installation error.
- **Damaged transmission cable** The low-voltage communication wire has been cut, pinched, or shorted during installation or service.
- **Optional controller wiring fault** If a centralized control or remote interface is installed, its transmission wiring may be landed incorrectly.
- **System wiring test not performed after install** Multi-head systems may need a wiring auto-correction routine run to learn indoor unit order and confirm topology.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power down the system** at the breaker and wait 60 seconds before working on any terminals.
2. **Locate the wiring diagram** for your exact Daikin model on the unit label or in the installation manual, and identify the transmission terminals (often labeled F1/F2, P1/P2, or S1/S2).
3. **Inspect all transmission wire connections** at the outdoor unit, indoor unit(s), and any optional controllers, verifying correct terminal landing, tight screw connections, and no crossed or reversed wires.
4. **Check indoor unit order and addressing** if you have a multi-split or VRV system, and correct any out-of-sequence or duplicate address settings per the installation guide.
5. **Verify continuity** of the transmission wiring with a multimeter from outdoor to indoor, looking for open circuits, shorts to ground, or shorts between comm wires.
6. **Restore power and run the system wiring test** or auto-address mode if your model supports it, following the service manual procedure to let the system learn the correct indoor unit configuration.
7. **Reset the system** by cycling power off and on, then confirm all indoor units are recognized and the UH code clears, and verify normal heating or cooling operation.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with low-voltage wiring or do not have access to the wiring diagram for your specific Daikin model. Also call a pro if you have corrected all visible wiring errors and verified continuity but the UH code persists, or if your system is a multi-split or VRV installation that requires a wiring test mode you cannot access. Communication faults can be model-specific and may require factory software or test equipment to diagnose control board issues downstream of the wiring itself.
