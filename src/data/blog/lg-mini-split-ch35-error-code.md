## LG Mini Split CH35 Error Code — What It Means

The CH35 error on your LG mini split or Multi V system indicates that the low-pressure sensor has detected an excessive pressure drop below 1 bar. This fault signals that refrigerant pressure on the suction side of the compressor has fallen too low for safe operation. The system shuts down to protect the compressor from damage. In most cases, this points to either a real loss of refrigerant or a sensor that is misreading normal pressure.

## Common Causes

- **Service valve closed or restricted** If the liquid or suction service valve on the outdoor unit is not fully open, refrigerant flow is choked and pressure drops rapidly.
- **Low refrigerant charge or refrigerant leak** A leak at a brazed joint, flare connection, or elsewhere in the piping allows refrigerant to escape, lowering system pressure below the sensor threshold.
- **Deformed or blocked refrigerant piping** Kinked, crushed, or internally restricted lineset tubing prevents normal refrigerant circulation and causes abnormal pressure readings.
- **Faulty low-pressure sensor** The sensor itself may be sending an incorrect signal to the control board even when actual system pressures are normal.
- **Electronic expansion valve (EEV) malfunction** An EEV stuck closed or not stepping properly restricts refrigerant flow and can trigger a low-pressure fault on the affected circuit.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Confirm both service valves are fully open** at the outdoor unit by turning the valve stems counterclockwise until they stop.
2. **Attach a manifold gauge set** to the service ports and compare suction and discharge pressures to the operating data in your model's service manual.
3. **Inspect all brazed joints, flare fittings, and lineset connections** for signs of refrigerant leaks such as oil staining, residue, or bubbles when using leak detector solution.
4. **Check the low-pressure sensor circuit** by measuring the sensor supply voltage at the outdoor PCB connector, which should read 5 V according to LG service documentation.
5. **Test the electronic expansion valves** on both indoor and outdoor units for proper stepping and movement, and listen for the characteristic clicking during operation.
6. **Replace the low-pressure sensor** if gauge readings are normal but the code persists, as this indicates the sensor is sending a false low-pressure signal.
7. **Power-cycle the system** after any repair by turning off the circuit breaker for two minutes, then restore power and monitor pressures and operation for at least one full cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not EPA-certified to handle refrigerant or if you do not own a manifold gauge set and leak detector. Diagnosing CH35 requires measuring live system pressures, testing sensor circuits with a multimeter, and potentially recovering and recharging refrigerant. If you find a leak, the repair often involves brazing or replacing piping, which requires specialized tools and skills. A pro can also access LG service data tables specific to your model and refrigerant type, compare readings accurately, and make sure the system is charged to the correct subcool or superheat target after repair.
