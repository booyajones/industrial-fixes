## LG Dishwasher bE Error Code — What It Means

The bE code on an LG dishwasher stands for bubble error. The unit has detected excessive suds or foam inside the tub and has shut down or prevented operation to avoid leakage. This is not a mechanical failure but a protective response to a condition that can cause water to overflow or leak.

The code appears most often when the wrong type of detergent has been used (regular hand soap or liquid dish detergent instead of automatic dishwasher detergent) or when too much dishwasher detergent was added. LG also notes that some eco-friendly or low-foam formulations can still create excess bubbles under certain load conditions. The code may appear sporadically depending on how dirty the dishes are. A secondary field factor is an unlevel dishwasher, which can worsen suds behavior and increase leakage risk, though the authoritative cause remains foam-related.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes suspect a control board or sensor fault and replace those parts, but bE is almost always a detergent-type issue. Before replacing any component, verify the detergent in use and run an empty cycle with no detergent to confirm whether foam clears.

## Common Causes

- **Wrong detergent type (~60%)** Using hand soap, liquid dishwashing detergent, or other non-dishwasher products creates excessive suds that trigger the bubble-error sensor.
- **Excessive detergent amount (~20%)** Even with the correct product, using too much automatic dishwasher detergent can produce enough foam to trip the bE code.
- **Eco-friendly or low-foam formulation behavior (~10%)** Some green or low-suds detergents still generate excess bubbles under heavy soil or certain water conditions, causing sporadic bE alerts.
- **Unlevel dishwasher installation (~8%)** A unit that is not level can worsen suds movement and increase the risk of foam detection and leakage, amplifying detergent-related faults.
- **Residual foam from previous cycles (~2%)** If the wrong detergent was used recently, leftover suds can persist through several cycles and continue triggering the error.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Verify the detergent in use.** Check the product packaging to confirm it is labeled as automatic dishwasher detergent, not hand dish soap or general liquid detergent.
2. **Run the dishwasher empty** with no detergent at all to determine whether foam persists from a previous cycle.
3. **Clear residual foam** if bE returns on the empty cycle. Set the rinse-aid dispenser to L0 (lowest or off), place 200 to 300 mL of milk in a bowl on the upper rack, and run the Normal wash cycle with the detergent dispenser empty. LG states that milk helps suppress remaining foam.
4. **Restore the rinse-aid setting** to your normal level after the foam-clearing cycle finishes.
5. **Check and level the dishwasher** if bE persists. Place a spirit level on the door sill or rack rails and adjust the front and rear leveling feet until the unit is level in both directions.
6. **Switch to powder or solid dishwasher detergent** or reduce the amount you use if the error occurs only occasionally with liquid or gel products.
7. **Test with a normal load** using the correct detergent type and a reduced amount. If bE does not return, the repair is complete.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the bE code persists after you have confirmed correct detergent type, cleared all foam with the milk procedure, leveled the dishwasher, and reduced detergent quantity. A technician will inspect for installation issues that may contribute to suds behavior or leakage risk and verify that no deeper mechanical or electronic fault is present. Professional service is also warranted if you are uncomfortable adjusting leveling feet or if the dishwasher shows signs of leakage or other error codes in addition to bE.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-15 (detergent correction), 1-2 hours to clear foam. A pro service call runs about $100-200 service call if installation leveling is required.
