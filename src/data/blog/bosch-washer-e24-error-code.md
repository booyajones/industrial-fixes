## Bosch E24 Error Code — What It Means

The E24 code on a Bosch dishwasher signals a drainage fault. The machine has detected that water is not evacuating properly, almost always because something is blocking the drain path. Bosch points to the filter and pump area first, then the drain hose, siphon connection, pump cover, or garbage-disposal connection as the typical culprits.

You will usually see water left standing in the tub after a cycle, the cycle pausing or stopping mid-program, and sometimes a gurgling or humming sound when the dishwasher tries to drain. The code appears because flow through the drain system is restricted or completely blocked.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the drain pump when the real problem is debris blocking the filter or impeller. Pull and clean the filter first, then check the pump cover and impeller for blockage before buying a new pump.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked drain filter (~50%)** Food debris, broken glass, or other material trapped in the filter assembly prevents water from reaching the pump.
- **Kinked or clogged drain hose (~20%)** A pinched, crushed, or internally blocked hose prevents water from leaving the machine even when the pump runs.
- **Loose or improperly seated pump cover (~15%)** When the cover under the filter is not fully locked in place, water cannot enter the pump chamber and the machine flags a drain fault.
- **Debris in the pump sump or impeller (~10%)** Small objects or sticky residue can jam the impeller so it cannot spin or move water through the drain line.
- **Garbage disposal knockout plug not removed (~5%)** If the dishwasher drains into a new disposal and the dishwasher inlet plug was not knocked out during installation, the drain path is completely sealed.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the breaker or unplug the dishwasher so you can safely open the tub and access the drain components.
2. **Remove the lower spray arm** by lifting or twisting it off its mount, then take out the cylindrical filter assembly at the bottom of the tub.
3. **Clean the filter** under running water and inspect the mesh and screen for tears or clogs, then check that the pump cover below the filter is seated fully and not loose.
4. **Inspect the pump sump** for debris around the impeller, removing any small objects, shards, or sticky buildup that could prevent the impeller from turning.
5. **Check the drain hose** where it exits the machine and runs to the sink or disposal, looking for kinks, pinches, or blockages, and verify the high loop or air gap is correctly installed.
6. **Verify the disposal connection** if your dishwasher drains into a garbage disposal, making sure the dishwasher inlet knockout plug has been removed and the port is open.
7. **Restore power and run a drain test** using the model's reset or rinse cycle to confirm water evacuates completely and the E24 code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if you have cleaned the filter, checked the pump cover and impeller, inspected the entire drain hose, and verified the disposal or air-gap connection but the E24 code returns and water still will not drain. A pro can test the drain pump electrically, check for a wiring fault in the control board, or diagnose a less common issue such as a failed drain-pump motor or a board that is not sending the drain signal. Also call if you are not comfortable working inside the tub or disconnecting plumbing under the sink.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $0-50 in parts if a hose or filter needs replacing, 20-45 min. A pro service call runs about $150-250 for a service call and cleaning or pump replacement.
