## Fujitsu Mini Split E:05 Error — What It Means

The E:05 error code on Fujitsu mini split systems indicates a problem with the indoor unit's temperature sensor. Depending on your model family, this can mean either an open indoor pipe sensor or a short in the indoor heat exchanger temperature sensor. The exact definition is model-dependent, so you must check your service manual to confirm which sensor circuit is being monitored. In either case, the indoor control board has detected that the thermistor is not delivering a normal resistance reading, either because the sensor itself has failed, the wiring is damaged, or the connection is loose.

## Common Causes

- **Unplugged or loose sensor connector** The thermistor connector at the indoor unit has worked loose from vibration or was not fully seated during installation or prior service.
- **Open or shorted thermistor** The sensor itself has failed internally, reading infinite resistance (open) or zero resistance (short), and can no longer report temperature accurately.
- **Damaged or corroded sensor harness** Wiring between the sensor and the indoor PCB has been cut, pinched, chewed by rodents, or corroded at the terminals, breaking the circuit.
- **Sensor out of position on the coil or pipe** The thermistor has slipped out of its mounting clip or lost thermal contact with the pipe or heat exchanger, causing erratic or out-of-range readings.
- **Defective indoor controller PCB** If the sensor and all wiring test normal, the input circuit on the indoor control board may have failed and cannot read the sensor correctly.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Power off the mini split** at the disconnect or circuit breaker and wait two minutes for capacitors to discharge before opening the indoor unit cover.
2. **Locate the indoor pipe or heat exchanger sensor** by following the thin two-wire thermistor lead from the indoor PCB to the coil or refrigerant line, and inspect the connector for full seating, corrosion, or bent pins.
3. **Disconnect the sensor plug** and use a multimeter set to ohms to measure resistance across the thermistor leads, then compare the reading to the resistance chart in your model's service manual (if the sensor reads infinite ohms, it is open; if near zero, it is shorted).
4. **Inspect the sensor harness** along its entire length for cuts, pinch points, rodent damage, or frayed insulation, and check that the thermistor is firmly clipped to the pipe or coil with good thermal contact.
5. **Replace the thermistor** if it reads out of range or the sensor body is damaged, making sure the new sensor is installed in the correct position and the connector clicks fully into place.
6. **Test or replace the indoor control PCB** if the sensor and wiring both test normal but the E:05 code returns immediately after a power reset, following your service manual's board diagnostic procedure.
7. **Restore power and run a test cycle** in cooling or heating mode, then confirm the error code clears and the unit operates normally without tripping the fault again.

## When to Call a Pro

If you are not comfortable working with live electrical components or do not own a multimeter and resistance charts for your model, call a licensed HVAC technician. Sensor faults can be straightforward, but misdiagnosis can lead to unnecessary part replacements or further damage to the control board. A professional can quickly confirm whether the issue is the thermistor, wiring, or PCB, and many Fujitsu warranties require authorized service for board-level repairs. If the error code returns after you have replaced the sensor and checked all wiring, a technician with factory diagnostic tools should inspect the indoor controller and verify proper refrigerant operation.
