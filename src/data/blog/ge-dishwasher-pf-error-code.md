## GE Dishwasher PF Error Code — What It Means

The PF code on a GE dishwasher indicates the control detected a loss or interruption of electrical power during operation. After power is restored, the machine enters a fault state and displays PF to alert you that the cycle was interrupted. This is a diagnostic message, not necessarily a component failure. The code can appear after a utility outage, a tripped circuit breaker, or any event that cuts power to the dishwasher, even momentarily.

## Common Causes

- **Utility power interruption** A brief outage or voltage dip from the grid will trigger the PF code when power returns.
- **Tripped circuit breaker** The dishwasher's dedicated breaker may have tripped due to overload or a fault on the branch circuit.
- **Loose power connection** A poor connection at the junction box, cord plug, or supply wiring can cause intermittent power loss.
- **Door latch or switch fault** A faulty door switch or latch can open the control circuit intermittently, mimicking a power failure.
- **Control board or power board failure** Corrosion, damaged solder joints, or failed components on the electronic control can cause false PF detection.
- **Shared circuit voltage drop** Another high-draw appliance on the same circuit can pull voltage down enough to interrupt dishwasher operation.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Check your home** for any signs of a recent power outage or other appliances that lost power at the same time.
2. **Press Start/Cancel** (or the equivalent reset sequence for your model) to clear the PF code and attempt to restart the cycle.
3. **Inspect the circuit breaker** at your panel and confirm it has not tripped. Reset it if necessary and verify the dishwasher is on a dedicated 15 or 20 amp branch circuit.
4. **Turn off power** at the breaker, then open the dishwasher's junction box or cord connection under the sink and check for loose, corroded, or heat-damaged wire connections. Tighten and clean as needed.
5. **Test the door latch and door switch** by opening and closing the door several times while listening for a firm click. Inspect the latch strike and switch wiring harness for damage or intermittent contact.
6. **Examine the control board** (with power off) for visible corrosion, burned traces, or loose solder around fuse clips and connector pins. Look especially at the power supply section and any thermal fuse if equipped.
7. **Replace the failed component** only after confirming the fault. If the PF returns with verified stable power and good connections, the electronic control or user interface board is the most likely culprit.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if the PF code returns repeatedly after you have verified stable incoming power and all visible connections are tight and clean. Diagnosing intermittent power faults and testing control board circuits under load requires a multimeter and familiarity with live voltage work. If you are uncomfortable working inside an energized junction box or removing the control board for inspection, a qualified appliance technician can isolate the fault path safely and confirm whether the board, door switch, or supply wiring is at fault.
