## LG Microwave F-5 Error Code — What It Means

The F-5 error code on an LG microwave indicates a damper switch error. The damper switch is part of the feedback circuit that monitors the position or operation of the air damper inside the microwave cavity. When the control board does not receive the expected signal from this switch, it throws the F-5 code and halts operation.

LG's official guidance states that this condition requires professional service to resolve. The fault is tied specifically to the damper switch circuit, not a door latch or generic sensor issue. Because LG does not publish detailed consumer-level troubleshooting for this code, repair involves inspecting the damper assembly, testing the switch and wiring, and replacing failed components.

## Common Causes

- **Failed damper switch** The switch itself has failed internally and no longer closes or opens its contacts properly, preventing the control board from reading correct damper position.
- **Broken or disconnected wiring harness** The wires or connector between the damper switch and the control board have become loose, corroded, or broken, interrupting the feedback signal.
- **Binding or misaligned damper mechanism** The physical damper blade or linkage is stuck, bent, or obstructed, preventing the switch from actuating even though the switch itself is good.
- **Door closure interference** Packing material, debris, or a misaligned door prevents the microwave from closing fully, which can interfere with normal damper operation and switch feedback.
- **Control board input fault** The main control board has a failed input circuit for the damper switch, so even a working switch cannot send a valid signal.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Disconnect power** at the breaker or unplug the microwave completely before opening any panels or touching internal components.
2. **Inspect the door** for packing material, debris, or anything preventing full closure, and verify the door closes firmly with no obstructions.
3. **Remove the outer cabinet** (usually rear screws and side tabs) to access the damper assembly and control board, following your model's service manual for panel removal.
4. **Locate the damper switch** near the air duct or cavity opening, trace its wiring back to the connector, and check for loose pins, corrosion, or obvious breaks in the harness.
5. **Test the damper switch for continuity** using a multimeter with the damper actuated and released, replacing the switch if it does not open and close as expected.
6. **Check the damper mechanism** for binding, debris, or broken linkage, and free or repair any mechanical obstruction that prevents smooth travel.
7. **Reconnect or replace the wiring harness** if connectors are damaged, then reassemble the cabinet, restore power, and run a test cycle to confirm the F-5 code clears.

## When to Call a Pro

LG states that the F-5 damper switch error requires service, and the repair involves disassembling the microwave cabinet and working near high-voltage components (even when unplugged, the capacitor can hold a dangerous charge). If you are not comfortable discharging the high-voltage capacitor, tracing low-voltage wiring, or using a multimeter to test switches, call a qualified appliance technician. A pro will have the service manual, correct replacement parts, and tools to safely diagnose the damper circuit and replace the switch, harness, or control board as needed.
