## Navien E241 Error Code — What It Means

The E241 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically signals an issue related to internal circulation or a sensor reading that falls outside normal operating range. Because Navien uses different diagnostic codes across model lines, the exact meaning of E241 can vary. Consult your owner's manual or the wiring diagram inside the unit to confirm what E241 represents for your specific model.

In general, circulation-related codes point to a problem with the internal recirculation pump, a flow sensor reporting abnormal readings, wiring connections that have come loose, or a control board that is not receiving the expected signals. The unit halts operation as a safety measure until the fault is corrected.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the flow sensor or control board when the real problem is a failed circulation pump or a loose connector. Check power to the pump and inspect all wire harnesses before ordering sensors or electronics.

## Common Causes

- **Circulation pump stopped or jammed (~40%)** The internal recirculation pump may have seized due to scale buildup, a worn bearing, or electrical failure, preventing flow through the heat exchanger.
- **Loose or corroded wiring harness (~25%)** Connectors between the control board, pump, and sensors can develop corrosion or vibrate loose over time, interrupting the signal path.
- **Flow sensor fault or drift (~20%)** A flow sensor that has accumulated debris or suffered electronic drift will report incorrect flow rates, triggering a safety lockout.
- **Control board logic error (~10%)** The main control board may misinterpret sensor inputs after a power surge or firmware glitch, displaying a false code.
- **Low voltage or weak power supply (~5%)** Insufficient incoming voltage can prevent the pump from spinning at full speed, causing the controller to flag a circulation problem.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and close the gas shutoff valve to make the unit safe to inspect.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the panel fasteners and carefully setting the cover aside.
3. **Locate the circulation pump** inside the unit and listen for any hum or vibration when you briefly restore power.
4. **Inspect all wire harnesses** for loose connectors, bent pins, or green corrosion, paying special attention to the pump and flow sensor leads.
5. **Check the inlet filter screens** on both the cold-water supply and any recirculation lines for debris that could restrict flow.
6. **Perform a power reset** by leaving the breaker off for 30 seconds, then restoring power and observing whether the code returns immediately or after a heat call.
7. **Consult your model's service manual** or wiring diagram to confirm the exact meaning of E241, then call a licensed technician if the code persists after basic checks.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional whenever the error persists after you have reset the unit, inspected connectors, and verified that inlet filters are clean. Diagnosing a circulation pump requires measuring electrical signals and water flow under operating conditions, and replacing the pump involves draining the heat exchanger, disconnecting gas and water lines, and verifying proper combustion after reassembly. Navien tankless heaters are gas appliances that demand careful attention to venting, combustion air, and pressure settings. A licensed technician has the training and test equipment to pinpoint sensor drift, board faults, and pump performance issues without guessing, and will warranty the repair.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $200-450.
