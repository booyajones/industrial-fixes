## Navien E328 Error Code — What It Means

The E328 error code on a Navien tankless water heater typically signals an ignition or flame sensing issue. The unit is unable to establish or maintain a stable flame during the startup sequence or while heating water. This can result from problems with gas supply, venting restrictions, or faulty ignition components.

Because tankless heaters require precise air-fuel ratios and proper exhaust flow, even minor blockages or supply irregularities can trigger this code. The heater's control board monitors flame presence through a sensor, and if the signal is weak or absent, it shuts down and displays the error to protect the system.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the igniter or flame rod first, but many E328 codes are caused by venting issues or low gas pressure. Check that vents are clear and the gas shutoff is fully open before replacing any sensors or igniters.

## Common Causes

- **Blocked or restricted vents (~35%)** Debris, bird nests, ice, or condensation buildup in the air intake or exhaust pipes can starve the combustion chamber of oxygen or prevent exhaust gases from venting, causing ignition failure.
- **Insufficient gas supply or pressure (~25%)** A partially closed gas valve, regulator problem, or inadequate line pressure prevents the burner from receiving enough fuel to ignite or stay lit.
- **Dirty or corroded flame rod (~20%)** Carbon deposits or corrosion on the flame sensor prevent it from detecting the flame signal, even when the burner ignites successfully.
- **Faulty igniter (~10%)** A worn or cracked hot-surface igniter may not reach the temperature needed to ignite the gas-air mixture reliably.
- **Control board or wiring fault (~7%)** Loose connections, damaged wires, or a failing control board can interrupt the ignition sequence or flame sensing circuit.
- **Improper venting installation (~3%)** Vent pipes that are too long, improperly pitched, or not meeting manufacturer specifications can cause draft issues and ignition problems.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** to the water heater at the circuit breaker or unplug the unit to make sure safe handling.
2. **Confirm the gas supply** by checking that the gas shutoff valve (usually a lever or knob on the gas line near the heater) is turned fully to the open position.
3. **Inspect the intake and exhaust vents** outside your home for blockages such as leaves, bird nests, snow, or ice. Remove any obstructions carefully.
4. **Check vent pipe runs** inside for proper pitch, secure connections, and signs of condensation pooling or disconnected sections. Consult your installation manual for correct pipe length and configuration.
5. **Power-cycle the heater** by restoring power and waiting two minutes, then turning the unit back on. Observe whether the error reappears.
6. **If the error persists**, call a licensed technician to measure gas pressure at the unit, clean or replace the flame rod, test the igniter, and verify control board operation.
7. **Document the error history** by noting how often the code appears, weather conditions, and any recent work on the heater or gas lines to help the technician diagnose the issue.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or HVAC technician trained on Navien equipment if free checks do not resolve the E328 code. Gas-fired appliances require precise adjustments to pressure, combustion air, and venting that only qualified professionals should perform. A technician will measure inlet gas pressure, inspect the heat exchanger for soot or scale, test the flame sensor and igniter with a multimeter, and verify that vent pipes meet code. Any work involving gas connections, pressure regulators, or burner assembly should always be handled by a pro to make sure safety and warranty compliance.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
