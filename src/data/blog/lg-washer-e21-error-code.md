## LG Washer E21 Error Code — What It Means

The E21 error on an LG washer signals a drain fault. The machine detects that water is not leaving the tub within the expected time frame. This is not a door lock, motor, or fill problem. It means something is blocking or restricting the drain path, preventing the pump from evacuating water properly. The washer stops the cycle and shows E21 to protect the pump and the tub seal.

The code appears most often during the drain or spin phase when the pump should be running. Water may remain visible in the drum, or the cycle may stop partway through. The underlying issue is almost always mechanical: debris in the filter, a kinked hose, a blocked house drain, or a failing pump.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain pump filter** Lint, coins, hair ties, and small debris collect in the filter or coin trap and block water flow out of the tub.
- **Kinked or blocked drain hose** The drain hose may be crushed, routed too high, or obstructed internally, preventing the pump from pushing water out.
- **Restricted house drain or standpipe** A slow or backed-up household drain causes water to return to the washer instead of flowing away.
- **Faulty or jammed drain pump** The pump impeller may be seized by debris, the motor may have failed, or mechanical damage prevents the pump from moving water.
- **Electrical or sensor fault** Broken wiring, loose connectors, or a control-board issue may stop the pump from receiving power or signal even when the drain path is clear.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Cancel the cycle and disconnect power** by unplugging the washer or switching off the breaker to make sure safe access to internal components.
2. **Locate and clean the drain filter.** Open the access panel at the lower front, place towels underneath, unscrew the filter cap slowly to let trapped water drain into a shallow pan, then remove and rinse the filter under running water to clear lint and debris.
3. **Inspect the drain hose** from the pump outlet to the standpipe or laundry sink. Straighten any kinks, verify the hose is not inserted too far into the standpipe, and check for internal blockages by disconnecting and flushing with water.
4. **Check the house drain** by pouring a bucket of water into the standpipe or sink. If it backs up or drains slowly, clear the household plumbing before restarting the washer.
5. **Access the drain pump** by tipping the washer or removing the lower panel. Turn the impeller by hand to confirm it spins freely, then reconnect power and run a drain or spin cycle to verify the pump motor runs and moves water.
6. **Test pump power** with a multimeter if the pump does not run. Check for voltage at the pump terminals during a drain cycle and inspect the wiring harness and connectors for damage or corrosion.
7. **Run a drain/spin test cycle** after each repair step to confirm the error clears and water evacuates normally. If E21 persists after mechanical and electrical checks, suspect the main control board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you have cleared the filter, verified the hose and house drain are open, confirmed the pump runs and the impeller turns freely, yet the E21 code still appears. Persistent errors after mechanical checks often point to a control-board fault, a pressure-sensor issue, or internal wiring damage that requires diagnostic tools and schematic knowledge. Also call for help if you are uncomfortable tipping the washer, accessing internal components, or working with live electrical circuits during testing.
