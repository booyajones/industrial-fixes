## LG Washer E21 Error Code — What It Means

E21 is not listed in LG's official US error code documentation, which uses codes like dE (door lock failure) and LE (motor overload). The exact meaning of E21 depends on your specific model and region. If your owner's manual or control panel shows E21, it likely points to a drain-related problem, since many appliance platforms use E21 to flag restricted or failed draining. Water remains in the tub after the cycle, or the washer stops mid-cycle without completing the spin.

Without manufacturer confirmation for LG, treat E21 as a possible drain fault and follow the basic LG drain troubleshooting steps: unplug the washer, check for standing water, inspect the drain filter for clogs, and verify that the drain hose is not kinked or blocked. Consult your model's service manual or contact LG support with your full model number to confirm the exact definition and recommended repair procedure.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners sometimes replace the drain pump first when the real culprit is a clogged filter or kinked hose. Clean the filter and inspect the entire drain path before ordering a new pump.

## Common Causes

- **Clogged drain filter or lint trap (~40%)** Lint, coins, and small debris accumulate in the filter and block water flow out of the tub.
- **Kinked or crushed drain hose (~25%)** The rubber drain hose is pinched behind the washer or routed too sharply, restricting flow.
- **Foreign object in drain pump impeller (~15%)** A sock, hairpin, or other item jams the impeller and prevents the pump from moving water.
- **Failed drain pump (~12%)** The pump motor coil is open, the impeller is cracked, or the pump housing is damaged.
- **Household plumbing backup (~5%)** A clogged standpipe, sink trap, or building drain prevents water from leaving the washer.
- **Electrical or control board fault (~3%)** A loose connector or failed relay prevents the pump from energizing during the drain cycle.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the washer** and turn off the water supply valves. Move the washer forward if needed to access the rear drain hose.
2. **Drain standing water manually** by placing the drain hose below the tub level into a shallow pan, or use the emergency drain tube (if equipped) located near the filter access.
3. **Locate and remove the drain filter**, typically behind a small access door at the front lower corner. Place towels underneath and twist the filter counter-clockwise to pull it out.
4. **Clean the filter thoroughly** under running water, removing all lint, coins, and debris. Inspect the filter housing and pump inlet for additional obstructions.
5. **Inspect the drain hose** from the pump outlet to the standpipe or sink connection. Straighten any kinks, clear blockages with a plumber's snake if needed, and confirm proper routing with no sags.
6. **Check the drain pump impeller** by reaching into the filter cavity (with the filter removed) and rotating the impeller by hand. It should spin freely without grinding or catching.
7. **Reinstall the filter** and hose, restore power, and run a drain or spin cycle to verify normal operation. If the code persists or the pump does not energize, replace the drain pump assembly or contact a technician for electrical diagnosis.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you have cleaned the filter and hose but the washer still will not drain, if the drain pump makes abnormal grinding or buzzing noises, or if you are uncomfortable disassembling the pump or testing electrical connections. A technician can verify pump voltage, test the control board drain relay, and replace the pump assembly safely. Also seek help if your model uses a non-standard drain system or if the error code definition is unclear in your owner's manual. Professional diagnosis ensures you replace only the failed component and avoids unnecessary part costs.

**Rough cost:** DIY runs about $15–50 in parts (hose or filter housing if damaged), 30–60 min. A pro service call runs about $120–250 including service call and pump replacement if needed.
