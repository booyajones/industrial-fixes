## Frigidaire Oven Won't Turn On — What's Happening

When your Frigidaire oven won't turn on, you're dealing with a loss of power somewhere in the chain from the wall to the heating elements or igniters. This isn't a single error code. It's a functional failure that means either no electricity is reaching the oven, a safety device has opened the circuit, or the control system isn't energizing the appliance. The exact behavior matters: some units show no lights or display at all, while others light up but produce no heat.

The problem usually sits in one of three areas. First is incoming power, especially on electric ranges that need a full 240 volts on two separate legs. Second is internal protection devices like thermal fuses that open during surges or overheating. Third is the control board or touchpad that distributes voltage to bake and broil circuits. Gas models add igniter and valve circuits to the list. Loose or burnt wiring can show up anywhere along the path.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Tripped breaker or partial power loss** Electric ranges need full 240V supply, and losing one leg of power will leave the oven dead or erratic while the cooktop may still partially work.
- **Blown thermal fuse** Many built-in and freestanding models use an internal thermal fuse that opens permanently during surges or overheating, cutting all power to the oven.
- **Failed main control board** The control board's relays distribute voltage to heating circuits, and relay or component failure on the board will prevent the oven from energizing.
- **Defective touchpad or control panel** A failed touchpad prevents the user interface from signaling the control board to turn on, leaving the oven unresponsive even when power is present.
- **Loose, burnt, or damaged wiring** Connections at the terminal block, control board, or component harnesses can loosen, corrode, or burn through, breaking the power path.
- **Control Lock or Demo Mode enabled** User-lock features or Demo Mode can disable the oven entirely, making it appear dead until the setting is cleared.
- **Weak or failed igniter (gas models)** On gas ovens, a weak igniter won't draw enough current to open the safety valve, so the oven powers on but produces no heat or flame.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the symptom by noting whether the display, lights, and cooktop (if applicable) are working, or if the entire unit is dead.
2. Check that the oven is plugged in, the outlet is live, and the circuit breaker has not tripped or partially tripped.
3. Test the outlet voltage with a multimeter, confirming full 240V is present on both legs for electric ranges.
4. Disable Control Lock (usually by holding a Lock or Cancel button for three seconds) and verify Demo Mode is off per your owner's manual.
5. Disconnect power, open the rear or top control panel, and inspect all wiring harnesses and connectors for burn marks, corrosion, or loose terminals.
6. Locate and test the thermal fuse (often near the control board or on the rear panel) for continuity, replacing it if open.
7. Test the main control board by checking for incoming line voltage and outgoing switched voltage to bake and broil circuits when a cycle is selected.
8. For gas ovens that power on but do not heat, observe the igniter during a bake cycle and have a qualified technician test and replace the igniter if it glows weakly or not at all.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you've verified power at the outlet and checked the breaker but still have no display or response. Control board and touchpad diagnosis requires safe handling of live 240V circuits and model-specific board layouts. For gas ovens, any work involving the igniter, gas valve, or burner assembly should be performed by a professional due to the risk of gas leaks and incomplete combustion. If you see burnt wiring, smell burning plastic, or find repeated thermal-fuse failures, stop and call for service to prevent fire hazards or further damage. For gas line, burner, or igniter work, or if you ever smell gas, stop and call a licensed technician.
