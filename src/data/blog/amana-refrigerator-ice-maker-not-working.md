## Amana Refrigerator Ice Maker Not Working — What's Happening

When an Amana ice maker stops working, the refrigerator either produces no ice at all or makes ice but won't dump it into the bin. Amana treats this as a troubleshooting problem rather than a single fault code. The issue may involve the ice maker being switched off, a temporary control glitch needing a reset, restricted water supply, a warm freezer, or a physical jam in the ejector arm or dispenser chute.

Amana separates true no-ice conditions from cases where ice forms but doesn't eject. If the unit is making ice but not releasing it, a reset is the first recommended fix. Problems on newly installed units often trace to door alignment, cabinet interference, or loose wiring and tubing connections at the freezer door hinge.

## Most Likely Causes

- **Ice maker switched off** The shutoff arm or control switch was moved to the off position accidentally during cleaning or bin removal.
- **Ice maker needs reset** A minor control fault or power interruption halted the cycle and requires a manual reset or power cycle to resume production.
- **Water supply issue** The refrigerator is not connected to a cold water line, the shutoff valve is not fully open, or the supply tube is kinked and restricting flow.
- **Freezer too warm** The freezer temperature is above the range needed for ice making, preventing proper freeze cycles.
- **Ice jam or bin not seated** Ice is caught in the ejector arm, the dispenser chute is clogged with clumped ice, or the storage bin is not installed correctly.
- **Installation or door alignment problem** On recently installed units, the door is misaligned or interfering with cabinets, or wiring and tubing at the freezer door hinge are loose or pinched.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Verify the ice maker is turned on and the shutoff arm or switch is in the correct operating position.
2. Check that the ice storage bin is fully seated and no ice is jamming the ejector arm or dispenser chute.
3. Inspect the water supply line to confirm the refrigerator is connected to a cold water source, the shutoff valve is fully open, and the 1/4 in. (6.35 mm) O.D. soft copper or PEX tubing is not kinked or pinched.
4. Confirm the freezer temperature is cold enough by checking the control settings and adjusting them colder if the compartment feels warm.
5. Perform an ice maker reset by pressing and holding the reset button if present, or unplug the refrigerator for one minute, reconnect power, and wait two hours for ice production to resume.
6. If the unit was recently installed, check door alignment and cabinet clearance, and inspect wiring and tube connections at the freezer door hinge for proper seating.
7. If ice is present but not dispensing, clear the dispenser chute and remove any clumped ice from the bin.
8. If all checks pass and the unit still fails to produce or eject ice, diagnose or replace the ice maker assembly or water-delivery components.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a technician if the ice maker remains off after a reset and power cycle, if you cannot locate or access the water supply shutoff valve, or if the freezer door hinge area shows damaged wiring or tubing that requires disassembly. A pro can perform component-level diagnosis of the ice maker assembly and water inlet valve and handle refrigerant-side issues if the freezer is running too warm despite correct control settings.
