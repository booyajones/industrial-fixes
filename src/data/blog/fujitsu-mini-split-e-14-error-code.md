## Fujitsu E:14 Error Code — What It Means

E:14 on a Fujitsu mini split indicates an outdoor PCB failure. This code tells you the system has detected a fault in the outdoor unit's printed circuit board (also called the outdoor control board or inverter PCB depending on your model). Unlike sensor or airflow faults, this code points to a problem with the electronics that manage compressor and fan operation in the outdoor cabinet. The outdoor unit stops running when this fault is present.

The fault is usually triggered by a failed board, a loose or corroded wire connector on the board, or an open circuit in the outdoor wiring. Power quality issues like voltage drop or poor grounding can also cause the outdoor PCB to register a fault and throw E:14.

## Common Causes

- **Failed outdoor PCB or inverter PCB** The control board in the outdoor unit has malfunctioned internally and can no longer manage system operation.
- **Loose or disconnected connectors on the outdoor board** Wire harness connectors on the outdoor PCB have worked loose, corroded, or been disturbed during service.
- **Open wiring or cable fault in outdoor unit** A broken or open circuit in the outdoor unit wiring prevents signals or power from reaching the board correctly.
- **Miswired connections after installation or service** Connections to the outdoor board were reversed or incorrectly terminated, causing the board to detect a fault.
- **Voltage drop or poor grounding at outdoor unit** Inadequate incoming power or a missing or corroded ground connection stresses the outdoor PCB and triggers a fault.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Turn off power to both the indoor and outdoor units at the breaker or disconnect. Wait 60 seconds, restore power, and observe whether the E:14 code returns after the system restarts.
2. Remove the outdoor unit service panel and visually inspect all wire harnesses and connectors on the outdoor PCB. Look for loose plugs, corrosion, burn marks, or physical damage.
3. Reseat each connector on the outdoor control board by unplugging and firmly reconnecting it. Check that no pins are bent and that each plug locks securely into its socket.
4. Check incoming power quality at the outdoor unit disconnect. Verify that voltage is stable and that the ground wire is intact and making solid contact with the chassis.
5. Inspect all internal wiring in the outdoor unit for cuts, pinches, or open circuits. Trace each harness from the board to motors, capacitors, and terminal blocks.
6. If all wiring and power checks pass and the code persists after a reset, replace the outdoor PCB or inverter PCB (exact name depends on your model). Follow the manufacturer's wiring diagram to transfer connections to the new board.
7. Restore power, run a full cooling or heating cycle, and confirm that the E:14 code does not reappear and that the outdoor fan and compressor operate normally.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed HVAC technician if you are not comfortable working with line-voltage wiring or printed circuit boards. E:14 requires verifying power quality, diagnosing board-level faults, and handling refrigerant-side components during board replacement. A technician has the proper tools to test voltage, check grounding, and swap the outdoor PCB safely without voiding warranties or risking electrical shock. If your system is still under warranty, contact Fujitsu or your installer before attempting any board replacement to preserve coverage.
