## Electrolux Dryer E71 Error Code — What It Means

The E71 error code on an Electrolux dryer indicates an outlet control thermistor fault. This means the main control board cannot read the outlet (exhaust) air temperature sensor signal correctly. The thermistor monitors the temperature of air leaving the drum, and when the control loses that signal, it throws E71 and stops the cycle to prevent overheating or unsafe operation.

## Common Causes

- **Failed outlet thermistor** The outlet temperature sensor itself has drifted out of range or failed open or short, so it no longer provides a valid resistance signal to the control board.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connectors** The plug or terminals between the thermistor and the harness have worked loose, corroded, or burned, breaking the signal path.
- **Damaged thermistor wiring harness** The wire insulation or conductors between the sensor and the control board are cut, pinched, melted, or shorted, preventing proper communication.
- **Defective main electronic control board** If the thermistor and wiring both test good, the control board itself may have a failed input circuit that cannot read the sensor.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the dryer** from the wall outlet and turn off the circuit breaker to make sure no power is present during diagnosis or repair.
2. **Access the outlet thermistor**, usually located on the blower housing or exhaust duct near the outlet of the drum, by removing the top or rear panel according to your model's service manual.
3. **Inspect the thermistor wiring harness and connectors** for looseness, corrosion, burned terminals, or damaged insulation, and repair or replace any damaged wiring or connectors you find.
4. **Disconnect the thermistor from the harness** and measure its resistance with a multimeter at room temperature, expecting approximately 4.9 kΩ to 6.5 kΩ (around 5,000 Ω at 70 °F).
5. **Replace the outlet thermistor** if the resistance reading is out of range or if the sensor does not respond smoothly to gentle heating.
6. **Test the circuit at the harness connector** with the thermistor disconnected, and if the harness or board-side connector shows a short or open when it should not, replace the wiring harness.
7. **Replace the main electronic control board** if the thermistor and wiring both test good, the connections are clean and tight, and the E71 code persists after reassembly and a test cycle.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if you are not comfortable working with electrical connections or multimeter diagnostics, if the dryer cabinet disassembly is beyond your skill level, or if you have replaced the thermistor and inspected the wiring but the E71 code returns. A professional has model-specific service literature, the correct OEM parts cross-reference, and the board-level diagnostic tools to isolate control board faults quickly and safely.
