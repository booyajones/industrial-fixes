## Maytag Dishwasher Not Dispensing Detergent — What's Happening

When your Maytag dishwasher is not dispensing detergent, the detergent cup lid is either not opening at the correct time during the wash cycle or the lid opens but the detergent stays stuck inside the cup and never washes away. This is a dispenser-opening problem, not a fault code.

Maytag treats this as a mechanical issue with the dispenser assembly or something physically blocking the door. The detergent dispenser and rinse aid door are replaceable parts when they no longer open or close properly.

[Jump to Fix](#fix)

## Most Likely Causes

- **Improper loading blocking the dispenser door** Large dishes, pans, or the upper rack set too low can physically block the dispenser door from swinging open fully during the cycle.
- **Old or caked detergent sticking the door** Detergent exposed to air can clump and cake, sticking the door shut or preventing the detergent from dissolving and washing away.
- **Dirty dispenser area preventing latch movement** Food debris or dried detergent buildup around the latch, hinge, or cup can keep the door from moving freely.
- **Insufficient water supply or fill** If the water supply valve is off or water delivery is restricted, the dishwasher cannot fill properly and dissolve the detergent.
- **Failed dispenser mechanism** The dispenser door, latch, spring, hinge pin, or full dispenser assembly can fail mechanically and prevent opening.
- **Control board not sending dispense signal** A bad main control board can prevent the dispense command from being issued, though this is checked only after ruling out mechanical causes.

## How to Diagnose and Fix {#fix}

1. Inspect the detergent cup after a cycle to see if detergent is still caked inside, the lid is still closed, or the cup is empty but detergent never washed away.
2. Check loading and verify that large cookware or the upper rack is not blocking the dispenser door, then reload per Maytag guidance and run a test cycle.
3. Replace old or clumped detergent with fresh automatic dishwasher detergent and store it sealed in a cool, dry place.
4. Clean the dispenser area thoroughly, removing any buildup around the door, latch, and cup so the lid can move freely.
5. Confirm the water supply valve is fully on and that the dishwasher is filling normally during the cycle.
6. Inspect the dispenser mechanics for a broken hinge pin, weak or broken spring, damaged latch, warped lid, or visible linkage failure.
7. If all mechanical checks pass and the dispenser still does not open, test whether the control board is issuing the dispense command and investigate the board or wiring as needed.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a pro if you've cleaned the dispenser, verified loading, confirmed water supply, and replaced the detergent but the door still will not open or the detergent remains stuck. A technician can inspect the dispenser assembly for broken internal parts like the hinge pin, spring, or latch and replace the full assembly or test the control board if the mechanical side checks out. Maytag dispenser kits are model-specific, so a professional can confirm the correct replacement part number and handle the installation.
