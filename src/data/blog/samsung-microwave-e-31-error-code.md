## Samsung Microwave E-31 Error — What It Means

The E-31 error code on Samsung microwaves indicates an open gas sensor error. The control board expects to read a complete circuit from the gas sensor but instead sees an open circuit condition. This means the sensor signal is not reaching the control, either because of a disconnected wire, a failed sensor element, or a problem with the control board input itself.

Samsung's official guidance is to unplug the microwave or turn off the breaker for 60 seconds, then restore power. If the code returns after the reset, Samsung recommends contacting a service technician.

## Common Causes

- **Unplugged or loose sensor connector** The gas sensor harness has worked loose or was not fully seated during a previous service, breaking the circuit.
- **Damaged wiring or harness** Wires in the sensor circuit have been pinched, burnt, corroded, or damaged by moisture, creating an open path.
- **Failed gas sensor element** The sensor itself has failed internally and reads open, even though the wiring is intact.
- **Control board input failure** The main control board is not reading the sensor correctly, even when the sensor and wiring test good.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Unplug the microwave** or switch off the circuit breaker and wait 60 seconds, then restore power to attempt a control reset as Samsung recommends.
2. **Confirm the exact model** and verify the display shows E-31 and not a similar code from another Samsung product line.
3. **Remove power completely** for safety, then open the cabinet and locate the gas sensor and its wiring harness on your model.
4. **Inspect all connectors** in the gas sensor circuit for looseness, corrosion, heat damage, or moisture, and reseat any loose plugs firmly.
5. **Test the sensor circuit** for continuity from the sensor through the harness to the control board, an open reading confirms a broken path or failed sensor.
6. **Replace the gas sensor** if the sensor itself reads open and the wiring and connectors are intact and undamaged.
7. **Replace the main control board** if the sensor and all wiring test normal but the E-31 code persists after reassembly and power-on.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a qualified appliance technician if the error returns after a 60-second reset, if you are not comfortable working inside the microwave cabinet with power disconnected, or if you lack a multimeter to test the sensor circuit. Because the exact sensor specifications are not published by Samsung in consumer-facing documents, a technician with access to the service manual for your model can measure resistance and voltage correctly and source the correct replacement sensor or control board. If the microwave is still under warranty, contact Samsung or an authorized service center before opening the unit.
