## Navien Tankless Water Heater E184 Error — What It Means

The E184 error code on a Navien tankless water heater indicates a communication failure between internal components or control boards. This typically means the main control board cannot communicate with a secondary component such as a remote controller, a cascade board in multi-unit systems, or another internal circuit. The error may appear intermittently or lock out the heater entirely until resolved.

Because communication errors can stem from loose connections, corroded terminals, damaged wiring, or failed circuit boards, diagnosis requires careful inspection of electrical pathways and may require voltage testing or board replacement. Navien systems rely on low-voltage signals between boards, so even minor wiring issues can trigger this fault.

## Before You Replace Anything

Homeowners often replace the main control board without first checking wiring connections and terminals. Inspect all connectors for looseness, corrosion, or moisture before ordering any circuit board.

## Common Causes

- **Loose or corroded wiring connection (~40%)** Vibration or moisture can loosen plugs or corrode terminals between the main control board and other components, breaking the communication path.
- **Failed main control board (~25%)** The main PCB can fail due to age, power surges, or moisture intrusion, losing the ability to send or receive signals.
- **Failed remote controller or display (~15%)** If a remote controller or external display is installed, a fault in that device or its wiring can trigger a communication error.
- **Cascade or accessory board fault (~12%)** In multi-unit installations or systems with accessory boards, a failed secondary board can disrupt communication with the main controller.
- **Damaged wiring harness (~8%)** Rodents, sharp edges, or improper installation can fray or cut wires between boards, severing the signal path.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. **Turn off power** at the circuit breaker and shut off the gas supply to the water heater.
2. **Remove the front cover** by unscrewing the fasteners and lifting the panel away to expose the internal components.
3. **Inspect all wiring connectors** on the main control board, cascade board (if present), and remote controller interface for looseness, corrosion, or moisture. Disconnect each plug, wipe terminals clean with a dry cloth, and firmly reseat.
4. **Check for visible wire damage** along cable runs, looking for pinched, frayed, or chewed insulation. Repair or replace any damaged wiring.
5. **Restore power and gas**, then monitor the unit during a hot-water draw. If the E184 error persists, consult the wiring diagram in your owner's manual and test voltages at the connectors with a multimeter, or proceed to call a qualified technician.
6. **If a remote controller is installed**, disconnect it temporarily and test the unit. A fault in the remote or its cable can trigger communication errors.
7. **Replace the main control board** only after confirming all wiring is intact and all other boards and accessories have been ruled out. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for transferring settings and connectors to the new board.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a licensed plumber or Navien service technician if you are uncomfortable working with electrical components, if the error persists after reseating all connectors, or if diagnosis requires voltage testing and board-level troubleshooting. Communication faults can be complex to trace, especially in cascade systems or installations with multiple accessories, and misdiagnosis can lead to unnecessary parts replacement. A qualified technician has the tools and training to test signal paths, interpret diagnostic modes, and replace boards correctly. Because tankless water heaters involve both gas and electricity, professional service reduces the risk of unsafe repairs or voiding your warranty.

**Rough cost:** A pro service call runs about $150-400.
