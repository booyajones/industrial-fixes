## Frigidaire Dryer AL-29 Error Code — What It Means

The AL-29 code does not appear in published Frigidaire service documentation under that exact name. Based on Frigidaire and Electrolux dryer fault conventions, the intended code is most likely E29, which indicates a water-level or pressure-sensing system fault. This code appears when the control board cannot read correct pressure signals from the sensor or switch, often due to a blocked tube, faulty switch, or wiring issue.

On steam-equipped dryers, E29 can also relate to water supply or internal plumbing problems. The dryer will not run normally until the pressure-sensing circuit is restored. This is not a heating element or airflow problem, despite the way the code may sound.

## Common Causes

- **Kinked or blocked pressure tube** The small rubber or vinyl hose connecting the pressure switch to the dryer drum or tub can become pinched, cracked, or filled with lint and moisture, preventing accurate pressure readings.
- **Failed pressure switch or water-level sensor** The switch itself may stick, corrode internally, or lose electrical continuity, causing the control board to log a fault even when the tube is clear.
- **Loose or corroded wiring connector** The harness plug at the pressure switch or the control board can work loose over time or develop oxidation that breaks the signal path.
- **Steam supply or plumbing fault (steam models only)** If your dryer has a steam function, a blocked water inlet, failed valve, or clogged internal reservoir can trigger pressure-related codes.
- **Main control board misinterpretation** In rare cases the board itself fails to process valid sensor signals correctly, though this is less common than mechanical or wiring issues.

## Step-by-Step Fix {#fix}

1. Unplug the dryer and turn off any water supply if it is a steam model.
2. Remove the top or rear access panel to locate the pressure switch, a small round or rectangular component usually mounted near the control board or on the blower housing.
3. Inspect the pressure tube (a small-diameter flexible hose) for kinks, cracks, blockages, or moisture inside, and clear or replace it if damaged.
4. Disconnect the wire harness from the pressure switch and examine the terminals and connector for corrosion, soot, or looseness, then clean or reseat as needed.
5. Test the pressure switch for continuity using a multimeter, following the procedure in your model's service sheet, or replace the switch if you cannot confirm proper operation.
6. Reconnect all wiring and tubing, reassemble the panels, restore power, and run a test cycle to verify the code clears.
7. If the code persists after replacing the switch and tubing and confirming clean connections, consult the service manual for control-board diagnostics or contact a qualified technician.

## When to Call a Pro

Call a professional if you cannot locate the pressure switch on your model, if testing electrical components is outside your comfort zone, or if the code returns after you have replaced the switch, tube, and verified all connections. Steam dryers with internal plumbing or complex control boards also benefit from factory-trained diagnosis. If your dryer is still under warranty or you are uncomfortable working with 240-volt wiring, a service technician can confirm the exact code, cross-reference it to your model's fault table, and order the correct parts the first time.
