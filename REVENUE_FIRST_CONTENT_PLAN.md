Date: 2026-04-24
Purpose: stop broad/random publishing and focus writing on the pages most likely to drive revenue fast.

1. Purchase intent after reading
2. Part or equipment order value
3. Search demand / repeatability
4. Affiliate fit today or soon after approval
5. Ability to funnel into contractor leads or replacement decisions
